:::warning Important
On initial load, the POS will get the default tax settings from WooCommerce. In general, users should keep the same settings as WooCommerce unless they have a specific need. Having different tax settings than the server will almost certainly cause the POS and WooCommerce to calculate different tax values, which can lead to discrepancies and problems with order synchronization. The one exception is [Calculate Tax Based On](#calculate-tax-based-on), which WCPOS 1.x does not copy from WooCommerce.
:::

## View All Tax Rates {#view-all-tax-rates}

Click the **View all tax rates** button to see all tax rates that have been downloaded to the POS. This shows:

- Tax rate names
- Tax rate percentages
- Applicable regions/locations

This is useful for verifying that the correct tax rates are loaded and for debugging tax calculation issues.

## Tax Calculation Settings {#tax-calculation-settings}

### Prices Entered With Tax {#prices-entered-with-tax}

Determines whether product prices include tax:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Yes** | Product prices include tax. The POS calculates tax by working backwards from the total price |
| **No** | Product prices do not include tax. The POS adds tax on top of the entered price |

Stores using [WooCommerce Tax](/integrations/woocommerce-tax) with automated taxes on are locked to **No** by WooCommerce, and the POS should mirror that.

### Calculate Tax Based On {#calculate-tax-based-on}

Determines the address used for tax calculation:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Customer shipping address** | Tax rates based on where the order will be shipped |
| **Customer billing address** | Tax rates based on the customer's billing address |
| **Shop base address** | Tax rates based on your store's physical location |

In WCPOS 1.x, including every 1.11 release, this setting is not copied from WooCommerce. The POS starts on **Shop base address** whatever WooCommerce's own *Calculate tax based on* option says, and **Restore Server Settings** sets it back to **Shop base address**. Changing the option in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax` does not change it in the POS.

To tax POS sales on the customer's address instead:

1. In the POS, open **Settings > Tax**.
2. Set **Calculate Tax Based On** to **Customer shipping address** or **Customer billing address**.
3. Repeat on each till. The POS does not share this choice between devices.

The POS saves the choice on each order, and WooCommerce calculates that order's tax from the same address, so the till and your store agree. After **Restore Server Settings**, set it again.

With WCPOS Pro, **Shop base address** means the address chosen in the store's own *Calculate Tax Based On* setting: the store's address (the default) or your WooCommerce base location. See [Setting Up Stores](/stores/setup).

Stores using [WooCommerce Tax](/integrations/woocommerce-tax) with automated taxes on are locked to the customer shipping address by WooCommerce. A different choice here still applies to POS orders, on both the POS and your store.

### Shipping Tax Class {#shipping-tax-class}

Determines how shipping costs are taxed:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Shipping tax class based on cart items** | Shipping tax is calculated based on the tax class of items in the cart |
| **Standard** | Shipping is taxed at the standard tax rate |
| **Reduced rate** | Shipping is taxed at a reduced tax rate |
| **Zero rate** | No tax is applied to shipping costs |

### Rounding {#rounding}

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Round tax at subtotal level** | Tax is calculated for the entire subtotal and then rounded, rather than rounding tax for each individual line item |

WCPOS follows WooCommerce's own tax maths so an order totals the same whether it's rung up at the till or placed on your website:

- **Full precision** — line and order totals are held at full precision and only rounded for display, so the amount saved to the order is the exact figure WooCommerce expects rather than a value pre-rounded to the display decimals.
- **Store rounding** — rounding uses your currency's cents precision and honours the **Round tax at subtotal level** setting above.
- **Compound taxes** — when a rate is marked *compound*, rates are applied in WooCommerce's priority order (`tax_rate_priority`), so a tax that stacks on top of another is calculated after it.

## Display Settings {#display-settings}

### Display Prices in the Shop {#display-prices-in-the-shop}

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Including tax** | Prices shown include tax amounts |
| **Excluding tax** | Prices shown do not include tax |

### Display Prices During Cart and Checkout {#display-prices-during-cart-and-checkout}

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Including tax** | Cart and checkout prices include tax |
| **Excluding tax** | Cart and checkout prices exclude tax |

### Price Display Suffix {#price-display-suffix}

Text that appears after prices to indicate tax status (e.g., "inc. VAT" or "ex. VAT").

### Display Tax Totals {#display-tax-totals}

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **As a single total** | All taxes are combined into one total line |
| **Itemized** | Each tax rate is shown as a separate line item |

## Restore Server Settings {#restore-server-settings}

Click to fetch and apply the current WooCommerce tax settings from your server. In WCPOS 1.x this also sets **Calculate Tax Based On** back to **Shop base address** (see [above](#calculate-tax-based-on)).

:::tip How and when to use it
WooCommerce is the source of truth for tax settings — the POS should mirror it. **Restore Server Settings** is the safe way to pull the latest configuration from your store, and is the right action whenever you've changed tax settings in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax` and want the POS to pick them up.

The only time to think twice: if you've **deliberately** changed a POS-side tax setting, such as **Calculate Tax Based On** set to a customer address, Restore Server Settings will overwrite it. Set it again afterwards.
:::

## Common Issues {#common-issues}

### POS and WooCommerce Showing Different Totals {#pos-and-woocommerce-showing-different-totals}

The most frequent tax problem is a mismatch between POS tax settings and WooCommerce tax settings. When these diverge, the same order will calculate different totals depending on where it's placed. Always keep POS settings in sync — use **Restore Server Settings** after changing anything in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax`.

Stores using WooCommerce Tax have one more cause to rule out: the first sale at a location your store has never taxed. See [WooCommerce Tax](/integrations/woocommerce-tax#what-to-expect).

### Tax-Inclusive Pricing Confusion {#tax-inclusive-pricing-confusion}

If "Prices Entered With Tax" is set to **Yes** but "Display Prices in the Shop" is set to **Excluding tax**, the displayed price will be lower than the entered price. This is correct behaviour — the POS is stripping the tax component for display — but it can surprise cashiers if they're expecting to see the sticker price.

### Customer Address and Tax Calculation {#customer-address-and-tax-calculation}

When "Calculate Tax Based On" is set to **Customer shipping address** or **Customer billing address**, the POS needs a valid address to look up the correct rate. If a walk-in customer has no address on file, WCPOS falls back to the **shop base address** for calculation. This is usually the right behaviour for in-store sales, but if you see unexpected rates for specific customers, check their address details.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

### Tax Amounts Are Incorrect {#tax-amounts-are-incorrect}

1. Go to **Tax Settings** in the POS and click **Restore Server Settings** to pull the latest configuration from WooCommerce.
2. Click **View all tax rates** and confirm the expected rates appear. If rates are missing, check that they're properly configured in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax > Standard rates`. With [WooCommerce Tax](/integrations/woocommerce-tax) automated taxes on, do not add rows by hand: a location's rows appear on the first sale there, and the POS picks them up on its next change check, or straight away with **Clear & re-download** on the **Tax rates** row in Store health.
3. Verify the product's tax class — products assigned to a "Zero rate" tax class won't have tax applied regardless of settings.

### Tax Is Showing as Zero {#tax-is-showing-as-zero}

- Check that tax is enabled in WooCommerce (`WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > General > Enable tax rates and calculations`).
- Use **View all tax rates** in the POS to confirm rates have been downloaded. If the list is empty, try a full sync.
- Confirm the product's tax status is set to "Taxable" (not "None" or "Shipping only").

### Rounding Discrepancies {#rounding-discrepancies}

Small rounding differences (a penny or two) between POS and WooCommerce can occur when "Round tax at subtotal level" is enabled in one system but not the other. Ensure this setting matches in both places.
