## Re-Installing a Previous Version {#re-installing-a-previous-version}

To go back to an earlier version of the plugin, you turn off auto-updates, download the version you want, and upload it over the one you have. Nothing is deleted on the way, so your orders, settings and receipt templates stay where they are.

:::warning Don't delete the plugin first
Upload the `.zip` over the plugin you have; that keeps everything. On 2.0, deleting the plugin from **Plugins** runs WCPOS's clean-up: it signs every cashier out, so each till has to sign in again, and it clears the sync data each till rebuilds. There is no reason to delete.
:::

If you are leaving 2.0, read [Rolling back](/getting-started/upgrading-to-2#rolling-back) first. With WCPOS Pro, roll back WCPOS Pro only. It contains the free plugin.

### Turn off auto-updates {#turn-off-auto-updates}

With auto-updates on, WordPress installs the newest version again by itself.

1. Go to **WP Admin → Plugins**.
2. On the WCPOS row (or the WCPOS Pro row), look at the **Automatic Updates** column. If it reads **Disable auto-updates**, click it.

### Download the version you need {#download}

Keep the `.zip` file zipped. WordPress installs the `.zip`, not a folder.

**WCPOS:**

1. Open the [WCPOS Advanced View](https://wordpress.org/plugins/woocommerce-pos/advanced/) on WordPress.org.
2. Scroll to **Advanced Options**. Under **Previous Versions**, choose the version you want.
3. Click **Download**.

**WCPOS Pro:**

1. Sign in to your account at [wcpos.com](https://wcpos.com/account/downloads) and open **Downloads**.
2. In **Version history**, find the version you want.
3. Click **Download** on that row.

To leave 2.0, choose the newest **1.11** release, not an older 1.x.

### Upload it over the current version {#install}

1. Go to **WP Admin → Plugins** and click **Add Plugin** at the top of the page.
2. Click **Upload Plugin**, choose the `.zip` file you downloaded, and click **Install Now**.
3. WordPress says **This plugin is already installed.** and shows the **Current** and **Uploaded** versions side by side. Check that **Uploaded** shows the version you want.
4. Click **Replace current with uploaded**.
5. WordPress says **Plugin downgraded successfully.** If it offers **Activate Plugin**, click it. Otherwise the plugin was already active and stays active.

Leave auto-updates off until you are ready to update again, then turn them back on, or later fixes won't arrive by themselves.

If you encounter any issues during the re-installation process or need further assistance, please contact our support team at support@wcpos.com.
