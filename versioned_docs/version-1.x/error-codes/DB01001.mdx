# DB01001: Connection Failed

## What This Means {#what-this-means}

The POS could not connect to its local database. WCPOS uses a local database to store products, customers, and other data for fast access and offline functionality.

## Common Causes {#common-causes}

- **Storage full** — Device has no storage space left
- **Database corrupted** — Local database files are damaged
- **Permission issue** — App doesn't have storage permissions
- **Browser storage disabled** — IndexedDB is disabled (web version)

## How to Fix {#how-to-fix}

### 1. Check Storage Space {#1-check-storage-space}

Ensure your device has available storage:
- Free up space if storage is full
- Delete unused apps or files
- Check available disk space

### 2. Clear App Data {#2-clear-app-data}

:::warning Check for unsaved work first
Clearing local data or reinstalling the app deletes anything on this device that hasn't reached your store — including sales taken while offline, open carts, and edits that didn't save. Before you clear anything, open **Store health → Logs** and check the header shows **0 stuck records** and **0 changes waiting to send**, then finish or note every open cart. If anything is waiting, or you can't open Store health, don't clear — ask your store owner or contact support.
:::

Only if steps 1, 3 and 4 didn't help, and only after the check above, reset the local database:
- **Desktop app:** Look for "Clear Data" in settings
- **Web browser:** Clear site data for the POS domain
- **Note:** data already on your store downloads again; anything that never reached your store is gone

### 3. Check Browser Settings (Web) {#3-check-browser-settings-web}

If using the web version:
1. Ensure IndexedDB is enabled
2. Don't use private/incognito mode (limited storage)
3. Allow the site to store data

### 4. Check Permissions {#4-check-permissions}

On desktop:
- Ensure the app has permission to write to its data directory
- Check if antivirus is blocking database access

### 5. Reinstall the App {#5-reinstall-the-app}

:::warning Check for unsaved work first
Clearing local data or reinstalling the app deletes anything on this device that hasn't reached your store — including sales taken while offline, open carts, and edits that didn't save. Before you clear anything, open **Store health → Logs** and check the header shows **0 stuck records** and **0 changes waiting to send**, then finish or note every open cart. If anything is waiting, or you can't open Store health, don't clear — ask your store owner or contact support.
:::

Only if other solutions fail, and only after the check above:
1. Uninstall the POS app
2. Restart your device
3. Reinstall the app
4. Login and sync data

## Data Recovery {#data-recovery}

If you have unsaved data:
- The app may have pending changes that couldn't be synced
- Contact support if you need help recovering data
- Data already on your store downloads again after a clear, but sales that never reached your store exist only on this device — clearing or reinstalling deletes them, so contact support before either
