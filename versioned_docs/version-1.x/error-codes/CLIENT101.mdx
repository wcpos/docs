## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS could not finish starting.

From the till this looks like a blank or stuck screen, or a loading spinner that never clears. Restart once, then export diagnostics with the app version and device engine if startup still fails.

## Your data {#your-data}

Starting the app does not change your saved data — any sales already recorded on this device stay on the device. Do not clear site data or reinstall before contacting support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Restart the app once; on web, fully reload the tab.
2. If startup fails again, note the app version and the device — the browser console records the startup error even when the POS cannot show it.
3. Do not clear site data or reinstall to fix startup — a till with unsent sales loses them; contact support first.
4. Export debug info if the app gets far enough to open the Logs screen; otherwise contact support with the console error.
