## What this means {#what-this-means}

You counted the drawer and closed the register. The till shows the register as counted; your store still has the session open.

The counted figures are held on this device and are sent again on the next connection, but until your store accepts them the shift is not closed and will not appear as a completed count.

## Your data {#your-data}

Your count is saved on this device. Nothing has been lost, and nothing needs recounting — but the figures exist only here until your store accepts them.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Leave the counted figures on screen and try closing again. The till keeps them, and most refusals clear on a second attempt.
2. If the close needed a manager's approval, ask the manager to approve it again. An approval is checked at the moment of the close and does not carry over to a later attempt.
3. Write the counted totals down before the device is handed over, restarted or taken off the network.
4. **Store admin:** check in WP Admin that the session is still open, and that the closing cashier's account has permission to manage cash. A session already closed on another device cannot be closed again here.
