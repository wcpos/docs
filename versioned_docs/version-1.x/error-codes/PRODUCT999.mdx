## What this means {#what-this-means}

Loading or updating products hit an unexpected problem.

Repeat what you were doing and check it saved. If products still look wrong, tap **Copy debug info** and send it to support.

## Your data {#your-data}

Sales and orders are not affected. If you were changing a product, check the change saved.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Repeat what you were doing and check it saved — most one-off product loading problems clear the second time.
2. If products still look wrong, tap **Copy debug info** on the **Logs** tab and send it to support.
