## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your store's list of changes moved unexpectedly, so WCPOS is re-checking it by itself. Nothing is lost.

Nothing to do — keep selling. WCPOS re-checks your store's changes by itself. If prices or stock still look out of date, tap **Check everything now** in **Store health → Database**.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing is lost, and nothing on this device is at risk. Until the re-check finishes, some prices or stock on this device may be out of date.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Keep selling — nothing on this device is lost, and the re-check runs by itself. The log entry may say what moved — for example that some store changes were skipped, or that the store's list went backwards. Either way it can be any kind of data.
2. If prices or stock still look out of date, open **Store health → Database** and tap **Check everything now**.
3. If a record still looks wrong after that check, or this code keeps reappearing, use **Copy debug info** on the **Logs** tab and contact support. Don't clear data to fix it.
