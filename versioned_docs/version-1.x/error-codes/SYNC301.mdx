## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS fell behind your store's changes and is catching up by itself. Nothing is lost.

Nothing to do — keep selling. WCPOS fetches the store changes it missed by itself. If prices or stock still look out of date later, tap **Check everything now** in **Store health → Database**.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing is lost, and nothing on this device is at risk. Until the catch-up finishes, some prices or stock on this device may be out of date.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Keep selling — nothing on this device is lost, and the catch-up runs by itself. The log entry may say how many store changes were missed; they can be any kind of data.
2. If prices or stock still look out of date later, open **Store health → Database** and tap **Check everything now**.
3. If a record still looks wrong after that check, or this code keeps reappearing, use **Copy debug info** on the **Logs** tab and contact support. Don't clear data to fix it.
