## What this means {#what-this-means}

The selected printer cannot be reached.

Check that the printer is powered on and connected, then try printing again.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Check the printer is powered on and its cable or network connection is seated.
2. For a printer the till talks to directly over the local network, confirm the till and printer are on the same network and the printer's address has not changed — DHCP renewals can move it; if it has, update the printer's address where you set it up in the POS.
3. For a cloud-printed receipt (CloudPRNT, Star Online, Server Direct Print, PrintNode), the till and printer do not need to share a network: check that the printer has internet access and is online in the provider's own dashboard, and that the provider settings in the POS still match — see [Cloud printing](../receipts/cloud-printing.mdx).
4. Print a self-test from the printer itself to confirm the hardware works, then try again from the POS.
