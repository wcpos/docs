## What this means {#what-this-means}

The selected printer cannot be reached.

Check that the printer is powered on and connected, then try printing again.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Check the printer is powered on and its cable or network connection is seated.
2. For a printer the till talks to directly over the local network, confirm the till and printer are on the same network and the printer's address has not changed — DHCP renewals can move it; if it has, update the printer's address where you set it up in the POS.
3. For a cloud-printed receipt the till and printer do not need to share a network; what to check depends on the delivery model ([Cloud printing](../receipts/cloud-printing.mdx#status)):
   - **Star CloudPRNT or Epson Server Direct Print** (the printer polls WCPOS): the printer card in the POS shows **Waiting** until the printer has checked in. Confirm the printer has internet access and that its poll URL and token match what WCPOS generated — see [Printer stuck on Waiting](../receipts/cloud-printing.mdx#troubleshooting).
   - **Star Online**: the card shows the device status Star Online reports; a device marked **Offline** or **Unknown** needs attention in the Star Online dashboard, not on the till.
   - **PrintNode**: the computer running the PrintNode desktop client must be awake with the client online, and the printer selected in the POS must match the one the client reports — see [PrintNode job never prints](../receipts/cloud-printing.mdx#troubleshooting).
4. Print a self-test from the printer itself to confirm the hardware works, then try again from the POS.
