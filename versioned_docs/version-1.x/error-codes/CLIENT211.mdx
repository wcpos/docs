## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS stopped because of a device-level crash.

Restart WCPOS and export diagnostics if the crash repeats.

## Your data {#your-data}

The outcome could not be confirmed — if you were saving something when it crashed, verify it completed before retrying.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Restart WCPOS — the crash happened at device level, not in your data.
2. Install pending updates for the app and the device's operating system — native crashes are often already-fixed bugs. On a managed till, whoever manages the device does this.
3. If the crash repeats on the same action, export debug info and contact support, noting exactly what was tapped when it crashed.
