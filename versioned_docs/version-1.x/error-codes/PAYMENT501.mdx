## What this means {#what-this-means}

The customer paid this order online — before reaching the counter, or on their own device while the cashier was ringing it up. The till took a payment for it as well, and your store only reported the online payment afterwards.

The customer has been charged twice. One of those charges needs refunding.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing is hidden and nothing is lost. The till's payment is kept in the order's ledger, marked failed with the reason `order_already_paid`, so the order carries a record of both charges. The online payment is the one your store counts as settling the order.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Open the order from the attention list on the Orders screen.
2. Confirm the online payment in WooCommerce — check the order's payment method and transaction id so you know which charge settled it.
3. Refund the payment taken at the till, through whatever you took it on: cash from the drawer, or a refund on the card terminal or gateway.
4. Note on the order what you refunded and why, so the double charge is explained if the customer or your accountant asks later.
5. Do not void or delete the order. It is a legitimate paid order; only the extra payment is wrong.
