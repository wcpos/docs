**Symptom:** after a refund, a red pop-up reads "The refund was returned to the customer, but the store couldn't keep its record."

**Do this now:** don't refund this order again. The money has already gone back.

:::warning

Refunding again would refund the customer twice.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Don't refund this order again from the till.
2. Open the order in **Orders** and read its order notes. They record the refund the payment provider made.
3. Ask a store admin to record the refund on the order (see below).

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No money is lost: the customer has been refunded. The order just doesn't show the refund yet, so its totals are out until a store admin records it.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Your store is failing to save refund records. Tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** and read its order notes. They record the refund the provider made.
2. Confirm the refund in the payment provider's dashboard.
3. Record it on the order by hand: **Refund**, with the refund via the gateway turned off, so the order's totals match.

</details>
