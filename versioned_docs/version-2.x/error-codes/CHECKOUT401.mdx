**Symptom:** a banner reads **Your store changed this order's totals**, and lists each amount that changed as the till's figure → your store's. There's no pop-up.

**Do this now:** check the new total before you take payment or hand over the goods.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Read the banner. Your store's amounts are the ones that count.
2. **If no payment was taken yet:** take payment for the new total.
3. **If payment was already taken:** check what the card terminal, drawer or gateway took, then charge or refund the difference before the customer leaves.
4. To see every amount that differs, open **Store health → Logs**, press **Errors** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The order is saved with your store's total, and the till stops recalculating it. The banner clears when the order leaves the cart.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If totals keep changing, tell whoever looks after your website, with the order numbers. From the third sale, the banner says so too.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The till and your store are working out totals differently.

1. Open the order in WP Admin and compare its totals with the till's. The till's log entry records which amounts differ.
2. If tax differs, check your store's tax settings first.
3. If a discount or pricing plugin recalculates orders, check what it changed.
4. If the difference has no explanation, send support the order number and the till's **Copy debug info** report.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number.

<summary>Code details for support</summary>
