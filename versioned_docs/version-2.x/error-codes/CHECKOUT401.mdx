## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your store's total for this order differs from what the till showed. The order is saved with the store's total.

Open the order and compare totals before the customer leaves. If money was already taken, charge or refund the difference. If the difference is unexpected, contact support.

## Your data {#your-data}

The order is saved, with your store's total. Payment may or may not have been taken yet — this check runs when the order saves, which can be before payment. If this keeps happening, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact WCPOS support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

Do this before the customer leaves.

1. Open the order and compare its total with what the till showed; [the log entry](#log-entry) records which amounts differ.
2. Check whether payment was already taken, and how much — on a card terminal, its screen or receipt; for cash, the amount tendered; for another gateway, its own record.
3. If money was already taken, charge or refund the difference through your normal process.
   - If no payment has been taken yet, take payment for the order's total as it now shows.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. Open the order in WP Admin and compare its totals with what the till showed; the log entry records which amounts diverged.
2. The usual causes are tax settings that differ from what the till expected, or a discount or pricing plugin recalculating on the server — review the store's tax settings first.
3. If the difference is unexplained, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact support with the order number.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows beside and under it (event code, record, operation, request, details) are for support too; you don't need to read them.
