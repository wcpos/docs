## What this means {#what-this-means}

This change is still on its way to your store.

A sale or change was saved on this device and sent, but your store hadn't confirmed it within the usual wait — usually because the store is slow to answer or the connection is unreliable. WCPOS keeps sending it until the store confirms. The store recognises a change it has already received, so pressing the same button again is safe. Entering the sale or record again by hand is not: that creates a second, separate one — a duplicate.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing is lost. The change is waiting on this device and sends automatically. Don't clear this device's data while it is waiting to send.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

:::warning Don't enter it again
Don't enter the sale or change again by hand — that creates a duplicate.
:::

1. Don't enter the sale or change again by hand — that creates a duplicate.
2. Wait for it to reach your store. A sale already shows in WCPOS (in the cart or, on WCPOS Pro, in **Orders**) because it is saved on this device.
3. To watch it go, open **Store health → Logs**: the header's **changes waiting to send** goes down as changes send, and reads 0 when nothing else is waiting.
4. If it stays waiting for a long time, check this device's connection and whether your store's website loads — see [SYNC121](/error-codes/SYNC121).
5. If it still hasn't appeared once the connection is fine, tap **Copy debug info** in [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry) and contact support.

### Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows beside and under it (event code, record, operation, request, details) are for support too; you don't need to read them.

</JourneyShot>

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. To confirm a sale reached the store, check **WooCommerce → Orders** in WP Admin.
