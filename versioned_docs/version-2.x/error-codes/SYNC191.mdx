**Symptom:** a pop-up says the order or coupon is "still on its way to your store", with a **Help** button. At checkout it adds "Wait a moment, then press Checkout again."

**Do this now:** don't enter the change again: it's saved on this device and sends by itself.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Wait. The change appears once your store confirms it.
2. **If it was a sale at checkout:** press **Checkout** again. Nothing has been charged, and your store recognises the order, so no second one is created.
3. **If it was anything else:** don't enter it again, or you create a second copy.
4. Open **Store health**, then **Database**. **changes waiting to send** reads 0 once your store has it; anything it held or refused is listed there.
5. **If it doesn't go down:** check the register bar for **Offline**. It sends once you're back online. Also check that your store's website loads: see [SYNC121](SYNC121.mdx).

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The change is saved on this device, and the till keeps sending it until your store confirms. Don't clear this device's data meanwhile.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If this appears often, your store is slow to answer the till. Tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The till waited 45 seconds for your store to confirm an order, or 15 seconds for other changes, then stopped waiting. The change is still queued.

1. In WP Admin, open **WooCommerce → Orders** and check the sale arrived once, with its order number.
2. If your store is slow to answer, see [Slow Server](../support/performance/server.mdx).

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number or the change you made.

<SupportContact />

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="SYNC191" />

The wait for the store's answer timed out (WriteOutcomeTimeoutError) and the write stays queued with outcome `still-queued`. The store dedupes a re-sent write on its mutation id.

</details>
