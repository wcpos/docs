**Symptom:** after you press **Connect**, a pop-up reads "The store address is missing or not a valid URL." with a **Help** button. Red text under **Connect** reads "URL is required" or "WCPOS login URL is invalid. Please ensure WCPOS plugin is properly configured".

**Do this now:** type your store's address under **Your store's address** and press **Connect**.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Clear the field and type your store's address under **Your store's address**, for example `mystore.com`. Spaces alone count as empty.
2. Press **Connect**.
3. **If it connects:** press **Sign in with WordPress** and carry on.
4. **If the red text reads "WCPOS login URL is invalid…":** the fault is on your store. Send whoever looks after your website a link to this page.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing on this device or on your store changed. WCPOS stopped before anything was saved.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If the address is right and the login message keeps coming back, your store is sending WCPOS a broken sign-in address. Only whoever looks after your website can fix that.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Your store's REST API index (`/wp-json/`) gives the sign-in address at `authentication` › `wcpos` › `endpoints` › `authorization`. Here it is there but isn't text.

1. Update WCPOS in WP Admin › **Plugins**, then the cashier presses **Connect** again. Don't deactivate and reactivate it instead: reactivating resets every role's POS permissions to the defaults.
2. If that doesn't clear it, open `/wp-json/` (or `/?rest_route=/` if that gives a 404) and look at `authorization`.
3. If it's a list, an object or a number, a security, caching or REST plugin changes it. Switch those off one at a time on a staging copy.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support with the red text and your store's address. Logs can't be opened from this screen.

<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="AUTH411" />

</details>
