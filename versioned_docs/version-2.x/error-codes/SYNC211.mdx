**Symptom:** a pop-up gives your store's reason, or **Store health → Database** lists **1 change never reached your server**. **Logs** says "Your store refused a value it was sent."

**Do this now:** correct the value your store's reason names, then send the change again.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It can hold a change your store doesn't have yet.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **Errors** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).
2. **If it was at checkout:** fix what the reason names in the cart and press **Checkout** again.
3. **If Database lists the change:** correct the value on this device, then press **Send again**. The till says **Queued to send again.** **Discard**, in **⋮**, keeps your store's version.
4. **If Database lists it under "1 change was held — your store asked for a current version":** no value is at fault. Choose between **Send again** and keeping your store's version as [SYNC221](SYNC221.mdx) describes.
5. **If it isn't listed:** enter the value again, corrected, where you entered it: in a record, or on a settings, sign-in or refund screen.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing reached your store. A change listed in **Database** stays on this device until you send or discard it. Otherwise your store's value is back, or there was nothing to save.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If your store keeps refusing a value that looks right, ask whoever looks after your website to check that record.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Find the record named in the log entry in WP Admin and compare the refused value with the till's. A plugin that adds its own checks on your store can reject values the POS accepts.
2. If the entry names a request instead of a record, follow your store's message for that request.
3. When the log has no details, the browser's developer tools (**Network** tab) show your store's response.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the refused value.

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="SYNC211" />

Your store answered with `pos_data_invalid` or `rest_invalid_param`, or with status 400 to a settings or sign-in request. A refused product, variation, customer or coupon edit is reverted and logged as [SYNC202](SYNC202.mdx).

</details>
