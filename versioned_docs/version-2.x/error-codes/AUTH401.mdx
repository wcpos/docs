**Symptom:** your store's address opens in a browser with a security warning instead of your store. WCPOS doesn't show the code AUTH401 at the till.

{/* source: error-registry.json:458, :461. Never raised at 6b2a2cb4f: TLS_UNTRUSTED and AUTH401 appear only in the registry, the generated tables, core.json:2459 and :2575 */}

**Do this now:** open your store's address in a browser on this device and read the warning it shows.

{/* source: error-registry.json:461. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:456 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open your store's address in a browser on this device. The padlock or warning page says what's wrong: expired, self-signed or made for another address.
2. Check the address you use for your store starts with `https://` and matches it exactly, with or without `www`.
3. Send whoever looks after your website a link to this page. Don't get past the warning by switching security off.
4. Once the browser opens your store with no warning, go back to WCPOS and try again.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:461; step 2 error-registry.json:462; step 3 error-registry.json:457 (escalation site-admin), :463, :464; step 4 error-registry.json:455 (retry after-change), :459 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing on your store changed. An untrusted connection is refused before anything is sent.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:456 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Only whoever looks after your website can renew or fix your store's certificate.

{/* source: error-registry.json:457, :463 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The store's TLS certificate isn't trusted: expired, self-signed, or issued for a different domain.

1. Open the store's address in a browser and read the certificate warning.
2. Renew or reissue the certificate for the exact domain the POS uses, with or without `www`.
3. Check automatic renewal. A renewal that fails silently is the most common cause.
4. Don't tell staff to bypass the warning. Fix the certificate.

{/* source: error-registry.json:461-464 */}

</details>
