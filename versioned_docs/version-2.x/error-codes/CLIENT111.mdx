**Symptom:** the till takes a long time to open a store, and **Logs** has an entry reading **Syncing is taking longer than expected to start**. No pop-up appears.

{/* source: core.json:2259 (health.logs.event.engine_ready_stalled, the row title); packages/sync-engine/src/readiness-watchdog.ts:5, :43 (first report after 15 s), :37 (the initial open of the store's local database);
    apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:314-322 (no showToast; first report info, repeats warn) */}

**Do this now:** wait a minute; WCPOS keeps opening the store and finishes on its own when it can.

{/* source: readiness-watchdog.ts:40 (keeps waiting, reports again every 60 s), :45-54 (stops once the store is ready); core.json:2624 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Wait. While the store is still opening, WCPOS adds another entry each minute.
2. Still waiting after that? Reload once: close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web.
3. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type CLIENT111 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).
4. **If the store opens:** there's nothing more to do.
5. **If it fails instead:** WCPOS stops waiting and logs an error with its own code. Open that code's page.

{/* source: step 1 readiness-watchdog.ts:6, :40; step 2 core.json:2624 (reload once), error-registry.json:1541-1560; step 3 catalogue level warn, logs-logic.ts:105-112 (Errors is error only), core.json:828, :694, :2131, :961;
    step 4 readiness-watchdog.ts:45-54; step 5 readiness-watchdog.ts:55-64, sync-log-observer.ts:309-313 (engine.ready-failed has its own code) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. This entry only reports a wait. Sales and changes already on this device stay there.

{/* source: readiness-watchdog.ts:29-41 (emits a diagnostic only, writes nothing); dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1541-1560 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If slow starts happen every day, check this device's connection to your store, then ask whoever looks after your website how fast your store responds.

{/* source: kept from current/CLIENT111.mdx; error-registry.json:1541-1560 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) and say how long the till took to open.
