## What this means {#what-this-means}

The selected printer cannot be reached.

Check that the printer is powered on and connected, then try printing again.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Check the printer is powered on and its cable or network connection is seated.
2. Print a self-test from the printer itself (see its manual). On most network printers the self-test page also shows its current IP address.
3. For a network printer, confirm the till and printer are on the same network. Then open **Settings → Printing**, press **Edit** on the printer under **Printers**, and check that **IP Address** matches the self-test page (and **Port**, if the page shows one) — a router can hand the printer a new address after a restart. Correct them, or use **Scan Network**, then press **Save**.
4. Open the printer's **Edit** screen in **Settings → Printing** and press **Test Print**, then print the receipt again.
