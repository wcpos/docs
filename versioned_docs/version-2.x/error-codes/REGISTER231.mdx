**Symptom:** the register's buttons are replaced by a notice: **This account can't open, count or close the register. A store manager can turn on manage_woocommerce_pos_cash for this role in POS → Access.**

**Do this now:** keep selling, and ask a manager to open, count or close the register.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It holds what was entered, which your store hasn't recorded.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Ask a manager to sign in on this till and do the open, count or close.
2. If the register is mid-count, sales stay blocked until someone with the permission closes it.
3. Once your role has the permission, sign out and sign in again, so the POS reads it afresh.
4. Then do the open, count or close again.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing is lost. The till keeps what was entered, but your store hasn't recorded the open, the cash movement or the close, and doesn't retry them by itself.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Opening, counting, closing and paid in, paid out and no sale all need the cash permission. Whoever looks after your website decides whether this role gets it.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Decide whether this role should run the till.
2. If it should, go to **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Access** and select the role.
3. Open **Advanced** (individual capabilities) at the foot of the screen and, under **WCPOS**, tick `manage_woocommerce_pos_cash`. [Till permissions](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx#till-permissions) calls it **Running the till**.
4. Tell the cashier to sign out and sign in again.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

If the role has the permission and it still happens, [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support).

<SupportContact />

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="REGISTER231" />

Your store refused an open, count, close or cash movement as forbidden. Logged as "Register session outbox request permanently refused", under the store's own code. Introduced in 2.0.1.

</details>
