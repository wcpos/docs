## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your store turned this down because the signed-in account's role doesn't have the cash permission. Opening the register, recording paid in, paid out and no sale, and counting and closing all need it.

Selling is not affected.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing is lost. The till keeps what was entered, but the store has not recorded the open, the cash movement or the close. A refused movement or close is not retried by itself.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tell your site administrator the code, REGISTER231.
2. Ask a store manager to sign in on this till and do the open, count or close. If the register is in the middle of a count, sales stay blocked until someone with the permission closes it.
3. Once your site administrator has given the role the permission (see On the store), reload the POS on the till. Then tap **Retry** on the register pane, or do the open, count or close again.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. Decide whether this role should run the till.
2. If it should, go to **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Access** and select the role. Open **Advanced** (*individual capabilities*) at the foot of the screen and, under **WCPOS**, tick `manage_woocommerce_pos_cash`. [Till permissions](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx#till-permissions) calls it **Running the till**.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support.
