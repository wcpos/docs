**Symptom:** **Store health**, under **Database**, shows a notice such as "Your server's clock is about 5 min ahead of this device." or "… behind this device." No pop-up appears.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/health/database.tsx:692-705, logs-logic.ts:307-312 (amount as "5 min"); core.json:2182, :2183, :828, :633; apps/main/lib/engine-fetcher.ts:345-355 (warn, no showToast); apps/main/lib/clock-skew.ts:19 (more than 60 s apart) */}

**Do this now:** set this device's date, time and time zone to update automatically.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2529-2539 (troubleshooting); kept from current/CLIENT171.mdx */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Read the notice in **Store health**, under **Database**. It says how far apart the clocks are and which one is ahead.
2. **If this device's time is wrong:** turn on automatic date, time and time zone in the device's settings.
3. **If it's already right:** ask whoever looks after your website to check your store's date, time and timezone.
4. Close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web. WCPOS compares the clocks again when a store opens.

{/* source: step 1 database.tsx:696-704, engine-fetcher.ts:346 (ahead of / behind); step 2 kept from current/CLIENT171.mdx; step 3 core.json:2184 (date, time and timezone settings);
    step 4 apps/main/lib/create-app-engine.ts:776, :1014-1016, packages/core/src/screens/main/logs/logs-logic.ts:255-258 (re-checked once per store open) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Sales are still saved. Only the times recorded on orders, receipts and reports can be wrong while the clocks disagree.

{/* source: core.json:2184; dataSafety, error-registry.json:2522-2541; kept from current/CLIENT171.mdx */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

The notice stays for up to 24 hours after the last check that found the clocks apart, even once they agree.

{/* source: logs-logic.ts:255-260 (24 h window), :285-298 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Check the web server's date, time and timezone; WCPOS reads the time from the Date header on your store's replies.
2. If the clock is wrong, ask your host to correct it.

{/* source: engine-fetcher.ts:336-342 (the Date header); core.json:2184; kept from current/CLIENT171.mdx (On the store) */}

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

If both clocks are right and the notice returns, open **Logs**, press **All**, type CLIENT171 in **Search Logs** and [send the entry to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support).
