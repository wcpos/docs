## What this means {#what-this-means}

This device's clock and your store's clock are more than a minute apart.

WCPOS compares this device's clock with the time your store's server reports in its replies. When they are more than a minute apart, order times, receipts and reports may be off until the two clocks agree.

## Your data {#your-data}

Sales are still saved. Only the times recorded on them may be off while the clocks disagree.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Expand [the log entry](#log-entry): it shows how far apart the two clocks are and which one is ahead.
2. Open this device's date and time settings and turn on automatic (network) time and time zone.
3. If the device was already set to update automatically and its time is right, the server's clock is the wrong one: ask your site administrator to check it.
4. Reopen WCPOS once the clock is fixed — on this device, or by your site administrator on the server (see On the store). The clocks are compared again each time a store is opened, and the warning stops once they agree.

### Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows beside and under it (event code, record, operation, request, details) are for support too; you don't need to read them.

</JourneyShot>

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. If the device was already set to update automatically and its time is right, the server's clock is the wrong one. Ask the host to check the server's date, time and time zone.
