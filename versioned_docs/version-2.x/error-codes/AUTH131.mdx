**Symptom:** a red pop-up reads "The saved store session was incomplete, so WCPOS returned to the store list.", with a **Help** button. At start-up the till shows the Connect screen.

{/* source: packages/core/src/contexts/app-state/index.tsx:76-90 (error, showToast), :246-269 (start-up recovery clears the pointer: Connect screen); core.json:2554 (title), :2670 (second line); packages/core/src/contexts/merchant-toast.ts:50-77; packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:866-891 (Help) */}

**Do this now:** choose the store again on the Connect screen, and don't clear this device's data.

{/* source: core.json:2670; error-registry.json:2559 */}

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It can hold sales your store doesn't have yet.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety local-only, error-registry.json:2554; the app's own line, core.json:2169 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Choose the store and press **Open POS**. The same data on this device opens.
2. **If you were switching stores:** you stay in the store you had open. Choose the other store again.
3. **If the store is no longer listed:** type its address and press **Connect**. If sales were waiting to send, contact support first.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:2557, :2559, core.json:493; step 2 app-state/index.tsx:192-221 (switchStore keeps the current session), packages/core/src/screens/main/hooks/use-switch-store.ts:23-25;
    step 3 error-registry.json:2557, :2560, core.json:2670, :477 (Connect), packages/core/src/screens/auth/components/url-input.tsx:38-58 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Only the till's record of which store was open is cleared. A store you add again opens a fresh copy, and sales waiting under the old one stay on this device but aren't picked up.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2557; app-state/index.tsx:232-235 ("The store's own database and its sales are untouched"), :262 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

This device isn't keeping what the app saves. Check its free space, and look in **Store health → Logs** for storage errors from the same time.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2557, :2561 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Nothing on your store causes this. The device lost part of what it saved: the site, the cashier's sign-in or the store.

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

If it repeats, [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support). The AUTH131 entry names what was missing.
