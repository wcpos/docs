## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your store received this change, but this device couldn't record that it was sent.

The sale or edit reached your store and was saved there. Afterwards, this device tried three times in a row to note that the change had been sent, and couldn't. So the change still looks unsent on this device, and WCPOS sends it again on the next sync. Your store recognises the change by its id and ignores the repeat, so nothing is saved twice. The only real risk is someone typing the sale or edit in again by hand: that creates a second, separate copy.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing is lost. Your store already has the change. Until a re-send is recorded on this device, it may still be counted as waiting to send.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

:::warning Don't enter it again
Don't enter the sale or edit again by hand — the store already has it, and a second entry creates a duplicate.
:::

1. Don't enter the sale or edit again by hand — the store already has it, and a second entry creates a duplicate.
2. Leave WCPOS to sync. The next re-send is ignored by the store and normally clears the entry; **changes waiting to send** in the **Store health → Logs** header goes down once it does.
3. If this keeps appearing, the device may be having trouble saving to its own storage — check its free storage, then tap **Copy debug info** in [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry) and contact support. Don't clear this device's data to fix it.

### Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows beside and under it (event code, record, operation, request, details) are for support too; you don't need to read them.

</JourneyShot>

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. To confirm a sale reached the store, check **WooCommerce → Orders** in WP Admin. It also shows in WCPOS, because it is saved on this device.
