**Symptom:** **changes waiting to send** in **Store health** doesn't go down. **Logs** lists "Could not record a sent change on this device" and says "Your store received this change, but this device couldn't record that it was sent."

{/* source: core.json:1901 (counter), database.tsx:666, logs/index.tsx:384; core.json:2292 (entry title), core.json:2505 (log line); the change stays queued: packages/sync-core/src/drainMutationQueue.ts:1179-1204; no pop-up: apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:704-713 */}

**Do this now:** don't enter the sale or edit again, because your store already has it.

{/* source: core.json:2621; error-registry.json:1480, :1483 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC231 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). It names the record.
2. Don't enter that sale or edit again by hand. A second entry is a second sale or record.
3. Leave WCPOS to sync. It sends the change again, and your store ignores the repeat.
4. Once a send is recorded, **changes waiting to send** goes down.

{/* source: step 1 All, not Errors, because SYNC231 is warn (sync-log-observer.ts:707-711; error-catalogue.json) and the Errors preset is level error only (logs-logic.ts:111); error-registry.json:1482;
    step 2 error-registry.json:1483, core.json:2621; step 3 drainMutationQueue.ts:1155-1158, :1181-1183 (re-push, the store dedupes on the change's id), error-registry.json:1484;
    step 4 drainMutationQueue.ts:1176 (a recorded send removes the change from the queue), core.json:1901 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing. Your store already has the change. Until this device records the send, it counts as waiting to send.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1479-1480; drainMutationQueue.ts:1179-1204 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

This device can't save to its own storage. Free up storage, then restart WCPOS. Don't clear this device's data to fix it.

{/* source: a streak of three or more: sync-log-observer.ts:699-713 (PERSISTENT_FAILURE_STREAK); error-registry.json:1485; the current page's warning */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

To confirm a sale reached your store, open **WooCommerce → Orders** in WP Admin. It already shows there.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1483 */}

</details>
