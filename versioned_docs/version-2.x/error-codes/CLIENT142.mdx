**Symptom:** a till on WCPOS 1.10 or 1.11 logs CLIENT142: "The search index did not answer in time, so WCPOS searched the catalogue directly instead." WCPOS 2 doesn't log this code.

{/* source: core.json:2537; error-registry.json:2177 (introduced 1.10.5); raised at v1.11.9 packages/query/src/engine-query.ts:241-253 (warn, showToast false), :358-362 (after 250 ms, :72);
    not raised at 6b2a2cb4f: packages/query/src/search-blob.ts:12-24 (the index was replaced), no call site outside generated files and tests */}

**Do this now:** nothing; search keeps answering, and the index takes over again once it responds.

{/* source: v1.11.9 engine-query.ts:255-269 (the scan answers), :300-302, :358-362 (the scan stands down once the index answers) */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Keep searching. Results come from the products on this device and can be a little slower.
2. On the web, keep the POS in its own window rather than a background tab.
3. Searches stay slow? Reload: close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web.
4. **If the code returns after that:** use **Copy debug info** and contact support.

{/* source: step 1 v1.11.9 engine-query.ts:255-269, error-registry.json:2167; step 2 engine-query.ts:66-69 (a follower tab or a backgrounded leader), error-registry.json:2169;
    step 3 core.json:2653, error-registry.json:2170 (on web reload the tab; on desktop and mobile close and reopen); step 4 error-registry.json:2171 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Your search was still answered, by a direct look through this device's products. Nothing on your store or on the till changed.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2164, :2167 (no catalogue data changed); kept from current/CLIENT142.mdx */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

WCPOS 2 no longer uses this search index, so it can't log this code. See [Upgrade to 2.0](../getting-started/upgrading-to-2.mdx) for whether your store can move.

{/* source: search-blob.ts:12-24 (6b2a2cb4f); no raise site at v2.0.0 or 6b2a2cb4f */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support with the debug info and the till's WCPOS version.
