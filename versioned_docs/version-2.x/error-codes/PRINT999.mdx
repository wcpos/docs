**Symptom:** no pop-up. A red number appears on **Store health**, and **Logs** has an entry reading "Printing hit an unexpected problem. No sale is affected."

{/* source: core.json:2498; use-ensure-system-printer.ts:21-24 and templates-sync.ts:107-118 (logged with no showToast); support/logs.mdx:18-22 (red number counts errors) */}

**Do this now:** carry on selling, because no sale is affected.

{/* source: core.json:2498; error-registry.json:1329. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**, press **Errors** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). It names what was being done.
2. **If it was a print:** print the receipt again with **Print receipt**.
3. **If it was receipt templates:** close the receipt and open it again while this device is connected. That fetches the templates again.
4. **If it was the Print Dialog printer:** leave **Settings → Printing** and open it again. That sets the printer up again.
5. **If it comes back:** press **Copy debug info** in **Logs** and contact support.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:1334, core.json:828, :694, :2133; step 2 error-registry.json:1335, core.json:1392; step 3 templates-sync.ts:107-118, use-templates-sync.ts:35-54 (runs on each mount); step 4 use-ensure-system-printer.ts:9-26, printing/index.tsx:71; step 5 error-registry.json:1336, core.json:2124 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Sales are untouched. Only the printing step named in the entry didn't finish.

{/* source: core.json:2498; dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1329 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If the same entry comes back each time, send support the log and the time it started.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1330 (support-with-export) */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with what you were printing.
