**Symptom:** checkout won't finish because a product in the cart has a SKU your store refuses: another product has the same SKU, or it isn't valid.

{/* source: core.json:2463 (summary); error-registry.json:549-566. Never raised at 6b2a2cb4f (only generated tables name SKU_DUPLICATE); a store SKU refusal maps to RECORD_INVALID_FIELD, packages/hooks/src/use-http-client/parse-wp-error.ts:86 (woocommerce_rest_invalid_product_sku): see CHECK.md */}

**Do this now:** remove that product from the cart and finish the rest of the sale.

{/* source: core.json:2579; current/CHECKOUT301.mdx At the till step 2. No warning box: dataSafety order-safe */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Remove the product's line from the cart, set the item aside, and press **Checkout** for the rest.
2. Tell a manager the product's name and SKU, so its SKU is made unique and valid on your store.
3. Once that's done, in **Store health → Database**, press **⋮** on **Products**, then **Check for changes now**. Do the same on **Variations** if it's a product option.
4. Add the item again and finish its sale.

{/* source: step 1 current/CHECKOUT301.mdx At the till step 2, core.json:1101 (packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/buttons/pay.tsx:250); step 2 core.json:2579, error-registry.json:561;
    step 3 packages/core/src/screens/main/health/database.tsx:347-362, core.json:1910, :756, :875; step 4 error-registry.json:562, current step 3 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The order is unchanged. Only the product with the refused SKU can't be sold until its SKU is fixed.

{/* source: dataSafety order-safe, error-registry.json:555; current/CHECKOUT301.mdx Your data, At the till step 2 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If more products are refused this way, ask whoever looks after your website to check SKUs across the catalogue.

{/* source: error-registry.json:561 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, search **Products** for the SKU.
2. If two products, or a product and a variation, share it, give each its own SKU.
3. If only one product has it, the SKU itself isn't valid. Correct it on that product.

{/* source: error-registry.json:561; current/CHECKOUT301.mdx On the store step 1 and its sub-point */}

</details>

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the product's name and SKU.
