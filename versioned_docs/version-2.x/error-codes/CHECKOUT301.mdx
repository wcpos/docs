Current versions no longer show this code.

**Symptom:** checkout won't finish because a product in the cart has a SKU your store refuses: another product has the same SKU, or it isn't valid.

**Do this now:** remove that product from the cart and finish the rest of the sale.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Remove the product's line from the cart, set the item aside, and press **Checkout** for the rest.
2. Tell a manager the product's name and SKU, so its SKU is made unique and valid on your store.
3. Once that's done, in **Store health → Database**, press **⋮** on **Products**, then **Check for changes now**. Do the same on **Variations** if it's a product option.
4. Add the item again and finish its sale.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The order is unchanged. Only the product with the refused SKU can't be sold until its SKU is fixed.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If more products are refused this way, ask whoever looks after your website to check SKUs across the catalogue.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, search **Products** for the SKU.
2. If two products, or a product and a variation, share it, give each its own SKU.
3. If only one product has it, the SKU itself isn't valid. Correct it on that product.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the product's name and SKU.
