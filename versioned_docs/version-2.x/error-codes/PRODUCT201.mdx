**Symptom:** a product shows a placeholder instead of its picture. There's no pop-up, and the product adds to the cart as normal.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/hooks/use-image-attachment/index.web.ts:58-59 (placeholder), :66-74 (failureCode PRODUCT201), :132-141 (error returned as state); packages/hooks/src/use-http-client/use-http-client.tsx:477-485 (debug row only, no pop-up); core.json:2482 */}

**Do this now:** keep selling, because the product is fine without its picture.

{/* source: core.json:2598; error-registry.json:964-985. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Carry on with the sale. The price and stock come from the product, not the picture.
2. The till tries the picture again the next time the product is shown, half a minute or more later.
3. If one product's picture never shows, tell a manager the product's name.
4. **If many pictures are missing:** tell a manager to check whether pictures show on your store's website.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2482; step 2 core.json:2598, index.web.ts:33 (ERROR_RETRY_DELAY_MS 30 000), :132-138 (evicted after the delay so a later mount retries);
    step 3 error-registry.json:976-977, current/PRODUCT201.mdx On the store step 1; step 4 current/PRODUCT201.mdx On the store step 2 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Orders and products are unchanged. Only the picture is missing on this till.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:964-985; current/PRODUCT201.mdx Your data */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If the same pictures stay missing, whoever looks after your website can check the image files.

{/* source: error-registry.json:976-978 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. If one product's picture never loads, re-save its image in WP Admin. The file can have been removed from the media library.
2. If many pictures fail at once, check whether they load on the store's website itself. The cause is then the site's media hosting or CDN.

{/* source: error-registry.json:976-978; current/PRODUCT201.mdx On the store steps 1-2 */}

</details>
