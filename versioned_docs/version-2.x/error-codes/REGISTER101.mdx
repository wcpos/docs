**Symptom:** a red pop-up says your store refused a paid in, paid out or no sale that the till recorded, with a **Help** button.

{/* source: packages/query/src/register-session/audit.ts:257-262 (showToast on money-moved); core.json:2543 (title), :230-232; Help opens this page once the logger bug is fixed:
    packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:764 files the row under the store's code (packages/query/src/register-session/audit.ts:244), bug number pending, see CHECK.md */}

**Do this now:** open the register panel and press **Retry** before you count the drawer.

{/* source: core.json:2659; error-registry.json:2295, :2302 */}

:::warning

The cash has already moved. Don't close the register until the movement goes through or is written down.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety money-moved, error-registry.json:2297; error-registry.json:2306 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tap your avatar, then **In the drawer**, to open the register panel.
2. Press **Retry** on the red notice at the top.
3. No **Retry** button? Your account can't manage cash, so ask a manager.
4. **If it's refused again:** write down the amount, type and reason, and tell a manager.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Not on the till, which keeps the only copy until your store accepts it. Until then, your store's expected cash is short or over by that amount. At close, the count names it: tick **I understand, close anyway** only once it is written down.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2300, :2304, :2306; session-store.ts:577-578; close: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/register-count.tsx:404-429, core.json:2910, :2912; packages/core/src/services/register-session/count-decision.ts:71, :80-84; packages/query/src/register-session/drawer-rules.ts:74-77 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Refusals on every movement point at the cashier's account. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2305 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, check that the cashier's role still has the Manage cash capability.
2. Check whether the register's session was closed on another device, then tell the cashier to press **Retry**.

{/* source: queue.ts:333-341; steps 1-2 error-registry.json:2305; Retry in step 2 error-registry.json:2302 */}

</details>
