## What this means {#what-this-means}

You recorded a paid in, a paid out or a no sale. The till accepted it and the cash physically moved, but your store refused to store it.

The movement still exists on this device. It does not exist in your store, so it is not in the expected drawer total your store reports, and it will not appear in that shift's register report.

## Your data {#your-data}

The movement is saved on this device and nowhere else. It was sent and your store turned it down, so nothing is queued to arrive later — until it is accepted, this device holds the only record that the cash moved.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

:::warning Do not close the register
Do not close the register until the movement has either gone through or been written down.
:::

1. Open the register panel. A notice at the top of the panel says how many the store turned down. Press **Retry** on it first — most refusals are a permission or session problem rather than a problem with what you typed.

   <JourneyShot manifest={ec} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[319, 0], [16, -37], [159, 10], [219, 0], [163, -5], [143, -33], [155, 0]]} alt="The register panel with the notice 1 movement refused by the store and Retry at the top, in the drawer $56.00, and Expected in the drawer unfolded: Float $55.00, then under Paid in a $1.00 movement, Docs capture, marked Refused">

   1. **1 movement refused by the store** — the notice at the top of the panel, counting the movements your store turned down.
   2. **Retry** — sends them to your store again.
   3. **in the drawer** — the cash this device expects. It still counts the refused movement; your store's total does not.
   4. **Expected in the drawer** — tap it to unfold the sum behind the drawer figure.
   5. **Float** — the cash the register opened with.
   6. **Paid in** — the paid ins of this session, with how many there are.
   7. **Refused** — the movement your store turned down, in red, with its reason and amount.

   </JourneyShot>

2. If [the log entry](#log-entry) names a field, that field is what your store rejected. Record the movement again with a valid amount and a reason.
3. If it keeps failing, write the amount, the type and the reason down before you count. Your store's expected total does not include this movement, so the drawer will read short (after a paid out) or over (after a paid in) by exactly that amount.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. If every movement is refused, the usual causes are the cashier's account losing permission to manage cash, or the register session being closed on another device. Check both in WP Admin.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.

</JourneyShot>
