**Symptom:** a pop-up reads "WCPOS queued too many requests at once and dropped some." and "Pause a moment, confirm your last action went through, then retry.", with **Help**.

{/* source: packages/hooks/src/use-http-client/request-queue.ts:40-45 (showToast); packages/core/src/contexts/merchant-toast.ts:69-72 (summary as title, action as description); core.json:2535, :2651; packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:873-890 (Help) */}

**Do this now:** pause a few seconds, then check your last action went through before you do it again.

{/* source: core.json:2651; error-registry.json:2126 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Pause a few seconds. WCPOS drops what was waiting and takes new requests again after about 5 seconds.
2. Check that your last action went through, such as a sale, a payment or a refund.
3. **If it went through:** carry on, and don't do it again.
4. **If it didn't:** do it once more.
5. Same pop-up again? Close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web. The queue starts empty.

{/* source: step 1 request-queue.ts:10-13 (BLOCK drops the backlog, then a 5 s penalty), :67-71; step 2 core.json:2651, use-http-client.tsx:366-369 (payments, close-sale, refunds use this queue);
    steps 3-4 error-registry.json:2119, :2126 (confirm, then retry); step 5 error-registry.json:2127, request-queue.ts:99 (a new queue per app load) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

A dropped request never reached your store, so it didn't half-happen. Requests already under way finish normally.

{/* source: request-queue.ts:21-27 (a dropped job rejects without running), :105-109 (in-flight requests finish); error-registry.json:2121 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it keeps coming back, note what you were doing. The fault is in the app, not your store; support needs the entry.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2124, :2128 (an app defect rather than a store problem) */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Open **Store health**, then **Logs**, press **Errors** and [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support). The entry names the dropped requests.

{/* source: catalogue level error; request-queue.ts:35-39 (up to 10 dropped requests named, with a count) */}
