**Symptom:** after a scan, a pop-up reads **Lookup failed** and the code stays in **Search products**. On the **Products** screen, it reads "Loading or updating products hit an unexpected problem." with **Help**.

{/* source: core.json:2043-2044 (packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/products/use-scan-feedback.ts:163-174; pos/products/use-barcode.ts:42-55, :215-228, :240, :368-372), core.json:1224;
    Products screen: packages/core/src/screens/main/products/use-barcode.ts:64-74, packages/core/src/contexts/merchant-toast.ts:69-72, core.json:2499; Help: packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:873-881 */}

**Do this now:** repeat what you were doing, then check that it worked.

{/* source: core.json:2615; error-registry.json:1347. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, :1349 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check your connection, then scan the product again.
2. If the scan fails again, clear **Search products** with the cross, search for the product by name and add it by hand.
3. If you were changing a product, open it and check that the change saved.
4. **If products still look wrong:** open **Store health**, then **Logs**, press **Errors** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2044; step 2 core.json:1224, versioned_docs/version-2.x/pos/product-panel/barcode-scanning.mdx:93; step 3 core.json:2615; step 4 error-catalogue.json:489 (error), core.json:2133, error-registry.json:1355 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Sales and orders aren't affected. If you were changing a product, check that the change saved.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1349; current/PRODUCT999.mdx Your data */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If every scan or product change fails this way, send support the log before you carry on.

{/* source: escalation support-with-export, error-registry.json:1350, :1356 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

This code means the cause has no code of its own yet. The entry's event code and details say what the till was doing.

1. Open the entry in **Store health → Logs** and note the event code.
2. Send it to support with **Copy debug info**.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1355-1356 */}

</details>
