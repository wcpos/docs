**Symptom:** after **Take**, a red pop-up reads "The store refused this payment, so nothing was taken and the order is still open. The store said:" and gives a reason.

{/* source: core.json:2759 (explicit title: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/use-tender-flow.ts:1304-1322), :2587 (second line), :2707; Help: packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:880 */}

**Do this now:** read your store's reason, then take the payment another way or ask a manager.

{/* source: core.json:2587; error-registry.json:731-732 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Read the reason after "The store said:" in the pop-up, or [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open) in **Store health → Logs** with **Errors** on.
2. Take the payment with a different payment method. The order is still open.
3. **If the reason is "Payment method not found":** ask a manager to tell whoever looks after your website, with the time and the method.
4. Once the cause is fixed, take the payment again with the method you first chose.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2759, error-registry.json:731, severity error :723; step 2 error-registry.json:732; step 3 error-registry.json:729, :733; step 4 error-registry.json:732 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Your store recorded nothing, no payment was taken, and the order is still open with its balance owing.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:726; docsBody :729 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Your store isn't accepting that payment method from the POS. Tell whoever looks after your website the code, PAYMENT213, and the reason.

{/* source: escalation site-admin, error-registry.json:727; current page At the till step 1 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. **"Payment method not found" for POS Cash or POS Card on WCPOS 2.0.0:** another plugin or the theme loaded the WooCommerce payment gateways before WCPOS added its own. Update WCPOS to 2.0.1 or later, or WCPOS Pro to 2.0.2 or later. If it still happens after updating, contact support. See [PAYMENT999](./PAYMENT999.mdx#payment-method-not-found).
2. Any other reason: check that the payment method is enabled in the WCPOS settings in WP Admin.

{/* source: error-registry.json:733 (fix line stale: current page and 2.0.1 fix kept, see CHECK.md), :734; LEDGER.md:51 */}

</details>
