**Symptom:** **Store health → Logs** has an entry with this code: "A background sync task stopped unexpectedly before finishing."

{/* source: core.json:2501 (summary); raise sites apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:264-278, :289-296, :428-436, :740-761, :780-789, :891-903; no pop-up of its own: sync-log-observer.ts:1144-1160 */}

**Do this now:** keep working, because the task runs again by itself.

{/* source: core.json:2617; error-registry.json:1397 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC401 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). Its title names the task.
2. If the code comes back within minutes, reload the app once: reload the browser tab, or close and reopen the app.
3. **If it still comes back:** press **Copy debug info** at the top of **Logs** (**Share debug info** in the phone and tablet apps) and send it to support.

{/* source: step 1 error-catalogue.json:672 (warn), so All, not Errors (logs-logic.ts:111-112); titles e.g. core.json:2339, :2308, :2264, :2234, :2291, :2254; step 2 error-registry.json:1398; step 3 error-registry.json:1399, core.json:2124, :2120, packages/core/src/screens/main/logs/index.tsx:344-354 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The task runs again by itself, and no order or product data is affected.

{/* source: core.json:2617; error-registry.json:1395, :1397; scheduler tasks retry after 30s, sync-log-observer.ts:740-761 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

A task that keeps stopping is a fault in the app, not a problem with your store. Support needs the debug info from step 3.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1399 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the entry's title.
