**Symptom:** a product with variations shows no price in the product list, and **Store health → Logs** has a warning entry with this code.

**Do this now:** check the product's price before you sell it.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check the price before you sell. If it's missing or looks wrong, don't sell at that price.
2. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type PRODUCT421 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). It names the product.
3. Tell a manager the product's name, so its prices are re-saved on your store.
4. Once that's done, in **Store health → Database**, press **⋮** on **Products**, then **Check for changes now**.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Orders and products are unchanged. Only the price shown for this product is in question.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If many products show this at once, tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The till reads a variable product's price range from data WCPOS adds to the product when it syncs.

1. In WP Admin, open the product and re-save it. This rebuilds the price data.
2. Check that each variation has a price.
3. If many products show this, a pricing plugin can be writing the price data in a form the till can't read. Note which plugin manages prices and contact support.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the product's name.
