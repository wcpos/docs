**Symptom:** in **Store health → Logs**, an entry reads "Your store's reply had stray text in it, which WCPOS tried to clean up." It has no pop-up of its own.

**Do this now:** check that what you were doing finished.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC151 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). Note its time.
2. Check that what you were doing at that time finished.
3. **If it finished:** WCPOS cleaned up the reply. Nothing else is needed.
4. **If it didn't:** try it again, or follow the pop-up or log entry that came with it.
5. **If the entry appears often:** tell whoever looks after your website. Something on your website adds the stray text.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. When WCPOS cleans up the reply, the request goes through as normal. When it can't, a separate entry says what failed.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

A plugin or theme on your website is printing text into your store's replies. Tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

A plugin, theme or PHP notice prints warnings or other output into the store's REST API replies. WCPOS strips it where it can, but that clean-up is best-effort.

1. Check the site's logs and the PHP error log for notices at the time on the till's log entry.
2. Fix or replace the plugin or theme that prints them, rather than relying on the clean-up.
3. The browser's developer tools show the raw reply, with the stray output before or after the JSON.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time on the entry.
