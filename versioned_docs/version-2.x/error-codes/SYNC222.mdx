**Symptom:** no message at the till. **Logs** lists "A clashing change overwrote your store's copy" and says "This device saved its version over a newer edit made in your store."

**Do this now:** nothing at the till, because the order is saved; have the listed fields checked in your store.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC222 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). It names the order, and **Overwritten fields** lists what the till replaced.
2. Ask whoever looks after your website to check those fields on the order in your store. If the entry says it shows only some of the fields, more were replaced: have the whole order checked.
3. **If the fields are right:** there is nothing to do.
4. **If your store's edit was the right one:** have it made again in your store, then press **Sync** on the order at the till.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing on the till. The order is saved in your store with this device's version. What can need redoing is your store's newer edit. The entry names the fields, not their old values.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Two places are changing the same orders at once, such as a status set in your store while the sale is open at the till. Agree where each kind of order edit happens.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, open **WooCommerce → Orders** and find the order. Check the fields the entry lists. The order changed in your store after the till last downloaded it; its notes can show who or what changed it: a person, a plugin or a payment notification.
2. Some listed fields can be the till's own changes, such as a line added or a status set at the till. Those are right as they are.
3. If your store's edit was the right one, make it again there. Nothing on the till needs sending again.
4. If it keeps happening, look for a status set here while the sale is open at the till, or a plugin that edits orders after they are created.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number.

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="SYNC222" />

Only orders are sent again by themselves after a clash. A second clash in a row is held as [SYNC221](SYNC221.mdx). **Overwritten fields** names at most 25 fields; the entry's message gives the full count.

</details>
