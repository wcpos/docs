**Symptom:** an action such as saving a sale failed, and **Logs** has a CLIENT201 entry: "This device ran out of memory". It shows only with **Verbose diagnostics** on.

:::warning Check before you repeat
Before repeating it, check your last sale or change saved. For a card, check the terminal or provider; don't charge twice.
:::

**Do this now:** close other apps and tabs, restart WCPOS, then check your last sale or change saved.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Close other apps or browser tabs; they share the device's memory with WCPOS.
2. Close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web.
3. Check it saved: **Store health**, under **Database**, counts it in **changes waiting to send** while it sends. Check the cart, and **Orders** with WCPOS Pro.
4. **Only if it didn't save:** do it again once.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

The result couldn't be confirmed. A sale counted in **changes waiting to send** is saved on this device and still sending.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If one action runs out of memory every time, or it happens daily, note what you were doing and contact support.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Without WCPOS Pro, check the till's last sale or change in **WooCommerce → Orders**.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Turn on **Verbose diagnostics**, repeat the action, press **All**, type CLIENT201 in **Search Logs** and [send the log](../support/logs.mdx#for-support). Verbose switches off after 24 hours.

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="CLIENT201" />

Logged at debug as `HTTP request failed`, when a request fails without a reply and the error reads out of memory. No pop-up; the calling action shows its own.

</details>
