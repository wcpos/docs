**Symptom:** a red pop-up reads "Signing in or staying signed in hit an unexpected problem.", or "The login didn't complete.", with a **Help** button.

**Do this now:** try once more, and if it fails again, sign out and sign back in.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Press the button you pressed again: **Log in**, **Sign in with WordPress** or **Re-authenticate**.
2. **If it fails again in the POS:** tap your avatar, press **Sign out**, and sign in again from the Connect screen.
3. **If it still fails:** in **Store health → Logs**, press **All**, type **AUTH999** in **Search Logs**, and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Sales not yet sent stay on this till and send once you're signed in.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

AUTH999 means the cause has no code of its own yet. Support finds it from the entry's event code.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Support works from the till's entry: its event code and any `serverCode`. Nothing on your store needs changing until support names the cause.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support). If it failed on the Connect screen, send the time and the pop-up's text: Logs can't be opened there.

<SupportContact />

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="AUTH999" />

A sign-in error logged without its own code is filed as AUTH999. When the sign-in window itself fails, the entry is a warning.

</details>
