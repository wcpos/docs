**Symptom:** in **Store health → Database**, a panel reads **1 change never reached your server**. **Logs** says "The store had already closed this order, so it refused a change to its lines."

{/* source: core.json:1953 (panel title), core.json:2448 (log line); no pop-up of its own: apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:577-588 */}

**Do this now:** keep the change, and ask a manager whether it is still needed.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It holds a change your store doesn't have yet.

:::

{/* source: registry dataSafety local-only, error-registry.json:200; the app's own line, core.json:2169 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC204 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). It names the order: ask a manager to check it.
2. **If the order is already right:** go to **Database** and press **Discard** in the panel's **⋮** menu.
3. **If the change is still needed:** once a manager has the order reopened, press **Send again**. The till says **Queued to send again.**

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:205; All, not Errors, because SYNC204 is warn and the Errors preset is level error only (logs-logic.ts:111); asking a manager error-registry.json:206; step 2 error-registry.json:206, store-health.mdx:67 (Discard is in the ⋮ menu), core.json:1963; step 3 error-registry.json:207, core.json:1958, core.json:1972, core.json:2564 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Your change stays on this device until you send it again or discard it.

{/* source: error-registry.json:203; core.json:1955, core.json:1965 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Orders are being closed while still open on a till. Agree who finishes each sale.

{/* source: error-registry.json:208 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, open **WooCommerce → Orders** and find the order named in the log entry. The till changed its items, fees, shipping or coupons, which your store refuses once an order is paid, completed, cancelled or refunded.
2. If its lines are already right, tell the cashier to discard the change. If not, set the order to a status that isn't closed, such as **Pending payment**, then tell the cashier to press **Send again**.

{/* source: error-registry.json:203 (what was sent, what counts as closed); packages/sync-core/src/woo/closed-order-refusal.ts:8-10 (paid, cancelled or refunded are closed; pending and failed are not); steps error-registry.json:206-207 */}

</details>
