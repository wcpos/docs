**Symptom:** after you opened the register, the till asks for the opening float again, with no message. Any paid in or paid out you recorded shows **Refused** in the register panel.

**Do this now:** count the float in the drawer and open the register again.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It holds any cash movements your store doesn't have.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Type the float you counted and press **Open register**. This starts a fresh session.
2. Note the time you reopened. Orders taken before it stay in your store but miss that shift's register report.
3. **If the register panel shows Refused movements:** write each one down for a manager. Your store will never get them.
4. **If the till asks for the float again:** ask a manager.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No order. Orders are saved and sent as usual, but carry no register session your store knows, so they won't appear in that shift's register report.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Each open being refused points at the register or the cashier's account. Tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, check that the register still exists and has not been retired.
2. Check that the cashier's role still has the **Running the till** capability (`manage_woocommerce_pos_cash`). See [Till permissions](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx#till-permissions).
3. If the store's reason names a field, check the opening float: plain digits with one decimal point, `10.50`, not `10,50` or `10.`.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

If nothing there is wrong, [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support).

<SupportContact />

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="REGISTER201" />

A permanent refusal of the session open. Logged as "Register session outbox request permanently refused", under the store's own code, with the field it named.

</details>
