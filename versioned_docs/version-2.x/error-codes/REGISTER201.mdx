**Symptom:** after you opened the register, the till asks for the opening float again, with no message. Any paid in or paid out you recorded shows **Refused** in the register panel.

{/* source: a refused open is no longer the current session: packages/query/src/register-session/drawer-rules.ts:32-49, session-store.ts:66; the till shows the open card: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/index.tsx:145-150, :219-221;
    no pop-up: packages/query/src/register-session/audit.ts:257-262 (showToast only for money-moved; REGISTER201 is local-only, error-registry.json:2346); movements fail with the session: packages/query/src/register-session/queue.ts:459-467;

**Do this now:** count the float in the drawer and open the register again.

{/* source: core.json:2661; error-registry.json:2351 */}

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It holds any cash movements your store doesn't have.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety local-only, error-registry.json:2346; the app's own line, core.json:2169; queue.ts:459-467 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Type the float you counted and press **Open register**. This starts a fresh session.
2. Note the time you reopened. Orders taken before it stay in your store but miss that shift's register report.
3. **If the register panel shows Refused movements:** write each one down for a manager. Your store will never get them.
4. **If the till asks for the float again:** ask a manager.

{/* source: step 1 packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/open-register-card.tsx:62-76, core.json:223; the new open is not held back by the refused one: queue.ts:393-403; error-registry.json:2351;
    step 2 error-registry.json:2354, current page step 3; step 3 the refused session's movements never send: queue.ts:459-467; step 4 error-registry.json:2347 (store-admin) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No order. Orders are saved and sent as usual, but carry no register session your store knows, so they won't appear in that shift's register report.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2349, :2354 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Each open being refused points at the register or the cashier's account. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2353 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, check that the register still exists and has not been retired.
2. Check that the cashier's role still has the Manage cash capability.
3. If the store's reason names a field, check the opening float: plain digits with one decimal point, `10.50`, not `10,50` or `10.`.

{/* source: steps 1-2 error-registry.json:2353, Retire: settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx:148; step 3 error-registry.json:2352, the log keeps the field: audit.ts:245 */}

</details>
