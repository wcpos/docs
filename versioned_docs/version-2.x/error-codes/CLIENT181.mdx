**Symptom:** after you pick another store, a pop-up reads "WCPOS couldn't switch to that store." with **Help**. Or it reads **Store switch failed** and "You're still on the current store. Try again; if it keeps failing, reload WCPOS."

{/* source: apps/main/lib/create-app-engine.ts:724-738 (showToast while the request is current); merchant-toast.ts:69-72; core.json:2552, :2668; logger/index.ts:873-890 (Help);
    packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/use-switch-store.ts:40-46; core.json:829, :2676 */}

**Do this now:** reload WCPOS and choose the store again; nothing on this device is lost.

{/* source: core.json:2668 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web.
2. [Switch to the store](../stores/index.mdx#switch-store) again: open your cashier sheet, press **Switch** on the **Store** row and pick the store.
3. Choose the cashier again if WCPOS asks.
4. **If switching keeps failing:** open **Store health**, then **Logs**, press **Errors** and use **Copy debug info**.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2668, :2676; step 2 docs stores/index.mdx:45-52; step 3 kept from current/CLIENT181.mdx; step 4 catalogue level error, logs-logic.ts:111-112, core.json:828, :694, :2133, :2124 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The store you were on keeps its data on this device, including anything still waiting to send. Don't clear local data or remove the site to fix this.

{/* source: core.json:2668 (nothing on this device is lost), :2676 (still on the current store); kept from current/CLIENT181.mdx (warning) */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Finish any payment first and stay online before you switch. **Switch** is greyed out while a payment runs or WCPOS can't reach your store.

{/* source: docs stores/index.mdx:47, :54 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) and name both stores.

{/* source: escalation, error-registry.json:2504-2518 */}
