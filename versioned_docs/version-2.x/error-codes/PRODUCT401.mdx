## What this means {#what-this-means}

The displayed stock may be older than the store stock.

Verify current stock in the store before completing a sale that could oversell the item.

## Your data {#your-data}

No data is at risk — the till's stock figure is simply older than the store's and catches up when sync recovers. If this persists, ask the person who manages your WordPress site.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Before selling a low-stock item, confirm the real quantity — check the shelf, or the product's stock in WP Admin — rather than trusting the till's number.
2. Refresh the till's copy: open **Store health → Database**, open the **⋮** menu on the **Products** row and choose **Check for changes now**.
   - For a product with variations (sizes, colours), do the same on the **Variations** row — each variation keeps its own stock.
3. If the figures still lag, sync is failing or paused. In [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry), choose **Errors** or **Sync** and deal with the codes you find there — for example [SYNC121](/error-codes/SYNC121) when the store cannot be reached. Stock catches up on its own once sync recovers.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. If several tills or your online shop sell the same stock, turn on **Prevent overselling** in **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout**. The store then refuses a sale whose quantities exceed the stock it has, even when a till's figure is out of date.
   - That check happens at checkout while the till is online; a sale completed offline is not reliably checked when it syncs later, so confirm stock by hand (At the till, step 1) before selling low-stock items offline.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row.

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support.
