Current versions no longer show this code.

**Symptom:** the stock the till shows for a product doesn't match the shelf or your store, because the till's copy hasn't caught up.

**Do this now:** check the real stock before you sell a low-stock item.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check the shelf, or ask a manager for the product's stock on your store, rather than the till's number.
2. In **Store health → Database**, press **⋮** on **Products**, then **Check for changes now**. For a product with options, do the same on **Variations**: each keeps its own stock.
3. **If the figures still lag:** open **Store health**, then **Logs**, press **Errors**, and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). Fix the connection problem it names.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Only the till's stock figure is older than your store's. It catches up once sync is working again.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If several tills or your online shop sell the same stock, ask whoever looks after your website to turn on **Prevent overselling**.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

With **Prevent overselling** on, your store checks stock again before it takes each payment, so an out-of-date till figure can't oversell.

1. Open **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout** and switch **Prevent overselling** on.
2. Reload the POS on each till.
3. A sale made offline is checked only when it reaches your store, so cashiers should confirm stock by hand while offline.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the product's name and both stock figures.
