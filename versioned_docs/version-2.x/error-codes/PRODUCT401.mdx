## What this means {#what-this-means}

The displayed stock may be older than the store stock.

Verify current stock in the store before completing a sale that could oversell the item.

## Your data {#your-data}

No data is at risk — the till's stock figure is simply older than the store's and catches up when sync recovers. If this persists, ask the person who manages your WordPress site.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. **At the till:** before selling a low-stock item, confirm the real quantity — check the shelf, or the product's stock in WP Admin — rather than trusting the till's number.
2. Refresh the till's copy: open **Store health → Database**, open the **⋮** menu on the **Products** row and choose **Check for changes now**.
3. If the figures still lag, sync is failing or paused. On the **Logs** tab, choose **Errors** or **Sync** and deal with the codes you find there — for example [SYNC121](/error-codes/SYNC121) when the store cannot be reached. Stock catches up on its own once sync recovers.
4. **Site administrator:** if several tills or your online shop sell the same stock, turn on **Prevent overselling** in **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout**. The store then refuses a sale whose quantities exceed the stock it has, even when a till's figure is out of date — at checkout when the till is online; a sale saved offline is checked when it reaches the store and, if refused, appears in **Store health → Database**.
