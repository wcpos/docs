## What this means {#what-this-means}

A scan or typed search found no matching product.

The usual causes are an active filter, a barcode that is missing or held in a different field, or a first sync that has not finished.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Clear the filters first — a category or stock filter silently narrows every search (a scanned code goes into the same search box).
2. Look the product up by name.
   - If it appears but a scan missed it, its barcode in the store doesn't match the scanned value.
   - If you typed the search, check spelling and stray spaces.
3. If the product is brand new, the local index may still be syncing — give the first sync time to finish, then try again.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. If the barcode is missing, add the scanned value in WP Admin, in the field named in **Barcode Field** (below).
   - For an item with variations (sizes, colours), put it on that variation itself — edit the product and expand the variation — not on the parent product.
2. If every scan misses, the store may be looking in the wrong field. Check **WP Admin > POS > Settings > General > Barcode Field** — it should name the field your products actually keep barcodes in (for example `_global_unique_id` for GTIN, `_sku` or `_barcode`).

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support.
