**Symptom:** a pop-up or a **Logs** entry reads "WCPOS encountered an unexpected error." The failure has no code of its own yet.

:::warning Check before you repeat
Check whether your last action went through before you try it again. For a payment, check the terminal or provider too.
:::

**Do this now:** check whether your last action went through before you try it again.

{/* source: core.json:2607 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check your last action. For a sale, open the order and check its status.
2. **If it didn't go through:** try it once more.
3. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**, press **Errors** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).
4. **If it repeats:** use **Copy debug info** and send it to WCPOS support.

{/* source: steps 1-2 core.json:2607, kept from current/CLIENT999.mdx; step 3 catalogue level error, logs-logic.ts:111-112, core.json:828, :694, :2133; step 4 core.json:2124, kept from current/CLIENT999.mdx */}

### In Store health, under Database

If **Check for changes now** or **Check everything now** fails, the pop-up says "Check your connection and try again. Logs has the details." If **Clear & re-download…** fails, it reads "Couldn't finish re-downloading" and the list's name. Clearing again removes that list from this device only; it downloads again.

{/* source: core.json:1910, :1985, :2683, :1911, :2008, :2685; packages/core/src/screens/main/health/use-manual-sync.ts:82-90, :111-119; database.tsx:329-341; clear confirmations core.json:1913-1916, :1990, :1907 (nothing on your store changes) */}
