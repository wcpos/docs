## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS couldn't confirm whether this refund went through.

Open the order and check its refunds before trying again — a retry could refund twice.

The refund request was sent, but the reply was lost — the request timed out, or the store answered with an error or asked WCPOS to wait and retry. The refund may or may not have been made. Each retry is a new refund request to the payment gateway, so retrying without checking can refund the customer twice.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing on this device is lost. Your store or the payment gateway may already have refunded the customer.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Don't refund again yet.
2. Open the order in **Orders** and check its refunds — they load straight from your store, so this device needs to be online. You can also open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** in WP Admin, where its refunds are listed.
3. For a card refund, also check — or ask your store owner to check — the payment gateway's own dashboard: it shows whether the refund was sent to the card.
4. If the refund is there, it went through — don't refund again. Only if it is in neither place, try the refund once more. If nobody can check the gateway, go to the next step instead.
5. If it is still unclear, tap **Copy debug info** on the **Logs** tab and contact support before another attempt.
