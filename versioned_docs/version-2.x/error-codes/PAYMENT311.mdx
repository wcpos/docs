## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS couldn't confirm whether this refund went through.

Open the order and check its refunds before trying again — a retry could refund twice.

The refund request was sent, but the reply was lost — the request timed out, or the store answered with an error or asked WCPOS to wait and retry. The refund may or may not have been made. Each retry is a new refund request to the payment gateway, so retrying without checking can refund the customer twice.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing on this device is lost. Your store or the payment gateway may already have refunded the customer.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Don't refund again yet.
2. Open the order in **Orders** and check its refunds — they load straight from your store, so this device needs to be online.
3. If the refund went back through a payment gateway — a card terminal, or a provider such as Vipps MobilePay — also check, or ask your store owner to check, that gateway's own dashboard: it shows whether the refund was sent, even when the order doesn't show it yet.
4. If the refund is there, it went through — don't refund again.
   - Only if it is in neither place, try the refund once more.
   - If nobody can check the gateway, go to the next step instead.
5. If it is still unclear, tap **Copy debug info** in [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry) and contact support before another attempt.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. You can also open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** in WP Admin, where its refunds are listed.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows beside and under it (event code, record, operation, request, details) are for support too; you don't need to read them.
