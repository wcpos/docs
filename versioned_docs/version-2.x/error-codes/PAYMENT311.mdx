**Symptom:** after a refund, a red pop-up reads "WCPOS couldn't confirm whether this refund went through." or "The store did not confirm the refund.", with a **Help** button.

{/* source: core.json:1031-1032, :1033-1034; packages/core/src/screens/main/orders/refund/refund-failure.ts:70-77, :122-127; logged in packages/core/src/screens/main/orders/refund/form.tsx:543-563 */}

**Do this now:** don't refund again until you've checked the order's refunds.

{/* source: core.json:1032, :1034; error-registry.json:790 */}

:::warning

The result couldn't be confirmed. Check before trying again.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety outcome-unknown, error-registry.json:792; the app's own line, core.json:2167 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open the order in **Orders** and check its refunds. They load from your store, so the till needs a connection.
2. For a refund to a card reader or a provider such as Vipps MobilePay, also check that provider's dashboard, or ask a manager to.
3. **If the refund is there:** it went through. Don't refund again.
4. **If it's in neither place:** try the refund once more from the order.
5. **If it's still unclear, or nobody can check the provider:** press **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact support first.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:797, core.json:1032, :1043; step 2 error-registry.json:799, current page At the till step 2; step 3 error-registry.json:797; step 4 error-registry.json:798;
    step 5 error-registry.json:799, escalation support-with-export :793, core.json:2124 (packages/core/src/screens/main/logs/index.tsx:352) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing on this till is lost. Your store or the payment provider could already have refunded the customer, and each new try is a new refund.

{/* source: docsBody error-registry.json:795; packages/core/src/screens/main/orders/refund/refund-failure.ts:63-66 (each retry mints a new key) */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If refunds often end this way, replies from your store are getting lost. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: docsBody error-registry.json:795; logSources :801-805 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** in WP Admin. Its refunds are listed there.
2. If your store and the payment provider disagree, send support the debug info from the till.

{/* source: error-registry.json:797, :799; current page On the store */}

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number and amount.
