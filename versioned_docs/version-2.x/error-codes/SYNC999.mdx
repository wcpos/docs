**Symptom:** a pop-up with **Help** reads **Failed to save order**, **Failed to save customer**, **… was not saved to the store** after a new customer, or **Syncing hit an unexpected problem.** Sometimes only Logs shows it.

**Do this now:** open the order or customer you were saving and check your change is there.

{/* source: core.json:2608; error-registry.json:1199-1200 */}

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It can hold changes your store doesn't have yet.

:::

{/* source: core.json:2169 (health.logs.safety_local_only); error-registry.json:1194 (local-only) */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. **If your change is there:** WCPOS sends it by itself, so leave it.
2. **If it isn't:** make the change again and save. The failed save wasn't kept.
3. **If a new customer wasn't saved:** the sale stays a guest sale with their addresses. In **Store health → Database**, press **Send again** on that customer to link it.
4. **If it repeats:** in **Store health → Logs**, press **Errors** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). Note the action it names, then [send the log](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) to support.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:1199, current/SYNC999.mdx (At the till 1); step 2 error-registry.json:1200, push-failure-code.ts:76-78 (failed before the engine owned the write);

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Your sales are safe on this device. A queued change sends by itself; only a save that failed before queueing needs redoing.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1197, :1199-1200; push-failure-code.ts:69-78 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

A 999 code means the cause has no dedicated code yet. Sending the log to support is how that code gets added.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1201-1202; current/SYNC999.mdx (At the till 3-4) */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support, say what you were saving, and [send the log](../support/logs.mdx#for-support).

{/* source: error-registry.json:1195 (support-with-export), :1202 */}
