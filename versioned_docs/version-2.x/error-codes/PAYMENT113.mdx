## What this means {#what-this-means}

The store answered: the invoice was sent to the customer, or it was cancelled, and the order on the store says so. Only this till's local copy of the order failed to save at that moment. The till's copy is replaced by the store's on the next sync.

## Your data {#your-data}

No money moved. Nothing needs to be sent again, and nothing needs to be cancelled again; a second attempt would be refused by the store as a repeat.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

**At the till** — nothing is needed:

1. Open the order in **Orders**: the store's copy shows the invoice line, or the open status after a cancel.
2. Carry on with the next sale.

**On the store** — for the site administrator:

1. If the till still shows the old state after a sync, check **Store health** for a stuck record on that order.
