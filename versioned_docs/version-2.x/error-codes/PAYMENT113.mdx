**Symptom:** after you send or cancel an invoice, a pop-up reads "The invoice was sent, but this till could not save it" or "The invoice was cancelled at the store", and says its copy will catch up on the next sync.

**Do this now:** nothing: don't send or cancel the invoice again, and carry on.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Don't press **Send again** or **Cancel invoice** again. Your store already did it.
2. Carry on. The till's copy of the order catches up on the next sync.
3. **If the till still shows the old state:** open the order in **Orders**. Your store's copy shows the invoice or the open status.
4. **If it still shows the old state after a sync:** check **Store health** for a stuck record.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. No money moved, and your store holds the correct order. The till's copy is replaced by your store's on the next sync.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If tills often fail to save their copy, tell whoever looks after your website which device.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The invoice was sent to the customer, or cancelled, and the order on your store says so. Only this till's local copy failed to save. Check the order in **WooCommerce → Orders**, and check **Store health** on that device for a stuck record.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support). In **Store health → Logs**, press **All** and type PAYMENT113 in **Search Logs**.
