**Symptom:** after you close the register, the count opens again with a red notice, **The store refused this close. Your count is kept.** The button at the foot reads **Retry**.

{/* source: the till reopens the count: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/index.tsx:95-103; packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/register-count.tsx:211, packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/refused-close-notice.tsx:23-35, core.json:2917;
    footer: register-count.tsx:485-492, packages/core/src/services/register-session/count-decision.ts:85, core.json:2916; refusedClose: packages/query/src/register-session/drawer.ts:59-65 */}

**Do this now:** don't count again; once the cause is fixed, press **Retry**.

{/* source: core.json:2662; count-decision.ts:101-107 (keptCount); register-count.tsx:139 (the count is filled in) */}

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It holds your count, which your store doesn't have yet.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety local-only, error-registry.json:2370; the app's own line, core.json:2169 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Ask a manager to get the refusal fixed. Until then, **Retry** is refused the same way.
2. Check the figures the count opened with, then press **Retry**.
3. **If the button reads Approve & close:** a manager approves the count on **Manager approval**, and the register closes.
4. **If it's refused again:** ask a manager.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:2375, :2376; queue.ts:333-342 (no automatic retry); step 2 error-registry.json:2378, register-count.tsx:139; step 3 count-decision.ts:49-54, :85, register-count.tsx:489, core.json:2871,
    packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/approve-sheet.tsx:52-75, :97, core.json:2872; step 4 error-registry.json:2371 (store-admin) */}

### Going back to selling {#back-to-selling}

While the count is open, sales wait. Press the back arrow to sell meanwhile; the register panel then keeps the red notice, with **Retry** for an account that can manage cash. Going back clears a manager's approval, so the next close asks again.

{/* source: back arrow register-count.tsx:464-474, core.json:242; count-decision.ts:95-98; packages/query/src/register-session/session-store.ts:224-231 (approval cleared); panel packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/register-panel.tsx:344-358; error-registry.json:2376 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. No order or sale is affected, and the till keeps your count, note and denominations.

{/* source: count-decision.ts:100-107 (the count, note and denominations come back from the closure row); error-registry.json:2373; current page */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If every close of this register is refused, tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2371, :2375; the store's reason is logged: packages/query/src/register-session/audit.ts:244-246 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Check in WP Admin whether the session is still open. A session already closed on another device refuses a second close.
2. If the log entry is about permission, give the closing cashier's role **Running the till** in Staff Permissions.
3. Then the cashier presses **Retry**. A manager's approval stands only for the same count.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:2375; step 2 settings/wp-admin/access.mdx:98-102; a permission refusal is REGISTER231 in the log (packages/query/src/register-session/queue.ts:158-159) but the count pane shows this page's notice (register-count.tsx:211, refused-close-notice.tsx:23); step 3 error-registry.json:2378, count-decision.ts:49-54 */}

</details>
