## What this means {#what-this-means}

You counted the drawer and closed the register. Your store refused the close, so the shift is still open there.

Your store turned the close down rather than missing it, so nothing retries it by itself. The till keeps your count, note and denominations. Once the cause is fixed, tap **Retry**.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order is affected and no sale is at risk. The till keeps your count, note and denominations, so there is nothing to write down.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Fix the cause first, or the next attempt is refused the same way. The usual ones are the closing cashier's account losing permission to manage cash, and a session already closed on another device.
2. Sales are blocked while this register is being counted. Ask a manager to restore permission to close.
3. If the close needed a manager's approval, that approval is checked at the moment of the close and does not carry over. Ask the manager to approve the new attempt.
4. Tap **Retry**. The count opens with your figures, so you can check them and close again.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. Check in WP Admin that the session is still open and that the closing cashier can manage cash.
   - If the same register keeps refusing closes, [the log entry](#log-entry) names the field or permission your store objected to.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[50, 0], [64, -10], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support.
