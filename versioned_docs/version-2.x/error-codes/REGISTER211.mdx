## What this means {#what-this-means}

You counted the drawer and closed the register. Your store refused the close, so the shift is still open there.

Your store turned the close down rather than missing it, so nothing retries it — reconnecting changes nothing. The register goes back to showing **Open register**, and the count has to be done again once the cause is fixed.

## Your data {#your-data}

**Write your counted figures down now, before anything else.** No order is affected and no sale is at risk, but the till does not keep the count through this: when the register rejoins the session, its copy of the figures is replaced by your store's, which has none.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Write down what you counted — notes, cash and each other tender — before touching the register again.
2. Fix the cause first, or the next attempt is refused the same way. The usual ones are the closing cashier's account losing permission to manage cash, and a session already closed on another device.
3. If the close needed a manager's approval, that approval is checked at the moment of the close and does not carry over. Ask the manager to approve the new attempt.
4. Tap **Open register**. Your store still has this shift open, so the till rejoins the existing session rather than starting a second one — the opening float and cashier shown will be the original ones.
5. Count again from the figures you wrote down, and close.
6. **Store admin:** check in WP Admin that the session is still open and that the closing cashier can manage cash. If the same register keeps refusing closes, the log entry names the field or permission your store objected to.
