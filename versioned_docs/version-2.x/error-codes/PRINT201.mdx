**Symptom:** after you press **Print receipt**, a pop-up reads "WCPOS could not confirm that the print job completed." Its second line names what to check, or reads "Check the printer before reprinting."

{/* source: core.json:2478 (title), :2594 (second line when no printer reason), :1392; print-failure.ts:63-65 (anything not a cancel, not automatic and not unreachable), :73-107 (toast, printer text unless generic); receipt-actions.tsx:95-100; merchant-toast.ts:50-77 */}

**Do this now:** look at the printer for a receipt before pressing anything.

{/* source: core.json:2167 (safety_outcome_unknown); error-registry.json:886 */}

:::warning

Check the printer before printing again, or the customer gets two receipts.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety outcome-unknown, error-registry.json:881; core.json:2167; error-registry.json:886 (a blind retry prints twice) */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check the printer: a receipt in the tray, paper, a closed cover, no error light.
2. **If a receipt came out:** hand it over and carry on selling. Don't print it again.
3. **If nothing printed:** press **Print receipt** (or **Print again**) on the receipt, once.
4. **If it fails again:** switch the printer off and on, check its connection, then press **Test Print** in **Settings → Printing**.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:887; step 2 error-registry.json:886; step 3 error-registry.json:888, core.json:1387, :1392, receipt-stage.tsx:187, print-sheet.tsx:84, use-receipt-document.ts:408-416 (a failed print leaves printedTo unset); step 4 error-registry.json:889, core.json:1708, :1752, printer-row.tsx:99-102 */}

### If you've closed the receipt {#closed-receipt}

With WCPOS Pro, open **Orders** and press **Receipt** on the order's row. Without Pro, a closed receipt can't be reopened.

{/* source: error-registry.json:888; orders/cells/receipt.tsx:16-44; core.json:731, :760, :1846 (upgrade line); pro-guard.tsx:13-65 (Orders is read-only without Pro) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The sale is saved and complete. Only the printed receipt is in doubt.

{/* source: print-failure.ts:73-107 (the print runs on a saved order; nothing is written back to it) */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If this happens often, send support the printer's **Copy setup report**, from its row's **⋯** menu, with the log.

{/* source: error-registry.json:882 (support-with-export); core.json:1493; hardware/printers/index.mdx:125, :239 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time of the sale.
