## What this means {#what-this-means}

Payment succeeded, but WCPOS could not refresh its status afterward.

The payment completed and should not be charged again; no action is required unless the displayed status stays stale.

## Your data {#your-data}

The payment succeeded — money moved. Do not charge the customer again; only the on-screen status is stale.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Do not charge the customer again. The store's payment page confirmed the payment — the **Payment completed for order #…** toast — and only the till's follow-up update failed.
2. Carry on with the next sale. If the paid order still shows as an open order in the cart, refresh the till's copy: open **Store health → Database**, open the **⋮** menu on the **Orders** row and choose **Check for changes now**. Once the paid status arrives, the order leaves the cart.
3. On WCPOS Pro you can refresh just that order instead: in **Orders**, open the order's **⋮** menu and choose **Sync**.
4. If it is still shown as open, reload the app (reload the browser tab on web; close and reopen the app on desktop and mobile). On start, the till finishes any sale whose payment it recorded.
5. **Site administrator:** to be certain, open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** — it shows as paid there.
