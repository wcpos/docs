**Symptom:** after a payment, a pop-up reads "Payment succeeded, but WCPOS could not refresh its status afterward." with a **Help** button. The paid order can still show as open.

**Do this now:** don't charge again; carry on with the next sale.

:::warning

The payment has been taken. Don't charge the customer again.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. If you saw **Payment completed for order #…**, that was your store confirming the payment. Only the till's follow-up update failed.
2. **If the paid order still shows as open in the cart:** in **Store health → Database**, open **⋮** on the **Orders** row and press **Check for changes now**.
3. On WCPOS Pro, you can refresh just that order: in **Orders**, open the order, then press **Sync**.
4. **If it still shows as open:** reload the app. On start, the till finishes the sale once your store's copy shows it paid.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The payment has been taken and your store recorded it. Only the till's on-screen status is out of date.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If paid orders often stay open after a reload, tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. To be certain, open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders**. It shows as paid there.
2. The till retries finishing the sale on each start and gives up after three starts where your store's copy isn't paid.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number. In **Store health → Logs**, press **All** and type PAYMENT101 in **Search Logs**.
