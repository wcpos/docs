**Symptom:** in the register panel, a red notice reads **1 movement refused by the store**, with **Retry**. The movement you reversed with **Undo** or **Void** is gone from the list, but your store still counts it.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/register-panel.tsx:359-367, core.json:2913, :2916; a refused reversal counts as a refused movement: packages/query/src/register-session/drawer.ts:67-71;
    the reversed movement leaves the list: register-panel.tsx:194, packages/query/src/register-session/drawer-rules.ts:64-72; Undo register-panel.tsx:669-680, core.json:241; Void register-panel.tsx:268-276, core.json:258;
    store still counts it: error-registry.json:2325, packages/query/src/register-session/queue.ts:66-74. No pop-up: severity warn, packages/query/src/register-session/audit.ts:263-266 */}

**Do this now:** don't record an opposite movement to cover it; the gap would stay.

{/* source: the till leaves the original out of its expected cash: packages/query/src/register-session/expected.ts:156-165, drawer-rules.ts:64-72; a new movement reaches both: queue.ts:482-485.
    The registry says the opposite (error-registry.json:2330), see CHECK.md */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tap your avatar, then **In the drawer**, to open the register panel.
2. Press **Retry** on the red notice at the top.
3. No **Retry** button? Your account can't manage cash, so ask a manager.
4. **If it's refused again:** leave it, and note its amount with the count.

{/* source: step 1 packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/register-bar.tsx:50-51, packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/user-sheet.tsx:199-221, core.json:285; step 2 register-panel.tsx:258-267 (Retry needs the cash permission), packages/query/src/register-session/session-store.ts:273-288 (Retry re-sends the reversal);

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The original stands in your store; only this till shows it reversed, so their expected cash differs by that amount until the count. If the reversal was a mistake, nothing needs doing.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2322 (local-only), :2325; queue.ts:66-74 (the refusal leaves the original standing); expected.ts:156-165; a mistaken reversal: error-registry.json:2329 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Your store reverses a movement once, only while its register is open. Ask a manager whether counting had started or another till reversed it first.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2325, :2328 (the two causes); Void only while the session is open: register-panel.tsx:273; queue.ts:69-71 (a void made on another device is final) */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Ask a manager, or [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support).
