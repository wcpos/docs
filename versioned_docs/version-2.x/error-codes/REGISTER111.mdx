**Symptom:** in the register panel, a red notice reads **1 movement refused by the store**, with **Retry**. The movement you reversed with **Undo** or **Void** is gone from the list, but your store still counts it.

**Do this now:** don't record an opposite movement to cover it; the gap would stay.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tap your avatar, then **In the drawer**, to open the register panel.
2. Press **Retry** on the red notice at the top.
3. No **Retry** button? Your account can't manage cash, so ask a manager.
4. **If it's refused again:** leave it, and note its amount with the count.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The original stands in your store; only this till shows it reversed, so their expected cash differs by that amount until the count. If the reversal was a mistake, nothing needs doing.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Your store reverses a movement once, only while its register is open. Ask a manager whether counting had started or another till reversed it first.

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Ask a manager, or [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support).
