## What this means {#what-this-means}

The till took a payment larger than the balance your store was holding for this order, and the store refused to apply it. The attempt is kept on the order as a failed payment, so nothing is hidden.

Nothing was applied to the order, but the money is already in the drawer or on the customer's card. So two things have to happen: the difference goes back to the customer, and the part that was genuinely owed is recorded against the order from the money you are already holding.

This normally means the order changed somewhere else — another till, or WooCommerce itself — after this till last read it.

## Your data {#your-data}

Money moved, and your store did not apply it. The difference between what was taken and what the order owed is owed back to the customer.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

**At the till** — do this before the customer leaves:

1. Check what your store says the order owed — that figure is the correct one.
2. Give the customer back the difference between what you took and what the order owed, before they leave.
3. Record the correct amount against the order from the money already in hand. Do not ask the customer to pay again: the refused payment was never applied, but the money for it is in your drawer.
4. Refresh the order on the till before taking any further payment against it.

**On the store** — for the site administrator:

1. Look at the order in WooCommerce: the refused attempt is on its payment list with the reason recorded.
2. If this keeps happening on one order, something else is settling it at the same time — a second till, or an online payment landing while the order is open at the counter.
