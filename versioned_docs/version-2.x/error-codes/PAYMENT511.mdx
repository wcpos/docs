**Symptom:** a pop-up says more was taken than the order still owed, and **Orders** shows **1 sale needs attention**.

**Do this now:** don't ask the customer to pay again: the money is already in your drawer or on their card. Do this before the customer leaves.

:::warning

The payment has been taken. Don't charge the customer again.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Go to **Orders**: the red band says what the order owed and what was taken.
2. In **Store health → Database**, open **⋮** on the **Orders** row and press **Check for changes now**.
3. On the payment screen, pick the same method, press **Exact …** (cash) or **Full balance …**, then **Take**. Never a card reader or gateway.
4. Give back the difference: cash from the drawer, or refund it on the card machine or provider's dashboard.
5. Press **Handled** to clear the entry.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The refused payment stays on the order as a failed row, never applied.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Another till or an online payment changed the order first. Tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. The refused attempt and its reason are only in **Store health → Logs** on the till that took it. **WooCommerce → Orders** shows only what your store accepted.
2. If it keeps happening on one order, something else is settling it at the same time: a second till, or an online payment landing while the order is open at the counter.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number and amount.
