## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS couldn't write a change to this device's storage.

Free up device storage, then check the change shows up. If it's missing, enter it again. Contact support if it repeats. Until you have checked, note what the change was — if WCPOS closes before the change is written, it is gone. Don't clear this device's data.

## Your data {#your-data}

Don't clear this device's data — it may hold sales that haven't reached your store. The change that failed may not be saved anywhere yet, so keep a note of it until you have checked it shows up. If this keeps happening, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact WCPOS support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Note what the change was — the record and the values you entered. If [the log entry](#log-entry) names a record, that is the change that may not have been saved.
   - Site and credential writes may name only the failed operation and error instead; note those details too.
2. Do not clear site data, reset the app, or uninstall: other changes that haven't reached your store yet, such as sales, exist only on this device, and clearing local storage deletes them permanently.
3. Free up device storage by removing other files or apps — a full disk or an exhausted browser storage quota is the most common reason a local write fails. Don't use the browser's "Clear browsing data" to make room — it deletes WCPOS's unsent sales too.
4. Check the change shows up — open the record. If it's missing, enter it again and save.
   - If it still won't save, restart WCPOS once and try again.
5. If saving keeps failing, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact support before attempting any repair.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows beside and under it (event code, record, operation, request, details) are for support too; you don't need to read them.
