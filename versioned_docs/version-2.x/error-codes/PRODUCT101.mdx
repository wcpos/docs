**Symptom:** after you edit a product, the field reads **Not saved** in red, **Store health → Database** lists **1 change never reached your server**, or a pop-up reads "Change rejected by your store — reverted".

**Do this now:** open **Store health** and read why the change didn't reach your store.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It can hold a product change your store doesn't have yet.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**, and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). It gives the reason.
2. Fix what it names: correct the field, or check your connection.
3. **If Database lists the change:** press **Send again**, or **Discard** in its **⋮** menu to keep your store's version.
4. **If it isn't listed:** edit the product again. It saves by itself and reads **Saved**.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing reached your store. A change listed in **Database** stays on this device until you send or discard it. After a revert, your store's value is back and your edit is gone.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If one product's changes keep failing, ask a manager to check that product.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Your store refused the change: a field it rejects, or a conflicting edit made in WP Admin.

1. Open the product in WP Admin and compare it with the till's version.
2. Re-apply the correct values there, then tell the cashier to discard the change on the till.
3. Check that the cashier's role can edit products.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the product's name.
