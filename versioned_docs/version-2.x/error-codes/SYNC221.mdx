**Symptom:** in **Store health → Database**, a panel reads **1 change was held — your store reported a newer version**. **Logs** says "A change on this device met a newer edit in your store."

{/* source: core.json:1918 (panel title), core.json:1920 (the needs-revision variant), core.json:2503 (log line); conflicted-mutations.tsx:169-177 */}

**Do this now:** choose which version to keep in **Store health → Database**.

{/* source: core.json:2619; error-registry.json:1439 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC221 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). Press **Go to Store health → Database**.
2. **If nothing is held for this record:** it settled by itself. Nothing to do.
3. **If a change is held:** **Open record** shows this device's version. Have it compared with the one in your store.
4. **To keep this device's version:** press **Send again**. It goes in over your store's, and the till says **Queued to send again.**
5. **To keep your store's version:** press **Use server version** and confirm. Some rows have **Discard device change** in the **⋮** menu instead.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Your store keeps its version and this device keeps its change until you choose. Later edits to that record wait too. Don't clear this device's data meanwhile.

{/* source: core.json:1922, error-registry.json:1437, :1439; drainMutationQueue.ts:1150 (later edits to the record are held back); core.json:2169 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Two places are editing the same records, such as stock on the till and in your store. Agree where each edit happens.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1441 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. When a change is held, open the same record in WP Admin and compare it with the till's version. Tell the cashier which one to keep.
2. If the panel reads **1 change was held — your store asked for a current version**, no value is at fault and the till can't tell which version is newer. **Send again** sends its change on top of your store's current version.
3. If the same records clash often, find where that data is edited in parallel.

</details>
