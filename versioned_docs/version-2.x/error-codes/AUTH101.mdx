**Symptom:** a red bar on the till reads **Session expired**, or **1 sale waiting to sync**, with a **Log in** button.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/components/session-hold-banner/index.tsx:126-142; core.json:502 (sales waiting), :508 (Session expired), :491 (Log in) */}

**Do this now:** press **Log in** and sign in as the same cashier.

{/* source: error-registry.json:358, :365. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:360 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Press **Log in** on the red bar. The WordPress sign-in window opens.
2. Sign in as the same cashier and carry on selling. A sale that was waiting sends itself once you are back in.
3. **If you closed the sign-in window:** the bar stays, and nothing reaches your store. Press **Log in** again.

{/* source: step 1 session-hold-banner/index.tsx:139 (Log in runs the sign-in flow), error-registry.json:365; step 2 error-registry.json:365, :366;
    step 3 packages/core/src/screens/main/hooks/use-rest-http-client/use-session-login-flow.ts:224-248 (closing the window keeps the sign-in failed; every request to the store fails until Log in) */}

### Check it worked {#check}

Open **Store health**, then **Database**. The figure **changes waiting to send** goes back to **0**.

{/* source: error-registry.json:366; support/store-health.mdx:40 (the figure) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Everything you saved stays on the till and is sent once you are signed in. **Session renewed automatically** means the till signed you back in by itself.

{/* source: error-registry.json:363; session-hold-banner/index.tsx:63-75; core.json:511 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Being asked to sign in several times a day points at your store, not the till. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: error-registry.json:368 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The till renews its sign-in by itself once. This message means your store refused that too.

1. In WP Admin, open **POS → Settings → Tools → Logs**. A refused sign-in is recorded there with its reason. See [Your store's log in WP Admin](../support/logs.mdx#wp-admin-logs).
2. Check whether a security plugin shortens sign-in lifetimes or clears sign-ins.
3. Check **Sessions**: signing a device out there shows this message on that device. See [Sessions](../settings/wp-admin/sessions.mdx#on-the-device).

{/* source: reference/role-endpoint-access.mdx:139 (renewed once, then AUTH101); steps 1-2 error-registry.json:368; step 3 settings/wp-admin/sessions.mdx:67 */}

</details>
