## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your session ended and you need to sign in again.

Sign in again to carry on. Anything you had already saved stays on the till and is sent for you once you are back in.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected. A sale you saved while the session was out is held on the device, not lost, and goes to your store by itself after you sign in.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Sign in again. WCPOS opens the WordPress sign-in window by itself; if you closed it, a **Please log in to continue** toast offers **Login**. Sign in as the same cashier.
2. You do not need to re-enter or re-save anything: a sale that was waiting sends itself once you are back in. To watch it go, open **Store health → Database** — **changes waiting to send** drops to 0.
3. If a sale is listed under **"… changes never reached your server"** instead, press **Send again** on its row.
4. Frequent expiries (several per day) usually come from the site: a security plugin shortening session lifetimes or clearing tokens. Ask the site administrator which security plugins run on the store, and to check **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Tools > Logs** — a refused sign-in is recorded there with its reason.
