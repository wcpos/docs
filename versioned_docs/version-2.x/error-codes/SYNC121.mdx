**Symptom:** the register bar says **Offline**, and after a few seconds a pop-up reads **Device went offline** or **Website is unreachable**, with a **Help** button. Its second line reads "Nothing to do — keep selling. Changes send automatically when the connection returns."

{/* source: Offline pill register-bar.helpers.ts:48-49, core.json:265, getting-started/offline.mdx:22-23; pop-up titles core.json:645, :882 (packages/core/src/screens/main/components/online-status/online-status-logger.tsx:54-69, after the 5 s settle at :13); second line core.json:2558 (packages/core/src/contexts/merchant-toast.ts:61-66); Help packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:873-891 */}

**Do this now:** keep selling, because your sales stay on this device until your store answers.

{/* source: core.json:2558; error-registry.json:61. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:56 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Keep selling. When your store answers, **Connection restored** appears and waiting sales send by themselves.
2. Check whether other websites load on this device. If none does, the device has no internet.
3. Open your store's website in a browser tab on this device.
4. **If your store's website doesn't load either:** the website or its hosting is down, not the till. Tell whoever looks after your website.
5. **If it loads but the till doesn't:** a VPN, firewall, ad-blocker or security plugin is blocking the till. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:615 (online-status-logger.tsx:70-75), getting-started/offline.mdx:104, :131-133; step 2 error-registry.json:62; steps 3-4 error-registry.json:63; step 5 error-registry.json:64, Offline pill shown when the store isn't answering: getting-started/offline.mdx:22 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Sales and changes stay on this device and send once your store answers. Don't clear this device's data meanwhile.

{/* source: core.json:2558; error-registry.json:61; getting-started/offline.mdx:104; core.json:2169 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If the till goes offline often while other websites load, tell whoever looks after your website the times.

{/* source: error-registry.json:63-64 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The till got no reply from your store.

1. Open the store's website from the same device. If it doesn't load there either, check the site and its hosting.
2. If it loads, check for a VPN, firewall, ad-blocker or security plugin blocking the till's REST API requests.
3. The browser's developer tools (Network tab), or **Advanced → Toggle Developer Tools** in the desktop app, show which request fails.

{/* source: packages/core/src/utils/store-request-failure-code.ts:4-8 (no reply at all is SYNC121); steps error-registry.json:63-64; step 3 error-registry.json:229 */}

</details>
