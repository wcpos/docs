**Symptom:** your store, not the till, records this code, in its POS log in WP Admin. It means "WCPOS Pro is disabled because its version does not match WCPOS."

**Do this now:** tell whoever looks after your website that the WCPOS and WCPOS Pro plugins need matching versions.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tell whoever looks after your website the code, LICENSE201, and what you were doing when it appeared.
2. Send them a link to this page. Their steps are under **If it keeps happening**.
3. Once they say both plugins are updated, reload the POS: refresh the page, or close and reopen the app.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Orders, products and customers are untouched.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

It points at the two plugins on your website being at different versions. Only whoever looks after your website can update them.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, open **POS → Settings → Tools → Logs** and open the entry. It names which of WCPOS and WCPOS Pro is behind. See [Your store's log in WP Admin](../support/logs.mdx#wp-admin-logs).
2. Open **Plugins** and update the older one. Updating both to their latest versions also fixes it.
3. If no Pro update is offered, or it won't download, fix that first: see [LICENSE301](LICENSE301.mdx).

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the till's log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support): its debug info lists your store's WCPOS versions.
