## What this means {#what-this-means}

More than one product matches this barcode.

WCPOS cannot pick between them safely, so nothing was added. Search for the product by name; your site administrator gives each product a unique barcode in the store to fix the clash.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected. If this persists, ask your site administrator.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. The scan added nothing and a **Several matches** message appeared. The scanned code is now in the product search box, so the products that share it are listed — add the right one by hand and finish the sale.
2. Clear any category or stock filters first — they narrow the same search and can hide some of the matches. Then note every product name in the list for your site administrator.
3. Tell your site administrator the code, PRODUCT321, and give them that list.
4. Once your site administrator has given each of those products its own barcode (see On the store), open **Store health → Database**, open the **⋮** menu on the **Products** row and choose **Check for changes now** — and on the **Variations** row too if any of the matches was a variation — then rescan. The scan should now add the product directly.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. In WP Admin, open each of those products and give each its own barcode.
2. Check the field the store uses for barcodes — **WP Admin > POS > Settings > General > Barcode Field** (by default the product's GTIN, `_global_unique_id`). A duplicate in the SKU or another barcode field can also match, so check those too.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.
