**Symptom:** during a sale, the customer display shows the welcome screen instead of the cart. On the till, **Store health → Logs** has an entry: "The customer display could not build the cart snapshot; the display shows the idle screen until the next change."

{/* source: core.json:2542; use-display-snapshot.ts:55-81, :96-107 (logged with no showToast), :109-110 (no snapshot); snapshot-source.tsx:55-58 (display.idle); hardware/customer-display/index.mdx:64, :178-182 */}

**Do this now:** carry on with the sale on the till, which keeps working.

{/* source: core.json:2658; error-registry.json:2283. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:2278 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Change a cart line, such as a quantity. The display builds its picture again on the next cart change.
2. **If it stays on the welcome screen:** open **Settings → Customer Display** and check the display reads **Connected** under **Paired displays**.
3. **If it's connected:** close the display page on the display device and open the same address again in the same browser.
4. **If it happens again:** note the order, then press **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and send both to support.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2658, use-display-snapshot.ts:55-81 (rebuilt when the order changes), error-registry.json:2283; step 2 core.json:1586, :1580, :1514, hardware/customer-display/index.mdx:49; step 3 hardware/customer-display/index.mdx:45; step 4 error-registry.json:2284, core.json:2124 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The sale goes on as normal on the till. The display only mirrors the cart, so nothing on it is saved.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:2278; snapshot-source.tsx:51-101 (publishes to the display, writes nothing) */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it happens on one sale and not others, note what's in that cart for support.

{/* source: use-display-snapshot.ts:42, :100-107 (logged once per order) */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number.
