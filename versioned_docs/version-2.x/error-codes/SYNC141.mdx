**Symptom:** in **Store health → Logs**, an entry reads "Your store asked WCPOS to slow down for a moment." When you connect a store, a pop-up says the same, with a **Help** button.

{/* source: core.json:2444; connect pop-up packages/core/src/screens/auth/hooks/use-api-discovery.ts:195-205 (429 mapped at packages/hooks/src/use-http-client/parse-wp-error.ts:131-132; title = summary: packages/core/src/contexts/merchant-toast.ts:69-72), Help packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:873-891; no pop-up of its own while syncing: apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:135, :527 (no showToast in the file) */}

**Do this now:** keep working, and don't retry by hand: WCPOS waits and tries again by itself.

{/* source: core.json:2560; error-registry.json:111. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:106 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Keep working. WCPOS waits, then sends again on its own.
2. Don't press anything to retry. Retrying by hand keeps your store limiting the till for longer.
3. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC141 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). Note how often it appears.
4. **If it appears every day:** ask whoever looks after your website about your store's rate limit.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2560, apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:500-520 (a retryable failure is an attempt); step 2 error-registry.json:111; step 3 All, not Errors, because SYNC141 is warn and the Errors preset is level error only (logs-logic.ts:111); step 4 core.json:2560 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Your changes stay on this device and go through on a later attempt. Nothing needs entering again.

{/* source: error-registry.json:106, :112 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it appears every day, your store's limit is too tight for the till. Ask whoever looks after your website about it.

{/* source: core.json:2560; error-registry.json:113 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Your store, or something in front of it, answered the till with "Too Many Requests".

1. Find which layer limits the till: a security plugin, a CDN or firewall rule, or your host's own limits.
2. Allow the POS's REST API traffic through that limit.
3. Match the throttle in that tool's dashboard against the endpoint and times in the till's log entry.

{/* source: error-registry.json:113-114, :119 (HTTP 429) */}

</details>
