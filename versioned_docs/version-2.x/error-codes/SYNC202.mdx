**Symptom:** a pop-up reads **Change rejected by your store — reverted**, then your store's reason and "See Store health for details.", with a **Help** button. The record shows your store's version again.

**Do this now:** read your store's reason, fix what it names, then make the edit again.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. To read the reason again, open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC202 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).
2. Fix what the reason names, such as a price, a SKU or a field left empty.
3. Open the record, make the edit again and save. Once it sends, check the record shows your change.
4. **If the reason says you aren't allowed:** ask a manager to let your account edit this kind of record.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Only this edit. Your store's version is back on this device, or the record is removed if your store deleted it. Sales and orders aren't affected, and a later edit to the same record still sends.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Ask whoever looks after your website to check the record in WP Admin.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. If the reason is a permission message, the user's role lacks that task: see [AUTH201](AUTH201.mdx).
2. If it names a field the till doesn't show, open the same record in WP Admin and check whether another plugin adds a rule to it.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

If the same edit is refused again with no clear reason, [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support).

<SupportContact />

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="SYNC202" />

Only edits to products, variations, customers and coupons are undone. A refused record created on this till, or an edit that couldn't be undone, stays in **Store health → Database** as [SYNC201](SYNC201.mdx).

</details>
