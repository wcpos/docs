**Symptom:** a payment you started in a browser tab didn't go through, and the tab now reads **The POS is open in another tab** and **Only one tab can run the register.** The screen shows no code.

{/* source: apps/main/components/parked-tab.tsx:11, :45-61 (no code rendered); packages/database/src/live-tab/live-tab.web.ts:53-54, :86-93 (a tab that lost the register parks);

**Do this now:** check the card reader and the order before you take this payment again.

{/* source: device-leg.ts:202-205 (after the reader approved, the capture needs the register), :239-242; core.json:2671 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Go back to the tab that runs the register, or press **Take over here** to carry on in this one.
2. **If it reads The other tab isn't answering:** close or reload the other tab, then carry on here.
3. **If it reads Local database unavailable:** press **Reload the app**.
4. Before taking the payment again, check the reader and the order. If the reader approved the card, don't charge again.

{/* source: step 1 parked-tab.tsx:11, :17, :54-60, live-tab.web.ts:12-16; step 2 parked-tab.tsx:15, live-tab.web.ts:11; step 3 parked-tab.tsx:16, :18, :52-53;
    step 4 device-leg.ts:202-205, :239-242 (an approved card's capture is not sent from this tab), kept from current/CLIENT161.mdx */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

This tab refused the request before sending it, so it started no new charge. Saved sales, carts and orders are unaffected. If the reader had already approved a card, this tab didn't tell your store.

{/* source: use-checkout-session.ts:394-398 (the hold comes before the checkout request); server-leg.ts:249-254; device-leg.ts:205-208; kept from current/CLIENT161.mdx */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Keep the POS in one browser tab. The desktop and mobile apps have no tabs to manage, so this code is web only.

{/* source: live-tab-gate.web.tsx:108-110; apps/main/components/live-tab-gate.tsx (no gate); packages/database/src/live-tab/index.ts:3-6 (native and desktop holds never refuse), index.web.ts:1 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support with the order number, what the reader showed and which tabs were open.
