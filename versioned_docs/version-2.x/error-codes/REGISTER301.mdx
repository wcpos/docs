## What this means {#what-this-means}

The count was over your store's variance limit, so it needed a manager's approval. Your store refused the approval and the register stayed open at the counting step.

## Your data {#your-data}

The count is held on this device and nothing is lost. The drawer figures you entered are still on screen, ready for another attempt.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Check the username and password. An approval is a normal WordPress sign-in and is case sensitive.
2. A cashier cannot approve their own count. The approver must be a different account from the one closing the register.
3. The approving account needs permission to manage register closures. A shop manager has it by default; a cashier does not.
4. If the approval still fails, the count can be left for a manager to close on their own sign-in. Write the counted totals down first.
5. **Store admin:** repeated refusals on one till are worth looking at. Each attempt is recorded, and a short drawer with several failed approvals is the pattern worth investigating.
