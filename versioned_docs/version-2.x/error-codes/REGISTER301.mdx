**Symptom:** on **Manager approval**, a pop-up and a line under the fields say **This account cannot approve closes** or **Approval failed — try again**. The register isn't closed.

**Do this now:** check the sign-in, or ask a different manager to approve the count.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check the **WordPress username** and **Password**. An approval is a normal WordPress sign-in, and case sensitive.
2. The approver must be another account than the one closing and the one who opened the register.
3. The approver's account must be allowed to approve closes. A shop manager is by default; a cashier isn't.
4. Press **Approve** again. It stays off while the sheet shows **Approval needs a connection**.
5. **If it still fails:** write the counted totals down and leave the count for a manager.

### If the close is refused later {#refused-at-close}

If your store refuses the approval when the close reaches it, the count comes back with **Approval needed** and **Approve & close**. Your count is kept; a manager approves it again.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The count is held on this device, ready for another attempt.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Repeated refusals on one till are worth looking at. Tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Each attempt is recorded. A short drawer with several failed approvals is the pattern worth investigating.
2. Approving needs `manage_woocommerce_pos_closures`. Shop Manager and Administrator have it; see [Till permissions](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx#till-permissions).

</details>
