**Symptom:** a WCPOS Pro update is offered on your website but won't download. Your store records this code in its POS log in WP Admin: "The updater is not authorized to download WCPOS Pro updates."

{/* source: core.json:2487; error-registry.json:1086, :1093-1095 (logSources wp-admin-pos-logs); getting-started/pro-license.mdx:113-118; no raise site in the monorepo at 6b2a2cb4f (git grep UPDATER_NOT_AUTHORIZED) */}

**Do this now:** tell whoever looks after your website that the Pro licence needs activating again so updates can download.

{/* source: core.json:2603; error-registry.json:1085 (site-admin). No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1084 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tell whoever looks after your website the code, LICENSE301, and what you were doing when it appeared.
2. Send them a link to this page. Their steps are under **If it keeps happening**.
3. **If the till shows Upgrade to Pro:** tell them that too. Once Pro is active, reload the POS (refresh the page or reopen the app).

{/* source: step 1 current LICENSE301 page (At the till); step 2 error-registry.json:1085; step 3 core.json:867, getting-started/pro-license.mdx:106, :109-110, :153 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Orders, products and customers are untouched.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1084 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

It's about the WCPOS Pro licence on your website, which the Pro download checks. Only whoever looks after your website can fix it.

{/* source: getting-started/pro-license.mdx:115-118; error-registry.json:1085 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, open **POS → Settings** and, under **Account**, open **License**. If **License Key** shows, paste the key and press **Activate**.
2. If it already shows activated, copy the full key from your WCPOS account (the page shows it shortened), press **Deactivate**, paste it back and press **Activate**.
3. Read the Pro row on **Plugins**. **Your WCPOS Pro license has expired.** means renew it from your account. Then check **Dashboard → Updates** again.
4. If it still fails, ask the host whether outbound HTTPS from the site to the update server is blocked.

{/* source: steps 1-2 error-registry.json:1089, getting-started/pro-license.mdx:46-49; step 3 getting-started/pro-license.mdx:98-100, :120, error-registry.json:1090; step 4 error-registry.json:1091 */}

</details>
