## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your WCPOS Pro license is not active for this store or device.

Your site administrator needs to activate the license for the named store.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected. If this persists, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact WCPOS support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tell your site administrator the code, LICENSE101.
2. Once your site administrator has activated the license (see On the store), reload the app to pick up the change.
3. Still locked? Use **Copy debug info** in [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry) and contact support with your license reference.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator / license holder.

1. Go to **WP Admin > POS > Settings > License**. An active license shows **"License … has been activated"**; otherwise the page shows the **License Key** field.
   - An upgrade card instead means the WCPOS Pro plugin is not active — activate it in **WP Admin → Plugins** first.
2. If it is not active, activate the license for the named store: paste the key from your WCPOS account into **License Key** and press **Activate**.
3. A site URL change (http to https, adding or dropping www, a domain move, a staging copy) leaves the license on the old address. Deactivate it there first: on the old site's **License** page if it is still reachable, or from your [WCPOS account page](https://wcpos.com/account/licenses).
   - If the old site is gone, email **support@wcpos.com** to reset the activation.
4. After a URL change, enter the key again on this site; do the same if the license shows active but the POS still disagrees. Copy the full key from your WCPOS account first (the page shows only a shortened one, and **Deactivate** clears it). If the page shows the license as activated, press **Deactivate** first. Then enter the full key in **License Key** and press **Activate**.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.

</JourneyShot>
