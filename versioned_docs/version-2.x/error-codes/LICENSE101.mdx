**Symptom:** a red pop-up appears about your expired WCPOS Pro licence, with a **Renew Now** button and no close button. **Notifications** lists it as **License**, with **Renew**.

**Do this now:** carry on selling, and tell whoever looks after your website that the Pro licence needs renewing.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Press **Renew Now** to see renewal on wcpos.com.
2. Tell whoever looks after your website. Only they can renew or activate the licence for your store.
3. Once they say it's done, reload WCPOS. On the web, reload the tab.
4. **If the pop-up comes back:** the licence still isn't active. Send them a link to this page.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Your sales and products are untouched, and the till keeps working while the licence is sorted out.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

The licence belongs to your store's address. A changed address, such as a move to https, leaves it on the old one.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, open **POS → Settings** and, under **Account**, open **License**. An upgrade card there instead means the WCPOS Pro plugin isn't active: activate it in **WP Admin → Plugins** first.
2. If it isn't active, paste the key from your WCPOS account into **License Key** and press **Activate**. **Thank You!** confirms it.
3. After an address change, deactivate it on the old site or from your WCPOS account first. Email support@wcpos.com if the old site is gone.
4. If it shows active but the till disagrees, copy the full key from your account, press **Deactivate**, paste it back and press **Activate**.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with your licence reference.
