## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your WCPOS Pro license is not active for this store or device.

Check the local, licensing-server, and updater status, then activate the license for the named store.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected. If this persists, export diagnostics and contact WCPOS support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

**On the store** — for the site administrator / license holder:

1. Go to **WP Admin > POS > Settings > License**. An active license shows **"License … has been activated"**; otherwise the page shows the **License Key** field. (An upgrade card instead means the WCPOS Pro plugin is not active — activate it in **WP Admin → Plugins** first.)
2. If it is not active, paste the key from your WCPOS account into **License Key** and press **Activate**.
3. A site URL change (http to https, adding or dropping www, a domain move, a staging copy) leaves the license on the old address. Have the full key from your WCPOS account ready first — once a license is active the page shows only a shortened key, and **Deactivate** clears it. Press **Deactivate**, then enter the key again and **Activate** it for the current address.
4. If the license shows active but the POS still disagrees, copy the full key from your WCPOS account first (the page shows only a shortened one, and **Deactivate** clears it). Then press **Deactivate**, paste the key into **License Key** and press **Activate**.
5. Still locked? On the till, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact support with your license reference.

**At the till:** after the license is activated, reload the app to pick up the change.
