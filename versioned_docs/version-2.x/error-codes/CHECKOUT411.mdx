**Symptom:** a banner on the order reads **Check this order's amounts before taking payment**, with the line "A line's saved price details couldn't be read, so its amount came from the totals already stored on it." There's no pop-up.

{/* source: core.json:1132 (title when the store hasn't changed the totals), :1130; packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/totals-changed-banner.tsx:146-179;
    no pop-up, warn: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/contexts/order-engine-warnings/index.tsx:32-40, :186-189 */}

**Do this now:** check the order total before you take payment.

{/* source: core.json:1132, :2656; error-registry.json:2235. No warning box: dataSafety order-safe */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check the order total, and each line's amount, against what you expect.
2. Remove the line whose amount looks wrong, and add the product, fee or shipping again. A newly added line has readable price details.
3. Compare the total before and after.
4. The banner stays on this order even after the fix, so go by the total.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:2235, current/CHECKOUT411.mdx At the till step 1; step 2 core.json:1130, :2656, error-registry.json:2236, current step 2 (product, fee or shipping; a freshly added line carries readable price details);
    step 3 current step 3; step 4 order-engine-warnings/index.tsx:42-51, :206-217 (held per order, no removal path; the banner doesn't name the line, see CHECK.md) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The order is safe. Only that line's amount is in question, and it's the amount already saved on the line.

{/* source: dataSafety order-safe, error-registry.json:2230; core.json:2540; current/CHECKOUT411.mdx Your data */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If newly added lines show this too, ask whoever looks after your website to check for a plugin that changes order lines.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2238; current/CHECKOUT411.mdx On the store step 1 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Lines added by a much older version of WCPOS, or restored from a backup, can carry price details (the price, whether tax is included, its tax status) this version can't read. On new lines, something is writing the POS price data in a format the app can't read.

1. Note any plugin that changes cart or order line data.
2. On the till, open **Store health → Logs**, press **All**, type CHECKOUT411 in **Search Logs** and open the entry.
3. Press **Copy** or **Share**, and send it to support with the order number.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2237-2238; current/CHECKOUT411.mdx What this means para 3, On the store steps 1-2; warn level so All: packages/core/src/screens/main/logs/logs-logic.ts:104-120, core.json:2131, :961, :2127-2128 */}

</details>
