## What this means {#what-this-means}

The POS hit a payment problem it has no dedicated code for. Most of the time this means the payment was **not** recorded: the message says "The payment wasn't recorded — nothing was taken."

## Your data {#your-data}

When the message says the payment wasn't recorded, nothing was charged and the order still owes its balance.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open the order: in the POS order list, or in *WP Admin > WooCommerce > Orders*.
   - It shows the payment (paid, or a payment note for this amount): there is nothing more to do. Do not take it again.
   - It does not show the payment: nothing was taken. Take the payment again, or use another payment method.
2. For a card terminal or online gateway, also check the terminal screen or the provider dashboard before retrying, so the customer is not charged twice.
3. Expand [the log entry](#log-entry) (*POS > Logs*):
   - **"Payment method not found"** for POS Cash or POS Card: see [Payment method not found on WCPOS 2.0.0](#payment-method-not-found).
   - Anything else: tap **Copy debug info** and send it to support with the time of the sale.

## "Payment method not found" on WCPOS 2.0.0 {#payment-method-not-found}

Cash and Card show in the till, but every payment fails, and the order stays open. This happens when another plugin, or the theme, loads the WooCommerce payment gateways before WCPOS has added its own. A later app update reports this as [PAYMENT213](./PAYMENT213.mdx).

- **Fix:** update the WCPOS plugin to 2.0.1 or later. If you use WCPOS Pro, update it to 2.0.2 or later, which includes the fix.
- Still seeing it after updating? Contact support with the log row from **Copy debug info**.
- This is not caused by Cloudflare or your host, and it does not need a rollback to 1.11.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.
