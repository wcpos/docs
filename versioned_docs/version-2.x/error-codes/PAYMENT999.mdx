## What this means {#what-this-means}

The POS hit a payment problem it has no dedicated code for. Most of the time this means the payment was **not** recorded: the toast says "The payment wasn't recorded — nothing was taken."

## Your data {#your-data}

When the toast says the payment wasn't recorded, nothing was charged and the order still owes its balance.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Open the order: in the POS order list, or in *WP Admin > WooCommerce > Orders*.
   - It shows the payment (paid, or a payment note for this amount): there is nothing more to do. Do not take it again.
   - It does not show the payment: nothing was taken. Take the payment again, or use another payment method.
2. For a card terminal or online gateway, also check the terminal screen or the provider dashboard before retrying, so the customer is not charged twice.
3. Expand the log entry (*POS > Logs*). The `error` line names the store's answer:
   - **"Payment method not found"** for POS Cash or POS Card: see [Payment method not found on WCPOS 2.0.0](#payment-method-not-found).
   - Anything else: tap **Copy debug info** and send it to support with the time of the sale.

## "Payment method not found" on WCPOS 2.0.0 {#payment-method-not-found}

Cash and Card show in the till, but every payment fails, and the order stays open. This happens when another plugin, or the theme, loads the WooCommerce payment gateways before WCPOS has added its own. A later app update reports this as [PAYMENT213](./PAYMENT213.mdx).

- **Fix:** update the WCPOS plugin as soon as the release that fixes this is out. Until then, contact support: there is a one-file fix that a site administrator can drop into `wp-content/mu-plugins/`.
- This is not caused by Cloudflare or your host, and it does not need a rollback to 1.11.
