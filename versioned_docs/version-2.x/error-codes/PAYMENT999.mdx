**Symptom:** a red pop-up reads "The payment wasn't recorded — nothing was taken. Try again." or "Payment handling hit an unexpected problem.", with a **Help** button.

**Do this now:** check whether the payment went through before you take it again.

:::warning

The result couldn't be confirmed. Check before trying again.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. **If the pop-up says nothing was taken:** take the payment again, or use another method.
2. Otherwise, open the order in **Orders**, or ask a manager to check your store. **If it shows the payment:** don't take it again.
3. For a card reader or gateway, also check its screen or the provider's dashboard before you take it again, so the customer isn't charged twice.
4. [Open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open) in **Store health → Logs** with **Errors** on, press **Copy debug info** and send it to support.

### "Payment method not found" on WCPOS 2.0.0 {#payment-method-not-found}

Cash and Card show, but every payment fails. Another plugin or the theme loaded the payment gateways before WCPOS. Ask whoever looks after your website to update WCPOS to 2.0.1 or later, and WCPOS Pro to 2.0.2 or later.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

When the pop-up says nothing was taken, nothing was charged.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Send support the debug info each time: this cause has no code of its own yet.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. "Payment method not found" isn't caused by Cloudflare or your host, and doesn't need a rollback to 1.11. A later app reports it as PAYMENT213.
2. Still seeing it after updating? Contact support with **Copy debug info** from the till.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time of the sale.
