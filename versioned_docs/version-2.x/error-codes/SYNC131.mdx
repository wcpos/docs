**Symptom:** in **Store health → Logs**, an entry reads "Your store had an error and didn't finish this request." When you connect a store, a pop-up says the same, with a **Help** button.

{/* source: core.json:2443; connect pop-up packages/core/src/screens/auth/hooks/use-api-discovery.ts:195-205 (title = summary: packages/core/src/contexts/merchant-toast.ts:69-72), Help packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:873-891; no pop-up of its own while syncing: apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:132-153, :525-533, :561-567 (no showToast in the file), packages/core/src/screens/main/hooks/use-rest-http-client/extract-valid-json.ts:47-52, :73-78 */}

**Do this now:** try what you were doing once more, and don't clear this device's data.

{/* source: core.json:2559; core.json:2169 */}

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It can hold changes your store doesn't have yet.

:::

{/* source: registry dataSafety local-only, error-registry.json:79; the app's own line, core.json:2169 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Try what you were doing again. Changes the till sends on its own are retried by themselves.
2. **If it works:** carry on.
3. **If it fails again:** open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC131 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).
4. Tell whoever looks after your website the time on the entry. The fault is on your website, not on this till.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2559, error-registry.json:88; automatic retry of a sync push: apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:500-520 (a retryable failure is an attempt, coded only once 3 in a row failed); step 3 All, not Errors: sync rows are written at warn (sync-log-observer.ts:105-107), and the Errors preset is level error only (logs-logic.ts:111); step 4 core.json:2559, error-registry.json:82 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing on this device. A change the till was sending stays here and is sent again; until then it counts under **changes waiting to send** in **Store health → Database**.

{/* source: error-registry.json:82, :88; sync-log-observer.ts:500-520; core.json:1901; support/store-health.mdx:40 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Your website has a fault of its own. Tell whoever looks after your website, with the time from the log entry.

{/* source: error-registry.json:82, :85 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Your store received the till's request and failed while handling it.

1. In WP Admin, open **WooCommerce → Status → Logs** and check the newest fatal-errors log around the time of the failure.
2. If it began after a plugin, theme or PHP update, roll that change back or disable it.
3. If WooCommerce's logs show nothing, ask your host for the server's PHP error log for the same time.
4. Once it's fixed, ask the cashier to try again.

{/* source: error-registry.json:82; steps error-registry.json:85-88 */}

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time it failed.

The entry carries the HTTP status, the endpoint and the store's own error code. Any status but 401, 403, 409, 426 or 429, or a reply the till couldn't read, is SYNC131.
