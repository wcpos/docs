**Symptom:** a pop-up reads **Order deleted on the store**, with a **Help** button. Its second line names the order and says the cart was kept as a new order, or the order was removed from this device.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/use-store-deleted-orders.ts:59-72; core.json:1214-1216; Help: packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:873-891 */}

**Do this now:** read the pop-up's second line to see whether this till kept the cart or removed the order.

{/* source: use-store-deleted-orders.ts:64-70; registry error-registry.json:184 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. To read it again, open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC203 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).
2. **If the cart was kept:** carry on with the sale. When you press **Checkout**, it reaches your store as a new order with a new number.
3. **If the order was removed by mistake:** ask whoever looks after your website to restore it. This till picks it up again on its next refresh.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:184; All, not Errors, because SYNC203 is warn (error-registry.json:176; the Errors preset is level error only, logs-logic.ts:111-112); step 2 error-registry.json:185 (says "Pay"; the button is Checkout: core.json:1101, pay.tsx:250), held until Checkout: packages/sync-engine/src/write-path/open-cart-hold.ts:4-8, :51-57; step 3 error-registry.json:186 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. A kept cart stays on this till and is sent when you press **Checkout**. Nothing else is waiting to send.

{/* source: dataSafety order-safe, error-registry.json:179; error-registry.json:182; packages/query/src/mutations/settle-store-deleted-order.ts:34-49 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Someone is trashing orders a till still has open. Ask whoever looks after your website to agree who closes open carts first.

{/* source: error-registry.json:187 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. To restore a removed order, open **WooCommerce → Orders → Trash** in WP Admin and restore it. The till picks it up again on its next refresh.
2. Close open carts on the till before trashing their orders.

{/* source: error-registry.json:186-187 */}

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number.

{/* source: the entry names the order number, use-store-deleted-orders.ts:73-78 */}

<ErrorMeta code="SYNC203" />

Your store answered the change with status 404 and the reason `woo_rxdb_sync_record_not_found`. An open cart is re-created on the same uuid; any other order is discarded.
