## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS could not confirm whether checkout completed.

Do not retry blindly. Check the store for the order first, then export diagnostics if its outcome is still unclear.

## Your data {#your-data}

The outcome could not be confirmed — verify before retrying. If this persists, export diagnostics and contact WCPOS support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Do not press **Process Payment** again and do not charge the customer again until you know what happened.
2. Check the payment side first — the payment provider's own screen, receipt or dashboard shows whether the customer was charged.
3. Refresh the till's orders: open **Store health → Database**, open the **⋮** menu on the **Orders** row and choose **Check for changes now**. If the order arrives as paid, it normally leaves the cart and, on WCPOS Pro, shows as paid in **Orders**. Without Pro, ask someone to check **WooCommerce → Orders** in WP Admin, searching by the total and the last few minutes.
4. If the provider or WooCommerce shows the order paid, the sale went through — do not create it again. If it is still in the cart, reload the app and the till finishes it.
5. If the order is not paid and the provider shows no charge, retry checkout once.
6. If it is still unclear, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact support before another attempt.
