**Symptom:** after you press **Charge** in the checkout window, a red pop-up reads "WCPOS could not confirm whether checkout completed.", with a **Help** button.

**Do this now:** check the order and the payment before you press **Charge** again.

:::warning

Don't press **Charge** again or charge the customer again until you know whether this sale was paid.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check the payment: the card terminal or provider's screen, or for cash, the drawer.
2. **With WCPOS Pro:** in **Store health → Database**, press **⋮** on **Orders**, then **Check for changes now**, and open the order in **Orders**.
3. **Without Pro:** ask a manager to find the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** by its total and the time.
4. **If it reads Processing or Completed, or the customer paid:** don't charge again. Reload the app so the till finishes the sale.
5. **If it isn't paid and nothing was charged:** press **Charge** once more.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

The order is kept, but whether it was paid isn't known. An order can exist unpaid, so go by its status.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it's still unclear, press **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact support.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The checkout started, then the till lost its answer: an error from the store, a timeout, or a lost connection while the payment ran.

1. In WP Admin, open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** and read its status and notes.
2. Check [the store's WCPOS log](../support/logs.mdx#wp-admin-logs) under **POS → Settings → Logs** at the time of the sale.

</details>

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number and the time.
