## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS could not confirm whether checkout completed.

Open the order and check its status before retrying. If it's paid or completed, don't charge again.

## Your data {#your-data}

The result couldn't be confirmed — check before trying again. If this keeps happening, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact WCPOS support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

:::warning Do not charge again
Do not press **Process Payment** (on the checkout's **Legacy** tab) again and do not charge the customer again until you know the order's status.
:::

1. Open the order and check its status. On WCPOS Pro, refresh the till's orders first — **Store health → Database**, the **⋮** menu on the **Orders** row, **Check for changes now** — then open the order in **Orders**.
   - Without Pro, ask your site administrator to open it (see [On the store](#on-the-store)).
2. Finding the order is not enough — an order can exist without being paid. If its status is **Processing** or **Completed**, the sale went through: don't charge again.
3. If the order is still unpaid, or you can't find it, check whether the customer paid — for a card, the payment provider's own screen, receipt or dashboard; for cash, the drawer and the amount tendered. If they paid, don't charge again.
   - If you found the order, reload the app and check its status again (on start, the till finishes any sale whose payment it recorded).
   - If you can't find it, or it still isn't paid, go to the last step.
4. If the order is not paid and the provider shows no charge, retry checkout once.
5. If it is still unclear, use **Copy debug info** in [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry) and contact support before another attempt.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. Without WCPOS Pro, open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** in WP Admin, searching by the total and the last few minutes.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.
