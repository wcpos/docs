## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. **In a browser:** press **Reload**; if the screen returns, hold **Shift** while reloading.
2. **Desktop app:** install the update and reopen, or download it from [Installation](../getting-started/installation.mdx#app-requirements).
3. **Phone or tablet:** install the update from TestFlight or the Google Play beta, then reopen.
4. **No update offered?** Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:295, update-required.tsx:42-47, pending/SYNC341.mdx:22; step 2 core.json:297, pending/SYNC341.mdx:23; step 3 core.json:296, error-registry.json:342, getting-started/installation.mdx:103-104;
    step 4 error-registry.json:343. The gate replaces the drawer, so Logs can't be reached from it: _layout.tsx:252, :315-320 */}

### If this till runs 1.11 {#till-on-1-11}

Ask whoever looks after your website to reinstall the 1.11 plugin, then reopen the till. Update once **changes waiting to send** in **Store health → Database** reads **0** and each open order is finished or saved with **Save to Server**. [Full steps](../getting-started/upgrading-to-2.mdx#unsent-sales).

{/* source: pending/SYNC341.mdx:21; getting-started/upgrading-to-2.mdx:152-159 (anchor at :152); core.json:1901 (changes waiting to send) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

On a 2.0 till, nothing: unsent sales send after the update. A 1.11 till loses them when it first opens 2.0.

{/* source: core.json:296-297, :2570; packages/sync-core/src/drainMutationQueue.update-required.test.ts:1-7 (the refusal leaves the sale queued); 1.11 path as the warning above */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Update every till each time your store's plugin is updated.

{/* source: reference/architecture.mdx:166 ("The app and the plugin are updated together"); pending/SYNC341.mdx:21 ("Do the same for every other till that hasn't opened 2.0 yet") */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The plugin now needs a newer app than this till runs, and your store pauses its syncing until the app is updated, as a protection.

1. A browser till loads the app version that matches your plugin, so a reload picks it up.
2. If no newer app is out yet, restore the previous WCPOS plugin version to resume syncing until it ships.
3. If a 1.11 till still has unsent sales, reinstall the 1.11 plugin from [Previous Versions](../getting-started/previous-versions.mdx) so it can send them, on every till that hasn't opened 2.0.
