**Symptom:** after you press **Connect**, red text under it reads "This store's hosting setup is blocking the app — the details page names the exact cause and fix.", with a **Learn more** link to this page. A pop-up with a **Help** button opens too.

**Do this now:** send whoever looks after your website a link to this page, since nothing at the till can change the limit.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Send whoever looks after your website a link to this page.
2. Leave the address as it is. Your store answered the POS, so the address is right.
3. Once they've raised the limit, press **Connect** again.
4. **If it connects:** press **Sign in with WordPress** and carry on.
5. **If the red text shows again:** the limit is still too low. Tell them again.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing on this device or on your store changed. The app stopped before it saved your store.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Your store's server sets a size limit on each request that the POS's sign-in doesn't fit. Only whoever looks after your website can raise it.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The server answered 400 with the sign-in in the `Authorization` header and 414 with it in the URL. No encoding in the app can shrink it.

1. On Apache, raise `LimitRequestFieldSize` (header) or `LimitRequestLine` (URL) in the vhost config.
2. On nginx, raise `large_client_header_buffers`.
3. Behind a proxy or CDN, the limit can sit at that layer too. Check both.
4. If the limits can't change, contact support about a smaller sign-in token for this site.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support with your store's address. Logs can't be opened from this screen.

<SupportContact />

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="AUTH441" />

Header path 400 and parameter path 414, from the auth test or the echo probes. Logged to the developer console as "Store host blocked authorization probes".

</details>
