**Symptom:** with no message, your avatar sheet shows the register **Open since** a time and a name you didn't enter. Paid ins or outs you recorded before show **Refused**.

{/* source: takeover: packages/query/src/register-session/queue.ts:155-156, :376-385, :645-697 (the store's session is adopted); no pop-up: severity warn, packages/query/src/register-session/audit.ts:263-266;
    packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/user-sheet.tsx:401-409, core.json:281; the dropped session's movements fail: queue.ts:337-342, :459-467; panel packages/query/src/register-session/drawer.ts:67-71, packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/register-panel.tsx:524-529, core.json:2915 */}

**Do this now:** check which drawer you are counting before you record any more cash.

{/* source: core.json:2664 */}

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It holds cash movements your store doesn't have.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety local-only, error-registry.json:2417; the app's own line, core.json:2169; error-registry.json:2424 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tap your avatar, then **In the drawer**, to open the register panel.
2. Check the **Float**. If it isn't what you counted in, you're on the other device's session.
3. Both devices now sell into one drawer, counted once. Agree with the other device who counts and closes.
4. **If a movement shows Refused:** record it again here. **Retry** can't send it, because it belongs to the dropped session.

{/* source: step 1 packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/register-bar.tsx:50-51, user-sheet.tsx:199-221, core.json:285; step 2 register-panel.tsx:455-458, core.json:252; error-registry.json:2422;
    step 3 error-registry.json:2420, :2423; step 4 error-registry.json:2424, current page step 3; a movement of a refused session fails again: queue.ts:459-467 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing is lost, but the session this device started was dropped for the one your store already had. Anything recorded here before the switch stays with it, outside the drawer total you now see.

{/* source: error-registry.json:2420, :2424 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

A register that keeps being taken over is used by two devices. That's expected when they share one drawer. If they don't, tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: current page, On the store step 1; error-registry.json:2423 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. The register list in WP Admin shows which sessions are open and on which register.
2. On the free plugin, every device shares the one register.
3. With WCPOS Pro, add a register for the second counter in the store editor, then pick it on that device with **Switch register**. See [Registers and Devices](../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#several-devices-on-one-register).

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:2425; step 2 settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx:56; step 3 current page, On the store step 2, core.json:292, stores/registers-and-devices.mdx:79, :112 */}

</details>
