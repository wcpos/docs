**Do this now:** ask whoever looks after your website to stop their cache storing your store's answers, then connect again.

{/* source: core.json:2640 (the app's own action line). No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1880 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Send whoever looks after your website a link to this page. Their cache must stop storing your store's answers.
2. Once they've changed the cache settings and cleared it, enter **Your store's address** and press **Connect** again.
3. **If it connects:** carry on selling.
4. **If the red text shows again:** the cache still answers for your store. Tell them again.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2640, error-registry.json:1883; step 2 core.json:2640 ("reconnect afterwards"), error-registry.json:1888 (purge first), core.json:482, :477; steps 3-4 connect-probes.ts:497-524 (the same checks run again on Connect) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing on this device is lost. WCPOS doesn't connect rather than risk showing one person's data to another. Anything already on this device stays there and sends once it connects.

{/* source: core.json:2640; connect-probes.ts:523-524 (blocking); dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1880; "sends once it connects" kept from the current HOST151 page */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

A cache in front of your store hands out stored answers without checking who is asking, so one cashier could see another's data. Only whoever looks after your website can fix it.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1882-1883 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Two differently-authenticated checks got the first one's answer. WordPress always answers per user, so a hosting cache is replaying it. Fix it even if WCPOS connected.

1. Exclude `/wp-json/` and `/?rest_route=` from page caching. LiteSpeed: disable REST caching or add a no-cache rule. WP Engine: extend the WooCommerce exclusion to `/wp-json/wcpos` and the `?rest_route=` form. Sucuri: set the caching level to honour Cache-Control.
2. The store's API already sends `Cache-Control: no-store` and `Vary: Authorization`; the cache ignores them. Point the host at those headers.
3. Purge the cache after changing the settings, then have the till connect again.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1883, :1886-1888; connect-probes.ts:474-499 (two probes, different lengths); WP Engine ?rest_route= form from the current HOST151 page */}

</details>
