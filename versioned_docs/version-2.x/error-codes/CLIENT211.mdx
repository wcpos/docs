## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS stopped because of a device-level crash.

Restart WCPOS and check your last sale or change saved. Install any app or system updates. Contact support if the crash repeats.

## Your data {#your-data}

The result couldn't be confirmed — check before trying again. If you were saving a sale or change when it crashed, check it saved before doing it again.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Restart WCPOS and check your last sale or change saved before doing it again: look at **Store health → Database** (a sale counted under **changes waiting to send** is saved and still sending), the cart, and Orders (WCPOS Pro) or **WooCommerce → Orders**. If a card was involved, check the terminal or the payment provider too — if the customer was charged, don't charge again. Only if it really didn't save, enter it again.
2. Install pending updates for the app and the device's operating system — native crashes are often already-fixed bugs. On a managed till, whoever manages the device does this.
3. If the crash repeats on the same action, tap **Copy debug info** on the **Logs** tab and contact support, noting exactly what was tapped when it crashed.
