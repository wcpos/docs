**Symptom:** an action such as saving a sale failed, and **Logs** has a CLIENT211 entry: "WCPOS stopped because of a device-level crash." It shows only with **Verbose diagnostics** on.

{/* source: packages/hooks/src/use-http-client/use-http-client.tsx:433-435, :481-485 (debug row, no pop-up), :497 (the action gets the error); packages/utils/src/logger/map-exception.ts:26-27 (access violation, breakpoint, SIGILL, native crash);
    core.json:2490; logger/index.ts:898-922 (debug rows persist only while verbose is on); core.json:2135 */}

:::warning Check before you repeat
Before repeating it, check your last sale or change saved. For a card, check the terminal or provider; don't charge twice.
:::

{/* source: core.json:2606; dataSafety outcome-unknown, error-registry.json:1152; kept from current/CLIENT211.mdx */}

**Do this now:** restart WCPOS, then check your last sale or change saved.

{/* source: core.json:2606; kept from current/CLIENT211.mdx */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web.
2. Check it saved: **Store health**, under **Database**, counts it in **changes waiting to send** while it sends. Check the cart, and **Orders** with WCPOS Pro.
3. **Only if it didn't save:** enter it again.
4. Install pending updates for WCPOS and the device's operating system. On a managed till, whoever manages the device does this.

{/* source: steps 1-4 kept from current/CLIENT211.mdx (its card check is in the warning above), core.json:2606; step 2 core.json:828, :633, :1901, :731 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

The result couldn't be confirmed. A sale counted in **changes waiting to send** is saved on this device and still sending.

{/* source: use-http-client.tsx:477-480 (the request's ending is unknown to the transport); core.json:1901; kept from current/CLIENT211.mdx */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If the crash repeats on the same action, note exactly what you tapped when it crashed and contact support.

{/* source: kept from current/CLIENT211.mdx (step 3) */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Without WCPOS Pro, check the till's last sale or change in **WooCommerce → Orders**.

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Turn on **Verbose diagnostics**, repeat the action, press **All**, type CLIENT211 in **Search Logs** and [send the log](../support/logs.mdx#for-support). Verbose switches off after 24 hours.

{/* source: logs-logic.ts:105-108 (debug rows only with verbose on; All); core.json:2131, :961; docs support/logs.mdx:42, :115 */}
