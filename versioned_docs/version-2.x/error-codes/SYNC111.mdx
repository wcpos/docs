## What this means {#what-this-means}

Some data stored on this device is damaged, so it can't sync.

Tap **Copy debug info** at the top of **Store health → Logs** and send it to WCPOS support. Don't reinstall WCPOS, sign out, or clear its data: changes that haven't reached your store yet exist only on this device.

## Your data {#your-data}

Don't clear this device's data — it may hold sales that haven't reached your store. Reinstalling the app or signing out can delete them too. Support can tell you whether any one kind of data is safe to re-download.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

:::warning Don't reinstall or clear data
Don't reinstall WCPOS, sign out, or clear its data.
:::

1. Keep selling if WCPOS still works.
2. In [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry), tap **Copy debug info** at the top and send it to WCPOS support, with what you were doing when the entry appeared.
3. Open **Store health → Database** and tell support how many **changes waiting to send** it shows, and whether a **"… changes never reached your server"** or **"… changes were held"** panel is showing — those changes exist only on this device.
4. The log entry does not say which data is damaged. Support may ask you to re-download one kind of data (its row's **⋮** menu → **Clear & re-download…**) once nothing is waiting to send.
   - Don't do this before support confirms which one: clearing the wrong data, or clearing while changes are waiting, can delete sales that never reached your store.
   - If changes can't send because of the damage, support recovers them first; never re-download while anything is waiting.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.
