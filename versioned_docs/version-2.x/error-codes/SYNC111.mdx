**Do this now:** keep selling if WCPOS still works.

{/* source: current/SYNC111.mdx:29 (keep selling); core.json:2557 */}

:::warning

Don't reinstall WCPOS, sign out or clear its data. It can hold sales that haven't reached your store.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **Copy debug info** (**Share debug info** on phones and tablets) and send it to support.
2. On **Database**, tell support the **changes waiting to send** count and any panel below it.
3. Clear nothing until support names the data to re-download. Then press **Clear & re-download…** in that row's **⋮** menu.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2557, :2124, :2120, logs/index.tsx:352, current/SYNC111.mdx:30; step 2 error-registry.json:38, core.json:1901, :1953, :1919, current/SYNC111.mdx:31;

### Before you clear Orders {#orders}

First finish or [**Save to store**](../pos/cart/open-orders.mdx#sync-order) every open cart: clearing **Orders** deletes a cart your store doesn't have. If one won't save, clear nothing.

{/* source: error-registry.json:39; core.json:38 (Save to store), :1990; support/troubleshooting/clear-local-data.mdx:25 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Not yet. Unsent changes stay on this device unless you reinstall, sign out or clear its data. A re-download clears only that row; your store keeps it.

{/* source: error-registry.json:38; current/SYNC111.mdx:15, :19; core.json:1913-1916, :1990 ("Nothing on the server changes"); error-registry.json:39 (only that data) */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Damaged again after a re-download? Don't clear everything; send debug info to support.

{/* source: error-registry.json:36, :41 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with what you were doing.
