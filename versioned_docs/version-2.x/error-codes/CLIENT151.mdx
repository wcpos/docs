**Symptom:** a red bar reading **Something went wrong:** and the error, with an X, replaces part of the screen. Or the whole screen reads **Something went wrong** with **Try again**. The screen shows no code.

{/* source: packages/components/src/error-boundary/fallback.tsx:67-86 (red bar, "Something went wrong:", the message, X), :35-61 (a narrow panel shows only a warning icon and the X);
    fallback-screen.tsx:9, :17-27 (whole-screen form); apps/main/app/(app)/(drawer)/(pos)/_layout.tsx:97, :108, (tabs)/index.tsx:13, (columns)/index.tsx:55; neither form renders the code */}

**Do this now:** press the X on the red bar, or **Try again** on the full screen, to draw that part again.

{/* source: fallback.tsx:18, :78-84 (X, labelled Dismiss, resets the boundary); fallback-screen.tsx:25 (Try again resets the boundary); core.json:2667 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Press the X, or **Try again**. The panel draws again, and a one-off failure clears.
2. If the message comes straight back, close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web.
3. If it appeared during an action, such as a sale or an edit, check that the action finished before you repeat it.
4. Same message after a reload? Open **Store health**, then **Logs**, press **Errors** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

The failure itself saved and lost nothing. A cart, order or product edit you'd already saved stays on this device and syncs as normal.

{/* source: error-boundary/index.tsx:30-49 (logs only, writes nothing); kept from current/CLIENT151.mdx */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

This is a fault in the app, not your store or device. The log entry names the error and the part of the screen that failed.

{/* source: error-boundary/index.tsx:16-24, :37-47 (message, stack, componentStack); kept from current/CLIENT151.mdx */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Use **Copy debug info** and contact support. With your consent, the same failure also reaches the developers.
