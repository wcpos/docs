**Symptom:** after you press **Connect**, red text under it says this store "reports the same identity as your saved site" and names that store, with a **Learn more** link to this page.

**Do this now:** don't remove the saved store until **Store health** on it shows nothing waiting to send.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Note the store named in the red text. It's already saved on this device.
2. **If it's the same store under another address:** open it from its saved card.
3. **If this address is a copy, such as a test site:** send whoever looks after your website a link to this page.
4. **If this one should replace the saved store:** once that store has nothing waiting to send, open **Actions** on its card, choose **Remove site**, confirm **Remove**, then **Connect**.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. WCPOS didn't save this store, so the saved one and its users are as they were. Removing the saved store signs its users out on this device.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Two websites give WCPOS the same identity. Whoever looks after your website can check which is the copy.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Each site's REST API index (`/wp-json/`) reports a `uuid`. The app saves it with the store and refuses a second site with the same one. A copied site, such as a staging clone, keeps the original's.

1. Open `/wp-json/` on both sites and compare `uuid`.
2. On the copy, update WCPOS in WP Admin › **Plugins**. From 1.10.21 it gives a copied site its own identity at its new address. Updating the original does nothing.
3. Check `uuid` again. Once they differ, the cashier presses **Connect** again.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support with both store addresses and the name in the red text. Logs can't be opened from this screen.

<SupportContact />

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="AUTH341" />

`saveConnectedSite` found a saved site with this `uuid` but a different normalised address or connection address. The app writes no log row for this.

</details>
