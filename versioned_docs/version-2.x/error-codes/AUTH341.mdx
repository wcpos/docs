## What this means {#what-this-means}

This store reports the same identity as a store already saved on this device.

Every store carries an identity that the WCPOS plugin creates once. When a site is copied, for example to make a staging site, the copy keeps the original's identity, so the app cannot tell the two apart and refuses to save the second one rather than mixing it into the first. The store you tried to connect is almost certainly a copy of the saved store named in the message.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected. The saved store was left exactly as it was; the new store was not saved.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

**On the store you tried to connect** — for whoever manages that WordPress site:

1. WP Admin → Plugins → update WCPOS to the latest version. From 1.10.21 the plugin gives a copied site its own identity the first time it runs at its new address.
2. Connect again from the till.

**At the till** — if the store cannot be updated yet:

1. On the connect screen, remove the saved store named in the message.
2. Connect the store you want by its address.

You can keep only one of the two connected until the copy has its own identity.

**If both addresses point at the same store** (for example a local network address and the public domain): remove the saved entry and connect again using the address this till will use from now on.
