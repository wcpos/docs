**Symptom:** a red pop-up reads **Error adding Hoodie to cart** (with your product's name), **Error adding Fee to cart**, **Error adding Shipping to cart** or **Failed to apply coupon. Please try again.**, with a **Help** button.

**Do this now:** try adding it again, because the cart is unchanged.

{/* source: core.json:2647, :2531; error-registry.json:2039, :2041. No warning box: dataSafety order-safe */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Add the product, fee or shipping again, or apply the coupon again.
2. **If it fails again:** add a different product once, to see whether the problem is that one item or every item.
3. **If only that item fails:** sell the rest, and tell a manager the product's name.
4. **If every item fails:** close WCPOS, open it again, and try once more.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:2041, core.json:2647; step 2 error-registry.json:2042; step 3 error-registry.json:2042 (one failing item points at that record); step 4 error-registry.json:2043 (everything failing points at the device; restart WCPOS) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing was added and nothing was removed. A new customer you created is saved, but not added to the order: search for them and add them again.

{/* source: dataSafety order-safe, error-registry.json:2036, :2039; packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/cart/add-customer.tsx:136-147, core.json:1193-1194 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it still fails after you reopen WCPOS, press **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact support.

{/* source: escalation support-with-export, error-registry.json:2037, :2043; core.json:2124 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

One product that always fails points at that product's record. Every item failing points at this device.

1. Note the product that fails and the time.
2. Send support the till's **Copy debug info** report with them.
