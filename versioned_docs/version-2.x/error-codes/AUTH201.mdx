## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your account does not have permission to perform this action.

Ask a store administrator to check the account's roles and capabilities, then try again.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected. If this persists, ask your store administrator.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. **At the till:** note what you were doing when it was refused — editing a product, a customer, an order — and which account is signed in. If it appeared on its own during sync, just pass on the entry in **Store health → Logs**: it records the refused request. Pass both to your administrator; carry on selling meanwhile if the refused action was not part of the sale.
2. **Site administrator:** go to **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Access** and select the account's role. Under **What a Cashier can do** (the heading names the role), tick the task that covers the refused action — **Use the POS**, **Create & edit products**, **Manage orders**, **Manage customers** and so on. A dash with a badge such as **3 of 7** means the task is only partly granted; ticking it grants the rest. **Restore defaults** puts a role back to what a fresh install grants — on a custom role WCPOS gives no POS access by default, so there it removes POS access instead.
3. **Site administrator:** if the account can sign in but every product, customer or order request is refused, check whether it holds **more than one role**. Role-editor plugins such as Members apply a *Deny* placed on any one role to the whole account, so an Administrator who is also a Customer loses whatever the Customer role denies. Remove the extra role or clear the *Deny* marks; see [A user with more than one role](/settings/wp-admin/access#more-than-one-role).
4. **Back at the till:** sign out and sign in again so the new permissions apply, then retry the refused action.
