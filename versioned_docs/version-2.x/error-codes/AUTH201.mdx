## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your account does not have permission to perform this action.

Nothing was lost. Ask your store admin to give your user role permission for this action, then try again.

## Your data {#your-data}

Nothing was lost — no order or product data is affected.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Note what you were doing when it was refused — editing a product, a customer, an order — and which account is signed in.
   - If it appeared on its own during sync, just pass on the entry in [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry): it records the refused request.
2. Pass both to your administrator; carry on selling meanwhile if the refused action was not part of the sale.
3. Back at the till, once your administrator has made the change: sign out and sign in again so the new permissions apply, then retry the refused action.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. First decide whether this account should be allowed to do it. If not, the refusal is working as intended — leave the role alone and have the action done by an account that is allowed.
2. If it should, go to **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Access** and select the account's role. Under **What a Cashier can do** (the heading names the role), tick the task that covers the refused action — **Use the POS**, **Create & edit products**, **Manage orders**, **Manage customers** and so on.
   - A dash with a badge such as **3 of 7** means the task is only partly granted; ticking it grants the rest.
   - **Restore defaults** puts a role back to what a fresh install grants — on a custom role WCPOS gives no POS access by default, so there it removes POS access instead.
3. If the account can sign in but every product, customer or order request is refused, check whether it holds **more than one role**. Role-editor plugins such as Members apply a *Deny* placed on any one role to the whole account, so an Administrator who is also a Customer loses whatever the Customer role denies. Remove the extra role or clear the *Deny* marks; see [A user with more than one role](/manage-store/staff-permissions#more-than-one-role).

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[50, 0], [64, -10], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support.
