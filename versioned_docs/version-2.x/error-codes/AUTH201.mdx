**Symptom:** a red pop-up reads "Your account does not have permission to perform this action.", or your store's own reason, with a **Help** button. A refused order change also waits in **Store health → Database**.

**Do this now:** tell whoever looks after your website what you were doing and which account is signed in.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Note what you were doing and who is signed in. Carry on selling if it wasn't part of the sale.
2. Ask whoever looks after your website to let your role do that task.
3. Once they have, tap your avatar, press **Sign out**, and sign in again.
4. Do it again. **For a refused order change:** open **Store health → Database**, find it under **changes never reached your server**, and press **Send again**.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. A refused order change waits on this till until you send it again or discard it. A refused product, customer or coupon change is put back to your store's version.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If your role shouldn't do this task, the refusal is working. Have someone whose role allows it do it.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Access**, choose the account's role and tick the task under **What a Cashier can do**. A badge such as **3 of 7** means partly granted. See [Change what a role can do](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx#change-a-role).
2. **Restore defaults** on a custom role removes POS access: WCPOS grants a custom role none.
3. If every request is refused, check whether the account holds more than one role. Remove the extra role or clear the *Deny* marks; see [A user with more than one role](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx#more-than-one-role).

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

If it's refused after the change, [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with what you were doing.

<SupportContact />

<details>
<summary>Code details for support</summary>

<ErrorMeta code="AUTH201" />

Your store answered 403, or with `rest_forbidden`, a `rest_cannot_*` reason or `woocommerce_pos_rest_forbidden`. A refused product, customer or coupon change logs [SYNC202](SYNC202.mdx) once it's put back.

</details>
