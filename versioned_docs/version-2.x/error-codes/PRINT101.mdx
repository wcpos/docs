## What this means {#what-this-means}

Automatic printing did not start for this receipt.

The sale is saved. Open the order's receipt and tap **Print Receipt**. Then check the automatic-printing settings and the printer so the next receipt prints by itself.

## Your data {#your-data}

The sale is saved. No order or product data is affected.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. On the receipt shown after the sale, press **Print Receipt** to print it by hand — the sale is unaffected. If no receipt is on screen, on WCPOS Pro open **Orders**, use the order's **Receipt** action and press **Print Receipt**.
2. If no receipt appears after sales at all, automatic showing is probably off: open the cart's settings (the sliders icon above the cart) and turn on both **Automatically show receipt after checkout** and **Automatically print receipt after checkout**. Automatic printing happens on that receipt, so it needs both.
3. Check the printer in **Settings → Printing**: the printer you expect is listed under **Printers**, and **Test Print** from its **Edit** screen prints.
4. If **Print Receipt** fails too, it is the printer or its connection — see [PRINT301](/error-codes/PRINT301). If manual printing works but automatic printing keeps failing with both settings on, use **Copy debug info** on the **Logs** tab and contact support.
