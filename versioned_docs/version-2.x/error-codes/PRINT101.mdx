**Symptom:** after a sale, a pop-up reads "Automatic printing did not start for this receipt." Its second line names what to check on the printer, or says how to print by hand.

**Do this now:** print the receipt by hand, because the sale is already saved.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. On the receipt after the sale, press **Print receipt**.
2. No receipt on screen? Press **Reprint** on the pop-up to open it, then **Print receipt**.
3. **If it prints:** carry on selling.
4. **If it doesn't:** open **Settings → Printing** and press **Test Print** on the printer's row.
5. **If no receipt appears after sales at all:** open the cart's settings (the sliders icon above the cart) and turn on **Automatically show receipt after checkout** and **Automatically print receipt after checkout**.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The sale is saved and complete. Only the automatic print didn't happen.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If printing by hand works but automatic printing fails after every sale, send the log to support.

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time of the sale.
