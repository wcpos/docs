**Symptom:** a card payment ends and a red pop-up gives the reason, such as **Declined or cancelled on the terminal**, or reads "The terminal did not take this payment."

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/use-tender-flow.ts:1426-1441 (title: the provider's sentence, else a reason label, else the summary); core.json:418, :415, :417, :2469; labels.ts:67-73 */}

**Do this now:** ask the customer for another card, or take the payment another way.

{/* source: core.json:2585; error-registry.json:692 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Read the reason in the pop-up or on the card reader's screen: declined by the bank, cancelled on the reader, or timed out before a card was presented.
2. Press **Try again** to send the payment to the reader again, with the same or another card.
3. Or press **Choose another way** to go back and pick a different payment method.
4. **If the customer says it went through:** check the order before charging again. A payment that reached the bank shows on the order.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:689, :691, core.json:415, :417, :418; step 2 core.json:462, packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/terminal-leg-view.tsx:306-315; step 3 core.json:463, terminal-leg-view.tsx:316-322;
    step 4 current page At the till (kept) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing was charged, and the order still owes its balance.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:686; docsBody :689 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If every card is refused, the problem is the card reader or its account, not the customers. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: error-registry.json:693 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Check that the card reader is online, then look at your payment provider's dashboard.
2. A single decline is between the customer and their bank. WCPOS records it but can't explain it.

{/* source: error-registry.json:693; current page On the store (kept) */}

</details>
