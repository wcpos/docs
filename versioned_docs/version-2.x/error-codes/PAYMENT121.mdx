**Symptom:** after a card payment, a red pop-up reads "The card was charged, but the order did not finish.", and the payment screen shows "The card was charged, but the order didn't finish."

**Do this now:** don't charge the card again, and don't close the payment screen. Do this before the customer leaves.

:::warning

The payment has been taken. Don't charge the customer again.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Don't close the payment screen. With a payment on the order, closing opens **Cancel payment**, which reverses it.
2. Reload the app: the browser tab on the web, or close and reopen the app. On start, the till retries finishing the sale.
3. **If the order is still open:** in **Store health → Database**, open **⋮** on the **Orders** row, press **Check for changes now**, then reload once more.
4. **If it's still open:** serve the customer and leave the order as it is. Ask a manager to have it finished.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The card was charged and your store holds the payment. The order is open rather than complete, so it needs completing, not paying.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

The finishing step keeps failing after the charge. Tell whoever looks after your website, with the **Support details** from the notice.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders**, and confirm the charge in your payment provider's dashboard. In a split sale, check every part was paid.
2. Set the order to the status your POS uses for sales paid that way: the **Order Status** column of the **Gateways** table in **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout**, on that gateway's row.
3. To find out why, expand **Support details** on the notice, or open the entry in **Store health → Logs**. Include it if you contact support.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number and amount.
