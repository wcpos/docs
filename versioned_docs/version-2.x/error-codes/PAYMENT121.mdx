## What this means {#what-this-means}

The terminal took the payment and your store has the money. The step that closes the order afterwards is the one that failed, so the sale is paid for but still sitting open.

This is not a payment problem, and charging the card again would take the money twice. The order just needs finishing.

## Your data {#your-data}

Money moved — the card was charged and your store holds the payment. The order is open rather than complete, so it needs completing, not paying.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

Do this before the customer leaves.

1. Do not take the payment again, and do not use the close button (**X**) on the payment pane — with a payment on the order, closing opens **Cancel payment**, which reverses it.
2. Reload the app (reload the browser tab on web; close and reopen the app on desktop and mobile). On start, the till retries finishing any sale whose card payment it recorded, and the finished order leaves the cart.
3. If the order is still open in the cart after reloading, open **Store health → Database**, open the **⋮** menu on the **Orders** row, choose **Check for changes now**, then reload once more.
4. If it is still open, serve the customer and leave the order as it is — the money is taken. Ask your site administrator to finish it (below).

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. If the order still will not complete on the till, open it in **WooCommerce → Orders** and confirm the charge in your payment provider's dashboard.
   - In a split sale, make sure every part was paid.
2. Then set it to the status your POS uses for sales paid that way: in **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout**, the **Order Status** column of the **Gateways** table shows it on the row of the gateway that took the payment.
3. To find out why, expand **Support details** on the notice at the till, or the entry in [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry): it names what the finishing step failed on — usually a connection that dropped at exactly the wrong moment. Include it if you contact support.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.
