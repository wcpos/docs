Current versions no longer show this code.

**Symptom:** a variation that was being added to a product isn't among the product's options. Nothing else on the product changed.

**Do this now:** check whether the variation is there before you add it again.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It can hold a product change your store doesn't have yet.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open the product's options in the product list and look for the variation. If it's there, don't add it again.
2. In **Store health → Database**, press **⋮** on **Variations**, then **Check for changes now**, and look again.
3. If it still isn't there, tell a manager the product and the options the variation should have.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing else on the product changed. Only this variation wasn't added.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If your store keeps refusing the variation, whoever looks after your website can check the product's attributes.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

Every attribute a variation uses must exist on the parent product and be marked as used for variations.

1. In WP Admin, open the product and check its attributes against the variation's options.
2. Correct the variation's fields, then add it again.
3. If your store still refuses it, send support the till's **Copy debug info** report.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the product's name and the variation's options.
