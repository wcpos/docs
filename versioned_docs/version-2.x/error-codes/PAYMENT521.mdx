**Symptom:** after you record a payment, a pop-up says the store had already closed the order. **Orders** shows **1 sale needs attention**.

**Do this now:** keep the customer with you: you hold money the order has no record of.

:::warning

The payment has been taken. Don't charge the customer again.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Go to **Orders**. The red band names the order, amount and payment method.
2. Decide with the customer or a manager whether the sale goes ahead.
3. **If it doesn't:** hand back the cash, or refund the charge on the card machine. Don't record a **Paid out** for it.
4. **If it does:** ask whoever looks after your website to reopen the order, then record the payment again from the money you already hold, with the same method you used the first time.
5. Press **Handled** to clear the entry.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing was saved on the order, and the till never retries the payment by itself.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Orders are being closed elsewhere while still open on a till. Tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, open **WooCommerce → Orders** and find the order named in the entry. It was cancelled, trashed or refunded before the till's payment arrived.
2. To let the sale go ahead, reopen it, for example by setting it back to **Pending payment**. Then tell the cashier to record the payment again.

</details>
