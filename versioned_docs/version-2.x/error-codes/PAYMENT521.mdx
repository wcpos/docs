## What this means {#what-this-means}

The store had already closed the order, so a payment taken at the till was not recorded.

The till took a manual payment (cash, or a card run on a separate machine), but the store had already cancelled, trashed or refunded the order and refused to record it. The toast reads *"The store had already closed order #…, so this … payment was not recorded. Return the money or reopen the order in WooCommerce."*

## Your data {#your-data}

Money moved, and your store did not record it. Nothing was stored on the order. The payment stays under **… sale needs attention** in **Orders** until you press **Handled** on its row.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

**At the till:**

1. Check the amount and method taken at the till: the row under **… sale needs attention** in **Orders** repeats the toast, and the same entry is in **Store health → Logs**.
2. If the sale should not go ahead, give that payment back the way it was taken: cash from the drawer, or a refund of that charge on the card machine. Do not record a paid out for cash handed back — the refused payment was never counted in the drawer's expected cash.
3. If the sale should go ahead, ask a site administrator to reopen the order in WooCommerce, for example by setting it back to **Pending payment**. Then record the payment again from the money you already hold, with the same record-only method. Take no new money from the customer.
4. Once the money is settled, press **Handled** on the row in **Orders**.

**On the store** — for the site administrator:

1. If this happens repeatedly, orders are being cancelled or refunded elsewhere while they are open on a till.
