**Symptom:** instead of the till, WCPOS shows **Something went wrong**, with **Try again** and **Reset app data and restart**.

**Do this now:** press **Try again**, which keeps everything on this device.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data, sign out or reinstall. This device may hold sales your store doesn't have yet, such as sales taken offline, and any of these deletes them for good. Press **Reset app data and restart** only if support asks you to.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Press **Try again**.
2. Same screen? Close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web.
3. Still stuck? Contact support, and leave the device as it is.
4. **If support asks you to reset:** press **Reset app data and restart**. Its question says first what would be lost. If it says anything is waiting to be sent, press **Cancel** and tell support.

### If it says Restart required {#restart-required}

The reset is set for the next start. Close WCPOS completely and open it again.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Not yet. **Try again** and a restart keep everything. A reset, signing out or reinstalling deletes sales this device hasn't sent, so reset only if support asks you to.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it fails on every start, leave the device as it is and contact support.

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support with the error line from that screen; once WCPOS opens again, [send the log](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) too.
