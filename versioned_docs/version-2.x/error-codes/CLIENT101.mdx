## What this means {#what-this-means}

WCPOS could not finish starting.

From the till this looks like a blank or stuck screen, or a loading spinner that never clears. Restart WCPOS (on the web, reload the tab) — a startup failure often clears on its own. If it still won't start, contact support before clearing anything.

:::warning Don't clear this device's data
This device may hold sales that haven't reached your store — for example sales taken while offline. While WCPOS can't start, they can't be sent, so clearing local data, signing out or reinstalling would delete them permanently. Contact support before clearing anything.
:::

## Your data {#your-data}

Don't clear this device's data — it may hold changes your store doesn't have yet. Your store's own data is safe and unaffected.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Restart WCPOS once; on the web, fully reload the tab — a transient startup failure often clears on its own.
2. If it still won't start, contact support before clearing anything.
   - Send **Copy debug info** from [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry) if WCPOS opens far enough to reach it.
   - Otherwise send the startup error; where to find it is under For support below.
3. Don't clear local data, sign out or reinstall while you wait — support will tell you whether anything on this device needs recovering first.

<details>
<summary>For support</summary>

If WCPOS doesn't open far enough to reach **Copy debug info**, send the error shown in the browser console (developer tools), or in the desktop app's **Advanced → Toggle Developer Tools**.

</details>

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support.

</JourneyShot>
