**Symptom:** instead of the till, WCPOS shows **Something went wrong**, with **Try again** and **Reset app data and restart**.

{/* source: apps/main/components/root-error-copy.ts:8-11; apps/main/components/root-error.tsx:158-175. The screen shows no code: root-error.tsx:164 (detail is the raw error) */}

**Do this now:** press **Try again**, which keeps everything on this device.

{/* source: root-error-copy.ts:9-10; root-error.tsx:55-61 (resets the start-up and the error screen only) */}

:::warning

Don't reset yet. A reset deletes every sale this device hasn't sent to your store.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety local-only, error-registry.json:1106; root-error.tsx:27-39 (reset deletes all local databases); root-error-copy.ts:52-57 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Press **Try again**.
2. Same screen? Close WCPOS completely and open it again, or reload the tab on the web.
3. Still stuck? Press **Reset app data and restart** just to read its question. Nothing is cleared yet.
4. **If it says nothing is waiting to be sent:** press **Reset and restart**.
5. **If it says anything else:** press **Cancel** and contact support.

{/* source: step 1 root-error-copy.ts:10; step 2 error-registry.json:1104, :1111; step 3 root-error.tsx:63-68 (the button opens the question), root-error-copy.ts:14;
    step 4 root-error-copy.ts:15, :45-49; step 5 root-error-copy.ts:16, :37-42 (count unknown), :52-57 (changes never reached your server), error-registry.json:1104, :1112 */}

### If it says Restart required {#restart-required}

The reset is set for the next start. Close WCPOS completely and open it again.

{/* source: root-error-copy.ts:17-19; root-error.tsx:79-82, :118-127 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Not yet. **Try again** and a restart keep everything. Only a reset deletes data, and its question says first what would be lost.

{/* source: root-error.tsx:55-61 (Try again touches no data), :27-39 (reset); root-error-copy.ts:36-58 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it fails on every start, leave the device as it is and contact support.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1104, :1112 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support with the error line from that screen; once WCPOS opens again, [send the log](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) too.

{/* source: root-error.tsx:164 (the error line); Logs can't be reached from this screen, error-registry.json:1114 */}
