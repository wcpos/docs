**Symptom:** on the **Store checkout** tab, after **Charge**, a red pop-up shows your store's message about the gateway, with a **Help** button.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/hooks/use-checkout-session.ts:134-141, :192-195 (store codes wcpos_payment_gateway_not_available / _not_found), :481-507 (title: the store's sentence); core.json:2589 (second line, app text), :2687, :1212; used by checkout.tsx:111, tender/legacy-tab.tsx:39 */}

**Do this now:** finish the sale with another payment method, such as cash, and tell a manager which gateway failed.

{/* source: core.json:2589; error-registry.json:772 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Take the payment with another method, such as cash, on the **Payments** tab.
2. Tell a manager the code, PAYMENT301, and the gateway's name, so whoever looks after your website can check it.
3. Once it's fixed, reload the app on the till so it picks up the gateway again.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2686, error-registry.json:772; step 2 error-registry.json:765, :768, current page At the till step 1; step 3 error-registry.json:776 */}

### If the method is greyed out {#greyed-out}

A tile under **… not available right now** is a problem on this till, not this error. The reason it shows, such as **Needs a connection** or **Turn on Bluetooth to use the reader**, says what to fix.

{/* source: error-registry.json:773; core.json:2722, :2731, :2768 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing was charged, and no order or product data is affected.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:767; current page Your data */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

The gateway needs checking on your store. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: escalation site-admin, error-registry.json:768 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Go to **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout**. Under **Gateways**, the gateway must be listed with its **Enabled** switch on.
2. If it isn't listed, check that its plugin is installed and active. It doesn't have to be switched on for your web store.
3. On WCPOS Free only the built-in cash and card methods can be enabled. Other gateways need WCPOS Pro.
4. If it worked yesterday, check whether it or WooCommerce updated. **WooCommerce → Status → Logs** can record gateway start-up errors.

{/* source: error-registry.json:774-775 (774 says enable it in WooCommerce → Settings → Payments; the current page and payment/gateways/index.mdx:95 say it needn't be: see CHECK.md) */}

</details>
