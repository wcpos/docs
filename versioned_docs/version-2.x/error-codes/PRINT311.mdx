**Symptom:** after **Send** in **Email Receipt** or **Download PDF**, a pop-up reads "This receipt could not be emailed or downloaded." Its second line gives your store's reason, such as "Your server did not send the email."

{/* source: core.json:2534 (title), :2650 (second line when no reason), :1373, :1377, :1371, :1399; email.tsx:153-177 (toast.text2 is the reason), use-download-receipt-pdf.ts:55-65; merchant-toast.ts:50-77 */}

**Do this now:** try the email or PDF again from the receipt; the sale is already saved.

{/* source: core.json:2650; error-registry.json:2105. No warning box: dataSafety order-safe, error-registry.json:2099 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. **For an email:** the dialog stays open. Check **Email Address**, then press **Send** again.
2. **For a PDF:** with a connection, free some storage, allow downloads and pick a place that can save the file, then press **Download PDF** again.
3. **If a queued email gave up:** open **Store health → Database**. Under **1 receipt email waiting to go out**, press **Send again** or **Remove** on its row.
4. **If it fails again:** [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open). Press **All** and type PRINT311 in **Search Logs**.

{/* source: step 1 email.tsx:68-71, core.json:1372, :1399; step 2 error-registry.json:2106, receipt-actions.tsx:40-48, :81-90, use-download-receipt-pdf.ts:42-47; step 3 queue.ts:469-493, queued-emails.tsx:120-131, :166-188, core.json:955, :953, :949; step 4 email.tsx:164 and use-download-receipt-pdf.ts:56 (warn for a blocked send), logs-logic.ts:111 */}

### If you're offline {#offline}

The button reads **Send when online** and the till says "Receipt email queued": it sends once this device reaches your store.

{/* source: email.tsx:79-103, :156-163, :234; core.json:1400, :1375, :1376 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The sale is saved; only the email or PDF didn't go.

{/* source: dataSafety order-safe, error-registry.json:2099; core.json:2650 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If every receipt email fails, tell whoever looks after your website: the site's outgoing email needs checking.

{/* source: core.json:2650; error-registry.json:2105 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Check that WordPress can send email: the site's outgoing mail or SMTP settings.
2. Once it sends, the till can press **Send** on the receipt again.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:2105; step 2 email.tsx:68-71, :153-154 (a refused send keeps the dialog open) */}

</details>
