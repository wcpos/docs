**Symptom:** a red pop-up gives your store's reason for refusing a change, or reads **Failed to save order**. **Store health → Database** shows **1 change never reached your server**.

{/* source: push-failure-code.ts:94-116 (title = the store's sentence, else the caller's: core.json:658 via use-sync-order.ts:54-56, orders/edit/form.tsx:346-347); checkout core.json:1217 via refusal-toast.ts:16-24 (pay.tsx:87-94); engine row sync-log-observer.ts:574-590; panel core.json:1953, rejected-mutations.tsx:157-161 */}

**Do this now:** keep the change, and read your store's reason first.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It holds a change your store doesn't have yet.

:::

{/* source: registry dataSafety local-only, error-registry.json:129; the app's own line, core.json:2169 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Database**. The change's row gives your store's reason.
2. **If it's still needed:** fix what the reason names, then press **Send again**. The till says **Queued to send again.**
3. **If it isn't:** press **Discard** in the row's **⋮** menu. Read the dialog: a record your store never saved is deleted from this device.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing retries it on its own; it stays here until you send or discard it. A change that isn't listed was undone: fix the reason and make it again.

{/* source: core.json:1955, :1965; unlisted = store's version restored: action hint core.json:2561, error-registry.json:127, :134 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Ask whoever looks after your website to check the record on your store.

{/* source: error-registry.json:137-138 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. If the reason says the user isn't allowed to make the change, their role lacks that task: see [AUTH201](AUTH201.mdx).
2. If it names a field the till doesn't show, open the same record in WP Admin and check whether another plugin adds a rule to it. Then tell the cashier to press **Send again**.

{/* source: error-registry.json:137; Send again error-registry.json:136, core.json:1958 */}

</details>
