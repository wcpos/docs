**Symptom:** an entry in **Store health → Logs** reads "License checking hit an unexpected problem."

**Do this now:** keep working; the POS doesn't lock up over licence checks.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Carry on selling. Licence checks don't stop the till.
2. **If Pro features stay locked:** ask whoever looks after your website to enter your licence key again. Send them a link to this page.
3. **Once they say it's done:** reload the POS: refresh the page, or close and reopen the app.
4. **If Pro features are still locked:** press **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact support with your licence reference.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Orders, products and customers are untouched.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it comes back after the key is entered again, send support the log.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, open **POS → Settings** and, under **Account**, open **License**. If it shows activated, copy the full key from your WCPOS account (the page shows it shortened) and press **Deactivate** first.
2. Paste the key into **License Key** and press **Activate**. **Thank You!** confirms it.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with your licence reference.
