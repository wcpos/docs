**Symptom:** after you take a payment, a pop-up says the order was already paid online. **Orders** shows **1 sale needs attention**.

{/* source: pop-up packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/payments/payment-refusal-attention.ts:46-53 (showToast, no own title, so the title is core.json:2476); band core.json:974, orders/index.mdx:261; customer paid twice error-registry.json:840 */}

**Do this now:** tell the customer the order was already paid, and you'll give the second payment back.

{/* source: error-registry.json:843 */}

:::warning

The payment has been taken. Don't charge the customer again.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety money-moved, error-registry.json:838; the app's own line, core.json:2166 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Go to **Orders**: the red band names the order, amount and method. **Store health → Database** also lists it.
2. Give it back the way it was taken: cash from the drawer, or refund the charge on the card machine or the provider's dashboard.
3. Don't record a **Paid out**, and don't press **Refund** on the order: that refunds the online payment.
4. Don't void or delete the order. Press **Handled** to clear the entry.

{/* source: step 1 orders/index.mdx:261-263, core.json:1063, :2174, registry-mismatches.md (payment-refusal-attention.ts:53 sync.record failed row, health/attention-panel.tsx:46); step 2 error-registry.json:845; step 3 error-registry.json:845 (no paid out: the drawer never counted it), :846, core.json:1019;
    step 4 error-registry.json:846, core.json:977, packages/core/src/screens/main/orders/attention-notice.tsx:86-90 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The online payment stands. The till payment stays on the order, marked failed (`order_already_paid`), never retried.

{/* source: error-registry.json:841; packages/sync-core/src/woo/moneyCommandAnswer.ts:41-45 (stored as a failed row); record-manual-payment.ts:260-286; retryPolicy never, error-registry.json:837 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Orders are paid online while still open on a till. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: error-registry.json:847 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, confirm the online payment on the order in **WooCommerce → Orders**.
2. Add an order note saying the duplicate till payment was returned, how and when. A card refund can take days to reach the customer.

{/* source: error-registry.json:847; ledger reason order_already_paid, error-registry.json:841; refund timing kept from the current page (provider side, see CHECK.md) */}

</details>
