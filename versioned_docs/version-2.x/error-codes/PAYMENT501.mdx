**Symptom:** after you take a payment, a pop-up says the order was already paid online. **Orders** shows **1 sale needs attention**.

**Do this now:** tell the customer the order was already paid, and you'll give the second payment back. Do this before the customer leaves.

:::warning

The payment has been taken. Don't charge the customer again.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Go to **Orders**: the red band names the order, amount and method. **Store health → Database** also lists it.
2. Give it back the way it was taken: cash from the drawer, or refund the charge on the card machine or the provider's dashboard. Later, check that the refund has settled: a card refund can take several days.
3. Don't record a **Paid out**, and don't press **Refund** on the order: that refunds the online payment.
4. Don't void or delete the order. Press **Handled** to clear the entry.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The online payment stands. The till payment stays on the order, marked failed (`order_already_paid`), never retried.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Orders are paid online while still open on a till. Tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, confirm the online payment on the order in **WooCommerce → Orders**.
2. Add an order note saying the duplicate till payment was returned, how and when. A card refund can take days to reach the customer.

</details>
