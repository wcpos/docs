**Symptom:** a scan doesn't find a product you know is there, soon after you open the store on this till.

**Do this now:** keep working, and search for the product by name if a scan misses.

{/* source: core.json:2623; error-registry.json:1523. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Clear **Search products** with the cross, search for the product by name and add it by hand.
2. The till loads the barcode settings again in the background. Scan again a little later.
3. If scans keep missing, reload the app once.
4. **If they still miss after a reload:** check your connection, then open **Store health → Logs**, press **Errors** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).

{/* source: step 1 core.json:1224, versioned_docs/version-2.x/pos/product-panel/barcode-scanning.mdx:93; step 2 error-registry.json:1528, sync-log-observer.ts:879-881 (the next config poll re-pulls the barcode collections);
    step 3 core.json:2623; step 4 current/PRODUCT411.mdx At the till step 3, core.json:2133 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Orders and products are unchanged. Only scans that match a barcode field are affected until the settings load.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1525; create-rxdb-sync-engine.ts:1518-1520 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If scans miss after every reload, tell whoever looks after your website that the till can't load its barcode settings.

{/* source: current/PRODUCT411.mdx At the till step 3 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The till asks your store which product fields hold barcodes when it opens the store. If that request fails or takes longer than five seconds, products arrive without barcodes until the till asks again.

1. Check that the store answers quickly and that nothing blocks WCPOS requests.
2. Check **WP Admin > POS > Settings > General > Barcode Field**.

{/* source: create-rxdb-sync-engine.ts:1500-1520 (hydration with a timeout; products seeded with no barcode carriers); sync-log-observer.ts:879 (5s hydrate timeout); versioned_docs/version-2.x/settings/wp-admin/general.mdx:110-118 */}

</details>
