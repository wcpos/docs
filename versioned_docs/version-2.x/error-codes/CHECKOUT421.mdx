**Symptom:** a banner on the order reads **Check this order's amounts before taking payment**, and says the order uses a tax rate your store no longer has. There's no pop-up.

**Do this now:** check the tax on this order before you take payment.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check the order's tax. Tax already saved on its lines for the missing rate is still counted.
2. **If the tax looks right:** take payment as usual.
3. **If the tax looks wrong:** start the sale on a new order, which uses your store's current tax rates.
4. Tell a manager, so whoever looks after your website checks whether a tax rate was deleted or edited.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The order is safe. Only its tax amount is in question.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If new orders show this too, ask whoever looks after your website to check your store's tax rates.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, go to **WooCommerce → Settings → Tax** and check whether a rate was deleted or edited recently. If it was removed on purpose, restart any orders that were open at the time.
2. If the rates haven't changed, the till may hold an old copy. In **Store health → Database**, press **⋮** on **Tax rates**, then **Clear & re-download…**. This affects only tax rates and signs no one out.
3. If it comes back once tax rates have downloaded, open **Store health → Logs**, press **All**, type CHECKOUT421 in **Search Logs**, open the entry, press **Copy** and send it to support with the order number.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the order number.
