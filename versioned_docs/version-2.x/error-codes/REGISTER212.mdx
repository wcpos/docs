## What this means {#what-this-means}

The drawer carry did not complete on this device. The store was not switched.

The carry closes the source register and opens the target on your store. A refused request changes neither register.

## Your data {#your-data}

If the connection was interrupted, check both registers before trying again: the store may have completed the carry even though this device did not receive its answer.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Check the source and target registers before moving cash or trying again.
2. If the target is already open, switch without carrying and check which drawer belongs to it.
3. Ask the store admin for help if the registers do not match the physical drawers.
