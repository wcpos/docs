**Symptom:** after **Switch to …** with **Take the drawer with you**, a red pop-up says **The drawer carry did not complete. Check both registers before trying again.** The till stays on this store.

**Do this now:** don't move cash or try again until both registers are checked.

:::warning

The result couldn't be confirmed. Your store could have carried the drawer.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Tap your avatar, then **Switch** on the **Store** row, and pick the other store. Its dialog says whether its register is open.
2. **If it says Its register is closed.:** the carry didn't happen, so you can try again.
3. **If its register is open:** the carry went through or another till opened it. Switch, then ask a manager which drawer is whose.

### If the pop-up says it was carried {#carried}

**The drawer was carried. This device records it at the next sync.** means it worked and the till switched. Nothing needs doing.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No sale is affected. A refused carry changes neither register; one cut off mid-way can have closed this register at its expected cash and opened the other with it.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If carries keep failing, or registers don't match the drawers, tell whoever looks after your website.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, check which register is open in each store, and that each matches a physical drawer.
2. A carry is only offered when this register is open, the till is online and the other store's register is closed.

</details>
