Current versions no longer show this code.

**Symptom:** the data on this device was saved by a different WCPOS version, after an app update or downgrade, and can't be opened yet.

{/* source: error-registry.json:269 (summary), :272 (follows an app update or downgrade); core.json:2451.
    No raise site at 6b2a2cb4f: no ERROR_CODES.SCHEMA_MISMATCH use, no server mapping (parse-wp-error.ts:101 maps only wcpos_update_required); wrapped-error-handler-storage.ts:557-559 */}

**Do this now:** update WCPOS on this device to the latest version, then reopen it.

{/* source: error-registry.json:270, :272; core.json:2567 */}

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data or reinstall WCPOS. It can hold sales your store doesn't have yet.

:::

{/* source: core.json:2168 (health.logs.safety_data_at_risk); error-registry.json:267 (data-at-risk), :273; packages/core/src/screens/main/logs/row-detail.test.tsx:463-480 (the row never says clear) */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. **In a browser:** reload the page; if the old version comes back, hold **Shift** while reloading.
2. **Desktop app:** install the update and reopen, or download the latest build from [Installation](../getting-started/installation.mdx#app-requirements).
3. **Phone or tablet:** update WCPOS from TestFlight (iPhone, iPad) or the Google Play beta (Android), then reopen.
4. **If it still won't open:** contact support, and say whether this device could hold unsent sales. Clear its data only when support says nothing is waiting.

{/* source: steps 1-3 current/SYNC311.mdx (At the till 1), error-registry.json:272, getting-started/installation.mdx:103-104;
    step 4 error-registry.json:270, :273-275 (contact support before clearing), error-registry.test.ts:328-347; support/troubleshooting/clear-local-data.mdx:111-118 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Your store's own data is safe. Sales this device hasn't sent stay on it, but can't be sent until its data opens; clearing it deletes them for good.

{/* source: current/SYNC311.mdx (Your data); error-registry.json:270, :273 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it comes back after an update, contact support and say what changed on this device.

{/* source: error-registry.json:272, :275 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support before you clear anything. Once WCPOS opens again, [send the log](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) too.
