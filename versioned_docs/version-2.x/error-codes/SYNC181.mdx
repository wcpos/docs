**Symptom:** no pop-up of its own. **Logs** says "Saving or loading on this device is very slow, and its outcome is not yet known." At checkout, the cart's tab stays on **Saving order…** under a **Local database unavailable** banner.

**Do this now:** carry on if WCPOS is working.

:::warning

The result couldn't be confirmed. Check your last change saved before you make it again.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC181 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).
2. **If WCPOS is working:** nothing to do, but if the entry names a save, check the record saved.
3. **If a screen stays stuck:** reload the tab on the web, or close and reopen the app. Then check the record: a running save isn't resent.

{/* source: step 1 All, not Errors, because the catalogue level is warn (src/data/error-catalogue.json) and the Errors preset is level error only (logs-logic.ts:111); context method and collection, wrapped-error-handler-storage.ts:209-213, error-registry.json:1999;
    step 2 error-registry.json:1997, core.json:2645; step 3 error-registry.json:1998 (reload abandons it; a save in flight is not resent), current/SYNC181.mdx:28 (desktop and mobile: close and reopen) */}

### If it happened at checkout {#at-checkout}

The order save took over 10 seconds, so checkout, saving and voiding wait until the banner clears by itself. If it stays, press **Reload the app**, then check the order before **Checkout** again.

{/* source: wrapped-error-handler-storage.ts:247-250 (10 s), :265-268 (clears on the next successful call), :318-322 (never retried: a retry invites a duplicate order); use-checkout-save.ts:50-52; use-storage-health.ts:20, :83-97; core.json:2049, :1101 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Not yet known. A stalled lookup changes nothing; a stalled save can still finish, or stop when you reload.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1995 (may still complete or fail later), :1998; current/SYNC181.mdx:22 (a lookup changes nothing) */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it comes back regularly, send the log to support.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1999 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time of the entry.
