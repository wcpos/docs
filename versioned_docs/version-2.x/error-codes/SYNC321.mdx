**Symptom:** **Store health → Logs** has an entry with this code: "Some data didn't sync this time." Its title says what didn't finish.

**Do this now:** keep selling and read the entry's title.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data until **changes waiting to send** in **Store health → Database** reads 0.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Open **Store health**, then **Logs**. Press **All**, type SYNC321 in **Search Logs** and [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open).
2. **"An order was cancelled but its tidy-up did not finish":** the cancellation went through and the leftover clears itself later.
3. **"A change was saved but its bookkeeping could not be released":** the change is queued for your store and the leftover clears itself later.
4. **Any other title:** some store changes didn't reach this device. Press **Go to Store health → Database** in the entry.
5. Settle anything listed at the top of **Database**. If an item still looks out of date, open **⋮** on its row and choose **Clear & re-download…**. Before you clear **Orders**, finish or sync every open order: one that was never synced exists only on this device.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing you entered. Until store updates download again, some items on this device can be out of date.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Press **Copy debug info** in **Logs** (**Share debug info** on phones and tablets) and send it to support.

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the entry's title.
