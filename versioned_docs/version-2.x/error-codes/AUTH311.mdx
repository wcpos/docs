**Symptom:** after you press **Connect**, red text under it reads "WCPOS API not found", with a **Learn more** link to this page. Or a pop-up reads "Your store isn't answering WCPOS's requests." with a **Help** button, and red text names what's missing.

**Do this now:** send whoever looks after your website a link to this page and the red text under **Connect**.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Copy down the red text under **Connect**. It says which part your store didn't show.
2. Send it, with a link to this page, to whoever looks after your website.
3. Once they've fixed it, press **Connect** again.
4. **If it connects:** press **Sign in with WordPress** and carry on.
5. **If the red text shows again:** your store still hides what WCPOS needs. Tell them again.

{/* source: step 1 use-api-discovery.ts:346-349, :367, :377; step 2 error-registry.json:430, :435; step 3 error-registry.json:431 (retry after-change), core.json:477; step 4 core.json:512; step 5 use-api-discovery.ts:440-443 (the same checks run on every Connect) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing on this device or on your store changed. WCPOS stopped before anything was saved.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

The WCPOS plugin is switched off, or something on your website hides its routes. Only whoever looks after your website can fix that.

{/* source: use-api-discovery.ts:332-337 (installed-but-stripped or absent), error-registry.json:434-435 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The app reads your store's REST API index (`/wp-json/`). It must list the `wcpos/v2` namespace and, under `authentication`, `wcpos` › `endpoints` › `authorization`. One of them is missing.

1. In WP Admin › **Plugins**, check WCPOS is installed and active.
2. Open `/wp-json/` and `/index.php?rest_route=/` in a browser. If neither shows JSON, something blocks the REST API.
3. If WCPOS is active but missing from that JSON, check firewall, proxy and CDN rules and plugins that disable or trim the REST API. See [Plugin Conflicts](../support/troubleshooting/plugin-conflicts.mdx#what-to-exclude).
4. If the routes are still missing, re-save permalinks (**Settings › Permalinks › Save**). Then the cashier presses **Connect** again.

{/* source: use-api-discovery.ts:303, :338-349, :360-378; error-registry.json:437, :438, :439, :440 (permalinks) */}

</details>
