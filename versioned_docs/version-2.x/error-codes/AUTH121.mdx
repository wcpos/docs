**Symptom:** after you press **Log in** and sign in, a red pop-up reads "The signed-in store account does not match the cashier on this till.", with a **Help** button. Then the till signs out.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/hooks/use-rest-http-client/use-session-login-flow.ts:113-122 (error, showToast), :127-132 (then signs out); core.json:2511 (summary as title), :2627 (second line), :491 (Log in: session-hold-banner/index.tsx:139); packages/core/src/contexts/merchant-toast.ts:50-77; packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:866-891 (Help) */}

**Do this now:** sign in again with your own account, not the one that was used.

{/* source: core.json:2627; error-registry.json:1614-1615. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1609 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Wait for the Connect screen. The till signs you out by itself.
2. Tap your own name. If it shows **Sign in again**, press **Re-authenticate** and sign in with your own WordPress account.
3. Press **Open POS** and carry on.
4. **If a second pop-up reads "Sign out failed":** tap your avatar, press **Sign out**, then sign in as yourself.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The other account's sign-in was not kept, and everything saved on this till stays on it.

{/* source: use-session-login-flow.ts:144-148 (returns before the tokens are saved); app-state/index.tsx:166-168 (only the pointer is cleared); dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1609 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

On a shared till, hand over with **Hand the till to** so each cashier signs in as themselves. See [Hand the till to another cashier](../getting-started/connect.mdx#switching-users).

{/* source: error-registry.json:1616; getting-started/connect.mdx:102-109 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

The till checks that the account the sign-in window returned is the cashier it asked for, so no order is filed under the wrong cashier. Nothing on your store needs to change. Each cashier needs their own WordPress account.

</details>
