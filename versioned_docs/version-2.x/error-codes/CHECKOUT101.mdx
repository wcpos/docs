**Symptom:** at checkout, a red pop-up reads **Checkout failed**, or "Checkout did not finish, and the cart is still safe to retry.", with a **Help** button.

**Do this now:** read the pop-up's second line, then try checkout again from the same cart.

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Read the pop-up's second line. It says what stopped checkout, such as a payment that didn't go through.
2. Check the cart. It is unchanged.
3. Press **Checkout** again, then **Take** on the payment screen.
4. **If it fails the same way:** [open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open) in **Store health → Logs**. It says whether your store refused the order.
5. **If your store refused one product:** remove it, finish the sale, and ask a manager to check that product.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. The cart is exactly as you left it, so you can try checkout again.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If every checkout fails, the problem is on your store. Tell whoever looks after your website the time it failed.

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

A repeated failure with a 500-class status in the till's log is a problem on the store.

1. In WP Admin, open **WooCommerce → Status → Logs** and look for a PHP error at the time of the failure.
2. If the log names one product, look into that product.

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time of the sale.
