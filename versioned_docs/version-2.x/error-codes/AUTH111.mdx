**Symptom:** after you press **Sign in with WordPress**, **Re-authenticate** or **Log in**, the login window on your store doesn't accept your username and password. That window answers by itself, so WCPOS doesn't show the code AUTH111.

{/* source: core.json:512, :494, :491; add-user-button.tsx:103, wp-user.tsx:161, session-hold-banner/index.tsx:85, :139; getting-started/connect.mdx:29 (a login window opens on your store);
    never raised: packages/core/src/screens/main/hooks/use-rest-http-client/use-session-login-flow.ts:201-202 ("the login page handles a wrong password itself — so never AUTH111"), auth-error-handler.test.tsx:114-118 */}

**Do this now:** type your username and password again in the login window, checking for typing mistakes.

{/* source: core.json:2626, error-registry.json:1591. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1586 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. In the login window, type your WordPress username and password again.
2. Still refused? Sign in to your website in a browser with the same username and password.
3. **If that fails too:** reset the password on your website, or ask whoever looks after your website to reset it.
4. **If it works there but not in WCPOS:** a login-protection or security plugin can block it. Send whoever looks after your website a link to this page.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:1591, connect.mdx:29; step 2 error-registry.json:1592; step 3 error-registry.json:1589, :1592; step 4 error-registry.json:1593 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing on this device or on your store changed. Anything not yet sent goes once you're signed in.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1586; getting-started/connect.mdx:132 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If the same username and password work on your website but keep failing in WCPOS, ask whoever looks after your website to check its login-protection and security plugins.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1593 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

WCPOS signs in through a login window on your store. A login-protection or security plugin can turn that sign-in away.

1. Sign in to WP Admin with the cashier's username and password. Reset the password there if it fails.
2. Check the login-protection and security plugins' settings. See [Plugin Conflicts](../support/troubleshooting/plugin-conflicts.mdx#security-and-login-plugins).
3. Check the cashier's role has **Use the POS**. See [Staff Permissions](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx#change-a-role).

{/* source: connect.mdx:29; error-registry.json:1592-1593; step 3 settings/wp-admin/access.mdx:42, :163 */}

</details>
