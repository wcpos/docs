**Symptom:** after **Take** with a gateway, a red pop-up shows the gateway's own message, or "The gateway did not accept this payment.", with a **Help** button.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/use-tender-flow.ts:1179-1187 (the gateway's message as title), :821-827; core.json:2470 (summary title when none), :2707; Help: packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:880 */}

**Do this now:** read the gateway's message, correct the details and press **Take** again, or take another payment method.

{/* source: core.json:2586; error-registry.json:711-712 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Read the gateway's message in the pop-up. It says what the gateway refused.
2. Correct the details, such as the email address or the option chosen, and press **Take** again.
3. **If it keeps refusing:** tell a manager. Take a different payment method once they've checked that nothing was charged (see [Is anything lost?](#your-data)).
4. **If you pressed Cancel invoice:** the invoice still stands. Check the order before taking another payment.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:711; step 2 error-registry.json:712, core.json:2707; step 3 error-registry.json:713, current page At the till;
    step 4 core.json:2836, :445, use-tender-flow.ts:1521-1527, packages/core/src/screens/main/orders/invoice-actions.tsx:39-45 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

The POS recorded no payment, and the order still owes its balance. If the gateway hit an error after it had started, money may have been taken: the store adds a note to the order asking you to check with the payment provider. Check that before you take another payment.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:706; docsBody :709; the order note after a mid-run gateway error, main's PAYMENT212 Your data (#1019 base), step 3 of the admin list below */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If the gateway refuses every payment, its settings need checking. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: error-registry.json:713 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

A gateway refuses by answering with its own message, by hitting an error, or by trying to send the customer to another site, which the POS can't follow. A message about one field shows on the payment form, with no code.

1. Check the gateway's settings on the WooCommerce payments page, including its credentials.
2. In **WooCommerce → Status → Logs**, look at source `woocommerce-pos` for a gateway error or a redirect. Many gateways keep their own log there too.
3. Check the order's notes. If the gateway hit an error after it started, ask your payment provider whether money was taken.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/payments/submit-gateway-payment.ts:132-143; fields_invalid use-tender-flow.ts:1171-1173; step 1 error-registry.json:713; steps 2-3 current page On the store and Your data (plugin side, not settled at 6b2a2cb4f: see CHECK.md) */}

</details>
