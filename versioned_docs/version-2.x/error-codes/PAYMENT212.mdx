## What this means {#what-this-means}

The payment gateway refused the details the POS sent it, or ended without taking the payment. Nothing was charged, nothing was sent to the customer, and the order still owes its balance.

The gateway's own message is on the log row, and is normally shown on the toast at the same time.

## Your data {#your-data}

No money moved. The order is unchanged and its balance is still owed.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

**At the till** — do this before the customer leaves:

1. Read the gateway's message on the toast or the log row.
2. Correct the details (the email address, the option chosen) and send again.
3. Or take a different payment method.

**On the store** — for the site administrator:

1. If the gateway keeps refusing, check its settings on the WooCommerce payments page.
