## What this means {#what-this-means}

The payment gateway the cashier chose on the tender screen refused the details the POS sent it, or ended without taking the payment. A gateway refuses in one of three ways: a notice from its own checks (an email address it will not accept, an option it needs), an error while it ran, or an attempt to send the customer to another site, which the POS does not allow.

Nothing was charged, nothing was sent to the customer, and the order still owes its balance. The gateway's own message is on the toast at the till and on the log row.

## Your data {#your-data}

No money moved. The order is unchanged, with its previous payment method put back, and its balance is still owed.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

**At the till** — do this before the customer leaves:

1. Read the gateway's message on the toast or the log row.
2. Correct the details (the email address, the option chosen) and send again.
3. If the gateway keeps refusing, take a different payment method and tell the store owner.

**On the store** — for the site administrator:

1. Check the gateway's settings on the WooCommerce payments page; a gateway that refuses every attempt is usually misconfigured or missing credentials.
2. The gateway's log, under *WooCommerce > Status > Logs*, carries its own reason.
