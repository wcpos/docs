**Symptom:** a banner reads **Local database unavailable — scanning, checkout, saving and voiding are blocked until the app is reloaded.**, with **Reload the app** and **View status**. Blocked actions show a red pop-up starting **Local database unavailable**.

{/* source: banner packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/components/storage-outage-banner.tsx:44-66, core.json:2048, :2049, :2051; shown on products (pos/products/index.tsx:620) and checkout (checkout.tsx:36, checkout-column.tsx:19, tender-checkout.tsx:31); raised by latchDegradedStorage, packages/database/src/plugins/wrapped-error-handler-storage.ts:430-455 (log only, no pop-up);
    blocked action packages/core/src/screens/main/hooks/use-storage-health.ts:83-97, core.json:1171 (title), :2643 (second line), Help logger/index.ts:873-891. The banner names no code */}

**Do this now:** press **Reload the app**, then check your last sale saved.

{/* source: core.json:2643; storage-outage-banner.tsx:34-42, :55-56; packages/core/src/utils/reload-app.ts:11-27 */}

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It can hold sales that haven't reached your store.

:::

{/* source: registry dataSafety data-at-risk, error-registry.json:1946; the app's own line, core.json:2168 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Press **Reload the app**. On the web, reload the tab rather than close it.
2. **If a pop-up says "This build can't reload itself":** close WCPOS completely and open it again.
3. Check your last sale or change saved. If it's missing, enter it again.

{/* source: step 1 storage-outage-banner.tsx:55-56, reload-app.ts:11-27 (web reloads the page), error-registry.json:1951, current/SYNC161.mdx:25 (reload rather than close); only a reload recovers, use-storage-health.ts:51-55;
    step 2 storage-outage-banner.tsx:34-42, core.json:2050; step 3 core.json:2643, error-registry.json:1952 */}

### In a private window {#private-window}

Closing a private or incognito window deletes its unsent sales. Keep it open until **Database** shows 0 **changes waiting to send**, no panel and no unsaved open order ([Save to store](../pos/cart/open-orders.mdx#sync-order)). Then use a normal window, without storage-cleaning extensions.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1953; current/SYNC161.mdx:27; core.json:1901, :38 (Save to store); panels support/store-health.mdx#needs-attention */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

A change from the seconds before storage stopped can be missing. Everything else stays unless you clear this device's data.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1949, :1952; dataSafety data-at-risk :1946, core.json:2168 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it comes back, send the log to support with what the device was doing: asleep, switching tabs or low on memory.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1954 */}

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time the banner appeared.

{/* source: escalation support-with-export, error-registry.json:1947 */}
