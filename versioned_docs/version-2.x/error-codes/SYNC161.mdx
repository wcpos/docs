**Symptom:** a banner reads **Local database unavailable — scanning, checkout, saving and voiding are blocked until the app is reloaded.**, with **Reload the app** and **View status**. Blocked actions show a red pop-up starting **Local database unavailable**.

**Do this now:** press **Reload the app**, then check your last sale saved.

:::warning

Don't clear this device's data. It can hold sales that haven't reached your store.

:::

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Press **Reload the app**. On the web, reload the tab rather than close it.
2. **If a pop-up says "This build can't reload itself":** close WCPOS completely and open it again.
3. Check your last sale or change saved. If it's missing, enter it again.

### In a private window {#private-window}

Closing a private or incognito window deletes its unsent sales. Keep it open until **Database** shows 0 **changes waiting to send**, no panel and no unsaved open order ([Save to store](../pos/cart/open-orders.mdx#sync-order)). Then use a normal window, without storage-cleaning extensions.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

A change from the seconds before storage stopped can be missing. Everything else stays unless you clear this device's data.

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If it comes back, send the log to support with what the device was doing: asleep, switching tabs or low on memory.

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

[Send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support) with the time the banner appeared.
