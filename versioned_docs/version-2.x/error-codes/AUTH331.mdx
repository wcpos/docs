**Symptom:** the till shows **Update the WCPOS plugin on your WordPress site.** with **Check again** and **Logout**. On the Connect screen, a store reads "Update the WooCommerce POS plugin on this site to continue", or red text under **Connect** reads "Please update your WCPOS plugin".

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/upgrade-required.tsx:26-52, core.json:298, :300, :693, :688 (Learn more); packages/core/src/screens/auth/components/site.tsx:90-97, core.json:472;
    packages/core/src/screens/auth/hooks/use-api-discovery.ts:313-329, core.json:742, packages/core/src/screens/auth/components/url-input.tsx:69-86 */}

**Do this now:** ask whoever looks after your website to update the WCPOS plugin, then press **Check again**.

{/* source: core.json:2629, :299; error-registry.json:1652 (escalation site-admin), :1654. No warning box: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1651 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Send whoever looks after your website a link to this page.
2. Once they have, press **Check again**. The till opens the POS once your store's plugin is new enough. No need to log out or retype the address.
3. **If the red text was under Connect:** press **Connect** again.
4. **If your store can't be updated yet:** contact support with the app version and your store's WCPOS version. They tell you which app version matches.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:1656; step 2 packages/core/src/hooks/use-plugin-compat-check.ts:51-115 (live re-check; ok opens the POS), packages/core/src/screens/main/compat-gate.tsx:47-53, error-registry.json:1658;
    step 3 use-api-discovery.ts:303-329 (the check runs on every Connect), core.json:477; step 4 error-registry.json:1659 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Nothing on this till or your store changed. **Logout** only signs you out, and this device's data stays.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:1651; compat-gate.tsx:36-45; packages/core/src/contexts/app-state/index.tsx:166-168 (logout clears the open-store pointer only) */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If WCPOS Pro is installed, it must be updated to a matching version too.

{/* source: error-registry.json:1656-1657 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

This app needs WCPOS 2.0.0 or newer.

1. In WP Admin, open **Plugins** and update WCPOS to the latest version. Update WCPOS Pro to a matching version at the same time.
2. If no update is offered, check **Dashboard → Updates**. For Pro, an unauthorized updater blocks updates: see [LICENSE301](LICENSE301.mdx).
3. Then the cashier presses **Check again**.

{/* source: packages/core/src/utils/wcpos-plugin-version.ts:15; error-registry.json:1656-1658; core.json:299 */}

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Contact support with the app version and your store's WCPOS version. Logs can't be opened from this screen.
