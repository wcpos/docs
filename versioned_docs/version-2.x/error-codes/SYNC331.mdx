## What this means {#what-this-means}

This device's copy of a record doesn't match your store's. Nothing you entered is affected.

In **Store health → Database**, use **Clear & re-download** on that list to replace this device's copy with the store's. Until then, the record may show an out-of-date price or stock. Check first that nothing is waiting to send — see Troubleshoot.

## Your data {#your-data}

No sales or unsent changes are affected. This device's copy of the record may show an out-of-date price or stock until it is replaced. If this keeps happening, use **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and contact WCPOS support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

1. Keep selling. Open **Store health → Database**: the record is named at the top — **"… can't download from your store"** for a record whose copy differs, or **"… must be removed from this device"** for one your store has deleted.
2. Before selling that item, check its price and stock against the shelf or with your site administrator (see [On the store](#on-the-store)).
3. Before clearing anything, check in **Store health → Database** that **changes waiting to send** is 0 and that no **"… changes never reached your server"** or **"… changes were held"** panel is showing — those changes exist only on this device, and clearing their list can lose them. Wait for the count to reach 0, and settle any listed change first ([SYNC201](/error-codes/SYNC201), [SYNC221](/error-codes/SYNC221)).
   - Before clearing **Orders**, also save every open cart on this device with **Save to Server** (or finish it) — a cart never saved to your store exists only on this device and is not counted in any of these checks, so clearing **Orders** deletes it.
   - If a cart cannot be saved, skip the **Orders** row and contact support.
4. Then open the **⋮** menu on the record's list (for example **Products**) and choose **Clear & re-download…** — the device drops its copy and fetches the store's, and a deleted record is removed. The entry can stay listed until the next background check; judge by whether the price and stock now match.
   - If the callout then names a record in a different list, repeat these steps — checks first — for that list.
5. If the till keeps showing a wrong price or stock after a re-download, use **Copy debug info** in [**Store health → Logs**](#log-entry) and contact support.

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. When the cashier checks a named record's price and stock with you, compare it with WP Admin.
2. If the same records keep coming back after a re-download, the cause is usually a bulk change that bypassed WooCommerce's save hooks — a CSV import, a migration plugin, WP-CLI or direct SQL. Prefer editing through WooCommerce, which keeps the till's checks accurate; WCPOS rebuilds its integrity data by itself once the same difference keeps repeating.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.
