## What this means {#what-this-means}

This record on the device does not match your store and needs local repair.

Nothing you entered is waiting to be sent. The device's copy of a record your store owns has drifted from the store's copy; repair it according to the status shown in the log.

## Your data {#your-data}

No sales or unsent changes are affected. The device's copy of the record may show an out-of-date price or stock until it is repaired. If this persists, export diagnostics and contact WCPOS support.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

1. Keep selling. Open **Store health → Database**: the record is named at the top — **"… can't download from your store"** for a record whose copy differs, or **"… must be removed from this device"** for one your store has deleted.
2. Before selling that item, check its price and stock against the shelf or with whoever manages the store (they can compare it with WP Admin).
3. Before clearing anything, check in **Store health → Database** that **changes waiting to send** is 0 and that no **"… changes never reached your server"** or **"… changes were held"** panel is showing — those changes exist only on this device, and clearing their collection can lose them. Wait for the count to reach 0, and settle any listed change first ([SYNC201](/error-codes/SYNC201), [SYNC221](/error-codes/SYNC221)). Then open the **⋮** menu on the record's collection row (for example **Products**) and choose **Clear & re-download…** — the device drops its copy and fetches the store's, and a deleted record is removed. The entry can stay listed until the next background check; judge by whether the price and stock now match. If the callout then names a record in a different collection, repeat this step — checks first — for that collection.
4. **Site admin:** if the same records keep coming back after a re-download, the cause is usually a bulk change that bypassed WooCommerce's save hooks — a CSV import, a migration plugin, WP-CLI or direct SQL. Prefer editing through WooCommerce, which keeps the till's checks accurate; WCPOS rebuilds its integrity data by itself once the same difference keeps repeating.
5. If the till keeps showing a wrong price or stock after a re-download, use **Copy debug info** on the **Logs** tab and contact support.
