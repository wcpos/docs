**Symptom:** **Store health → Database** shows **1 record needs attention**: "*name* can't download from your store", or "*name* must be removed from this device" for one your store deleted.

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/health/attention-panel.tsx:42-64; core.json:2174, :2178, :2177; raise site apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:394-410 (sync.record, so it reaches the panel: logs-logic.ts:200-244) */}

**Do this now:** check that item's price and stock against the shelf before you sell it.

{/* source: error-registry.json:318 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Keep selling. **View log ›** in the notice opens this code's entry.
2. In **Database**, check **changes waiting to send** reads 0 and no "never reached your server" or "held" panel shows. Those changes exist only here.
3. Before clearing **Orders**, finish each open cart or press **Save to store** in its order details. If one won't save, leave **Orders** alone.
4. On the record's row, such as **Products**, open **⋮**, choose **Clear & re-download…**, then press **Clear & re-download**.
5. If the notice then names another list, repeat steps 2 to 4 there.

### Check it worked {#check}

Price and stock now match your store. The notice can stay until the next background check.

{/* source: error-registry.json:320; apps/main/lib/sync-log-observer.ts:411-421 (apply.escalation-cleared after a complete integrity sweep) */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

Nothing you entered. Until it's replaced, the record can show an out-of-date price or stock.

{/* source: core.json:2453; error-registry.json:315; database.tsx:309-310 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

Something is changing your store's records without the usual save. Tell whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: error-registry.json:321 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Compare the named record in WP Admin with what the till shows.
2. If the same records come back after a re-download, look for bulk changes that skip WooCommerce's save hooks: a CSV import, a migration plugin, WP-CLI or direct SQL.
3. Edit through WooCommerce instead. WCPOS rebuilds its own check data once the same difference keeps repeating.

{/* source: error-registry.json:321; packages/sync-core/src/hybridChangeSignal.ts:10-25 (tier 2 catches hook-bypassing writes); configChangeSignal.ts:29 */}

</details>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

If the price or stock is still wrong after a re-download, [send the log to support](../support/logs.mdx#for-support).
