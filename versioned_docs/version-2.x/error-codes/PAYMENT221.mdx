**Symptom:** after **Cancel payments**, a red pop-up reads "A payment could not be voided, so that money is still held." with a **Help** button.

{/* source: core.json:2745 (packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/cancel-payment-view.tsx:84), :2472 (summary title: no own title, packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/use-tender-flow.ts:1679-1693, :1710-1717); Help: packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:880 */}

**Do this now:** tell the customer, before they leave, which payments were reversed and which are still held.

{/* source: current page At the till step 2; core.json:2588 */}

:::warning

The payment has been taken. Don't charge the customer again.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety money-moved, error-registry.json:747; the app's own line, core.json:2166 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. [Open the entry](../support/logs.mdx#open) in **Store health → Logs** with **Errors** on. It names the payments still held.
2. To try the void again, press **Cancel payment**, then **Cancel payments**.
3. **If a payment is still held:** refund it from the order, or ask a manager to refund it in WooCommerce.
4. Don't treat the sale as reversed until every named payment is voided or refunded.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:2760, use-tender-flow.ts:1689 (context.paymentId), error-registry.json:752, severity error :744; step 2 core.json:2760, :2736, :2745, error-registry.json:753; step 3 core.json:2588, :2760, error-registry.json:753;
    step 4 error-registry.json:754 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

The sale record isn't lost, but the named payments are still held on the customer's card or counted in the till, until they're returned by hand.

{/* source: docsBody error-registry.json:750; current page Your data */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If voids keep failing, refund instead and tell whoever looks after your website. Your store answered with a refusal; this isn't a dropped connection.

{/* source: error-registry.json:753; current page What this means */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Open the order in **WooCommerce → Orders** and refund the named payments through your normal refund process.
2. A card authorization that's never captured falls off the card by itself, but the bank sets when. Check before telling the customer it has gone.

{/* source: error-registry.json:753; current page On the store (step 2: bank side, not settled at 6b2a2cb4f, see CHECK.md) */}

</details>
