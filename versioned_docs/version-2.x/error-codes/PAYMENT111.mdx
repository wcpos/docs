**Symptom:** after **Take**, a pop-up reads "The store recorded this payment, but the till could not save its own copy." or "Your store has closed this sale — this till couldn't save its copy."

{/* source: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/use-tender-flow.ts:714-756 (land, notMirrored), called at :963-969, :1189-1195, :1227-1235 and Try again :794-844; core.json:2465 (with :2581 and Help), :2756 (with :2757, no Help: showErrorCode false); :2707 */}

**Do this now:** don't take the payment again; carry on from the balance the payment screen shows.

{/* source: error-registry.json:602-603 */}

:::warning

The payment has been taken. Don't charge the customer again.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety money-moved, error-registry.json:597; the app's own line, core.json:2166 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Carry on from the balance on the payment screen: it already counts this payment. Take whatever is still owed; if nothing is, the sale completes.
2. Don't close the payment screen to "reopen" the order. With a payment on it, closing opens **Cancel payment**, which gives money back.
3. **If the till still looks out of date after the sale:** in **Store health → Database**, open **⋮** on the **Orders** row and press **Check for changes now**.

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. Your store has the payment and the sale is recorded. Only the till's copy is behind, and it asks your store for a fresh copy straight away.

{/* source: error-registry.json:600; core.json:2581; one refresh: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/commands/land-answer.ts:51, :61. Offline exception: see CHECK.md */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

The till can't save to its own storage. Tell whoever looks after your website which device.

{/* source: error-registry.json:606 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. Nothing needs repairing on your store: it accepted the payment. **WooCommerce → Orders** shows the order's current status.
2. On the device that keeps failing, check **storage used** in **Store health → Database**, and the device's free space. Its local database could be full or failing.

{/* source: error-registry.json:606; current page On the store; support/store-health.mdx:39 */}

</details>
