## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your store took this payment and it is on the order there. What failed afterwards was smaller: the till could not update its own copy of the order to match.

The money is safe and the sale is recorded. Until the till catches up, its own screen may still show a balance that your store no longer considers owing — which is why the toast tells the cashier not to take the payment again.

WCPOS asks the store for a fresh copy of the order the moment this happens, so in most cases the till corrects itself within a few seconds.

## Your data {#your-data}

Money moved and your store has it. Never take this payment again — only the till's local copy of the order is behind, and the store's record is the one that counts.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

**At the till** — do this before the customer leaves:

1. Do not take the payment again.
2. Give the till a few seconds; the balance usually corrects itself once the refreshed order arrives.
3. If it does not, close and reopen the order.
4. Carry on with the sale from the corrected balance.

**On the store** — for the site administrator:

1. Open the order in WooCommerce and confirm the payment is on the order's payment list — it will be.
2. If tills repeatedly fail to save their own copy, check Store health → Storage on that device: the local database may be full or degraded.
