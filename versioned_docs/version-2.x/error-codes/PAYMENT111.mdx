## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your store took this payment and it is on the order there. What failed afterwards was smaller: the till could not update its own copy of the order to match.

The money is safe and the sale is recorded. The payment pane already counts this payment in what is left to pay, but other views on the till — the cart and order lists — may lag until the till catches up, which is why the message tells the cashier not to take the payment again.

WCPOS asks the store for a fresh copy of the order the moment this happens, so in most cases the till corrects itself within a few seconds.

## Your data {#your-data}

Money moved and your store has it. Never take this payment again — only the till's local copy of the order is behind, and the store's record is the one that counts.

## Troubleshoot {#troubleshoot}

### At the till {#at-the-till}

:::warning Do not take it again
Do not take the payment again.
:::

Do this before the customer leaves.

1. Carry on from the balance the payment pane shows — it already counts this payment. Take whatever is still owed; if nothing is, the sale completes.
2. Do not use the close button (**X**) to "reopen" the order. With a payment on it, closing opens **Cancel payment**, which gives money back rather than refreshing anything.
3. If the till still looks out of date after the sale, open **Store health → Database**, open the **⋮** menu on the **Orders** row and choose **Check for changes now**.

**Store health → Database**, where these steps send you:

3. **Performance** — how often this till checks your store, and how your server is coping. See [Performance](../support/store-health.mdx#performance).

6. **records on this device** — every product, order, customer and other record stored here.
7. **storage used** — what the POS takes up on this device, cached images included.
8. **changes waiting to send** — sales and edits on their way to your store. Red above 0. After an outage, it's back to 0 once everything has reached your store. It leaves out open orders you haven't synced ([Save to store](../pos/cart/open-orders.mdx#sync-order)) and changes listed in a panel ([When something needs you](../support/store-health.mdx#needs-attention)): those haven't reached your store either.
9. **The table** — each kind of record, on this device and in your store. See [What's on this device](../support/store-health.mdx#coverage).

### On the store {#on-the-store}

For the site administrator.

1. Nothing to repair on the store: it accepted the payment. The order in **WooCommerce → Orders** shows its current status.
2. If a till repeatedly fails to save its own copy, check **Store health → Database** on that device — the **storage used** figure, and whether the device is low on free space. Its local database may be full or failing.

## Find the entry in the log {#log-entry}

Every code is logged in **Store health → Logs**. Press **Errors**, then tap the entry with this code to open it under its row. The picture shows an example entry, AUTH201; on your till the entry shows this page's code.

<JourneyShot manifest={logs} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[17, 28], [549, -9], [-54, -97], [131, 0], [101, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="An error entry opened under its row in Store health → Logs: what the server said, what to do, Open record, the Help link, the event code with Copy, and the record, operation and request">

1. **Errors** — the filter that's on.
2. **The entry** — the code in **Status** is its error code.
3. **What happened and what to do** — what your store said, when it said something, then one line on what to do. When it matters, a warning comes first, such as **Payment has been taken — don't charge again.** or **Don't clear this device's data**.
4. **Help** — opens the code's page in these docs.
5. **Open record** — opens the order, product or customer the entry is about, when this till has it, your store has WCPOS Pro, and you're allowed to edit it.
6. **Copy** — copies the whole entry for support. The rows under it are for support; you don't need to read them.

</JourneyShot>
