Current versions no longer show this code.

**Symptom:** PAYMENT401 means "The payment terminal did not finish pairing with WCPOS." The card reader isn't ready to take payments on this till.

{/* source: core.json:2475; error-registry.json:818. Not raised anywhere in the app at 6b2a2cb4f (only generated tables), so the page doesn't say where it shows: see CHECK.md */}

**Do this now:** wake the card reader, check its connection, and pair it again.

{/* source: core.json:2591; error-registry.json:814 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. Check the basics: the reader is charged, on the right network or Bluetooth, and not still paired to another till.
2. Keep the reader awake and on the same network as the till, then restart pairing from its settings.
3. For a Bluetooth reader, press **Connect a reader** in **Settings → Card readers** on the till.
4. **If it still won't pair:** contact your payment provider.

{/* source: step 1 error-registry.json:822; step 2 error-registry.json:821; step 3 error-registry.json:821, core.json:1524, :1522, hardware/card-readers/index.mdx:90; step 4 escalation payment-provider, error-registry.json:817 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

No. No order or product data is affected, and nothing was charged.

{/* source: dataSafety no-impact, error-registry.json:816; current page Your data */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If the reader won't stay paired, contact your payment provider. Tell whoever looks after your website too.

{/* source: escalation payment-provider, error-registry.json:817; current page Your data */}
