**Symptom:** a red pop-up reads "WCPOS could not confirm whether the terminal charged the payment." with a **Help** button.

{/* source: core.json:2468 (summary title, no own title: packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/use-tender-flow.ts:1333-1346, :828-836, :1679-1693; packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/payments/use-record-manual-payment.ts:183-193); Help: packages/utils/src/logger/index.ts:880; packages/core/src/contexts/merchant-toast.ts:50-77 */}

**Do this now:** don't run the card again until you've checked the card reader's screen and your payment provider's dashboard.

{/* source: core.json:2584; error-registry.json:666-667 */}

:::warning

The result couldn't be confirmed. Check before trying again.

:::

{/* source: dataSafety outcome-unknown, error-registry.json:661; the app's own line, core.json:2167 */}

## Fix it at the till {#at-the-till}

1. If the payment screen says "The store has not answered the last payment.", press **Try again**. It won't be taken twice.
2. Check the card reader's own screen, then your payment provider's dashboard. The provider's record decides whether the charge went through.
3. Ask a manager to check the order on your store. The payment could already be recorded there.
4. **If the dashboard shows no charge:** take the payment again.
5. **If it shows pending or unclear:** contact your payment provider before you do anything else.

{/* source: step 1 core.json:447, :462, packages/core/src/screens/main/pos/checkout/tender/tender-pane.tsx:81-97, use-tender-flow.ts:794-798 (retryCommand resends the open commands); step 2 error-registry.json:667; step 3 error-registry.json:668;
    step 4 error-registry.json:669; step 5 error-registry.json:670 */}

## Is anything lost? {#your-data}

The sale isn't lost, but the money is unconfirmed: the card could be charged without the till hearing back. Trust the provider's dashboard.

{/* source: dataSafety outcome-unknown, error-registry.json:661; error-registry.json:667; retryPolicy never, :660 */}

## If it keeps happening {#keeps-happening}

If many card payments end this way, tell your payment provider and whoever looks after your website.

{/* source: escalation payment-provider, error-registry.json:662; logSources :672-675 */}

<details>
<summary>For whoever looks after your website</summary>

1. In WP Admin, check **WooCommerce → Orders**: a payment that succeeded could already be recorded on the order. A cashier with WP Admin access can check this too.
2. Compare it with the provider's dashboard before the till takes the payment again.

{/* source: error-registry.json:668-669; current page On the store */}

</details>
