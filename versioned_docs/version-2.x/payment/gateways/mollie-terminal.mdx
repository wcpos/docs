:::info Pro Feature
Mollie Terminal needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) 2.0.0 or newer.
:::

Mollie Terminal sends the sale from the till to a [Mollie](https://www.mollie.com/products/point-of-sale) card terminal. The customer pays on the terminal, and the sale finishes on the till by itself. It takes **euro** only, and works in the POS only: it is never offered on your web store's checkout.

Setting it up for the first time? Go to [Set it up](#setup).

## Take a payment {#take-payment}

A manager [sets it up](#setup) once, with the terminal on your store's live Mollie account. [Open the register](../../pos/register/index.mdx#open) before you start.

:::warning
With **Open and close the register** switched on, a closed register can't record the payment, and a card you've already charged would have to be refunded.
:::

1. Press **Checkout**, then tap the **Mollie Terminal** tile on the tender pane.
2. Check the terminal on the tender pane: **Terminal:** and its name. To use another one, tap **Change** and pick it. **Change** isn't there when the store has only one terminal enabled, or locks the choice.
3. Press **Send … to Mollie Terminal**. The amount goes to the terminal.
4. The customer taps, inserts or swipes their card on the terminal. The till shows **On the terminal** with the steps **Sent**, **On terminal**, **Approved** and **Captured**.
5. When Mollie confirms the payment, the sale is paid and the receipt is ready, as for any [checkout](../../pos/checkout/index.mdx#paid).

{/* SHOT owed: the tender pane with the Mollie Terminal tile, Terminal:, Change and Send. Needs Mollie Terminal enabled and a terminal on dev-pro (none on 2026-11-13); never press Send. */}

Each till remembers which terminal it last used. See [On a Card Reader](../../pos/checkout/taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).

A tip the customer adds on the terminal is recorded on the order as a **Tip** line. See [Tips](../../pos/checkout/tips.mdx).

### Cancel a payment on the terminal {#cancel}

While the till waits, press **Cancel on terminal**. The till shows **Cancelling — waiting for the terminal to confirm**.

### Payment cancelled or declined {#cancelled}

After **Payment cancelled**, a decline or **Timed out — payment cancelled**, the payment didn't go through: press **Try again** to send the payment again, or **Choose another way** to pay with another method.

### The terminal hasn't confirmed {#not-confirmed}

Once the customer has started paying, **Cancel on terminal** can't stop it: cancel on the terminal itself. If the till still can't get an answer, it offers **Release this payment**.

:::warning Before you take the money again
Released doesn't mean cancelled. If the terminal approved the payment, charging again charges the customer twice.
:::

1. Look at the terminal.
2. If it shows the payment approved, **don't charge again** and don't press **Release this payment**. Stay on the screen: the sale finishes when Mollie confirms it.
3. If it shows no approval, if you can't tell, or if an approved sale still hasn't finished after a few minutes, ask a manager to check the payment in the Mollie Dashboard.
   - **The manager finds it paid:** **don't charge again** and don't press **Release this payment**. Stay on the screen, or see [The customer already paid](#already-paid).
   - **The manager confirms it didn't go through:** press **Release this payment**. The till shows **Payment released**, and the order can be paid another way. The store keeps checking the terminal.

## On the Legacy tab {#legacy-tab}

You can also take the payment on the checkout's [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx), the order's payment page. Open the **Legacy** tab and choose **Mollie Terminal**. The panel you see depends on whether a manager has turned on **QR code payments**.

**If the panel shows Terminal and QR code**, stay on **Terminal** and press **Start Terminal Payment**. Press **Cancel Terminal Payment** to stop. To show a code instead, see [Pay by QR code](#qr-code).

**If the panel shows a Send button:**

1. Pick the terminal if the panel asks.
2. Press **Send … to** followed by the terminal's name. The panel shows the same steps as the tile.
3. To stop, press **Cancel on terminal**, as in [Cancel a payment](#cancel). After it stops, **Try again** sends it again.

Either way, the POS records the payment as it does on the tile.

## Pay by QR code {#qr-code}

In the Netherlands and Belgium, the customer can pay by scanning an iDEAL or Bancontact QR code with their banking app instead of using the terminal. A manager turns the methods on first, under **QR code payments** in [the settings](#settings).

1. At checkout, open the [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx) and choose **Mollie Terminal**.
2. Switch the payment panel from **Terminal** to **QR code**.
3. Pick **iDEAL** or **Bancontact** if both are on, and press **Show QR code**.
4. The customer scans it and pays on their phone. The sale finishes as with the terminal.

Bancontact takes at most €1,500.00 per QR payment; use the terminal for more.

## Refunds {#refunds}

Refund a Mollie Terminal sale from the POS and choose **Refund to Mollie Terminal**, or refund it from the WooCommerce order screen. The money goes back through Mollie, in full or in part. See [Refunds](../../pos/refunds.mdx#destination).

A Mollie refund starts as pending. WCPOS Pro checks it with Mollie every ten minutes, then marks it refunded or failed and adds a note to the order.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

### The customer already paid: don't charge again {#already-paid}

**The customer paid but the till is still waiting.** **Don't charge again.** Stay on the screen: the sale finishes when Mollie confirms the payment. If it doesn't, or the till offers **Release this payment**, follow [The terminal hasn't confirmed](#not-confirmed).

**"An earlier Mollie payment for this sale was paid".** A payment for this sale already went through at Mollie but isn't on the order yet. **Don't charge again.** Wait a few minutes and reload. If the message says the payment doesn't match the order, a manager checks it in the Mollie Dashboard first.

### Other problems {#other-problems}

**"No terminals set up for Mollie Terminal".** The store has no active terminal to offer. A manager checks that **Mode** is **Live** with a live key, and that the terminal is active on that Mollie account.

**"In use by order #…".** That terminal is busy with another sale. Pick another terminal, or finish or cancel the other sale first.

**"Needs a connection".** The till is offline. Mollie Terminal works only online: take the payment another way, or wait for the connection.

**"Timed out — payment cancelled".** Nobody paid in time, so the payment was cancelled. Check the terminal is switched on and online, then press **Try again**, or **Choose another way**.

**A sale in another currency is refused.** Mollie Terminal takes euro only.

**Still stuck?** A manager can look for Mollie messages under `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Status > Logs`. Report a problem with the extension on [GitHub](https://github.com/wcpos/mollie-terminal-for-woocommerce/issues). For your Mollie account or the terminal itself, contact Mollie support, or see Mollie's [terminal setup guide](https://docs.mollie.com/docs/setting-up-terminal).

## Set it up {#setup}

An administrator does this once per store, in WP Admin. You need WCPOS Pro 2.0.0 or newer, a live Mollie account with its **Point of Sale** payment method turned on, and euro as your store currency.

:::note Before you start
In the Mollie Dashboard, turn on the **Point of Sale** payment method for your profile, and buy your terminals there. Mollie sells terminals in the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, France and the UK, and may ask to verify your account first. Terminals exist only on a **live** account, so you need your live API key.
:::

### Install the extension {#install}

Open `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions` and install **Mollie Terminal**, or upload the release zip from [GitHub](https://github.com/wcpos/mollie-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) under `Plugins > Add Plugin`, then **Upload Plugin**. Use the zip attached to the release, not the source code archive. See [Extensions](../../extensions/index.mdx#installing-an-extension).

Without WCPOS Pro 2.0.0 or newer, the extension does nothing and shows a notice in WP Admin.

### Add your API key {#api-key}

1. In the Mollie Dashboard, go to **Developers > API keys** and copy the **Live API key**. Keys come in pairs, test and live, for each website profile. The test key can't drive a terminal.
2. Go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` and open **Mollie Terminal**.
3. Set **Mode** to **Live**. It starts on **Test**.
4. Paste the live key into **Mollie API Key**. Already use the Mollie Payments for WooCommerce plugin? Set **API key source** to **Reuse the official Mollie plugin's key** instead, and leave the key box empty.
5. Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the page.

### Choose your terminals {#terminals}

Once the key is saved, **Default terminal** lists the active terminals on your Mollie account, each by name and ID. Pick the one a till starts on.

To keep some terminals away from the till, pick the ones it may use under **Enabled terminals**. Leave it empty to allow every active terminal. Click **Save changes**.

There is no webhook to set up in the Mollie Dashboard and no Profile ID to paste: the extension sends its webhook address with every payment, and lists terminals across your whole account.

### Turn it on for the POS {#enable}

Go to `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`, find **Mollie Terminal** in the **Gateways** table and switch **Enabled** on. That screen saves as you go; there's no Save button. This is the only on/off switch: you don't need to enable it in WooCommerce's own payment list. See [Enabling a Gateway](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

1. **Mollie Terminal** — the extension's row in the **Gateways** table.
2. **Enabled** — switch it on. The till shows the **Mollie Terminal** tile once it reloads.

### Check it worked {#check}

1. Back on the Mollie Terminal settings page, scroll to **Mollie Terminal Diagnostics**. **Active environment** reads `live`, **API key source** ends in **configured**, and there's no yellow test-mode notice.
2. **Default terminal** is a list with your terminals in it, not an empty text box.
3. On a till, reload the POS and press **Checkout**. The **Mollie Terminal** tile is among the ways to pay, with **Terminal:** and your terminal's name.
4. Take a small sale on the terminal, then refund it.

<Anchor id="no-terminal-list" />

**Default terminal is still a text box?** No key is saved, or your site couldn't reach Mollie: check the key and save again. **The list has no terminals?** Check **Mode** is **Live** with your live key (terminals don't exist on a test account), and that the terminal is active in Mollie.

### The settings {#settings}

All of these are on `WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Mollie Terminal`.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The name on the tile at the till. Starts as "Mollie Terminal". |
| **Description** | Text shown with the method on the order-pay page. |
| **Mode** | **Test** or **Live**. Starts on **Test**, which shows a warning: the test key can't drive a terminal. Use **Live** to take payments. |
| **API key source** | **Use the key entered below**, or **Reuse the official Mollie plugin's key** (the test or live key matching **Mode**). If the Mollie plugin has no key set, the key below is used. |
| **Mollie API Key** | Your key for the selected mode. Ignored while the key source reuses the Mollie plugin's key. |
| **Default terminal** | The terminal a till starts on. Listed live from your Mollie account; inactive terminals are hidden, because Mollie can't reactivate them. |
| **Enabled terminals** | Only these can be chosen at the till. Empty means every active terminal. The default terminal is always offered, so change it too when you retire a terminal. Not shown while the terminal list can't be loaded; your saved choice stays. |
| **QR code payments** | **iDEAL** and **Bancontact**: lets the cashier show a QR code on screen instead. Each must be active on your Mollie profile. iDEAL QR also works in **Test** mode, so you can try it without a terminal; Bancontact QR needs **Live**. See [Pay by QR code](#qr-code). |
| **Lock terminal selection** | The till always uses the default terminal, and the cashier can't change it. Needs a **Default terminal**. |
| **Checkout debug logs** | Shows **Show logs**, **Copy** and **Clear** on the payment panel. Leave it off unless support asks: payments are always logged under `WooCommerce > Status > Logs`. |

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={4} offsets={[[178, 0], [178, 0], [178, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, WooCommerce Payments, Mollie Terminal: the QR code payments list open with iDEAL and Bancontact, and Lock terminal selection and Checkout debug logs both unticked">

1. **QR code payments** — click the box and pick **iDEAL**, **Bancontact** or both to offer a QR code at the till.
2. **Lock terminal selection** — tick it to keep every till on the default terminal.
3. **Checkout debug logs** — leave it unticked unless support asks.

### How the store keeps up with Mollie {#how-it-works}

For administrators; there is nothing here to set up.

- While a payment waits, the till checks with Mollie every two seconds. Mollie also tells your store directly, which is usually sooner. An order changes only after the store has checked the update with Mollie.
- If a refund request gets no answer, the store looks for a refund Mollie already made before it asks again.
- A QR code payment left open at Mollie is cancelled after five minutes with nobody paying, when the order is paid another way or cancelled, or when the payment page is closed. A check every ten minutes cancels anything still open after ten minutes on an unpaid order.
