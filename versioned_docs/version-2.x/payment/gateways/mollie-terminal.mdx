:::info Pro Feature
Mollie Terminal needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) 2.0.0 or newer. Without it the extension does nothing and shows a notice in WP Admin.
:::

Mollie Terminal sends the sale from the till to a [Mollie](https://www.mollie.com/products/point-of-sale) card terminal. The customer pays on the terminal, and the sale finishes on the till by itself. It works in the POS only: it is never offered on your web store's checkout.

:::note Before you start
- In the Mollie Web App, turn on the **Point of Sale** payment method for your profile, and buy your terminals there. Mollie sells terminals in the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, France and the UK, and may ask to verify your account first.
- Your store's currency must be **euro**. A sale in any other currency is refused.
- Terminals exist only on a **live** Mollie account, so you need your live API key.
:::

## Set it up {#setup}

A manager does this once, in WP Admin.

1. **Install the extension.** Open `POS Settings > Extensions` and install **Mollie Terminal**, or upload the release zip from [GitHub](https://github.com/wcpos/mollie-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) under `Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin`. Use the zip attached to the release, not the source code archive. See [Extensions](../../extensions/index.mdx#installing-an-extension).
2. **Add your key.** Go to `WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Mollie Terminal`. Set **Mode** to **Live**, then either paste your live key into **Mollie API Key**, or set **API key source** to reuse the key from the Mollie Payments for WooCommerce plugin. Save.
3. **Choose your terminals.** Once the key is saved, the terminals on your Mollie account are listed. Pick a **Default terminal**. If some terminals shouldn't be used at the till, list the ones that should under **Enabled terminals**. Save.
4. **Switch it on for the POS.** Go to `POS > Settings > Checkout` and enable **Mollie Terminal**. This is the only on/off switch. See [Enabling a Gateway](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

There is no webhook to set up in the Mollie dashboard and no Profile ID to paste: the extension sends its webhook address with every payment, and lists terminals across your whole account.


### The settings {#settings}

All of these are on `WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Mollie Terminal`.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The name on the tile at the till. Starts as "Mollie Terminal". |
| **Description** | Text shown with the method on the order-pay page. |
| **Mode** | **Test** or **Live**. Starts on **Test**, which shows a warning: the test key can't drive a terminal. Use **Live** to take payments. |
| **API key source** | **Use the key entered below**, or **Reuse the official Mollie plugin's key** (the test or live key matching **Mode**). If the Mollie plugin has no key set, the key below is used. |
| **Mollie API Key** | Your key for the selected mode. Ignored while the key source reuses the Mollie plugin's key. |
| **Default terminal** | The terminal a till starts on. Listed live from your Mollie account; inactive terminals are hidden, because Mollie can't reactivate them. |
| **Enabled terminals** | Only these can be chosen at the till. Empty means every active terminal. The default terminal is always offered, so change it too when you retire a terminal. Not shown while the terminal list can't be loaded; your saved choice stays. |
| **QR code payments** | **iDEAL** and **Bancontact**: lets the cashier show a QR code on screen instead. See [Pay by QR code](#qr-code). |
| **Lock terminal selection** | The till always uses the default terminal, and the cashier can't change it. Needs a **Default terminal**. |
| **Checkout debug logs** | Shows **Show logs**, **Copy** and **Clear** on the payment panel. Leave it off unless support asks: payments are always logged under `WooCommerce > Status > Logs`. |

## Take a payment {#take-payment}

1. Press **Checkout**, then tap the **Mollie Terminal** tile on the tender pane.
2. Check the terminal on the tender pane: **Terminal:** and its name. To use another one, tap **Change** and pick it. **Change** isn't there when the store has only one terminal enabled, or locks the choice.
3. Press **Send … to Mollie Terminal**. The amount goes to the terminal.
4. The customer taps, inserts or swipes their card on the terminal. The till shows **On the terminal** with the steps **Sent**, **On terminal**, **Approved** and **Captured**.
5. When Mollie confirms the payment, the sale is paid and the receipt is ready, as for any [checkout](../../pos/checkout/index.mdx#paid).

The till checks with Mollie every two seconds while it waits, and Mollie's own message to your store usually confirms the payment even sooner. Each till remembers which terminal it last used. See [On a Card Reader](../../pos/checkout/taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).


A tip the customer adds on the terminal is recorded on the order as a **Tip** line. See [Tips](../../pos/checkout/tips.mdx).

### Cancel a payment on the terminal {#cancel}

While the till waits, press **Cancel on terminal**. The till shows **Cancelling — waiting for the terminal to confirm**, then **Payment cancelled**.

Once the customer has started paying, Mollie may no longer let the till cancel. Cancel on the terminal itself. If the till still can't get an answer, **Release this payment** frees the order to be paid another way; check the terminal before you take the money again.

After a cancel, a decline or a time-out, press **Try again** to send the payment again, or **Choose another way** to pay with another method.

### On the Legacy tab {#legacy-tab}

Mollie Terminal is also offered on the checkout's [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx), the order's payment page. Choose **Mollie Terminal**, pick the terminal if the panel asks, and press **Send … to** followed by the terminal's name. The panel shows the same steps, with **Cancel on terminal** while it waits and **Try again** after it stops. The POS records the payment as it does on the tile. With **QR code payments** on, the extension's own panel shows instead; see [Pay by QR code](#qr-code).

## Pay by QR code {#qr-code}

In the Netherlands and Belgium, the customer can pay by scanning an iDEAL or Bancontact QR code with their banking app instead of using the terminal.

1. A manager turns on the methods under **QR code payments** in the settings. Each must also be active on your Mollie profile.
2. At checkout, open the [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx) and choose **Mollie Terminal**.
3. Switch the payment panel from **Terminal** to **QR code**, pick **iDEAL** or **Bancontact** if both are on, and press **Show QR code**.
4. The customer scans it and pays. The sale finishes as with the terminal.

Bancontact takes at most €1,500.00 per QR payment; use the terminal for more. iDEAL QR also works in **Test** mode, so you can try it without a terminal. Bancontact QR needs **Live**.

## Refunds {#refunds}

Refund a Mollie Terminal sale from the POS and choose **Refund to Mollie Terminal**, or from the WooCommerce order screen. The money goes back through Mollie, in full or in part. See [Refunds](../../orders/refunds.mdx#refund-destination).

A Mollie refund starts as pending. WCPOS Pro asks Mollie again every ten minutes and marks it refunded or failed, with a note on the order. If a refund request gets no answer, the extension looks for the refund Mollie already made before creating another, so the customer is never refunded twice.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**"No terminals set up for Mollie Terminal".** The store has no active terminal to offer. In the settings, check **Mode** is **Live** with a live key, and the terminal is active on that Mollie account.

**"In use by order #…".** That terminal is busy with another sale. Pick another terminal, or finish or cancel the other sale first.

**"Needs a connection".** The till is offline. Mollie Terminal works only online: take the payment another way, or wait for the connection.

**No terminals in the Default terminal list.** Terminals don't exist on a test account: set **Mode** to **Live** with your live key. Check the terminal is active in Mollie, and that your site can reach Mollie.

**"Timed out — payment cancelled".** Nobody paid in time, so the payment was cancelled. Press **Try again**, or **Choose another way**.

**A sale in another currency is refused.** Mollie Terminal takes euro only.

**"An earlier Mollie payment for this sale was paid".** A payment for this sale already went through at Mollie but isn't on the order yet. Don't charge again: wait a few minutes and reload. If the message says the payment doesn't match the order, check it in the Mollie dashboard first.

**The customer paid but the till is still waiting.** Stay on the screen. The order follows what Mollie says, so a payment Mollie confirms isn't lost. If it doesn't finish, look under `WooCommerce > Status > Logs` for Mollie messages.

<details>
<summary>How open payments are cleaned up</summary>

On the Legacy tab with **QR code payments** on, the extension's own payment panel starts the payment. A payment there that nobody finishes can stay open at Mollie, so the extension cancels it when:

- nobody pays within five minutes;
- the order is paid another way, or cancelled in WooCommerce;
- the payment page is closed mid-payment;
- a check every ten minutes finds a payment still open after ten minutes on an unpaid order.

A payment that has already reached the terminal can't be cancelled from the store; cancel it on the terminal. Mollie's state always wins: every update is checked with Mollie before the order changes.

</details>
