# SumUp Terminal Gateway

The SumUp Terminal gateway enables you to accept card payments using SumUp card readers directly within WCPOS. This gateway provides seamless integration with SumUp's payment processing system for in-person transactions.

## Installation {#installation}

<Steps>
  <StepCard number="1" title="Install SumUp Terminal for WooCommerce">

Install from `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`, or download the latest **plugin zip asset** (not the GitHub source-code zip or tarball) from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/sumup-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) and upload it via `Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin`.

  </StepCard>
  <StepCard number="2" title="Configure SumUp Settings">

1. Navigate to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments`
2. Find **SumUp Terminal** in the payment methods list
3. Click on **SumUp Terminal** to access settings
4. Enter your **SumUp API Key** (available from your SumUp merchant dashboard)
5. Save the settings

:::note
You do not need to enable the SumUp Terminal gateway in WooCommerce settings. It will be enabled specifically for the POS in a later step.
:::

  </StepCard>
  <StepCard number="3" title="Pair Your SumUp Terminal">

1. On the same settings page, locate the **Pair Reader** section
2. On your SumUp device, navigate to the pairing screen to display the pairing code
3. Enter the pairing code displayed on your SumUp device
4. Click **"Pair Reader"** to establish the connection
5. Wait for confirmation that the reader has been successfully paired

:::warning Important
The reader must be successfully paired before you can process payments. Ensure the pairing process is completed before proceeding.
:::

  </StepCard>
  <StepCard number="4" title="Enable in WCPOS" isLast>

1. Go to `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`
2. Find the **SumUp Terminal** gateway in the list
3. Enable the gateway for use in the POS
4. Save your settings

  </StepCard>
</Steps>

## Usage {#usage}

### Processing Payments {#processing-payments}

1. **Add Items**: Add products to your cart in the POS and press **Checkout**
2. **Select Gateway**: Tap the SumUp Terminal tile on the tender pane. A version of the extension without a tender-pane tile is on the **Legacy** tab instead
3. **Start Payment**: Press **Send** to send the amount to your SumUp device
4. **Customer Payment**: Customer completes payment on the SumUp terminal
5. **Automatic Completion**: The sale completes when the payment is approved

### Payment Controls {#payment-controls}

On the tender pane, the till shows each step of the payment and offers **Cancel on terminal**. On the **Legacy** tab, the payment page shows a **Terminal** picker with **Send to terminal**, **Cancel** and **Try again**.

Older versions of the extension use their own payment page on the Legacy tab, with these options:

- **Start New Payment**: Initiate a payment request on the connected terminal
- **Cancel Payment**: Cancel a payment currently in process
- **Payment Status**: Monitor the current status of the payment process

### Order Management {#order-management}

- **Automatic Updates**: Orders are automatically marked as completed upon successful payment
- **Payment Tracking**: All payment details are recorded in the order notes
- **Receipt Generation**: Standard POS receipts are generated after successful payments

## Hardware Compatibility {#hardware-compatibility}

The gateway's **POS connection** setting chooses how the POS reaches the reader:

- **Solo over the cloud**: the store drives a SumUp Solo over the internet. Works from every till: web, desktop and the iOS and Android apps.
- **Bluetooth reader**: the WCPOS iOS or Android app drives a **Solo Lite**, **Solo** or **Air** over Bluetooth with SumUp's own app SDK. The web and desktop tills cannot reach a Bluetooth reader. Enter your SumUp **Affiliate Key** in the gateway settings for this mode. Then, in the app, go to **Settings → Card readers** and tap **Open SumUp reader settings**, which opens SumUp's own screen to sign in and choose the reader. With this connection, the SumUp tile is hidden on a web or desktop till.

## Offline Payments {#offline-payments}

With **Offline card payments** turned on, a **Solo Lite** or **Solo** connected by Bluetooth to the WCPOS iOS or Android app can take card payments while the POS has no internet connection. The reader approves the card on the spot. The app stores the payment and uploads it to SumUp when the connection returns, and the order's payment is then confirmed against SumUp's record.

:::warning You carry the risk
An offline payment is approved by the reader without asking the card's bank. When it uploads, the bank can still decline it (for example for insufficient funds or a blocked card), and **your business carries that loss**. The longer a payment waits to upload, the higher that risk, so reconnect as soon as you can. Offline payments that have not uploaded are lost if the app is deleted or the device is lost or reset.
:::

### Requirements {#offline-payments-requirements}

<RequirementsList>
  <RequirementItem label="SumUp opt-in">SumUp enables offline payments per merchant account. Ask SumUp to enable them for yours.</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Reader">Solo Lite (firmware 2.2.1.25 or later) or Solo (firmware 3.3.31.0 or later), connected by Bluetooth. The Air and Tap to Pay cannot take offline payments.</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Cards">Visa and Mastercard.</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Region">Europe (SumUp processes offline payments through Worldpay).</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="First payment">At least one successful online card payment on the account first.</RequirementItem>
</RequirementsList>

### Turning It On {#offline-payments-turning-on}

1. Go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` and open **SumUp Terminal**.
2. Set **POS connection** to **Bluetooth reader**.
3. Tick **Offline card payments** and save.
4. In the app, connect the Solo Lite or Solo while online. Open the app with a connection before an outage: a till started without one cannot take SumUp offline payments until it has been online once.

When the till is offline, the SumUp tile stays available for that reader, and the checkout notes that the payment can still be declined when it uploads. SumUp limits how long a device can stay offline and how much it can take before it must upload. When a limit is reached, SumUp refuses further offline payments until the device reconnects.

Older versions of the extension have no tender-pane tile: the gateway is on the **Legacy** tab and works with an internet-connected Solo only.

## Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

### Common Issues {#common-issues}

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Reader won't pair">

  - For **Solo over the cloud**, pair a SumUp Solo; Bluetooth readers connect from the WCPOS app instead
  - Verify your SumUp Solo device is connected to WiFi and online
  - Check that the device is in pairing mode and pairing code is entered correctly
  - Confirm your SumUp account has API access enabled
  - Ensure stable internet connection during pairing

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="Payment not processing">

  - Verify the reader is properly paired
  - Check that your SumUp account is active and in good standing
  - Ensure the device has sufficient battery and connectivity
  - Confirm API key is correct and active

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="Order not completing">

  - Check internet connection stability
  - Verify webhook settings in your SumUp account
  - Ensure the payment was successful on the SumUp terminal
  - Check WordPress error logs for API communication issues

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

### API Rate Limits {#api-rate-limits}

The gateway is optimized to reduce API calls and avoid rate limiting:
- Payment status is checked efficiently
- Unnecessary API requests are minimized
- Automatic retry logic handles temporary failures
