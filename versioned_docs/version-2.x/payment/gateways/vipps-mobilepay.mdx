# Vipps MobilePay Gateway

:::info Pro Feature
Taking Vipps MobilePay at the till needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx). Without Pro, the gateway still works on your online store's checkout.
:::

The customer pays with the Vipps or MobilePay app on their own phone. They scan a QR code on your screen, or approve a payment request you send to their phone. No card reader is needed. The gateway runs on the checkout's **Legacy** tab, on your store's own payment page.

Your store needs the gateway set up first. See [Set up Vipps MobilePay](#setup).

## Take a Vipps payment {#take-payment}

Press **Checkout**, open the **Legacy** tab and choose **Vipps MobilePay**. Its controls open under it.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Legacy** tab — gateways that run on their own payment page. See [The Legacy Tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx).
2. **Vipps MobilePay** — the gateway. Your store may give it another name; see [Settings](#settings).
3. **Phone number (optional)** — only for **Send to Phone**. Leave it empty for a QR code.
4. **Generate QR Code** — shows a code for the customer to scan. See [With a QR code](#qr).
5. **Send to Phone** — sends the payment request to the number you typed. It lights up once there's a number. See [Send it to their phone](#send-to-phone).
6. **Remaining** — what the customer approves in the app: the whole order.
7. **Process Payment** — you don't need it for Vipps. The sale completes by itself when the customer approves.

Vipps takes the whole order in one payment. If part of the order is already paid, the Legacy tab is greyed out: [cancel that payment](../../pos/checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx) first, or take the rest another way.

### With a QR code {#qr}

1. Press **Generate QR Code**. A QR code appears under the buttons, with **Waiting for payment...** under it.
2. Turn the screen to the customer. They scan the code with the Vipps or MobilePay app and approve the payment.
3. The till checks with Vipps every two seconds. When the customer approves, the page says **Payment successful!** and the receipt screen opens by itself, the same as for any [paid sale](../../pos/checkout/index.mdx#paid).

While the till waits, both buttons are greyed out and a **Cancel Payment** button shows under them. If nobody approves within five minutes, the page says **Payment expired. Please try again.**

### Send it to their phone {#send-to-phone}

Type the customer's phone number and press **Send to Phone**. A payment request appears in their Vipps app, and they approve it there. The page waits, and finishes, as for a QR code.

- **Norwegian numbers only.** Type eight digits, or put `47`, `+47` or `0047` in front. An eight-digit number is always read as Norwegian, so for a MobilePay customer in Denmark or Finland, use the QR code.
- **The first time, you may be asked to press again.** If Vipps hasn't turned on [Direct Push](#enabling-direct-push) for your account, the page says **Direct push not available for your account. Click Send to Phone again.** Press **Send to Phone** again: a new window opens on the Vipps page for that number, and the request goes to the customer from there. From then on, every **Send to Phone** works this way.
- **After the customer pays** in that way, the extra window says **Payment complete** and closes by itself. If the browser keeps it open, close it and go back to the checkout.

### Stop a payment {#cancel}

Press **Cancel Payment** while the till is waiting. The payment is cancelled with Vipps and the page says **Payment cancelled.** Nothing was taken. Press **Generate QR Code** or **Send to Phone** to try again, or pay another way: pick another gateway on the Legacy tab, or go back to the **Payments** tab.

The same **Payment cancelled.** shows when the customer declines in their app.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Payment expired. Please try again.** Five minutes passed with no answer from the customer. Press **Generate QR Code** or **Send to Phone** again.

**Payment cancelled.** and you didn't press Cancel. The customer declined in their app. Check with them, then try again.

**Invalid phone number. Use a Norwegian number, e.g. 4741234567.** The number isn't a Norwegian mobile number. Check it, or use the QR code.

**Popup blocked. Please allow popups and try again.** The browser stopped the Vipps window from opening. Allow pop-ups for your store's site in the browser settings, then press **Send to Phone** again.

**Failed to create Vipps payment.** Your store couldn't start the payment with Vipps. Check the keys and **Test Mode** in [Settings](#settings), that your site runs on HTTPS, and that the order is in a currency Vipps takes (see [Where it works](#supported-markets)). The details are in `WooCommerce > Status > Logs`, under the source `wcpos-vipps`.

**Vipps payment was accepted, but … Please try again.** The customer approved, but the order wasn't completed. Before you take payment again, check the order's notes in WooCommerce, so the customer doesn't pay twice.

**Send to Phone opens a window instead of going straight to the app.** Your account is on **Web Redirect**. That works, with one extra step for the customer. To go straight to the app, see [Turn on Direct Push](#enabling-direct-push).

## Set up Vipps MobilePay {#setup}

:::note Before you start
You need a Vipps MobilePay merchant account with the ePayment API, its four API keys, [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx), and a site on HTTPS, which the Vipps API requires.
:::

1. **Install** WCPOS Vipps MobilePay from `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`. See [Installing an Extension](../../extensions/index.mdx#installing-an-extension). Or download the **plugin zip asset** (not the source-code zip) from the [releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/wcpos-vipps/releases) and upload it in `Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin`.
2. **Add your keys.** Open `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > WCPOS Vipps MobilePay` and enter the **Merchant Serial Number**, **Client ID**, **Client Secret** and **Subscription Key** from the [Vipps MobilePay portal](https://portal.vippsmobilepay.com/). Save.
3. **Turn it on for the POS** in `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`. See [Enabling a Gateway](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway). It then appears on the checkout's Legacy tab.

:::tip Already using the official Vipps plugin?
If [Checkout with Vipps MobilePay](https://wordpress.org/plugins/woo-vipps/) is set up on your site, WCPOS Vipps MobilePay copies its keys, test keys included, the first time it loads. Check them and save.
:::

The **Enable/Disable** box on the WooCommerce screen is for your online store's checkout only. The POS uses the gateway once it's turned on in Checkout Settings, whether that box is ticked or not.

### Settings {#settings}

These are on `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > WCPOS Vipps MobilePay`.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable/Disable** | Offers Vipps MobilePay on your online store's checkout. Not needed for the POS. Off to start with. |
| **Title** | The gateway's name on the Legacy tab and online. Starts as `Vipps MobilePay`. Checkout Settings can give the POS its own title. |
| **Description** | The line under the name. Starts as "Pay with Vipps MobilePay via QR code or push notification." |
| **Merchant Serial Number**, **Client ID**, **Client Secret**, **Subscription Key** | Your live keys from the Vipps MobilePay portal. The gateway isn't offered until all four are filled in. |
| **Auto Capture** | On to start with: the money is taken as soon as the customer approves. Turn it off only if you capture reserved payments yourself, outside WCPOS. |
| **Phone payment method** | How **Send to Phone** reaches the customer. **Direct Push** (the start) goes straight to their Vipps app. **Web Redirect** opens the Vipps page in a new window. Switches to **Web Redirect** by itself when Vipps refuses Direct Push. |
| **Debug Log** | Writes each payment step to `WooCommerce > Status > Logs`, and adds a **Show Log** panel under the payment controls. Leave it off unless support asks. |
| **Test Mode** | Sends payments to Vipps' test system instead, using the four **Test** keys below it. Turn it off before you take real payments. |

### Turn on Direct Push {#enabling-direct-push}

With Direct Push, **Send to Phone** goes straight to the customer's Vipps app, with no extra window. Vipps has to allow it on your sales unit first:

1. Log in to the [Vipps MobilePay portal](https://portal.vippsmobilepay.com/).
2. Contact your Vipps key account manager, partner manager or customer service.
3. Tell them: "I need PUSH_MESSAGE enabled on my MSN for use with a POS integration".

When they confirm, set **Phone payment method** back to **Direct Push** and save. The gateway doesn't switch back by itself. While it's on **Web Redirect**, the setting's own description reminds you how to turn Direct Push on.

In **Test Mode**, a refused Direct Push uses the Vipps page for that one payment and leaves the setting as it is.

### Refunds {#refunds}

Refund the order from the POS and choose **Refund to Vipps MobilePay**, or refund it from the WooCommerce order screen. The money goes back to the customer through Vipps. See [Refunds](../../orders/refunds.mdx). If Vipps refuses, the refund isn't recorded and the message says **Vipps refund request failed.** Try again, or refund in cash.

## Where it works {#supported-markets}

| Country | App | Currency |
|---|---|---|
| Norway | Vipps | NOK |
| Denmark, Finland | MobilePay | DKK, EUR |

Your merchant account decides which countries and currencies you can take. **Send to Phone** works with Norwegian numbers only; the QR code works everywhere.

## Getting help {#getting-help}

- Account and keys: Vipps MobilePay customer service.
- The Vipps API: the [Vipps MobilePay developer docs](https://developer.vippsmobilepay.com/).
- A fault in the gateway: the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/wcpos/wcpos-vipps).
