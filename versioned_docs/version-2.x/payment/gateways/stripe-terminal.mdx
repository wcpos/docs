# Stripe Terminal Gateway

Stripe Terminal puts a Stripe card reader at your till. The till sends the amount to the reader, the customer taps, inserts or swipes their card on it, and the sale completes once Stripe captures the payment and nothing is left to pay. This page sets it up once, connects a reader on each till, and takes the first payment.

Just installed it? Start with [Set up in WP Admin](#setup): your Stripe keys, the readers you allow and the POS switch. Already set up? [Connect a reader on this till](#card-readers) or [take a payment](#processing-payments).

:::info Pro Feature
Stripe Terminal needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) 2.0.0 or newer. Without it the extension does nothing.
:::

## Pick your readers {#readers}

Stripe sells three kinds of reader. Which one a till can use depends on where the POS runs.

- **Smart reader** — works on every till: browser, desktop app, iPhone, iPad and Android. Stripe Reader S700 and S710, BBPOS WisePOS E. It sits on your network and your store sends it the payment.
- **Bluetooth reader** — the iOS and Android apps only. Stripe Reader M2, BBPOS WisePad 3. It pairs with the phone or tablet.
- **Tap to Pay** — coming to the iOS and Android apps in an app update. No reader: the customer taps their card on the phone itself.{/* pending: roadmap#113, front desk 2026-11-09: worded as coming in an app update until Paul confirms Apple's entitlement; restore the shipped wording then */}

A browser or the desktop app can't reach a Bluetooth reader or a phone's contactless chip, so those tills use a smart reader. Buy readers from the Terminal shop in your Stripe Dashboard.

:::tip Which Bluetooth reader
Stripe sells the **Stripe Reader M2** in the United States and the **BBPOS WisePad 3** outside it, in the UK, Europe, Canada, Australia and other countries. Check the Terminal shop for yours.
:::

## Set up in WP Admin {#setup}

An administrator does this once per store. Have your Stripe Dashboard login to hand.

1. **Install the extension.** Download the plugin zip, `stripe-terminal-for-woocommerce.zip`, from the release assets on [GitHub](https://github.com/wcpos/stripe-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) and upload it with `Plugins > Add Plugin`, then **Upload Plugin**. The GitHub "Source code" zip won't install.
2. **Open the gateway's settings.** Go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` and open **Stripe Terminal**.
3. **Paste your secret keys.** The page shows **How to get this key** under each key field. In short:
   - **Live Secret Key**: open the [API keys page](https://dashboard.stripe.com/apikeys) of your Stripe Dashboard in live mode. Under **Standard keys**, click **Reveal** next to **Secret key** and copy it. It starts with `sk_live_`.
   - **Test Secret Key**, to try it first: the same steps on the [test-mode API keys page](https://dashboard.stripe.com/test/apikeys). It starts with `sk_test_`.

   Copy the **Secret key**, not the **Publishable key**: the fields take only secret keys. Stripe shows a new key only once, so paste it straight away. Once saved, a key shows as dots with *(saved; leave blank to keep)*.
4. **Choose a Terminal location** for Bluetooth readers, and for Tap to Pay once your app has it. Create one in test mode and one in live mode in the Stripe Dashboard under **Terminal → Locations**, then pick them as **Terminal location (testing)** and **Terminal location (live)**.
5. **Tick the readers this store allows** under **POS connections**:
   - Smart readers on every till: tick **Smart readers**.
   - Bluetooth readers on the iOS and Android apps: tick **Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay** and untick **Smart readers**.

   With both ticked, every till uses smart readers. See [Smart readers, Bluetooth or both](#connections).
6. **Click Save changes**, then check the page. Under each key you pasted, the line reads **Stripe API key is valid.** Below the fields, the **WooCommerce POS checkout** row reads **Signing secret stored.**, and **Stripe Terminal Locations** lists your locations.
7. **Turn it on for the POS.** Go to `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout` and switch on **Enabled** in the **Stripe Terminal** row of the **Gateways** table. That screen saves as you go; there's no Save button. You don't need to enable it in WooCommerce's own payment list; see [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx).
8. **Check it worked.** Reload the POS on a till. Under **Settings → Card readers**, your smart readers are listed, and at **Checkout** the tender pane has a **Stripe Terminal** tile. Then run a [test sale](#testing).

{/* SHOT owed: WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Stripe Terminal on the 2.0 extension, numbered: Live Secret Key with its valid line, POS connections, Terminal location (live), the WooCommerce POS checkout row. Dev sites run the 0.0.x extension; tracked in the climb's reshoot.md */}

:::warning Choose a Terminal location before the first connection
Without one, a Bluetooth reader can't connect (nor Tap to Pay, once your app has it) and the till shows *Select a Terminal location in the Stripe Terminal settings before connecting a mobile reader.*
:::

<details>
<summary>Every setting on the gateway's page</summary>

- **Title** and **Description** — the name and line customers see. The till shows the title on the tile.
- **Live Secret Key** and **Test Secret Key** — the **Secret key** from the API keys page of your Stripe Dashboard, live or test mode; see [step 3](#setup).
- **Default terminal** — the smart reader a till pays with until it picks another.
- **Enabled terminals** — limits tills to the smart readers you select. Leave it empty to allow every reader on the account.
- **Lock terminal selection** — *Cashiers cannot change the terminal at POS checkout.* Needs a **Default terminal**.
- **POS connections** — which kinds of reader tills may use: *Smart readers (the store drives the reader over the network)* and *Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay (the POS app drives the reader)*. See [Smart readers, Bluetooth or both](#connections).
- **Terminal location (testing)** and **Terminal location (live)** — where Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay register when the app connects them.
- **Test Mode** — *Enable Test Mode*. A site without HTTPS stays in test mode: Stripe Terminal needs HTTPS for live payments.
- **Phone Orders (MOTO)** — *Enable phone order payments via Stripe Terminal*. See [Phone orders (MOTO)](#phone-orders-moto).
- **Reader Keep-Warm (Experimental)** — pings the reader so the first payment after a quiet spell isn't slow. Not recommended when several registers share one reader.

Below the fields, the page lists your **Stripe Terminal Locations** and the **WooCommerce POS checkout** row, which shows whether the webhook's signing secret is stored.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Use a restricted key instead</summary>

A restricted key gives the plugin only the access it needs. On the API keys page, click **Create restricted key** and name it, for example "WooCommerce POS". Set every **Terminal** permission to Write, **PaymentIntents** to Write and **Refunds** to Write, and set **Account**, **Charges** and **PaymentMethods** to Read. Click **Create key** and paste it into the key field. It starts with `rk_live_` or `rk_test_`.

A restricted key can't set up the webhook for you, so the **WooCommerce POS checkout** row reads *Signing secret missing*. To have it set up automatically, use the standard **Secret key**.

</details>

### Smart readers, Bluetooth or both {#connections}

**POS connections** is a store setting: it decides which kind of reader every till uses.

- **Smart readers** only: every till pays with a smart reader.
- **Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay** only: the iOS and Android apps pay with a Bluetooth reader or the phone. On a browser or desktop till, the Stripe tile is folded under **… not available right now**, with the line *Only the app's readers are allowed for this payment method. Allow smart readers on its WooCommerce settings page to pay from this till.*
- **Both**: tills use the smart readers.{/* pending: roadmap#523 — native tills offered both kinds once the app reads the second connection */} Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay stay unused until you untick **Smart readers**.

<details>
<summary>Changing reader types or upgrading</summary>

Unticking a kind changes only what tills pay with next. Every payment refunds through the connection it was taken on, smart reader or app, and Stripe's webhook keeps working. An upgrade from an older version keeps the saved connection, or smart readers if only a **Default terminal** was saved. A new install has both ticked, so the apps use smart readers until you untick **Smart readers**.

</details>

## Connect a reader on this till {#card-readers}

Every till keeps its own reader. In the POS, tap **Settings** at the bottom of the side menu, then **Card readers**.

### Smart readers {#smart-readers}

Register each smart reader in the Stripe Dashboard with the code the reader shows. Until you do, it isn't listed under **Default terminal**, **Enabled terminals** or on the till.

The **Smart readers** section has one row per reader the store allows: the reader's name with the gateway's beside it, the Terminal location under it, and **Online** or **Offline**. The till pays with the row marked **Selected**. To switch, tap **Use this reader** on another online reader; checkout then pays with that one.

<details>
<summary>Which reader is used automatically?</summary>

- **Nothing chosen yet**: the till uses the **Default terminal** while it's online and free.
- **Only one smart reader in the store**: no choice is needed. While it's online and not busy with another sale, the till pays with it.
- **Set by the store**: the **Default terminal** under **Lock terminal selection**. While it's online it's **Selected** on every till, and no row offers **Use this reader**.

</details>

### Bluetooth readers {#bluetooth-readers}

In the iOS or Android app:

1. Switch the reader on and keep it near the phone or tablet.
2. In **Settings → Card readers**, tap **Connect a reader**. A store with more than one reader gateway asks which: **Connect a reader with**, then **Stripe Terminal**.
3. The row reads *Looking for readers… Turn the reader on and keep it near this device.*, then *1 reader found. Is this yours?* or *3 readers found. Which one is yours?* Tap **Connect** beside yours.
4. **The first connection installs a reader software update.** The row reads **Installing reader software** with a progress bar, and *Keep the reader on and charged. The first update takes about 10 minutes.* Charge the reader above half before you start, and stay on the screen.
5. The row reads **Connected**, with the reader's battery, for example **Connected · 80%**.

Later connections take a few seconds, and the till reconnects to the same reader at the next sale. While no reader is connected, the **Bluetooth readers** section reads *No reader connected yet. Turn the reader on and keep it near this phone.*

### Tap to Pay {#tap-to-pay}

Tap to Pay is coming to the iOS and Android apps in an app update; this is how it works once your app has it.{/* pending: roadmap#113, front desk 2026-11-09: worded as coming in an app update until Paul confirms Apple's entitlement; restore the shipped wording then */} On a phone with the iOS or Android app, the **This phone** section has a **Tap to Pay on iPhone** or **Tap to Pay on Android** row. A shop manager or administrator taps **Set up Tap to Pay**. Anyone else sees **Not set up · ask an administrator**.

The first time, the phone asks you to accept the provider's terms: on an iPhone, Apple asks you to link an Apple ID and accept its Tap to Pay terms. Once set up, the row reads **Ready**. Tap to Pay needs a connection to take a payment.

<details>
<summary>What a reader row can say</summary>

Each reader row shows the reader's name, its serial number, the Terminal location and one line of state:

- **Connected · 80%** — ready to take payments, with the battery left. Tap to Pay reads **Ready**.
- **Not connected** — the till remembers this reader but isn't connected now. Tap **Connect**.
- **Connecting to …** — wait. The button reads **Connecting…**.
- **Installing reader software** — the update; see [Bluetooth readers](#bluetooth-readers), step 4.
- **No readers found. Turn the reader on and try again.** — tap **Try again**, or **Can't see your reader?** for the reader guide.
- **Could not connect: …** — Stripe's reason follows. Tap **Try again**, or **Help**.
- **Turn on Bluetooth to connect a reader.** — Bluetooth is off on this device.
- **Allow access to the reader in app settings.** — the app hasn't been given permission to use Bluetooth. Allow it in the phone's settings.

The **⋮** menu beside a row has **Forget**: the till stops reconnecting to that reader at the next sale. You can connect it again from this page. **Disconnect**, the button on a connected row, lets go of the reader for now. If a reader is stuck held by the app, the menu also offers **Release the reader**.

</details>

## Test before you go live {#testing}

Tick **Test Mode** on the gateway's settings page (`WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Stripe Terminal`) and save. Payments then go to Stripe's test account and no money moves. Run a test sale with the steps in [Take a payment](#processing-payments).

- **Smart readers**: a simulated reader registered on your Stripe test account is listed with your real ones, so you can run a sale without hardware.
- **Bluetooth readers** and real smart readers: pay with Stripe's physical test card, ordered from the Terminal shop in your Stripe Dashboard. The amount's last two digits decide the outcome: `.00` approves, `.05` declines, and `.02` asks for the PIN `1234`.

When you're done, untick **Test Mode** and save before you take a real payment.

## Take a payment {#processing-payments}

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, the till won't record a payment while the register is closed, and a card you've already taken would have to be refunded. See [Open the Register](../../pos/register/index.mdx).
:::

1. Build the cart and press **Checkout**.
2. Tap the **Stripe Terminal** tile on the tender pane.
3. Check the reader. A smart reader reads **Terminal: …**, with **Change** to pick another when the store allows it. A Bluetooth reader reads **Connected · 80%**, or **No reader connected** with a link to **Settings → Card readers**.
4. Press **Send … to Stripe Terminal**, then let the customer pay:
   - **Smart reader**: the till reads **Waiting for the customer on the terminal** while the customer taps, inserts or swipes on the reader.
   - **Bluetooth reader**: the till reads **Present card on the reader**.
   - **Tap to Pay**, once your app has it: the phone shows its own Tap to Pay screen, and the customer taps their card or phone on yours.

   The till ticks off **Sent**, **On terminal**, **Approved** and **Captured**.
5. With nothing left to pay, the receipt screen opens: print or email the receipt, then press **New sale**. See [See it paid](../../pos/checkout/index.mdx#paid). If part of the total is left, take the rest another way; see [Split Payments](../../pos/checkout/split-payments.mdx).

To stop, press **Cancel on terminal**. A declined card shows **Payment failed: …** with **Try again** and **Choose another way**. See [On a Card Reader](../../pos/checkout/taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).

The tile has no way to key in a card. For a phone order, see [Phone orders (MOTO)](#phone-orders-moto).

{/* SHOT: tender pane with Stripe tile — reshoot */}

Tips customers add on the reader land on the order as a **Tip** fee. The WisePad 3, WisePOS E, S700 and S710 can ask for a tip; the M2 can't. Turn on tipping for your readers in the Stripe Dashboard.

## Phone orders (MOTO) {#phone-orders-moto}

MOTO (mail order, telephone order) takes a card from a customer who isn't there: you key the card number, expiry date and security code into the reader's screen instead of tapping a card. Phone orders run only on the checkout's [**Legacy**](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx) tab, in Stripe's own panel. The **Stripe Terminal** tile on the **Payments** tab has no keyed card entry.

The **Phone Order** switch shows only when all three of these are done:

1. **Stripe turns on MOTO for your account.** It isn't on by default: ask [Stripe support](https://support.stripe.com/contact).
2. **Tick Phone Orders (MOTO).** Go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Stripe Terminal`, tick **Phone Orders (MOTO)** and save.
3. **Use an S700, S710 or WisePOS E smart reader.** The switch shows once one of these is connected in Stripe's panel. It never shows for a P400, M2, WisePad 3, Tap to Pay or a simulated reader.

While **Phone Orders (MOTO)** is ticked, Stripe's own panel replaces the usual one for every Stripe Terminal payment on the Legacy tab, not only phone orders. With it unticked, the Legacy tab shows a **Terminal** picker with **Send to terminal**, **Cancel** and **Try again**, recorded like a payment from the tender pane.

To take a phone order:

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} maxWidth={720} offsets={[[88, 0], [0, 0], [-30, 0]]} alt="The checkout's Legacy tab with Stripe Terminal chosen on the gateway's payment page, its Available Readers list starting below, and Process Payment">

1. **Legacy** — the tab that shows the store's own payment page inside the till.
2. **The store's payment page** — choose **Stripe Terminal** here, then scroll down to **Available Readers**, **Connect** and **Phone Order**.
3. **Process Payment** — the till's button under the page, as for any [Legacy](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx) payment. A phone order starts with **Pay with Terminal** on the page.

</JourneyShot>

1. Press **Checkout**, open the **Legacy** tab and choose **Stripe Terminal**.
2. Under **Available Readers**, press **Connect** on your reader.
3. Switch on **Phone Order**.
4. Press **Pay with Terminal**. The reader shows a card entry screen instead of asking for a tap.
5. Key in the card number, expiry date and security code on the reader.

:::tip
Stripe treats a phone order as a card-not-present payment, so in-person card pricing and its fraud liability shift don't apply.
:::

## If something goes wrong {#common-issues}

**The reader won't connect.** A smart reader must be on Wi-Fi or Ethernet, online in your Stripe Dashboard and registered there. A Bluetooth reader needs the iOS or Android app, Bluetooth on, and the app allowed to use Bluetooth.

**"Select a Terminal location…".** The gateway has no Terminal location for the mode it's in, test or live. See [Set up in WP Admin](#setup), step 4.

**The key line says *Invalid live API key…*.** The **Live Secret Key** field holds a test key, a publishable key or a typo. Live keys start with `sk_live_` or `rk_live_`. Copy the **Secret key** again; see [Set up in WP Admin](#setup), step 3.

**The key line says *Live key provided, but charges are not enabled for the account.*** Stripe can't take live payments on your account yet. Finish activating it in your Stripe Dashboard.

**The WooCommerce POS checkout row reads *Signing secret missing*.** Paste a secret key for the mode the gateway is in, test or live, and click **Save changes**. A restricted key can't set up the webhook; use the standard **Secret key**.

**Payments stay in test mode.** A site without HTTPS stays in test mode: Stripe Terminal needs HTTPS for live payments.

**The first connection is taking ages.** That's the reader's software update, about 10 minutes. Keep the reader on and charged, and stay on the screen. The next connection takes seconds.

**The Stripe tile says it's not available.** On a browser or desktop till, the store allows only the app's readers. See [Smart readers, Bluetooth or both](#connections). Offline, card readers wait for the connection; see [Offline Mode](../../getting-started/offline.mdx).

**Card readers says *Only the app's readers are allowed for this store.*** The same cause, on a browser or desktop till: allow **Smart readers** under **POS connections**, or use the iOS or Android app. See [Smart readers, Bluetooth or both](#connections).

**The card is declined.** Ask for another card or another way to pay. In test mode, check the amount's last two digits.

**No Phone Order switch.** Phone orders run on the **Legacy** tab only, not on the **Stripe Terminal** tile. Check that **Phone Orders (MOTO)** is ticked, that you're connected to an S700, S710 or WisePOS E, and that it isn't a simulated reader.

**A phone order fails.** Stripe hasn't turned on MOTO for your account yet, or the card details were keyed wrong.

**Still stuck?** Report a bug on [GitHub](https://github.com/wcpos/stripe-terminal-for-woocommerce/issues), check [Stripe's Terminal documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/terminal) for the reader, or contact Stripe support about your account.
