# Windcave Terminal Gateway

Windcave Terminal sends a sale from the till to a Windcave terminal. The customer pays on the terminal, and the till records the payment once Windcave confirms it.

This page starts at the till. Setting the gateway up is under [Set up Windcave Terminal](#setup).

:::info Pro Feature
Windcave Terminal needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) 2.0 or later, and version 1.0 or later of the extension.
:::

:::warning Not certified by Windcave yet
**Use it for test payments only**, with Windcave's **UAT** (test) credentials. Windcave approves every point-of-sale integration before it takes real payments, and hasn't approved this one yet. It also hasn't been run on a physical terminal: this page comes from Windcave's HIT documentation and the extension's code.
:::

## Take a card payment {#take-payment}

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, the till won't record a payment while the register is closed, and a card you've already taken would have to be refunded. See [Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) for the setting.
:::

Press **Checkout** in the cart. Windcave Terminal is a tile on the **Payments** tab, next to your other ways to pay.

1. Tap the **Windcave Terminal** tile.
2. Check the terminal named above the keypad: **Terminal:** and its Station ID. With more than one, press **Change** and tap the Station at your counter. The till remembers your choice for next time.
3. To take part of the balance on the card, type the amount. The rest stays due for another payment.
4. Press **Send … to Windcave Terminal**. The till shows **On the terminal** with four steps: **Sent**, **On terminal**, **Approved** and **Captured**.
5. The customer taps, inserts or swipes the card on the terminal. When the payment is captured and nothing is left to pay, the receipt opens. If some is left, take it with another tile.

**Details**, under the steps, opens a log of the payment, with the Station it went to. There's no tip, surcharge or cash-out on the terminal.

### When the terminal asks a question {#terminal-questions}

A terminal can stop and ask something, such as whether the customer's signature matches. In this version the **Payments** tab doesn't show those questions. The **Legacy** tab does: choose **Windcave Terminal** there and the payment page shows the question with the terminal's own buttons, such as **YES** and **NO**. See [The Legacy Tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx).

If your terminals ask for signatures, take Windcave payments on the **Legacy** tab. It takes the whole order in one payment.

### Cancel a payment {#cancel}

Press **Cancel on terminal** before the customer pays. This works only while the terminal itself is offering **CANCEL**. If it isn't, the till says *The terminal is not offering cancel right now. Cancel on the terminal.* Cancel it on the terminal.

A cancel is a request. If the card was already read, the payment can still go through: the till keeps checking and shows the result. Wait for it before you take payment another way.

## If a payment goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

:::warning Could not confirm the terminal result. Check Windcave before taking payment again.
The till asked again for a payment it had already sent, and Windcave's answer was unclear. The customer may have paid. Don't take the payment again until a manager has found the sale in the Windcave portal: it's listed under the order number.
:::

## Refund a Windcave payment {#refunds}

A refund goes back through Windcave to the Station that took the sale, for the full amount or part of it.

1. In the POS, open the order's refund. See [Refunds](../../pos/refunds.mdx).
2. Choose **Refund to Windcave Terminal** and press **Process Refund**.
3. Your store waits up to 20 seconds for Windcave's answer, then adds the result to the order notes.

A refund in WP Admin, with WooCommerce's **Refund … via Windcave Terminal** button, works the same way.

If a refund stays **pending**, check it in the Windcave portal before you refund again.

## Set up Windcave Terminal {#setup}

An administrator does this once per store in WP Admin, then picks a Station on each till. Your store reaches each terminal through Windcave over the internet, so there's nothing to pair, and any till can use any of your terminals.

### Requirements {#requirements}

You need HIT credentials from Windcave, a **HIT username** and a **HIT key**, and the **Station ID** of each terminal. Ask Windcave for **UAT** ones, and for a **Vendor ID** once they certify the integration. For a development account, use Windcave's [Integration Requirements form](https://www.windcave.com/merchant-attended-developer-hit).

### Install the extension {#install}

Open `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`, find **Windcave Terminal for WooCommerce**, install it and activate it. You need version 1.0 or later; version 0.1 is for WCPOS before 2.0. See [Extensions](../../extensions/index.mdx#installing-an-extension).

You can also upload the **plugin zip** (not the source-code zip) from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/windcave-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) under `Plugins > Add Plugin > Upload Plugin`.

Without WCPOS Pro 2.0, WP Admin shows *Windcave Terminal requires WooCommerce POS Pro 2.0 or newer.* and the gateway isn't added. Activation is refused on PHP older than 7.4, or without the DOM extension: ask your host to enable **php-xml**.

### Enter your HIT credentials {#credentials}

Go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` and open **Windcave Terminal**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={1} offsets={[[410, 0], [410, 0], [410, 0], [850, 0], [410, 0], [410, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, Windcave Terminal settings: Environment UAT (testing), empty HIT username, HIT key, Station IDs and Vendor ID, and POS name WCPOS">

1. **Environment** — leave it on **UAT (testing)**, the default. **Production** is for after Windcave certifies the integration, with production credentials.
2. **HIT username** — from Windcave, for the environment you chose.
3. **HIT key** — from Windcave. Once saved, the field stays blank: leave it blank to keep the saved key.
4. **Station IDs** — one per line, one for each terminal. Windcave issues them; they aren't found automatically.
5. **Vendor ID** — leave it empty until Windcave issues one.
6. **POS name** — what your POS is called at Windcave. `WCPOS` by default.

</JourneyShot>

Below them, **Result notifications (FPRN)** is optional. With it ticked, Windcave also tells your store when a result is ready, as a backup to the till's own checks. Your site must be reachable from the internet for it to work.

Click **Save changes**.

### Switch it on in WCPOS {#enable}

Go to `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`, find **Windcave Terminal** in the **Gateways** table and switch **Enabled** on. That screen saves as you go; there's no Save button.

Leave **Enable/Disable** on the WooCommerce settings page off. The POS uses the gateway once **Enabled** is on here, whether that box is ticked or not. See [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={2} offsets={[[172, 0], [-26, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, POS Settings, Checkout: two rows of the Gateways table, Vipps MobilePay switched on and Windcave Terminal switched off">

1. **Windcave Terminal** — the extension's row in the **Gateways** table.
2. **Enabled** — the switch beside the gateway ID; switch it on. The till shows the **Windcave Terminal** tile once it reloads.

</JourneyShot>

### Choose each till's Station {#till-terminal}

On each till, open **Settings > Card readers**. Every Station ID you entered is listed under **Smart readers**, all marked **Online**: the extension doesn't ask Windcave whether a terminal is switched on. Press **Use this reader** on the Station at that counter; its row shows **Selected**. The cashier can still press **Change** at checkout.

With only one Station there's nothing to choose: it's already **Selected** on every till. See [Choose a smart reader](../../hardware/card-readers/index.mdx#smart-readers).

### Check it worked {#check}

1. Back on the Windcave Terminal settings page, the line under the settings reads `uat: HIT credentials configured (not verified).` It means the username and key are saved, not that Windcave accepted them. `HIT credentials missing.` means one is empty.
2. On a till, reload the POS and press **Checkout**. The **Windcave Terminal** tile is among the ways to pay, with **Terminal:** and a Station ID above the keypad.
3. Take a small sale on a UAT terminal, then refund it. There's no test-connection button: this first sale is what checks the credentials and the Station ID.

### Upgrading from version 0.1 {#upgrade}

- Update WCPOS, WCPOS Pro and the app to 2.0 first, then the extension.
- A payment in progress during the upgrade carries over. Nothing is charged twice.
- A payment that 0.1 set aside carries over if its order is still unpaid. Otherwise the order gets a note: *Windcave TxnRef … was set aside before the 1.0.0 upgrade and is no longer followed automatically.* Check that payment in the Windcave portal: if it was approved, refund it there or record it on the order by hand.
- These 0.1 settings are gone: **Default Station ID**, **Lock terminal selection**, **Checkout logs** and **Log level**. Tills now choose their Station under **Settings > Card readers**.
- Set aside is gone too, and terminal receipt text is no longer added to the order as a private note.
- Refunding a sale taken by 0.1: see [Refund a Windcave payment](#refunds).

## Settings reference {#settings}

On `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Windcave Terminal`:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable/Disable** | Offers the gateway on your online store's checkout. Leave it off; the POS has its own switch under `POS > Settings > Checkout` |
| **Title** | The tile's name at the till. Defaults to `Windcave Terminal` |
| **Description** | Defaults to `Pay in person on the Windcave terminal.` |
| **Environment** | **UAT (testing)** (default) or **Production**. Each has its own HIT credentials; production needs Windcave certification. A payment started in one environment is always checked in that one |
| **HIT username** | Issued by Windcave, for the environment you chose |
| **HIT key** | Issued by Windcave. Never shown once saved; leave it blank to keep it |
| **Station IDs** | One per line, one per terminal. Duplicates are dropped |
| **Vendor ID** | Agreed with Windcave during certification. Leave it empty until then |
| **POS name** | The name your POS goes by at Windcave. Defaults to `WCPOS` |
| **Result notifications (FPRN)** | Windcave also tells your store when a result is ready, as a backup to the till's checks. Off by default |

## Logs and support bundle {#logs-and-support-bundle}

At the foot of the settings page are the support bundle, the credentials line and a link to the logs.

1. **Credentials line** — `HIT credentials missing.` on this store, which isn't set up. Yours should read `HIT credentials configured (not verified).`
2. **View logs** — opens `WooCommerce > Status > Logs`, source `windcave-terminal`.
3. **Download support bundle** — a file for WCPOS support.

The logs record each request to Windcave and its reply, with the HIT key and card numbers masked.

When a payment goes wrong, click **Download support bundle** and send the file to WCPOS support with the **order number**. WCPOS Pro adds this button: the bundle covers every POS payment provider on your site, with secrets and card numbers removed.

## What it doesn't do {#scope-and-limitations}

- Real payments, until Windcave certifies the integration.
- Tips, surcharges, cash-out, or authorising now and capturing later.
- Answering the terminal's questions on the **Payments** tab. Use the **Legacy** tab.
- Finding terminals on its own: you type in each Station ID.
- Checking the HIT credentials before the first payment.

<details>
<summary>How it works, for developers</summary>

The gateway uses Windcave **HIT (Host Initiated Transaction)** over HTTPS: `uat.windcave.com` for UAT and `sec.windcave.com` for production. Each terminal is addressed by its **Station ID**.

A payment sends a Purchase for the amount on the till, with `Order #` and the order number as the merchant reference, then WCPOS Pro checks its status until it completes. If the till asks again, the extension first queries the same transaction reference rather than starting another Purchase. A Purchase that Windcave hasn't registered within 30 seconds counts as not sent.

With **Result notifications (FPRN)** on, each Purchase asks Windcave to call your store's WCPOS payments webhook, `wp-json/wcpos/v2/payments/webhook` with `provider=windcave` and the transaction reference. The call is only a hint: the store then asks Windcave for the result itself.

A refund is a HIT matched Refund against the original Windcave transaction, for the amount you enter. The store checks its status every 2 seconds for up to 20 seconds.

</details>
