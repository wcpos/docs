# Windcave Terminal Gateway

The till sends the order total to a Windcave terminal, the customer pays on the terminal, and the sale finishes on its own when Windcave approves it. This page is in two parts: [taking a payment](#take-payment) at the till, and [setting it up](#setup) in WP Admin.

:::warning Not certified by Windcave yet
Windcave requires every point-of-sale integration to pass its QA certification before it takes real payments. This one hasn't passed yet. Until it does, run it with Windcave's **UAT** (test) credentials only.
:::

:::info Pro Feature
The Windcave Terminal extension needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx), both to install it from the extension directory and to use it at the till.
:::

## Take a payment {#take-payment}

:::note Before you start
Your store needs to be online: the payment runs on your store's own payment page, inside the POS. Someone with WP Admin access has to [set the gateway up](#setup) first.
:::

1. Build the cart and press **Checkout**.
2. Open the **Legacy** tab. Your store's payment page opens inside the POS. See [The Legacy Tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx).
3. Choose **Windcave Terminal**. Your store may have given it another name.
4. Pick the terminal under **Terminal**. With one terminal, or a locked default, it is already chosen. With two or more and no default, you must pick one.
5. Press **Start Terminal Payment**. The panel says **Sending to terminal…** and the full order total goes to that terminal.
6. The customer taps, inserts or swipes their card on the terminal. Whatever the terminal shows appears in the panel too, with its buttons. When the terminal asks you to check a signature, answer **YES** or **NO** in the panel.
7. When Windcave approves, the panel says **Payment complete — finishing order…** and the POS moves on to the receipt.

You don't need **Process Payment** at the foot of the Legacy tab: the sale finishes on its own when the terminal approves.

The terminal is always charged the whole order, so it can't share a sale with another method. Once a payment has been taken on the order, the Legacy tab can't be used; [cancel that payment](../../pos/checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx) first.

### Stop or set aside a payment {#payment-controls}

:::warning A payment set aside can still go through
Before you take payment another way, look at the terminal. If it shows the payment approved, don't charge the customer again. If you can't tell, ask your manager. When a set-aside payment is approved after the order was paid another way, the order gets a note saying so: refund that payment in the Windcave portal.
:::

- **Cancel Terminal Payment** replaces **Start Terminal Payment** while a payment runs. It presses **CANCEL** on the terminal when the terminal offers it. When it doesn't, the panel says so: cancel on the terminal itself, or set the payment aside.
- **Set payment aside** appears when a payment has run for 5 minutes with no answer, or when the terminal won't cancel. It lets you take payment another way. It does **not** cancel the payment at Windcave: a check on your store keeps following it up every 10 minutes, even after the till is closed.
- **Reloading the page** picks up the payment already in progress, with **Cancel Terminal Payment** showing.

## After the sale {#receipts}

When Windcave sends receipt text from the terminal, it is saved to the order as a **private note**. The POS still shows its own receipt as usual.

## Refunds {#refunds}

Refunds from WooCommerce aren't supported in this version. Refund in the Windcave portal (Payline) or on the terminal, then record the refund on the order in WooCommerce by hand. See [Refunds](../../pos/refunds.mdx).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Select a terminal first.** Pick a terminal under **Terminal**, then press **Start Terminal Payment** again.

**The terminal is still finishing an earlier transaction.** Finish or cancel that transaction on the terminal, then start again.

**Payment declined. You can try again.** The card was declined. Press **Start Terminal Payment** to try again, or take another method.

**Timed out waiting for the terminal.** Five minutes passed with no result. Check the terminal. If the customer paid, wait; if not, press **Cancel Terminal Payment** or **Set payment aside**.

**Windcave has no record of this transaction. Start the payment again.** The payment never reached Windcave. Press **Start Terminal Payment** again.

**Windcave already approved a payment for this order that could not be applied.** Don't charge the card again. Check the order notes and the Windcave portal first, and call your manager.

**This order was already paid by another payment.** The terminal approved, but the order had already been paid another way, so the customer has paid twice. Don't try again. Refund the terminal payment in the Windcave portal; the order notes name it.

**The terminal payment could not be verified.** Windcave's answer didn't match this order, for example its total changed. Check the payment in the Windcave portal before trying again.

**This payment was started in UAT** (or **production**). Someone changed **Environment** while the payment was running. Switch it back to finish the payment, or check it in the Windcave portal.

**Windcave reported code …: Check the Station ID and HIT settings.** Windcave refused the request, often because a Station ID is wrong. Ask whoever set up the gateway to check the [settings](#settings), or set the payment aside.

<details>
<summary>Messages while setting up</summary>

**Windcave HIT credentials are not configured.** The **HIT username** or **HIT key** is empty. Enter both and save, and check **Environment** matches them.

**No Station IDs configured** in the **Terminal** list. Add the Station IDs Windcave gave you, one per line, and save.

**Activation is refused.** The server needs **PHP 7.4** or newer and the PHP DOM extension (**php-xml**). Ask your host to enable it.

</details>

## Set it up {#setup}

This part is for whoever runs the store's WP Admin.

<Steps>
  <StepCard number="1" title="Install the extension">

Open `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`, find **Windcave Terminal for WooCommerce**, click **Install**, then **Activate**. See [Installing an Extension](../../extensions/index.mdx#installing-an-extension).

Without the directory, download the **plugin zip** (not the source-code zip) from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/windcave-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) and upload it with `Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin`. WooCommerce must be active.

  </StepCard>
  <StepCard number="2" title="Get HIT credentials from Windcave">

Ask Windcave for a **HIT username**, a **HIT key** and one **Station ID** for each terminal. For a development account, use Windcave's [Integration Requirements form](https://www.windcave.com/merchant-attended-developer-hit).

UAT and production credentials are separate. Use UAT ones until Windcave certifies this integration.

  </StepCard>
  <StepCard number="3" title="Fill in the gateway settings">

1. Go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` and open **Windcave Terminal**.
2. Leave **Environment** on **UAT (testing)**, the default. **Production** is for after certification, with production credentials.
3. Enter the **HIT username** and **HIT key**.
4. Enter your **Station IDs**, one per line.
5. Optional: set a **Default Station ID**, and tick **Lock terminal selection** so cashiers always use it.
6. Leave **Vendor ID** empty. Windcave issues one during certification.
7. Optional: tick **Result notifications (FPRN)**, so Windcave also tells your store when a result is ready, as a backup to the till's own checks.
8. Save.

There is no test-connection button. The credentials and Station IDs are checked by the first payment, so run a test payment on a UAT terminal.

  </StepCard>
  <StepCard number="4" title="Turn it on for the POS" isLast>

Go to `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`, find **Windcave Terminal** and switch **Enabled** on. See [Enabling a Gateway](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

Leave **Enable/Disable** in the WooCommerce settings off. The POS uses the gateway once **Enabled** is on here, whether that box is ticked or not.

  </StepCard>
</Steps>

## Settings reference {#settings}

On `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Windcave Terminal`. The steps above cover the ones you need; open the list for every setting and its default.

<details>
<summary>All 15 settings</summary>

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable/Disable** | Lists the gateway on the online store too. Leave it off: online, it can only take payment on an existing order's payment page, not at the normal checkout. Off by default. The POS has its own switch, under `POS > Settings > Checkout` |
| **Title** | The name cashiers choose on the payment page. Defaults to `Windcave Terminal` |
| **Description** | The line shown under the title. Defaults to `Pay in person on the Windcave terminal.` |
| **Environment** | **UAT (testing)** (default) or **Production**. Each has its own HIT credentials; production needs Windcave certification |
| **HIT username** | Issued by Windcave, for the environment you chose |
| **HIT key** | Issued by Windcave, for the environment you chose |
| **Station IDs** | One Station ID per line, one per terminal. Windcave issues them; they aren't found automatically |
| **Default Station ID** | The terminal chosen at the start, and the one used when none is picked |
| **Lock terminal selection** | Cashiers can't change the terminal: the default is always used. Works only when a default is set |
| **Vendor ID** | Issued by Windcave during certification. Leave it empty until then |
| **POS name** | The name your POS goes by at Windcave. Defaults to `WCPOS` |
| **Result notifications (FPRN)** | Windcave also tells your store when a result is ready, as a backup to the till's checks. Off by default |
| **Checkout logs** | Shows **Show logs**, **Copy** and **Clear** on the payment panel. Off by default |
| **Log level** | **Off**, **Errors only** or **Debug** (the default) |
| **Support** | **Download support bundle**, and a link to the logs |

</details>

## Logs and support bundle {#logs-and-support-bundle}

Logs are in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Status > Logs`, under the source `windcave-terminal`. **Log level** starts on **Debug**, which records every request to Windcave and every answer, with the HIT key and card numbers masked.

When a payment fails, click **Download support bundle** on the gateway's settings page and send the `.json` file to support with the **order number**.

<details>
<summary>What the support bundle holds</summary>

- Your store's setup: the WordPress, WooCommerce, PHP and extension versions, the environment and the number of terminals.
- The gateway settings, with the HIT key masked and the HIT username cut to its first three characters.
- Payment attempts from up to 20 recent orders, without receipts.
- The last 1,000 lines of the two newest `windcave-terminal` log files. These can hold terminal receipt text, with card numbers masked.

If WooCommerce keeps its logs in the database, the bundle has no log lines: export them from `WooCommerce > Status > Logs` and send them too.

</details>

## What it doesn't do {#scope-and-limitations}

- Refunds from WooCommerce.
- Tips, surcharges and cash-out.
- Currencies without two decimal places.
- Part-payments: the terminal is always charged the whole order.
- Finding terminals on its own: you type in each Station ID.
- The online store's normal checkout. Online, it can only take payment on an existing order's payment page.

<details>
<summary>How it works, for developers</summary>

The gateway uses Windcave **HIT (Host Initiated Transaction)** over HTTPS. **Environment** picks the UAT or production endpoint, and each terminal is addressed by its **Station ID**.

Starting a payment records a transaction reference on the order, then sends a Purchase for the order total and currency. The panel asks Windcave for the status every 1.5 seconds and mirrors the terminal's display lines and enabled buttons. With **Result notifications (FPRN)** on, Windcave also calls the store when a result is ready.

The order completes only when the result is approved and matches the attempt recorded on the order: the reference, the amount, the environment, and the order's total and currency. Windcave gets 30 seconds to register a new transaction before "no record" counts as declined. A scheduled check every 10 minutes follows up payments that went quiet or were set aside.

</details>
