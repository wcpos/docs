# Square Terminal Gateway

The till sends the amount to a [Square Terminal](https://squareup.com/hardware/terminal), the customer pays on the Terminal, and the sale moves on by itself when Square approves it. This page is in two parts: [taking a payment](#take-payment) at the till, and [setting it up](#setup) in WP Admin.

:::info Pro Feature
Square Terminal needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) 2.0 or newer.
:::

## Take a payment {#take-payment}

:::note Before you start
- The till is online, and the Terminal is switched on. If the **Square Terminal** tile is missing, ask whoever runs WP Admin to [set it up](#setup).
- With **Open and close the register** switched on, the register is open. The till won't record a payment while it's closed, and a card already charged on the Terminal would have to be refunded. See [Open the Register](../../pos/register/index.mdx).
:::

1. Build the cart and press **Checkout**.
2. On the **Payments** tab, tap the **Square Terminal** tile.
3. Check the terminal line, **Terminal:** and the Terminal's name. With more than one Terminal, press **Change** and tap the one at your till. Until you pick one, the pane says **Choose a terminal**.
4. Leave the amount as the balance due, or type a smaller amount to take part of it on the card. See [Split Payments](../../pos/checkout/split-payments.mdx).
5. Press **Send $45.00 to Square Terminal** (the button names your amount). The pane shows **Starting the terminal…**, then **Waiting for the customer on the terminal**.
6. The customer taps, inserts or swipes their card on the Terminal.
7. When Square approves, the steps run through **Approved** and **Captured**. If that clears the balance, the sale completes and the receipt is shown; if not, take the rest the same way or another way.

While the payment runs, the pane shows:

- **ON THE TERMINAL** and the amount sent.
- One status line, such as **Waiting for the customer on the terminal**.
- The method and the Terminal's name.
- Four steps: **Sent**, **On terminal**, **Approved**, **Captured**.
- **Details**: each step Square reported, with the time, and the reference numbers support will ask for. **Copy** (**Share** on some devices) hands the log to whoever helps you.

<details>
<summary>Payment status messages and what to do</summary>

- **Approved**, then **Captured**: the money is in. If that clears the balance, the receipt shows; if not, take the rest.
- **Payment cancelled**: the Terminal confirmed your cancel. Press **Try again** or **Choose another way**.
- **Timed out — payment cancelled**: nobody paid within 5 minutes. Press **Try again** or **Choose another way**.
- **Cancelling — waiting for the terminal to confirm**: the Terminal hasn't answered your cancel. Wait, or press **Release this payment**. See [Stop a payment](#stop).
- **Payment released**: the order is free to be paid another way, but the card may still have been charged on the Terminal. Look at the Terminal's screen first.

</details>

### Stop a payment {#stop}

**Cancel on terminal** asks Square to cancel the payment on the Terminal. The pane says **Cancelling…**, and then **Payment cancelled** when the Terminal confirms. Press **Try again** to send it again, or **Choose another way** to go back to the tiles.

If the Terminal doesn't confirm (it's switched off, out of signal or stuck), the pane says **Cancelling — waiting for the terminal to confirm** and offers **Release this payment**, with the line *Frees the order to be paid another way. The store keeps checking the terminal.*

:::warning Check the Terminal before you charge again
Releasing doesn't cancel the payment at Square. Look at the Terminal's screen before you take the money another way.
:::

**Release this payment** frees the order, and the pane says **Payment released**. Then go by what the Terminal shows:

- **The payment went through:** don't charge again. The store keeps checking the Terminal, about every 10 minutes, and records the payment on the order.
- **The payment was cancelled:** press **Choose another way** and take the money another way.
- **You can't tell** (the Terminal is off, has no signal or shows nothing): you can take the money another way. If the Terminal payment turns up later, the store records it on the order too; refund one of the two from the order. See [Refunds](../../orders/refunds.mdx).

If nobody pays on the Terminal within 5 minutes, the payment is cancelled and the pane says **Timed out — payment cancelled**.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**No terminals set up for Square Terminal.** No Terminal is paired with the extension yet. Ask whoever runs WP Admin to [pair one](#pairing).

**In use by order #…** That Terminal is running another order's payment. Finish or cancel that payment first, or pick another Terminal.

**Payment failed** and a reason. Press **Try again**, or **Choose another way**. **Details** shows what Square said.

**Cancelling — waiting for the terminal to confirm.** The Terminal hasn't answered the cancel. Wait, or press **Release this payment**. See [Stop a payment](#stop).

**The Terminal paid but the till is still waiting.** The till keeps checking with Square and moves on when Square answers. Don't take the money another way while you wait: look at the Terminal's screen first.

**The tile isn't there.** The gateway isn't switched on for the POS. See [Turn it on for the POS](#enable).

<details>
<summary>Setup problems, for whoever runs WP Admin</summary>

<details>
<summary>The Terminal isn't in the list</summary>

It has to be paired with this extension: use **Create Device Code** and enter the code on the Terminal. A Terminal paired through the Square Dashboard or the Square app doesn't show until it's paired here. If **Check for readers** lists it under *Other devices Square can see at this location*, it exists but belongs to another application. Also check the **Location ID** matches the location the Terminal is signed in to.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The Terminal won't pair</summary>

The code lasts 5 minutes: create a fresh one. The Terminal must be online and signed out of the Square app or any other integration, because the code signs it in to the account and **Location ID** set here. The **Environment** must match that account.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Validate Settings fails</summary>

If you connected, check the **Square account** row still says **Connected to Square**. With your own access token, check it's for the selected **Environment**: a sandbox token doesn't work in **Live**, and the other way round. Check the **Location ID** belongs to that account.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Reconnect to Square required</summary>

Square authorizations renew by themselves. When a renewal can't finish, the extension ends the authorization and asks you to reconnect. Press **Reconnect to Square**; nothing else needs changing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where to find the logs</summary>

`WooCommerce > Status > Logs`, source `square-terminal-for-woocommerce`. Key steps are also written to the order's notes, starting `Square Terminal:`.

</details>

</details>

## Set it up {#setup}

This part is for whoever runs the store's WP Admin. Your store sends each payment to Square, and Square sends it to the paired Terminal, so the till needs a connection to the store. Do these five in order:

1. [Install the extension](#install) from the extension directory.
2. [Connect to Square](#connect) and choose the location.
3. [Pair your Terminal](#pair-your-terminal), or use Square's test devices in sandbox.
4. [Turn it on for the POS](#enable).
5. [Check it worked](#check) with a test payment.

### Install the extension {#install}

Open `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`, find **Square Terminal**, click **Install**, then **Activate**. See [Installing an Extension](../../extensions/index.mdx#installing-an-extension).

Without the directory, download the **plugin zip** (not the source-code zip) from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/square-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) and upload it with `Plugins > Add Plugin`, then **Upload Plugin**. Without WCPOS Pro 2.0 the extension does nothing and says it needs it.

### Connect to Square {#connect}

1. Go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` and open **Square Terminal**.
2. Under **Square account**, choose the **Environment**: **Test mode (sandbox)** (the default) to try it out, or **Live** for real payments. Choose it **before** you connect: a connection covers one environment only.
3. Click **Connect to Square**. The button names the environment, such as **Connect to Square (sandbox)**. Sign in to Square and approve what it asks for. Back on the settings screen, the row says **Connected to Square**, with the environment and your merchant ID.
4. Paste the **Location ID**: the ID of the Square location the Terminal takes payments for. It's a text box, not a list. Square shows your location IDs in the [Square Developer Console](https://developer.squareup.com/apps): open an application, choose **Sandbox** or **Production** to match the **Environment**, then **Locations**. Sandbox and production each have their own locations. If you connected and have no application yet, add one there first: it's free, and any name will do.
5. Under **Which Square device do you use?**, leave **Square Terminal**.
6. Click **Save changes**.

1. **Environment** — choose it before you connect (step 2).
2. **Connect to Square (sandbox)** — the button names the environment you chose (step 3).
3. **Location ID** — paste the location's ID (step 4).

</JourneyShot>

Already using the official WooCommerce Square plugin? **Environment** and **Location ID** are pre-filled from it. Save to apply. No credentials are shared between the two plugins, so you still connect here.

Prefer your own access token? Open **Advanced settings** and paste it instead of connecting. Square shows it in the [Square Developer Console](https://developer.squareup.com/apps): open your application, choose **Credentials** in the left pane, choose **Sandbox** or **Production** at the top, then **Show** in the access token box, and copy it. The sandbox token goes in **Sandbox Access Token**, the production one in **Production Access Token**.

### Pair your Terminal {#pair-your-terminal}

Under **Terminal**, click **Create Device Code** and enter the code on the Terminal within 5 minutes. Then click **Check for readers**. The full steps, what the list shows and the sandbox test devices are in [Paired Terminals and test devices](#pairing).

### Turn it on for the POS {#enable}

Go to `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`, find **Square Terminal** in the **Gateways** table and switch **Enabled** on. That screen saves as you go; there's no Save button. See [Enabling a Gateway](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={2} offsets={[[80, 0], [-30, 0], [-45, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, POS Settings, Checkout: the Gateways table, with the Square Terminal row (gateway ID sqtwc) still switched off under Enabled">

1. **Gateways** — every payment gateway installed in WooCommerce, in the order the till shows them.
2. **Square Terminal** — the extension's row, gateway ID `sqtwc`.
3. **Enabled** — switch it on. The till shows the **Square Terminal** tile once it reloads.

</JourneyShot>

:::note
Square Terminal is for staff at the till only; it's never offered at your online shop's checkout. The **Enabled** toggle on the WooCommerce **Payments** list has no effect on it.
:::

### Check it worked {#check}

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={3} offsets={[[10, -30], [10, -30]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, WooCommerce Payments, Square Terminal: the Terminal group, with the Validate Settings and Check for readers buttons in the Terminal pairing row">

1. **Validate Settings** — step 1.
2. **Check for readers** — step 2.

</JourneyShot>

1. Back on the Square Terminal settings screen, under **Terminal**, click **Validate Settings**. It says *Square credentials and location verified.* If it doesn't, see [Validate Settings fails](#goes-wrong).
2. Click **Check for readers**. Your Terminal is listed under **Paired with this plugin — selectable at checkout**. In **Test mode (sandbox)** there's nothing to pair, so skip this.

3. On a till, reload the POS, build a cart and press **Checkout**. The **Payments** tab has a **Square Terminal** tile, and the pane shows **Terminal:** with your Terminal's name. In **Test mode (sandbox)**, Square's test devices are listed there instead.
4. Take a test payment. In **Test mode (sandbox)**, choose the test device **Sandbox: Success (charge $25 or less)**, keep the amount at $25 or less and press **Send … to Square Terminal**. The steps run through **Approved** and **Captured**. See [test devices](#pairing).
5. Switch the **Environment** to **Live** and connect again when you're ready for real payments: a connection covers one environment only.

## Paired Terminals and test devices {#pairing}

A Terminal has to be paired with **this extension** before a cashier can pick it. Pairing makes a Square *device code*, and that's the only way the extension can reach the Terminal.

1. Under **Terminal**, click **Create Device Code**. The screen shows *Pairing code: … — enter it on the Terminal within 5 minutes.*
2. On the Terminal, open the device-code sign-in screen and enter the code. If the Terminal is signed in to the Square app or another integration, sign it out of that first: the device-code screen isn't reachable while it's in use elsewhere.
3. Click **Check for readers**. The Terminal now shows under **Paired with this plugin — selectable at checkout**.

**Check for readers** lists what Square can see, in two groups:

- **Paired with this plugin — selectable at checkout**: the Terminals cashiers can pick.
- **Other devices Square can see at this location**: Terminals set up by another application. They can't be picked here until you pair them with a fresh code.

**Validate Settings** asks Square whether your connection (or access token) and the location work. When they do, it says *Square credentials and location verified.*

:::note An empty list before pairing is normal
Square only lists Terminals paired with a device code. A Terminal running the Square app doesn't appear at all until a device code is entered on it.
:::

<details>
<summary>Test mode: Square's test devices instead of a Terminal</summary>

In **Test mode (sandbox)** there are no device codes and nothing to pair. The till lists Square's test devices instead, where a paired Terminal would be. Each one plays out one result without hardware:

| Test device | What it plays out |
|---|---|
| **Sandbox: Success (charge $25 or less)** | An approved card payment. Keep the amount at $25 or less. |
| **Sandbox: Success with 20% tip** | A payment with a tip. Square takes no tips here, so it ends in a failed payment: **The terminal confirmed a different amount**. |
| **Sandbox: Buyer cancels on terminal** | The customer cancels on the Terminal. |
| **Sandbox: Immediate timeout** | Nobody pays, and the payment times out straight away. |
| **Sandbox: Offline terminal (stays pending)** | A Terminal that never answers. Use it to try [Stop a payment](#stop) without hardware. |

</details>

## Webhooks {#webhooks}

A webhook is **optional**. It lets Square tell your store the moment a payment changes, so the till moves on sooner. Without one, the till asks Square for the result while it waits, and the store's background check, about every 10 minutes, records any result the till missed. Either way, the store records a payment only when Square confirms the payment itself; a checkout Square marks finished isn't enough on its own.

<details>
<summary>Add a webhook</summary>

This only works if you set the extension up with your own access token, under **Advanced settings**. If you used **Connect to Square**, you authorized the WCPOS application, not one of your own, so you have no application to add a subscription to and no signature key to copy. Payments still confirm through the till's own checks and the background check.

With your own access token, use your application in the [Square Developer Dashboard](https://developer.squareup.com/apps):

1. On the settings screen, in the **Webhooks** row under **Terminal**, click **Copy** to copy the webhook URL.
2. In the Square Developer Dashboard, open your application and go to **Webhooks**.
3. Add a subscription for the **`terminal.checkout.updated`** event, with that URL as the notification URL.
4. Copy the subscription's **Signature Key** into **Webhook Signature Key** under **Advanced settings**, and save.

The **Webhooks** row then says **Verified … ago** once a signed webhook has arrived.

:::warning The URL must match exactly
Square signs each webhook with the notification URL it was given. If the URL in Square differs from the one on the settings screen by a single character, every delivery fails. Use **Copy** rather than typing it.
:::

<details>
<summary>Upgrading from version 0.x</summary>

Version 1.0.0 shows a new webhook URL. If you already have a subscription, edit its notification URL in Square to the one the settings screen shows now; editing keeps its signature key. **Webhook URL override** doesn't change this URL; it only applies to the extension's own payment page. If your site's public address differs from the one shown, its webhooks won't verify: payments still confirm through the till's own checks and the background check.

</details>

Square's webhook subscriptions belong to a Square application and can't be added with a seller's access token, so the extension can't add the subscription for you.

</details>

### If webhooks stop verifying {#webhook-troubleshooting}

The **Webhooks** row says **Not verified yet** until a webhook arrives and verifies under the **current** settings.

<details>
<summary>Payments have run and it still says Not verified yet</summary>

Check:

1. **Webhook Signature Key** matches the subscription's key in Square.
2. The notification URL in Square matches the settings screen exactly.
3. The subscription includes `terminal.checkout.updated`.
4. Your site is reachable from the internet over HTTPS. Square's dashboard shows each delivery attempt.

Changing the environment, the webhook URL or the signature key resets the row until the next webhook arrives. That's on purpose: a webhook verified under the old settings says nothing about the new ones.

</details>

## Settings reference {#settings}

On `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Square Terminal`, in the order setup runs. Open a group to see its settings.

<details>
<summary>Square account</summary>

- **Environment**: **Test mode (sandbox)** (default) or **Live**. A connection covers one environment.
- **Connect to Square (sandbox)** or **(production)**: authorizes this site with Square for that environment. Shows **Connected to Square**, the environment, the merchant and **Disconnect** once done.
- **Location ID**: the ID of the Square location the Terminal takes payments for, from the Developer Console's **Locations** page.
- **Which Square device do you use?**: **Square Terminal** (this page, the default) or **Square Reader**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Terminal</summary>

- **Create Device Code**, **Check for readers**, **Validate Settings**: pair a Terminal, list what Square can see, check the setup. See [Paired Terminals and test devices](#pairing).
- **Webhooks**: whether a signed webhook has arrived, and the URL to **Copy**. See [Webhooks](#webhooks).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Checkout behaviour</summary>

- **Skip receipt screen**: the Terminal goes back to idle without showing its own receipt screen.
- **Collect signature**: asks the customer to sign on the Terminal, where the payment supports it.
- **Checkout debug logs**: a copyable log panel on the extension's own payment page. For support; safe to leave off.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Advanced settings</summary>

- **Sandbox Access Token**, **Production Access Token**: only needed if you don't connect above. From your application's **Credentials** page in the Square Developer Console.
- **Webhook Signature Key**: optional. Lets the store verify webhooks; payments confirm without it.
- **Webhook URL override**: for the extension's own payment page only. See [Webhooks](#webhooks).

**Advanced settings** is closed by default on the settings screen too.

</details>

Below the groups, **Download support bundle** makes a file to share with WCPOS support. It covers every POS payment provider on the site, with secrets and card numbers removed.

## Square Reader instead of a Terminal {#square-reader}

Set **Which Square device do you use?** to **Square Reader** to take the payment in the Square Point of Sale app, with a Square Reader paired to that phone or tablet. The settings screen then shows a five-step checklist, **Set up your Square Reader**.

<details>
<summary>The five Square Reader setup steps</summary>

1. Connect your Square account.
2. Choose your location.
3. Register your store with Square. Open [Square's developer site](https://developer.squareup.com/apps) and sign in with your normal Square login. Click **+** (Add an application), type any name and save. On the page that opens, find **Production Application ID** and press **Copy**, then paste it under **Paste your Application ID**. Application IDs start with `sq0idp-`; one starting `sandbox-` is the test one.
4. Give Square your store's return address. Click **Copy address** on the checklist. On Square's developer site, click **Point of Sale API** in the left menu, scroll to the **Web** section, paste the address into **Web callback URL** and press **Save**.
5. Set up the phone or tablet. Install the free Square Point of Sale app, sign in with the same Square account as step 1, and pair your Reader under **≡ More → Settings → Hardware**.

</details>

At the till, the payment runs on the extension's own payment page, on the [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx): press **Open Square Point of Sale** and finish the payment in the Square app. It works only:

- in **Live**, not in test mode;
- on an Android or iOS phone or tablet with the Square Point of Sale app installed. Anywhere else the button is turned off and says why.

## Refunds {#refunds}

Refund a Square Terminal payment from the order, and the money goes back to the customer's card through Square. Part refunds work too. See [Refunds](../../orders/refunds.mdx).

A payment taken on the extension's own payment page, such as one from a version before 1.0.0, is refunded from the Square Dashboard instead.
