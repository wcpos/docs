# Mercado Pago Terminal Gateway

Mercado Pago Terminal sends a sale from the till to a Mercado Pago **Point** terminal. The customer pays on the terminal, and the till records the payment once Mercado Pago confirms it.

This page starts at the till. Store admins: [Set up Mercado Pago Terminal](#setup) covers the access token, the webhook, the terminals and how to check it worked.

:::info Pro Feature
Mercado Pago Terminal needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) 2.0 or later, and version 1.0 or later of the extension.
:::

:::warning Before the first sale (admin)
Take a small payment on your own card and refund it: see [Check it worked](#check).

<details>
<summary>What has and hasn't been tested</summary>

Version 1.0 hasn't been run on a real Point terminal or a Mercado Pago account yet. It has been tested against a stand-in for Mercado Pago, built from Mercado Pago's documentation. Version 0.1, on a live account and a real Point Smart, listed the terminals, switched one to PDV mode, and created and cancelled a payment. Not yet tried with either version: a paid card payment, a decline, cancelling on the terminal, Mercado Pago's notifications, refunds, and Mercado Pago's sandbox.

</details>
:::

## Take a card payment {#take-payment}

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, the till won't record a payment while the register is closed, and a card you've already taken would have to be refunded. See [Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) for the setting.
:::

1. Press **Checkout** in the cart and tap the **Mercado Pago Terminal** tile on the **Payments** tab. Your store may have given it another name.
2. Look above the keypad. If you see **Choose a terminal**, tap the terminal at your counter. If you see **Terminal:** and a name, that's the one this till uses; press **Change** to pick another. The till remembers your choice.
3. To take part of the balance on the card, type the amount. The rest stays due for another payment. **Full balance** puts the whole amount back.
4. Press **Send … to Mercado Pago Terminal**. The till shows **Waiting for the customer on the terminal**.
5. The customer pays on the terminal. When the money is taken and nothing is left to pay, the receipt opens. If some is left, take it with another tile.

<details>
<summary>What the steps on the till mean</summary>

While the payment runs, the till lights **Sent**, **On terminal** and **Captured** in turn. **Captured** means the money is taken. **Approved** stays dark for Mercado Pago, which takes the money in one step.

</details>

The terminal doesn't print a ticket. Print or email the POS receipt as usual; see [Receipts at Checkout](../../receipts/at-checkout.mdx). There's no tip on the terminal.

### Cancel a payment {#cancel}

**Cancel on terminal** works only until the payment appears on the terminal's screen. Once the customer can see it, the till can't take it back:

1. **Cancel the payment on the terminal itself**, or ask the customer not to pay.
2. Wait for the till. It may stay on **Cancelling — waiting for the terminal to confirm** while it checks. The customer can still pay until the terminal cancels, so don't take payment another way yet.
3. The till shows **Payment cancelled** once the terminal cancels. If nobody cancels, Mercado Pago drops the payment after 5 minutes, and the till shows **Payment failed: Timed out on the terminal**.

If the till offers **Release this payment** instead, don't press it until a manager has checked Mercado Pago: see [The till offers Release this payment](#other-messages) under Other messages.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

### When the customer may have paid {#may-have-paid}

:::warning Don't take the payment again
In these cases the card may already be charged. A manager checks your Mercado Pago account first, where the payment is listed as **Order #** and the order number.
:::

**The card was charged, but the order didn't finish.** Mercado Pago took the money, but the till couldn't finish the order. Don't charge again. Reload the app, and the till tries again to finish the sale; if the order is still open, a manager finishes it. See [Card charged, order not finished](../../pos/checkout/troubleshooting.mdx#card-charged-order-open).

**Payment failed: The terminal confirmed a different amount.** The terminal took an amount other than the one the till sent, so the till didn't record it, but the card may be charged. Ask a manager to find the payment in Mercado Pago and compare it with the order.

**The till closed or lost its connection during a payment.** Don't send the payment again from another till. With the [webhook](#webhook) set up, your store records a payment Mercado Pago confirms even with the till closed. Open the order: if it still shows unpaid, a manager checks Mercado Pago before you take payment again.

## Refund a Mercado Pago payment {#refunds}

A refund goes back through Mercado Pago to the customer's card, for the full amount or part of it.

1. In the POS, open the order's refund. See [Refunds](../../pos/refunds.mdx).
2. Choose **Refund to Mercado Pago Terminal** and press **Process Refund**.
3. Your store asks Mercado Pago for the refund and adds the result to the order notes.

A refund in WP Admin, with WooCommerce's **Refund … via Mercado Pago Terminal** button, works the same way. Sales taken with version 0.1 refund the same way too.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="A refund stays pending">

  Mercado Pago hadn't finished the refund when it answered, and your store may not update it on its own. Find the refund in your Mercado Pago account before you refund again: if it went through, the customer has their money.

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem question="Mercado Pago refuses the refund">

  Some card acquirers only allow refunds on the terminal. Refund on the Point terminal or in your Mercado Pago account, then record it in WooCommerce with **Refund … manually**.

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem question="I refunded on the terminal or in Mercado Pago">

  Your store isn't told about refunds made there, so the order still shows the full payment. Record the refund in WooCommerce with **Refund … manually**, so no money goes back twice.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Set up Mercado Pago Terminal {#setup}

An administrator does this once per store in WP Admin, then picks a terminal on each till. Your store reaches each terminal through Mercado Pago over the internet, so there's nothing to pair, and any till can use any of your terminals.

### Requirements {#requirements}

<RequirementsList>
  <RequirementItem label="Mercado Pago Account">A seller account with Point, and an application under **Your integrations** with its access token</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Terminal">A Mercado Pago Point terminal, such as a Point Smart, logged in with the same account</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Currency">Your store's currency must be your Mercado Pago account's currency. Nothing is converted</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="WCPOS">WCPOS Pro 2.0 or later, and version 1.0 or later of the extension</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Server">WordPress 6.0 or newer, WooCommerce, and PHP 7.4 or newer. Your site must be reachable from the internet for the webhook</RequirementItem>
</RequirementsList>

If your currency has no cents, such as Chilean pesos, set **Number of decimals** to **0** under `WooCommerce > Settings > General`. Mercado Pago refuses amounts with decimals on those accounts.

### Install the extension {#install}

Open `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`, find **Mercado Pago Terminal for WooCommerce**, install it and activate it. You need version 1.0 or later; version 0.1 is for WCPOS before 2.0. See [Extensions](../../extensions/index.mdx#installing-an-extension).

You can also upload the **plugin zip** (not the source-code zip) from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/mercadopago-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) under `Plugins > Add Plugin > Upload Plugin`.

Without WCPOS Pro 2.0, WP Admin shows *Mercado Pago Terminal requires WooCommerce POS Pro 2.0 or newer.* and the gateway isn't added.

### Enter your access token {#credentials}

Log in to **Mercado Pago Developers** with your seller account and open **Your integrations**. Create an application if you have none, then open it. Under **Production credentials**, copy the **Access token**: it starts `APP_USR-`. That's the token for real payments. The one under **Test credentials** only reaches Mercado Pago's sandbox, which hasn't been tried with this extension.

Then go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` and open **Mercado Pago Terminal**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={1} offsets={[[80, 0], [410, 0], [850, 0], [410, 0], [410, 0], [410, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, Mercado Pago Terminal settings: Enable unticked, Title Mercado Pago Terminal, the default Description, Mode Test, and empty Access token and Webhook secret fields">

1. **Enable** — leave it unticked. It offers the gateway on your online store; the POS has [its own switch](#enable).
2. **Title** — the tile's name at the till.
3. **Description** — shown under the title on the store's payment page.
4. **Mode** — a label for your own records. The hint under it is out of date: the access token, not **Mode**, decides whether payments are real.
5. **Access token** — paste your production token here. Once saved, the field stays blank: leave it blank to keep the saved token.
6. **Webhook secret** — leave it for now. You fill it in when you [add the webhook](#webhook).

</JourneyShot>

Click **Save changes**.

### Add the webhook {#webhook}

The webhook lets Mercado Pago tell your store as soon as a payment ends. Payments still work without it, because the till and your store keep asking Mercado Pago, but set it up so a paid order finishes even if the till closed.

1. Copy the webhook URL from the description under **Webhook secret**. It ends in `wp-json/wcpos/v2/payments/webhook?provider=mercadopago`.
2. In Mercado Pago Developers, open **Your integrations**, your application, then **Webhooks**. Add the URL and select the **Order (Mercado Pago)** event.
3. Copy the **secret signature** Mercado Pago shows, paste it into **Webhook secret** and click **Save changes**.

Your store refuses any notification that isn't signed with that secret, so a webhook without the secret does nothing.

### Switch your terminals to PDV {#terminals}

Point terminals ship in **STANDALONE** mode, where they can't take payments from a till. Only terminals in **PDV** mode are offered at checkout.

1. Log in on each terminal with the same Mercado Pago account, if you haven't.
2. Reload the Mercado Pago Terminal settings page. Under **Mercado Pago diagnostics**, a table lists each terminal with its **ID**, **Terminal** name and **Mode**.
3. Click **Switch to PDV** on each terminal in **STANDALONE** mode, then **restart that terminal**. Mercado Pago needs the restart.

The table lists up to 50 terminals.

### Switch it on in WCPOS {#enable}

Go to `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`, find **Mercado Pago Terminal** in the **Gateways** table and switch **Enabled** on. That screen saves as you go; there's no Save button.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={2} offsets={[[172, 0], [-26, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, POS Settings, Checkout: three rows of the Gateways table, Cash on delivery, Mercado Pago Terminal and Mollie Terminal, all switched off">

1. **Mercado Pago Terminal** — the extension's row in the **Gateways** table.
2. **Enabled** — the switch beside the gateway ID; switch it on. The till shows the **Mercado Pago Terminal** tile once it reloads.

</JourneyShot>

Leave **Enable** on the WooCommerce settings page off. The POS uses the gateway once **Enabled** is on here, whether that box is ticked or not. See [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

### Choose each till's terminal {#till-terminal}

On each till, open **Settings > Card readers**. Your terminals are listed under **Smart readers**, by the name Mercado Pago gives them. A terminal marked **Offline** hasn't been switched over yet: see [Switch your terminals to PDV](#terminals). Press **Use this reader** on the terminal at that counter; its row shows **Selected**. The cashier can still press **Change** at checkout.

With only one terminal in PDV mode there's nothing to choose: it's already **Selected** on every till. See [Choose a smart reader](../../hardware/card-readers/index.mdx#smart-readers).

### Check it worked {#check}

1. On the Mercado Pago Terminal settings page, the line under **Mercado Pago diagnostics** reads **Credentials accepted. Restart the terminal after switching to PDV.** (one message). That means Mercado Pago accepted the access token. If it reads *Mercado Pago access token is missing.*, no token is saved; any other text is Mercado Pago's answer to the token.
2. Each terminal you'll use shows **PDV** under **Mode**.
3. On a till, reload the POS and press **Checkout**. The **Mercado Pago Terminal** tile is among the ways to pay, with your terminals above the keypad.
4. Take a small sale on your own card, then refund it.

### Upgrading from version 0.1 {#upgrade}

- Update WCPOS, WCPOS Pro and the app to 2.0 first, then the extension.
- **Add the new webhook** and its secret: see [Add the webhook](#webhook). The 0.1 webhook address is gone, and notifications without a signature are refused.
- A payment in progress during the upgrade carries over. Nothing is charged twice.
- These 0.1 settings are gone: **Default terminal**, **Enabled terminals**, **Lock terminal selection**, **Checkout debug logs** and **Log level**. Tills now choose their terminal under **Settings > Card readers**.
- **Mode** no longer picks the environment: check that the saved access token is the one you mean to use.
- The payment now runs on the **Payments** tab. The **Legacy** tab still works, for the whole balance only.
- Sales taken by 0.1 refund as usual: see [Refund a Mercado Pago payment](#refunds).

## Settings reference {#settings}

On `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Mercado Pago Terminal`:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable** | Offers the gateway on your online store's checkout. Leave it off; the POS has its own switch under `POS > Settings > Checkout` |
| **Title** | The tile's name at the till. Defaults to `Mercado Pago Terminal` |
| **Description** | Defaults to `Pay in person on a Mercado Pago Point terminal.` |
| **Mode** | **Test** (default) or **Live**. A label only: the access token decides whether payments are real, whatever the field's own description says |
| **Access token** | Mercado Pago Developers → **Your integrations** → your application → **Production credentials**. Never shown once saved, only its last characters; leave it blank to keep it. Finish open payments before you replace it |
| **Webhook secret** | The secret signature from your application's webhook in Mercado Pago. Its description shows your store's webhook URL. Saved and shown like the token |

## Logs and support bundle {#logs-and-support-bundle}

At the foot of the settings page are the support bundle, the diagnostics line and a link to the logs.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={3} offsets={[[260, 0], [80, 0], [-20, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, foot of the Mercado Pago Terminal settings on a store not set up yet: Support bundle with its Download support bundle button, Mercado Pago diagnostics with the line Mercado Pago access token is missing, and View logs">

1. **Diagnostics line** — *Mercado Pago access token is missing.* on this store, which isn't set up. Yours should read *Credentials accepted.*, with your terminals listed under it.
2. **View logs** — opens `WooCommerce > Status > Logs`, source `mercadopago-terminal`.
3. **Download support bundle** — a file for WCPOS support.

</JourneyShot>

The logs record each request to Mercado Pago and its reply, with the token and card numbers removed.

When a payment goes wrong, click **Download support bundle** and send the file to WCPOS support with the **order number**. WCPOS Pro adds this button: the bundle covers every POS payment provider on your site, with secrets and card numbers removed. It also says when your store last received a signed webhook.

## What it doesn't do {#scope-and-limitations}

- Tips, or authorising now and capturing later.
- Print a ticket on the terminal.
- Take payments on terminals in **STANDALONE** mode.
- Record refunds made on the terminal or in Mercado Pago.
- Convert currencies: the store and the Mercado Pago account must use the same one.
- More than 50 terminals per account.
- Set a store-wide default terminal, or limit which terminals tills may use, from WP Admin. Each till picks its own.
- HPOS (WooCommerce's order tables): the extension doesn't declare support for it yet.

<details>
<summary>How it works, for developers</summary>

The gateway uses Mercado Pago's **Orders API** for Point (`POST /v1/orders` with `type: point`), through WCPOS Pro's payment providers. Each payment creates a Mercado Pago order for the amount on the till, with the terminal ID, `print_on_terminal: no_ticket`, an external reference for the payment and an idempotency key, so a retry can't charge twice. The order expires after 5 minutes (filter `mptfwc_order_expiration_time`).

The till asks for the status every 2 seconds. The webhook, `wp-json/wcpos/v2/payments/webhook?provider=mercadopago`, must carry a valid `x-signature` (HMAC-SHA256 with the webhook secret); its body is never trusted, and the store fetches the order from Mercado Pago again. WooCommerce POS checks unanswered payments every 10 minutes as a backstop.

Terminals come from `/terminals/v1/list`; only those in PDV mode count as online. The filters `mptfwc_order_payload` and `mptfwc_poll_interval_ms` from version 0.1 are gone.

</details>
