# Authorize.net Tap to Pay

The customer taps their card on your phone, in Authorize.net's own app. You type the amount into that app yourself: the till doesn't send it. The till then finds the payment in Authorize.net and records it on the order.

This is separate from the [WCPOS app's own Tap to Pay](../../hardware/card-readers/index.mdx#tap-to-pay), which uses Stripe Terminal. Store admins: [Set up Authorize.net Tap to Pay](#setup) covers the credentials, the phone and how to check it worked.

:::info Pro Feature
Authorize.net Tap to Pay needs [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) 2.0 or later.
:::

:::warning Beta: take a test payment first
**Version 0.1.0 is a beta, and a real tap in the Authorize.net app hasn't been tried yet.** Before you take a customer's card, take a small payment on your own card and refund it.

<details>
<summary>What has and hasn't been tested</summary>

Version 0.1.0 was tested against Authorize.net's sandbox, with a card-present sale made through Authorize.net's API standing in for the tap: the sale, the match, a void, and a refund after settlement. Not yet tried: a real tap in the app, the Legacy tab's list of matching payments after a real tap, and card readers paired with the Authorize.net app. Report problems with the order number, the time of the sale and a screenshot.

</details>
:::

## Take a payment {#take-payment}

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, the till won't record a payment while the register is closed, and a card you've already taken would have to be refunded. See [Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) for the setting.
:::

1. Press **Checkout** in the cart.
2. On the **Payments** tab, tap the **Authorize.net Tap to Pay** tile (your store may have renamed it). Above the keypad the till shows **Terminal:** and your phone's name, **Authorize.net app** unless a manager changed it.
3. Press **Send … to Authorize.net Tap to Pay**, for example **Send $10.00 to Authorize.net Tap to Pay**. The till shows **Waiting for the customer on the terminal**.
4. In the Authorize.net app on your phone, enter **exactly the same amount**. Don't add a tip in the app: see [Tips](../../pos/checkout/tips.mdx).
5. The customer taps their card on the phone.
6. Wait for the till. Once it finds the payment, the till records it, and the receipt opens when nothing is left to pay.

Take the tap within 5 minutes of pressing Send. After 5 minutes the till cancels the payment, shows **Timed out — payment cancelled**, and no longer looks for it.

<details>
<summary>How the till finds the payment</summary>

While the till waits, WCPOS keeps checking Authorize.net for a payment of that amount. It records one by itself when exactly one has arrived since 30 seconds before you pressed **Send**, and **Auto-match** is on. Before recording it, WCPOS checks the payment with Authorize.net, including the amount and currency. A payment is recorded on one order only, ever.

The till lights **Sent**, **On terminal**, **Approved** and **Captured** as the payment goes through.

</details>

### Cancel a payment {#cancel}

Press **Cancel on terminal**. Cancelling in WCPOS stops nothing in the Authorize.net app, so tell the customer not to tap.

The till checks Authorize.net one last time. If exactly one new payment of that amount has arrived, it is recorded and the sale completes instead. Otherwise the payment is cancelled.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

### When the customer may have paid {#may-have-paid}

:::warning Don't take the card again
In these cases the money may already be in your Authorize.net account while the order is still unpaid. Follow the Legacy tab steps below, or call a manager.
:::

**The till keeps waiting after the customer tapped.** The till records a payment by itself only when it's sure which one is yours. Otherwise it keeps waiting, and after 5 minutes it times out. That happens when:

- the amount in the app differs from the till, for example a tip was added in the app;
- another payment of the same amount arrived at the same time, from another register or your online shop;
- a manager has turned **Auto-match** off in the gateway's settings.

The **Payments** tab can't ask you which payment is yours. The order's [**Legacy**](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx) tab can, for payments from the last 15 minutes. The cashier or a manager does this:

1. With the same order at checkout, choose the **Legacy** tab, then **Authorize.net Tap to Pay**.
2. Press **Send 10.00 USD to Authorize.net app** (on this tab the button names the phone, not the tile). Don't take the card again.
3. The till shows a list headed, for example, **2 Authorize.net payments of USD 10.00 could be this one. Pick the one you just took.**
4. Each line shows the card type, the last 4 digits, the time and the Authorize.net transaction number. Press the one with the customer's card and the time of the tap. The order is paid.

If no list appears, don't take the card again: a manager finds the payment in your Authorize.net account and [refunds it there](#refunds).

**The customer tapped after you cancelled.** Cancelling doesn't stop the app. Record the payment from the Legacy tab as above, or a manager refunds it in your Authorize.net account.

**Payment failed: The terminal confirmed a different amount.** This is not the tip case above: it appears only when Authorize.net itself reports another amount or currency for the payment the till picked, for example when the store's currency isn't on the account. The till doesn't record the payment, and adds an order note. Don't use the Legacy tab: call a manager, who finds the payment in your Authorize.net account and refunds it.

A payment stuck on the till for another reason: see [When a card reader payment gets stuck](../index.mdx#card-reader-problems).

## Refund an Authorize.net payment {#refunds}

1. In the POS, open the order's refund. See [Refunds](../../pos/refunds.mdx).
2. Choose **Refund to Authorize.net Tap to Pay** and press **Process Refund**.

Before Authorize.net settles the payment (usually overnight), only a full refund is possible, and it voids the payment. After it settles, refund all of it or part of it back to the card. A refund from the order in WP Admin works the same way.

<details>
<summary>Refund messages</summary>

- **Authorize.net settles this payment overnight. Refund the full amount to void it now, or refund part of it after it settles.** — refund all of it, or wait until tomorrow for a partial refund.
- **An identical refund was sent in the last 8 hours. Check the Authorize.net Merchant Interface before trying again.**
- **This Authorize.net payment was already voided.** — the full payment was voided before settlement.
- **Authorize.net did not confirm the refund. Check the Authorize.net Merchant Interface before trying again.**
- **Authorize.net cannot refund this payment now. Check the Authorize.net Merchant Interface.**
- **This payment was taken in the other Authorize.net environment.** — switch **Environment** back, or refund it in the Merchant Interface.
- **This payment has no Authorize.net transaction to refund.**
- **The refund amount must be more than zero.**

</details>

## Set up Authorize.net Tap to Pay {#setup}

A manager sets the gateway up once in WP Admin and signs the phone in to the Authorize.net app.

### Requirements {#requirements}

<RequirementsList>
  <RequirementItem label="Processor">Your Authorize.net merchant account must be on the TSYS processor, with Tap to Pay switched on by Authorize.net. Check this with Authorize.net before you buy phones</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Authorize.net Account">A US merchant account, its API Login ID and Transaction Key, and the Transaction Details API switched on</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Phone">As Authorize.net states it: Tap to Pay on iPhone needs an iPhone XS or newer on iOS 18 or later; Tap to Pay on Android needs Android 15 on a phone that is Tap to Pay Ready. Sign in to the Authorize.net 2.0 app with a user of the same Authorize.net account</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Currency">USD: the Authorize.net 2.0 app takes USD only</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="WCPOS">WCPOS Pro 2.0 or later, and a connection at the till</RequirementItem>
  <RequirementItem label="Server">WooCommerce 7.0+, PHP 7.4+, and a server clock that is right to within 30 seconds</RequirementItem>
</RequirementsList>

### Install the extension {#install}

In `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`, find **Authorize.net Tap to Pay for WooCommerce (beta)** and click **Install**. WCPOS installs and activates it, and turns on **Auto-update**, so new beta versions install by themselves. See [Extensions](../../extensions/index.mdx#installing-an-extension) and [Auto-updates](../../extensions/index.mdx#auto-updates).

<details>
<summary>Install from a downloaded zip instead</summary>

Download `authorize-net-terminal-for-woocommerce.zip` from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/authorize-net-terminal-for-woocommerce/releases) and upload it via `Plugins > Add Plugin`, then **Upload Plugin**. The GitHub "Source code" zip won't install.

</details>

### Get your API credentials {#credentials}

In the Authorize.net Merchant Interface:

1. Open **Account → Settings → API Credentials & Keys** and copy the **API Login ID**. Authorize.net shows a **Transaction Key** only when it's created: if you don't have yours saved, create a new one and copy it straight away. A new key replaces the old one, so if your online shop already uses this account, reuse its key or update the shop too. Use your live account's credentials for **Production**; a sandbox account's work only with **Sandbox**.
2. Open **Account → Settings → Transaction Details API** and turn it on. Without it your store can't read payments, and the till never records one.

### Enter the credentials {#enter-credentials}

Open `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` and click **Authorize.net Tap to Pay**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={1} offsets={[[410, 0], [410, 0], [410, 0], [410, 0], [-30, 0], [410, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, Authorize.net Tap to Pay settings on a store not set up yet: Title Authorize.net Tap to Pay, Environment Sandbox, empty API Login ID and Transaction Key fields reading No saved value, Auto-match ticked, Reader name Authorize.net app">

1. **Title** — the tile's name at the till.
2. **Environment** — choose **Production**. Keep **Sandbox**, the default, only to try the store side without the phone. See [Sandbox and production](#environments).
3. **API Login ID** — paste it here.
4. **Transaction Key** — paste it here. Once saved, both fields stay blank and show only the last 4 characters: leave them blank to keep the saved values. Saving doesn't test the credentials: [Check it worked](#check) does.
5. **Auto-match** — leave it ticked. Without it, the till never records a payment by itself.
6. **Reader name** — the name the till shows above the keypad.

</JourneyShot>

Click **Save changes**.

### Set up the phone {#phone}

Install the Authorize.net app on the phone and sign in with a user of the Authorize.net account whose credentials you entered: your live account for **Production**.

### Switch it on in WCPOS {#enable}

There's no **Enable** box on the gateway's settings page. Open `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={2} offsets={[[-22, 0], [50, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, POS Settings, Checkout: three rows of the Gateways table, Card switched on, Authorize.net Tap to Pay and Direct bank transfer switched off">

1. **Authorize.net Tap to Pay** — the extension's row in the **Gateways** table.
2. **Enabled** — switch it on. The till shows the tile once it reloads.

</JourneyShot>

The gateway is never offered on your online shop's checkout. See [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

### Check it worked {#check}

1. On a till, reload the POS and press **Checkout**. The **Authorize.net Tap to Pay** tile is among the ways to pay.
2. Tap it: above the keypad the till shows **Terminal:** and your **Reader name**.
3. Make sure **Environment** is **Production**, then take a small sale on your own card.
4. Refund it in full the same day: before settlement a full refund voids it.

It worked when the till records the payment by itself and opens the receipt, and the order's payment shows the Authorize.net transaction ID.

If the till waits with **Connection unstable, still trying**, check the credentials, the **Environment** and the Transaction Details API.

## Sandbox and production {#environments}

**Sandbox** is the default and uses `apitest.authorize.net`. **Production** uses `api.authorize.net` and takes real money. Credentials belong to one environment: enter the ones for the environment you chose. The store side works on Sandbox: the beta was tested there with sales made through Authorize.net's API. Authorize.net doesn't say whether its app can sign in to a sandbox account, so try a real tap on Production.

Switch **Environment** only when no till is taking an Authorize.net payment: a waiting payment fails. A payment taken in one environment can't be refunded from the till after you switch.

## Settings reference {#settings}

On `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Authorize.net Tap to Pay`:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The tile's name at the till. Default **Authorize.net Tap to Pay** |
| **Environment** | **Sandbox** (default) or **Production** |
| **API Login ID** | From the Merchant Interface: **Account → Settings → API Credentials & Keys**. Shown only as its last 4 characters once saved; leave it blank to keep it |
| **Transaction Key** | Stored on the server only and never shown again. Saved and shown like the API Login ID |
| **Auto-match** | Records the payment by itself when exactly one Authorize.net payment fits. On by default |
| **Reader name** | The name the till shows above the keypad. Default **Authorize.net app** |

A credential pasted with hidden characters is refused with **Authorize.net credentials cannot contain control characters.** Paste it again.

**Enter the Authorize.net API Login ID and Transaction Key first.** means a credential is missing: enter both and click **Save changes**.

## What it doesn't do {#scope-and-limitations}

- Send the amount to the phone: you type it into the app.
- Tips in the app: the amount must match exactly.
- Show the list of possible payments on the **Payments** tab: pick from it on the [Legacy tab](#may-have-paid).
- Physical card terminals. Readers paired with the Authorize.net app are untested.
- Manual capture, or a payment Authorize.net holds for review: the till keeps waiting.
- Non-US accounts or currencies other than USD.
- Very busy accounts: version 0.1.0 looks only at the 50 newest payments Authorize.net hasn't settled yet. If 50 or more other payments on the account (any register, your online shop included) arrive after the tap before the till finds it, the till misses it.
- Tell two registers apart: two registers taking the same amount at the same moment can each record the other's payment.
