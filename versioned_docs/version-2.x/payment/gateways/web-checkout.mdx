# Web Checkout Gateway

:::info Pro Feature
You need [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/free-vs-pro.mdx) to take payments with Web Checkout.
:::

Web Checkout lets the customer pay for a POS order on your online store's own payment page, with the payment methods your web store offers, such as an online card form or a wallet. Use it when the customer wants to pay a way the till doesn't take.

Go straight to: [Take a payment](#take-payment) · [Set it up](#set-up) · [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong)

## Take a payment {#take-payment}

Web Checkout is on the checkout's **Store checkout** tab (called **Legacy** before WCPOS 2.0.1). It needs a connection, and it charges the whole order: if a payment has already been taken, see [Part of the order is already paid](#split) first.

:::warning Stay on the checkout until you press Charge
The till doesn't watch the other tab. It closes the sale only after you press **Charge**, and only once the customer has finished on their tab, so keep the checkout open while they pay.
:::

1. Ring up the sale as usual and press **Checkout**.
2. Open the **Store checkout** tab. Your store's payment page loads inside the POS.
3. Choose **Web Checkout**. Its note and a **Pay for this order** link appear under it. The note says to click **Process Payment**: on the till, that button is **Charge**.
4. Press **Pay for this order**. Your online store's payment page for this order opens in a new browser tab; in the desktop app, it opens in your computer's web browser.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[28, 44], [30, 44], [-26, 0], [157, 94], [-42, 0], [-30, 0], [-30, 0], [230, 0], [-78, 0]]} maxWidth={1024} alt="Checkout on the Store checkout tab: the store's payment page inside the POS with the order and its gateways, Web Checkout chosen with its note and the Pay for this order link, and the Charge $45.00 button, beside the order's number and the amount remaining">

1. **Payments** tab — the tiles: Cash, Card and the gateways that have a tile of their own.
2. **Store checkout** tab (**Legacy** on older versions) — gateways that run on the store's payment page. See [Store checkout](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx).
3. **Close** — back to the cart without taking payment. The order stays open.
4. **Web Checkout** — choose it here.
5. **Pay for this order** — opens the payment page for the customer (step 4).
6. **Checkout Settings** — for a payment page that looks wrong; see [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong).
7. **Charge $45.00** — press it once the customer has finished on their tab (step 7).
8. **The order** — its number, customer and time.
9. **Remaining** — what is left to pay. Web Checkout charges the whole order, so it can't be used after another payment has been taken.

</JourneyShot>

5. Hand the screen to the customer. Their tab shows your online store's payment page for the order. They choose a payment method and press **Pay for order**, as they would on your website.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[-30, 0], [-30, 0], [-30, 0], [-30, 0]]} maxWidth={1024} alt="The online store's payment page for the order in a browser tab: a warning that this is a guest order, the order's items and total, the store's payment methods for the web with Cash on delivery chosen, and the Pay for order button">

1. **Guest order warning** — WooCommerce shows it when the browser is signed in to your store and the order has no customer account. The customer can carry on.
2. **The order** — the items and total from the till.
3. **Payment methods** — the ones switched on for your online store, such as a card form or a wallet. This store has only offline methods, with Cash on delivery chosen.
4. **Pay for order** — the customer presses it to pay with the method they chose.

</JourneyShot>

6. When the customer has finished, their tab shows **Order received**. Check its **Payment** line, leave that tab open and go back to the POS.

   :::warning Offline methods take no money
   Cash on delivery, Direct bank transfer and Check payments take no money online, but the customer has still finished on their tab, so **Charge** closes the sale with nothing collected. Make sure that is what you want before you press it.
   :::

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[722, 2]]} maxWidth={1024} alt="The online store's Order received page: the order number, date, total and Payment: Cash on delivery">

1. **Payment** — the method the customer used. Cash on delivery, Direct bank transfer or Check payments mean no money was taken. The same row shows the order number and total.

</JourneyShot>

7. Press **Charge $45.00** (it names the order's total). Once the customer has finished, the till shows the paid screen and the receipt, as for any sale. If they haven't, the till stays on this page and checks the order every 3 seconds for 2 minutes; if the customer finishes in that time, it closes the sale by itself. See [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong).
8. Close the customer's tab once the till shows the receipt.

### Part of the order is already paid {#split}

Store checkout charges the whole order, so once a payment has been taken, the Store checkout tab says **Store checkout can't be used once a payment has been taken**. You have two choices:

- **Take the rest on a tile**, such as Cash or Card, on the Payments tab. See [Split Payments](../../pos/checkout/split-payments.mdx).
- **Cancel the payments taken so far**, then use Web Checkout for the whole order. Before anything is undone, the till lists what happens to each payment: cash goes back to the customer, other payments are voided on their payment method, and one that can't be voided is refunded from the order. See [Cancelling a Payment](../../pos/checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx).

:::tip The customer wants to pay later, on their own phone
Web Checkout opens the payment page on the device running the POS. To send the customer a link they can pay from later, use [Email Invoice](./email-invoice.mdx).
:::

## Set it up {#set-up}

Someone who manages your store in WP Admin does this once, before the first Web Checkout sale. Web Checkout is a free plugin, but the POS offers it only with WCPOS Pro; without Pro, the till takes Cash and Card. It uses the payment methods your online store already has. It needs no account or keys.

<details>
<summary>Store manager: install it and switch it on</summary>

1. **Install it.** Web Checkout isn't in `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`. Download **woocommerce-pos-web-checkout-gateway.zip** (not the source-code zip) from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/web-checkout-gateway/releases), upload it in `WP Admin > Plugins > Add Plugin`, then **Upload Plugin**, and activate it.
2. **Switch it on for the POS.** In `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`, switch on **Web Checkout** and save. See [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway). It never shows at your online checkout: customers reach it only through the POS.
3. **Check your online payment methods.** The customer pays with whatever is switched on for the web in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments`. At least one must be ready to take money.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Change the name or message</summary>

The name and the note on the payment page are the gateway's **Title** and **Description**, in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Web Checkout Gateway`. The default note reads "Go to the checkout page to pay for the order. After payment, click the Process Payment button to proceed to the receipt." The button it names now reads **Charge**. To rename it for the POS only, change its **Title** under **POS settings** in the gateway's **⋮** menu in [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#payment-gateways); the note always comes from WooCommerce.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="desktop" step={4} offsets={[[-50, 0], [-50, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="WP Admin, WooCommerce settings for the Web Checkout Gateway: the Title field holding Web Checkout and the Description field holding the default note">

1. **Title** — the name in the list on the Store checkout tab.
2. **Description** — the note under Web Checkout on the payment page. Press **Save changes** after editing either one.

</JourneyShot>

</details>

<details>
<summary>For developers: how it works</summary>

The plugin adds a WooCommerce gateway with the ID `wcpos_web_checkout`. It is switched off for the online checkout in code, so only the POS can offer it. Its payment fields print the description and a link to the order's own payment URL (`$order->get_checkout_payment_url()`), opened in a new window.

The gateway takes no money itself. When **Charge** posts an order that is already paid, created by the POS, with `wcpos_web_checkout` chosen, the plugin sends the payment page to the order-received page, and the POS closes the sale from there. An unpaid order is not moved on. The source is in the [web-checkout-gateway repository](https://github.com/wcpos/web-checkout-gateway).

</details>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Web Checkout isn't in the list on the Store checkout tab.** The plugin isn't active, or Web Checkout isn't switched on in [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway). Without WCPOS Pro, the POS offers only Cash and Card.

**"No store payment methods are enabled for the POS."** Nothing is switched on for the Store checkout tab. Ask whoever looks after the store to switch Web Checkout on in Checkout Settings.

**Charge doesn't move on to the receipt.** The customer hasn't finished on their tab yet. The till checks again for 2 minutes after you press **Charge**. Before asking the customer to pay again, look at their tab: if it still shows the payment page, they haven't finished. If it shows the order confirmation, press **Charge** again, and if the till still doesn't move on, check the order in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Orders`.

**No payment method the customer can use appears on the payment page.** Your online store has none switched on that suits them. Switch one on for the web in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments`.

**The payment page inside the POS looks wrong.** A theme or plugin may be interfering with it. See [A Gateway's Payment Page Looks Wrong](../../pos/checkout/troubleshooting.mdx#checkout-settings-troubleshooting).
