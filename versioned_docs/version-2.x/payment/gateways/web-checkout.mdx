# Web Checkout Gateway

:::info Pro Feature
Web Checkout is a free plugin, but the POS offers it only with [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/free-vs-pro.mdx). Without Pro, the till takes Cash and Card.
:::

Web Checkout lets the customer pay for a POS order on your online store's own payment page, with the payment methods your web store offers, such as an online card form or a wallet. Use it when the customer wants to pay a way the till doesn't take. The order stays unpaid until they finish; then you press **Process Payment** and the till moves on to the receipt.

Go straight to: [Take a payment](#take-payment) · [Set it up](#set-up) · [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong)

## Take a payment {#take-payment}

Web Checkout is on the checkout's **Legacy** tab, because it runs on a WooCommerce payment page rather than as a tile. It needs a connection, and it charges the whole order.

:::warning Stay on the checkout until you press Process Payment
The till doesn't watch the other tab. It closes the sale only when you press **Process Payment** on an order the customer has paid, so keep the checkout open while they pay.
:::

1. Ring up the sale as usual and press **Checkout**.
2. Open the **Legacy** tab. Your store's payment page loads inside the POS.
3. Choose **Web Checkout**. Its note and a **Pay for this order** link appear under it.
4. Press **Pay for this order**. Your online store's payment page for this order opens in a new browser tab; in the desktop app, it opens in your computer's web browser.
5. Hand the screen to the customer. They choose a payment method and pay, as they would on your website.
6. When the customer has paid, their tab shows your store's order confirmation. Close that tab.
7. Back in the POS, press **Process Payment**. If the order is paid, the till shows the paid screen and the receipt, as for any sale. If it isn't, the till stays on this page.

### Part of the order is already paid {#split}

A legacy gateway charges the whole order, so once a payment has been taken, the Legacy tab says **A legacy gateway can't be used once a payment has been taken**. To use Web Checkout, [cancel the payments taken so far](../../pos/checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx) first, or take the rest on a tile. See [Split Payments](../../pos/checkout/split-payments.mdx).

:::tip The customer wants to pay later, on their own phone
Web Checkout opens the payment page on the device running the POS. To send the customer a link they can pay from later, use [Email Invoice](./email-invoice.mdx).
:::

## Set it up {#set-up}

Someone who manages your store in WP Admin does this once, before the first Web Checkout sale. Web Checkout uses the payment methods your online store already has. It needs no account or keys.

1. **Install it.** Web Checkout isn't in `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Extensions`. Download the **plugin zip asset** (not the source-code zip) from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/wcpos/web-checkout-gateway/releases), upload it in `WP Admin > Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin` and activate it.
2. **Switch it on for the POS.** In `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`, switch on **Web Checkout** and save. See [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway). It never shows at your online checkout: customers reach it only through the POS.
3. **Check your online payment methods.** The customer pays with whatever is switched on for the web in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments`. At least one must be ready to take money.

<details>
<summary>Change the name or message</summary>

The name and the note on the payment page are the gateway's **Title** and **Description**, in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Web Checkout Gateway`. The default note reads "Go to the checkout page to pay for the order. After payment, click the Process Payment button to proceed to the receipt." To rename it for the POS only, change its **Title** under **POS settings** in the gateway's **⋮** menu in [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#payment-gateways); the note always comes from WooCommerce.

</details>

<details>
<summary>For developers: how it works</summary>

The plugin adds a WooCommerce gateway with the ID `wcpos_web_checkout`. It is switched off for the online checkout in code, so only the POS can offer it. Its payment fields print the description and a link to the order's own payment URL (`$order->get_checkout_payment_url()`), opened in a new window.

The gateway takes no money itself. When **Process Payment** posts an order that is already paid, created by the POS, with `wcpos_web_checkout` chosen, the plugin sends the payment page to the order-received page, and the POS closes the sale from there. An unpaid order is not moved on. The source is in the [web-checkout-gateway repository](https://github.com/wcpos/web-checkout-gateway).

</details>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Web Checkout isn't in the list on the Legacy tab.** The plugin isn't active, or Web Checkout isn't switched on in [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway). Without WCPOS Pro, the POS offers only Cash and Card.

**"No legacy gateways are enabled for the POS."** No gateway without a tile is switched on. Switch Web Checkout on in Checkout Settings.

**Process Payment doesn't move on to the receipt.** The order isn't paid yet. Ask the customer to finish on the payment page, wait until it says the order is received, then press **Process Payment** again.

**The customer can't pay on the payment page.** Your online store has no payment method the customer can use. Switch one on for the web in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments`.

**The payment page inside the POS looks wrong.** A theme or plugin may be interfering with it. See [A Gateway's Payment Page Looks Wrong](../../pos/checkout/troubleshooting.mdx#checkout-settings-troubleshooting).
