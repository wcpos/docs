Each payment method your store offers at the till appears as a tile on the [checkout](/pos/checkout) tender pane, or on its Legacy tab. Cash and Card come with every store; other WooCommerce gateways need WCPOS Pro.

## Default Payment Methods {#default-payment-methods}

The free plugin offers two payment methods, Cash and Card, and no others. Both are recorded at the till, so a sale paid with them completes even when the store can't be reached.

### Cash {#cash}

- Enter the amount handed over; the change is shown as you type
- The only method that gives change (with Pro, WooCommerce's cash on delivery does too)
- Several cash payments can go on one order
- No additional configuration required

### Card {#card}

- For a card terminal that isn't connected to the POS
- Take the payment on the terminal, then record it at the till
- No direct integration required

## Additional Payment Gateways (Pro) {#additional-payment-gateways-pro}

:::info Pro Feature
Additional payment gateways require [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/pro-license).
:::

With Pro, you can enable any installed WooCommerce gateway for the POS. How it works at the till depends on the gateway:

- **Recorded at the till** — WooCommerce's cash on delivery, bank transfer and cheque gateways are recorded by the cashier, like Card, and complete offline.
- **Card readers** — terminal gateways such as **Stripe Terminal** (including Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay) and **SumUp** are driven from the tender pane. A terminal gateway that hasn't been built for the tender pane is offered on the Legacy tab instead.
- **Pay-later gateways** — such as [Email Invoice](./gateways/email-invoice.mdx), which sends an invoice and leaves the order unpaid until the customer pays it.
- **Any other WooCommerce gateway** — appears on the **Legacy** tab, which opens the gateway's own payment page inside the POS. A legacy gateway charges the whole order, so it can't be part of a [split payment](/pos/checkout#split-payments).

Refunds for gateways that support them run on your store, against the payment they apply to. See [Refunds](/orders/refunds).

## Enabling and Disabling Gateways {#enabling-and-disabling-gateways}

Payment gateways are managed in the WordPress admin:

1. Go to `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`
2. You'll see a list of all installed WooCommerce payment gateways
3. Toggle each gateway on or off for the POS independently of your online store
4. Drag to reorder — the order is the order of the tiles at the till

This means a gateway can be active on your website but disabled in the POS, or vice versa. See [Checkout Settings](/settings/wp-admin/checkout) for full details.

## Testing Payments {#testing-payments}

Before going live with a new gateway:

1. **Cash gateway** — always available for testing the checkout flow without real transactions.
2. **Stripe Terminal** — Stripe provides a [simulated reader](https://docs.stripe.com/terminal/testing) for test mode. Enable test mode in your Stripe dashboard first.
3. **Third-party gateways** — check the gateway's own documentation for sandbox/test mode instructions. Most WooCommerce gateways support a test mode toggle.

:::tip
If a newly enabled gateway doesn't appear at checkout, try refreshing the POS. Gateway changes in WP Admin take effect on the next page load.
:::

## Common Issues {#common-issues}

### Gateway Not Appearing at Checkout {#gateway-not-appearing-at-checkout}

- Confirm the gateway is enabled for POS in `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`.
- Check that the gateway's plugin is installed and active. The gateway doesn't have to be switched on in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments` to work in the POS.
- Gateways other than Cash and Card need WCPOS Pro.
- Look on the **Legacy** tab: a gateway that hasn't been built for the tender pane is listed there.
- Refresh the POS after making changes.

A tile that is shown but can't be used is listed with its reason, for example **Needs a connection** while you're offline, or a prompt to update the app.

### Gateway Displays Incorrectly {#gateway-displays-incorrectly}

This applies to gateways on the **Legacy** tab, whose payment page loads your site's theme styles and scripts. Use the **Checkout Settings** button on that page to selectively disable styles or scripts. See [Checkout Troubleshooting](/pos/checkout#checkout-settings-troubleshooting) for details.

### Payment Processing Fails {#payment-processing-fails}

- Check your site's error logs (`WP Admin > POS > Support > Logs`) for gateway-specific errors.
- For a card reader, open the payment's log on the tender pane; it shows each step the reader reported.
- Ensure your SSL certificate is valid — most payment gateways require HTTPS.
- If using a staging or local environment, confirm the gateway supports test/sandbox mode.
