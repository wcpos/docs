Reports shows what a till, a store or every store sold over a period: the total and how it compares, then cards for payments, products, cashiers, taxes and refunds. Open it from **Reports** in the side menu. Everything is worked out on the device from the orders it has downloaded.

:::info Free and Pro
On the free plugin, Reports shows today on this device's register. [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/free-vs-pro#at-a-glance) opens earlier days, other registers, every store and the closures history.
:::

## Read the sales {#sales}

The top of the screen answers "how did we do?" for the period, the store and the register in the bar.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[-22, 0], [-22, 0], [-22, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [-24, 0], [-22, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="Reports on a tablet for yesterday across all registers: the scope in the bar, the till strip, the period, cashier and status chips, Print summary, the $520.00 total with Orders, Average order and Items, the vs yesterday chip and the By hour chart" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Scope** — the store and register the figures cover. See [Choose the store and register](#scope).
2. **Till strip** — this device's register: **Open** or **Closed**, since when and who opened it, and the drawer sum **Opening float + Cash sales − Cash refunds = Expected in drawer**. **Paid in** and **Paid out** join the sum when the session has any. **Print X-report** prints the open session; a closed register offers **Print closure** instead. The arrow at the end opens the closures history (Pro). See [Reconciliation](./reconciliation.mdx). A device with no register reads **No register on this device**.
3. **Period** — the days the figures cover. See [Choose the period](#dates).
4. **Cashier** — **Everyone**, or one cashier's sales.
5. **Status** — **Completed & processing** counts the orders that are sold. **Every status** adds pending and on hold orders.
6. **Print summary** — prints a **Sales summary** of the period. See [Print or export](#print).
7. **Total** — the sales for the period, and under it the change against the comparison, as a percentage. Red is down, green is up.
8. **Orders, Average order, Items** — each with its own change against the comparison. A **—** means there is nothing to compare with.
9. **Comparison** — what the changes are measured against. For a single day, choose **vs yesterday** or **vs last** and the weekday. See [How comparisons work](#comparisons).
10. **Chart** — **By hour** for a day, **By day** for longer periods, or **Running total** to watch the period add up. The dashed line is the period you compare with.

</div>

## Choose the period {#dates}

Tap the period to open the presets and the calendar.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} alt="The period menu: Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last Week, This Month and Last Month beside a November calendar with the 6th selected, and the Done button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Today** and **Yesterday** — one day, compared with the day before or the same weekday last week.
2. **This Week** and **Last Week** — a calendar week. This Week stops at today.
3. **This Month** and **Last Month** — a calendar month. This Month stops at today.
4. **Calendar** — tap a day for that day, or two days for the range between them.
5. **Done** — applies the days you tapped.

</div>

On the free plugin, every period but **Today** carries a lock. Tapping one shows **Earlier days are in WCPOS Pro** with a **See Pro** button.

### How comparisons work {#comparisons}

- **A day** is compared with yesterday or with the same weekday last week, whichever you choose on the comparison chip.
- **Today** is a live day, so it is compared at the same time of day: at 3pm, with yesterday up to 3pm.
- **A week**, or any range of up to seven days, is compared with the same days a week earlier. The chip reads **vs the week before** and can't be changed.
- **A whole calendar month** is compared with the whole month before. **This Month**, and any range longer than seven days, is compared with the same number of days starting a month earlier. The chip reads **vs the month before**.
- A **—** under a figure means the comparison period has no orders.

## Choose the store and register {#scope}

Tap the scope in the bar.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} alt="The scope menu with a Store group (All stores, AL Store, AL 12345 Store) and a Register group (All registers, Register)" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **All stores** — every store in one report. It appears only with more than one store.
2. **A store** — one store's sales.
3. **All registers** — every register in the store, and orders taken online.
4. **A register** — one till's sales.

</div>

On the free plugin, the store and register you're signed in to are the only ones you can open. The others carry a lock, and choosing one shows **Other registers are in WCPOS Pro** (or **Other stores**, or **All stores**) with **See Pro**.

The cashier and status chips below the period narrow the figures further. **Everyone** opens a list of the cashiers who have signed in on this device. On the Cashiers card, a cashier who never has shows as a number, such as **#8**.

## Read the cards {#cards}

Scroll down for the cards. Each one opens a panel with the full list.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[-26, 0], [-26, 0], [-26, 0], [-26, 0], [-26, 0]]} alt="The report cards for This Week: Orders with its averages and a status bar, Payments by method, Where sold split between In store and Online, and Top products" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Orders** — **Average order**, **Median**, **Largest**, **Items Sold**, **Items per order** and **Discounts**, then a bar of **Completed**, **Processing** and **Refunded** orders.
2. **Needs you** — how many orders are still processing, on hold or pending. **Nothing needs you** when none are.
3. **Payments** — sales by payment method, with each method's share. **UNPAID** is money an order still owes. **Unknown** is an order that records no payment method.
4. **Where sold** — **In store** against **Online**. Switch it to **Registers**, or to **Stores** when the scope is **All stores**. An order with no register reads **No register**.
5. **Top products** — the best sellers by sales, with each one's share and how many sold.

</div>

Further down:

- **Categories** — sales by product category. **Uncategorised** is a product in no category. **Unknown product** is a line whose product isn't on this device, and a note says how many lines that is, such as **58 of 117 lines have no local product**.
- **Brands** — sales by brand, or **No brand**. It appears when your products carry brands, or when the store tracks cost of goods.
- **Cashiers** — sales per cashier.
- **Taxes** — tax per rate, each **on** the net sales it was charged on.
- **Refunds** — how much was refunded, on how many of the period's orders, and how much you kept.

## Open a panel {#panels}

Tap a card's arrow. On a tablet the panel slides in beside the cards. On a phone it opens as its own page, and **Sales** at the top takes you back.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[-18, 0], [0, 0], [-18, 0], [30, 26], [0, 0]]} alt="The Top products panel for This Week: All categories filter, the report preview with Qty, Amount, Cost, Profit and Margin % columns, a status line saying cost is missing on 134 of 134 items, and Close, Export CSV and Print report" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Scope** — the period and register the panel covers.
2. **Filter** — Top products narrows to one category (**All categories**), Sales by brand to one brand.
3. **Status line** — the orders and the total. With cost of goods on, it also says how many items have no cost. See [Cost, profit and margin](#margin).
4. **Export CSV** — saves the list as a CSV file. In a browser it downloads; in the app it opens the share sheet.
5. **Print report** — prints the list with the **Template** and **Printer** shown above the preview.

</div>

The panels are **Orders**, **Sales by payment method**, **Top products**, **Sales by category**, **Sales by brand**, **Sales by cashier**, **Taxes collected** (net, tax and gross per rate), **Refunds**, and **Channels**, **Registers** or **Stores** from Where sold.

## Leave orders out {#leave-out}

A test sale or a staff purchase can skew the day. Open the **Orders** panel and untick the orders to leave out. Every figure on the screen is worked out again without them.

A chip next to the status then reads, for example, **2 orders left out**. Tap it to get back to the Orders panel, or tap **Put them back** to count them again. Leaving an order out only changes this report on this device: the order itself is untouched. Untick every order and the Orders card reads **Every order is left out**.

## Print or export {#print}

- **Print summary** at the top prints the **Sales summary**: the period's figures as they stand, with any orders you left out kept out.
- **Print report** in a panel prints that panel's list.
- **Export CSV** in a panel saves that list.

Printing uses your [receipt templates](../receipts/index.mdx) and the printer you chose. While the report or a template is still on its way, the print sheet says so: **Waiting for the store**, **Loading the report**, **Still loading orders for this range** or **Loading templates**.

## Cost, profit and margin {#margin}

When your store tracks [cost of goods](../products/index.mdx#cost-of-goods-sold), the Top products, Sales by category and Sales by brand panels add **Cost**, **Profit** and **Margin %** columns. A product with no cost shows **—** there, and the status line says how many items are missing one, such as **cost missing on 3 of 40 items**, so you know how far to trust the margin.

## Closures {#closures}

The till strip shows this device's register. With WCPOS Pro, its arrow opens **Closures**: every numbered closure of the registers you can see, to reprint, recount or check. **Export CSV** there saves the closures listed. **Export closures** under **Tools** in `WP Admin > POS > Settings` downloads every closure. The day's procedure is in [Reconciliation](./reconciliation.mdx).

## Longer ranges in WooCommerce Analytics {#woocommerce-analytics}

For order-level exports or any date range, use `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Analytics`. It reads the same orders. With WCPOS Pro:

- The **Orders** report's order-type filter adds **POS Orders** and **Online Orders**.
- The **Revenue** report adds **Store**, **Cashier** and **Payment Method** filters, each once there is more than one.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**"Report access is not permitted".** Your user doesn't have the *view reports* capability. See [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).

**The figures look low.** Check the status chip: **Completed & processing** leaves out pending and on hold orders. Check for a **left out** chip, then the scope and cashier.

**A product shows as Unknown product.** That product isn't on this device, so its line can't be given a category. The sales still count in the total.

**A cashier shows as a number.** That cashier has never signed in on this device. The sales are theirs; only the name is missing.

**Print waits.** **No template can print offline · manage templates in WP Admin** means this device holds no template it can print offline. Templates are managed in WP Admin. **The saved printer cannot print this template** means the template and the printer don't match: pick another printer in the sheet.

**"Could not print. Try again." or "Export failed. Try again."** Try again. If printing keeps failing, check the printer in the sheet.
