This page is for whoever balances the day: the close rules to set once, the day at the till in order, then the closures in **Reports**, where you check, recount and export them, and what to do when the drawer is out. The count itself is walked screen by screen in [Close the register](../pos/reconciliation.mdx).

1. [Set the close rules](#before-your-first-close) once.
2. [Run the day at the till](#opening-the-till): open, move cash, count and close.
3. [Check the closures](#closures) in Reports, [recount](#recount) one if the count was wrong, and [export](#export) them.
4. [Find a variance](#variance-handling) when the drawer is short or over.

:::note This page needs the register switched on
Everything here depends on **Open and close the register**. With it off, the POS sells without a register session and there's nothing to count or close. See [Registers](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register).
:::

:::info Free and Pro
Opening, counting and closing the register, the numbered closure and **Export closures** in WP Admin are in the free plugin. On the free plugin, Reports shows today on this device's register and its last closure. [WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/free-vs-pro.mdx#at-a-glance) adds the **Closures** view: earlier days, every register and every store.
:::

## Before your first close {#before-your-first-close}

Decide these once, then keep them across shifts. They live in `WP Admin > POS > Settings > General`, under **Register** ([Registers](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register)); with Pro, on each store's **Registers** page.

- **Opening float.** A typical till starts the day with $100–$200 in small notes and coins for change. Set it as the **Opening float** and it fills in the amount when a cashier opens the register. The opening card also offers the **last count**, what was counted at the last close.
- **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than …** A count that is out by more than this needs a manager's approval before the register closes. Off, any difference closes without one.
- **Remind the cashier to close at …** Past this time, the register shows **Overdue** and the panel's button reads **Close register · it's past** the time.
- **Who counts and who approves.** A cashier with the *view reports* capability sees the expected cash while counting. A cashier without it counts **blind**: no expected figure and no difference, and no closure details. An approval needs a manager account that is neither the cashier closing nor the one who opened the register. See [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).
- **One drawer, one register.** Every device on one counter shares the register and its session, so the drawer is counted once. A second cash drawer is a second register (Pro). See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx).

## Open the register {#opening-the-till}

Put the float in the drawer. The cart shows **Open register** with **Cash in the drawer** filled in. Count the float, correct the amount if it differs, and press **Open register**. While it's closed the POS is price check only; the card's **Last closure** row opens the register panel with the last closure. Step by step: [Open the register](../pos/register/index.mdx#open).

A register already open on another device doesn't ask for a float: this device joins that session ([REGISTER221](../error-codes/REGISTER221.mdx)).

## During the shift {#during-the-shift}

Tap your avatar in the bar, then **In the drawer**, to open the **register panel**. It's the only way in: the bar has no register button. The panel shows the cash **in the drawer**, **Session sales**, **Paid in**, **Paid out** and **No sale**, the session from the float to **Expected in the drawer**, then **Print X-report** and **Close register**. When there are any, it also lists **Other tenders** (card and every non-cash method) and the **Last closure**. See [Move cash in and out](../pos/register/index.mdx#cash-movements) for the labelled panel.

- **Cash that moves without a sale.** **Paid in** for cash going into the drawer, **Paid out** for cash leaving it (a drop to the safe, a supplier paid from the till). Each needs an amount and a reason. A movement entered by mistake: **Void** on its row while the register is open.
- **Open the drawer for change.** Press **No sale**. The sheet asks **Why open the drawer?**: pick **Change**, **Mistake** or **Drawer check**, or **Other…** and type a reason, then press **Open the drawer**. No money moves, but the opening is counted: the closure lists **No sales** and **Drawer openings**.
- **Check the drawer mid-shift.** **Print X-report** prints the session as it stands, without closing anything. The till strip at the top of **Reports** shows the same sum: **Opening float + Cash sales − Cash refunds = Expected in drawer**, with **Paid in** and **Paid out** when the session has any ([The till strip](./index.mdx#closures)).
- **Park orders rather than abandon them.** A parked order shows up in [Orders](../orders/index.mdx); an abandoned cart leaves no trail. Use coupons or line [discounts](../pos/cart/discounts.mdx) the same way every time, so the closure reads the same.
- **Hand over without closing.** Tap your avatar, then pick the next cashier under **Hand the till to**, or **Another account…** to sign one in. Each name shows how many open carts they have. The drawer stays open and the POS reloads as them. See [Your cashier sheet](../pos/register/index.mdx#cashier-sheet).

## Count and close {#the-close-procedure}

Finish the last sale, then press **Close register** in the register panel. While you count, the products read **Counting the drawer. Items can be added after the count.** The **‹** at the top goes back to selling if a customer walks up; count again before you close.

<JourneyShot manifest={eodManifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[10, 30], [196, 0], [-31, 0], [338, 0], [209, 0], [134, 0], [61, -34]]} alt="Counting the drawer: the products dimmed behind Counting the drawer, the counted cash, the expected cash with the drawer short, the note, Count by notes and coins, Other tenders and Close & print" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **‹ Back to selling** — leave the count and sell again.
2. **Cash counted, float included** — what's in the drawer.
3. **Expected** — what the register expects, and how far your count is **short** or **over**, or **Exact**. A cashier who counts blind doesn't see it.
4. **Note** — **Explain any difference**. The note is kept on the closure.
5. **Count by notes and coins** — tap a note or coin once for each one you count. The amount becomes their total; **Clear** starts again.
6. **Other tenders** — a **Card counted** box, and one for each other payment method, for the total from the terminal or the slips.
7. **Close & print** — writes the closure. It reads **Approve & close** when the difference is over your limit.

</div>

Expected cash is worked out for you:

> **Expected cash = Opening float + Cash sales − Cash refunds + Paid in − Paid out**

1. **Approval, if the count is over the limit.** The button reads **Approve & close**, and a manager enters their WordPress username and password on this device. The store refuses the cashier who is closing and the cashier who opened the register. A blind count isn't exempt: the cashier never sees the difference, but the store checks it and asks. Approval needs a connection. See [REGISTER301](../error-codes/REGISTER301.mdx) if it's refused.
2. **The closure.** The sheet says **Closure N written** with what you counted against what was expected. Press **Print closure** for the closing report (the Z-report); once printed, the sheet says **Printed on** and the time. Press **Done**. See [File the closure](../pos/reconciliation.mdx#closure).
3. **Match the card.** Run the batch total on the card terminal and compare it with the **Card** line under **Other tenders** on the closure. Settle the batch now if your terminal doesn't do it for you. For a hosted checkout such as Square, see [Web Checkout](../payment/gateways/web-checkout.mdx).
4. **Drop the cash.** Put tomorrow's float back in the drawer and bag the rest with the printed closure for the safe.

## Check the closures {#closures}

Open **Reports**. The till strip at the top shows this device's register. After the close it reads **Closed**, then **Last closure** with its number and the **Drawer result**, and its button prints the closure again (**Print closure**). On the free plugin, **Last closure** opens that closure. With Pro, the arrow at the end of the strip opens **Closures**: every register session and closure you can see.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **All** — every session in the period, open and closed.
2. **Open** — the register sessions still running.
3. **Closed** — the numbered closures only.
4. **Sessions** — how many sessions, open and closed.
5. **Cash at open** — the floats.
6. **Cash activity** — cash sales less cash refunds, plus or minus paid in and paid out.
7. **Cash at close** — what was counted, and how many counts.
8. **Discrepancy** — the period's differences added up, with how many counts were **short**, **over** and **exact**.
9. **Open session** — a register still open, since when, by whom and how many sales, with its **Expected** cash. **Print X-report** prints it as it stands.
10. **A closure** — **Closure N** and its register, opened → closed and who closed it, the cash counted and **Exact**, short or over. Tap it to [read the closure](#closure-detail).
11. **Export CSV** — saves the closures listed. See [Export closures](#export).

- Each day has a heading with the cash counted that day and the day's result. The list loads 50 at a time; **Load more** at the end loads the next 50.
- The period, the store and register, and **All cashiers** at the top narrow the list, as on the [Reports](./index.mdx#scope) screen. Closures go back 92 days.
- A closure's badge says what happened to it after the count: **Corrected** (recounted or given a late sale or movement), **Unsynced** (not yet on the store) or **Carried to** another store.
- Closures need a connection. Offline, the view says **Unavailable offline**, and a closure this device has never opened can't be opened.

### Read a closure {#closure-detail}

Tap a closure. On a tablet it opens beside the list, and **×** or **Close** goes back to it.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[112, -26], [112, -26], [112, -26], [370, 0], [112, -170], [195, 0]]} alt="Closure 31 open beside the list: the store, register, times and closer, Counted, Expected and a 90 dollar short result, the cashier's note, the drawer from the float to the counted cash, the card total under Other tenders, and Recount, Close and Print closure" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Counted** — the cash counted at the close. A blind cashier sees only this figure.
2. **Expected** — what the register expected.
3. **Result** — **Exact**, short or over.
4. **Note** — the cashier's note from the count.
5. **Drawer** — every line from the **Float** (when and by whom the register opened) through cash sales, cash refunds, paid in and paid out to **Expected in the drawer** and **Counted**.
6. **Recount…**, **Close** and **Print closure** — [recount](#recount) the drawer, close the panel, or print the closure again.

</div>

Further down the closure:

- **Other tenders** — the total for each other payment method, such as **Card**.
- **Session** — **Cashiers**, **Sales**, **Items sold**, **Discounts**, **Average sale** and **Drawer openings**.
- **Closure paper** — **printed once**, **printed N times** or **not printed yet**.
- **Late corrections** — a sale, refund or movement that reached the store after the closure was written. The closure is never rewritten: the correction sits beside it.
- **Corrections** — each **Recount**, **Late sale** and **Late movement**, with **Approved by** and the manager's name. A line above says what was first recorded and where it settled, such as **Settled at … after 1 recount**.

### Recount a closure {#recount}

When the count was wrong (a bag of coins found the next morning, a note stuck in the drawer), recount the closure instead of changing the next float. Press **Recount…** on the closure.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[170, 0], [170, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [60, -38]]} alt="The Recount sheet over closure 31: cash counted and card counted boxes, Count by notes and coins, Reason for recount, Manager approval with WordPress username and password, and Record count, greyed out until the form is filled" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **cash counted** — the cash you counted this time.
2. **card counted** — one box like it for each other payment method on the closure.
3. **Count by notes and coins** — the same counter as at the close.
4. **Reason for recount** — required, up to 500 characters.
5. **Record count** — saves the recount. It stays greyed out until there's an amount and a reason.

</div>

Below the reason, **Manager approval** asks for a manager's WordPress username and password. A user who can manage closures doesn't see it.

The original figures stay on record beside the new ones, and the closure shows **Corrected**. Recounting needs a connection and a closure that has reached the store: offline the button says **Connect to recount**, and an unsynced closure says **Sync this closure to recount**.

### Export closures {#export}

- **From the POS (Pro).** **Export CSV** in the Closures view saves the closures listed, for the period, store, register and cashier you chose, as `closures-<from>-<to>.csv`. In a browser it downloads; in the app it opens the share sheet. Each row has the business day, closure number, register, store, opened, closed and who closed it, then expected, counted and variance for each payment method, and **Unsynced** or **Corrected**. It's off while the **Open** tab is selected: open sessions aren't closures yet.
- **From WP Admin (free and Pro).** `WP Admin > POS > Settings`, under **Tools**, **Export closures**, then **Download CSV**. It downloads every closure for the stores you can reach, with no date range, exactly as recorded at the close: number, register, business day, float, counted and expected totals, variance, each payment method and tax rate, and running totals. It needs a user who can manage the POS and view reports.

## Variance handling {#variance-handling}

If counted cash doesn't match expected, work through these in order before closing:

1. **Recount the drawer.** Recount, especially the coins, and enter the new figure. After the close, a manager can still [recount the closure](#recount) in Reports; the original figures stay on record beside the corrected ones.
2. **Check that every device has synced.** A sale that hasn't reached the store yet isn't in the expected figure. The count warns **N sales not yet on the server — the closure will record them**: you can still close, and they're added beside the closure as **Late corrections** when they arrive. On a device that joined the session, **Figures arrive with the next sync** keeps **Close register** off until the figures are in. Bring every device online before you count.
3. **Check for parked orders.** Filter [Orders](../orders/index.mdx) to *POS - Open*: a sale rung up but not paid shows here. Complete it now or expect the variance.
4. **Paid in and paid out.** Confirm every cash drop and payout is in the register panel. A movement the store refused is listed at the top of the panel with **Retry** (see [REGISTER101](../error-codes/REGISTER101.mdx)).
5. **Refunds.** A cash refund given in WP Admin instead of the POS isn't in the drawer sum. See [Refunds](../orders/refunds.mdx).
6. **Wrong change given.** The most common cause. Small differences (under $5) are usually here; large ones need looking into.

The variance is recorded on the closure whether you find the cause or not. A trail of small documented variances is better than untracked ones.

## Several cashiers or stores {#multi-cashier-or-multi-store-scenarios}

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Several cashiers worked the same till today">

The register has one session however many cashiers sell on it, so the drawer is counted and closed once. The session records who opened it and the closure records who closed it. Each cashier sees their own figure under **Your sales today** on their cashier sheet, and the closure's **Session** band lists the **Cashiers**.

To pass the till on, use **Hand the till to** on the cashier sheet: the drawer stays open, and open carts wait for their cashier. For a separate count per cashier, close the register at the handover and let the next cashier open it again with a float. Each close is its own numbered closure.

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="One cashier worked at multiple stores today">

Each store has its own register, so each one is opened and closed on its own. Use **Switch** beside **Store** on the cashier sheet to move between stores, and close the register in each store at the end of the day.

With Pro, the store and register menu at the top of Reports switches between registers and stores, so you can review each store's closures from one device.

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="Each store has a different person closing">

That's the normal pattern. Each closer counts and closes their own store's register. With Pro, a manager can review every register's closures the next morning in the **Closures** view, for every store they can reach.

  </AccordionItem>

A sale or refund that reaches the store after the closure was written is recorded as a late correction against that closure. The closure itself is never rewritten: the correction sits beside it, and the closure shows **Corrected**, so the closing report and the settled figure can both be read.

## Best practices {#best-practices}

- **Close every day.** Skipping a day means tracking a variance across two days, which is much harder.
- **Keep the printed closing report**, even on quiet days. The closure is also kept in WCPOS and can be exported as CSV.
- **Don't share user accounts.** Each cashier on their own WordPress user means the closure names the right people.
- **Cross-check weekly against WooCommerce Analytics** (`WP Admin > WooCommerce > Analytics`): same orders, different view. If the totals differ, a parked order was usually completed late or a refund was given in WP Admin rather than the POS.
- **Keep the float the same.** Set an **Opening float** so every opening starts from the same figure.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**"Unavailable offline" in Closures.** The Closures view and recounts need the store. Check the connection; a closure this device has opened before can still be read.

**Recount… is greyed out.** **Connect to recount** means the device is offline. **Sync this closure to recount** means the closure hasn't reached the store yet: wait for the sync.

**"Closure details are restricted while blind counting is on".** A cashier who counts blind can't open closures. A manager reads them.

**"This closure is outside available history".** Closures in the POS go back 92 days. Older ones are in the WP Admin export.

**"Export failed. Try again."** Try again. In WP Admin, **Your session has expired** means reload the page and sign in again; **Check that you have permission to view reports** means your user can't export closures. See [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).

:::info With WCPOS Pro
Pro adds more registers per store, earlier days and the **Closures** view with recounts and **Export CSV** in the POS. See [More registers with Pro](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#more-registers-with-pro).
:::
