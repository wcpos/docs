Each till keeps its own copy of your products, so the product list is fast and sales keep going when the internet drops. The POS keeps that copy up to date by itself. On a new device, while you're online, search finds products that haven't downloaded yet, so you can start selling straight away.

Here for something specific?

- [A price or stock level is out of date](#refresh) — tap the sync arrow.
- [A product is missing from the list](#goes-wrong)
- [A new device is still downloading](#first-download)
- [**Something went wrong** when the POS opens](#boot)

## Sync now, or start over {#refresh}

The sync arrow sits beside the product count, at the bottom right of the product list.

- **Tap the sync arrow** to check your store for changes now, instead of waiting for the [next check](#changes).
- **Press and hold it** for two options: **Sync** does the same as a tap, and **Clear and Refresh** clears the products on this device and downloads them again.

**Clear and Refresh** starts at once, with no confirm. It also clears the search and resets the filters. It clears products and their variations only: orders and customers stay. The first products come back right away, and the rest download in the background, as on a [new device](#first-download).

Changes waiting to send aren't touched: a stock or price edit that hasn't reached your store yet is kept in the queue and still sends. To see what is waiting, open [Store health → Database](#progress).

[Store health](../support/store-health.mdx#clear-and-redownload) can also clear and download any one kind of record again, with a confirm first. To wipe everything on a device, see [Clear All Local Data](../support/troubleshooting/clear-local-data.mdx); you rarely need it.

## When a product is missing or out of date {#goes-wrong}

<span id="faq"></span>

- **A product isn't in the list** — check the filters first. **In Stock** is on by default and hides products with no stock; see [Show out-of-stock products](../pos/product-panel/display-settings.mdx#show-out-of-stock-products). Then search for it by name or SKU: search asks your store for anything not on this device.
- **A product never shows, even in search** — it may be marked online only, and those are never sent to the POS. See [POS Only Products](./pos-only-products.mdx).
- **A price or stock level is out of date** — wait for the next check, or [tap the sync arrow](#refresh). A device that's offline keeps its old copy until it's back online.
- **A barcode won't scan while offline** — only products already on this device scan offline. The message names the code and says it isn't on this device.
- **Something went wrong when the POS opens** — see [When the POS starts](#boot).

## On a new device {#first-download}

When you sign in on a new device, the POS opens straight away with the first page of products, in the order your product list is sorted. Searching asks your store for anything that isn't on the device yet, so you can find and sell any product from the start.

The rest of the catalogue downloads in the background, a few products at a time, once the till has been idle for about a minute:

1. The products you're looking at, in the order you have them sorted. Changing the sort starts this again in the new order.
2. Then every other product, out of stock ones included, so the till can sell anything even when it's offline.

On a large catalogue this can take a while, and it never gets in the way of a sale. Once every product is on the device, search answers from the device instead of asking your store.

:::info Offline before the download finishes
Offline, the till only sells and scans what is already on the device. If it goes offline before its first products arrive, it can't sell until it's back online.
:::

## How far along it is {#progress}

Open **Store health** from the pulse icon in the menu. The **Database** tab compares what is on this device with what is in your store.

The other rows fill in as the POS needs them, so a count below your store's total is normal on a new till. See [What's on this device](../support/store-health.mdx#coverage).

## How changes reach the till {#changes}

A till that's online checks your store for changes about once a minute, and downloads only what changed: a new price, new stock, a new or deleted product. You don't need to do anything.

To make the checks more or less frequent, choose a preset in [Store health → Performance](../support/store-health.mdx#sync-presets). **Balanced** checks every minute and is the default. **Eco** checks every five minutes, to go easy on a slow server. **Realtime** checks every ten seconds. The preset is set on each device separately.

When the till is offline, sales keep working and are sent once it's back online. Changes made in your store in the meantime arrive at the next check.

## When the POS starts {#boot}

Each time the POS opens, it shows the logo and a progress bar.

<div style={{ maxWidth: 360 }}>

![The boot screen: the POS logo with a blue progress bar below it, about 40% full](/img/2.x/products/sync/en/tablet-light-regular--s1-1-boot-screen.png)

</div>

The bar tracks the POS opening this device's own database and your sign-in. It doesn't track products: they download after the POS has opened, while you work. The screen stays light even when the POS uses a dark theme.

If the POS can't start, it shows **Something went wrong** instead:

- **Try again** — starts again and keeps everything on this device. Use this first.
- **Reset app data and restart** — deletes everything stored on this device and starts over, after you confirm. Your sales download again only if they reached your store. The confirm says how many changes have never reached your store; any it counts are lost for good.

:::warning Try again first
Reset only if **Try again** keeps failing. If the confirm counts changes that never reached your store, or says it couldn't check, ask for help before you reset. Sales that exist only on this device can't be recovered.
:::
