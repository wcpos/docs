:::info Pro Feature
The Products screen needs [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/pro-license). Without Pro, the screen shows your products under a blur, with **Upgrade to Pro** and **View Demo**. You can still sell every product from the [Product Panel](../pos/product-panel/index.mdx).
:::

The Products screen is where you look after your catalogue from the till: change stock and prices, fix a barcode, or check what a product looks like in your store. Open it from **Products** in the menu. Every change goes to your store, so it shows in WooCommerce and on every other till.

Go straight to: [Change stock](#inline-stock-editing) · [Change prices](#inline-price-editing) · [Edit a product](#product-actions) · [Edit a variation](#edit-variation)

## Products screen or Product Panel {#products-screen-vs-pos-product-panel}

To add products to a sale, use the [Product Panel](../pos/product-panel/index.mdx). To change your catalogue, open **Products**; this needs WCPOS Pro.

## Find a product {#search--filters}

<span id="filtering-products"></span>

- **Search** — type a name, SKU or barcode. Search asks your store for anything that isn't on this device yet.
- **Scan** — with a barcode scanner, scan a product. The POS shows **Barcode scanned** with the code and how many products match, and puts the code in the search field. Which field counts as the barcode is set in [Barcode Field](../settings/wp-admin/general.mdx#barcode-field).
- **Filters** — the buttons under the search field narrow the list: **In Stock**, **Featured**, **On Sale**, **Category**, **Tags** and **Brands**. **Category**, **Tags** and **Brands** open a list to pick from. Press the cross on an active filter to take it off.

### Sort and resize the columns {#sorting}

<span id="product-list"></span>

Tap a column heading to sort by it, and tap it again to reverse the order. Drag the right edge of a heading to make the column wider or narrower. To choose which columns show, see [Display settings](#display-settings).

<details>
<summary>Which columns sort, and what is kept</summary>

- The arrow beside the heading shows which way the list is sorted.
- You can sort by **Product**, **ID**, **Type**, **SKU**, **Barcode**, **Stock**, **Stock Status**, **Price** and **Date Created**.
- A new device sorts by **Product**, A to Z. The sort you choose is kept on this device.
- A new column width isn't saved in your settings.

</details>

### The foot of the list {#footer}

- **Tax basis**, such as **Shop base** — what your store calculates tax on. Tap it to see the tax rates in use.
- **Showing 10 of 212** — how many products are on screen, out of those on this device that match your search and filters. More load as you scroll.
- **Sync arrow** — tap it to check your store for changes now.

<details>
<summary>A low count, and starting over</summary>

- On a new device the second number can start low, while your catalogue is still downloading.
- Press and hold the sync arrow for **Clear and Refresh**, which clears the products on this device and downloads them again. See [Refresh, or start over](sync.mdx#refresh).

</details>

## Change stock, prices and names in the list {#key-features}

Each row's stock and prices can be changed in place, with the same keypads as the cart: tap the number and a keypad opens from it. A kept change goes to your store.

### Stock {#inline-stock-editing}

Tap the number under **Stock** and type the new quantity, or use **−** and **+**. Stock is a whole number, and **−** stops at 0. Tap the tick or press Enter to keep it; Escape leaves it as it was. In the iOS and Android apps, type the number into the box.

### Manage {#manage-stock-toggle}

The **Manage** switch under the number turns stock tracking on or off for the product:

- **On** — your store counts the stock down as you sell. The number can be changed.
- **Off** — the stock isn't counted, and the number is greyed. **Stock Status** says whether the product can be sold.

### Prices {#inline-price-editing}

- **Regular Price** — tap it, type the new price, then tap the tick or press Enter to keep it. Escape leaves it as it was. In the iOS and Android apps, type the price into the box.
- **Sale Price** — can be changed only while the product is on sale. To put a product on sale, add a sale price in its [dialog](#product-actions).
- **Price** — the price the till charges. It follows the other two and can't be changed itself.

A variable product's prices come from its options, so they can't be changed on its row. Change each option's price on the option's own row: see [Variable products](#variable-products).

### Name {#inline-name-editing}

Tap a product's name in the list, type the new name, then press Enter or tap outside the box to keep it.

### Cost of Goods Sold {#cost-of-goods-sold}

If your store uses WooCommerce's Cost of Goods Sold, turn on the **Cost of Goods Sold** column in [Display settings](#display-settings) to see each product's cost beside its prices. Tap a cost, type the new one, then tap the tick or press Enter to keep it. Escape leaves it as it was. In the iOS and Android apps, type the cost into the box.

:::note Turn it on in WooCommerce first
Cost of Goods Sold is a WooCommerce feature. Turn it on in **WooCommerce > Settings > Advanced > Features**.
:::

### If you can't change a field {#permissions}

A greyed field whose tip says **You don't have permission to edit products** means your account can't change products. An administrator can grant it in **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Access**. Variations have their own permission, and so does deleting.

## Edit a product {#product-actions}

Press **›** at the end of a row to open the product's dialog.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[-40, 0], [95, 0], [-14, 0], [-10, 0], [-40, 0], [-30, 0], [-20, 0], [-20, 0], [75, 0], [-30, 0]]} alt="Edit Aim Analog Watch: Form and JSON tabs, rows for Name, Regular Price 45.00, SKU, Barcode with Remove, Stock 81 with Stop tracking, Tax, Categories, Status and Hidden fields, then buttons for Sale Price, Featured, Virtual, Downloadable and Custom field, and Sync and Close at the foot" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Name** — the product's name, as your store shows it.
2. **Regular Price** — the price when it isn't on sale.
3. **Remove** — takes the field off the product, and its button comes back at the bottom.
4. **Stop tracking** — stops counting this product's stock. The row then reads **Not tracked**, with **Track quantity** to turn it back on.
5. **Tax** — the product's tax class, or **Not taxable**.
6. **Show** — lists the hidden fields. See [Advanced fields](#advanced-fields).
7. **Sale Price** — adds a field the product doesn't have yet. **Featured**, **Virtual** and **Downloadable** work the same way.
8. **Custom field** — adds a field of your own. See [Advanced fields](#advanced-fields).
9. **Sync** — takes your store's copy of this product. See [Sync or delete](#delete).
10. **Close** — closes the dialog. There's no Save button: each change saves as you make it.

</div>

Each change saves as you make it. **Saved** shows beside the field for a moment. If a change can't be saved, **Not saved** shows in red and stays there until your next change.

<span id="advanced-fields"></span>

<details>
<summary>Advanced fields and troubleshooting</summary>

- **Hidden fields** — press **Show** to list them. They are custom fields that plugins keep for themselves. Change them only if you know what they're for.
- **Custom field** — adds a custom field of your own, with a name and a value.
- **JSON** — the tab at the top shows the product as this device holds it, for troubleshooting.

</details>

A variable product's dialog has a **Variations** row that reads **Managed in the product list**: you edit its options from its row in the list, not here. See [Variable products](#variable-products).

<details>
<summary>Why can't I edit variations in this dialog?</summary>

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[280, 0], [330, 0], [75, 0]]} alt="Edit Abominable Hoodie: Stock reads Not tracked with Track quantity, and a Variations row reads 15 variations, Managed in the product list" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Managed in the product list** — the product's options are edited from its row in the list.
2. **Not tracked** — the stock isn't counted at the product level. **Track quantity** turns it on.
3. **Sync** — takes your store's copy of the product.

In WooCommerce each option is a variation with its own price, stock and settings, so it has its own dialog. The product's dialog only counts them.

</details>

## Variable products {#variable-products}

A variable product, such as a hoodie in five sizes and three colours, has a row of its own and a row for each option. The row shows the product's sizes and colours, and how many options it has, such as **15 variations**.

Tap **15 variations** to open the options. How they open depends on **Variations** in [Display settings](#display-settings):

- **Open in place** — the options fill the list, with **Products** above them to go back. This is where a new device starts.
- **Expanded rows** — the options open as rows under the product.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[-4, -30], [150, 0], [-30, 0], [0, -28]]} alt="Abominable Hoodie opened in place: Products and the product's name above the table, Size and Color filters, and one row per option such as L, Blue with stock 100, a Manage switch, In Stock and $69.00" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Products** — goes back to the list you came from.
2. **Abominable Hoodie** — the product whose options you're looking at.
3. **Stock** — each option has its own stock and **Manage** switch.
4. **›** — opens the option's own dialog. See [Edit a variation](#edit-variation).

</div>

Each option's row has its own stock, **Manage** switch and prices, which you change the same way as a product's. Press **›** on an option to open its own dialog.

### Edit a variation {#edit-variation}

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[95, 0], [-10, 0], [-40, 0], [-30, 0], [-20, 0], [75, 0]]} alt="Edit L, Blue: rows for Regular Price 69.00, SKU and Barcode with Remove, Stock 100 with Stop tracking, Tax, Status and Hidden fields, buttons for Sale Price and Custom field, and Sync and Close at the foot" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Regular Price** — this option's own price.
2. **Stop tracking** — stops counting this option's stock.
3. **Tax** — this option's tax class. See below for **Same as parent**.
4. **Show** — lists the option's hidden fields.
5. **Sale Price** — puts this option on sale.
6. **Sync** — takes your store's copy of this option.

</div>

An option has its own price, SKU, barcode, stock, tax and status. Its **Tax** row can follow the product: it then reads **Same as parent**, with the product's tax class beside it. Press **Set for this variation** to choose the option's own class. To follow the product again, choose **Same as parent** in the list.

## Sync or delete {#delete}

At the bottom of a product's or an option's dialog:

- **Sync** — takes your store's copy of this product, in case it was changed somewhere else.
- **Delete** — asks first, then deletes it in your store.

:::warning Deleting removes it from your store
Delete doesn't only remove it from this till:

- **A product** goes to the Trash in WooCommerce. The confirm reads: *Are you sure you want to delete this product? Deleted products will be placed in the Trash on the server.*
- **A variation** is deleted for good. The confirm reads: *Are you sure you want to delete this variation? Deleting a variation is permanent, there is no Trash for variations.*

**Delete** shows only for an account that is allowed to delete products.
:::

A product deleted in WooCommerce also leaves every till at the next check for changes.

## Display settings {#display-settings}

Press the settings button beside the search field to open **Product Settings**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[0, 0], [0, 0], [205, 0], [-30, 0]]} alt="Product Settings: Variations set to Open in place with Expanded rows beside it, then Columns, each with a drag handle and a switch, and Product with SKU, Barcode and Attributes under it, and Restore Default Settings and Close at the foot" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Open in place** — a variable product's options fill the list. Where a new device starts.
2. **Expanded rows** — the options open as rows under the product.
3. **Restore Default Settings** — puts the columns back. See [Restore Default Settings](#restore-default-settings).
4. **Close** — your choices are kept.

**Variations** here is the same setting as on the [Product Panel](../pos/product-panel/variations.mdx#change-style): change it in one place and the other follows. Your choices apply on this device only.

### Columns {#available-columns}

Each column has a row under **Columns**: the dotted handle at the left, then a switch, then the column's name. Turn the switch on to show the column, or off to hide it. Drag a row up or down by its handle to move that column left or right in the table. **Product** has three extra lines you can show under the name: **SKU**, **Barcode** and **Attributes**. **Price**, **Regular Price** and **Sale Price** can each show the tax as well.

<details>
<summary>Available columns and their defaults</summary>

| Column | Shows | On for a new device |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Image** | The product's picture | Yes |
| **ID** | The product's ID in WooCommerce | No |
| **Product** | The name, and for a variable product its options | Yes |
| **Type** | Simple, variable and so on | No |
| **SKU** | The SKU | No |
| **Barcode** | The barcode | No |
| **Stock** | The quantity, with the **Manage** switch | Yes |
| **Stock Status** | In stock, out of stock or on backorder | Yes |
| **Categories** | The product's categories | Yes |
| **Tags** | The product's tags | No |
| **Brands** | The product's brands | No |
| **Cost of Goods Sold** | The cost, if your store uses it | No |
| **Price** | The price the till charges | Yes |
| **Regular Price** | The price when not on sale | Yes |
| **Sale Price** | The sale price | Yes |
| **Date Created** | When the product was added | No |
| **Date Modified** | When it last changed | No |

</details>

### Restore Default Settings {#restore-default-settings}

**Restore Default Settings**, at the bottom of **Product Settings**, puts the columns back as a new device has them.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

- **A product isn't in the list** — check the filters, then search for it by name or SKU. A product marked online only never reaches the POS: see [POS Only Products](pos-only-products.mdx).
- **A price or stock level looks out of date** — open the product and press **Sync**, or tap the sync arrow under the list.
- **Not saved** — the change didn't reach the product. Check the value and try again. If it keeps failing, hover or press and hold the field: if its tip says **You don't have permission to edit products**, see [If you can't change a field](#permissions).
- **A field is greyed** — your account isn't allowed to change it. See [If you can't change a field](#permissions).
- **Sale Price can't be changed** — the product isn't on sale. Add a sale price in its dialog.
- **There's no Delete button** — your account isn't allowed to delete products.
