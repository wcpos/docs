:::info Pro Feature
The **Coupons** screen needs [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/pro-license). Applying a coupon your store already has works on the free plugin too: see [Applying Coupons](./applying-coupons.mdx).
:::

The **Coupons** screen is your store's list of WooCommerce coupons, inside the POS. Use it to look a coupon up, make a new one for a promotion, change an amount or an expiry date, or take an old coupon away. Every change goes to your store, so the coupon is the same in **WP Admin › Marketing › Coupons** and on your website, and your other tills get it when they sync.

Open it from the **Coupons** icon in the menu on the left. On a phone, open the menu first.

Jump to: [Make a coupon](#creating-coupons) · [Change a coupon](#edit) · [Delete a coupon](#delete) · [Use a coupon on a sale](#apply)

Newest coupons are at the top. Scroll down to load more.

9. **>** — opens the coupon to [change it](#edit).

### Filter the list {#filters}

Press a filter and choose from its list. The filter then shows what you chose, with a **✕** to take it off. With two or more filters on, **Clear all** takes them all off.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Discount Type** — the filter, open.
2. **Percentage Discount** — a percentage off the items.
3. **Fixed Cart Discount** — an amount off the whole order.
4. **Fixed Product Discount** — an amount off each matching item.

## Make a coupon {#creating-coupons}

1. Press **+** at the top of the Coupons screen.
2. Type a code, or press **Generate**.
3. Choose the discount and type the amount.
4. Choose when it expires.
5. Add any limits you need, then press **Add Coupon**.

<div style={{maxWidth: '559px', marginInline: 'auto'}}>

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[-20, 0], [-14, 0], [-20, 0], [-20, 0], [36, 30], [-30, 0], [14, -30], [12, -30]]} alt="The Add Coupon dialog: a generated code, the Generate button, Percentage chosen with 10 percent, 1 Week chosen for expiry with its date, the One-time use switch, buttons to add limits such as Minimum spend, and the Add Coupon button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Code** — what the customer gives you at the till. Capitals don't matter there.
2. **Generate** — makes a code such as **POS-UA43**, without letters that are easy to misread (0 and O, 1, I and L).
3. **Discount** — **Percentage**, **Amount off order** or **Amount off products**. See the [types](#coupon-types).
4. **Amount** — the percentage, or the amount in your store's currency.
5. **Expires** — **Never**, **End of Day**, **1 Week**, **1 Month** or **Pick a Date**. **End of Day**, **1 Week** and **1 Month** run to the end of that day in your store's time zone, and the date shows below.
6. **One-time use** — the coupon stops working after it's used once.
7. **Minimum spend** and the other buttons — add a limit or a setting to the coupon. See [What each setting does](#fields).
8. **Add Coupon** — saves the coupon to your store. **Cancel** closes without saving.

</div>

</div>

The new coupon is in the list, and in the cart's coupon list, straight away.

To make a coupon during a sale and put it on the order at once, use **New coupon** in the cart: see [Discounts](../pos/cart/discounts.mdx#order-level-discounts).

### Coupon types {#coupon-types}

The POS and WooCommerce name the three types differently:

- **Percentage** (WooCommerce: *Percentage discount*) — a percentage off the items in the cart. `10` takes 10% off.
- **Amount off order** (WooCommerce: *Fixed cart discount*) — an amount off the whole order. `5.00` takes $5 off the order.
- **Amount off products** (WooCommerce: *Fixed product discount*) — an amount off each matching item. `2.00` takes $2 off each T-shirt.

### What each setting does {#fields}

The form always shows **Status**. Each button under it adds one more setting: the spend and use limits, **Internal note** and the rest. Each added setting gets a **Remove** button that takes it off again. Open the list below for what each one does.

<details>
<summary>Coupon settings and limits</summary>

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **Active**, **Draft** or **Pending**. **Active** is **Published** in WooCommerce and in the list's **Status** column. A Draft or Pending coupon shows **Inactive**, and leaves the list the next time the till fetches coupons. A new coupon is Active unless you choose otherwise. |
| **Minimum spend** | The order must reach this amount before the coupon applies. |
| **Maximum spend** | The coupon doesn't apply above this amount. |
| **Total uses** | How many times the coupon can be used, by everyone together. Not offered while **One-time use** is on. |
| **Uses per customer** | How many times one customer can use it. |
| **Max discounted items** | How many items the coupon takes money off. Only for **Percentage** and **Amount off products**. |
| **Cannot be combined** | The till refuses this coupon if the order already has another coupon. |
| **Also grants free shipping** | Free shipping methods that need a coupon become available on the order. |
| **Internal note** | The coupon's description in WooCommerce. The cart's coupon list shows it under the code, and a receipt printed from your store shows it in place of the code, so customers can see it. Don't put anything private in it. |
| **Custom field** | A key and value of your own, stored on the coupon. |

</details>

Some WooCommerce settings aren't in the POS form: **Products** and **Exclude products**, **Product categories** and **Exclude categories**, **Exclude sale items** and **Allowed emails**. Set them in **WP Admin › Marketing › Coupons**. They still apply at the till; see [When the till refuses a coupon](./applying-coupons.mdx#refused). **Exclude sale items** also skips an item whose price you lowered at the till: see [How POS Price Changes Interact with Coupons](../pos/cart/discounts.mdx#how-pos-price-changes-interact-with-coupons), and for developers the [POS Discount Reference](../reference/pos-discounts.mdx).

## Change a coupon {#edit}

:::warning No Save button
Each change goes to your store as you make it. **Close** doesn't undo anything: to go back, change the field again.
:::

Press **>** at the end of the coupon's row. On a phone, tap the coupon.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[-20, 0], [-14, 0], [-20, 0], [-38, 0], [96, 0], [-30, 0], [110, 0], [36, -24], [-14, 0], [84, 0], [-20, 0]]} alt="The Edit welcome5 dialog: the code with Generate, Amount off order chosen with 5.00, 1 Month chosen for expiry, One-time use on, Status Active, Minimum spend with Remove, Hidden fields with Show, and the Sync and Close buttons" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Code** — change it here, or press **Generate** for a new one.
2. **Generate** — makes a new code. The old code stops working.
3. **Discount** — the type.
4. **Amount** — the amount.
5. **Expires** — the date it stops working.
6. **One-time use** — on here, so **Usage** in the list shows **0 / 1**.
7. **Status** — **Active**, **Draft** or **Pending**.
8. **Remove** — takes a setting off the coupon. A coupon saved in WP Admin often shows **Minimum spend** and **Maximum spend** at **0.00**, which sets no limit.
9. **Show** — the coupon's hidden custom fields, which plugins use.
10. **Sync** — fetches this coupon again from your store, for a change made in WP Admin since.
11. **Close** — closes the dialog.

The **JSON** tab shows the coupon as your store sends it.

If **Sync** can't reach your store, a message says **Couldn't refresh. The copy on this device is unchanged — check your connection and try again.**

## Delete a coupon {#delete}

**Delete** is at the bottom of the coupon's dialog, for staff allowed to delete coupons.

1. Open the coupon and press **Delete**.
2. Tick **Confirm**, then press **Delete**.

The dialog warns: **Deleted coupons will be placed in the Trash on the server.** The coupon goes to the **Trash** in WooCommerce and can't be used. To bring it back, open **WP Admin › Marketing › Coupons**, choose **Trash** and restore it. To stop a coupon working without deleting it, give it an expiry date instead: after that day the till refuses it.

## Who can make and change coupons {#permissions}

Making, changing and deleting coupons each need their own permission, set in **WP Admin › POS › Access**:

- **Create & edit coupons** — a Cashier has it by default. Without it, **+** is greyed out and the list can't be changed.
- **Delete coupons** — a Cashier doesn't have it by default. Without it, the coupon's dialog has no **Delete**.

Hover the greyed-out **+** to see why. See [Accessing the POS](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx) to change a role.

## Use a coupon on a sale {#apply}

In the cart, press **+** and choose **Add Discount**, then switch to **Coupon**, pick the coupon and press **Apply**. This works on the free plugin too. See [Applying Coupons](./applying-coupons.mdx).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**A coupon made in WP Admin isn't in the list.** Press the arrow beside **Showing … of …** at the bottom of the list to fetch the latest. Hold it down for **Clear and Refresh**, which empties this device's copy of the list and fetches it all again.

**+ is greyed out.** On the free plugin it says **Upgrade to Pro**. Otherwise your role can't create coupons: see [Who can make and change coupons](#permissions).

**A coupon says Inactive.** It has expired, or its status isn't **Active**. Open it and change **Expires** or **Status**.

**The till refuses a coupon.** See [When the till refuses a coupon](./applying-coupons.mdx#refused).
