:::info Pro Feature
The ATUM extension links ATUM locations to [stores](../stores/index.mdx), and stores come with [WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).
:::

[ATUM Multi-Inventory](https://stockmanagementlabs.com/addons/atum-multi-inventory/) keeps a product's stock in more than one place: a warehouse, a shop, a market stall. The **WCPOS ATUM Integration** extension links each WCPOS store to one of those places, its **location**. The tills in that store then count that location's stock, and can use its prices and SKUs. A sale takes the stock from that location, not from the shop's total.

Cashiers have nothing to set up: see [What the cashier sees](#what-the-cashier-sees) and [Sales and refunds](#stock). Administrators start with [Install the extension](#install). Stock or prices look wrong? See [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong).

## What the cashier sees {#what-the-cashier-sees}

Selling doesn't change. What changes is the numbers behind each product:

- **Stock** is this store's location only. ATUM keeps a product's stock at one location as an **inventory**, so this holds for each product that has an inventory for the location. A product the location has run out of shows as out of stock, even if the warehouse has plenty, and the **In Stock** filter hides it. A product with no inventory for the location keeps its ordinary WooCommerce stock, price and SKU.
- **Prices** come from ATUM when the store is set to; see [Choose where prices come from](#pricing-source).
- **SKUs** are the location's own when the store is set to; see [Use the location's SKUs](#location-skus).

## Sales and refunds {#stock}

A sale rung up in a linked store takes its stock from that store's location. A refund or a cancelled order puts it back in the same place. ATUM does the counting itself, so each movement shows in ATUM's stock history against the order.

## Install the extension {#install}

:::note Before you start
You need [ATUM Inventory Management](https://wordpress.org/plugins/atum-stock-manager-for-woocommerce/) and its [Multi-Inventory](https://stockmanagementlabs.com/addons/atum-multi-inventory/) add-on, both active, with your locations created in ATUM. You also need WCPOS Pro and an administrator login to **WP Admin**.
:::

1. In **WP Admin**, go to **POS → Settings** and choose **Extensions**.
2. Pick the **Inventory** tab and find **WCPOS ATUM Integration**.
3. Press **Install**. It installs and switches on in one go.

See [Install an extension](./index.mdx#installing-an-extension) for updates and auto-updates.

## Link a store to a location {#store-location}

Do this once for each store that has its own stock. Each product you sell in the store also needs an inventory for that location in ATUM: a product without one keeps its ordinary WooCommerce stock, price and SKU at the till.

1. In **WP Admin**, go to **POS → Stores** and click the store's name.
2. In the store's menu, under **Extensions**, open **ATUM Inventory**.
3. Under **Inventory location**, pick the location this store sells from. **No location (use default stock)** leaves the store on the shop's ordinary WooCommerce stock.
4. Two more settings appear: **Pricing source** and **Use location-specific SKUs from ATUM**. Set them as below, or leave them as they are.
5. Changes save by themselves. On each till in the store, press and hold the [sync button](../pos/product-panel/display-settings.mdx#footer) (<Icon name="arrow-rotate-right" />) and choose **Clear and Refresh**. The stock now matches the location in ATUM.

### Choose where prices come from {#pricing-source}

**Pricing source** has three choices:

- **Default (WooCommerce prices)** — ATUM's prices are not used. The till shows the product's [per-store price](../stores/setup.mdx#per-store-pricing) where it has one for this store, and the WooCommerce price otherwise.
- **WCPOS Pro (per-store pricing)** — the same as **Default**: per-store price where there is one, WooCommerce price otherwise.
- **ATUM (per-inventory pricing)** — the regular and sale price come from the location's inventory in ATUM. Where ATUM has no price for it, the till keeps the price it would show with **Default**.

The stock comes from the location whichever you choose.

### Use the location's SKUs {#location-skus}

Tick **Use location-specific SKUs from ATUM** when each location labels its stock with its own SKU. The till shows the location's SKU, and a search for it finds the product. Where ATUM has no SKU for the location, the product's own SKU stays.

## Edit products from the POS {#write-back}

With WCPOS Pro, the **Products** screen in the POS menu shows the location's numbers. An edit made there goes back to the location in ATUM when the product has exactly one inventory for the store's location. Only that location changes: other locations are left alone.

To change a location's stock from a till:

1. In the POS menu, open **Products** and search for the product.
2. Click its **Stock** number, type the new count and press **Enter**.
3. Check it in ATUM: open the product there and look at its inventory for the location, which now shows the new count.

For a product with exactly one inventory for the store's location, these fields go back:

| Field | Goes back to ATUM |
| --- | --- |
| **Stock** | Every change. |
| **Regular price**, **Sale price** | Only when the store's **Pricing source** is **ATUM**. |
| **SKU** | Only when **Use location-specific SKUs from ATUM** is ticked. |

Nothing goes back to ATUM:

- for a new product. Add the location's stock in ATUM.
- when the product has no inventory for the location. The extension never adds one.
- when the product has more than one inventory for the location. The extension can't tell which to change, so it changes none of them.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**The till shows the shop's total stock, not the location's.** Check that the store has an **Inventory location**, and that the product has an inventory for it in ATUM. Then press and hold the sync button and choose **Clear and Refresh**. Also check the extension's version on the **Extensions** screen: versions before 0.1.4 show the total stock with WCPOS 2.0.

**There is no ATUM Inventory page under Extensions in the store's menu.** The page needs WCPOS Pro, the extension active, and both ATUM Inventory Management and ATUM Multi-Inventory active.

**"No ATUM locations found. Create locations in ATUM settings."** ATUM has no locations yet. Create them in ATUM, then reload the store's page.

**There is no Pricing source.** It appears once the store has an **Inventory location**.

**A stock change on the Products screen didn't reach ATUM.** The product has no inventory for the store's location, or has more than one. See [Edit products from the POS](#write-back).

<details>
<summary>For developers: stored data, REST fields and filters</summary>

- A store keeps its settings in three post meta keys: `_wcpos_atum_inventory_location` (an `atum_location` term ID), `_wcpos_pricing_source` (`default`, `wcpos_pro` or `atum`) and `_wcpos_atum_sku_override` (`1` or empty). The WCPOS stores endpoint returns them as `atum_inventory_location`, `pricing_source` and `atum_sku_override`.
- Product and variation responses for the POS are changed at priority 20, after WCPOS Pro's per-store prices at priority 10. The stock status is set from the location's quantity alone.
- When the POS saves an order, the extension adds `mi_inventories` to each line item, and filters `atum/multi_inventory/order_item_inventories` to the store's location. ATUM then reduces and restores the stock, and records the order ID in `atum_inventory_orders`.
- Product edits are written to `atum_inventory_meta` for updates only, never creates, and only when exactly one inventory matches the location (or the request names one with `inventory_id`).
- `wcpos_atum_is_supported` switches the extension on or off. By default it is on when ATUM Multi-Inventory is active.
- Requirements: WordPress 5.9 or later, PHP 7.4 or later.

Source: [github.com/wcpos/wcpos-atum](https://github.com/wcpos/wcpos-atum).

</details>
