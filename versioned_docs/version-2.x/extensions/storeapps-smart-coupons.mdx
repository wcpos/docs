The **WCPOS StoreApps Smart Coupons** extension lets a store-credit coupon from [Smart Coupons](https://woocommerce.com/products/smart-coupons/) by StoreApps, such as a gift card, pay toward a POS sale. With WCPOS Pro, you take the card on the checkout's payment page, the right amount comes off it, and the receipt shows the balance left.

:::warning Add Discount can't take store credit
In WCPOS 2.0, the cart's **Add Discount** only applies percentage, fixed cart and fixed product coupons. A store-credit coupon there gets **Coupon not found**, with or without this extension. With WCPOS Pro, take it on the payment page instead: see [Take a gift card at the till](#at-the-till).
:::

## Take a gift card at the till {#at-the-till}

Before you start, the administrator has [installed this extension](#install) with Smart Coupons by StoreApps active. You also need [WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) and a payment method on the checkout's [Legacy tab](../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx): that tab opens the store's own payment page, which has a coupon box.

1. Build the cart, press **Checkout** and open the **Legacy** tab. The store's payment page opens in the checkout.
2. Type the gift card's code in **Coupon code** and press **Apply coupon**. The order total drops by the amount the card covers.
3. Choose the payment method the customer pays the rest with, take the payment on that page, then press **Process Payment**.
4. On the receipt, check the coupon's discount row for **Store credit balance**, as in [What the receipt shows](#receipt). If it's missing, see [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong).

Without Pro, the **Legacy** tab has no payment methods, so there is no payment page to take the card on. Take the payment as usual and ask the customer to keep their gift card for a later purchase: nothing has come off it.

## What the receipt shows {#receipt}

The coupon's discount row ends with the card's balance after the sale. On the **Standard Receipt**, a store-credit coupon whose description is **Gift card** prints:

> Discount · Gift card — Store credit balance: $61.50

The balance is the card's balance when the receipt is made, which at checkout is right after the sale. Your receipt template needs nothing new. If the coupon has no description, the row still ends with **Store credit balance**. To print **Gift Card** instead of **Discount** on that row, see [For developers](#developers).

## What it does {#what-it-does}

StoreApps keeps gift-card balances for online orders, but on its own it misses orders paid at the POS. When a POS order carries a store-credit coupon, the extension:

- **Takes off what was spent, and no more.** It records how much of the card the order used, so StoreApps deducts that amount and leaves the rest on the card.
- **Lowers the balance when the order is paid.** Never when the coupon goes on, and never twice.
- **Forgets a coupon you took off.** If the coupon is removed before payment, nothing is deducted for it.
- **Notes it on the order.** See [The note on the order](#order-note).
- **Prints the balance on the receipt.** See [What the receipt shows](#receipt).

With StoreApps Smart Coupons switched off, the extension does nothing.

## Install the extension {#install}

:::note Before you start
You need an administrator login to **WP Admin**, and Smart Coupons by StoreApps (sold on WooCommerce.com) active on the site. The extension works only with that plugin, not with other plugins that have "Smart Coupons" in their name.
:::

### With WCPOS Pro {#install-pro}

1. In **WP Admin**, go to **POS → Settings → Extensions**.
2. Find **WCPOS StoreApps Smart Coupons** and press **Install**.
3. Press **Activate**.

See [Install an extension](./index.mdx#installing-an-extension) for updates and auto-updates.

### With the free plugin {#install-free}

The Extensions screen lists the extension, but its buttons need Pro. Instead:

1. Download `wcpos-storeapps-smart-coupons.zip` from the extension's [latest release](https://github.com/wcpos/wcpos-storeapps-smart-coupons/releases/latest).
2. In **WP Admin**, go to **Plugins → Add New Plugin** and press **Upload Plugin**.
3. Choose the file you downloaded and press **Install Now**.
4. Press **Activate Plugin**.

There is nothing to set up after that. Create store-credit coupons or gift cards in WooCommerce as usual.

## The note on the order {#order-note}

When the order is paid, the extension adds one private note to it. Staff see it on the order in **WP Admin → WooCommerce → Orders**:

> StoreApps Smart Coupons store credit recorded for WCPOS: Coupon store100 used $38.50. Current balance: $61.50.

Use it to check which card paid, how much it covered, and what was left on it. The note is added once per order.

## Refunds and cancellations {#refunds}

The extension doesn't put credit back. When you refund or cancel an order paid partly with store credit, StoreApps decides what happens to the balance. Check the coupon's amount in **WP Admin → Marketing → Coupons** afterwards.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Coupon not found** when you apply a gift card from **Add Discount**. Add Discount can't take store credit. Use [the payment page on the Legacy tab](#at-the-till) instead.

**The balance didn't go down after the sale.** Check that both **Smart Coupons** and **WCPOS StoreApps Smart Coupons** are active in **Plugins**. The deduction happens when the order is paid, not when the coupon goes on. An order with no store-credit note was not deducted by the extension.

**The receipt has no balance.** A receipt printed while the till is offline has no balance. Print it again once the till is back online. If it's still missing, your receipt template doesn't print the discount row's label; see [Receipt Data](../receipts/receipt-data.mdx#discounts).

<details>
<summary>For developers: stored data and hooks</summary>

<span id="developers"></span>

The extension adds no WCPOS coupon fields. It works through WooCommerce order meta and StoreApps' own balance logic. It applies only to POS orders (`wcpos_is_pos_order()`: `created_via` is `woocommerce-pos`) and only to coupons of type `smart_coupon`.

- **Usage.** On `woocommerce_order_after_calculate_totals` it writes StoreApps' `smart_coupons_contribution` order meta (coupon code → amount used) and `wc_sc_environment`, when StoreApps hasn't. It drops entries for coupons no longer on the unpaid order.
- **Payment-page coupon box.** That form is a plain front-end POST to `WC_Order::apply_coupon()`, which StoreApps doesn't treat as an order context. While an unpaid POS order with a `smart_coupon` line recalculates outside a REST request, the extension reports a REST context so StoreApps' own order-discount logic runs.
- **Deduction.** A POS order is paid from `pos-open` or `pos-partial`, and StoreApps only deducts on a move from `pending` or `failed`. On `woocommerce_order_status_changed` from any other status to a paid status or `on-hold`, it calls StoreApps' `WC_SC_Coupon_Process::update_smart_coupon_balance()`, which marks each coupon line so it never deducts twice.
- **Order note.** Added on the move to `processing`, `completed` or `on-hold`, once per order (`_wcpos_storeapps_smart_coupons_audit_note_added`).
- **Receipt.** It filters `woocommerce_coupon_get_description` while a WCPOS receipt renders, and on the receipt data filter it adds `gift_card: true` to the store-credit row in `discounts` and appends the balance to its `label`. A template can then print:

```text
{{#discounts}}{{#gift_card}}Gift Card{{/gift_card}}{{^gift_card}}{{i18n.discount}}{{/gift_card}} · {{label}}{{/discounts}}
```

- **Hook names.** Version 0.2.3 still hooks the pre-2.0 names `woocommerce_pos_receipt_data`, `woocommerce_pos_before_template_render` and `woocommerce_pos_after_template_render`. WCPOS 2.0 still fires them after their `wcpos_` names, and WordPress logs a deprecation notice for each when `WP_DEBUG` is on. See [Hook naming](./index.mdx#hook-naming).

Source: [github.com/wcpos/wcpos-storeapps-smart-coupons](https://github.com/wcpos/wcpos-storeapps-smart-coupons).

</details>

:::info With WCPOS Pro
The till takes a gift card on the Legacy tab's payment page, and you install and update the extension from **POS → Settings → Extensions**. Without Pro, install it by hand from its release, as above.
:::
