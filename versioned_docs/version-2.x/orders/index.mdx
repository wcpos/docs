:::info Pro Feature
The Orders page needs [WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx). Without Pro you see a blurred preview of the list, and you manage orders in WP Admin under **WooCommerce → Orders**.
:::

**Orders** in the menu lists the sales your store has taken. Use it when a customer comes back: find the sale, reprint the receipt, refund it, or put it back in the cart to change it. You never need to switch to WP Admin for this.

Go straight to: [Find a sale](#find-an-order) · [Reprint a receipt](#receipt-reprinting) · [Change a sale](#reopening-orders) · [Refund a sale](#refunds)

## Find an order {#find-an-order}

The list opens on **your own sales at this store**: the **Cashier** pill shows you, and the **Store** pill shows the store you are signed in to (or **POS** when the site has only the one store). Newest sales come first, grouped under a heading for each day in the store's time zone.

To find a sale, type in **Search Orders** at the top. Search matches the order number, the customer's name, company and phone (billing or shipping), the billing email, and the products in the order by name or SKU. See [What Search Matches](../reference/search.mdx#orders).

With a barcode scanner, scan the order-number barcode on a printed receipt while the Orders page is open. The scan lands in **Search Orders** and brings up that sale. See [Barcode Scanners](../hardware/scanners/index.mdx).

1. **Search Orders**: type an order number, a name, a phone number or a product.

## Open an order {#order-details}

Tap an order's row to open it. With a mouse, click the arrow (<Icon name="chevron-right" />) at the end of the row, or select a row with the arrow keys and press **Enter**. The order opens in a panel on the right, on its **View** tab.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[350, 0], [24, -29], [26, -29], [19, -29], [28, -29], [31, -29]]} alt="An order opened from the Orders page on its View tab: the date, store and cashier line, the items, totals and cash payment, and the Delete, Refund, Sync, Close, Receipt and Re-open buttons in the footer" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Order details**: when the order was placed, at which store, by which cashier, and where it came from.
2. **Delete**: sends a saved order to the Trash on the store. See [Delete an order](#delete).
3. **Refund**: opens the refund form. See [Refunds](#refunds).
4. **Sync**: fetches the latest copy of this order from the store. See [Sync one order](#sync).
5. **Receipt**: the order's receipt, to print or email again. See [Reprint a receipt](#receipt-reprinting).
6. **Re-open**: puts the order back in the cart to change it. See [Re-opening Orders](#reopening-orders).

</div>

The panel has three tabs:

- **View**: the order as the customer bought it. See [What View shows](#view-tab).
- **Edit**: change the order's details in a form. See [Editing Orders](#editing-orders).
- **JSON**: the order's raw data, for support and troubleshooting. Products, customers and coupons have the same **JSON** tab when you open them.

The buttons at the foot of the panel are the actions: **Re-open**, **Receipt**, **Sync**, **Delete** and **Refund**. Which ones you see depends on the order; see [Order Actions](#order-actions). If the order isn't on this device, the panel says **Order not found**.

### What View shows {#view-tab}

View shows the whole order on one scroll: status, items, totals, payment, refunds and customer.

<details>
<summary>Every part of View, top to bottom</summary>

- **Status**: the order's status badge. A partly refunded order shows **Partially refunded**; an open order shows **POS open** or **POS partial**.
- **Order details**: the date and time, the store (when the order has one), the cashier, and an icon for where the order came from.
- **Items**: each line with its quantity, item, price and total, plus any fees. An order with no lines shows **No line items.**
- **Totals**: Subtotal, Discount, Shipping, Fees and Tax, with one line for each tax rate. After a refund, the refunded amount shows in red and the last line is **Net Payment**; otherwise it is **Total**.
- **Payment**: how the order was paid, with the amount. A paid order shows **Captured** with the date it was paid, and the transaction ID when there is one. An order paid in several parts lists each part under **Payments**. An emailed invoice shows where and when it was sent.
- **Refunds**: each refund made on the order, when there are any.
- **Customer**: name, email and phone, the customer's note, the billing address, the shipping address when it differs, and any tax IDs.

</details>

## Order Actions {#order-actions}

<details>
<summary>Which buttons an order shows</summary>

| Button | What it does | When you see it |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Re-open** | Puts the order back in the cart to change it and take payment again. | Always, with Pro. |
| **Receipt** | Shows the receipt to print or email. | Once the order is saved to the store. |
| **Send again** | Sends the payment invoice to the customer again. | When the order is waiting for an emailed invoice to be paid. |
| **Sync** | Fetches this one order from the store again. | Once the order is saved to the store. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the order. | Always, with Pro. |
| **Refund** | Opens the refund form. | When the order is saved to the store and is Completed, Processing or On hold. |
| **Cancel invoice** | Withdraws the emailed invoice. Needs a connection. | When the order is waiting for an emailed invoice to be paid. |

</details>

### Receipt Reprinting {#receipt-reprinting}

To print or email a receipt again:

1. Open the order and press **Receipt** at its foot.
2. Print it, or email it to the customer. See [Reprint a receipt](../receipts/index.mdx#reprint).

For one-click reprints from the list, turn on the **Receipt** column in [Display Settings](#display-settings). Each saved order then has a receipt button on its row. An order that hasn't reached the store yet has no receipt button.

### Editing Orders {#editing-orders}

Open the order's **Edit** tab to change an order's details in a form: the status, customer, customer note, billing and shipping addresses, payment method, currency, transaction ID, tax IDs and metadata. Line items are not changed here; to change them, re-open the order.

### Re-opening Orders {#reopening-orders}

Press **Re-open** at the foot of the order to put the order back in the cart, where you can:

- Add or remove line items
- Change quantities or prices
- Update the customer
- Add notes or metadata

The order moves to **POS - Open** while it is in the cart. After making changes, go through checkout again to complete the updated order.

Payments already taken stay on the order. Checkout lists them and asks only for what is still owed, shown as **Remaining**: nothing if the cart is unchanged and nothing was refunded, or the difference if you added items. See [Checkout](../pos/checkout/index.mdx).

A sale with a card payment still in progress can't be re-opened. The message says which payment is still in motion: cancel that payment first. See [Cancelling a Payment](../pos/checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx).

:::note Stock on a re-opened sale
If the sale had already reduced stock (payment was taken before it was re-opened), every line change in the cart moves stock straight away: adding a line or raising a quantity reduces the product's stock, removing a line or lowering a quantity returns it. Completing the sale again does not reduce stock a second time.
:::

### Refunds {#refunds}

You can issue full or partial refunds from the POS, either back to the original payment method (when the gateway supports it) or as cash from the till. Open the order and press **Refund** at its foot.

For a step-by-step refund at the till, see [Refunds at the Till](../pos/refunds.mdx). For refund destinations, partial refunds and how refunds appear on receipts, see [Refunds](./refunds.mdx).

### Sync one order {#sync}

Press **Sync** to fetch the latest copy of this order from the store, for example after someone changed it in WP Admin. If the store can't be reached, you see "Couldn't refresh this order. The copy on this device is unchanged". Check your connection and try again.

### Delete an order {#delete}

Press **Delete**, then confirm. A saved order goes to the **Trash** on the store, where an admin can restore it from WP Admin. An order that never reached the store is deleted for good, and the confirmation says so.

If your WordPress role isn't allowed to delete orders, the store refuses and you see "You don't have permission to delete orders on the server". The order stays.

## Work with the list {#the-list}

The list keeps loading more orders as you scroll. The footer shows how many are listed (**Showing 10 of 240**) and the sync button (<Icon name="arrow-rotate-right" />). Press it to fetch new and changed orders; long-press it for **Clear and Refresh**, which clears the search, puts the pills back to your own sales at this store, and downloads the orders again.

### Filter the list {#filters}

The pills under the search bar narrow the list. Filtering runs on the server, so it covers all your orders, not only the ones already on the device.

<details>
<summary>All order filters</summary>

| Pill | What it filters by |
|------|--------------------|
| **Status** | The order status, such as Completed or Processing. |
| **Payment Method** | How the order was paid. |
| **Select Customer** | One customer. |
| **Select Cashier** | Who took the sale. Remove it to see every cashier's sales. |
| **Store** | One store, or where the order came from: **POS**, **Online Store** or **WP Admin**. With no store chosen the pill reads **Created via**. |
| **Select register** | The register the sale was taken on. |
| **Date range** | A range of days. The calendar has quick picks for **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Week**, **Last Week**, **This Month** and **Last Month**; press **Done** to apply. |

</details>

Each set pill has an **×** that removes it. Once you have changed two or more pills, **Clear all** appears at the end of the row and puts the pills back to your own sales at this store.

You can also filter from a row: press a status badge to show only that status, or a customer or cashier name to show only theirs.

### Sort and size the columns {#sort-and-resize}

Click a column header to sort by it, and click again to flip between ascending and descending. **Status**, **Order Number**, **Customer**, **Date Created**, **Payment Method** and **Total** can be sorted. The day headings show only while the list is sorted by **Date Created**. Your sort is kept for next time.

To make a column wider or narrower, drag the right edge of its header. The last column and the **Actions** column can't be resized, and widths go back to normal when you leave the page.

### When the list is empty {#empty-list}

**No orders yet** means this store has no sales from you yet. Sales you take appear here.

**No orders match these filters** means your search or pills hide everything. Press **Clear filters** to clear the search and put the pills back to your own sales at this store.

## Sales that need attention {#needs-attention}

Sometimes a payment is taken at the till, but the store then refuses it. For example, the order had already been paid online, or the store could take only part of the payment. When that happens, a red band appears under the search bar: **1 sale needs attention** (or **3 sales need attention**).

Each entry names the order, the amount and how it was paid, and says what to do, such as "refund that payment" or "refund the difference". The band lists the three newest, then "and N more".

1. Press **Open order** to see the sale.
2. Settle it with the customer: refund the payment or the difference, or take the balance.
3. Press **Handled** to clear the entry.

The band never retries a refused payment for you. It shows only the sales you took: another cashier signing in on the same device doesn't see your entries.

## Display Settings {#display-settings}

Click the sliders icon (<Icon name="sliders" />) at the end of the search bar to open **Order Settings**. Switch each column on or off. **Customer** and **Total** have extra lines you can switch on: the customer's billing and shipping address under the name, and the payment method under the total. That is why **Billing Address** and **Shipping Address** appear twice: the indented pair under **Customer** adds the address beneath the name, and the pair further down are columns of their own. Drag a column by the grip on its left to move it in the list. **Restore Default Settings** puts every column back.

## Order Statuses {#order-statuses}

Most POS orders go straight to **Completed**, because payment is collected at the till. Orders saved with **Save to Server** in the cart are **POS - Open** and can be re-opened later to finish the sale. See [Open Orders](../pos/cart/open-orders.mdx).

POS orders use the standard WooCommerce order statuses plus two of WCPOS's own.

<details>
<summary>All order statuses</summary>

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **POS - Open** | Saved from the POS but not yet paid (parked or held orders). |
| **POS - Partial Payment** | Part of the total is paid and the rest is still due. |
| **Pending** | Awaiting payment. |
| **On hold** | Awaiting action, such as a payment to confirm. |
| **Processing** | Payment received, order being fulfilled. |
| **Completed** | Order fulfilled and complete. |
| **Failed** | The payment failed. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled. |
| **Refunded** | The order was fully refunded. |

</details>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**The sale you want isn't listed.** The list starts on your own sales at this store. Remove the **Cashier** pill to see everyone's, or change the **Store** pill.

**"No orders match these filters".** Press **Clear filters**, then search again.

**A new sale from another till is missing.** Press the sync button in the footer. If it is still missing, long-press it and choose **Clear and Refresh**.

**"Couldn't refresh this order".** The store couldn't be reached. The copy on the device stays as it was; try again once you're connected.

**"You don't have permission to delete orders on the server".** Your WordPress role can't delete orders. Ask a manager.

**Re-open is refused because a payment is "still in motion".** Cancel that payment first, then re-open. See [Cancelling a Payment](../pos/checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
