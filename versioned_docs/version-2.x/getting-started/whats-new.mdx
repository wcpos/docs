WCPOS 2.0 is the biggest release since the plugin was first published. Every sale used to finish on WooCommerce's own order-pay page, which capped what the till could do: one payment method per order, no cash without a round trip to the server, and no card reader talking directly to the POS. 2.0 replaces that with a checkout that belongs to the POS, and rebuilds the register around it.

Before you update, read [Upgrading to 2.0](/getting-started/upgrading-to-2). It needs WooCommerce 9.0.

## A new register {#a-new-register}

The whole POS has one visual language now, on phones, tablets, desktops and in the browser. Forms and pickers slide in from the side as panels instead of popping up over the cart. A scale setting (compact, regular, spacious) picks a size for your device and can be overridden. The bar at the top names the register you're on and shows whether the store is reachable.

Two parts of the layout are yours to set: which side the cart sits on, and a row of quick filter buttons above the products.

## Checkout that belongs to the POS {#checkout}

Pressing **Pay** opens the tender pane: a keypad for what the customer hands over, the change shown as you type, and the payment methods your store offers.

- **Split payments.** Take part in cash and the rest on a card, split evenly between several cards, or by item. The running balance stays on screen until the order is paid.
- **Cash completes offline.** A cash sale finishes at the till even when the store can't be reached, and syncs when the connection returns.
- **A slow connection holds the sale instead of failing it.** If the store is slow to answer, the till waits and tells you; it never rings the same sale twice.
- **Quick discount.** **Add Discount** takes an amount or a percentage off the order and records it as a proper discount. It replaces the old habit of adding a negative fee, which is no longer possible.
- **Tips.** A tip taken on the reader or at the till is recorded on the order as a Tip line.
- **Legacy tab.** Gateways that still use their own payment page are one tap away, so nothing you rely on today stops working.

See [Checkout](/pos/checkout) and [Cart Discounts](/pos/cart/discounts).

## Card readers at the register {#card-readers}

Card terminals are driven from the tender pane, with the reader's progress shown on screen and cancel one tap away. Available with [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/free-vs-pro):

- **Stripe Terminal:** the smart readers you may already use, plus Bluetooth readers and **Tap to Pay** on a phone or tablet.
- **SumUp:** the SumUp Solo over the network, and the Solo, Solo Lite and Air over Bluetooth.
- **Mollie Terminal.**

The till remembers which reader it uses for each gateway, and a reader's settings live on the gateway's own WooCommerce settings page. See [Payment Gateways](/payment).

## Registers and the cash drawer, in the free plugin {#registers-and-the-cash-drawer}

Every till has a name. A cashier opens the register with a float, records cash paid in or out during the day, and closes it with a count. The closing report is printed once and filed as a numbered closure, so the end of every day is a document you can find again, not a screen you saw once. Closures can be exported as CSV from POS settings.

Managers add registers in **WP Admin → POS → Registers** and decide which cashiers may open, count and close. If you don't use a cash drawer, register sessions can be switched off.

With Pro, the **Reports** screen adds a **Closures** view (the open session and its X-report, closures by business day) and a **Sales** view with margin by category and brand. See [End-of-Day Reconciliation](/pos/reconciliation) and [Reports](/reports).

## Customer-facing display {#customer-facing-display}

A second screen that shows the customer their cart and total as it's rung up. It's a web page served by your store: pair any tablet, phone or monitor with a browser to the till using a short code, and choose a display template from the gallery in **WP Admin → POS → Templates → Display**. Available with Pro.

## Receipts and refunds built for fiscal rules {#receipts-and-refunds}

Receipts now carry the register name, the software version and a QR code. Offline sales keep the time they were really made and the register they were made on. A refund or a void is recorded as its own write-once record and prints its own receipt, as a credit note that names the sale it corrects.

This is the groundwork that country-specific fiscal rules (such as France's and Spain's) will build on. Certification for a particular country is not part of 2.0.

## Coupons in the free plugin {#coupons-in-the-free-plugin}

Applying a WooCommerce coupon code at the till no longer needs Pro. The Coupons management screen in the POS stays a Pro feature.

## Requirements and removals {#requirements-and-removals}

- **WooCommerce 9.0 or later** is required. The POS stops at a notice on older stores.
- **The app and the plugin move together.** A 2.0 app needs the 2.0 plugin; apps from 1.10.3 onwards keep working against the 2.0 plugin until they're updated.
- **Magnetic-stripe card readers** are no longer supported.
- **Negative fees** can no longer be added; use **Add Discount**.
- **WooCommerce Analytics** counts a POS order when it's paid, so open or partly paid orders no longer appear in sales totals until they're completed.

## Not in 2.0 {#not-in-2-0}

- **Surcharges.** Legally uneven across countries and no gateway exposes them cleanly; they will be their own effort.
- **Country-specific fiscal certification** (France's exact dataset, Spain's VeriFactu submission). The groundwork is in 2.0 so nothing has to be redone when those land.
- **Third-party extension points for the register layout.** The layout settings are for WCPOS's own panels for now.
