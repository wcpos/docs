WCPOS 2.0 is the biggest release since the plugin was first published. Every sale used to finish on WooCommerce's own order-pay page, which capped what the till could do: one payment method per order, no cash without a round trip to the server, and no card reader talking directly to the POS. 2.0 replaces that with a checkout that belongs to the POS, and rebuilds the register around it.

Before you update, read [Upgrading to 2.0](./upgrading-to-2.mdx). It needs WooCommerce 9.0.

## A new register {#a-new-register}

The whole POS has one visual language now, on phones, tablets, desktops and in the browser. Forms and pickers slide in from the side as panels instead of popping up over the cart. A scale setting (compact, regular, spacious) sizes the POS for your device, and **Auto** follows the window size. The bar at the top names the store you're in (and the register, when the store has more than one) and shows an **Offline** badge when the store can't be reached.

1. **Cart Settings** — where you choose which side the cart sits on, where open orders go, how lines are sorted, your quick discount keys and the cart's columns. See [Cart on the left or right](#cart-side).
2. **Search products** — type part of a name, a SKU or a barcode. The filter bar below it can hold [your own buttons](#quick-filters).
3. **Add to cart** (+) — adds something that isn't a product tile: a new customer, a miscellaneous product, a fee, a discount or shipping.
4. **Discount (10%)** — a [quick discount](#quick-discount) on the whole order. The cross takes it off.
5. **Checkout** — opens the tender pane, with the amount to pay on the button. See [Checkout](#checkout).

</div>

Two parts of the layout are yours to set: which side the cart sits on, and a row of quick filter buttons above the products.

### Cart on the left or right {#cart-side}

In the cart, press **Cart Settings** and choose **Cart left** or **Cart right** under **Panel position**. The cart starts on the right; the change takes effect at once, and the products move to the other side.

1. **Panel position** — **Cart left** or **Cart right**.
2. **Open orders position** — whether the strip of orders still open sits at the **Top** or the **Bottom** of the cart.
3. **Sort items** — the order lines appear in, such as **Newest at top**. Fees and shipping always stay last.

Below these, **Quick Discounts** sets the three percentage keys (–5%, –10% and –15% to start with) that the cashier sees on the price keypad; tap a key to change it.

## Checkout that belongs to the POS {#checkout}

Pressing **Checkout** opens the tender pane: a keypad for what the customer hands over, the change shown as you type, and the payment methods your store offers.

- **Split payments.** Split by **Even**, **Amount**, **Percent** or **Item**: part in cash and the rest on a card, evenly between several cards, or by what each person bought. What is still owed stays on screen as **Remaining** until the order is paid.
- **Recorded payments complete offline.** A sale paid with cash or another payment recorded at the till (card on an external terminal, cash on delivery, bank transfer or cheque) finishes even when the store can't be reached, and syncs when the connection returns. Payments through an integrated reader or an online gateway still wait for a connection.
- **A slow connection holds the sale instead of failing it.** If the store is slow to answer, the till waits and tells you; it never rings the same sale twice.
- **Quick discount.** **Add Discount** takes an amount or a percentage off the order and records it as a proper discount. See [Quick discount](#quick-discount).
- **Tips.** A tip the customer adds on a card reader is recorded on the order as a fee line named **Tip**.
- **Legacy tab (WCPOS Pro).** Gateways that still use their own payment page are one tap away (see [The Legacy Tab](../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx)), so nothing you rely on today stops working. A legacy gateway charges the whole order, so it can't be used once a payment has been taken on the order, unless you cancel the payments taken so far.

### Quick discount {#quick-discount}

In the cart, press **+** (**Add to cart**), then **Add Discount**. On the **Quick discount** tab, enter the amount, choose **$** for money or **%** for a percentage, and press **Add Discount**.

</div>

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Quick discount** — an amount or a percentage off the whole order.
2. **Coupon** — applies one of your store's WooCommerce coupon codes instead.
3. **Amount** — tap it to enter the number on the keypad. A percentage has to be above 0 and no more than 100.
4. **$ / %** — money or a percentage.

</div>

1. **Add Discount** — adds it to the order. **Cancel** closes the panel without adding anything.

The discount is saved on the order as a discount, not as a fee, so WooCommerce and your reports count it as one. It shows in the cart as **Discount (10%)**, with the amount taken off, and the cross beside it removes it. It replaces the old habit of adding a negative fee, which is no longer possible. ## Card readers at the register {#card-readers}

Card terminals are driven from the tender pane, with the reader's progress shown on screen and cancel one tap away. Available with [WCPOS Pro](./free-vs-pro.mdx):

- **Stripe Terminal:** the smart readers you may already use, plus Bluetooth readers and **Tap to Pay** on a phone or tablet.
- **SumUp:** the SumUp Solo over the network, and the Solo, Solo Lite and Air over Bluetooth.
- **Mollie Terminal.**

The till remembers which reader it uses for each gateway. A reader on the network is set up on the gateway's own WooCommerce settings page; Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay are set up in the app, in **Settings → Card readers**. See [Payment Gateways](../payment/index.mdx).

## Registers and the cash drawer, in the free plugin {#registers-and-the-cash-drawer}

A cashier opens the register with a float, records cash paid in or out during the day, and closes it with a count. The count is filed as a numbered closure, and its closing report (the Z-report) is printed from the completion sheet with **Print closure**, so the end of every day is a document you can find again, not a screen you saw once. Closures can be exported as CSV from **Reports → Closures**.

Register sessions are off until you turn them on, in **WP Admin → POS → Settings → General**, under **Register**: **Open and close the register**, **Opening float**, **Ask a manager when the count is out** and **Remind the cashier to close**. Which roles may handle cash is set on the **Access** tab, and approving a closure that's out is for administrators and shop managers. The free plugin has one register, shared by every device on the counter. Naming registers, more registers and more stores come with Pro. See [Register settings](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx), [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx) and [Stores, Registers and Devices](../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx).

The **Reports** screen has a **Closures** view (the open session and its X-report, closures by business day) and a **Sales** view with margin by category and brand, when your products have a cost. The free plugin shows today on its register; Pro opens earlier days, every register and every store. See [End-of-Day Reconciliation](../pos/reconciliation.mdx) and [Reports](../reports/index.mdx).

## Customer-facing display {#customer-facing-display}

A second screen that shows the customer their cart as it's rung up: each line, fees, shipping, coupons and discounts, the totals, and the payments taken with what is left to pay. It's a web page served by your store, so any tablet, phone or monitor with a browser can be the display. Available with Pro.

To pair one, open **Settings → Customer Display** on the till and press **Generate pairing code**. On the display, open the **Display page URL** shown there and enter the 6-digit code before it expires, 10 minutes later.

1. **Pairing code** — type it on the display. Press **Generate pairing code** again for a new one.
2. **Display page URL** — the page to open on the display. **Copy URL** and **Open in browser** sit beside it.
3. **Paired displays** — each display paired with this till, shown as **Connected** or **Disconnected** with when it was last seen. **Forget** unpairs it.

From then on the display follows the till's cart by itself. Choose its look from the gallery in **WP Admin → POS → Templates → Display**.

## Receipts and refunds built for fiscal rules {#receipts-and-refunds}

Receipts now carry the register name and the software version, and have a place for a QR code required by local tax rules, left empty until a country's rules need one. Offline sales keep the time they were really made and the register they were made on. A refund or a void is recorded as a separate record that can't be changed, and prints its own receipt, as a credit note that names the sale it corrects.

This is the groundwork that country-specific fiscal rules (such as France's and Spain's) will build on. Certification for a particular country is not part of 2.0.

## Coupons in the free plugin {#coupons-in-the-free-plugin}

Applying a WooCommerce coupon code at the till no longer needs Pro: it's the **Coupon** tab of [Add Discount](#quick-discount). The Coupons screen in the POS, for browsing the catalogue, stays a Pro feature; coupons are still created in WP Admin → Marketing → Coupons.

## Requirements and removals {#requirements-and-removals}

- **WooCommerce 9.0 or later** is required. On an older store the plugin doesn't start, and WP Admin shows a notice saying so.
- **The app and the plugin move together.** A 1.11 app that meets the 2.0 plugin stops at an update notice until it's updated: the app and the plugin must both be on 2.0. See [Upgrading to 2.0](./upgrading-to-2.mdx).
- **Magnetic-stripe card readers**, an experimental Pro feature, are no longer supported.
- **Negative fees** can no longer be added; use [Add Discount](#quick-discount).
- **WooCommerce Analytics** counts a POS order when it's paid, so open or partly paid orders no longer appear in sales totals until they're completed.

## Not in 2.0 {#not-in-2-0}

- **Surcharges.** Legally uneven across countries and no gateway exposes them cleanly; they will be their own effort.
- **Country-specific fiscal certification** (France's exact dataset, Spain's VeriFactu submission). The groundwork is in 2.0 so nothing has to be redone when those land.
- **Third-party extension points for the register layout.** The layout settings are for WCPOS's own panels for now.
