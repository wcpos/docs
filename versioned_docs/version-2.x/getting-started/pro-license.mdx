WCPOS Pro is a paid WordPress plugin. It carries everything in the free WCPOS plugin, so you install it on its own, in place of the free one. This page goes from buying a licence to seeing **Pro** in the POS.

:::note You need a WordPress administrator
Installing and activating happen in WP Admin, not in the POS. Whoever does it needs a WordPress login that can install plugins.
:::

## Buy a licence {#purchase-a-license}

Pro is one plugin on your WordPress site, not something you buy per device: every device and every cashier connected to that site gets Pro.

Go to [wcpos.com/pro](https://wcpos.com/pro) and buy a licence. Buying, plan changes and renewals all happen on wcpos.com: the POS has no checkout of its own. **Upgrade to Pro** at the top of the POS opens the same page (see [What the free version shows](#free-version)). Then [install the plugin](#installation).

Pro adds the Products, Orders, Customers and Coupons screens, reports for earlier days and every register, integrated card readers, the customer display, refunds, more registers and more stores. [Free vs Pro](./free-vs-pro.mdx) has the full list.

## Install the Pro plugin {#installation}

1. Sign in at wcpos.com and open your [Downloads](https://wcpos.com/account/downloads). Download the Pro plugin. It's a zip file: keep it zipped.
2. In WP Admin, go to **Plugins**, add a new plugin and press **Upload Plugin**.
3. Choose the zip, press **Install Now**, then **Activate Plugin**.

:::note Coming from the free plugin?
You don't need to remove the free plugin first. When Pro starts, it turns the free plugin off and shows a notice saying so. You can then delete the free plugin from your **Plugins** page.
:::

## Activate your licence key {#activate}

1. Copy your key from the [Licenses](https://wcpos.com/account/licenses) page of your account.
2. In WP Admin, go to **POS** > **Settings**. Under **Account**, open **License**.
3. Paste the key into **License Key** and press **Activate**.

The screen then reads **Thank You!** and **License … has been activated.**, with your key shortened in the middle. If it shows a message instead, see [If something goes wrong](#troubleshooting).

## Check the POS runs Pro {#checking-licence-status}

Reload the POS: refresh the browser page, or close and reopen the desktop or mobile app. The upgrade strip is gone, and the Products, Orders, Customers and Coupons screens show your store's data instead of a blurred preview.

To be sure, tap the version number at the foot of the menu (**v 2.0.0**, or **Version 2.0.0** on a phone). **About WCPOS** opens.

## Updates {#updates}

New versions of Pro show up under **Dashboard** > **Updates** and on the **Plugins** page, like any other plugin. You can also download the latest zip from your [Downloads](https://wcpos.com/account/downloads).

The Pro row on the **Plugins** page tells you where your licence stands: valid and receiving updates, inactive, or expired.

## If something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

<details>
<summary>The licence screen shows a message from wcpos.com</summary>

The message comes from the licence server, which checks your key. Check for spaces before or after the pasted key, and that it's the key from your [Licenses](https://wcpos.com/account/licenses) page.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The licence has reached its activation limit</summary>

A licence activates on 2 sites: one live site and one development site. Subdomains count separately, so `example.com`, `staging.example.com` and `dev.example.com` are three. Hosts with built-in dev, test and live sites often hit this. See and free your sites on your [Licenses](https://wcpos.com/account/licenses) page, or email **support@wcpos.com**: extra activations are granted on request.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Activate fails with a network error</summary>

Activation goes from the browser you're using for WP Admin straight to wcpos.com. Check that this browser can open wcpos.com, then press **Activate** again with ad or privacy blockers turned off, or in another browser.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Pro never offers updates</summary>

Update checks go from your WordPress server to `updates.wcpos.com`. A security plugin or your host's firewall can block them: ask your host to allow outgoing requests to `updates.wcpos.com`.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The upgrade strip is still there after activating</summary>

Reload the POS (refresh the page, or close and reopen the app). If **About WCPOS** still shows **Status** **Free**, open WP Admin, go to **POS** > **Settings** and, under **Account**, open **License**. Check that it reads **License … has been activated.**

</details>

<details>
<summary>Your licence has expired</summary>

Pro keeps working: nothing in the POS switches off. The **Plugins** page says "Your WCPOS Pro license has expired. Please renew to receive updates." Renew from your [account](https://wcpos.com/account/licenses) to get updates again; your key stays the same.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Moving the site to a new domain</summary>

Free the activation on the old site first: in WP Admin, go to **POS** > **Settings**, under **Account** open **License**, and press **Deactivate**. Then activate the same key on the new site. Deactivating the plugin itself doesn't free the activation. If the old site is gone, free it from your [Licenses](https://wcpos.com/account/licenses) page or email **support@wcpos.com**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Uninstalling Pro and your data</summary>

Deleting the Pro plugin removes Pro's own working data and translation files. It keeps your Pro settings, including the licence key, and your stores, so reinstalling picks up where you left off. Order and product data is never touched.

The translation files Pro shares with the free plugin go only when the free plugin is no longer installed.

For a clean slate that also removes settings, the licence key and stores, turn on the full wipe before you delete the plugin: add `define( 'WCPOS_REMOVE_ALL_DATA', true );` to `wp-config.php`, or set the `wcpos_remove_all_data` option to `yes`. The constant wins when both are set. A full wipe can't be undone.

</details>
