WCPOS 2.0 is a major update: a new register, a new checkout and a cash drawer. This page is for whoever clicks **Update**. Read it to the end before you do.

:::warning Sync every till first
The first time a till opens 2.0, it clears the 1.11 data on that device. A sale the till hasn't sent to your store by then is lost. Let every till finish syncing before you update.
:::

## Can you upgrade today? {#can-you-upgrade-today}

Open **WP Admin → Plugins** and check two things.

- **WooCommerce is 9.0 or later.** 2.0 needs it. Older than 9.0: see [If your store isn't ready yet](#if-your-store-isnt-ready-yet), and don't update WCPOS first.
- **You have WCPOS Pro if you use a card terminal extension.** From 2.0, Stripe Terminal, SumUp, Mollie Terminal and the other terminal extensions need Pro. See [Free vs Pro](./free-vs-pro.mdx).

Both true: carry on with [Before you click Update](#before-you-click-update).

## Before you click Update {#before-you-click-update}

1. **Sync every till.** Open each till, then open **Store health → Database** from its menu. Its **changes waiting to send** must read **0**. Anything above 0 hasn't reached your store yet: keep the till online and wait. 2.0 starts each device with a new local database and does not carry over anything a 1.11 till hasn't sent.
2. **Back up the site**, or ask your host for a restore point. The update doesn't change your orders, but a major update deserves a way back.
3. **Pick a quiet hour.** Every till reloads into the new register, and a sale that is half-way through checkout on the old version has to be rung up again.
4. **Check your Pro licence is active**, in **WP Admin → POS → Settings → License**. Pro updates arrive only with a valid licence. See [Installing WCPOS Pro](./pro-license.mdx).
5. **Tell your cashiers about discounts.** If they take money off by adding a *negative fee*, that stops working. 2.0 has **Add Discount** instead. See [What changes on day one](#what-changes-on-day-one).
6. **Update your terminal extensions too.** Stripe Terminal, SumUp and Mollie Terminal each have a 2.0 release. An extension that hasn't been updated still shows on the **Legacy** tab of checkout, but it can't drive a reader from the tender pane.

## Update the plugin and the tills {#updating}

1. **Update the plugin.** On a free site, update **WCPOS**. With Pro, update **WCPOS Pro** only: it already contains the free plugin and switches off the separate copy. A notice says so, and you can delete the free plugin.
2. **Open the POS on every device** and let it finish its first sync. Browser tills pick up 2.0 as soon as the plugin is updated. Desktop and mobile apps update through their usual channels.
3. **Check each till is on 2.0.** Tap the version at the foot of the menu (**v 2.0.0**, or **Version 2.0.0** on a phone). **About WCPOS** opens.

The app and the plugin must both be on 2.0. A till that is out of step stops at a screen saying so:

- **A 1.11 app on a 2.0 store** asks for an app update. See [SYNC341](../error-codes/SYNC341.mdx). If it still has sales it hasn't sent, don't update it yet: see [A 1.11 till still has unsent sales](#unsent-sales).
- **A 2.0 app on a store still on 1.11** reports **AUTH331**. Update the plugin, then reconnect. See [AUTH331](../error-codes/AUTH331.mdx).

## What changes on day one {#what-changes-on-day-one}

Your cashiers see a new register the first time they open the POS. Most of it works as before; these are the changes they will notice. [What's new in 2.0](./whats-new.mdx) has the full list.

### Discounts replace negative fees {#discounts}

A fee can no longer be negative. To take money off, press **+** in the cart and choose **Add Discount**. Orders that already carry a negative fee are unchanged.

### Checkout is the tender pane {#checkout}

Pressing **Checkout** opens a keypad beside the cart instead of WooCommerce's payment page. Type what the customer hands over and the change is shown. One sale can be split across cash and cards.

### Also new on day one {#also-new}

- **A new look.** Forms and pickers slide in as panels. A **Scale** setting in **Settings → Theme** sizes the POS for your device. You can put the cart on the left and add your own quick filter buttons. See [What's new: A new register](./whats-new.mdx#a-new-register).
- **WooCommerce Analytics counts a POS order once it's paid.** Orders still open or only partly paid at the till no longer count as sales, so totals that included them will be lower.
- **Receipts carry the register name and the software version.** A refund prints its own receipt, headed **Refund**, naming the sale it corrects. Custom receipt templates keep working, and show the new details only if you add them. See [Receipts](../receipts/index.mdx).
- **POS Only products get their catalog visibility set to Hidden**, once, when the plugin updates, so they stay out of your online shop.
- **Magnetic-stripe card readers are no longer supported.** Take cards on a paired reader (Pro) or on your own terminal, and record the payment at the till.

<details>
<summary>Registers and the cash drawer: off until you turn them on</summary>

2.0 can open and close the register with a float and a count, in the free plugin too. Nothing changes on day one: it stays off until a manager turns on **Open and close the register** in **WP Admin → POS → Settings → General**, under **Register**. With it on, the POS asks for a float before the first sale, and **Close register** counts the drawer and writes a numbered closure.

The free plugin has one register, shared by every device. More registers come with Pro. See [Registers](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) and [Open the Register](../pos/register/index.mdx).

</details>

## If your store isn't ready yet {#if-your-store-isnt-ready-yet}

**Stay on WCPOS 1.11.** It keeps working, and it keeps receiving fixes for six months after 2.0 is released.

1. **Turn off automatic updates** for WCPOS and WCPOS Pro. In **WP Admin → Plugins**, each plugin row has an **Enable auto-updates** / **Disable auto-updates** link. Make sure it reads **Enable auto-updates**, which means they are off. This stops an automatic update from reaching a till mid-shift.
2. **Update WooCommerce to 9.0 or later** when you're ready. WooCommerce's own release notes cover that update.
3. **Come back to [Before you click Update](#before-you-click-update).**
4. **Once you're on 2.0, turn automatic updates back on** if you used them, or later fixes won't arrive by themselves.

### If WCPOS updated before WooCommerce did {#if-wcpos-updated-first}

The POS won't open until WooCommerce is 9.0 or later. Nothing is lost. Either update WooCommerce now, or [reinstall WCPOS 1.11](./previous-versions.mdx) and turn off automatic updates as above.

## Rolling back {#rolling-back}

### A 1.11 till still has unsent sales {#unsent-sales}

The till shows an update screen and its sales haven't reached your store. They are still on that device, until it opens 2.0. To send them:

1. **Leave that till as it is.** Don't update the app, and on a browser till don't reload the page: either opens 2.0, which clears the unsent sales.
2. **[Reinstall the 1.11 plugin](./previous-versions.mdx)** on your store.
3. **Let the till sync** until **changes waiting to send** in **Store health → Database** reads **0**. Its sales are now in the store.
4. **Update to 2.0 again**, from [Update the plugin and the tills](#updating).

### Go back to 1.11 {#back-to-1-11}

If 2.0 isn't working for you, tell us on [Discord](https://wcpos.com/discord) first. Most upgrade-day problems have a quicker fix than a rollback.

:::warning Sync before you roll back
Rolling back to 1.11 also starts each till from an empty local database. Let every 2.0 till finish syncing first, or its unsent sales are lost.
:::

To roll back, [reinstall the 1.11 plugin](./previous-versions.mdx). Browser tills and apps still on 1.11 carry on as before.

**A desktop or mobile app already on 2.0 can't connect to a 1.11 plugin** (it reports [AUTH331](../error-codes/AUTH331.mdx)), and the app stores don't offer older versions. Use the browser POS at `yourdomain.com/pos` on that till until you upgrade again.

Orders made on 2.0 are ordinary WooCommerce orders and stay where they are. Register sessions and closures recorded on 2.0 stay in the database, but 1.11 doesn't show them.
