## What is WCPOS? {#what-is-wcpos}

WCPOS is a point of sale (POS) for WooCommerce. It sells from the same products, stock and prices as your online store, and every sale becomes a WooCommerce order. It runs in a web browser, or as an app for Windows, macOS and Linux, with iOS and Android apps in beta.

## Find your way around {#find-your-way-around}

This is the POS after a search for "watch", with the **In Stock** filter on and one watch in the cart. Everything else in these docs starts from here. [POS Screen Overview](../pos/index.mdx) takes each part in turn.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[36, -12], [124, 0], [-30, 0], [10, 28], [72, 0], [0, -72], [-42, 0], [40, 28], [0, 0]]} alt="The POS with a watch in the cart: the menu down the left, the search box and products, the product count, and on the right the store, notifications, your avatar, the customer and the Checkout button">

1. **Menu** — **POS** is where you sell. **Products**, **Orders**, **Coupons** and **Customers** need [WCPOS Pro](./free-vs-pro.mdx#management-screens). **Reports** has today's sales and closures. **Health**, **Settings** and **Support** sit at the foot.
2. **Register bar** — the store this device is on, and the register's name when the store has more than one. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx).
3. **Notifications** — announcements, update notices and licence reminders from WCPOS. See [Notifications](../support/notifications.mdx).
4. **Your avatar** — the cashier sheet: the cash drawer, handing the till to someone else, and signing out. See [Open the Register](../pos/register/index.mdx#cashier-sheet).
5. **Search** — type a name or SKU, or scan a barcode. See [Search and Filtering](../pos/product-panel/search-filtering.mdx).
6. **Products** — tap one to add it to the cart. See [The Product Panel](../pos/product-panel/index.mdx).
7. **Product count** — how many products match. The arrow beside it refreshes from your store.
8. **Customer** — who the sale is for. Tap to choose someone else. See [The Cart](../pos/cart/index.mdx).
9. **Checkout** — names the total. Press it to take payment. See [Checkout](../pos/checkout/index.mdx).

</JourneyShot>

On a phone, the menu is behind the button at the top left, and the cart is the **Cart** tab at the bottom.

## Get Started in 5 Minutes {#get-started-in-5-minutes}

Cashiers: if your store already uses WCPOS, [start at step 2](#open-the-pos). Step 1 is for the store owner.

<Steps>

<StepCard number={1} title="Install the plugin (store owner)">

Install the free [WCPOS plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/woocommerce-pos/) on your WordPress site:

1. In WP Admin, go to **Plugins** and add a new plugin.
2. Search for "WCPOS".
3. Press **Install Now**, then **Activate**.

See [Installation](./installation.mdx) for what your server and devices need.

</StepCard>

<StepCard number={2} title="Open the POS">

<Anchor id="open-the-pos" />

**In a browser:** go to `yourdomain.com/pos` and sign in with your WordPress username and password.

**In the app** ([download it](#where-it-runs)): type your store's address, press **Connect**, then **Sign in with WordPress**, and press **Open POS**.

See [Connect](./connect.mdx) for more stores and more cashiers on one device.

</StepCard>

<StepCard number={3} title="Let the POS start">

Each time the POS opens, it shows the WCPOS logo over a bar. Wait for the bar to fill: then the POS opens. In the app, with nobody signed in yet, the **Connect** screen opens instead. If it doesn't open, see [If the POS won't start](#wont-start).

<details>
<summary>What the bar is doing</summary>

The bar fills as the POS opens what it keeps on this device, checks how it reaches your store (in a browser), and opens the store you used last: in the app as the same cashier, in a browser as whoever is signed in to WordPress.

The bar is about starting the POS, not about your products. Those download next, while you sell.

</details>

</StepCard>

<StepCard number={4} title="Sell while your products download">

You don't need to wait for the whole catalogue. Products keep downloading while you sell, and a search asks your store for anything that isn't on this device yet.

<details>
<summary>Why the product count starts low</summary>

The POS downloads the catalogue in small batches in the background, so it doesn't overload your store's server.

With **All products** showing, **Showing … of …** under the products counts what's on this device, so it starts low on a new device and climbs. See [Product Synchronisation](../products/sync.mdx).

</details>

</StepCard>

<StepCard number={5} title="Make your first sale" isLast>

If the POS says **Price check only until the register is open**, [open the register](../pos/register/index.mdx) first. **Search**, the products and **Checkout** are labelled in [Find your way around](#find-your-way-around), above. Then, for a cash sale:

1. Type in **Search** or scan a product, and tap the product into the cart.
2. On a phone, open the **Cart** tab. Press **Checkout**. The amount due fills in.
3. Choose **Cash**, then type what the customer hands you, or tap a quick amount. The change appears as you type.
4. Press **Take**. The paid screen shows the change to give and the receipt.
5. Print or email the receipt, then press **New sale** for the next customer.

Here a customer hands over $50.00 for a $45.00 sale:

<JourneyShot manifest={checkoutManifest} device="tablet" step={8} offsets={[[-40, 0], [-70, 0], [-75, 0], [-90, 0], [-4, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The payment screen with $50.00 tendered on a $45.00 cash sale: the close button, To pay $45.00, Change $5.00 as the big figure, Tendered $50.00 under it, Cash picked and the Take button">

1. **✕** — back to the cart, with the order still on it.
2. **To pay** — the balance still due.
3. **Change** — what to give back.
4. **Tendered** — what you typed or tapped.
5. **Cash** — the method this payment goes on.
6. **Take** — names the amount and the method. Press it to record the payment.

</JourneyShot>

<details>
<summary>See the paid screen (from another sale): change, Print Receipt, New sale</summary>

<JourneyShot manifest={checkoutManifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[279, 0], [260, 0], [292, 0], [292, 0], [0, 0], [48, -38], [49, -38], [128, 0]]} alt="The paid screen after a cash sale: the change to give, the amount paid and tendered, the Template picker on Standard Receipt and the Printer picker, the receipt preview, and the Email Receipt, Download PDF, Print Receipt and New sale buttons">

1. **Change** — what to give back.
2. **Paid … · tendered … in cash** — what the sale came to and what the customer handed you.
3. **Template** — which receipt this sale gets. See [Customising Receipts](../receipts/customise.mdx).
4. **Printer** — where it prints. See [Print Receipt](../receipts/at-checkout.mdx#print-receipt).
5. **Preview** — the receipt as it will print.
6. **Print Receipt** — prints it.
7. **Email Receipt** — sends it to the customer. See [Email Receipt](../receipts/at-checkout.mdx#email-receipt).
8. **New sale** — an empty cart for the next customer, back on your default customer.

</JourneyShot>

</details>

See [Checkout](../pos/checkout/index.mdx) for the whole sale, and [Payment Methods](../payment/index.mdx) for cards and other ways to pay.

</StepCard>

</Steps>

## If the POS won't start {#wont-start}

**The bar stops partway.** In a browser, the POS is checking how it reaches your store. On a slow connection this can take a little while, and it carries on by itself.

**"Something went wrong".** The POS couldn't start on this device. Under the message it shows what failed. Press **Try again**: it keeps everything on this device. If the same message comes back, note it and [contact support](../support/index.mdx).

:::warning

Don't press **Reset app data and restart** on that screen unless support asks you to. It deletes everything on this device, including sales not yet sent to your store. Before it does, it asks you to confirm, and warns you about any changes that haven't reached your store.

:::

**"The POS is open in another tab".** Only one tab runs the POS. Press **Take over here**, or go back to the other tab.

**The app opens on the Connect screen.** Nobody is signed in on this device yet, or you signed out. See [Connect](./connect.mdx#troubleshooting) if signing in won't finish.

## Key Features {#key-features}

The free plugin rings up sales: cash, cards taken on your own terminal, one register with its cash drawer, receipts, coupons in the cart, barcode scanning, and sales that carry on when the connection drops. See [Free vs Pro](./free-vs-pro.mdx) for the full comparison.

<details>
<summary>What the free plugin does</summary>

<FeatureGrid>
  <FeatureCard icon="search" title="Fast Local Search">
    Products are kept on the device, so search answers at once
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="wifi" title="Keeps Selling Offline">
    Search products and customers, and take cash for a sale that reached your store before the connection dropped. See [Offline Mode](./offline.mdx)
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="barcode" title="Barcode Scanning">
    A USB or Bluetooth scanner, or the device camera
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="credit-card" title="Cash & Card Payments">
    Cash with change worked out, cards taken on your own terminal, and split payments
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="print" title="Receipt Printing">
    Print or email receipts from your store's templates
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="calculator" title="Automatic Tax">
    Tax worked out from your WooCommerce tax settings
  </FeatureCard>
</FeatureGrid>

</details>

## Pro Features {#pro-features}

[WCPOS Pro](https://wcpos.com/pro) adds the screens for running the store from the POS (products, orders, customers, coupons, and reports for earlier days), refunds, connected card readers and other payment gateways, the customer display, and more registers and stores.

<details>
<summary>What Pro adds</summary>

<FeatureGrid>
  <FeatureCard icon="box" title="Products Management" variant="pro">
    Change stock and prices right in the POS
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="file-invoice" title="Orders and Refunds" variant="pro">
    Find an older order, reprint its receipt, edit it or refund it
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="users" title="Customers Management" variant="pro">
    Add and edit customers
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="chart-pie" title="Reports" variant="pro">
    Earlier days, every register and every store
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="credit-card" title="Card Readers" variant="pro">
    Connected readers such as Stripe Terminal and SumUp, and other payment gateways
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="store" title="Registers and Stores" variant="pro">
    Several registers per store, and several stores from one WooCommerce site
  </FeatureCard>
</FeatureGrid>

</details>
