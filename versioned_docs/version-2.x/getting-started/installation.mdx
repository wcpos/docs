WCPOS is a plugin for your WooCommerce site. Install it once and the POS is ready in a browser at `yourdomain.com/pos`. The desktop and mobile apps are optional: they open the same POS and sign in to the same site.

Installing is a job for whoever looks after the website. Cashiers can start at [Open the POS](#accessing-the-pos).

## Check your site is ready {#server-requirements}

- **WooCommerce 9.0** or later, installed and active. Check under **Plugins** in WP Admin.
- **WordPress 5.6** or later, or 5.8 with Pro. Check on the **Dashboard** in WP Admin.
- **PHP 7.4** or later. Check in your hosting account, or ask your host.
- **Permalinks** set to anything except **Plain**. Check under **Settings › Permalinks** in WP Admin.

WCPOS 2.0 needs WooCommerce 9.0; 1.x ran on older versions. If your store is on WCPOS 1.x today, read [Upgrading to 2.0](./upgrading-to-2.mdx) before you update anything.

For a busy store, the server matters as much as these minimums. See [Server performance](../support/performance/server.mdx#minimum-server-requirements).

## Install the plugin {#installing-the-plugin}

1. In WP Admin, go to **Plugins** and add a new plugin.
2. Search for **WCPOS**. In the results the plugin is **WCPOS – Point of Sale (POS) plugin for WooCommerce**, by kilbot.
3. Press **Install Now**, then **Activate**.

WordPress installs the current release from the WordPress.org plugin directory. This page describes WCPOS 2.0 or later: once the plugin is active, [Check what's installed](#check-versions) shows its version under **Server**.

WP Admin now has a **POS** menu. **View POS** opens the POS, **Settings** holds the plugin's settings, and **Templates** holds the receipt templates. On the free plugin the menu also has **Upgrade to Pro**.

## Open the POS {#accessing-the-pos}

1. In a browser, go to `yourdomain.com/pos`, or choose **POS › View POS** in WP Admin.
2. Sign in with your WordPress username and password.
3. If the store has several registers, the POS asks **Which register is this device at?** the first time. Pick the one you're at. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx).
4. If the cart shows an **Open register** card, enter the cash in the drawer and press **Open register**. It shows only with **Open and close the register** turned on, while the register is closed. See [Registers](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx).

Ready to serve a customer? Follow [Checkout](../pos/checkout/index.mdx) to make your first sale. Here is the POS open, with a search typed in and the **In Stock** filter on. Clear both to see the rest of your products, or see [Search & Filtering](../pos/product-panel/search-filtering.mdx#no-match) if one is missing:

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[40, 0], [114, 0], [-9, 22], [141, 0], [44, 0], [44, 0], [44, 0]]} alt="The POS open in a browser: the menu down the left with POS selected and Health, Settings and Support at its foot, the product search, the store name and the cashier's avatar above the cart">

1. **POS** — opens the register screen: products on the left, the cart on the right. On a phone, they're on separate tabs. See [Checkout](../pos/checkout/index.mdx).
2. **Register bar** — the store you're selling for, and the register when the store has more than one. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx).
3. **Your avatar** — your cashier sheet: the drawer, handing over and signing out. In a browser it also holds the **Desktop App** link. See [Your cashier sheet](../pos/register/index.mdx#cashier-sheet).
4. **Search products** — find a product by name, SKU or barcode. See [Products](../pos/product-panel/index.mdx).
5. **Health** — whether this device and your store are talking properly, and what to do if not. See [Store Health](../support/store-health.mdx).
6. **Settings** — language, printing, card readers, barcode scanning, theme and more. See [Settings](../settings/index.mdx).
7. **Support** — the WCPOS community on Discord, inside the POS. See [Support](../support/index.mdx).

</JourneyShot>

The first time you open it, the POS copies your products onto the device so it can keep selling through a dropped connection. See [Offline](./offline.mdx).

Administrators and shop managers can use the POS straight away. For everyone else, the plugin adds a **Cashier** role: give it to the people who sell. See [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).

### Do you need to be signed in to WordPress? {#sign-in}

Yes. The POS uses your WordPress account: there's no separate POS login. If this browser isn't signed in to WordPress, `/pos` opens the WordPress sign-in page, and the POS opens once you've signed in.

## Get the desktop and mobile apps {#app-requirements}

The browser needs nothing else. Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, with JavaScript on, in a normal window: a private window can clear the POS's data on the device mid-session, and deletes it when it closes.

The apps run the same POS in their own window. Download the one for your device:

- **Windows** — Windows 10 or later, 64-bit (x64). Download the [Windows installer](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/win32-x64).
- **macOS** — macOS 12 Monterey or later. Download for [Apple silicon](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/darwin-arm64) or [Intel](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/darwin-x64).
- **Linux** — 64-bit (x64). Download the [.deb for Debian and Ubuntu](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/linux-x64) or the [.rpm for Fedora and Red Hat](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/linux-x64-rpm).
- **iPhone and iPad** — iOS and iPadOS 16.4 or later. Join the [TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/JGBdVRrW).
- **Android** — Android 10 or later. Join the [Google Play beta](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.wcpos.main).

Open the app and add your store on its **Connect** screen. See [Connect to Your Store](./connect.mdx).

In the POS in a browser, the **Desktop App** link in your cashier sheet opens the desktop app's download page too.

2. **Desktop App** — the desktop app's download page.

## With WCPOS Pro {#with-pro}

### Which download is Pro? {#which-download}

Once installed, the two plugins have these names on your **Plugins** page (the WordPress.org listing above uses a longer name for the free one):

- **WCPOS – Point of Sale for WooCommerce** is the free plugin, from WordPress.org.
- **WCPOS Pro – Point of Sale for WooCommerce** is Pro. It comes only from the [Downloads](https://wcpos.com/account/downloads) page of your wcpos.com account, as a zip file.

### Install Pro over the free plugin without losing data {#pro-over-free}

Install Pro with the free plugin still in place: you don't remove anything first. When Pro starts, it switches the free plugin off and shows a notice saying so.

Your data stays. Pro has the free plugin's code inside it, so it uses the POS settings, receipt templates and registers already on your site. Switching the free plugin off doesn't delete them, and your orders, products and customers belong to WooCommerce: neither plugin removes them.

Then activate your licence key: see [Activate your licence key](./pro-license.mdx#activate).

## If something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

**"WCPOS requires WooCommerce 9.0 or higher"** at the top of WP Admin. WooCommerce is missing, switched off or older than 9.0. Install or update it, then activate it. On a store still running WCPOS 1.x, follow [Upgrading to 2.0](./upgrading-to-2.mdx#if-your-store-isnt-ready-yet). {/* pending: docs#675 */}

**"WCPOS requires PHP 7.4 or higher"**. Ask your host to move the site to a newer PHP version.

**"WooCommerce REST API requires pretty permalinks to work correctly"**, or `yourdomain.com/pos` shows your site's page-not-found. See [`/pos` shows page not found](#pos-not-found).

**"Update the WCPOS plugin on your WordPress site."** The app is newer than the plugin. Update WCPOS in **Plugins**. See [AUTH331](../error-codes/AUTH331.mdx).

**"Update the POS app on this device."** The plugin is newer than the app. In a browser, reload the page. In an app, install its update. See [SYNC341](../error-codes/SYNC341.mdx).

**The sign-in window shows an error or a blank page.** A security, firewall or login plugin is usually in the way. See [Plugin Conflicts](../support/troubleshooting/plugin-conflicts.mdx#security-and-login-plugins).

**"This account cannot use the POS"** after you sign in. Your WordPress role isn't allowed to use the POS. See [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx#goes-wrong).

**Still stuck?** Ask on [Discord](https://wcpos.com/discord). For licence or private questions, email support@wcpos.com. See [Support](../support/index.mdx).

### `/pos` shows page not found {#pos-not-found}

Your site's page-not-found at `yourdomain.com/pos`, signed in or not, has two usual causes:

- **Permalinks are set to Plain.** The POS address needs any other setting. WP Admin shows **"WooCommerce REST API requires pretty permalinks to work correctly"**: press **Enable permalinks** on the notice, or open **Settings › Permalinks**, choose any other setting and press **Save Changes**.
- **The POS has another address.** An administrator can change `pos` to something else: look for **POS base** in **Settings › Permalinks**. See [Open the POS in a browser](./connect.mdx#web).
