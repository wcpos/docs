WCPOS is a plugin for your WooCommerce site. Install it once and the POS is ready in a browser at `yourdomain.com/pos`. The desktop and mobile apps are optional: they open the same POS and sign in to the same site.

Installing is a job for whoever looks after the website. Cashiers can start at [Open the POS](#accessing-the-pos).

## Check your site is ready {#server-requirements}

- **WooCommerce 9.0** or later, installed and active. Check under **Plugins** in WP Admin.
- **WordPress 5.6** or later. Check on the **Dashboard** in WP Admin.
- **PHP 7.4** or later. Check in your hosting account, or ask your host.
- **Permalinks** set to anything except **Plain**. Check under **Settings › Permalinks** in WP Admin.

WCPOS 2.0 needs WooCommerce 9.0; 1.x ran on older versions. If your store is on WCPOS 1.x today, read [Upgrading to 2.0](./upgrading-to-2.mdx) before you update anything.

For a busy store, the server matters as much as these minimums. See [Server performance](../support/performance/server.mdx#minimum-server-requirements).

## Install the plugin {#installing-the-plugin}

1. In WP Admin, go to **Plugins** and add a new plugin.
2. Search for **WCPOS**. The plugin is **WCPOS – Point of Sale (POS) plugin for WooCommerce**, by kilbot.
3. Press **Install Now**, then **Activate**.

WordPress installs the current release from the WordPress.org plugin directory. To see which version you have, use [Check what's installed](#check-versions).

WP Admin now has a **POS** menu. **View POS** opens the POS, **Settings** holds the plugin's settings, and **Templates** holds the receipt templates. On the free plugin the menu also has **Upgrade to Pro**.

You can also download the plugin from [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/woocommerce-pos/) and add it with **Upload Plugin** on the same screen.

:::info With WCPOS Pro
Pro is its own plugin and contains everything in the free one: install **WCPOS Pro** and activate your licence. If the free plugin is installed too, it stands aside while Pro is active. See [Installing WCPOS Pro](./pro-license.mdx#installation).
:::

## Open the POS {#accessing-the-pos}

In a browser, go to `yourdomain.com/pos`, or choose **POS › View POS** in WP Admin. Sign in with your WordPress username and password.

Administrators and shop managers can use the POS straight away. For everyone else, the plugin adds a **Cashier** role: give it to the people who sell. See [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).

The first time you open it, the POS copies your products onto the device so it can keep selling through a dropped connection. See [Offline](./offline.mdx#how-the-local-database-works).

## Get the desktop and mobile apps {#app-requirements}

The browser needs nothing else. Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, in a normal window: a private window deletes the POS's data on the device when it closes.

The apps run the same POS in their own window. Download the one for your device:

- **Windows** — Windows 10 or later, 64-bit (x64). Download the [Windows installer](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/win32-x64).
- **macOS** — macOS 12 Monterey or later. Download for [Apple silicon](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/darwin-arm64) or [Intel](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/darwin-x64).
- **Linux** — 64-bit (x64). Download the [.deb for Debian and Ubuntu](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/linux-x64-deb) or the [.rpm for Fedora and Red Hat](https://updates.wcpos.com/v1/electron/download/linux-x64-rpm).
- **iPhone and iPad** — iOS and iPadOS 16.4 or later. Join the [TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/JGBdVRrW).
- **Android** — Android 10 or later. Join the [Google Play beta](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.wcpos.main).

Open the app and add your store on its **Connect** screen. See [Connect to Your Store](./connect.mdx).

In the POS in a browser, the **Desktop App** link in your cashier sheet opens the desktop app's download page too.

## Check what's installed {#check-versions}

Tap the version number at the foot of the menu, such as **v 2.0.0**, to open **About WCPOS**. On a phone it reads **Version 2.0.0**, in the menu that the **Menu** button opens.

- **Application** — the version of the POS on this device, and where it runs: **Web**, **Desktop**, **iOS** or **Android**.
- **Server** — the **WCPOS Plugin** version, or **WCPOS Pro Plugin** with Pro, then the WooCommerce and WordPress versions on your site.
- **License** — **Status** reads **Pro** or **Free**.
- **Release Notes** — opens the current WCPOS versions and what changed in them.

## If something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

**"WCPOS requires WooCommerce 9.0 or higher"** at the top of WP Admin. WooCommerce is missing, switched off or older than 9.0. Install or update it, then activate it. On a store still running WCPOS 1.x, follow [Upgrading to 2.0](./upgrading-to-2.mdx#if-your-store-isnt-ready-yet).

**"WCPOS requires PHP 7.4 or higher"**. Ask your host to move the site to a newer PHP version.

**"WooCommerce REST API requires pretty permalinks to work correctly"**, or `yourdomain.com/pos` shows your site's page-not-found. Permalinks are set to **Plain**. Press **Enable permalinks** on the notice, or open **Settings › Permalinks**, choose any other setting and press **Save Changes**.

**`/pos` still isn't found.** An administrator may have changed the address: look for **POS base** in **Settings › Permalinks**. See [Open the POS in a browser](./connect.mdx#web).

**"Update the WCPOS plugin on your WordPress site."** The app is newer than the plugin. Update WCPOS in **Plugins**. See [AUTH331](../error-codes/AUTH331.mdx).

**"Update the POS app on this device."** The plugin is newer than the app. In a browser, reload the page. In an app, install its update. See [SYNC341](../error-codes/SYNC341.mdx).

**The sign-in window shows an error or a blank page.** A security, firewall or login plugin is usually in the way. See [Plugin Conflicts](../support/troubleshooting/plugin-conflicts.mdx#security-and-login-plugins).
