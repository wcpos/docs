WCPOS comes in two editions. The **free plugin** rings up sales: cash, cards taken on a separate card terminal, one register with its cash drawer, receipts, coupons in the cart, and sales that carry on through a dropped connection. **WCPOS Pro** adds the screens for running the store from the POS (Products, Orders, Customers, Coupons, and Reports beyond today), refunds, connected card readers and other payment gateways, the customer display, more registers and more stores.

If you need a till that records sales, the free plugin is enough. If you also want to change stock and prices, look up an old order, refund a customer or take cards on a paired reader, that's Pro. Prices, and what happens when a licence ends, are under [Pricing](#pricing).

## What's in the free plugin {#whats-in-the-free-plugin}

The free plugin from [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/woocommerce-pos/) covers ringing up a sale, from the first product to the receipt: cash and card, one register, coupons in the cart, receipts and printing, offline sales, barcode scanning and customer tax IDs, on every platform.

<details>
<summary>The free plugin, feature by feature</summary>

<FeatureGrid>
  <FeatureCard icon="cash-register" title="Cash and card">
    **Cash** works out the change. **Card** records a payment you took on your own terminal. Split a sale between them. See [Payment Methods](../payment/index.mdx).
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="calculator" title="One register">
    One register, shared by every device on the counter. Open it with a float, move cash in and out, then count the drawer and close it with a numbered closing report. Today's sales and closures are in [Reports](../reports/index.mdx). See [Open the Register](../pos/register/index.mdx).
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="gift" title="Coupons in the cart">
    Apply your WooCommerce coupons to a sale. WooCommerce's rules still apply: expiry, usage limits, and product and category restrictions. See [Applying Coupons](../coupons/applying-coupons.mdx).
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="cart-shopping" title="Cart and product search">
    Search or scan products, change quantities and discount a line. Add a product that isn't in your catalogue, a fee, a discount or shipping. See [The Cart](../pos/cart/index.mdx).
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="receipt" title="Receipts and printing">
    Print or email the receipt, using any of the templates your store publishes from the [template gallery](../receipts/customise.mdx). Print to a thermal printer over the network, Bluetooth or USB.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="sync" title="Offline sales">
    Products and orders are kept on the device, so the till stays quick and keeps selling through a dropped connection. Orders sync back to WooCommerce when it returns. See [Offline Mode](./offline.mdx).
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="barcode" title="Barcode scanning">
    Scan into the cart with the device camera, or a USB or Bluetooth scanner. See [Barcode Scanners](../hardware/scanners/index.mdx).
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="file-invoice" title="Customer Tax IDs">
    A tax ID field on each customer (VAT, ABN, GSTIN, CPF and others), copied onto the order so it prints on the receipt. See [Customer Tax IDs](../settings/wp-admin/customer-tax-ids.mdx).
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="globe" title="Every platform, your language">
    The browser, the desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, and the iOS and Android apps. See [Installation](./installation.mdx). Set the POS [language](../settings/store/general.mdx#language) in Settings.
  </FeatureCard>
</FeatureGrid>

</details>

:::note Customers and coupons on the free plugin
You can choose an existing customer for a sale and apply a coupon in the cart. Adding or editing customers, and creating coupons, need Pro. You can always create coupons in **WP Admin › Marketing › Coupons**.
:::

## What Pro adds {#what-pro-adds}

Pro opens the rest of the POS: the management screens, more ways to take payment, and more registers and stores.

### Management screens {#management-screens}

| Screen | What you can do with Pro |
|---|---|
| **[Products](../products/index.mdx)** | Change stock and prices right in the product list. |
| **[Orders](../orders/index.mdx)** | Find an older order, re-open it, reprint its receipt, and refund it. |
| **[Customers](../customers/index.mdx)** | Add customers and edit their details. |
| **[Reports](../reports/index.mdx)** | Earlier days, every register and every store, and the closures history. |
| **[Coupons](../coupons/index.mdx)** | Look up your coupons, and create new ones if you're allowed to. |

### Payment gateways {#payment-gateways}

The free plugin records cash, and card payments taken on a separate card terminal. Pro also takes payment through connected card readers and other payment services.

With Pro, you can turn on your store's other payment methods (WooCommerce calls them gateways) for the POS in [Checkout settings](../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx). The WCPOS options below each need their own plugin.

### Support {#support}

- **Priority support** comes with a licence. See [Pricing](#pricing) for how long.

## Pricing {#pricing}

Pro is sold from [wcpos.com/pro](https://wcpos.com/pro). Each licence covers two sites: one live site and one for development.

- **Yearly** — $129. Updates and priority support for one year. It doesn't renew by itself.
- **Lifetime** — $399, paid once. Updates for as long as Pro is made.

Prices as of November 2026.

When a Yearly licence ends, nothing switches off: the Pro version you have keeps working, with all its features. New updates and priority support stop until you renew.

:::tip Try it first
Every Pro screen's **View Demo** button opens that screen on the WCPOS demo store at [demo.wcpos.com/pos](https://demo.wcpos.com/pos).
:::

## Ready to upgrade? {#ready-to-upgrade}

[Installing WCPOS Pro](./pro-license.mdx) has the download and activation steps.

- **Pro 1.8 and later** is a standalone plugin. Deactivate and delete the free plugin before you activate it.
- **Pro before 1.8** was an add-on that ran alongside the free plugin. Moving from it, follow the same steps.
