## What's coming after 2.0 {#what-were-working-on}

The next three releases each have one theme. They are planned in this order.

### 2.1: Fiscal compliance {#fiscal-compliance}

For stores in countries whose law requires the till to prove that no sale was altered.

- **Tamper-evident records**: signed records, numbering with no gaps, official closure reports, an audit file to export and, where the country demands it, reporting to the tax authority from the POS. France (NF525) and Spain (VeriFactu) come first. 2.0 laid the groundwork; see [Receipts and refunds built for fiscal rules](./whats-new.mdx#receipts-and-refunds).
- **Report templates**: choose the layout of the end-of-day report (the Z-report) and of shift summaries, and print them on A4 or thermal paper.
- **Scale barcodes**: scan a barcode printed by a weighing scale, and the POS adds the product at the price or weight in the barcode.
- **Exchanges**: take a return and sell the replacement in one transaction.
- **Card payments offline**: starting with SumUp's Solo and Solo Lite over Bluetooth. The reader approves the payment and sends it when the connection returns. SumUp has to switch this on for your account. To see what already works with no connection, read [Offline Mode](./offline.mdx).

### 2.2: Control what each cashier can do {#cashier-permissions}

Today a cashier can use nearly everything the POS offers.

- **Permissions by role**: decide which roles may give refunds and discounts, change a line's price, void or reopen orders, see reports and see other cashiers' sales. A control a cashier may not use is hidden or locked. Every permission starts switched on, so nothing changes in your store until you turn one off.
- **Till settings for the whole store**: set a till setting once for every cashier, such as whether the receipt opens after checkout, and decide which ones a cashier may still change.

### 2.3: Works with your other plugins {#your-other-plugins}

The WooCommerce plugins your online store runs, working at the counter.

- **Loyalty, gift cards and subscriptions**: earn and spend points, sell and redeem gift cards and store credit, and start a subscription at the till.
- **Pricing rules and email lists**: a bulk price or discount rule applies at the till as it does online, and an email taken at the counter joins the same mailing list.
- **More WooCommerce extensions**: Product Add-Ons, Product Bundles, Composite Products, Bookings, Product Options and Product Vendors.
- **Order notifications**: an alert with sound at the till when a new online order comes in, which stays until someone acknowledges it. Made for restaurants, where an order needs attention at once.

### Not yet planned {#later}

Ideas with no release yet are on the board too, for example email templates for POS orders that differ from your online order emails. If one of them matters to your store, [ask](#request-or-vote).

## Already in WCPOS {#already-shipped}

### Shipped in 2.0 {#shipped-in-2-0}

2.0 rebuilt the register and its checkout: the tender pane with split payments, cash and other recorded payments that complete offline, registers with a float and numbered closures, card readers driven from the till, and the customer-facing display. Read [What's new in 2.0](./whats-new.mdx) for the full list.

## Ask for a feature {#request-or-vote}

The features many stores ask for move up the roadmap. Ask in one of two places:

- **[Discord](https://wcpos.com/discord)**: the quickest way to reach us and other store owners. Post in `#forum` for a feature request, or `#general` for a quick question.
- **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/wcpos/discussions)**: propose a feature, or add your use case to one someone has already proposed. Many roadmap items started as a discussion.

:::tip Say what you're trying to do
Tell us the job, not only the feature name: what happens at your counter today, and what you want to happen instead. A real example shapes the design more than a vote.
:::
