**Store health** shows what sits on this device, what hasn't reached your store yet, and how hard the POS is working your server. Open it from the pulse icon at the foot of the menu. It has three tabs: **Database**, **Performance** and **Logs**. On a tablet or computer it opens on **Database**; on a phone you pick a tab from a list.

A red number on **Store health** counts new errors in the log. Opening **Logs** clears it.

Here because something went wrong? Start with the message you saw:

- [**Order not saved — the store refused it**](#order-refused) at checkout
- [**… was not saved to the store**](#customer-not-saved) after adding a new customer
- [**checking…** that won't clear](#stuck-checking) in the table
- [**changes waiting to send** that won't go down](#waiting-to-send)

## Database {#database}

The Database tab answers two questions: is this till ready to sell, and did everything reach your store?

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[52, 0], [112, 0], [112, 0], [112, 0], [0, 40], [0, 40], [0, 40], [0, 40], [100, -369]]} alt="Store health on the Database tab: the three tabs, the four figures at the top and the table of what is on this device" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Store health** — the pulse icon at the foot of the menu, with a red number when the log has new errors.
2. **Database** — what is on this device and what is waiting to send.
3. **Performance** — how often this till checks your store, and how your server is coping. See [Performance](#performance).
4. **Logs** — everything this device did, for 30 days. See [Logs](#logs).
5. **Ready to sell** — appears once the first products are on the device. Being offline doesn't change it. Until then it reads **Preparing…**.
6. **records on this device** — every product, order, customer and other record stored here.
7. **storage used** — what the POS takes up on this device, cached images included.
8. **changes waiting to send** — sales and edits not yet sent to your store. Red above 0. After an outage, it's back to 0 once everything has reached your store.
9. **The table** — each kind of record, on this device and in your store. See [What's on this device](#coverage).

</div>

### When something needs you {#needs-attention}

When something needs a decision, a panel appears between the figures and the table. Stuck records, refused changes and held changes wait for you to choose an action. Receipt emails marked **waiting** are the exception: they retry by themselves while the till can reach your store. Find yours by its title:

- **N records need attention** — [Records that need attention](#stuck-records)
- **N changes never reached your server** — [Changes that never reached your server](#refused-changes)
- **N changes were held — your store reported newer versions** — [Changes held](#held-changes)
- **Your server's clock is about … ahead of this device** — [Server clock warning](#clock)
- **N receipt emails waiting to go out** — [Receipt emails](#queued-emails)

#### Records that need attention {#stuck-records}

**N records need attention** names a record that can't upload, can't download from your store, or must be removed from this device, with the reason. The record's row in the table shows a red **stuck** badge.

- **Fix record** — opens the record so you can correct it.
- **Retry** — tries again now, when trying again can help.
- **View log ›** — opens [Logs](#logs).

#### Changes that never reached your server {#refused-changes}

**N changes never reached your server** means your store refused a change, such as a sale or a new customer, and gave its reason. The POS won't retry it on its own.

- **Send again** — rebuilds the change from the record as it is on this device now and sends it. Correct the record first if the reason asks for it. **sent again N×** counts the tries.
- **Discard**, in the **⋮** menu — drops this device's change and keeps your store's version. If the record never reached your store, discarding deletes it from this device, and the dialog says so.

#### Changes held because your store had a newer version {#held-changes}

**N changes were held — your store reported newer versions** means someone changed the same record first, on another till or in WooCommerce. This device held its change instead of overwriting theirs.

:::warning Compare before you send
**Send again** here is not a plain retry: this device's change goes in over the newer version in your store. Use **Open record** first to see what your store has now. Only a delete asks you to confirm.
:::

- **Open record** — shows the record so you can compare.
- **Send again** — keeps this device's change and sends it on top of your store's version.
- **Use server version** — drops this device's change and puts your store's version back on this device. Nothing is sent. On some rows, such as a change that would delete the record, the same choice is **Discard device change** in the **⋮** menu.

#### Server clock warning {#clock}

**Your server's clock is about … ahead of this device** (or **behind**) means the two clocks are more than a minute apart, so order times, receipts and reports may be wrong. Ask whoever runs your website to check its date, time and timezone. The warning stays for 24 hours after the gap was last seen.

#### Receipt emails waiting to go out {#queued-emails}

**N receipt emails waiting to go out** lists receipts emailed from this till that haven't left yet.

- **waiting** — tried again while the till can reach your store.
- **failed** — has stopped trying, and shows why. Press **Send again**, or **Remove** to drop it. Remove can't recall an email that has already started to send.

:::warning Deletes are flagged
A row marked **Deletes from your store** removes the record from your store if it goes through. **Send again** on it asks you to confirm with **Send delete**.
:::

### What's on this device {#coverage}

The table lists each kind of record: how many are **On device**, how many your store has **On server**, a **Coverage** bar and the **Size**. Under it, the storage breakdown says what else takes up space.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[91, 0], [10, 30], [231, 0], [231, 0], [149, 0], [-34, 0]]} alt="The lower half of the Database tab: the collection table with coverage bars, the storage breakdown rows and the Check everything now button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Customers** — a partial bar is normal: customers download a little at a time while the till is quiet. **Orders** read partial too, because the till keeps open and recent orders and older ones download when someone opens them.
2. **⋮** — the row's menu: [check now or clear and re-download](#clear-and-redownload).
3. **Cached images** — product pictures kept so the till works offline.
4. **Other app storage** — space the POS uses that it can't put under another row.
5. **How syncing works** — opens [Download & Sync](../products/sync.mdx). The lines above it say when this till last checked your store, when the **On server** totals were updated, and when the next check is due.
6. **Check everything now** — runs a full check against your store straight away.

</div>

The breakdown only shows rows that hold something. Others you may see:

- **Logs & sync bookkeeping** — the activity history, working copies and app settings for this store.
- **Other cashiers on this store** — the same store signed in as someone else on this device.
- **Other stores on this device** — a count and a size, never their names.
- **Signed-out stores & older versions** — left behind by earlier sign-ins and app updates.

**checking…** in the **On server** column means the till doesn't have a fresh total from your store yet; totals refresh every 15 minutes. **server busy…** means the till is easing off your server; see [Your server, over time](#server-load).

### Check now, or clear and re-download {#clear-and-redownload}

Each row's **⋮** menu has two actions.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[-37, 0], [-37, 0]]} alt="A collection row's menu open, with Check for changes now and Clear and re-download" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Check for changes now** — checks your store for that kind of record now, instead of waiting for the next check. Use it after you change something in WooCommerce.
2. **Clear & re-download…** — removes this device's copy and fetches it again. The dialog says how many records and how much space go, and that your store keeps them. While it runs, the row reads **Re-downloading — N so far**.

</div>

After a clear, a low count is expected: records that download on demand come back as they're used. Prefer this to [clearing all local data](troubleshooting/clear-local-data.mdx), which signs you out.

### Registers {#registers}

At the foot of the tab, **Registers** checks receipt numbering on each till over the last 30 days. It asks your store, so it needs a connection.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[261, -205], [-28, 0], [141, -101]]} alt="The Registers panel at the foot of the Database tab: two registers, each with the sales checked, the counters used and a tick or warning per check" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Registers** — receipt numbering on each till. On a busy store only the newest sales are checked, and the panel says so.
2. **Refresh** — asks your store again.
3. **A register** — how many sales were checked and the receipt counters they used, then one line per check: skipped numbers, a number used twice, till clocks against your server, and closures. A tick means the check passed; a warning sign names what to look at. Press the arrow on a warning for the detail.

</div>

A last line may count sales from a till or register session your store has no record of.

## Performance {#performance}

The Performance tab shows how often this till checks your store for changes, and how your server is coping.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[383, 0], [111, -74], [181, -22], [181, -22], [181, -22], [141, -20], [151, -16], [456, 0]]} alt="The Performance tab: the status line with the last 24 hours, the hourly uptime strip, the Eco, Balanced and Realtime cards and the two sliders" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **✓ Normal** — or **⚠ Needs attention** when the till is offline, sync is blocked, the first download failed or a kind of record has an error. Then the last 24 hours on this till: requests, data and typical response time.
2. **Engine uptime** — one cell per hour for the last 24 hours. Green: requests and no errors. Amber: at least one request failed that hour. Empty: the app was closed.
3. **Eco** — gentlest on your server.
4. **Balanced** — the default, right for most stores.
5. **Realtime** — fastest updates, for tills that need to see each other's sales quickly.
6. **Check for changes** — how often the till asks your store what changed, from 5 seconds to 5 minutes.
7. **Records per request** — how many records it fetches at a time, 10 to 100.
8. **Right now** — when the next check is due, and about how many requests an hour these settings make.

</div>

### Sync presets {#sync-presets}

| Preset | Checks every | Records per request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Eco** | 5 min | 25 |
| **Balanced** (default) | 60 s | 50 |
| **Realtime** | 10 s | 75 |

Choose **Eco** on shared or budget hosting. Choose **Realtime** when several tills must see each other's changes fast and your server can take it. Moving a slider makes the setting **Custom**; **Reset to Balanced** puts the default back. Changes apply at once.

:::note Each till has its own setting
The preset is saved on this device only. Change it on every till you want to tune.
:::

A countdown longer than your setting is normal. Checks are spread out a little so tills don't all ask at once, and after ten quiet minutes the till checks less often. It speeds up again when someone uses it. For the numbers behind this, see [Sync Performance](../reference/sync-performance.mdx).

### Your server, over time {#server-load}

Further down, **Your server, over time** shows the pace the till is keeping and two charts for the last 24 hours. Server load counts everything running on your server, not just the POS.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[456, 0], [91, 0], [91, -84], [106, -84]]} alt="Your server, over time: the pace line, the Server load chart and the POS requests chart" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Right now** — the next check, and about how many requests an hour your settings make.
2. **Pace** — **Normal pace** here. See the other states below.
3. **Server load** — the load your server reports, with the latest figure on the right. If your server doesn't report it, this says so and the rest of the page still works.
4. **POS requests** — how many requests this till made, with the latest figure on the right.

</div>

The pace line reads one of these:

- **Normal pace** — the till checks at your setting. It may add that your server reports elevated or high load; the till only slows down if its answers slow down too.
- **Easing off — checking N× less often** — your server asked the POS to slow down, returned errors, timed out or answered slowly. The till slows itself and speeds up again when your server recovers.
- **Paused until …** — your server asked the POS to wait until that time. You can keep selling while it waits.

The till recovers from both by itself. If **Easing off** or **Paused** shows most days, your hosting is struggling: see [Server Performance](performance/server.mdx), or choose **Eco**.

## Logs {#logs}

The Logs tab lists what this device did: sales, syncs, errors. Entries are kept for 30 days.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={7} offsets={[[55, -38], [19, -38], [21, -38], [16, -38], [-29, 0], [600, 0], [6, 28], [22, 28], [17, 28], [14, 28], [-28, 0], [-32, 0]]} alt="The Logs tab: four figures for today, the search field, the filter buttons, the Verbose diagnostics switch and Copy debug info" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **events today** — everything logged today.
2. **errors today** — errors logged today.
3. **stuck records** — records the log shows as stuck. **Database** lists them with what to do.
4. **changes waiting to send** — the same figure as on Database.
5. **Copy debug info** — copies a report for support. On a phone or tablet app it's **Share debug info**.
6. **Search Logs** — find an entry by its words or code.
7. **All** — every entry.
8. **Actions** — only what a person did.
9. **Errors** — only errors.
10. **Sync** — only syncing.
11. **Verbose diagnostics** — logs extra detail for support. It switches itself off after 24 hours.
12. **Watching for changes** — when this till last checked your store, and when it checks next.

</div>

Tap an entry to open it: what happened, whether a payment was taken, what to do next, and a link to the [error code](../error-codes/index.mdx) page. Quote the **Event code** when you contact support. Entries show in the language the till uses today, whatever it used when they were written.

:::note Logs and Database can differ
**stuck records** on Logs counts what the log saw. Database also lists refused and held changes. When the two differ, act on **Database**.
:::

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

### "Order not saved — the store refused it" at checkout {#order-refused}

Your store turned the order down, and the message gives its reason. No payment was taken, and you're back at the cart with everything still in it. The order's tab reads **Save refused**. Fix what the reason names, such as a quantity or a price, and press **Checkout** again. Pressed again with nothing changed, it only shows the same message. If the reason isn't something you can fix in the cart, press **Store health** on the message and use [Send again or Discard](#refused-changes).

### "… was not saved to the store" after adding a new customer {#customer-not-saved}

The customer was made on this till, perhaps offline, and your store refused it. The sale goes through as a guest sale, with the customer's addresses on it. In **Store health → Database**, find the customer under [changes never reached your server](#refused-changes) and press **Send again**. Once your store accepts the customer, the POS links the order to them, for up to 30 days. See [SYNC999](../error-codes/SYNC999.mdx).

### A row stays on "checking…" {#stuck-checking}

If a row in the table reads **checking…** for more than about 15 minutes, your store isn't answering for that kind of record. Open [Logs](#logs) and filter **Errors**.

### "changes waiting to send" won't go down {#waiting-to-send}

The till may be offline or paused. Look at the status on [Performance](#performance), then at the panels on [Database](#needs-attention).
