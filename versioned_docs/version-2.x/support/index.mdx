Stuck on something these pages don't answer? Ask in the WCPOS Discord, right from the POS. This page shows where to ask, and what to send so someone can help you the first time.

1. Check the [common problems](#before-asking) first. Many answers are a search away.
2. [Copy your debug info](#what-to-send) from **Store health → Logs**.
3. [Post in Discord](#in-app-support) with the debug info and what happened.

For licences, billing or anything private, [email support](#where-to-ask) instead.

## Ask from the POS {#in-app-support}

First, [copy your debug info](#what-to-send). Then press **Support** at the foot of the menu. The WCPOS Discord opens inside the POS, so you can read what others asked and post your own question without leaving the till. Choose **general**, then press **Login** to sign in and post your question.

## Send what we need {#what-to-send}

Most questions are answered faster with the versions you run and the errors the POS has seen. The POS gathers both for you.

Open **Store health** in the menu, then **Logs**, and press **Copy debug info**: it goes to your clipboard. In the iOS and Android apps the button reads **Share debug info** and opens the share sheet instead. Paste or share it into your message, and say:

- what you were doing,
- what you expected, and what happened instead,
- any code the screen showed, such as `SYNC201`,
- whether it happens every time.

A screenshot of the screen helps too. Ready? Go back to [Ask from the POS](#in-app-support), sign in, and paste your debug info with your question.

<details>

The debug info holds your app, WordPress, WooCommerce and WCPOS versions, the store, the connection, changes waiting to send, stuck records and the latest errors.

Open a row to see its detail. **Copy** takes just that entry, and **Help — CODE** opens the [error code](../error-codes/index.mdx) page that explains it. The [Logs](./logs.mdx) page explains the filters and **Verbose diagnostics** in full.

</details>

## Where to ask {#where-to-ask}

- **Discord** — how-to questions, bugs and ideas. Answers stay where the next person can find them. [wcpos.com/discord](https://wcpos.com/discord)
- **The ask box at [wcpos.com/support](https://wcpos.com/support)** — type a question and get an answer drawn from the WCPOS documentation, with links. When it can't answer, it points you to Discord.
- **Email [support@wcpos.com](mailto:support@wcpos.com)** — licences, billing, refunds, and anything you'd rather not post in public.

:::info With WCPOS Pro
Priority support is in the Pro channels on Discord. See [Pro License](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).
:::

<details>
<summary>Connect Discord for Pro support</summary>

1. In your [wcpos.com account](https://wcpos.com/account/licenses), press **Connect Discord** on your licence. Or, in the Discord server, type `/link` with your licence key.
2. The Pro channels open for your Discord account.

Each licence has seats for up to five people.

</details>

## Before you ask {#before-asking}

Many answers are a search away:

- **An error code on screen** — look it up in [Error Codes](../error-codes/index.mdx).
- **Products, orders or customers missing or out of date** — check **Store health → Database**. See [Store Health](./store-health.mdx#database).
- **The POS won't load, or shows old data** — see [Clear Local Data](./troubleshooting/clear-local-data.mdx), then [Plugin Conflicts](./troubleshooting/plugin-conflicts.mdx) and [Cloudflare](./troubleshooting/cloudflare.mdx).
- **The POS is slow** — see [Performance](./performance/index.mdx).
- **Totals differ from WooCommerce** — see [Totals Disagree](./troubleshooting/totals-disagree.mdx).
- **Someone may have asked already** — search the Discord before you post.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**The Support screen stays blank.** The Discord needs an internet connection. Check the connection, or open [wcpos.com/discord](https://wcpos.com/discord) in a browser.

**"Couldn't copy debug info".** The copy didn't reach your clipboard. Click into the POS window and press **Copy debug info** again, or take a screenshot of the **Logs** screen instead.
