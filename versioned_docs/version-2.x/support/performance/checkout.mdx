Checkout is slow when your store is slow to answer the till. A sale waits for your store twice: when you press **Checkout**, the order is saved to your store, and when you press **Take**, your store records the payment before the receipt shows. This page shows where the till waits, how to measure your store, and what to change.

:::note Most fixes are on your website
The changes on this page are made on your website, mostly in WP Admin. If someone else runs your website, send them this page.
:::

## Where the till waits for your store {#where-it-waits}

| You press | The till waits for your store to | What you see while it waits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Checkout** | save the order | The checkout opens at once, but the keypad doesn't respond and **Take** reads **Saving order…**. After 4 seconds: "The store isn't answering. Keep waiting, or go back to the cart." |
| **Take**, on Cash, Card or another payment recorded at the till | record the payment | The receipt shows once your store has recorded the payment. If your store doesn't answer, the till asks again after 1, 3 and 9 seconds. |
| A card reader tile | create the payment, then confirm what the reader did | **Waiting for the customer on the terminal**. After repeated errors: **Connection unstable — still waiting**. See [Taking a Payment](../../pos/checkout/taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader). |
| The **Legacy** tab | send its payment page | A loading placeholder, and **Process Payment** greyed out until the page loads. See [The Legacy Tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx). |

Each wait is a request to your store, so a store that answers slowly makes every sale slow. Offline, a payment recorded at the till doesn't wait: the sale is held on this device and sent when the store is back. See [Offline Mode](../../getting-started/offline.mdx).

## Measure your store {#measure}

**Store health → Performance** shows how fast your store has answered this till over the last 24 hours.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Store health** — the pulse icon near the foot of the menu.
2. **Performance** — this tab.
3. **Status line** — the requests this till made in the last 24 hours, and the **typical response**: how long your store took to answer, on average. Saving an order at checkout is one of those requests.
4. **Engine uptime** — one cell per hour. Amber means at least one request to your store failed that hour.
5. **Balanced** — how often this till checks your store for changes. On shared or budget hosting, **Eco** asks less often and leaves your store more room for sales. See [Sync presets](../store-health.mdx#sync-presets).
6. **Check for changes** — the same setting as a slider, from 5 seconds to 5 minutes.
7. **Right now** — about how many requests an hour these settings make.

</div>

Further down, **Your server, over time** charts your server's load beside the POS's requests.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[456, 0], [571, -222], [560, 0], [101, -84], [110, -84]]} alt="The lower half of the Performance tab: the pace line, the Server load chart and the POS requests chart" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Right now** — the requests an hour these settings make.
2. **Your server, over time** — the section heading.
3. **Pace** — **Normal pace**, or **Easing off** when your server answered slowly, timed out or returned errors. See [Your server, over time](../store-health.mdx#server-load).

To see what a change buys you, time a test sale before and after it. Count from **Checkout** until **Take** stops reading **Saving order…**, and from **Take** until the receipt shows. Change one thing at a time, and test with each payment method you use, not only Cash.

## Speed up saving and paying {#saving}

Work down this list. The first one that applies to your store is the likeliest cause.

### Order storage {#order-storage}

If WooCommerce still keeps orders in WordPress posts (legacy storage), every save at checkout gets slower as your store grows. Check which storage you use and switch to HPOS: see [Order Storage (HPOS)](./order-storage.mdx).

### Plugins that run when an order is saved {#2-plugin-interference-during-order-creation}

Some plugins do their own work each time an order is saved or paid, and the till waits for them. An accounting plugin that sends the order to Xero, for example, holds every sale until Xero answers. Plugins that often do this:

- Accounting and bookkeeping (Xero, QuickBooks)
- Email marketing tools that act on new orders
- Inventory and stock systems
- Shipping calculators
- Analytics and tracking

To find the one:

1. On a [staging site](../troubleshooting/plugin-conflicts.mdx#before-you-start-use-a-staging-site), turn off every plugin except WooCommerce and WCPOS.
2. Time a test sale.
3. If it's much faster, turn the plugins back on one at a time, timing a sale after each, until it slows down again.
4. Ask that plugin's developer whether its work can run in the background instead.

The full method is in [Plugin Conflicts](../troubleshooting/plugin-conflicts.mdx#step-2-the-plugin-elimination-method).

### Your hosting {#hosting}

If **Server load** is high or the **typical response** is slow on every request, not just at checkout, your server is short of resources. Ask your host about:

- CPU and memory use while you sell
- The PHP memory limit and the number of PHP workers
- Slow database queries, and old data that could be cleaned up
- Object caching (test it with the POS before you rely on it)
- A larger plan, or a VPS instead of shared hosting

See [Server Performance](./server.mdx) for recommended specifications.

## Speed up the Legacy tab's payment page {#legacy-page}

Only gateways on the **Legacy** tab load a web page at checkout. It's a stripped-down copy of the WooCommerce page customers use to pay for an order online, sent by your website each time. Your theme's header, footer and layout are already gone, but the styles and scripts your theme and plugins add to every page still load, and they can make the page slow or break it.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[28, 34], [28, 34], [0, -273], [-30, 0], [-30, 0]]} alt="The Legacy tab at checkout: the payment page from the store with the Checkout Settings button at its top right, and the Process Payment button below it" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Payments** — tiles recorded at the till. They load no page.
2. **Legacy** — gateways that run on your store's own payment page.
3. **Payment page** — sent by your website. **Checkout Settings** is at its top right.
4. **Process Payment** — greyed out until the page has loaded.
5. **✕** — back to the cart, with the order kept.

</div>

To remove styles and scripts from this page:

1. Sign in to the POS as a shop manager or administrator. A cashier can open the form but not save it: saving needs the `manage_woocommerce` capability.
2. Press **Checkout**, open the **Legacy** tab, and press **Checkout Settings** on the payment page.
3. Untick a style or script to remove it. Start with your theme's styles, then scripts your gateway doesn't need.
4. Press **Submit**, then try a test payment with each Legacy gateway you use.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[28, 34], [-28, 0]]} alt="The Checkout Settings form open on the Legacy tab's payment page, listing the page's styles and scripts with a checkbox each" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Legacy** — the tab the form belongs to.
2. **Checkout Settings** — the form, over the payment page. Every style and script on the page has a checkbox, ticked if it loads.

</div>

The form has three parts:

- **Disable All Styles and Scripts** — **Disable wp_head** and **Disable wp_footer** remove everything the page's header or footer would load, including what the payment form needs. Use them only to test whether styles and scripts are the cause.
- **Disable Selected Styles** — one entry per style.
- **Disable Selected Scripts** — one entry per script.

Entries ending *always removed on the POS payment page* are removed already and can't be ticked.

:::warning Keep what your gateway needs
If a payment method stops working after you remove a script, tick it again and press **Submit**.
:::

<details>
<summary>What WCPOS removes on its own, and options for developers</summary>

The payment page always drops the admin bar, the extra links WordPress adds to a page's header, and these WooCommerce styles and scripts:

- Styles: `admin-bar`, `woocommerce-general`, `woocommerce-inline`, `woocommerce-layout`, `woocommerce-smallscreen`, `woocommerce-blocktheme`, `wp-block-library`
- Scripts: `admin-bar`, `wc-add-to-cart`
- With WooCommerce Stripe older than 11.1.1, also its `wc-stripe-upe-classic` script and styles and `stripelink_styles`.

For developers:

- The filters `wcpos_payment_template_dequeue_style_handles` and `wcpos_payment_template_dequeue_script_handles` add handles to the removed lists.
- The template can be overridden by copying it to `yourtheme/woocommerce-pos/payment.php`. The original is [templates/payment.php](https://github.com/wcpos/woocommerce-pos/blob/main/templates/payment.php) in the plugin.
- Only styles and scripts added with `wp_enqueue_style` and `wp_enqueue_script` can be removed. A theme or plugin that prints its code straight into the page can't be; ask its developer to enqueue it, or remove it with that plugin's own hooks.

</details>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**"The store isn't answering. Keep waiting, or go back to the cart."** Your store is taking more than 4 seconds to save the order. Keep waiting, or press **✕** to go back to the cart with the order kept. If you see it often, [measure your store](#measure) and work down [Speed up saving and paying](#saving).

**"The store has not answered the last payment. Try again to confirm it; it will not be taken twice."** Press **Try again**.

**"The payment form didn't load — check the connection to your store, then retry."** The Legacy tab waited 90 seconds for the payment page, reloaded it once, and gave up. Check the connection and press **Retry**. If the page is slow every time, see [Speed up the Legacy tab's payment page](#legacy-page).

**"You do not have permission to modify checkout settings."** You pressed **Submit** in **Checkout Settings** as a cashier. Sign in as a shop manager or administrator.

**Still slow after all this.** If checkout keeps taking more than 10 seconds, ask on [Discord](https://wcpos.com/discord). Press **Copy debug info** in **Store health → Logs** and paste it in, with your typical response time and what you've tried. See [Help & Troubleshooting](../index.mdx).

:::info With WCPOS Pro
Gateways on the Legacy tab need [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx). The free version takes Cash and Card.
:::
