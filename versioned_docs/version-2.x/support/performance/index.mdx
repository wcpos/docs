When the POS feels slow, the cause is usually your store's website, not the till. The till sends every sale to your store and keeps asking it what changed, so a slow website shows up at the till.

Start with what you see:

- [**Checkout takes several seconds**](#slow-checkout)
- [**Changes take a while to reach the till**](#slow-updates)
- [**Store health shows a slow or failing server**](#slow-server)
- [**The screen is slow, but your store answers quickly**](#slow-device)

Not sure? [Read Store health first](#store-health): it shows how your store has been answering this till.

## Read Store health first {#store-health}

Open **Store health** from the pulse icon at the foot of the menu, then tap **Performance**. It shows how this till's requests to your store went over the last 24 hours.

Read three things, not just **✓ Normal**, which only says the till is online and syncing:

- A **typical response** measured in seconds, amber hours on **Engine uptime**, or **Easing off** under **Your server, over time**: your website is struggling. Go to [Store health shows a slow or failing server](#slow-server).
- A **typical response** well under 2 seconds, green hours and **Normal pace**: your website is keeping up. If the till still feels slow, go to [The screen is slow, but your store answers quickly](#slow-device).

1. **Right now** — the same line as in the shot above.
2. **Pace** — **Normal pace** here. **Easing off** means the till is checking less often because your website asked it to slow down, returned errors, timed out or answered slowly. **Paused until …** means your website asked the POS to wait. You can keep selling in both.

The till eases off and recovers by itself, so there's nothing to switch. If **Easing off** or **Paused** shows most days, your hosting is struggling. Every part of the tab is explained in [Store Health](../store-health.mdx#performance).

## Find what's slow {#whats-slow}

Start with what the cashier sees.

### Checkout takes several seconds {#slow-checkout}

When you press **Checkout**, the till sends the order to your store, and an online payment waits for your store to record it. If that's slow, check in this order:

1. **Order storage.** On a store with many orders, WooCommerce's older order storage is the most common cause, and it gets slower as the store grows. See [Order Storage (HPOS)](./order-storage.mdx).
2. **Plugins that run when an order is saved**, such as accounting or email marketing plugins that send the order somewhere else first. See [Plugin interference](./checkout.mdx#2-plugin-interference-during-order-creation).
3. **A gateway on the Legacy tab.** These load a payment page from your website inside the POS, and themes and plugins can slow it down. Cash and card on the other tabs don't load a page. See [Checkout Performance](./checkout.mdx).

### Changes take a while to reach the till {#slow-updates}

A price changed in WooCommerce, or another till's sale, shows up late. The till picks up changes at its next check: every minute on **Balanced**, every 5 minutes on **Eco**.

- To fetch one kind of record now, open its **⋮** menu on the **Database** tab and choose **Check for changes now**. See [Check now, or clear and re-download](../store-health.mdx#clear-and-redownload).
- If tills must see each other's changes fast and your website can take it, choose **Realtime**. See [Sync presets](../store-health.mdx#sync-presets).
- If **Pace** reads **Easing off**, checks are already slower than your setting because your website is struggling. A faster preset won't help: fix the server first.

### Store health shows a slow or failing server {#slow-server}

A typical response measured in seconds, amber hours on **Engine uptime**, or **Easing off** most days all point at your hosting. Choose **Eco** to lighten the load while you look at it, then work through [Server Performance](./server.mdx): the [minimum server](./server.mdx#minimum-server-requirements), [503 errors](./server.mdx#503-errors) and the [WooCommerce versions](./server.mdx#woocommerce-version-compatibility) known to slow the POS.

:::note Each till has its own setting
The preset is saved on the device you change it on. Change it on every till you want to tune.
:::

### The screen is slow, but your store answers quickly {#slow-device}

Check **Performance** in Store health first: a **typical response** well under 2 seconds, green **Engine uptime** cells and **Normal pace** mean your website is keeping up. Then the device is the likely cause. Try the POS on another device. If it's fast there, close other apps or browser tabs on the slow one. If it's slow everywhere, open **Store health → Logs**, press **Copy debug info** (**Share debug info** in the phone and tablet apps) and send it to [support](../index.mdx).

## How the till protects your server {#for-developers}

The till sends only what changed, spreads its checks out, and slows itself down before your website falls over. Those pauses only affect background updates: you can still search, scan and sell, though an online payment still needs your store to answer. For the rules and the numbers behind them, see [Sync Performance](../../reference/sync-performance.mdx).
