The POS keeps a copy of your store on each device, so it can sell quickly and keep selling offline. **Clear All Local Data** deletes that copy and downloads it again from your store. It fixes a device whose data has gone wrong in a way nothing smaller can repair. It's the last thing to try, not the first.

:::warning Sales that haven't reached your store are lost
Anything this device hasn't sent yet is deleted with the rest, and it can't be recovered. That can include a completed sale, with its money already in your drawer. [Check first](#check-unsent).
:::

## Try these first {#try-first}

Each of these fixes one thing and leaves the rest of the device alone.

- **One kind of record looks wrong** (products, customers, orders): **Clear & re-download…** on its row in [Store health → Database](../store-health.mdx#clear-and-redownload). Nothing else on the device changes and you stay signed in.
- **A change in WooCommerce hasn't arrived:** **Check for changes now** on the same row menu. See [Download & Sync](../../products/sync.mdx).
- **Language, currency or number format is wrong on this device:** **Restore Server Settings** at the foot of **Settings → General** puts back your WooCommerce store's values. See [General settings](../../settings/store/general.mdx#restore-server-settings).
- **A screen shows a red "Something went wrong" panel:** close it, or reload. See [CLIENT151](../../error-codes/CLIENT151.mdx).

## Check nothing is waiting to send {#check-unsent}

Store health counts only the store and cashier signed in now. Each cashier's sales, in each store, are kept apart on the device, and a clear deletes all of them. So check every cashier who uses this device, in every store they use: each must show **0** before you clear.

For each of them, open **Store health** and look at **changes waiting to send** on the **Database** tab.

## What gets cleared {#what-gets-cleared}

Only this device. Your store, and every other device, are untouched.

**Cleared from this device:**

- Products, customers, orders, coupons and taxes held here
- Sales and changes not yet sent
- Printers and barcode scanners set up on this device
- Its register choice
- Changes made in **Settings → General** on this device
- Its activity log in Store health
- The desktop app's stores and sign-ins

**Kept in your store:**

- Everything this device already sent: sales, customers, edits
- Your WooCommerce products, customers and orders
- Settings made in WP Admin
- Your registers, and the closures already sent

After the clear, set up the printers again in [Printing](../../hardware/printers/index.mdx), and the scanners in [Barcode scanning](../../settings/store/barcode.mdx).

## Clear all local data {#clear}

1. Open **Settings** from the foot of the menu, then **General**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and press **Clear All Local Data**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[40, 0], [60, 0], [-115, 0], [-100, 0]]} alt="Settings on the General page, scrolled to the bottom: Restore Server Settings, and below it Clear All Local Data" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Settings** — the gear at the foot of the menu.
2. **General** — this device's store settings.
3. **Restore Server Settings** — the smaller fix: puts back the settings copied from your WooCommerce store, such as language, currency and number format, and deletes nothing. See [General settings](../../settings/store/general.mdx#restore-server-settings).
4. **Clear All Local Data** — the last button on the page. It asks before it deletes anything.

</div>

3. Read the confirmation. It starts with a warning about sales that haven't reached your store:
   - **N changes on this device have never reached your server and will be permanently lost.** Press **Cancel** and [send them first](#check-unsent).
   - **The app could not check for changes waiting to be sent…** It says this whenever this store shows nothing waiting, because it can't vouch for other cashiers and stores on the device. If **changes waiting to send** read **0** for everyone who uses this device, go on.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[-24, 0], [0, 32]]} alt="The Clear all local data? confirmation over Settings, with its warning about sales that never reached the server, and the Cancel and Clear all local data buttons" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Cancel** — closes the confirmation. Nothing is deleted.
2. **Clear all local data** — deletes everything stored on this device. It can't be undone.

</div>

4. Press **Clear all local data**. The POS restarts and downloads your store again.

## What happens next {#what-happens-next}

**In the browser**, the page reloads and you're still signed in, because you're signed in to WordPress. **In the desktop app**, the app asks **Are you sure?** once more. Press **Yes**: it restarts as if it were new, and you connect your store and sign in again. See [Connect to your store](../../getting-started/connect.mdx).

Then the POS downloads your products, customers and recent orders, as it did the first time. See [Download & Sync](../../products/sync.mdx). If your store has several registers, the POS asks which one this device is. See [Choosing a store and a register](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#choosing-a-store-and-a-register).

## When the app won't open {#app-wont-open}

If the whole app stops on **Something went wrong**, with **Try again keeps everything on this device**, there are two buttons:

- **Try again** — tries to open the app again and touches no data. Always try it first.
- **Reset app data and restart** — clears all local data from this screen. It asks **Reset app data and restart?** and says how many changes would be lost, as the confirmation in Settings does. Press **Reset and restart**, or **Cancel**.

The app may not know whether anything is waiting to send. Then the warning says the reset **may** include completed sales that never reached your server. If you took sales offline that you can't see in WooCommerce, don't reset: contact support.

The desktop app can also clear itself from its menu bar: **Window → Advanced → Clear App Data**, then **Yes**. Use it when the app won't open far enough to reach Settings.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**"Restart required".** The app can't restart itself. Close it completely and open it again: it clears its data as it starts.

**"Reset didn't finish".** The clear stopped partway, and nothing new can be saved until it finishes. Press **Reload and retry**. If there's no button, close the app completely and open it again.

**"Could not clear local data".** Close the app completely, open it again, and try once more.

**The first download is slow.** A big store takes a while. You can watch it in [Store health → Database](../store-health.mdx#database). Records that download on demand, such as older orders, come back as you open them.

**The problem came back after a clear.** Don't clear it again. Note what you see and [contact support](../index.mdx).
