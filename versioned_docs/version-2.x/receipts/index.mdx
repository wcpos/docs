Every receipt WCPOS prints is a template filled in with the sale. You choose the templates in **WP Admin → POS → Templates**, and the till prints the first active one unless the cashier picks another. The same screen holds invoices, gift receipts, packing slips and kitchen tickets.

Most of this page is for the store owner setting that up. For the cashier, [At the till](#at-the-till) covers the receipt after a sale, and [Receipts and the Paid Screen](./at-checkout.mdx) has the details.

:::tip Just want to change your receipt?
Start at **[Customise Your Receipt](./customise.mdx)**. It walks through the three easiest ways: picking a different bundled template, asking AI to tweak one, or editing by hand.
:::

## At the till {#at-the-till}

When a sale is paid, the paid screen shows its receipt. Then tap one of:

- **Print**, which prints the receipt shown, on the printer chosen under **Printer**.
- **Email**, which sends the customer WooCommerce's invoice email for the order. It doesn't use the receipt template shown here.
- **Download PDF**, which saves the receipt as a file. It needs a connection.

To print it again later:

1. Open **Orders** from the side menu.
2. Find the order, and tap **Receipt** on its row.
3. Print it from the receipt that opens.

More on this in [Reprinting Receipts](./at-checkout.mdx#reprinting-receipts).

### A different receipt for one sale {#one-sale}

The cashier can pick another active template from **Template** on the paid screen. The pick lasts for that sale only, and with one active template the picker is hidden.

## Choose your receipt {#how-it-works}

The store owner sets this up once, in WP Admin:

1. In WP Admin, go to **POS → Templates**. The **Receipts** tab lists **Your Templates** at the top, with the **Template Gallery** below.
2. In the gallery, narrow the cards with **Search templates...**, **Category**, **Format** and **Direction**. **Preview** shows a template filled in with **Sample Data** or with one of your orders.
3. Click **Use Template**. Your own copy joins **Your Templates**, already active, at the bottom of the list.
4. Drag it to the top of **Your Templates**. The till uses the first active template as its default, and the built-in **Legacy PHP Template** stays ahead of yours until you move yours above it or turn it off.

To change what a template says or how it looks, edit your copy. See [Customise Your Receipt](./customise.mdx).

## Send each template to a printer {#template-printers}

In the app, **Settings → Printing** lists the templates the till can print under **Receipt templates**, with **Report templates** and **Closure templates** below. Each row shows:

- **The template**, such as the built-in **Legacy PHP Template**.
- **Its type**: **HTML** for the print dialog; a thermal template shows its paper, such as **Thermal 80mm**.
- **The printer**: **Auto** picks a printer that suits the template's type and paper width, and opens the **Print Dialog** when none does. Choose a printer to always send this template there.

Adding a printer is covered in [Printers](../hardware/printers/index.mdx#adding-a-printer).

## What prints on a receipt {#what-prints}

This is the **Standard Receipt** as the gallery previews it, with sample data. Below it, each part and where it comes from.

<div style={{maxWidth:'520px', marginInline:'auto'}}>

![The Standard Receipt preview: the Coffee Monster logo, name, address, phone and EIN at the top left; the order number, date, cashier and customer at the top right; the items with a discount line; Subtotal, Total, Refunded and Net Total; a Returned items box; a Customer note box; the Cash payment with tendered and change; and a thank-you line](/img/2.x/receipts/index/en/standard-receipt-gallery-preview.webp)

- **Top left** (logo, name, address, phone and tax ID): your store details. Without Pro, they come from **POS → Settings** (**Store details** and **Store tax IDs**), and the logo is your WordPress site logo. With Pro, each store has its own on its **Details** and **Tax** pages.
- **Top right** (order number, date, cashier and customer): the order.
- **The middle** (items, discount and totals): the order, priced the way your tax setting says. See [Adaptive tax display](#adaptive-tax-display).
- **The two boxes** (Returned items and Customer note): the order's refunds and note, when it has them.
- **Paid** (near the bottom): each payment, with the cash tendered and the change.
- **The bottom** (the thank-you line): with Pro, each store's **Receipt messages**. Otherwise the receipt prints a standard thank-you.

</div>

Tax IDs are covered in [Store Tax IDs](../settings/wp-admin/store-tax-ids.mdx), and each store's details (Pro) in [Fill in the store's pages](../stores/setup.mdx#store-pages). Every field a template can print is listed in [Receipt Data](./receipt-data.mdx).

## Pro: a receipt for each store {#pro-features}

With [WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx), each store can print its own receipt. In **POS → Stores**, open a store:

- **Receipts**: the store's **Receipt Templates** list says **Same as site** until you click **Customise**. Then tick what cashiers can print there; the first ticked template is the store's default, and you drag to change the order. **Use site template** goes back. The same page has the store's **Receipt messages**.
- **Details**: **Logo and address**, with **Use site logo** or **Upload logo**, and the store's **Contact** details.

See [Fill in the store's pages](../stores/setup.mdx#store-pages) and [Per-store templates](./customise.mdx#per-store-assignments).

## Receipts outside the till {#beyond-the-till}

- **Email**: **Email Receipt** at the till sends WooCommerce's own customer invoice email, not a WCPOS template. Change how it looks in **WooCommerce → Settings → Emails**.
- **Download PDF**: the server builds the PDF, so it needs a connection.
- **Online customers**: turn on **Let customers download receipts** to add a **Receipt** button to their orders in My Account. It is off until you turn it on, and **Receipt template for customers** chooses which template they get. See [Storefront receipts](./storefront.mdx).

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="For template editors: the receipt data">

Every template prints from the same [receipt data](./receipt-data.mdx): store details, items, totals, tax, payments, refunds and more. Each amount also has a ready-formatted `_display` version, such as `$29.99` instead of `29.99`.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Your new template isn't the one that prints.** The till uses the first active template in **Your Templates**. Drag yours to the top, or turn off the ones above it. For a single sale, the cashier can choose it from **Template** on the paid screen.

**There's no Template picker on the paid screen.** Only one receipt template is active. Turn on another in **Your Templates** to give cashiers a choice.

**A template can't be picked while offline.** **Legacy PHP Template** and the WP Overnight templates are built on the server. Make an HTML or thermal template the default if the till often loses its connection.

**The logo is missing.** Without Pro, the receipt uses your WordPress site logo, so set one in your theme. With Pro, open the store's **Details** and choose **Use site logo** or **Upload logo**. If the logo is missing only when printing from a browser tab, see [Logo Missing in the Browser](../hardware/printers/logo-missing-browser.mdx).

**Nothing prints, or it prints to the wrong printer.** Check the template's printer in **Settings → Printing**, then see [When nothing prints](../hardware/printers/index.mdx#when-nothing-prints).

## Template gallery {#template-gallery}

The gallery ships with ready-made templates you can use as they are or customise. Filter by **Category** (Receipt, Invoice, Gift Receipt, Credit Note, Purchase Order, Kitchen Ticket, Bar Ticket), **Format** (**HTML (browser print)** or **ESC/POS (thermal printer)**) and **Direction** (**Left-to-right** or **Right-to-left**). Each card has **Preview** and **Use Template**; **Use Template** makes an editable copy in *Your Templates* and leaves the gallery's original as it was.

When the gallery's version of a template you copied is newer, the card says **Update available**. **Install new version** adds the new version as a separate copy, so your edits stay.

### Reports, closures and customer displays {#other-templates}

The other tabs in **POS → Templates** work the same way. **Reports** holds the layouts for a printed report (Report, Report (Letterhead), and Thermal Report in 80mm and 58mm). **Closures** holds the end-of-shift slips (Closure, Closure (Detailed), Thermal Closure in 80mm, 58mm and 80mm RTL, and Count Slip (80mm)). **Display** holds the customer display templates, which need Pro. Reports and closures can't use the Legacy PHP engine.

### Third-party document templates {#third-party-document-templates}

With WP Overnight's **PDF Invoices & Packing Slips for WooCommerce** active, its invoice and packing slip join your receipt templates. They need a connection to print.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="How the WP Overnight templates work">

WCPOS adds **Invoice (WP Overnight)** and **Packing Slip (WP Overnight)** to the receipt templates, turned on. They hand the order to WP Overnight's document API, so they keep the invoice and packing-slip numbering, layout, branding, legal and tax fields, and template changes set up in that plugin.

They are there only while the WP Overnight plugin is active, and the server renders them. Use a bundled HTML or thermal template when you need to print offline. See [WP Overnight templates](./customise.mdx#wp-overnight-invoice-and-packing-slip-templates).

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

### Adaptive tax display {#adaptive-tax-display}

Most bundled templates print prices with or without tax to match your WooCommerce tax display setting.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Which templates follow the setting, and how">

Standard, Standard RTL, Minimal/Modern, Narrow, Invoice, Quote and the Simple Thermal templates **follow your WooCommerce tax display setting**:

- **Stores that show prices with tax** (EU/UK/AU) print gross prices with a "Tax included" line.
- **Stores that show prices without tax** (US/CA) print net prices with tax added as a separate line, labelled **Total Tax**.
- The grand total always shows the gross amount actually charged.

The **Detailed** templates are formal tax invoices: their item lines are always without tax, followed by a breakdown of each tax rate. Only the tax heading follows your setting.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Template engines {#template-engines}

Each template uses one of three engines: **HTML (Offline)**, **XML (Receipt Printer)** or **PHP (Legacy)**. HTML and XML print offline; PHP needs the server.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="The three engines">

You choose the engine when you create a template, or the gallery card decides it when you click **Use Template**. It **can't be changed once the template is saved**: start a new template to switch.

<FeatureGrid>
  <FeatureCard icon="code" title="HTML (Offline), recommended">
    Logicless HTML with <code>{"{{variable}}"}</code> placeholders. Renders on the device, so printing **works offline**. For the print dialog.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="print" title="XML (Receipt Printer)">
    XML that gives both a screen preview and **ESC/POS commands** for thermal receipt printers. Same template, two outputs. For Epson, Star, and other thermal printers.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard icon="terminal" title="PHP (Legacy)">
    Server-side PHP using WooCommerce functions. Kept for existing <code>yourtheme/woocommerce-pos/receipt.php</code> overrides and the built-in **Legacy PHP Template**. Needs a connection to the server to render, and receipts only.
  </FeatureCard>
</FeatureGrid>

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
