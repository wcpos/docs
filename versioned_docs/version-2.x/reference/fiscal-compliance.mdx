Some countries require the till to be certified, to keep records no one can alter, or to report each sale to the tax authority. Check this page before you take live sales with WCPOS in one of them.

:::warning WCPOS is not a certified till
WCPOS 2.0 holds no fiscal certification in any country. If the law where you trade requires a certified till, WCPOS can't be that till yet. Before you take live sales, ask your accountant or your tax authority what your country requires of a till. If you're a cashier, ask your store owner.
:::

## What 2.0 already records {#what-2-0-records}

2.0 lays the groundwork that country rules build on. Every till already keeps these records, in the free plugin as well as Pro:

- **The register on every sale.** Each order and each receipt names the register it was rung up on, and the receipt names the WCPOS version.
- **The real time of an offline sale.** A sale made with no connection keeps the time it was made and its register, however late it reaches your store.
- **Refunds and voids as their own records.** A refund or a void never changes the sale. It is a separate record that can't be edited, and it prints its own receipt, a credit note that names the sale it corrects.
- **Numbered closures.** Each time a register closes, the closure is written once and numbered. See [Count and close](../pos/register/index.mdx#close).
- **A place for a fiscal QR code** on the receipt, left empty until a country's rules fill it.

None of this is signed or sent to a tax authority yet, so it does not make WCPOS compliant on its own. See [Receipts and refunds built for fiscal rules](../getting-started/whats-new.mdx#receipts-and-refunds).

## How tax is worked out {#how-wcpos-handles-tax}

Your store's figures are the ones that count. If the total the POS showed differs from the one your store works out, the POS says **Your store changed this order's totals**: see [Totals Disagree](../support/troubleshooting/totals-disagree.mdx).

<details>
<summary>How the POS and your store work out tax</summary>

The POS works out tax on the device, from the tax rates it copies from WooCommerce, so the cart shows a total even with no connection. When the order reaches your store, WooCommerce works out its totals again from the order's lines.

So a tax rule or plugin that works through WooCommerce's order totals applies to POS sales too. A plugin that acts only on your website's checkout page does not, because a POS order never passes through that page. Which address sets the rate is **Calculate tax based on**: see [Tax Settings](../settings/store/tax.mdx#calculate-tax-based-on).

</details>

<details>
<summary>For developers: filling the fiscal fields</summary>

A fiscal plugin fills the receipt's fiscal fields, such as the QR code and the signature, through the `wcpos_fiscal_snapshot_enrich` filter, when the sale's record is written. It runs while the record is being numbered, so it must work locally with no network call: send anything to a tax authority after the record exists. The fields are listed in [Receipt Data](../receipts/receipt-data.mdx#fiscal). A field changed on a live receipt through `wcpos_receipt_data` does not replace the recorded fiscal values.

</details>

## Country by country {#country-status}

Laws and dates change. Each summary below was checked in November 2026 against the tax authority's own page, linked in the section. Where they differ, the authority's page is right.

### France: NF525 {#france-nf525}

Since 2018, French law (article 286 of the General Tax Code) requires a business that records customer payments in till software to use a system that meets anti-fraud rules, shown by a certificate such as NF525 or by the software publisher's attestation. See the [French tax administration's guidance](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/export/pdf/12315) (in French). WCPOS is not certified for NF525 and has no attestation. The records French rules ask for are planned for [2.1](../getting-started/roadmap.mdx#fiscal-compliance), with Spain.

### Spain: VeriFactu {#spain-verifactu}

VeriFactu (Royal Decree 1007/2023) sets rules for the software a business uses to issue invoices, tills included. Companies must comply from 1 January 2027 and other businesses from 1 July 2027. See the [Spanish Tax Agency's VeriFactu page](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/sistemas-informaticos-facturacion-verifactu/cuestiones-generales.html) (in Spanish). WCPOS does not support VeriFactu yet. The records it asks for are planned for [2.1](../getting-started/roadmap.mdx#fiscal-compliance), with France.

### Norway: Cash Register Systems Act {#norway-kassasystemloven}

The Norwegian Cash Register Systems Act (_kassasystemloven_) requires the supplier of a till to file a product declaration with the Norwegian Tax Administration (Skatteetaten). See [Skatteetaten's cash register page](https://www.skatteetaten.no/bedrift-og-organisasjon/starte-og-drive/rutiner-regnskap-og-kassasystem/kassasystem/) (in Norwegian). There is no product declaration for WCPOS.

### Chile: SII {#chile-sii}

WCPOS does not connect to Chile's Servicio de Impuestos Internos (SII), so it can't issue validated electronic receipts (_boletas_). See the [SII's questions on electronic receipts](https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/bol_electr_vtas_serv/001_380_6568.htm) (in Spanish). You can [change the receipt template](../receipts/customise.mdx), but nothing sends a receipt to the SII.

### EU: One Stop Shop (OSS) {#eu-oss}

WCPOS has nothing built for the EU One Stop Shop VAT scheme, and makes no OSS report. The POS charges the rates you set in WooCommerce, by the address in **Calculate tax based on**, and your store works the order out again with the same rates. To charge another EU country's VAT on a sale you ship there, that country's rates must be in WooCommerce and the order must be taxed by the customer's address: see [How tax is worked out](#how-wcpos-handles-tax).

### Other countries {#other-regulated-markets}

Germany, Italy, Austria, Portugal and the Czech Republic have their own fiscal till rules. WCPOS supports none of them yet. France and Spain come first, and each further country builds on the same records.
