Stores are set up in WP Admin, your site's WordPress dashboard, by an administrator. This page walks through one store: creating it, setting up its registers, choosing its staff, and checking what a cashier sees at sign-in. New to stores? Start with [Stores](./index.mdx). Working the till? There is nothing to set up here: [What cashiers see](#what-cashiers-see-at-login) shows how you pick your store when you sign in.

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

- **[WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) is installed and the licence is active.** You need Pro to have more than one store. Without it, the POS runs a single store that uses your WooCommerce settings. With Pro active, **Stores** appears at the top of the **POS** menu in WP Admin.
- **Your cashiers can sign in to the POS.** [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx) decides who can use the POS at all. A store can only narrow that list.
- *(Optional)* **A logo file**, PNG or JPG.

## Create the store {#create-the-store}

1. In WP Admin, go to **POS → Stores**.
2. Click **Add New Store**.

<JourneyShot manifest={wpAdmin} device="desktop" step={1} offsets={[[110, 0], [125, 0]]} alt="WP Admin, POS → Stores: three stores listed with their address, authorized users and number of registers, an Open POS button on each row, and the Add New Store button at the top">

1. **Stores** — under **POS** in the WP Admin menu. It only shows with Pro active.
2. **Add New Store** — opens an empty store, ready to name.

</JourneyShot>

3. The new store opens on its **Details** page. Type a name in **Store name**, at the top. Cashiers see this name when they choose a store at sign-in, and in the POS.
4. Click **Create**, at the top right.

<JourneyShot manifest={wpAdmin} device="desktop" step={2} maxWidth={720} offsets={[[0, 0], [-30, 0]]} alt="A new store in WP Admin before it is created: an empty Store name field, the Logo and address fields, and the Create button at the top right">

1. **Store name** — the only field you need before **Create**.
2. **Create** — makes the store. From then on there is no save button: every change saves as you make it.

</JourneyShot>

After **Create**, changes save as you make them: the top of the page says **Saving…**, then **Saved**. If you leave while a change is still saving, the browser asks you to confirm. If you see **Couldn't save.** instead, go to [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong).

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="What the Stores list shows">

Each store has a row with its **Store** name, **Address**, **Authorized Users** (**All** when nobody is picked on its **Staff** page), number of **Registers**, and an **Open POS** button that opens the POS at that store.

You can also reach your stores from **POS → Settings → General**. Once you have a store, its **Stores** section lists each one with its **Address** and an **Edit** button, and **Manage stores** opens the full list.

<JourneyShot manifest={wpAdmin} device="desktop" step={6} maxWidth={720} offsets={[[-25, 0], [130, 0]]} alt="The Stores section of POS → Settings → General: each store with its address and an Edit link, and the Manage stores button below">

1. **Edit** — opens that store's pages.
2. **Manage stores** — opens **POS → Stores**.

</JourneyShot>

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Fill in the store's pages {#store-pages}

A store's settings are split across pages, listed in the menu on the left (on a narrow screen, tap **Show store menu**). A new store needs three of them:

1. On **Details**, enter the store's address (**Address 1**, **Address 2**, **Country**, then town or city, state and postcode) and its **Contact** details: **Phone**, **Email**, **Web address**. The address prints on receipts, with the logo if you add one. To rename the store later, change **Store name** here.

<JourneyShot manifest={wpAdmin} device="desktop" step={3} maxWidth={820} offsets={[[185, -18], [-30, 0], [70, -6]]} alt="The Details page of a store called Harbour Street: the store menu on the left with Store and Selling groups, then Store name, Logo and address with Upload logo and the address fields, and Contact with Phone, Email and Web address">

1. **The store's menu** — **Store** pages first, then **Selling**. A badge shows where each page stands: here **Tax** says **Set up**, **Registers** says **1 open**, and **Staff** counts the people ticked.
2. **Address 1** — the start of the address that prints on receipts.
3. **Contact** — how customers can reach this store: **Phone**, **Email** and **Web address**.

</JourneyShot>

2. Open **Registers** and [set up the tills](#registers).
3. Open **Staff** and [choose who can use the store](#staff).

Open the other pages only when this store needs something of its own, such as opening hours or a different currency. **All stores**, at the top of the menu, takes you back to the list. To edit a store later, click its name in **POS → Stores**.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Store settings reference: what each page sets">

The menu has two groups: **Store** (Details, Opening hours, Currency & language, Tax) and **Selling** (Receipts, Customer display, Registers, Staff). A plugin that adds store settings, such as [ATUM](../extensions/atum.mdx), gets a page of its own under a third group, **Extensions**.

Once the store is created, some entries carry a badge: **Registers** shows how many tills are open (**1 open**), or how many there are when none is; **Tax** shows **Set up** when sales at this store won't be taxed (tax is off in WooCommerce, there are no rates, or no rate matches the store); **Staff** shows how many people are ticked.

| Page | What you set there |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | **Logo and address**, which print on receipts, and **Contact**: **Phone**, **Email**, **Web address**. **Upload logo**, or **Use site logo** if the site has one. |
| **Opening hours** | Time slots for each day, **Copy to Tue–Fri**, and **Notes**. Printed on receipts. |
| **Currency & language** | **Currency** and its **Symbol position**, **Language** (the POS screen and receipts), and **Timezone** (close reminders and report dates). |
| **Tax** | **Calculate tax using** this store's address, the shop base address, or the customer's billing or shipping address, with the **Rates that apply**. Below it, the **Tax number printed on receipts**: **Add tax number** (**Type**, **Value**, **Country**, and an optional **Label**), or leave it empty and receipts print the site's [Store Tax IDs](../settings/wp-admin/store-tax-ids.mdx). |
| **Receipts** | **Receipt messages** (**Thank-you line**, **Returns policy**, **Footer**: plain text, with `<strong>`, `<em>`, `<b>`, `<i>`, `<br>` and `<p>` allowed), the **Receipt template** list (**Same as site** until you click **Customise**; then tick what cashiers can print, the first ticked is the default, and drag to change the order), and a **Receipt preview** below them. See [Per-store templates](../receipts/customise.mdx#per-store-assignments). |
| **Customer display** | The **Display template** the customer-facing screen shows during a sale. |
| **Registers** | The store's tills and its cash rules. See [Set up the registers](#registers). |
| **Staff** | Who can use this store. See [Choose who can use the store](#staff). |

The rates themselves are set in **WooCommerce → Settings → Tax**; the **Tax** page only shows which apply.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Set up the registers {#registers}

Open **Registers** in the store's menu. A register is one cash drawer with its own float and count. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](./registers-and-devices.mdx) for how they fit together.

### Tills {#tills}

A store comes with one register, so a store with a single till needs nothing added. Your first store takes over the register your site already had; each store after that gets a new one. The **Tills** table lists the store's registers once the store has been created. Until then it says **Save the store first to add registers.**

1. Give the register the name cashiers should see: choose **Rename** from its **⋯** menu.
2. In **Opening float**, type the cash this till should start with. The amount is filled in for the cashier the next time the till is opened. A till with no float saved starts at its last count instead.
3. If the store has another cash drawer, click **+ Add register**. It is called **Register 2**, **Register 3** and so on until you rename it.

<JourneyShot manifest={wpAdmin} device="desktop" step={4} maxWidth={820} offsets={[[100, 0], [258, 0], [-30, 0], [290, 0]]} alt="The Registers page of Harbour Street after a second register was added: the Tills table with Café till and Front counter, each with its opening float, status and ⋯ menu, + Add register below">

1. **Opening float** — what the cash field starts at when a cashier opens this till. Here **Front counter** starts at **150.00** and **Café till** at **100.00**.
2. **Status** — **Open since** a time, with who opened it; **Counting**, with who is counting; or **Closed**. A till that is open or counting can't be retired: count and close it in the POS first.
3. **⋯** — **Rename**, **Move to** another store, or **Retire**.
4. **+ Add register** — another cash drawer at this store. **Café till** was added this way.

</JourneyShot>

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="The Tills table: status and the ⋯ menu">

- **Register** — the register's name, as the POS shows it.
- **Opening float** — the cash a cashier starts with at this till.
- **Status** — **Open since** a time, with who opened it; **Counting**, with who is counting; or **Closed**, with the last closure's number and date.
- **⋯** — **Rename**, **Move to** another store, or **Retire**. A till that is open or counting can't be retired: count and close it in the POS first. A register belongs to one store at a time.

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

### Cash drawer rules {#cash-drawer-rules}

Most stores want this rule on: cashiers start with a float and count the drawer when they close. With it off, the POS goes straight to the cart.

If cashiers at this store should count the drawer and the section shows **Site defaults**:

1. Click **Customise**.
2. Turn on **Open and close the register each day**. It is this store's version of **Open and close the register** in the site's settings.

<JourneyShot manifest={wpAdmin} device="desktop" step={8} maxWidth={720} offsets={[[78, 0], [-30, 0], [-45, 0], [-30, 0]]} alt="The Cash drawer rules section of Harbour Street after Customise: the Site defaults chip, and Open and close the register each day, switched off and marked Site default">

1. **Site defaults** — every rule in this section follows the site's.
2. **Open and close the register each day** — off here. Click the switch to turn it on for this store.
3. **Site default** — this rule follows the site's setting until you change it here.
4. **Customise** — this store's own cash drawer rules, instead of the site's.

</JourneyShot>

Until you click **Customise**, the store follows the site's rules, which are set in [Register Settings](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) (**POS → Settings → General**). **Customise** changes them for this store only.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Optional cash drawer rules: manager approval, close reminder, going back to the site's rules">

With **Open and close the register each day** on, you can turn on two more rules:

- **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than** an amount (**5.00** when first turned on). If the count is out by more than that, a manager must approve it before the register closes.
- **Remind the cashier to close at** a time (**17:30** when first turned on). Past that time the register shows **Overdue**, and while the cart is empty the **Checkout** button becomes **Close register**.

A rule is marked **Site default** while it follows the site. After you change one, **Use site default** undoes that rule, and **Reset to site defaults** undoes all three.

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

### Reports & closures {#reports-and-closures}

**Closure printout**, further down the **Registers** page, sets what prints when a till is closed, and **Sales report** sets what prints when a sales report is run. Both say **Same as site** until you click **Customise**. **Use site template** switches back.

## Choose who can use the store {#staff}

Open **Staff** in the store's menu.

- **Everyone with POS access** — the default. Good for a single store.
- **Only these people** — tick each person who may sign in to this store. The list shows everyone with POS access, whether they have it through their role or were given it individually. When the list has more than ten people, **Search people** helps you find someone.

<JourneyShot manifest={wpAdmin} device="desktop" step={5} maxWidth={720} offsets={[[150, 0], [105, 0]]} alt="The Staff page of Harbour Street: Only these people is chosen, and a count above the list of people says how many of them are ticked">

1. **Only these people** — limits the store to the people ticked below.
2. **A ticked person** — can sign in to this store. The count above the list says how many are ticked.

</JourneyShot>

:::warning Unticking the last person opens the store to everyone
If you untick the last person, the store switches to **Everyone with POS access** and a note tells you so. To remove someone, keep at least one other person ticked.
:::

Someone who works at more than one store needs a tick on each store's **Staff** page.

## What cashiers see {#what-cashiers-see-at-login}

To check the new store, sign in to the POS as one of its cashiers. What you see next depends on whether the device is already in use at another store.

**If sign-in offers the new store:**

1. Under **Choose a store**, pick the new store. If it is the only store the cashier can use, it is picked already and the heading says **Store**.
2. Press **Open POS**.

**If sign-in offers only another store** (the device is already in use there):

1. Open the POS at that store.
2. Choose **Switch store** in the cashier sheet (tap the avatar) and pick the new store. See [Choosing a store and a register](./registers-and-devices.mdx#choosing-a-store-and-a-register).

Either way, check that the register bar at the top of the cart names the new store. Each sale is recorded against the store it was rung up at.

On a new device, **Choose a store** lists every store the cashier can use:

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} alt="The desktop app's sign-in screen for the Harbour and Vine site: Maya Chen signed in as a cashier, Choose a store listing Harbour Street, picked, and Market Hall, each with its number on the right, and the Open POS button">

1. **Harbour Street** — a store Maya Chen can use, picked here.
2. **Market Hall** — another store she can use, ready to pick instead.
3. **Open POS** — enabled once a store is picked. With only one store, it's picked already and the heading says **Store**.

</JourneyShot>

If the cashier can't use any store, the screen says **No stores for this user**: tick them on a store's **Staff** page, or set it to **Everyone with POS access**.

When a store has more than one register, the bar names the register as well. A status appears beside it when the register needs attention. [Open the Register](../pos/register/index.mdx#open) shows the bar.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Register statuses: Closed, Counting, Approval needed, Overdue">

Each status follows the store's [cash drawer rules](#cash-drawer-rules):

| Status | When it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Closed** | The register isn't open. The cash field starts at the till's **Opening float** once one is set, otherwise at the last count. |
| **Counting** | Someone is counting the drawer to close it. |
| **Approval needed** | The count is out by more than your limit, so a manager has to approve it. |
| **Overdue** | The register is still open after your close time. |

An open register with nothing to do shows no status.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Per-store prices and taxes {#per-store-pricing}

A product can have its own price or tax at one store, while its stock stays shared. Skip this section unless a store needs it.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Optional: set different prices or taxes for this store">

1. Open the product in **WP Admin → Products**.
2. On the **General** tab, tick **Enable *store name* specific prices.** for the store.
3. Enter the **Regular price**, and a **Sale price** if you want one.
4. Update the product.

<JourneyShot manifest={wpAdmin} device="desktop" step={7} maxWidth={820} offsets={[[-20, 0], [-30, 0]]} alt="The Product data box of a product, General tab: below the normal prices, Enable Harbour Street specific prices. is ticked, with that store's own Regular price of 16.00; the other stores' boxes and the specific taxes boxes are not ticked">

1. **Enable *store name* specific prices.** — one box for each store. The **specific taxes** boxes are further down, below **Tax class**.
2. **The store's Regular price** — what cashiers at that store charge. The product's own **Regular price**, above, stays the price everywhere else.

</JourneyShot>

Cashiers at that store get that price; every other store uses the normal WooCommerce price. **Enable *store name* specific taxes.** does the same for **Tax status** and **Tax class**, for a store in a different tax zone. For a variable product the boxes are on each variation, under **Variations**; a variation's taxes box sets only **Tax class**.

:::tip Stock per location
Per-store prices share one stock count. For stock counted per location, see [ATUM Multi-Inventory](../extensions/atum.mdx). With ATUM, a store whose **Pricing source** is **ATUM** uses the location's prices instead, where ATUM has a price for the product; see [Choose where prices come from](../extensions/atum.mdx#pricing-source).
:::

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Couldn't save.** shows at the top of the page, with the reason. The change was not saved. Press **Retry** on the message, or reload the page and check what was kept.

**Save the store first to add registers.** Click **Create** first; the **Tills** table appears once the store exists.

**A register won't retire.** It has a session that is open or counting. Count and close it in the POS, then **Retire** it.

**No stores for this user** at sign-in. The cashier isn't on any store's **Staff** list. Tick them, or set a store to **Everyone with POS access**.

**There's no Stores menu.** Pro isn't active. Check the licence in **POS → Settings → License**; see [Installing WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).

## Edge cases {#edge-cases}

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="What happens to old orders when I add a store?">

Nothing. A sale is recorded against its store when it's rung up, so a new store doesn't take over older orders. To move one order to another store, open it in **WooCommerce → Orders** and change **POS Store**.

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="How do I delete a store?">

**Move store to Trash**, at the top right of any of the store's pages (it asks first), or **Trash** in the list. A trashed store leaves the store picker, but its orders still show its name, address and logo, and **Restore** brings it back with its settings. While one of its registers is open or counting, the store can't be trashed, and it stays where it is: close the register first.

**Delete Permanently** in the Trash removes it for good, and is refused while one of its registers is open or counting. Its orders then show **Store #** and its ID.

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="Can a store use a different currency?">

Yes, on **Currency & language**. Prices, totals and receipts at that store use its currency. This changes how amounts are shown; it does not convert them. Your payment methods must accept that currency, and any exchange rate is yours to work out.

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="What if Pro is turned off?">

The site goes back to its one register. See [If Pro is deactivated](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#if-pro-is-deactivated).

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
