:::info Pro Feature
More than one store needs [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/pro-license). Without Pro, the POS runs one store built from your WooCommerce settings, and there's no Stores screen.
:::

This page walks through one store from an empty list to a cashier signing in to it. For what stores are for, start with [Stores](/stores).

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

- **WCPOS Pro is installed and the licence is active.** Pro then puts **Stores** at the top of the **POS** menu in WP Admin.
- **Your cashiers can sign in to the POS.** Who has POS access at all is set in [Access](/settings/wp-admin/access). A store can only narrow that list.
- *(Optional)* **A logo file**, PNG or JPG.

## Create the store {#create-the-store}

1. In WP Admin, go to **POS → Stores**.
2. Click **Add New Store**.
3. Type the store's name where it says **Store name**, at the top of the page. It shows in the store picker at sign-in and in the POS.
4. Click **Create**.

From then on the page saves by itself: the top corner says **Saving…**, then **✓ Saved**. If it says **⚠ Not saved**, see [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong).

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="What the Stores list shows">

Each store has a row with its **Store** name, **Address**, **Authorized Users** (**All** when nobody is picked on its **Staff** page), number of **Registers**, and an **Open POS** button that opens the POS at that store.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

You can also reach your stores from **POS → Settings → General**. Once you have a store, its **Stores** section lists each one with its **Address** and an **Edit** button, and **Manage stores** opens the full list.

## Fill in the store's pages {#store-pages}

The store's menu, down the left, has two groups: **Store** (Details, Opening hours, Currency & language, Tax) and **Selling** (Receipts, Customer display, Registers, Staff). Every page saves as you go. **Rename** beside the name changes it later, and **All stores** goes back to the list.

For a new store:

1. On **Details**, enter the store's address (**Address 1**, **Address 2**, **Country**, then city, state and postcode) and its **Contact** details: **Web Address**, **Phone**, **Email**. The address prints on receipts, with the logo if you add one.
2. [Set up the registers](#registers).
3. [Choose who can use the store](#staff).

The other pages are there for what this store does differently.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Store settings reference: what each page sets">

| Page | What you set there |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | **Logo and address**, which print on receipts, and **Contact**: **Web Address**, **Phone**, **Email**. **Upload logo**, or **Use site logo** if the site has one. |
| **Opening hours** | Time slots for each day, **Copy to Tue–Fri**, and **Notes**. Printed on receipts. |
| **Currency & language** | **Currency** and its **Symbol position**, **Language** (the POS screen and receipts), and **Timezone** (close reminders and report dates). |
| **Tax** | **Calculate tax using** this store's address, the shop base address, or the customer's billing or shipping address, with the **Rates that apply**. Below it, the **Tax number printed on receipts**: **Add tax number**, or leave it empty and receipts print the site's [Store Tax IDs](/settings/wp-admin/store-tax-ids). |
| **Receipts** | **Receipt messages** (**Thank-you line**, **Returns policy**, **Footer**), the **Receipt template** cashiers can print, and a **Receipt preview**. See [Per-store templates](/receipts/customise#per-store-assignments). |
| **Customer display** | The **Display template** the customer-facing screen shows during a sale. |
| **Registers** | The store's tills and its cash rules. See [Set up the registers](#registers). |
| **Staff** | Who can use this store. See [Choose who can use the store](#staff). |

The rates themselves are set in **WooCommerce → Settings → Tax**; the **Tax** page only shows which apply.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Set up the registers {#registers}

Open **Registers** in the store's menu. A register is one cash drawer with its own float and count. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](./registers-and-devices.mdx) for how they fit together.

### Tills {#tills}

Every store already has one register. The first store you create takes over the register your site already had; every store after that gets a register of its own. The **Tills** table lists them once the store is saved; before that it says **Save the store first to add registers.**

1. To name the register as cashiers will see it, choose **Rename** from its **⋯** menu.
2. Type its **Opening float**: what the cash field starts at when a cashier opens this till. It shows **200.00** until you change it, and a change applies from the next opening.
3. Only for another cash drawer, click **+ Add register**. The new one is named **Register 2**, **Register 3** and so on, ready to rename.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="The Tills table: status and the ⋯ menu">

- **Register** — the register's name, as the POS shows it.
- **Opening float** — the cash a cashier starts with at this till.
- **Status** — **Open since** a time, with who opened it; **Counting**, with who is counting; or **Closed**, with the last closure's number and date.
- **⋯** — **Rename**, **Move to** another store, or **Retire**. A till with a session still open can't be retired: close its session first. A register belongs to one store at a time.

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

### Cash drawer rules {#cash-drawer-rules}

These rules start as the **Site defaults** from [Register](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) in **POS → Settings → General**. Click **Customise** to change them for this store only.

**Open and close the register each day** is the one most stores need: cashiers start with a float and count the drawer at close. Off, the POS goes straight to the cart.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="Optional cash drawer rules: manager approval, close reminder, going back to the site's rules">

While the register opens and closes each day, two more rules can be turned on:

- **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than** an amount (**5.00** when first turned on). A count out by more needs a manager before the register closes.
- **Remind the cashier to close at** a time (**17:30** when first turned on). After it, the register shows **Overdue** and the **Checkout** button becomes **Close register** while the cart is empty.

Each rule says **Site default** while it follows the site; once you change it, **Use site default** puts that one back, and **Reset to site defaults** puts back all three.

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

### Reports & closures {#reports-and-closures}

**Closure printout** and **Sales report** choose what prints when a till is closed or a sales report is run. Each is **Same as site** until you click **Customise**; **Use site template** goes back.

## Choose who can use the store {#staff}

Open **Staff** in the store's menu.

- **Everyone with POS access** — the default. Good for a single store.
- **Only these people** — tick each person who can sign in to this store. Past ten people, **Search people** narrows the list.

:::warning Unticking the last person opens the store to everyone
Untick the last person and the store switches to **Everyone with POS access**, with a note saying so. To take someone off, make sure at least one other person stays ticked.
:::

A cashier who works at more than one store is ticked on each.

## What cashiers see {#what-cashiers-see-at-login}

To check the new store, sign in as one of its cashiers. A device that already works at one of the cashier's stores offers only that store, so use a device that doesn't.

1. Sign in to the POS with the cashier's WordPress account.
2. Under **Choose a store**, pick the new store and press **Open POS**. If it's the only store they can use, it's already picked, under **Store**.
3. Check that the register bar at the top of the cart names the store.

The store list shows every store the cashier can use.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} alt="The sign-in screen with a cashier signed in, Choose a store listing two stores with their numbers, one picked, and the Open POS button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **A store they can use** — picked here. The number after the name is the store's ID in WP Admin.
2. **Another store** — every store whose **Staff** page includes them is listed.
3. **Open POS** — enabled once a store is picked. With only one store, it's picked already and the heading says **Store**.

</div>

If the cashier can't use any store, the screen says **No stores for this user**: tick them on a store's **Staff** page, or set it to **Everyone with POS access**.

In the POS, the register bar at the top of the cart names the store, and the register too when the store has more than one. Beside it, a status shows when the register needs something, following the store's [cash drawer rules](#cash-drawer-rules):

| Status | When it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Closed** | The register isn't open. The cash field starts at the till's **Opening float**. |
| **Counting** | Someone is counting the drawer to close it. |
| **Approval needed** | The count is out by more than your limit, so a manager has to approve it. |
| **Overdue** | The register is still open after your close time. |

An open register with nothing to do shows no status. [Open the Register](../pos/register/index.mdx#open) has the bar on screen, with every status.

A cashier who can use more than one store changes store with **Switch store** in their cashier sheet. Each sale is recorded against the store it was rung up at.

## Per-store prices and taxes {#per-store-pricing}

Pro can give a product its own price at one store, with shared stock:

1. Open the product in **WP Admin → Products**.
2. On the **General** tab, tick **Enable *store name* specific prices.** for the store.
3. Enter the **Regular price**, and a **Sale price** if you want one.
4. Update the product.

Cashiers at that store get that price; every other store uses the normal WooCommerce price. **Enable *store name* specific taxes.** does the same for **Tax status** and **Tax class**, for a store in a different tax zone. Variations have the same boxes.

:::tip Stock per location
Per-store prices share one stock count. For stock counted per location, see [ATUM Multi-Inventory](/extensions/atum).
:::

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**⚠ Not saved** at the top of the page. The site didn't take the change. Press **Retry** on the message, or reload the page and check what's there.

**Save the store first to add registers.** Click **Create** first; the **Tills** table appears once the store exists.

**A register won't retire.** It has a session that is open or counting. Count and close it in the POS, then **Retire** it.

**No stores for this user** at sign-in. The cashier isn't on any store's **Staff** list. Tick them, or set a store to **Everyone with POS access**.

**There's no Stores menu.** Pro isn't active. Check the licence in **POS → Settings → License**; see [Installing WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/pro-license).

## Edge cases {#edge-cases}

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="What happens to old orders when I add a store?">

Nothing. A sale is recorded against its store when it's rung up, so a new store doesn't take over older orders. To move one order to another store, open it in **WooCommerce → Orders** and change **POS Store**.

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="How do I delete a store?">

**Move store to Trash**, at the foot of the store's menu, or **Trash** in the list. A trashed store leaves the store picker, but its orders still show its name, address and logo, and **Restore** brings it back with its settings.

**Delete Permanently** in the Trash removes it for good, and is refused while one of its registers is open or counting. Its orders then show **Store #** and its ID.

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="Can a store use a different currency?">

Yes, on **Currency & language**. Prices, totals and receipts at that store use its currency. It changes how money is shown, not the amounts: your payment methods must accept that currency, and exchange rates are yours to handle.

  </AccordionItem>

  <AccordionItem question="What if Pro is turned off?">

The site goes back to its one register. See [If Pro is deactivated](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#if-pro-is-deactivated).

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
