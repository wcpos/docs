WCPOS uses three words for the parts of your point of sale: **store**, **register** and **device**. Once you know which is which, you know what to set up and whether the free plugin covers it.

## Three words {#three-words}

A **store** is a place you sell from. It has its own address, taxes, prices and staff. If you have one shop, you have one store and you never need to think about it.

A **register** is a cash drawer. You open it with a float, sell, and close it with a count; the count becomes a numbered Z report. If you have one drawer, you have one register, and that is all the free plugin needs.

A **device** is any screen you use the POS on: a browser, the desktop app, a phone. Devices do not hold money. Three devices on one counter all ring on the same register and the same Z.

:::note A WCPOS store is not your WooCommerce site
The apps' [Connect screen](/getting-started/connect) calls each WooCommerce site you sign in to a store; a WCPOS store is a place you sell from inside that one site, and with Pro one site can have several.
:::

## When you need more {#when-you-need-more}

- **Another cash drawer** → another register (Pro). Each drawer gets its own float, count and Z.
- **Another shop, or another set of prices** (a trade counter, a wholesale list) → another store (Pro). It has its own prices and taxes.
- **Each store has its own register.** A trade counter and a retail counter are two stores, so they are two registers, each with its own count and Z.

## Choosing a store and a register {#choosing-a-store-and-a-register}

Cashiers pick the store at sign-in. On the free plugin there is only one, and with Pro the [store picker](/stores/setup#what-cashiers-see-at-login) lists the stores the cashier is authorized for.

Then the POS finds the register:

- **The store has one register** — the POS uses it without asking.
- **The store has several** — the cashier picks one. This happens once per device; the device remembers its register from then on.

A device that has been wiped with **Clear all local data**, or a reinstalled app, goes through the same step again.

To move a device to another register in the same store, open the user menu (the avatar) and choose **Switch register**. It is only offered when the store has more than one register, and it is disabled while a sale is in progress: finish or park the sale first, because a sale started on one register has to be completed on that register. **Switch store** (Pro, for cashiers authorized for more than one store) picks the register again the same way: silently when the new store has one, from the picker when it has several.

The bar at the top of the cart names the store, and the register as well when the store has more than one.

## Several devices on one register {#several-devices-on-one-register}

Devices that share a register share its session. The first device to open the register sets the float; a device that opens it later joins the session that is already open (see [REGISTER221](/error-codes/REGISTER221)). Every device then sells into the same drawer, and the drawer is counted and closed once, on any of them. Agree who counts before the shift ends.

## Common setups {#common-setups}

| Your shop | Stores | Registers | Devices | Edition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One shop, one counter | 1 | 1 | 1 | Free |
| One drawer used from several screens (browser, desktop app, phone) | 1 | 1, shared | Several | Free |
| Market stall or pop-up on a phone | 1 | 1 | 1 | Free |
| One shop, two cash drawers | 1 | 2 | One or more per drawer | Pro |
| Two shops | 2 | One or more each | One or more each | Pro |
| A retail counter plus a trade or wholesale counter | 2 (the trade prices are their own store) | One per store | One or more | Pro |

## Where the settings live {#where-the-settings-live}

- **Free:** **`WP Admin > POS > Settings > Registers`** shows your one register, with its name and default float. Every device uses it; more registers need Pro. See [Registers](/settings/wp-admin/registers).
- **Pro:** each store's registers are in the store editor (**POS → Stores**, open the store, **Registers**, **Add register**). The first store you create takes over the site's existing register; every store after that gets a register of its own. See [Setting Up Stores](/stores/setup).
