Settings live in three places. Most of what you change at the till is in **Settings** in the POS, and it changes this device only. What every till shares, the store owner sets in WordPress. And some screens have their own **Settings** button for how that screen looks.

- [**Settings** in the POS](#open) changes this device, for the store it's on. Anyone who can open the POS can change it.
- [`WP Admin > POS > Settings`](#wp-admin) changes every till in the store. Administrators and shop managers can change it, and so can a cashier whose role has **Manage POS settings**.
- [A screen's own **Settings**](#screen-settings) changes how that one screen looks, on this device. Anyone who can open the POS can change it.

## Open Settings in the POS {#open}

Press the gear at the foot of the menu, between **Store health** and **Support**. Settings opens on **General**, with its seven pages down the left. Press a page to open it. Each change saves as you make it: there's no Save button to press. On a phone, open the menu at the top left first: see [On a phone](#phone).

## What stays on this device {#this-device}

What you change in **Settings** in the POS is kept on this device, for the store it's on. Another till in the same store keeps its own settings, and nothing you change here reaches WordPress. Turning off **Manage POS settings** for a role in WordPress doesn't hide this screen: see [Access and Roles](./wp-admin/access.mdx).

**General** is the page to watch. The store name, language, default customer, and currency and number format are copied from WooCommerce once, and you can change them here for this device. The store's address is copied the same way, but you can't change it here. After that first copy, a change made in WooCommerce reaches none of them until you press **Restore Server Settings** at the foot of **General**.

:::warning Restoring overwrites this device's changes
**Restore Server Settings** loads your store's settings from WooCommerce again and puts them back over any change made on this device: everything on **General**, the address included, and the tax display choices on **Tax**. Printers, card readers, the customer display, barcode scanning and the theme stay as they are.
:::

## Settings every till shares {#wp-admin}

The store owner sets these in WordPress, in `WP Admin > POS > Settings`. They apply to every till in the store: for example which payment methods show at checkout and who can use the POS at all. See [Settings in the WordPress Admin](./wp-admin/index.mdx).

To change them you need a WordPress user that can manage the POS. Administrators and shop managers can; a cashier can't unless the owner turns on **Manage POS settings** for their role. See [Access and Roles](./wp-admin/access.mdx).

A few of these settings, such as the customer a new cart starts with, are copied to each till's **General** page when the till is set up. A till keeps its copy until someone presses **Restore Server Settings** there: see [What stays on this device](#this-device).

## A screen's own settings {#screen-settings}

The product panel, the cart, and the **Products**, **Orders**, **Customers** and **Coupons** screens each have a **Settings** button (<Icon name="sliders" />) for how that screen looks: which columns or details show, how it sorts, what it opens on. Each change saves as you make it, on this device only. Each screen's settings are explained with that screen:

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**A change on one till doesn't show on the other.** Settings in the POS are kept per device. Make the same change on each till or, for something every till should share, ask the store owner to change it in [`WP Admin > POS > Settings`](#wp-admin).

**The store name or currency is still the old one after a change in WooCommerce.** Open **Settings**, then **General**, and press **Restore Server Settings**. It overwrites any change made on this device on **General** (see [What stays on this device](#this-device)). See [Store Details, Language and Currency](./store/general.mdx).

**There's no POS menu in WP Admin.** Your WordPress user can't manage the POS. Ask an administrator to turn on **Manage POS settings** for your role. See [Access and Roles](./wp-admin/access.mdx).
