**General** holds the store's name and address, the language the POS shows, the customer each new cart starts with, and how money is written. Open it from **Settings** at the foot of the menu: it's the first page in the list.

These settings start as your WooCommerce store's settings. A change saves at once (there is no Save button) and applies to this device only. Your WooCommerce settings and the other tills stay as they were. To go back to the store's values, use [Restore Server Settings](#restore-server-settings).

## Store address {#store-address}

**Store Base Country**, **Store Base State**, **Store Base City** and **Store Base Postcode** are greyed out and marked **Set in WooCommerce**. They're your store's address, and the POS uses it to work out tax when a sale is taxed at the [shop base address](tax.mdx#calculate-tax-based-on), so it must match what your store uses.

To change the address:

1. Press **Open** on any of the four rows. Your WooCommerce settings open in the browser.
2. Change **Store Address** under `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > General` and save.
3. Back in the POS, press [Restore Server Settings](#restore-server-settings). The POS doesn't pick up a new address on its own.

A store with no city set shows a dash in **Store Base City**. On a phone, **Set in WooCommerce** and **Open** sit under each value.

## Language {#language}

**Language** changes the POS screens on this device and how amounts are written on receipts and the customer display. It does not translate the words printed on receipts, such as "Subtotal": those come from your store, see [Translations](../../support/translations.mdx).

It starts as your WordPress site's language.

## Default customer {#default-customer}

Every new cart starts with the **Default Customer**: **Guest** for a walk-in sale, or a customer you pick from the list. Turn on **Default Customer is Cashier** to start every new cart with the cashier who is signed in; the **Default Customer** list is then greyed out.

Both start as your store's [Default POS Customer](../wp-admin/general.mdx#default-pos-customer) and [Use Cashier Account](../wp-admin/general.mdx#use-cashier-account). A change here applies to new carts on this device. A cart that is already open keeps its customer, and you can change the customer of any sale in the cart: see [Choose the customer](../../pos/cart/index.mdx#customer-selector).

## Money format {#money-format}

:::warning Keep Currency and Number of Decimals the same as your store
**Currency** goes onto every new order this device sends to WooCommerce, and **Number of Decimals** is used to round payments and change at checkout. A value different from your store's gives orders in the wrong currency or payments that don't match the order total. A cart that is already open keeps the currency it started with.
:::

## Restore server settings {#restore-server-settings}

**Restore Server Settings** loads your store's settings again. Use it after you change something in WooCommerce, such as the store address or currency, and when the POS and your store disagree.

:::warning Restoring overwrites several settings at once
The store's name, address, language, default customer and money format go back to your store's values, even if you changed them on this device.
:::

<details>
<summary>Which settings are restored?</summary>

Restoring also refreshes the store's tax settings on the [Tax](tax.mdx) page. **Thousands Group Style** is a setting of this device only, so it stays as it is.

</details>

1. Scroll to the foot of **General** and press **Restore Server Settings**. **Restore server settings?** opens.
2. Press **Restore Server Settings** to go ahead, or **Cancel** to leave everything as it is.
3. When it's done, **Restored** shows for a moment under the button, and the page shows your store's values.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[0,38],[0,38]]} alt="The Restore server settings? dialog, saying the settings were copied from your WooCommerce store and restoring overwrites local changes, with Cancel and Restore Server Settings" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Cancel** — closes the dialog. Nothing changes.
2. **Restore Server Settings** — puts back your store's values on this device.

</div>

Restoring needs a connection to your store. Offline, the page says **Couldn't reach the store** and nothing changes. Press **Try again** when you're back online.

## If something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

**The store name, address or currency is out of date.** The POS copies them from your store the first time it loads it and doesn't update them on its own, so a rename or a change in WooCommerce doesn't reach the POS by itself. Press [Restore Server Settings](#restore-server-settings).

**Couldn't reach the store.** The device is offline or your store didn't answer. Check the connection, then press **Try again**. Your settings stay as they were until a restore succeeds.

**Prices show the wrong symbol or separators.** Someone changed **Currency & numbers** on this device. Set the rows back by hand, or press [Restore Server Settings](#restore-server-settings). Restoring also puts back the store's name, address, language and default customer on this device.

**The POS shows the wrong language.** Change **Language** on this page. Each device keeps its own.

**New carts start with the wrong customer.** Check **Default Customer** and **Default Customer is Cashier** on this device. For every device, change [Default POS Customer](../wp-admin/general.mdx#default-pos-customer) in WP Admin, then press **Restore Server Settings** on each device.
