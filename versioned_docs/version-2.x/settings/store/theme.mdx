**Theme** changes how the POS looks: its colours, the size of its buttons and text, its icons and its fonts. It changes nothing about a sale, a price or a receipt. Your choice is saved on this device, for the store you're working in, so the till by the window can be dark while the one in the back stays light.

Open it from **Settings**, the cog at the foot of the menu, then **Theme**. A change shows at once and is saved straight away; there is no Save button.

## Choose a theme {#choose}

Tap a tile and the whole POS changes, this screen included. Try one at the till; you can always come back to **Light**.

1. **Theme** — this page, under **Settings**.
2. **System** — follows your device. When the device switches to dark mode, the POS switches to **Dark**, and back to **Light** when it switches back. The line above the tiles then reads **Following System Theme**.
3. **Light** — the outlined tile is the theme in use, and **Current Theme** above the tiles names it. A new device starts on **Light**.
4. **Dark** — light text on a dark background, for a dim shop or an evening shift.
5. **Ocean** — one of three colour schemes, with **Sunset** and **Monochrome**. **Monochrome** is high-contrast greys, for a screen in bright light or a cashier who needs strong contrast.
6. **Paper till** — one of four shop styles, with **Warm shop**, **Bold money** and **Market stall**. Each brings its own colours, fonts and icons. See [What each theme changes](#each-theme).
7. **Auto** — the [Scale](#scale) a new device starts on: the POS sizes itself to the window.
8. **Compact** — smaller buttons and text, so more fits on the screen.
9. **Spacious** — larger buttons and text, easier to read and press. **Regular** sits between the two.

## Make everything bigger or smaller {#scale}

**Scale** sets the size of buttons, text and spacing everywhere in the POS. Leave it on **Auto** unless the screen feels cramped or too sparse.

- **Auto** — picks the size from the window: **Compact** on a narrow window such as a phone, **Spacious** on a wide desk monitor, **Regular** in between.
- **Compact**, **Regular**, **Spacious** — one size, whatever the window.

<details>
<summary>Where Auto switches, and touch screens</summary>

**Auto** uses **Compact** below 640 pixels wide, **Spacious** from 1600 pixels wide, and **Regular** between. Resize the browser or desktop window and the size follows.

On a touch screen, **Compact** keeps every button at least 44 pixels high so it stays easy to press; text and spacing still shrink. With a mouse, buttons get the full **Compact** size.

</details>

## Pick the icons and fonts {#icons-and-fonts}

**Icons** and **Fonts** start on **Match theme**, which follows the [table above](#each-theme). Pick a named family to keep it whatever theme you choose.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[130, 0], [-32, 0], [110, 0], [130, 0], [-32, 0], [175, 0]]} alt="Settings, Theme, further down: the Icons list with Match theme selected and six sample icons on each row, and the Fonts list with Match theme selected and a sample of each font" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Match theme** — the icons your theme uses. A new device starts here.
2. **Samples** — six icons you meet on every screen, drawn in that family, so you can compare before you pick.
3. **Phosphor** — one of three icon families, with **Tabler 1.5** and **Hugeicons**. Pick one to use it with every theme.
4. **Match theme** — the fonts your theme uses. A new device starts here.
5. **Samples** — **Aa** for text, **12.50** for prices and **Total** for headings, each in that font.
6. **DM Sans · Fraunces** — one of five font sets, with **System**, **DM Sans · Instrument Serif**, **Space Grotesk** and **Manrope**. **System** is your device's own font.

</div>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

- **The POS turned dark on its own** — **System** is on and the device went into dark mode. Pick **Light** or **Dark** to keep one look all day.
- **Another till looks different** — each device keeps its own theme. Set it on that device too.
- **The fonts or icons didn't change with the theme** — **Icons** or **Fonts** is set to a named family. Set it back to **Match theme**.
- **Buttons are too small to press, or too few fit on the screen** — change [Scale](#scale).
- **The theme changed after you switched stores** — each store keeps its own theme on this device. Set it again for that store.
