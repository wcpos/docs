With a keyboard on the counter, you can ring up a sale without reaching for the mouse. Hold **Alt** (**Option ⌥** on a Mac) and press a key. **Alt + /** shows them all.

:::note Where shortcuts work
In a browser and in the desktop app, on the POS screen. They work even while the cursor is in a text field. {/* pending: monorepo#3357 */} The phone and tablet apps have none, and neither do the other screens (Orders, Products, Settings).
:::

## See every shortcut {#list}

Press **Alt + /**. The list slides in from the cart's side of the screen.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Search products** — puts the cursor in the product search, ready to type or scan. See [Search & Filtering](../../pos/product-panel/search-filtering.mdx).
2. **Checkout** — opens the tender pane for the order in the cart. See [Checkout](../../pos/checkout/index.mdx).
3. **New cart** — starts an empty cart. The order you were on stays in [Open Orders](../../pos/cart/open-orders.mdx).
4. **Previous cart** — steps back to the open order before this one.
5. **Next cart** — steps forward to the next open order.
6. **Add discount** — opens **Add Discount** for the order. See [Discounts](../../pos/cart/discounts.mdx).
7. **Void order** — voids the order in the cart at once. See [Void](../../pos/cart/order-actions.mdx#void).
8. **Keyboard shortcuts** — this list.

</div>

A grey row can't run right now: **Checkout** with an empty cart, for example. Press any other row to run it, the same as its keys. On Windows, a note at the foot warns about [Firefox](#goes-wrong). Press **Close**, or **Alt + /** again, to put the list away.

## The shortcuts {#shortcuts}

| Press | What happens | Works when |
|---|---|---|
| **Alt + F** | The cursor goes to **Search products**. On a narrow screen, the Products tab opens first. | You're not at checkout or on the paid screen |
| **Alt + P** | **Checkout** opens. | The cart isn't empty |
| **Alt + N** | An empty cart. The order you were on stays open. | You're on an order, not already on an empty cart |
| **Alt + ←** / **Alt + →** | The previous or next open order. | Two or more orders are open |
| **Alt + D** | **Add Discount** opens. | You're on an order |
| **Alt + V** | The order in the cart is voided. | You're on an order |
| **Alt + /** | The shortcuts list opens or closes. | Any time on the POS screen, checkout included |

All but **Alt + /** wait while you're at checkout or on the paid screen. The cart shortcuts also wait while the cart is out of sight: behind the register picker, the **Open register** card, the count, or the open orders list.

:::warning Alt + V doesn't ask first
The order is voided as soon as you press it. The message that appears has an **Undo** button: press it straight away if you didn't mean to.
:::

The shortcuts are fixed. There's no setting to change them.

## Move through the Orders list {#orders}

On the **Orders** screen in a browser or the desktop app, click an order (or **Tab** into the list), then:

- **↓** / **↑** moves the highlight to the next or previous order.
- **Enter** opens the highlighted order.

:::info Pro Feature
The Orders screen comes with WCPOS Pro. See [Orders](../../orders/index.mdx).
:::

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Nothing happens.** Open the list with **Alt + /**: if the shortcut's row is grey, it can't run yet, for the reason in [the table](#shortcuts). Check you're on the POS screen, the register is open, and the open orders list isn't covering the cart.

**Alt + F or Alt + D opens the Firefox menu.** On Windows, Firefox may keep those two for itself. Press the row in the shortcuts list instead, or use Chrome, Edge or the desktop app.

**On a Mac, Option + N puts an accent in the search box instead.** Some keyboard layouts use Option + a letter to type an accent, and while you're typing in a field the POS lets the accent through. Click outside the field, then press the shortcut again.
