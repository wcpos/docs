The Tax page shows how the POS works out tax and how it shows it. Your tax rules live in WooCommerce: the POS copies them each time it loads your store and uses them for every sale. Open it from **Settings** at the foot of the menu, then **Tax**.

## The Tax page {#tax-page}

The page has three parts: **Calculation**, **Display** and **Tax Rates**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[245,0],[0,-36],[245,0],[245,0],[245,0],[-55,0],[-55,0],[-55,0]]} alt="Settings, Tax: Calculate tax based on, four rows set in WooCommerce with their switches greyed out, the three display choices, Price Display Suffix and the View all tax rates button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Enable Taxes** — on when WooCommerce calculates tax. Off, the POS adds no tax to a sale. The switch is greyed out because the value is [set in WooCommerce](#set-in-woocommerce).
2. **Open** — opens WooCommerce's tax settings for your store in the browser. Each greyed-out row has its own **Set in WooCommerce** note and **Open** link.
3. **Prices Entered with Tax** — on when your product prices already include tax.
4. **Shipping Tax Class** — the tax class used for shipping lines.
5. **Round Tax at Subtotal Level** — on when tax is rounded once for the order rather than line by line.
6. **Display Tax Totals** — **Itemized** shows each tax on its own line in the cart and on receipts; **As a single total** adds them into one line.
7. **Display Prices in the Shop** — whether prices on product tiles and rows show with or without tax.
8. **Display Prices During Cart and Checkout** — the same for the cart, checkout and receipts.

</div>

Above the greyed-out rows, **Calculate tax based on** picks which address sets the tax rate: see [Calculate tax based on](#calculate-tax-based-on). Under the display rows are **Price Display Suffix** and **View all tax rates**: see [How tax is shown](#display-settings) and [Check the tax rates](#view-all-tax-rates).

## Calculate tax based on {#calculate-tax-based-on}

| Choice | The rate comes from |
|---|---|
| **Customer shipping address** | where the order is shipped to |
| **Customer billing address** | the customer's billing address |
| **Shop base address** | your store's own address, from **General** |

This follows WooCommerce's **Calculate tax based on**: to change it for every till, change it in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax`.

When the order has no such address, the POS falls back the way WooCommerce does. With **Customer shipping address**, it uses the billing address, then the shop address. With **Customer billing address**, it uses the shop address. A walk-in sale with no customer is taxed at the shop address.

The choice travels with each POS order, so your store works out that order's tax the same way. Under the products, a button such as **Shop base** shows which address the cart is using: press it to see the address and the rates it matched.

## How tax is shown {#display-settings}

These four only change how amounts are shown. The tax charged is the same either way. They follow your WooCommerce settings: to change one for every till, change it in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax`.

| Row | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Display Tax Totals** | **Itemized**: one line per tax, such as "State" and "County", in the cart and on receipts. **As a single total**: one tax line. |
| **Display Prices in the Shop** | **Including tax** or **Excluding tax** on product tiles and rows. |
| **Display Prices During Cart and Checkout** | **Including tax** or **Excluding tax** on cart lines, in checkout, on receipts and on the customer display. |
| **Price Display Suffix** | WooCommerce's text after a price on your website, such as "inc. VAT". |

## Check the tax rates {#view-all-tax-rates}

Press **View all tax rates** to see every rate this device has downloaded, one tab per tax class. They're the rows from `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax`. Opened from the POS, as here, the rates that apply to the cart's address are marked: see [Which rates apply to this sale](#rates-for-this-address).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[363,0],[363,0],[347,0],[347,0],[-34,0]]} alt="The Tax rates panel opened from the POS: tabs for Tasa estándar, Reduced rate and Zero rate, four standard rates with the two US rates marked, and the foot reading 8 tax rates, 4 apply to this address (US)" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **US, 10%, priority 1** — a marked row: this rate applies to the address the cart is using.
2. **US, AL, 12345, 123456, 2%, priority 2** — also marked. It only applies in Alabama at those postcodes, and **Compound** means it's worked out on top of the 10%.
3. **GB, Surcharge** — rows without the mark don't match this address.
4. **GB, VAT, priority 1** — WooCommerce matches rates in priority order. Only one rate per priority applies to an address in each class.
5. **Close** — back to where you were.

</div>

Across the top is one tab per tax class with how many rates it holds; on a phone, you pick the class from a list instead. The columns are WooCommerce's: **Any** means every value, and **Not on shipping** under a name means the rate isn't charged on shipping. The foot gives the total across every class (**… of … tax rates synced** while some are still downloading). Press the sync button beside it to fetch changes from your store, or press and hold it, then **Clear and Refresh**, to download every rate again.

### Which rates apply to this sale {#rates-for-this-address}

Under the products, press the tax address button, such as **Shop base**, to see the address the cart is using and the rates it matched. **View all tax rates** in that panel opens the same list with those rates marked, and the foot says how many apply, such as "4 apply to this address (US)".

## Restore server settings {#restore-server-settings}

**Restore Server Settings** is at the foot of [General](general.mdx#restore-server-settings), not on this page. It loads your store's settings again, tax included, and puts back any value you changed on this device.

1. Press **Restore Server Settings**.
2. In **Restore server settings?**, press **Restore Server Settings** again to confirm, or **Cancel**.
3. **Restored** appears beside the button when it's done.

It needs a connection. Offline, the page says **Couldn't reach the store** and your settings stay as they were.

## Set in WooCommerce {#set-in-woocommerce}

Four rows can't be changed in the POS. Your store works these out when it saves an order, so a different value at the till would only give a different total. Change them in WooCommerce: press **Open** on the row, or go to `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax`. The POS picks up the new values the next time it loads your store.

### Enable Taxes {#enable-taxes}

WooCommerce's **Enable tax rates and calculations**, under `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > General`. Off, no sale has tax and the POS hides the tax address button under the products.

### Prices Entered with Tax {#prices-entered-with-tax}

On: the price you set already includes tax, and the POS works the tax out from inside it. Off: tax is added on top.

### Shipping Tax Class {#shipping-tax-class}

**Based on cart items** uses the tax class of what's in the cart. A named class, such as **Standard** or **Reduced rate**, taxes shipping at that class's rates.

### Round Tax at Subtotal Level {#rounding}

On: tax is added up across the order, then rounded once. Off: each line's tax is rounded on its own. The two can differ by a cent or two on a large order.

## How the POS works out tax {#tax-maths}

The POS uses your store's rates and WooCommerce's own tax maths:

- **Rounding** — each tax is worked out with extra decimal places, then rounded to your currency's decimals. With [Round Tax at Subtotal Level](#rounding) off, that happens line by line; on, it happens once on the order's tax total.
- **Compound taxes** — a rate marked **Compound** is worked out on top of the rates before it, in [priority](#view-all-tax-rates) order.

With [WooCommerce Tax](../../integrations/woocommerce-tax.mdx#what-to-expect) automated taxes, the first sale at a new location can differ once, until that location's rates reach the POS.

## If something goes wrong {#troubleshooting}

**The POS and WooCommerce show different totals.** Check the four rows under **Set in WooCommerce** match your store, then press **Restore Server Settings** on [General](general.mdx#restore-server-settings). With WooCommerce Tax, the first sale at a new location can differ once: see [WooCommerce Tax](../../integrations/woocommerce-tax.mdx#what-to-expect). For the notice the cart shows, see [Totals disagree](../../support/troubleshooting/totals-disagree.mdx).

**No tax on a sale.** Check **Enable Taxes** is on. Press **View all tax rates**: if the class is empty, add rates in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Tax`, then press and hold the sync button at its foot and choose **Clear and Refresh**. Check the product's **Tax status** is **Taxable** and its tax class has rates.

**The wrong rate.** Press the tax address button under the products, such as **Shop base**, to see which address the cart is using. A customer with no address on file is taxed at the shop address. With [WooCommerce Tax](../../integrations/woocommerce-tax.mdx) automated taxes on, don't add rows by hand: a location's rows appear after its first sale.

**Prices look lower than the sticker.** **Prices Entered with Tax** is on and a display row is set to **Excluding tax**. The POS is showing the price without its tax; the total charged is the same.

**A cent or two out.** Rounding differs between the till and the store. Check **Round Tax at Subtotal Level** in WooCommerce, then press **Restore Server Settings** on [General](general.mdx#restore-server-settings) so the POS has the same value.
