**Settings** is where you set up how the POS works on this device: the language and the way money is written, how tax shows, the printers, card readers and scanners at this till, a customer display, and how the POS looks. There's no Save button: a change takes effect as soon as you make it.

What you change here is kept on this device. Another till keeps its own settings, so set each till up on that till.

## Open Settings {#open}

Click **Settings** near the foot of the side menu, between **Store health** and **Support**. Settings opens on **General**, with its seven pages listed beside it. Click a page to open it.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Settings** — in the side menu, under **Store health** (the pulse with the red count) and above **Support**.
2. **General** — the store's name, the language, the default customer on a new cart, and how money is written: currency, decimals and separators. **Restore Server Settings** is at its foot. See [General](./general.mdx).
3. **Tax** — what tax is worked out from, and whether prices show with or without it. See [Tax](./tax.mdx).
4. **Printing** — the receipt printers this device prints to, and which printer each receipt template uses. See [Printers](../../hardware/printers/index.mdx).
5. **Card readers** — the card readers this till takes payments with: connect, disconnect or forget one. Card readers come with WCPOS Pro. See [Payment Gateways](../../payment/gateways/index.mdx).
6. **Customer Display** — pair a second screen that faces the customer. Press **Generate pairing code**, open the **Display page URL** on the other screen, then enter the code there. Customer displays come with WCPOS Pro.
7. **Barcode Scanning** — how the POS tells a scan from typing, the scanner at this till, and a test box to try it. See [Barcode Settings](./barcode.mdx).
8. **Theme** — the colours, the size of buttons and text, the icons and the fonts. See [Theme](./theme.mdx).

</div>

A row that reads **Set in WooCommerce** shows a value your store sends, such as its address or **Enable Taxes**. You can't change it here: press **Open** beside it to change it in WooCommerce.

## Keyboard shortcuts {#shortcuts}

There's no settings page for keyboard shortcuts, and they can't be changed. On the POS screen, press **Alt + /** to see them all. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](./hotkeys.mdx).

## Settings in WP Admin {#wp-admin}

Who can use the POS, the payment gateways at checkout, registers and their floats, the emails sent for POS orders, and your store's tax IDs are set in WordPress, under `WP Admin > POS > Settings`, not in the app. See [WordPress Admin Settings](../wp-admin/index.mdx).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Another till looks or works differently.** Each device keeps its own settings. Open **Settings** on that till and set it there too.

**A value doesn't match WooCommerce.** Press **Restore Server Settings** at the foot of **General**. It loads your store's settings again and puts back any value you changed on this device. See [Restore Server Settings](./general.mdx#restore-server-settings).

**No card readers are set up for this store.** Card readers are set up on the payment gateway's page in WooCommerce first, and they need WCPOS Pro. See [Payment Gateways](../../payment/gateways/index.mdx).

**Customer displays are a Pro feature.** Your store runs the free plugin. See [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx). If it says **Customer displays need a newer version of WCPOS Pro**, update the Pro plugin in WordPress.
