Open **Settings** and choose **Barcode Scanning**. Most stores never change anything here: a scanner works the moment you plug it in or pair it. Come here to turn on a scan sound, or when scans don't come through the way you expect.

Changes save as you make them, and there's no **Save** button. They belong to this device, so set them on each till that has a scanner.

**Scans not working?** Start with [Test your scanner](#test). It shows what the POS received and offers the fix.

1. **Barcode Average Time Input Threshold (ms)** — how fast the keys must come to count as a scan. See [Tell a scan from typing](#settings).

## Tell a scan from typing {#settings}

Not sure what to set? [Scan into the test](#test). It times your scanner and offers the fix.

<details>
<summary>Adjust scan detection</summary>

A scanner in keyboard mode, which is nearly every scanner, types the barcode as if it were a very fast keyboard. The POS times the gaps between keys. When the average gap is under the threshold, the keys are a scan; when it's over, they're typing. The **Scanning** section sets where that line falls.

- **Barcode Average Time Input Threshold (ms)** — starts at **24**. Keys whose average gap is under this many milliseconds count as a scan. Raise it for a slow scanner; lower it if fast typing is taken for a scan.
- **Barcode Minimum Length** — starts at **8**. The shortest code that counts as a scan. Set it to your shortest barcode; 8 fits an EAN-8.
- **Barcode Scanner Prefix** — starts empty. One character your scanner adds before every code. The POS removes it before it looks the code up.
- **Barcode Scanner Suffix** — starts empty. One character your scanner adds after every code, removed the same way.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Remove an extra character</summary>

Leave **Barcode Scanner Prefix** and **Barcode Scanner Suffix** empty unless your scanner is set to add a character. If it puts `A` before every code and `Z` after, type `A` as the prefix and `Z` as the suffix: the scan `A4006381333931Z` is looked up as `4006381333931`. The POS removes the character only at that end of the code, never from the middle. A trailing Enter needs no suffix: the POS ignores it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>When a barcode is too short</summary>

A scan shorter than **Barcode Minimum Length** is never looked up. The POS shows **Barcode scanned:** with the code, and **Barcode must be at least 8 characters long** (or whatever your minimum is). From a keyboard-mode scanner, it says so only when the keys look like a scan, all digits or ending with Enter, and at most once every 30 seconds. A short burst of letters is taken for fast typing and left alone.

</details>

## Choose a scan sound {#sound}

Scan sounds let you hear whether a product went into the cart or the scan needs attention, while you keep your eyes on the customer. To choose one:

1. Switch on **Play a sound on scan**, under **Sound**. The rest of the sound settings open below it.
2. Press a theme's name to choose it. Press its **▶** and **▶✕** to hear its success and failure sounds.
3. Drag **Volume**, and press the **▶** beside it to hear the result.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Play a sound on scan** — turns scan sounds on and shows the options below it.
2. **Sound theme** — **Classic**, **Checkout** or **Soft**. Press a theme's name to choose it.
3. **▶** — plays that theme's success sound, the one for a product going into the cart.
4. **▶✕** — plays its failure sound.
5. **Volume** — drag the slider. It starts towards the quiet end.
6. **▶** beside **Volume** — plays the chosen theme's success sound at that volume.
7. **Success sound** — on to start. Turn it off to hear only the failures.
8. **Failure sound** — on to start. Turn it off to hear only the successes.

</div>

<details>
<summary>Compare sound themes</summary>

Each theme has a success sound and a failure sound. The app describes them like this:

| Theme | Success / failure |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | Bright rising blip / low buzz. The theme you start with. |
| **Checkout** | Supermarket-style flat beep / double low tone |
| **Soft** | Gentle chime / muted knock, for quiet shops |

The previews play at the volume you've set, so press them to pick a theme and a volume together.

</details>

**When each sound plays.** The success sound plays when a scanned product goes into the cart. The failure sound plays for everything else the POS can say after a scan: **Barcode not found**, **Several matches**, **Choose a variation**, out of stock, and the rest in [What the screen says](../../pos/product-panel/barcode-scanning.mdx#messages). **Searching store…** is silent, because the result is still to come.

<details>
<summary>Vibration on iPhone, iPad and Android</summary>

In the iPhone, iPad and Android apps there's one more switch, **Failure vibration**, on to start. The device vibrates on a failed scan, with the failure sound or on its own if **Failure sound** is off. It needs **Play a sound on scan** on. The ▶✕ preview plays the sound only, without the vibration. A browser and the desktop app don't have it.

</details>

:::tip A quiet counter
For a gentle sound, choose **Soft** and turn the volume down. For silence, in the iPhone, iPad or Android app, keep **Play a sound on scan** and **Failure vibration** on, and turn **Success sound** and **Failure sound** off. Then only a failed scan makes itself felt, as a vibration.
:::

## Connect a scanner {#scanner}

The **Scanner** section is for a scanner the POS knows by the device rather than by its speed. Almost every scanner pairs as a Bluetooth or USB keyboard and needs nothing here: pair it in your device's settings and scan.

- **Scanner setup guide** opens the [Scanner Setup Wizard](../../hardware/scanners/setup-wizard.mdx), which asks a few questions and walks you through setup or a fix.
- **Connect a scanner directly (advanced)** opens the sheet for a USB or Bluetooth scanner out of keyboard mode. On Android the row reads **Register a scanner** instead. See [Connect a scanner directly](../../hardware/scanners/index.mdx#direct).

In Safari and Firefox there's no **Scanner** section: those browsers can't connect a scanner directly.

## Test your scanner {#test}

The **Barcode Scanning Test** at the foot of the screen shows what the POS made of each scan, judged against the settings above.

1. Get ready to scan. In a browser or the desktop app, click outside any text field. In the iPhone, iPad or Android app, tap **Keypress Event**.
2. Scan any barcode.
3. Read the verdict under the chart: **Detected as a scan**, or why it was treated as typing.
4. If it offers a fix, press **Apply**.
5. Scan again to check.

Here the same barcode was scanned twice: once slowly, so it was treated as typing, and once at scanner speed.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Keypress Event** — every key that arrived, including an Enter at the end.
2. **Detected Barcode** — the code the POS will look up, after the prefix and suffix are removed.
3. **Timing chart** — one bar per key, against a dashed line at your threshold. A bar over the line, in amber, is a key that came too slowly.
4. **Verdict** — **Detected as a scan**, or why it was treated as typing. When a setting is in the way, it offers the fix with an **Apply** button that saves it.
5. **Recent attempts** — your last few tries, each marked **scan**, **typing** or **too short**, with its average gap between keys.
6. **Troubleshooting guide** — opens the [Scanner Setup Wizard](../../hardware/scanners/setup-wizard.mdx) for a fix by symptom.

</div>

The fixes the test offers are **Raise threshold** for a scanner a little too slow, **Lower minimum length** for a code under your minimum, and stripping a leading character as your prefix. For a walk through every verdict, see [Check a scan in the test panel](../../hardware/scanners/setup-wizard.mdx#test-panel).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Nothing shows in the test.** First check where you're scanning. In a browser or the desktop app, a scan into a text field types into that field, so click outside it before you scan; in the iPhone, iPad or Android app, tap **Keypress Event** first. If the test still stays empty, check that the scanner is paired or plugged in: see [Connect a scanner](#scanner).

**The verdict says "Treated as typing".** The scanner is slower than your threshold. Press **Apply** on the suggestion, or raise **Barcode Average Time Input Threshold (ms)** and scan again.

**Typing in the product search is taken as a scan.** This happens in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, where the POS judges search typing by its speed too. Lower the threshold, or raise **Barcode Minimum Length**. In a browser or the desktop app, typing into a field never counts as a scan.

**The code has an extra character at the start or end.** Your scanner adds it. Type that character as the **Barcode Scanner Prefix** or **Barcode Scanner Suffix**. An Enter at the end needs nothing.

**"Barcode must be at least 8 characters long".** The code is shorter than **Barcode Minimum Length**. Lower it to the length of your shortest barcode.

**No sound on a scan.** Check that **Play a sound on scan** is on, that **Success sound** or **Failure sound** is on for the one you expect, and that the device isn't muted. In a browser, sound starts only once you've clicked or tapped somewhere on the page, so the first scan after the page opens can be silent.

**The scan reaches the POS but no product comes up.** The POS says why. See [What the screen says](../../pos/product-panel/barcode-scanning.mdx#messages). Which product field holds the barcode is set in [Barcode Field](../wp-admin/general.mdx#barcode-field).
