Checkout Settings decide how a sale gets paid and what happens to the order afterwards. You set them once, as the store owner, in `WP Admin > POS > Settings > Checkout`. Cashiers never see this screen; they see its results on the till.

Changes on this screen save as you make them: a **Saving** and then **Saved** message shows, and there is no Save button. The one exception is a gateway's [POS settings](#gateway-pos-settings) dialog, where you press **Save**.

Tills read these settings when the POS starts, so reload the POS on each till after a change.

## The screen at a glance {#at-a-glance}

From top to bottom the screen has **Prevent overselling**, the **Admin emails**, **Customer emails** and **Cashier emails** switches, and the **Gateways** table. A store on WooCommerce's older order storage also sees a warning at the top; see [Order storage](#order-storage).

What you can do here:

- [Offer a payment method at the till](#enabling-a-gateway)
- [Choose which payment method comes first](#reordering-gateways)
- [Rename a tile, or round cash totals](#gateway-pos-settings)
- [Choose an order's status after checkout](#order-status)
- [Stop the till selling stock you don't have](#prevent-overselling) (off on a new store)
- [Choose which emails a POS order sends](#email-notifications)

## Payment Gateways {#payment-gateways}

The **Gateways** table lists every payment gateway installed on your site, plus the POS's own **Cash** and **Card**. Each row is one gateway: a drag handle, **Default**, the gateway's name and ID, an **Enabled** switch, its **Order Status**, and a **⋮** menu.

<details>
<summary>Gateway table columns</summary>

| Column | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| Drag handle | Drag the row up or down to move that tile on the till. See [Choose which payment method comes first](#reordering-gateways). |
| **Default** | The gateway already chosen on your store's own payment page, which the [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx) shows. Only a gateway that is switched on can be picked. The tiles on the Payments tab don't use it; see [Choose which payment method comes first](#reordering-gateways). |
| **Gateway** | The gateway's name. |
| **Gateway ID** | WooCommerce's identifier for it, such as `pos_cash`. You need it only when a developer asks. |
| **Enabled** | Offer it at the till, or not. |
| **Order Status** | The status a sale paid this way is given. See [Order Status](#order-status). |
| **⋮** | **POS settings**, and for any gateway but Cash and Card, **WooCommerce settings**. See [Rename a tile, or round cash totals](#gateway-pos-settings). |

</details>

:::note Free vs Pro
Without [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx), only **Cash** and **Card** can be switched on. On every other row the **Enabled** switch, **Order Status** and **⋮** are greyed out, and a note above the table says so, with an **Upgrade to Pro** link.
:::

### Offer a payment method at the till {#enabling-a-gateway}

1. Install and activate the gateway's plugin, if it isn't one of the POS's own.
2. Find the gateway in the **Gateways** table.
3. Switch **Enabled** on.
4. Reload the POS. The till reads your payment methods each time it starts.

The gateway is now a tile at checkout, or a gateway on the [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx) if it runs on its own payment page.

### Choose which payment method comes first {#reordering-gateways}

Drag a row by its handle to move it. The tiles at the till follow this order, and when checkout opens on a new sale, the first tile that can be used is already picked. To have Cash picked for most sales, drag Cash to the top. The **Default** column doesn't change which tile is picked; only the order does.

This is where your settings show up at the till, in the tender pane:

### Rename a tile, or round cash totals {#gateway-pos-settings}

**⋮ > POS settings** opens a dialog for that gateway. It changes what the till shows, and nothing on your website:

- **Title** — the name on the tile.
- **Description** — saved with the gateway. The till doesn't show it; only the **Title** appears on the tile.
- **Rounding** (Cash only) — round cash totals to the coins you have: **0.05**, **0.10**, **0.25**, **0.50**, **1**, **5** or **10**. **No rounding** is the default. Card and online payments keep the exact amount. See [Cash rounding](../../payment/index.mdx#cash-rounding).

Press **Save** to keep the change, or **Cancel**.

**⋮ > WooCommerce settings** opens the gateway's own settings in WooCommerce, where you connect the account and set what your website shows.

### POS vs Online Store {#pos-vs-online-store}

The **Enabled** switch here is separate from WooCommerce's own payment settings. A gateway can be:

- **On for the POS, off online** — cash, for example, which a website can't take.
- **On online, off for the POS** — a gateway that needs the customer at a computer, such as PayPal checkout.
- **On for both.**

:::note
A gateway's plugin must be installed and active before the gateway shows in the table. It does not have to be switched on in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Settings > Payments`: the POS lists every gateway WooCommerce knows about. If one is missing, check that its plugin is active.
:::

## Order Status {#order-status}

Each gateway has its own **Order Status**: the status an order gets after checkout. To change it, find the payment method in the **Gateways** table and pick a status in its **Order Status** column; it saves straight away. Choose it by how the money arrives:

- **Money taken at the counter:** choose **Processing** or **Completed**.
- **Money the customer will transfer on their own**, without a pay link: choose **On hold**, the default for BACS and Cheque.
- **Pay later through the order's pay link**, a "pay by invoice" workflow, for example with BACS: choose **Pending payment**. The order stays unpaid until the customer pays through the link.

<details>
<summary>Send a payment link</summary>

WooCommerce keeps a Pending payment order payable: it is not marked paid, stock is not reduced, and the pay link works while the order stays Pending payment. The POS does not send the link. The order needs a customer email, and you email the invoice from the order screen in WooCommerce or share the link another way.

</details>

**Cash**, **Card** and most gateways start on **Completed**, because the money is taken at the counter. **BACS** (direct bank transfer) and **Cheque** start on **On hold**, because the money arrives later. Any WooCommerce order status can be picked. If an older version of the POS set one status for every gateway, each gateway starts with that status instead.

### How the till treats the status {#order-status-at-the-till}

Finishing checkout and receiving the money are two different things:

- **Checkout finishes** at the till when the payments the cashier records cover the total, for Cash, Card, card readers and tiles such as Direct bank transfer. The order is then given the status you chose for the gateway. A split sale takes the status of the gateway with the largest payment, or the earliest of them on a tie. For a gateway on the Legacy tab, the POS finishes the sale once the order reaches that status.
- **The money counts as received** in WooCommerce only when the order is **Processing** or **Completed**. An order left **On hold** or **Pending payment** has finished checkout but is not yet paid.

<details>
<summary>The Email Invoice gateway and Pending payment</summary>

The **Email Invoice Gateway** sends the invoice and sets **Pending payment** itself, so leave its **Order Status** on anything else. Set to **Pending payment**, the till shows no **Invoice sent** screen, and the order gets no **Send again** or **Cancel invoice**. See [Email Invoice](../../payment/gateways/email-invoice.mdx#set-up).

</details>

## Prevent Overselling {#prevent-overselling}

**Prevent overselling** stops the POS selling more of a product than you have. It is **off** on a new store; until you switch it on, the till sells whatever is in the cart. After switching it on or off, reload the POS on each till. Products that allow backorders still sell; see [Backorders still win](#prevent-overselling-backorders).

With the switch on, the till won't add more of a product than you have, checkout is blocked while a cart line is short, and your store checks stock again before it takes each payment. Saving a sale that is still open is never refused.

<details>
<summary>The three stock checks, and what the cashier sees</summary>

1. **When a product goes in the cart.** The cashier can't add a product, or raise its quantity, past what you have. The till says **Only 3 available for Hoodie**, or **Hoodie out of stock**, and leaves the cart as it was. See [Out-of-stock products](../../pos/product-panel/display-settings.mdx#show-out-of-stock-products) for every message.
2. **While the sale is in the cart.** If stock drops while a sale is open, for example because another till sold the last one, a notice above the cart lines lists each short line with **Reduce** or **Remove**, the line shows how many are available, and **Checkout** dims until the cart is fixed. A payment in the tender pane is refused the same way. See [When checkout is blocked](../../pos/cart/line-items.mdx#checkout-blocked).
3. **When a payment is taken, on your store.** Before any money moves, and again when the sale closes, your store checks the order's stock. If the till's figures were out of date, the store refuses the payment with **Cannot complete order: 1 item(s) exceed available stock.**, and the POS shows that reason. On the [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx), the reason shows on the store's payment page. A sale sent later from an offline till can be refused the same way; see [Order not saved — the store refused it](../../support/store-health.mdx#order-refused).

Your store doesn't check a sale that is only being saved, so an open sale syncs freely.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why your store checks as well as the till</summary>

WooCommerce doesn't check stock when an order arrives through its REST API, which is how the POS sends orders. Two busy tills, or a till whose stock figures are out of date, could sell more than you have. The check on your store catches what the till couldn't see.

</details>

### How stock is decided {#prevent-overselling-stock-rules}

Whether stock is tracked on the product, or on each variation, decides the limit.

<details>
<summary>Stock rules by product setup</summary>

| Product setup | With the switch on |
|---|---|
| **Manages stock** (a quantity is tracked) | Sales stop at the stock quantity. Several lines of the same product count toward the same limit. |
| **Variation tracks its own stock** | The limit is per variation. |
| **Variation uses the parent's stock** | All of the product's variations share the parent's quantity. |
| **Doesn't manage stock** | The stock status decides: **Out of stock** can't be sold; **In stock** and **On backorder** can. |

</details>

### Backorders still win {#prevent-overselling-backorders}

A product's own **Allow backorders?** setting in WooCommerce comes first. Use it to let some products sell past zero while the switch is on:

- **Do not allow** — sales stop when stock runs out.
- **Allow** — the product always sells, even below zero.
- **Allow, but notify customer** — the product always sells. When a sale goes past the stock, the till says **Hoodie will be backordered**.

A product on backorder never blocks checkout.

### Prevent Overselling vs. "Show out-of-stock products" {#prevent-overselling-vs-display}

These two settings do different jobs:

- **Prevent overselling** (this page) checks quantities: as products go in the cart, while the sale is open, and again when it's paid. It applies to every till in the store.
- **Show out-of-stock products** is a setting on each device, in the product panel. Off, products with none left are hidden and a scan of one is refused. On, they stay in the list, greyed out and marked **Sold out**. See [Out-of-stock products](../../pos/product-panel/display-settings.mdx#show-out-of-stock-products).

Turning Prevent overselling off does not change what the product panel shows.

:::note
Tills get Prevent overselling with your store's details when the POS starts. Your store's own check when an order is paid applies straight away, even on a till that hasn't been reloaded.
:::

## Email Notifications {#email-notifications}

Three switches choose which WooCommerce emails POS orders send, separately from your website's own email settings:

- **Admin emails** — **New order**, **Cancelled order** and **Failed order** to the recipients set in WooCommerce. On.
- **Customer emails** — **Order on hold**, **Processing order**, **Completed order**, **Refunded order** and **Failed order** to the customer. On.
- **Cashier emails** — **New order** to the cashier who made the sale. Off.

Switch a group on and its emails show as tick boxes, all ticked at first. Untick any you don't want. See [Email Notifications](./email-notifications.mdx) for when each email goes, who receives it, and common setups.

## Order storage {#order-storage}

On a store that still uses WooCommerce's older order storage, a warning sits at the top of the screen: **Checkout in WCPOS gets slower as your catalog and order history grow, because this store uses legacy order storage.** It has a link to WooCommerce's order storage setting and to **How to switch**. On a store that already uses High-Performance Order Storage, there's no warning. See [Order Storage](../../support/performance/order-storage.mdx).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**A gateway isn't in the table.** Its plugin isn't installed or active. Activate it, then reload this screen.

**The Enabled switch is greyed out.** Your store runs the free version, which offers only Cash and Card. See [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).

**A gateway is switched on but isn't at checkout.** Reload the POS. Then look on the [Legacy tab](../../pos/checkout/legacy-tab.mdx): gateways that run on their own payment page are there, not in the tiles.

**The wrong tile is picked when checkout opens.** The till picks the first tile it can use, in the table's order. Drag the gateway you want to the top. The **Default** column doesn't change it.

**Orders paid by bank transfer show as On hold.** That's the default for BACS and Cheque, because the money arrives later. When a transfer arrives, change that order's status in `WP Admin > WooCommerce > Orders`. Change the gateway's **Order Status** here only if the money will already be in for every future sale paid that way: the setting applies to new sales, not to orders already taken.

**The till sold more than you had.** **Prevent overselling** is off, or the product allows backorders. See [Backorders still win](#prevent-overselling-backorders).
