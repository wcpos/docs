Each time someone signs in to the POS, the store keeps a session for that device: one per sign-in in the desktop app, and usually one per browser for the POS in a browser. **Sessions** lists them person by person, so you can sign out a lost tablet or a cashier who has left without touching the device.

Open it at **WP Admin > POS > Settings > Sessions**. Administrators and Shop Managers can use it. A Shop Manager sees everyone's sessions except an administrator's.

## Find a person's devices {#find}

The screen has two columns.

- **People on the left.** Everyone with at least one session you may manage. You're at the top with a **You** chip, then the rest by most recent activity. The number on each row is how many sessions that person has, and a green dot means **Active now**. Type in **Filter users** to search by name or username.
- **Their sessions on the right.** Select a person to see their name, their username and how many sessions they have, the **Logout All** button, and one card per device, the most recent first.

Each card shows:

| On the card | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Application** | The kind of app the person signed in from, with its version: **Desktop Application** or **Web Application**. Below it, the browser and the operating system. |
| **Last active** | When the device last signed in or renewed its sign-in, as _Just now_, _5 minutes ago_ or _3 hours ago_. A POS in use renews about every 30 minutes. |
| **IP** | The internet address the device signed in from, or _N/A_. |
| **Created** | When the person signed in on that device. |
| **Expires** | When the sign-in runs out and the person has to sign in again: 30 days after **Created**. Using the POS doesn't push it back. |
| **User Agent** | Press it to see the raw text the device sent. Support may ask for it. |

**Active now** means the person signed in or renewed a sign-in in the last 5 minutes. A till that is in use but between renewals doesn't show it, so read **Last active** before you decide a device is idle.

## Sign a device out {#signing-devices-out}

1. Select the person on the left.
2. Find the device by its **Application**, **IP** and **Created** time.
3. Press **Terminate** on its card. **Terminate session?** asks you to confirm. Press **Terminate**.

To sign the person out everywhere at once, press **Logout All** instead. **Terminate all sessions?** asks you to confirm. Press **Logout All**.

**Session terminated successfully** or **Sessions terminated successfully** confirms it, and the session leaves the list.

### What happens on the device {#on-the-device}

The store stops answering that device straight away. The next time the POS reaches the store, it opens the WordPress sign-in window, and the POS shows **Session expired**: the same as when a sign-in runs out. See [AUTH101](../../error-codes/AUTH101.mdx).

Nothing on the device is wiped. Sales that haven't reached the store wait on the device and send once someone signs in again. On the Connect screen the person's row shows **Sign in again**. See [Connect to your store](../../getting-started/connect.mdx#multiple-users-per-store).

Ending your own session from here signs your POS out, not WP Admin.

:::warning Ending a session doesn't change the password
Anyone who knows the password can sign straight back in. For a cashier who has left, also remove their POS access in [Access](./access.mdx) or change their password.
:::

:::tip Lost or stolen device
Change the password of each person who was signed in on it. That ends every POS session they have, on every device, and stops anyone signing back in with the old one. They sign in again on their other devices. If you only need to end that one device, **Terminate** its session here.
:::

## Other ways a session ends {#other-ways}

You don't have to clear sessions by hand. A session also ends when:

- it reaches **Expires**, 30 days after the sign-in;
- the person's password is changed or reset: every POS session they have ends;
- the person signs out of WordPress in a browser: the POS session in that browser ends, and their other devices stay signed in.

<details>
<summary>For developers: session length and the session limit</summary>

The 30 days come from the `wcpos_jwt_refresh_token_expire` filter, and the 30-minute renewal from `wcpos_jwt_access_token_expire`. See [Token Expiry Coverage](../../reference/role-endpoint-access.mdx#token-expiry-coverage).

A person can hold up to 200 sessions. When a new sign-in would go over that, the store removes the sessions that have been idle longest, and only ones not seen for a week. A device in use is never removed this way.

</details>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**No active sessions found.** Nobody you can manage is signed in to the POS.

**A person you expect isn't listed.** They have no session that hasn't expired, or they are an administrator and you're signed in as a Shop Manager.

**The device signed straight back in.** Someone there still knows the password. Change it, or remove the person's POS access in [Access](./access.mdx).
