General is the first screen of the POS settings in WordPress: **`WP Admin > POS > Settings > General`**. It holds the store-wide choices every cashier's POS works with. Each change saves as soon as you make it, and a **Saved** message confirms it. There's no Save button.

:::note Who can change these
A user with **Manage POS settings** (an Administrator or Shop Manager by default) can open this screen. A cashier can't. See [Accessing the POS](./access.mdx).
:::

## Where these settings show up in the POS {#in-the-pos}

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} alt="The POS with Aim Analog Watch in the cart: the register bar, the product search field, the product tile, the cart's customer and the line's quantity" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Register bar** — the store you're on. With **Open and close the register** on, it also shows the register's status, such as **Closed** or **Overdue**. See [Register](#register).
2. **Search** — scans find products by the field you chose in **Barcode Field**. See [Barcode Field](#barcode-field).
3. **Products** — with **Enable POS only products** on, a product set to *Online Only* doesn't show here. See [Enable POS only products](#enable-pos-only-products).
4. **Customer** — every new cart starts with the **Default POS customer**, here **Guest**. Tap it to choose someone else; see [Choose the customer](../../pos/cart/index.mdx#customer-selector).
5. **Quantity** — the line's quantity, here **1**. It takes a fraction such as 0.5 only while **Enable decimal quantities** is on. See [Enable decimal quantities](#enable-decimal-quantities).

</div>

The screen runs from top to bottom in the order below: **Store details**, **Register**, **Products**, **Customers**, **Privacy** and **Advanced**.

## Store details {#store-details}

Who your store is on POS receipts and invoices: **Store name**, **Phone**, **Email** and **Refund & Returns Policy**, a short statement printed on POS receipts.

Leave a field blank to use the default shown in grey inside it:

| Field | Default when left blank |
|---|---|
| **Store name** | Your WordPress site title |
| **Phone** | None |
| **Email** | The "From" address of your WooCommerce emails, then the WordPress admin email |
| **Refund & Returns Policy** | None |

**Store tax IDs** sit below these fields: the VAT number, ABN, GSTIN or other business numbers that print on receipts and invoices. See [Store Tax IDs](./store-tax-ids.mdx).

:::info With WCPOS Pro
Once you've created stores, this block becomes a **Stores** list, and each store keeps its own details, receipt template, prices, tax rates and users. See [Setting up stores](../../stores/setup.mdx).
:::

## Register {#register}

**Open and close the register** turns register sessions on: cashiers open with a float, record cash in and out, and count the drawer at close. Off, the POS sells without a session.

While it's on, three more lines appear: **Opening float**, **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than …** and **Remind the cashier to close at …**. [Register settings](./registers.mdx#one-register) explains each one. The free plugin has one register; **More registers and stores with Pro** links to the upgrade.

## Products {#products}

### Enable POS only products {#enable-pos-only-products}

Adds a visibility choice to every product in WP Admin: **POS & Online**, **POS Only** or **Online Only**. A *POS Only* product is hidden from your online store, and an *Online Only* product is hidden from the POS.

Leave it off if every product sells in both places. Switching it off hides nothing, and each product keeps its choice for when you switch it back on. See [POS Only Products](../../products/pos-only-products.mdx) to set visibility on one product or many.

### Enable decimal quantities {#enable-decimal-quantities}

Lets a line's quantity be a fraction, such as `0.25`, `1.5` or `2.75`. Use it for things sold by weight, length or time: 0.5 kg of coffee beans, 1.5 hours of labour.

With it on, the quantity keypad in the cart gets a decimal point. With it off, there's no decimal point, and your store refuses an order that has a fractional quantity.

:::note Selling by weight
WCPOS doesn't connect to a weighing scale or read weights from one. Weigh the item, then type the weight as the line's quantity: for a product priced per kilogram, 350 g is a quantity of `0.35`.

WCPOS also doesn't read the weight or price encoded in the barcode labels that some scales print.
:::

### Restore stock on order delete {#restore-stock-on-order-delete}

On by default. When an order is moved to the trash or deleted from the POS, the stock of its products goes back up, so removing a mistaken sale puts the items back on the shelf.

:::warning Only for orders deleted from the POS
Orders deleted in WP Admin don't get their stock back. Restoring an order from the trash doesn't take the stock back out.
:::

Turn it off if you count stock another way and don't want deleted orders to change it.

### Barcode Field {#barcode-field}

Choose where your product barcode numbers are saved. When a cashier scans a barcode or types one into **Search products**, the POS finds the product whose barcode is in that place.

Pick that place from the **Barcode Field** list. Most stores use one of two:

<a id="default-option"></a>

- **WooCommerce's barcode field**, the default. It's the **GTIN, UPC, EAN or ISBN** box on the product's Inventory tab, and the one to use on a new store. The list shows it as `_global_unique_id`.
- **The product's SKU**, if your barcodes are your SKUs. The list shows it as `_sku`.

If a barcode plugin saves your codes, choose that plugin's field instead. For a field that isn't in the list, you need its exact stored name (the meta key), not the name the plugin shows on screen: find it in the plugin's instructions or in **Plugin and custom barcode fields** below. Type that name and choose **Create "…"**.

<a id="other-common-options"></a>
<a id="third-party-plugin-meta-keys"></a>

<details>
<summary>Plugin and custom barcode fields</summary>

#### Common plugins → meta key {#common-plugins-meta-key}

If a barcode plugin stores your codes, use its meta key instead:

| Plugin or field | Meta key |
|---|---|
| WooCommerce GTIN, UPC, EAN or ISBN *(default)* | `_global_unique_id` |
| Product SKU | `_sku` |
| EAN plugins (European Article Number) | `_ean`, `_ean13`, `_ean8` |
| UPC plugins (Universal Product Code) | `_upc`, `_upc_code` |
| GTIN plugins (Global Trade Item Number) | `_gtin`, `_gtin13`, `_gtin14` |
| Generic barcode fields | `_barcode`, `_product_barcode` |
| WooCommerce Product Barcodes | `_barcode_text` |
| ATUM Inventory | `_atum_barcode` |

<a id="custom-meta-keys"></a>

The **Barcode Field** list offers every meta key your products and variations already use, plus `_sku` and `_global_unique_id`. For any other meta key that holds the barcode, type its name exactly, including a leading underscore if it has one, and choose **Create "…"**.

#### Finding your plugin's meta key {#finding-your-plugins-meta-key}

1. Edit a product in WP Admin and give it a barcode with your plugin.
2. Look up the meta key name in the plugin's documentation.
3. Or look in the `wp_postmeta` table of your database for the key that was created for that product.

</details>

When you change the field, the POS re-downloads your products on its next sync so scans match the new field. You don't need to clear anything by hand.

## Customers {#customers}

### Automatically generate username from customer email {#automatically-generate-username-from-customer-email}

On by default. When a cashier adds a customer in the POS, WooCommerce makes the username from the customer's email address. Off, the cashier types a username in the customer form. The POS setting applies only to customers added in the POS; your online checkout keeps its own WooCommerce setting.

### Default POS customer {#default-pos-customer}

The customer every new cart starts with, and the details it fills in. **Guest** means the order belongs to no account. Search the dropdown to pick any WordPress user instead, for example a "Walk-in customer" account you made for counter sales. If the dropdown is greyed out, turn off [Use cashier account](#use-cashier-account) below.

The cashier can always change the customer in the cart. See [Choose the customer](../../pos/cart/index.mdx#customer-selector).

### Use cashier account {#use-cashier-account}

Tick it to start every new cart with the cashier who is signed in as the customer. While it's ticked, **Default POS customer** is greyed out.

### Let customers download receipts {#storefront-receipts}

Off by default. On, your online store's **My Account > Orders** gets a **Receipt** button, so customers can download their own receipt as a PDF made with your WCPOS receipt template. It shows on orders that are processing, completed or refunded.

Turning it on shows **Receipt template for customers**. It starts on **Use active receipt template**, so customers get the same receipt as the POS; pick another template to give them a different one, such as a full-page invoice. See [Online Store Receipts](../../receipts/storefront.mdx).

### Tax IDs {#customer-tax-ids}

WCPOS keeps a tax-ID field on your customers and copies it onto their orders at checkout. Plugins store that field under different names, so WCPOS finds the plugin you have and writes to its field: the section says **Auto-detected** or **No plugin detected**. **Advanced: use a different field name** lets you set the name yourself. See [Customer Tax IDs](./customer-tax-ids.mdx).

## Privacy {#privacy}

**Allow anonymous usage data and error reports** lets WCPOS learn about your setup and what goes wrong, to find bugs. Until you choose, the section asks you with **Allow** and **No thanks**. You can change your answer at any time.

**Learn what's collected** opens the full list on the screen.

<details>
<summary>What's collected</summary>

- **Collected:** your setup (PHP and WooCommerce versions, country, currency), your store's size (roughly how many products, orders and POS users), which screens and features you use, and errors with the versions involved.
- **Never collected:** customer details, order contents, prices, your site address, or anything that could identify you or your customers.

</details>

## Advanced: Force SSL {#force-ssl}

On by default. It keeps the POS on a secure connection, so leave it on.

<details>
<summary>What Force SSL does</summary>

The POS opens over HTTPS: someone who goes to your POS page over plain `http://` is sent to `https://`, and the POS links WCPOS builds use `https://`. Turn it off only if your site has no SSL certificate.

</details>

## If something goes wrong {#if-something-goes-wrong}

**"Your store is behind Cloudflare"** at the top of the screen
: Your site runs through Cloudflare, and its security checks can stop the POS reaching your store. If the POS shows HOST121 or a security check, follow **How to add the rule**. See [Cloudflare](../../support/troubleshooting/cloudflare.mdx).

**Scans don't find products after you change Barcode Field**
: The POS re-downloads products on its next sync. To do it now, press and hold the sync arrow beside the product count in the POS and choose **Clear and Refresh**. See [Refresh, or start over](../../products/sync.mdx#refresh).

**An order with a quantity like 0.5 won't save**
: Turn on [Enable decimal quantities](#enable-decimal-quantities).
