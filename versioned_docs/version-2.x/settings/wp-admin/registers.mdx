The **Registers** screen holds the register your cashiers open with a float, count and close. You'll find it at **`WP Admin > POS > Settings > Registers`**.

For what a register is, and how it differs from a store and a device, see [Stores, Registers and Devices](/stores/registers-and-devices).

## One register in the free plugin {#one-register}

The free plugin has exactly one register. The screen shows it as a single card:

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What the register is called on receipts and closures. A new site starts with *Register*; change it to whatever your staff call the till. |
| **Default float** | The cash the drawer starts the day with. It pre-fills the amount when a cashier opens the register. Leave it blank to pre-fill the cash counted at the last close instead. |

In place of an **Add register** button, the screen says *One register per site. Two devices share it; more registers need WCPOS Pro.* Every device that signs in uses this register. A browser, the desktop app and a phone on the same counter all sell into the same drawer and share one register session, so the drawer is counted and closed once.

:::note Not the Sessions screen
[Sessions](./sessions.mdx) lists who is signed in to the POS and on which devices. A *register session* is something else: the time between opening the register with a float and closing it with a count.
:::

## More registers with Pro {#more-registers-with-pro}

A second cash drawer needs a second register, and that is a [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/free-vs-pro) feature. With Pro, registers belong to stores and are added in each store's editor (**POS → Stores**, open the store, **Registers**, **Add register**):

- The first store you create takes over this register as it is; no new register is made for it.
- Every store after that gets a register of its own.
- A register belongs to one store.

See [Setting Up Stores](/stores/setup).

### If Pro is deactivated {#if-pro-is-deactivated}

The free plugin goes back to its one register. A session that is still open on any other register can be counted and closed, but those registers cannot be opened again until Pro is back.
