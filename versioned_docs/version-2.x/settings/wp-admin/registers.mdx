The register your cashiers open with a float, count and close is set up in **POS → Settings → General**, under **Register**. This page covers each setting and what the cashier sees because of it.

For what a register is, and how it differs from a store and a device, see [Stores, Registers and Devices](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx).

## Turn the register on {#one-register}

1. In WordPress, go to **POS → Settings → General** and scroll to **Register**.
2. Turn on **Open and close the register**. The rules below it appear.
3. Check the **Opening float**. The first time you turn the register on, it is set to **200.00**. Change it to what your drawer starts the day with.
4. Optional: turn on **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than** and set the amount.
5. Optional: turn on **Remind the cashier to close at** and set the time.

Each change saves as soon as you make it or leave the field. A field you leave empty goes back to its last value.

Each setting is a sentence with its value inside it:

- **Open and close the register** turns register sessions on. Cashiers open with a float, record cash in and out, and count the drawer at close. Off, the POS goes straight to the cart and there is nothing to open or count.
- **Opening float** is the cash the drawer starts with. It fills in **Cash in the drawer** when a cashier opens the register, and the cashier can type what is really there. It shows only while the register is on.
- **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than …** is off by default; on, it starts at **5.00**. A closing count that is short or over by more than this needs a manager's approval before the register closes.
- **Remind the cashier to close at …** is off by default; on, it starts at **17:30**. After this time the register shows **Overdue**, and an empty cart offers **Close register** instead of **Checkout**. See [At closing time](#overdue).

Turning an optional rule on fills in its starting value; turning it off clears it, and turning it on again starts from that value again. Under the settings, a link reads **More registers and stores with Pro**.

The free plugin has exactly one register. Every device that signs in uses it: a browser, the desktop app and a phone on the same counter all sell into the same drawer and share one register session, so the drawer is counted and closed once. There is no setting to name it, and the register bar shows only the store.

## What the cashier sees {#what-the-cashier-sees}

When the register is closed, the cashier counts the drawer and opens it from the cart column.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Register status** — **Closed** here. It can also show **Overdue** (see [At closing time](#overdue)) or **Approval needed** (see [When the count is out](#count-out)).
2. **Register bar** — the store this device sells for. With Pro and more than one register in a store, it names the register too.
3. **Price check band** — while the register is closed, cashiers can search and read prices, but nothing goes in a cart.
4. **Cash in the drawer** — starts at your **Opening float**. The cashier types what they really counted.
5. **Last count** — a chip with the count from the last closure, one tap to use it. Cashiers who count blind don't see it. See [Access](./access.mdx).
6. **Open register** — starts the session. A figure different from the float shows as an **Opening variance**.

</div>

The whole session, from float to closure, is walked in [Open the Register](../../pos/register/index.mdx).

### At closing time {#overdue}

**Remind the cashier to close at** doesn't close anything and doesn't stop sales. Once the time passes, on the store's clock, the POS reminds whoever is at the till:

- The register bar shows **Overdue**.
- With the cart empty, **Close register** takes the place of **Checkout**. A cart with items in it still checks out as usual.
- In the register panel, the button reads **Close register · it's past 17:30**, with your time.

A register opened after the time is due at the same time the next day. One left open overnight stays **Overdue** until someone closes it.

### When the count is out {#count-out}

At close, the cashier enters what they counted, float included, and the difference shows as they type.

<JourneyShot manifest={registerManifest} device="tablet" step={7} offsets={[[150, 0], [-90, 0], [-12, -24]]} alt="Closing the register: the cash counted field, the expected amount marked Exact, and the Close & print button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Cash counted, float included** — what the cashier counted.
2. **Expected** and the difference — **Exact** here. A count that is out shows the amount **short** or **over**; past your limit it adds *Over the … limit · a manager must approve*.
3. **Close & print** — closes the register. When the difference is more than your limit in **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than**, the button reads **Approve & close** and a manager signs in to approve. Approval needs a connection.

</div>

Who may approve, and who counts blind, is set in [Access](./access.mdx). Closures and approvals are kept in [Reconciliation](../../reports/reconciliation.mdx).

:::note Not the Sessions screen
[Sessions](./sessions.mdx) lists who is signed in to the POS and on which devices. A *register session* is something else: the time between opening the register with a float and closing it with a count.
:::

## More registers with Pro {#more-registers-with-pro}

A second cash drawer needs a second register, and that is a [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/free-vs-pro) feature. With Pro, registers belong to stores. The **Register** section in General settings changes once Pro is active:

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem question="Before your first store">

- **Open and close the register** and the two rules apply to the site's one register, as in the free plugin.
- **Opening float** stays here, for that one register.
- Under the settings, the section says *To run more than one register, create a store and add registers on its page.* with an **Add store** link.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem question="Once you have a store">

- **Open and close the register** and the two rules become **Site defaults**. The section says *Site defaults. Each store can customise these on its page.*
- **Opening float** is gone from this section. Each register's float is set on its store's **Registers** page.
- Under the settings, **Manage stores** opens the list of stores.

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

When you create stores:

- The first store you create takes over the register you already have, as it is; no new register is made for it.
- Every store after that gets a register of its own.
- A register belongs to one store at a time.

### A store's Registers page {#store-registers}

Open **POS → Stores**, click the store, and choose **Registers** in the store's menu. The **Tills** table lists the store's registers; **+ Add register** adds one, named **Register 2**, **Register 3** and so on, ready to rename. A new store needs **Create** first; until then the table says **Save the store first to add registers.**

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem question="The Tills table: columns, statuses and the ⋯ menu">

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Register** | The register's name, as the POS shows it. |
| **Opening float** | The cash a cashier starts with at this till. A change applies from the next opening. |
| **Status** | **Open since** a time **·** who opened it, with a green dot. **Counting**, with who is counting and an amber dot. **Closed · last closure #** its number and date, with a grey dot, or just **Closed** before the first closure. |
| **⋯** | **Rename**, **Move to** another store, or **Retire**. A register with a session open or counting can't be retired: the message says **Close its session first**. |

  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem question="Cash drawer rules for one store">

Under the table, **Cash drawer rules** holds the same three settings as General: **Open and close the register each day**, **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than** and **Remind the cashier to close at**. They start as the **Site defaults** from this page, and each says **Site default** while it follows them. Click **Customise** to change them for this store only. **Use site default** puts one rule back; **Reset to site defaults** puts back all three.

  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

The full walk, with the store's receipts and staff, is in [Setting Up Stores](../../stores/setup.mdx#registers).

### If Pro is deactivated {#if-pro-is-deactivated}

The free plugin goes back to its one register. A session that is still open on any other register can be counted and closed, but those registers cannot be opened again until Pro is back.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Register could not be saved.** The opening float didn't save. Check your connection and type it again.

**The Opening float field is greyed out.** It is loading or saving. Wait a moment; if it stays grey, reload the page.

**Cashiers never see Overdue.** Check that **Remind the cashier to close at** is on. With Pro, check the store's own **Cash drawer rules**: a store that customised them doesn't follow General. A register opened after the time is due the next day.

**Save the store first to add registers.** A new store has no **Tills** table until it exists. Click **Create**, then add registers.

**Close its session first.** The register you tried to retire is open or being counted. Count and close it in the POS, then retire it.
