The register your cashiers open with a float, count and close is set up in one place: **`WP Admin > POS > Settings > General`**, under **Register**.

For what a register is, and how it differs from a store and a device, see [Stores, Registers and Devices](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx).

## One register in the free plugin {#one-register}

The free plugin has exactly one register. Every device that signs in uses it: a browser, the desktop app and a phone on the same counter all sell into the same drawer and share one register session, so the drawer is counted and closed once. It has no name of its own; receipts and closures show the store.

The **Register** section of General settings is a few sentences with the value inside each one:

| Setting | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open and close the register** | The switch that turns register sessions on. Cashiers open with a float, record cash in and out, and count the drawer at close. Off, the POS sells without a session. |
| **Opening float** | The cash the drawer starts the day with. It pre-fills the amount when a cashier opens the register. Shown only while the switch is on. |
| **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than …** | Turn it on to require a manager's approval when the closing count differs from the expected cash by more than this amount. Off, any difference closes without approval. |
| **Remind the cashier to close at …** | Turn it on to remind whoever is signed in at this time of day that the register is still open. |

Turning an optional rule on fills in its default; turning it off clears it. A line under the fields says *More registers and stores with Pro*.

:::note Not the Sessions screen
[Sessions](./sessions.mdx) lists who is signed in to the POS and on which devices. A *register session* is something else: the time between opening the register with a float and closing it with a count.
:::

## More registers with Pro {#more-registers-with-pro}

A second cash drawer needs a second register, and that is a [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/free-vs-pro) feature. With Pro, registers belong to stores and are added in each store's editor (**POS → Stores**, open the store, **Registers**, **Add register**):

- The first store you create takes over this register as it is; no new register is made for it.
- Every store after that gets a register of its own.
- A register belongs to one store.

See [Setting Up Stores](/stores/setup).

### If Pro is deactivated {#if-pro-is-deactivated}

The free plugin goes back to its one register. A session that is still open on any other register can be counted and closed, but those registers cannot be opened again until Pro is back.
