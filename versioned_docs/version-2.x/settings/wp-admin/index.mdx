The settings that apply to the whole store, and to every till, are in WordPress: **`WP Admin > POS > Settings`**. You need the **Manage POS settings** permission to see them; Administrators and Shop Managers have it, Cashiers don't ([Access](./access.mdx)).

:::note Not the Settings screen in the POS
The **Settings** screen inside the POS (printer, barcode scanner, theme and display) is kept on each device and changes nothing on the server. This page is about the store-wide settings in WP Admin.
:::

## Find a setting {#settings-at-a-glance}

The menu down the left of **POS > Settings** has three groups. On General and Checkout each change saves as soon as you make it; only a gateway's POS settings have a **Save** button.

### Settings {#settings-group}

- **General**: store details, the register, products, customers, privacy and **Force SSL**. See [What's on General](#whats-on-general).
- **Checkout**: overselling, the POS emails, and the payment gateways with the order status each one sets. See [What's on Checkout](#whats-on-checkout).
- **Cloud Print**: printers that fetch receipts over the internet, the rules that print orders automatically, and the print queue. See [Cloud Printing](../../receipts/cloud-printing.mdx).
- **Access**: what each WordPress role can do in the POS, including the Cashier role. See [Access](./access.mdx).
- **Sessions**: who is signed in to the POS, on which devices, and signing a device out. See [Sessions](./sessions.mdx).
- **Extensions**: browse, install, update and turn off WCPOS extensions. A number beside the name counts the updates waiting. See [Extensions](../../extensions/index.mdx).

### Tools {#tools-group}

- **Export closures**: **Download CSV** of every register closure this store has recorded, one row per closure, with the figures as they were at closing. See [Reconciliation](../../reports/reconciliation.mdx).
- **Logs**: the store's own WCPOS log, with errors and warnings. A number beside the name counts the entries you haven't read. See [Logs](../../support/logs.mdx#wp-admin-logs).

### Account {#account-group}

- **License**: enter and **Activate** your WCPOS Pro **License Key**, or **Deactivate** it. See [Installing WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx#activate).

An extension can add a page of its own under **Tools** or **Account**.

### What's on General {#whats-on-general}

General has six sections, top to bottom:

- **Store details**: name, phone, email, returns policy and the store's **Tax IDs**. See [Store details](#store-details).
- **Register**: open and close the register, the opening float and the close rules. See [Register](./registers.mdx).
- **Products**: POS only products, decimal quantities, restore stock on order delete, and the **Barcode Field**. See [General Settings](./general.mdx).
- **Customers**: generated usernames, the **Default POS customer**, **Let customers download receipts**, and customer **Tax IDs**. See [General Settings](./general.mdx#default-pos-customer).
- **Privacy**: anonymous usage data. See [General Settings](./general.mdx#privacy).
- **Advanced**: **Force SSL**. See [Force SSL](#force-ssl).

### What's on Checkout {#whats-on-checkout}

Checkout has three parts, top to bottom:

- **Prevent overselling**. See [Checkout Settings](./checkout.mdx#prevent-overselling).
- The **Admin emails**, **Customer emails** and **Cashier emails** switches. See [Email Notifications](./email-notifications.mdx).
- The payment **Gateways** table: which gateways the till offers, in the order it shows them, and the **Order Status** each one gives a POS order. See [Checkout Settings](./checkout.mdx#payment-gateways). Each gateway's POS settings set the title and description the till shows and, for a cash gateway, **Rounding**: **No rounding** or an increment from **0.05** to **10**. See [Cash rounding](../../payment/index.mdx#cash-rounding).

<details>
<summary>Email notifications, registers and tax IDs</summary>

**Email Notifications** are the three email switches on **Checkout**. **Registers** is the **Register** section of **General**. **Store Tax IDs** are part of **Store details**, and **Customer Tax IDs** are under **Customers**, both on **General**.

</details>

## Store details {#store-details}

The top of **General** holds your shop's details, used on POS receipts and invoices:

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Store name** | Your shop's name. |
| **Phone** | The shop's phone number. |
| **Email** | The shop's email address. |
| **Refund & Returns Policy** | A short statement printed on POS receipts. |
| **Tax IDs** | The shop's tax numbers. See [Store Tax IDs](./store-tax-ids.mdx). |

Leave a field blank to keep the value WooCommerce already has, which the empty field shows in grey. If WooCommerce has none either, **Store name** falls back to the site title and **Email** to the sender address of your WooCommerce emails.

:::info With WCPOS Pro
Until you create a store, **Store details** shows these same fields and a **Manage stores** button. Once you have one, the section becomes **Stores**, a list of your stores with an **Edit** button each, and each store's details are set on the store. See [Set up a store](../../stores/setup.mdx#store-pages).
:::

## Force SSL {#force-ssl}

Leave **Force SSL** on. It is under **Advanced** at the bottom of **General**, it is on by default, and it keeps every connection between the POS and your site secure (HTTPS).

<details>
<summary>What Force SSL changes, and when to turn it off</summary>

With **Force SSL** on, opening the POS at an `http://` address sends you to the `https://` one, and the POS's sign-in, checkout and receipt links use HTTPS even when the site's own address starts with `http://`, as it can behind a proxy that handles SSL.

Turn it off only on a site that has no SSL certificate, such as a test site on your own computer. The POS then follows the site's own address.

</details>

## With WCPOS Pro {#pro}

Pro adds **POS > Stores** to the WP Admin menu. Each store has its own details, receipt templates, registers and staff, and its own **Customer display** page. There, the **Display template** list starts with **Same as site**, followed in brackets by the name of the site's display template (**Store default** when the site has none active): the store follows whatever template the site uses. Pick another template to give this store its own. A template appears in the list once it is enabled under **POS > Templates**, on the **Display** tab. See [Set up a store](../../stores/setup.mdx).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**There is no Settings item under POS.** Your account doesn't have the **Manage POS settings** permission. Ask an Administrator, or see [Access](./access.mdx).

**General shows Stores where Store details was.** You have WCPOS Pro and at least one store. Press **Edit** on a store to change its details.

**"WCPOS could not download its receipt fonts from cdn.jsdelivr.net."** This warning shows in WP Admin to anyone who manages WooCommerce. Until the download works, receipts use a basic font that only covers Latin letters, so text in other alphabets may not print properly. WCPOS tries again every hour. If your host blocks outgoing requests, ask them to allow `cdn.jsdelivr.net`.

**"WCPOS could not publish its receipt font map."** WCPOS could not save the file that tells receipts where the fonts are. Receipts use the basic Latin font until it works, and WCPOS tries again every hour. Ask your host to check that the WordPress uploads folder is writable.

**"Your store is behind Cloudflare" at the top of General.** WCPOS has noticed your site runs through Cloudflare, whose security checks can stop the POS reaching your store. If the POS shows **HOST121** or a security-check window, see [Cloudflare](../../support/troubleshooting/cloudflare.mdx).
