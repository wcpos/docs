The Customers screen lists everyone who can be the customer on a sale: your store's WordPress users. With WCPOS Pro you add, edit and delete them here, without going to WP Admin.

:::info Pro Feature
Adding, editing and deleting customers needs [WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx). Without it, the list sits blurred behind an upgrade card. You can still put an existing customer on a sale: see [Customers on a sale](#on-a-sale).
:::

## Customers on a sale {#on-a-sale}

Each sale starts with your store's default customer: whoever [Default POS Customer](../settings/wp-admin/general.mdx#default-pos-customer) names, or the cashier when [Use Cashier Account](../settings/wp-admin/general.mdx#use-cashier-account) is on. Tap the customer's name in the cart to choose someone else. See [Choose the customer](../pos/cart/index.mdx#customer-selector).

<JourneyShot manifest={cartManifest} device="tablet" step={2} alt="The cart's Select Customer search with Smith typed, the Guest option at the top and matching customers below" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Select Customer** — type part of a name, email or phone number.
2. **Guest** — a sale with no customer account.
3. **A customer** — tap to put them on the order, with their billing and shipping addresses.

</div>

## Find a customer {#find}

Type in **Search Customers**. Every word you type must match, in any order, across the name, email, username, company, phone and tax ID fields, so `jane acme` finds Jane whose company is Acme. See [What Search Matches](../reference/search.mdx#customers).

The list starts sorted by last name, A to Z. Tap a heading such as **First Name**, **Email** or **Date Created** to sort by it, and tap it again to reverse. The footer counts the customers listed against your total.

## Add a customer {#add-new-customer}

1. Press **Add new customer** beside the search. The **Add Customer** form opens empty.
2. Type the customer's **Email**. It's the only field you must fill.
3. Add the **Name**, **Phone** and anything else you have: open **Optional customer details** below.
4. Press **Add Customer**. A message names the customer, and the form closes.

To add a customer in the middle of a sale, use **Add new customer** in the cart's add menu instead. It's the same form, and the customer goes straight onto the order. See [Add a new customer](../pos/cart/index.mdx#new-customer).

<details>
<summary>Optional customer details</summary>

The form starts with **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Username**:

- **Username** — can only be set now, when you add the customer. Leave it empty and your store makes one from the email while [Automatically Generate Username from Customer Email](../settings/wp-admin/general.mdx#automatically-generate-username-from-customer-email) is on, as it is by default.

Everything else starts as a button below them; press one to add that row:

- **Company**, **Billing Address** and **Shipping Address**. **Shipping Address** is **Same as billing** until you press **Use a different address**.
- **Password** — leave it out and your store makes one.
- **Tax ID** — a type, the ID and a country. A new row starts with a type for the billing country, or for your store's country when there's no billing address. "Format does not match the selected type." is a warning only: the ID still saves.
- **Custom field** — a key and a value, saved on the customer's account.

A customer's role is set in WP Admin, not here.

</details>

## Edit a customer {#edit-customer}

1. Press the **›** at the end of the customer's row. With the **Actions** column hidden, tap the row instead; on a phone, tap the customer. The **Edit** dialog opens with the customer's name in its title.
2. Change a field. There's no **Save** button: the change saves a moment after you stop typing.
3. Check for the green **Saved** mark beside the field. A password saves when you leave its field. A value the form can't accept, such as an email with no `@`, shows an error under the field and isn't saved.
4. Press **Close** when you're done.

<details>
<summary>Other fields and controls</summary>

- **Username** is locked: it's set at creation.
- **Role** is locked: it's set in WordPress.
- **Sync** saves anything still waiting, then fetches the customer again from your store. Use it when the customer looks out of date.
- **JSON** shows the customer's record as it is stored on this device, for when support asks what the POS holds.

</details>

## Delete a customer {#delete-customer}

:::warning Deleting is permanent
Deleting a customer deletes their WordPress user on your store. There is no Trash for WordPress users, so it can't be undone.
:::

In the **Edit** dialog, press **Delete**. Tick **Confirm** and press **Delete** again.

**Delete** shows only to a cashier whose role may delete users. Only an administrator can edit or delete a staff account, such as a shop manager or another cashier. See [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).

## Display Settings {#display-settings}

Press **Display options** (the sliders, beside **Add new customer**) to open **Customer Settings**. Switch a column on or off, or drag it by its grip (⠿) to move it. These settings are kept on this device only.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

- **Add new customer is greyed** — rest on it to see why: an upgrade to WCPOS Pro, or a permission an administrator grants in [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).
- **"Failed to save customer"** — the form stays open with what you typed. Press **Add Customer** again.
- **"Not saved" beside a field** — that change wasn't written. Type it again.
- **"Couldn't refresh. The copy on this device is unchanged"** — **Sync** couldn't reach your store. Check your connection and press **Sync** again.
- **The customer was saved, but not added to this order** — from the cart's **Add new customer**. Search for them in the cart and add them again.
- **A customer you expect isn't found** — offline, search only finds customers already on this device. See [On the device and in your store](../reference/search.mdx#device-and-store).
- **The list looks wrong** — press and hold the sync button in the footer and choose **Clear and Refresh**. It clears this device's copy of the customer list and downloads it again; nothing changes on your store.
