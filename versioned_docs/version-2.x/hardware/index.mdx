## Card reader compatibility {#card-reader-compatibility}

Connecting a card reader to WCPOS needs [WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx). Without it, you can still [take a card on your own terminal](../pos/checkout/taking-a-payment.mdx#card) and record it at checkout.

Which card readers a till can use depends on **how you run WCPOS** and on the reader's connection type. **Each payment gateway supports its own readers**, so check the reader against the gateway you plan to use ([Stripe Terminal](../payment/gateways/stripe-terminal.mdx#readers), [SumUp](../payment/gateways/sumup-terminal.mdx#hardware-compatibility), [all card terminals](../payment/gateways/index.mdx#hardware-terminal-gateways)) before buying.

| Reader | Works on |
|---|---|
| [**Smart reader**](./card-readers/index.mdx#smart-readers): sits on your shop's network, and your store sends it the payment | Every till: browser, desktop app, iPhone, iPad, Android, if your gateway supports the reader |
| [**Bluetooth reader**](./card-readers/index.mdx#connect-a-reader): pairs with one phone or tablet | The iOS and Android apps, if your gateway supports the reader and the store lets tills use the app's readers ([Stripe connections](../payment/gateways/stripe-terminal.mdx#connections)) |
| [**Tap to Pay**](./card-readers/index.mdx#tap-to-pay): the phone itself is the reader | Coming in an app update: the iOS and Android apps, with Stripe Terminal, when the store lets tills use the app's readers{/* pending: roadmap#113, front desk 2026-11-09: worded as coming in an app update until Paul confirms Apple's entitlement; restore the shipped wording then */} |

A browser or the desktop app can't reach a Bluetooth reader or a phone's contactless chip.

<details>
<summary>Example reader models</summary>

- **Smart readers:** Stripe Reader S700, BBPOS WisePOS E.
- **Bluetooth readers:** Stripe Reader M2, BBPOS WisePad 3, SumUp Solo Lite.

## Set up a card reader {#set-up-a-card-reader}

1. Ask whoever manages your store to set up the reader on the payment gateway's page in WooCommerce ([Payment Gateways](../payment/gateways/index.mdx#hardware-terminal-gateways)).
2. On this till, open **Settings → Card readers** and choose your reader. See [Card Readers](./card-readers/index.mdx).

If that page reads **No card readers are set up for this store**, ask whoever manages your store to check the payment gateway setup; see [Card Readers](./card-readers/index.mdx#open).
