Reader settings live under **POS → Settings → Card readers**. The page lists the card readers **this device talks to directly** — Bluetooth readers such as the Stripe WisePad 3 or the SumUp Solo Lite, and Tap to Pay on an iPhone. Each device keeps its own readers; they are not synced between tills.

:::tip Internet-connected terminals aren't on this page
A countertop terminal on your shop's network (Stripe WisePOS E or S700, a SumUp Solo over the cloud, Mollie or Windcave terminals) is set up on its **payment gateway's page** in WooCommerce, and appears at checkout from any till. Your Stripe or SumUp **account** and the reader's **Terminal Location** live there too — see [Payment Gateways](/payment/gateways).
:::

## Connect a reader {#connect-a-reader}

<Steps>
  <StepCard number="1" title="Scan">

Go to **Settings → Card readers** and tap **Connect a reader**. If more than one card provider on your store uses a reader, pick the provider first. Switch the reader on and keep it near the till; readers the app finds appear as cards with their model and serial number.

  </StepCard>
  <StepCard number="2" title="Connect">

Tap **Connect** on your reader's card. **The first connection installs the reader's software update — allow about 10 minutes.** The row shows the percentage, and the reader restarts partway through; that is part of the update. Keep the reader switched on and charged. Later connections take a few seconds.

  </StepCard>
  <StepCard number="3" title="Done" isLast>

The row shows **Connected** and the reader's battery. At checkout, the pay sheet for that provider shows one line — the reader and its battery — and the **Send** button is live. The reader reconnects by itself the next time you open that provider's keypad; you only come back here to change readers.

  </StepCard>
</Steps>

## What the row shows {#what-the-row-shows}

Each reader is one row: the **model** (WisePad 3, Stripe Reader M2, Solo Lite), its **serial number**, the **provider** as a small label, and on the right its state — **Connected · 82%**, or the button that applies. While something is happening, one line under the name says what: _Looking for readers…_, _Connecting to WisePad 3…_, _Installing reader software_ with a progress bar, or what went wrong and what to do.

## Disconnect and Forget {#disconnect-and-forget}

- **Disconnect** releases the reader but **remembers** it. It reconnects on its own at the next card sale. Use it to hand the reader to another till for a while.
- **Forget** (under **⋮** on the row, then confirm) releases the reader **and** drops it from this device. It will not reconnect at the next sale; connect it again from this page when you want it back.

A reader that is remembered but not connected keeps **Disconnect** under **⋮**. If the app says the reader is _still held by an earlier session_ — after signing out, or switching stores — tap it: that releases the reader so a new scan can find it.

## SumUp readers {#sumup}

SumUp's reader is paired inside SumUp's own screens. The SumUp row has one button, **Open SumUp reader settings**, which signs you in to SumUp if needed and opens its pairing screen. Once a reader is saved there, the row shows it as connected.

## Tap to Pay on iPhone {#tap-to-pay}

On an iPhone, a provider that supports Tap to Pay shows a **Tap to Pay on iPhone** row. Setting it up is for **administrators**: a cashier sees _Ask an administrator to set this up_ and no button. Tapping **Set up** shows Apple's introduction and terms, then prepares the phone — a progress line says so, and it takes about a minute the first time. When the row says **Ready**, the iPhone is the reader: customers tap their card, phone or watch on the back of it.

## When something goes wrong {#troubleshoot}

Every problem is one line on the row with **Try again** and **Help**. Pick the line you see:

- **No readers found.** Switch the reader on and keep it within arm's reach; a reader that was connected to another phone or tablet stops advertising until that connection is dropped. On Android, **Location** must be on as well as Bluetooth — Android needs it to scan for Bluetooth devices.
- **Turn on Bluetooth to connect a reader.** Bluetooth is off on this device.
- **Allow access to the reader in app settings.** The app was refused Bluetooth (or, on Android, Location) permission — grant it in the phone's Settings app and come back.
- **Not supported at the Terminal location** (Stripe). The gateway's Terminal Location is in the wrong country for that reader: a WisePad 3 cannot work on a US location. Create a Location for your country and choose it on the gateway's settings page — see [Stripe Terminal → Bluetooth readers](/payment/gateways/stripe-terminal#bluetooth-readers) — then **Try again**.
- **Still held by an earlier session.** Tap **Disconnect** under **⋮**, then scan again.
- **Connecting a reader needs a connection to the store.** The till is offline; reconnect to the internet first.
- **The pay sheet says _No reader connected_.** Tap **Connect in Settings → Card readers** on that line; it brings you here.
