**Settings → Card readers** is where a till picks the card reader it takes payments with. Each till keeps its own reader: what you connect here is not shared with your other tills.

:::info Pro Feature
Card readers need [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).
:::

**Before a till can use a reader**, a shop manager or administrator sets up the payment gateway in WooCommerce: the provider account, the Terminal location and which kinds of reader the store allows. If that isn't done yet, ask them; the steps are in [Payment Gateways](../../payment/gateways/index.mdx#hardware-terminal-gateways).

A till can use three kinds of reader:

- [**Smart reader**](#smart-readers) — sits on your shop's network, and your store drives it. Works on every till: browser, desktop app, iPhone, iPad and Android.
- [**Tap to Pay**](#tap-to-pay) — the phone itself takes the card. iOS and Android apps only.
- [**Bluetooth reader**](#connect-a-reader) — pairs with this phone or tablet, such as a Stripe Reader M2, WisePad 3 or SumUp Solo Lite. iOS and Android apps only.

A browser or the desktop app can't reach a Bluetooth reader or a phone's contactless chip, so those tills list smart readers only.

## Open Card readers {#open}

In the POS, tap **Settings** at the bottom of the side menu, then **Card readers**.

Once a gateway has readers this till can use, the page lists them in up to three sections: **Smart readers**, **This phone** (Tap to Pay) and **Bluetooth readers**. If your store allows only the app's readers, a browser or desktop till reads *Only the app's readers are allowed for this store. Allow smart readers on the gateway's WooCommerce page to pay from this till.*

## Choose a smart reader {#smart-readers}

A smart reader sits on your shop's network, and your store sends it the payment. It's registered with your payment provider and allowed on the gateway's page; it then shows here on every till, with no pairing.

The **Smart readers** section reads *The store drives these over the network. This till pays with the selected one.* Each row shows the reader's name, the gateway as a small label, its location, and **Online** or **Offline**.

- **Selected** — the reader this till pays with.
- **Use this reader** — on another online reader: tap it to switch this till to that one. Do it once on each till, so each counter sends payments to its own reader.
- **Set by the store** — the store has locked tills to one reader. There is nothing to choose.

At checkout the till reads **Terminal:** and the reader's name, with **Change** to pick another when the store allows it.

## Connect a Bluetooth reader {#connect-a-reader}

:::note Before you start
- **Stripe needs a Terminal location.** A Stripe Bluetooth reader registers at the gateway's Terminal location when it connects. Without one the row reads *Could not connect: This gateway has no Terminal location*. See [Stripe Terminal setup](../../payment/gateways/stripe-terminal.mdx#setup).
- **Charge the reader.** Its first connection installs a software update that takes about 10 minutes.
:::

In the iOS or Android app:

1. **Switch the reader on** and keep it near the phone or tablet.
2. **Tap Connect a reader** in **Settings → Card readers**. If the till asks **Connect a reader with**, pick the payment gateway. A SumUp reader is chosen in SumUp's own screen instead; see [SumUp readers](#sumup).
3. **Tap Connect beside your reader.** The row lists each reader it finds with its model and serial number; match the serial number to yours. While it searches, the row reads:
   - *Looking for readers… Turn the reader on and keep it near this device.*
   - then *1 reader found. Is this yours?* or *3 readers found. Which one is yours?*
4. **Wait while it connects.** The row reads **Connecting to** and the reader's name. The first time, it then reads **Installing reader software** with a progress bar: stay on the screen until it finishes.
5. **Check the row reads Connected** with the reader's battery, for example **Connected · 82%**.

Later connections take a few seconds. You don't need to come back here before each sale: when you pick that payment method at checkout, the till reconnects to the same reader on its own.

While no reader is connected, the **Bluetooth readers** section reads *No reader connected yet. Turn the reader on and keep it near this phone.*

### SumUp readers {#sumup}

SumUp pairs its readers in its own screens. Tap **Connect a reader**, and pick SumUp if the till asks: SumUp's screen opens, signs you in if needed, and lets you choose the reader. Afterwards the SumUp row has one button, **Open SumUp reader settings** (named after the payment method), to go back to that screen.

- Until you sign in, the row reads **Sign in to SumUp to use its reader.**
- Once a reader is saved there, the row reads **Saved in the SumUp app**, and **Connected** while the reader is in use.

Which SumUp readers work: see [SumUp Terminal](../../payment/gateways/sumup-terminal.mdx#hardware-compatibility).

## Set up Tap to Pay {#tap-to-pay}

With Tap to Pay the phone is the reader: the customer taps their card, phone or watch on it. It works with Stripe Terminal in the iOS and Android apps.{/* pending: roadmap#113 — Tap to Pay on iPhone needs Apple's publishing entitlement before App Store builds carry it */}

1. In **Settings → Card readers**, the **This phone** section has a **Tap to Pay on iPhone** or **Tap to Pay on Android** row, reading **Not set up**.
2. Tap **Set up Tap to Pay**. Only a shop manager or administrator sees the button; anyone else sees **Not set up · ask an administrator**.
3. The first time, accept the provider's terms. On an iPhone, Apple asks you to link an Apple ID and accept its Tap to Pay terms.
4. The row becomes **This device** and reads **Ready**.

Tap to Pay needs a connection to take a payment.

## Disconnect, forget or release a reader {#disconnect-and-forget}

Each Bluetooth or Tap to Pay row has a button and a **⋮** menu.

- **Disconnect** — the button on a connected row. The till lets go of the reader for now but remembers it, and reconnects at the next card sale. Use it to lend the reader to another till for a while.
- **Forget** — under **⋮**. The till asks *Forget WisePad 3? It will not reconnect at the next sale. You can connect it again from this page.* Tap **Forget** to drop the reader from this till.
- **Release the reader** — under **⋮**, only when the app is still holding a reader it isn't using, for example after you sign out or switch stores. Tap it, then **Connect** again.

<details>
<summary>Everything a reader row can say</summary>

Each row shows the reader's model (**WisePad 3**, **Stripe Reader M2**), its serial number, the gateway as a small label, the Terminal location, and one line of state:

- **Connected · 82%** — ready, with the battery left. Tap to Pay reads **Ready**.
- **Not connected** — the till remembers this reader but isn't connected now. Tap **Connect**.
- **Connecting to …** — wait. The button reads **Connecting…**.
- **Installing reader software** — the update, with a progress bar. The button reads **Installing…**.
- **Saved in the SumUp app** — a SumUp reader chosen in SumUp's screen.
- **No readers found. Turn the reader on and try again.** — see [If something goes wrong](#troubleshoot).
- **Could not connect:**, **Could not disconnect:** or **Could not forget the reader:** — the provider's reason follows. **Try again** and **Help** sit below.
- **Turn on Bluetooth to connect a reader.**, **Allow access to the reader in app settings.**, **Sign in to SumUp to use its reader.** — see below.

</details>

## At checkout {#at-checkout}

When you pick a Bluetooth reader's payment method, checkout shows one line about the reader:

- **WisePad 3 · Connected · 82%** — ready to send the amount.
- **Connecting…** or **Updating reader software** — the till is reconnecting to the reader it remembers. Wait.
- **No reader connected** or **Could not reconnect to the reader** — tap **Connect in Settings → Card readers**, which brings you to this page. Connect the reader, then go back to the sale.

Taking the payment itself: see [On a Card Reader](../../pos/checkout/taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).

## If something goes wrong {#troubleshoot}

<details>
<summary>The reader can't be found, won't connect or is still updating</summary>

**No readers found. Turn the reader on and try again.** Switch the reader on, charge it, and hold it close to the device. Then tap **Try again**. **Can't see your reader?** under the line opens this page.

**Turn on Bluetooth to connect a reader.** Bluetooth is off on this device. Turn it on in the device's settings.

**Allow access to the reader in app settings.** The app was refused permission to use the reader. Open the device's Settings app, find WCPOS and allow Bluetooth. On Android, also allow **Location**: Android needs it to scan for Bluetooth readers.

**Sign in to SumUp to use its reader.** Tap **Open SumUp reader settings** and sign in.

**Could not connect: This gateway has no Terminal location.** Choose a Terminal location on the Stripe gateway's page. See [Stripe Terminal setup](../../payment/gateways/stripe-terminal.mdx#setup).

**Could not connect:** and another reason. Tap **Try again**. If it keeps failing, tap **Help**, which opens this page, and check the reason with your payment provider.

**The update is still installing.** The first update takes about 10 minutes. Keep the reader on and charged, and stay on the screen.

**A reader you know is there can't be found after a sign-out or store switch.** Tap **⋮** on its row, then **Release the reader**, and connect again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The page has no reader for this till</summary>

**No card readers are set up for this store.** No gateway on your store has a reader this till can use. An administrator sets one up on the gateway's page. See [Payment Gateways](../../payment/gateways/index.mdx#hardware-terminal-gateways).

**Only the app's readers are allowed for this store.** The store allows Bluetooth readers and Tap to Pay only, which a browser or desktop till can't use. Use the iOS or Android app, or allow smart readers on the gateway's page. For Stripe, see [Smart readers, Bluetooth or both](../../payment/gateways/stripe-terminal.mdx#connections).

</details>
