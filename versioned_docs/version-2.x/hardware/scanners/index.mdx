Almost every barcode scanner works with the POS the moment you plug it in or pair it. This page takes a new scanner from the box to its first scan, and shows where to go when a scan doesn't come through.

## Ways to scan {#ways-to-scan}

- **Keyboard mode** — how nearly every scanner comes out of the box. The scanner types the barcode, and the POS tells a scan from typing by its speed. Nothing to set up.
- **Camera** — no scanner needed. Press the scan button beside the product search and point the camera at the barcode. See [Camera scanning](../../pos/product-panel/barcode-scanning.mdx#camera-scanning).
- **Direct connection** — for a scanner switched out of keyboard mode with a barcode from its manual. The POS then knows the scanner itself, so speed, keyboard layout and which box has the cursor stop mattering. See [Connect a scanner directly](#direct).

## Connect your scanner {#connect}

1. **USB scanner:** plug it in.
2. **Bluetooth scanner:** put it in pairing mode (the manual says how) and pair it in your device's own Bluetooth settings, as you would a keyboard.
3. Open the POS and scan a product's barcode. It goes straight into the cart. You don't need to tap the search field first, but in a browser or the desktop app keep the cursor out of text fields, because anything going into a field counts as typing.

If the product came up, you're done. To check the scanner's settings or watch a scan arrive, open **Settings** and choose **Barcode Scanning**.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Barcode Average Time Input Threshold (ms)** — how fast the keys must come to count as a scan. The POS starts at 24. Raise it for a slow scanner; lower it if fast typing is being taken for a scan.
2. **Barcode Minimum Length** — the shortest code that counts as a scan. The POS starts at 8, the length of an EAN-8.
3. **No setup needed for most scanners** — what this section says while no scanner has been connected directly. A keyboard-mode scanner never appears here.
4. **Scanner setup guide** — opens the [Scanner Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.mdx), which asks a few questions and walks you through setup or a fix.
5. **Direct connection** — **None** until you connect a scanner directly. See [Connect a scanner directly](#direct).
6. **Barcode Scanning Test** — scan into this page to see what the scanner sends. See [Test a scan](#test).

</div>

Between them sit **Barcode Scanner Prefix** and **Barcode Scanner Suffix**, for characters your scanner adds before or after each code; the POS removes them before it looks the code up. A trailing Enter needs no suffix: the POS ignores it. Under **Sound**, **Play a sound on scan** is off to start; switch it on to choose a **Sound theme**, the **Volume**, and the sounds for a good and a failed scan.

These settings belong to this device, so set them on each till that has a scanner.

## Test a scan {#test}

Scan any barcode while **Barcode Scanning** is open. In the iPhone, iPad or Android app, tap **Keypress Event** first. The test shows the keys as they arrived, the code the POS read from them, and whether it counted as a scan. This one came from a slow scanner, so it was treated as typing:

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Keypress Event** — every key the scanner sent, including an Enter at the end.
2. **Detected Barcode** — the code the POS will look up, after the prefix and suffix are removed.
3. **Timing chart** — one bar per key, against a dashed line at your threshold. A bar over the line is a key that came too slowly.
4. **Verdict** — **Detected as a scan**, or why it was treated as typing. Here the keys averaged 35ms against a 24ms threshold, so the POS suggests a new threshold; **Apply** saves it.
5. **Recent attempts** — your last few tries, each marked **scan**, **typing** or **too short**, with its average gap between keys.
6. **Troubleshooting guide** — opens the [Scanner Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.mdx) for a fix by symptom.

</div>

If the verdict says the scanner is **sending Shift as separate keys**, set the scanner to a US keyboard layout with a barcode from its manual, or [connect it directly](#direct).

## Connect a scanner directly {#direct}

Keyboard mode is right for most stores. Connect directly when scans come out garbled, land in the wrong box, or get lost when no box is selected. First switch the scanner out of keyboard mode by scanning the setup barcode in its manual, such as *SPP mode*, *serial mode* or *HID POS mode*. If it's a Bluetooth scanner, remove it in your device's Bluetooth settings and pair it again in its new mode.

In **Barcode Scanning**, press **Connect a scanner directly (advanced)**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[-24, 0], [222, 0], [455, 12], [300, 0], [240, 0]]} alt="The Connect a scanner directly sheet in a browser: Connect over USB, Connect over Bluetooth serial, the note about keyboard-mode scanners, and My scanner isn't in the list with a Bluetooth service-class UUID field and Add" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Connect over USB** — for a USB scanner in HID POS mode. Pick it from the list that opens.
2. **Connect over Bluetooth serial** — for a scanner in serial (SPP) mode. A USB scanner in serial mode shows in the same list.
3. **Keyboard mode note** — a scanner still in keyboard mode never shows in these lists. Your device won't let any app take over a keyboard.
4. **Bluetooth service-class UUID** — for a Bluetooth serial scanner that is out of keyboard mode but still missing from the list. Type the UUID from its manual, press **Add**, then connect again.
5. **Find your scanner's service UUID** — opens the [Scanner Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.mdx), which says where to look.

</div>

What the sheet offers depends on where you run the POS:

| Where you run the POS | What the sheet offers |
|---|---|
| Desktop app, Chrome or Edge | **Connect over USB** and **Connect over Bluetooth serial** |
| iPhone or iPad | **Connect Bluetooth scanner**, for a Bluetooth LE scanner |
| Android | **Register a scanner**: press **Register scanner**, scan once with it, name it and press **Save scanner**. It stays in keyboard mode, but the POS knows its scans by the device rather than by speed |
| Safari or Firefox | No **Scanner** section at all. Use keyboard mode or the camera, or switch to the desktop app or Chrome |

A connected scanner gets a row under **Scanner** with its status: **Connected** while the link is live; **Not in range**, **Not plugged in** or **Not connected** while it isn't; **Registered** on Android. Its **⋮** menu has **Remove**. Keyboard-mode scanners keep working alongside it.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Nothing happens when you scan.** Check the scanner beeps or lights, and that your device's Bluetooth settings say **Connected**, not just paired. Then scan into a notes app: if the code appears there, the scanner works, and the [test](#test) shows why the POS took it for typing.

**The verdict says "Treated as typing".** The scanner is slower than your threshold. Press **Apply** on the suggestion, or raise **Barcode Average Time Input Threshold (ms)**.

**Typing in a search box is taken as a scan.** Lower the threshold, or raise **Barcode Minimum Length**.

**Scans come out garbled.** The scanner's keyboard layout doesn't match the device's. Set it to a US layout with a barcode from its manual, or [connect it directly](#direct).

**Your scanner isn't in the list when you connect directly.** It's still in keyboard mode. Scan the mode barcode from its manual, pair it again if it's Bluetooth, and try again.

**The scan reaches the POS but no product comes up.** The POS says why: **Barcode not found**, **Several matches**, **Choose a variation**. See [Scan a barcode](../../pos/product-panel/index.mdx#scan). Which product field holds the barcode is set in [Barcode Field](../../settings/wp-admin/general.mdx#barcode-field).

For a step-by-step fix by platform and symptom, use the [Scanner Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.mdx).
