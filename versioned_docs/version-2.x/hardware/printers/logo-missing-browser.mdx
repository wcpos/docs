**What you see:** you sell in a web browser, and the receipt printer prints the whole receipt except the store logo. The **Preview** on the paid screen shows the logo. The same receipt printed from the desktop or mobile app has it.

## Check the printer first {#test-print}

Make sure the printer can print a picture at all. Go to **Settings → Printing** and press **Test Print** on your receipt printer's row, not on the built-in **Print Dialog** row: that row's test page has no picture. Every row has the same two buttons; the **Print Dialog** row below shows where they are.

- **The WCPOS mark prints, your logo doesn't.** The printer is fine. The logo's address is the problem: [fix it](#fix).
- **The rest of the test page prints, but not the mark.** The printer itself fails at pictures, whichever app you print from. Start with the [setup wizard's questions](./setup-wizard.mdx#troubleshoot).

## Fix it {#fix}

**Quickest: print from the desktop app.** It prints the logo with nothing to set up, so use it if the till already has it installed.

To keep printing from the browser, find the logo's address. Right-click (or press and hold) the logo in the receipt **Preview** and choose **Open image in new tab**. Compare its site name, the part between `https://` and the next `/`, with the one in the POS's address bar. If they differ, that is the likely cause.

<details>
<summary>For whoever manages your website</summary>

Pick one:

- **Use a logo from your own site.** Upload the logo to the media library under the store's address and pick that file as the receipt logo. Reload the POS and print again.
- **Allow the header where the logo lives.** If your uploads must stay on a CDN, add `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` for your store's address on its image files. Reload the POS and print again.

</details>

## Why it happens {#why-it-happens}

In a browser, the POS turns the logo into dots before it sends the receipt to the printer. A browser only allows that for a picture from the same address as the POS page, such as your store's own media library. A picture from another address needs that server's permission, a header called `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`. Without it, the browser hands the POS nothing, and the receipt prints without the logo rather than not at all.

The logo comes from another address when:

- an image CDN or a media offload plugin serves your uploads from its own domain
- the logo's address and the POS's differ only by `www.`

The desktop and mobile apps fetch the logo themselves, so it prints there. Receipts printed through the **Print Dialog** show it too.

## Still missing? {#still-missing}

Right after a receipt prints without the logo, go to **Settings → Printing**, open **More actions** on the printer's row and press **Copy setup report**.
