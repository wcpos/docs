A cash drawer doesn't connect to the POS. It plugs into the **receipt printer**, and the POS asks the printer to open it. This page wires the drawer, turns it on in the app, and tests it.

:::note You need a receipt printer first
Add the printer before the drawer. See [Printer Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.mdx). The built-in **Print Dialog** and cloud printers can't open a drawer.
:::

## Plug the drawer into the printer {#wire-it-up}

1. Find the socket on the back of the printer marked **DK**, **Drawer** or **Cash Drawer**.
2. Plug in the drawer's cable. Its plug looks like a telephone plug. The network socket beside it is wider: don't use that one.
3. Switch the printer off and on.

## Turn it on in the app {#switch-it-on-in-the-app}

The switch is **Auto-open cash drawer**, one per printer. It's off for a new printer. You can turn it on while you add the printer, or later from the printer's row. Already added your printer? Go to [Test the drawer](#test-the-drawer).

:::note Every receipt opens the drawer
With **Auto-open cash drawer** on, the drawer opens every time that printer prints a receipt, card sales and reprints too. See [When the drawer opens](#when-it-opens).
:::

### While you add the printer {#while-adding}

In **Settings → Printing**, press **Add printer**. When the search finishes, press **More options**.

3. **Auto-open cash drawer** — the printer opens the drawer each time it prints a receipt.

Finish adding the printer as [Printer Setup Wizard](./setup-wizard.mdx) shows. The wizard's test page never opens the drawer, so test the drawer next.

### Test the drawer, or change a printer you have {#test-the-drawer}

1. In **Settings → Printing**, press **Edit** on the printer's row.
2. Turn on **Auto-open cash drawer** if it's off. **Cash drawer connector** appears under it: the pin the drawer is wired to. **Pin 2 / Drawer 1** suits most drawers. Pick **Pin 5 / Drawer 2** for a second drawer, or when the drawer's manual says pin 5.
3. Press **Open drawer**, at the foot of **Edit printer** beside **Test Print**. It opens the drawer once, without printing. Check that the drawer springs open. If it stays shut, see [If the drawer doesn't open](#when-it-doesnt-open), even if the app says **Cash drawer opened**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={7} offsets={[[685, 0], [685, 0], [47, -30], [36, -30]]} alt="Settings → Printing with Edit printer open for a network printer, scrolled down to the drawer settings: Auto-open cash drawer on, Cash drawer connector set to Pin 2 / Drawer 1, and the Open drawer and Test Print buttons at the foot" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Auto-open cash drawer** — on here. The printer opens the drawer each time it prints a receipt.
2. **Cash drawer connector** — the pin the drawer is wired to, here **Pin 2 / Drawer 1**. It shows only while **Auto-open cash drawer** is on.
3. **Open drawer** — opens the drawer once, without printing.
4. **Test Print** — prints a test page on this printer. With **Auto-open cash drawer** on, as here, it opens the drawer too.

## When the drawer opens {#when-it-opens}

With **Auto-open cash drawer** on, the drawer opens whenever that printer prints a receipt:

- a receipt printed at checkout, by hand or automatically;
- a receipt you print again later;
- **Test Print** on the printer's row.

It opens for every payment method, not only cash.

It stays shut for register reports: the X-report, the handover and the closure. See [Open the Register](../../pos/register/index.mdx).

## Open the drawer without a sale {#no-sale}

To make change or check the float, use **No sale** in the register panel. It opens the drawer and records why, with no money moving.

:::note Before you start
- The register is open. See [Open the Register](../../pos/register/index.mdx).
- Your default receipt printer has **Auto-open cash drawer** on. **No sale** opens the drawer on that printer. To make a printer the default, open its **More actions** menu in **Settings → Printing** and press **Set Default**.
:::

Tap your avatar at the end of the register bar, then **In the drawer**, to open the register panel.

2. **In the drawer** — the cash the register expects. A No sale doesn't change it. **Print X-report** at the foot never opens the drawer.

Press **No sale**. The sheet asks **Why open the drawer?**

1. **Change** — one of the ready reasons, with **Mistake** and **Drawer check**. Tap one to fill in the reason. **Other…** clears the field so you can type your own.
2. **Reason** — the reason that's recorded. Until there is one, the sheet says **Add a reason**.

## If the drawer doesn't open {#when-it-doesnt-open}

Start with **Open drawer** in **Edit**. It opens the drawer without printing a receipt, so you can see whether the drawer opens at all. Then open the symptom that matches.

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem question="Open drawer works, but receipts don't open the drawer">

The wiring is right. Check that **Auto-open cash drawer** is on for the printer that prints your receipts.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem question="Open drawer shows “The printer did not accept the job.”">

The printer didn't take the command. Print a test page from the printer's row first. See [Receipt Printers](./index.mdx#when-nothing-prints).

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem question="The app says Cash drawer opened, but nothing happens">

Check the cable is in the drawer socket, not the network socket. Then try the other **Cash drawer connector**: the drawer may be wired to pin 5.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem question="The drawer clicks but doesn't open">

Check the drawer's key. Most drawers have a lock on the front, and one key position keeps the drawer shut. Then check the voltage: drawers are made for 12 V or 24 V, and the drawer has to match what the printer puts out. The printer's manual says which.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem question="Edit warns: Cash drawer kicks depend on printer language/emulation">

The **Printer Language** doesn't match the printer's make, for example a Star printer set to ESC/POS. Set the language to match the printer's emulation mode.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem question="No sale closed, but the drawer stayed shut">

The No sale is recorded either way. Check that your default receipt printer has **Auto-open cash drawer** on. If the printer refused, the sheet stays open with the error: press **Open the drawer** again. That only opens the drawer, it doesn't record a second No sale.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem question="A low-cost printer doesn't respond">

Some low-cost printers only understand an older version of the drawer command. For a printer set to **Epson**, **Open drawer** sends Epson's newer command, which these printers ignore. Some printers have no drawer socket, or don't drive one. The printer's manual says whether it supports a cash drawer.

</AccordionItem>
<AccordionItem question="The drawer opens when Auto-open cash drawer is off">

A custom thermal template can carry its own `<drawer />` command, and that opens the drawer whatever the switch says. Remove the tag from the template to stop it. See [Thermal Templates](../../receipts/thermal-templates.mdx).

</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

In **Settings → Printing**, open the printer's **More actions** menu and press **Copy setup report**. Paste it into your message, with the printer and drawer models.
