**Symptom:** an Epson receipt printer printed once, then nothing. The printer looks healthy: its light is on, it has paper, and a self-test from its own buttons prints. Restart it before you try anything else.

## Restart the printer {#power-cycle-first}

1. Switch the printer off, or unplug it.
2. Wait about ten seconds, then switch it on and wait for its light to settle.
3. Print again:
   - In the setup panel, press **Try again**.
   - For a printer you've already added, go to **Settings → Printing** and press **Test Print** on its row. See [Change a printer later](./setup-wizard.mdx#edit-printer).
   - At checkout, check nothing printed first, then press **Print Receipt** again. A receipt that did print would come out twice.

:::tip Restart before you change any setting
A restart clears a held job, and tells a stuck printer from a wrong setting. If printing fails again, or the restart changes nothing, go to [If restarting doesn't help, or it stops again](#if-it-comes-back).
:::

## What the app shows {#what-you-see}

- **Setting up a printer:** the panel says **Nothing printed** and, under it, *"The printer is holding jobs. Turn it off and on again, then try again."*
- **At checkout:** the receipt doesn't come out, and the till shows [PRINT301](../../error-codes/PRINT301.mdx) (it couldn't reach the printer) or [PRINT201](../../error-codes/PRINT201.mdx) (it couldn't confirm the print).

## Why it happens {#why-it-happens}

An Epson can take a job and then **hold** it instead of printing it. It doesn't reject the job or show an error. While it holds that one job, it refuses every other job, from any till and on any connection, including the app's test page.

The hold sometimes clears by itself after a few minutes, and sometimes lasts for hours. A restart clears it straight away. That's why a printer can look fine, act as if it's dead, and then start working again while you're still looking for the cause.

Only the stuck printer stops. Other printers on the same till keep printing their receipts.

## If restarting doesn't help, or it stops again {#if-it-comes-back}

A restart clears the hold, not whatever caused it. Match what you see:

**It works after a restart, then sticks again.** Something keeps sending the printer jobs it blocks. For an Epson built for the EU or UK since August 2025, check [Epson Secure Printing](./secure-printing.mdx).

**A restart changes nothing, and the printer's web page still opens.** Check [Server Direct Print](./server-direct-print.mdx). This setting on the printer can stop every receipt printing while the printer's web page still opens, and restarting doesn't change it.

**The first receipt is fine and later ones come out as gibberish.** This is usually the printer's memory not keeping up with images, not a hold. In [Still not printing?](./setup-wizard.mdx#troubleshoot), say the test page came out garbled, then pick **The first receipt is fine, then later ones turn to gibberish**.

**It's a Bluetooth printer.** Bluetooth printers stop answering for other reasons. See [Pairing a Bluetooth receipt printer](./bluetooth-pairing.mdx#turn-it-off-and-on).

## Still stuck? {#still-stuck}

Press **Copy setup report** and paste it into your message to us. It's on the **Nothing printed** panel, and in the **More actions** menu on the printer's row in **Settings → Printing**. It lists what the app tried and what the printer answered.
