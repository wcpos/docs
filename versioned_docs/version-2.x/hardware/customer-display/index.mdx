A customer display is a second screen that faces the customer, such as a tablet or a monitor with a web browser. It shows the cart as you ring it up, line by line, with the total.

:::info Pro Feature
Customer displays need WCPOS Pro. Without it, **Settings → Customer Display** says so: see [Free vs Pro](../../getting-started/free-vs-pro.mdx#customer-display).
:::

Each display is paired with one till, and that till sends it the cart directly. Keep the till and the display on the same Wi-Fi network: guest networks often block the link between them.

## Pair a display {#pair}

You need the till and the display device side by side. A pairing code works once and expires after 10 minutes.

1. **On the till**, tap **Settings** at the bottom of the side menu, then **Customer Display**.
2. **Tap Generate pairing code.** A six-digit code appears, with when it expires.
3. **On the display device**, open the **Display page URL** shown under the button: your site's address followed by `wcpos-display/`. **Copy URL** copies it; on a browser till, **Open in browser** opens it in a new tab.
4. **Enter the code.** The display page reads **Pair this display**. Type the code in **Pairing code**, change **Display name** if you like (it starts as *Customer display*), and tap **Pair display**.
5. **Check the till.** The display appears under **Paired displays**.

1. **Customer Display** — in **Settings**, after Card readers.
2. **The pairing code** — type it on the display. One code pairs one display.
3. **Generate pairing code** — makes a new code. Tap it again if the code expires before you use it.
4. **Display page URL** — the address to open on the display, with **Copy URL** and **Open in browser**.
5. **Paired displays** — reads *No displays are paired with this device.* until a display enters a code.

To fill the screen, use the browser's full-screen mode on the display device. The browser remembers the pairing: reopen the same address in the same browser and the display reconnects, with no new code.

## Paired displays {#paired-displays}

**Paired displays** lists the displays paired with this till. Each row shows the display's name, when the till last saw it, and **Connected** or **Disconnected**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[215, 0], [-22, 0]]} maxWidth={720} alt="Settings, Customer Display with one display under Paired displays: Counter display, last seen less than a minute ago, Connected, and its menu button">

1. **Connected** — the display named *Counter display* is linked to this till right now. **Disconnected** means the display page is closed or can't reach the till.
2. **⋮** — the row's menu, with **Forget**.

</JourneyShot>

A display shows one till's sales. To give each counter its own screen, pair a display on each till.

## What the customer sees {#what-the-customer-sees}

What the display shows comes from its template. **Ledger**, the default display template, lists the items with a totals panel beside them on a wide screen, and docks the total at the bottom of a narrow one. With Ledger:

- **Between sales**: the store's logo, or its name if it has no logo, and a welcome line.
- **During a sale**: each line with its quantity, name and line total under **Your items**, and a panel with **Subtotal**, any fees, shipping, discounts and tax, then **Total**. When the customer saves money on the sale, it adds **You saved**. When a customer is attached to the order, it greets them by first name.

The display follows the sale through payment.

The logo is the store's own: the one on the store's **Details** page, which also prints on receipts. A store that uses the site logo shows your WordPress site logo. See [Store setup](../../stores/setup.mdx#store-pages).

## Choose the template {#template}

Every display shows the site's **Live** display template, unless its store picks its own. A display picks up a change within about a minute, with no new pairing.

<details>
<summary>Change the template for all stores</summary>

1. In WP Admin, go to **POS → Templates** and open the **Display** tab, next to the receipt templates.
2. Turn on **Enabled** for the template you want.
3. Select it in the **Live** column.

The gallery itself is covered in [Reports, closures and customer displays](../../receipts/index.mdx#other-templates).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Change the template for one store</summary>

1. In WP Admin, go to **POS → Stores**, click the store, then **Customer display** in its menu.
2. Under **Display template**, which reads **Same as site**, click **Customise**.
3. Pick a template. The list holds the templates enabled on the **Display** tab. The page saves as you go.

**Use site template** puts the store back on the site's **Live** template.

</details>

## Forget a display {#forget}

To unpair a display, open **⋮** on its row under **Paired displays** and choose **Forget**, then **Forget** again to confirm. The display page then reads *This display was removed. Pair it again.* To use that screen again, [pair it](#pair) with a new code.

## If something goes wrong {#troubleshoot}

When a display can't connect for 30 seconds, it says why on its screen and keeps trying.

<details>
<summary>This display can't reach the POS on this network.</summary>

The display reached your store but not the till. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks often keep devices apart, so use your shop's main network.

</details>

<details>
<summary>This display can't reach your store.</summary>

The display device has no internet connection, or can't reach your site. Check its Wi-Fi, then reload the display page.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Waiting for the POS.</summary>

The till this display is paired with isn't running. Open the POS on that till.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The POS answered but didn't finish setting up this display.</summary>

Restart the POS on the till and try again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>This display was removed. Pair it again.</summary>

Someone chose **Forget** for this display on the till. [Pair it](#pair) with a new code.

</details>

<details>
<summary>That pairing code is invalid or expired.</summary>

Codes last 10 minutes and work once. Tap **Generate pairing code** on the till and type the new one. After several wrong codes the page reads *Too many attempts. Try again shortly.*: wait a moment before trying again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>No customer display template is configured.</summary>

No display template is enabled. Enable one on the **Display** tab in **POS → Templates**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The display shows the welcome screen during a sale.</summary>

The picture of the cart couldn't be built, logged as [DISPLAY101](../../error-codes/DISPLAY101.mdx). Carry on with the sale: the display catches up the next time the cart changes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The Generate pairing code button is greyed out.</summary>

The page reads *The customer display service is not running on this device.* Reload the POS, and if it stays greyed out, check **Logs** for the reason.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Customer displays need a newer version of WCPOS Pro.</summary>

The Pro plugin on your site is older than customer displays. Update WCPOS Pro in WordPress, then reload the POS.

</details>
