Press **Checkout** in the cart. The tender pane opens with the first payment method selected and the whole balance ready to take. You pick how the customer pays, enter what they hand over, and press the button that names the amount. Take [cash](#cash), [a card on your own terminal](#card), or [a card on a connected reader](#card-reader). [Checkout](./index.mdx) walks through a whole sale; this page is the payment itself.

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, the till won't record a payment while the register is closed, and a card you've already taken would have to be refunded. Open the register before you charge the customer. See [Open the register](../register/index.mdx#open).
:::

Discounts and coupons go on in the cart, before you press **Checkout**. See [Cart Discounts](../cart/discounts.mdx).

## Take cash {#cash}

1. Tap **Cash** if it isn't already selected.
2. Tap a quick amount, or type what the customer hands you on the keypad. There is no decimal key: the digits fill in from the right, so **2**, **0**, **0**, **0** is $20.00.
3. The change shows in green under the amount.
4. Press **Take $45.00 in Cash** and give the change. The button names the balance, not the note: only what's due goes on the order.

That pays it off: see [Finish the sale](#finish). If you took less than the balance, see [Take part of the balance](#part-payment).

When a cash payment settles the sale and your store rounds cash, the pane shows **Rounded from** and the unrounded amount, and the difference goes on the order as a **Rounding** line. Change is always shown in the order's own currency.

## Record a card from your own terminal {#card}

**Card** is for a card terminal that isn't connected to the POS.

1. Take the payment on your terminal and wait until it shows the payment approved.
2. Tap **Card**. The amount starts at the balance, or at what you typed on the last tile; **Full balance** puts the balance back.
3. Press **Take $45.00 in Card**.

A card can't take more than is due. Type more and the line under the amount says **Only $45.00 is due — Card can't give change**, and the button is greyed out until you lower the amount.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[0, 0], [-40, 0], [-40, 0], [-40, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The tender pane with Card selected and $60.00 typed on a $45.00 order: the warning that only $45.00 is due and Card can't give change, the Full balance button and the greyed-out Take button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Card** — the selected payment method.
2. **Amount** — more than the balance.
3. **Only $45.00 is due** — why the payment can't be taken.
4. **Full balance** — puts the amount back to the balance.
5. **Take** — greyed out until the amount is the balance or less.

</div>

The other methods your store records at the till, such as cheque or bank transfer with WCPOS Pro, work the same way. See [Payment Methods](./payment-methods.mdx).

## On a Card Reader {#card-reader}

:::info Pro Feature
Card readers at the till require [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).
:::

A card reader connected to the POS has its own tile, and the till sends it the amount.

1. Tap the reader's tile. If you have more than one terminal, **Change** beside **Terminal** picks another.
2. Press **Send $45.00 to** and the payment method's name.
3. Follow the steps under **On the terminal**: **Sent**, **On terminal**, **Approved**, **Captured**. The customer presents the card on the reader.
4. When it's captured, the payment joins the order like any other.

To stop, press **Cancel on terminal**. When the till says **Cancel on the reader**, cancel on the reader itself. If a payment fails, **Try again** sends it once more and **Choose another way** goes back to the tiles.

The till remembers which reader you used for each payment method, on this device. Which readers work depends on how you run WCPOS; see [Card reader compatibility](../../hardware/index.mdx#card-reader-compatibility). Pairing a reader is covered on its gateway's page, under [Payment Gateways](../../payment/gateways/index.mdx).

<details>
<summary>When a tile can't be used</summary>

Tiles that can't be used right now are folded under **not available right now**, each with the reason:

- **Needs a connection** — the device is offline. On a sale your store already has, Cash, Card and your other manual methods still work: they show **Sent later**, and the payment is sent when the device is back online. See [A Tile Says Needs a Connection](./troubleshooting.mdx#needs-a-connection).
- **No terminals set up for** the method — no reader for this method is set up, or none of them is online.
- **In use by order** and a number — another sale is using that terminal.
- **Update the app to use** the method — this version of the app can't drive it.
- **Takes the whole order, so not once a payment has been taken** — the method can't finish a part-paid order.

</details>

## Take part of the balance {#part-payment}

For a card on your own terminal, take each part on the terminal and wait for approval before you record that amount here. Type less than the balance on any tile. The line under the amount says **Part payment** and what will be left, and the button adds it: **Take $20.00 in Card · $25.00 left**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[-40, 0], [-40, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The tender pane with $20.00 typed on a $45.00 card payment: the line saying Part payment with $25.00 left after this, and the button Take $20.00 in Card, $25.00 left" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Amount** — less than the balance.
2. **Part payment** — what's left after this payment.
3. **Take** — names the amount and what will be left.

</div>

Press it, and the payment joins the order. The pane stays open on the same method with the rest filled in.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[-40, 0], [-40, 0], [-40, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [-36, 0], [-36, 0]]} alt="The tender pane after a $20.00 card payment: Cancel payment at the top, Remaining $25.00, Card still selected, the button Take $25.00 in Card that pays it off, and the payment listed as Captured beside Paid and Remaining" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Cancel payment** — voids the payments recorded on this order. The till can't reach your own terminal, so it lists a card taken there as **Void $20.00 on Card**: void it on the terminal too. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
2. **Remaining** — replaces **To pay** once a payment is in.
3. **Amount** — the rest, ready to take.
4. **Card** — still selected. Tap another tile to take the rest another way.
5. **Take** — **pays it off** when this payment clears the balance.
6. **The payment** — its method, amount and status: **Captured** means it's taken.
7. **Paid** and **Remaining** — the running balance.

</div>

To take the rest, leave **Card** selected or tap another tile, and press **Take** again. If the customer pays in more parts, type the next amount first.

To divide the order evenly, by amount, by percent or by item before you start, use **Split**. See [Split Payments](./split-payments.mdx).

## Finish the sale {#finish}

When the balance reaches zero, the sale completes. There is nothing more to press. The paid screen shows the change, how it was paid and the receipt. In this picture, a $50.49 sale was paid with $55.00 in cash.

<JourneyShot manifest={checkoutManifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[279, 0], [260, 0], [292, 0], [292, 0], [0, 0], [48, -38], [49, -38], [128, 0]]} alt="The paid screen: the change to give, how it was paid, the template and printer pickers, the receipt preview, and the buttons to print, email, download a PDF or start a new sale" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Change** — what to give back.
2. **Paid** — what was paid and, for cash, what the customer handed over. A sale paid more than one way names each method and how many payments.
3. **Template** — which receipt this sale gets.
4. **Printer** — where it prints.
5. **Preview** — the receipt as it will print.
6. **Print** — prints it.
7. **Email** — sends it to the customer.
8. **New sale** — an empty cart for the next customer.

</div>

**Download PDF** saves the receipt as a file.

With **Automatically show receipt after checkout** off in the cart's settings, a message says **Payment completed for order** with its number instead, and the cart is ready for the next sale. See [Receipts at Checkout](../../receipts/at-checkout.mdx).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**The card was charged, but the order didn't finish.** On a card reader, the charge went through but the sale didn't complete. Don't charge the card again, and don't close the pane with **X**: with a payment on the order, that opens **Cancel payment**. Reload the app: as it starts, the till tries again to finish the order. If the order is still open, see [Finish an order after the card was charged](../../error-codes/PAYMENT121.mdx#troubleshoot).

**The store has not answered the last payment. Try again to confirm it; it will not be taken twice.** Don't take the card on your terminal again. Press **Try again** on this message to confirm the same payment.

<details>
<summary>Why the till asks you to try again</summary>

The till sent the payment but got no reply, so it can't tell whether the store recorded it. **Try again** sends the same payment, not a new one, and the store records it once. Until then the pane shows only this message, even after a restart.

</details>

**Open the register before completing the sale.** The register is closed. Open it, then press **Take** or **Send** again. See [Open the register](../register/index.mdx#open).

**Choose a register before completing the sale.** Your store has more than one register and this device hasn't picked one yet. Pick the register you're on. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#choosing-a-store-and-a-register).

**Take is greyed out.** Read the line under the amount. On a card, the amount is more than is due: tap **Full balance**.

**You took the wrong amount or method.** Press **Cancel payment** at the top of the pane. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).

**Payment failed: Card declined.** The reader didn't take the payment. Press **Try again**, or **Choose another way** to take it on another tile.
