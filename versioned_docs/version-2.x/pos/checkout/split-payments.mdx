One order can be paid in more than one go: part in cash and the rest on a card, or shared between friends. It stays one order, with every payment listed on it, until nothing is left to pay. This page starts from the tender pane; [Checkout](./index.mdx) walks through a whole sale.

- **Part in cash, the rest on a card** — [Take part of the payment](#part-payment).
- **Equal payments, or a set amount first** — [Split the order](#split).
- **Each customer pays for their own items** — [Split by item](#by-item).

## Take part of the payment {#part-payment}

When the customer pays some now and the rest another way, tap the tile they're paying with, type the part they're paying and press **Take**. You don't need to set anything up first. A card on your own terminal works as it does for a whole sale: take the card there first, then record it here. A paired reader takes the amount from the till; see [On a Card Reader](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[-36, 0], [-36, 0], [64, 0], [2, 0], [14, 0], [-70, 0]]} alt="The tender pane with $20.00 typed on a $104.00 order: the amount, the line saying what is left after this payment, the Split button, the Cash tile, the Take button and the remaining balance beside the order" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **The amount** — what this payment takes. Type it on the keypad.
2. **Part payment · … left after this** — what will still be due once you take it.
3. **Split** — plan the payments before you take them. See [Split the order](#split).
4. **Cash** — the payment method for this payment, outlined when chosen. Tap **Card** or another tile instead.
5. **Take** — names the amount, the payment method and what's left. Press it to record this payment.
6. **Remaining** — what's still due on the order.

</div>

After **Take**, the amount changes to what's left, $84.00 here, ready for the next payment. Pick the next tile, such as **Card**, and take the rest.

## Split the order {#split}

Press **Split** beside the amount to share the order out before you take anything. Choose how to divide it, and the tender pane lines up the payments for you.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[4, 0], [4, 0], [4, 0], [4, 0], [-10, -30], [-36, 0]]} alt="Split open on a $104.00 order: the Even, Amount, Percent and Item tabs, and tiles for two to six ways with the amount each" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Even** — the same amount for each payment. Tap **2 ways** up to **6 ways**.
2. **Amount** — the first payment is a set amount, and the rest is one more payment. See [By amount or percent](#amount).
3. **Percent** — the first payment is a share of the order, and the rest is one more payment.
4. **Item** — each customer pays for their own items. See [Split by item](#by-item).
5. **2 ways** — each tile shows what every payment would be. Tap one to choose it.
6. **✕** — back to the keypad without a split.

</div>

Each payment can be on a different payment method. The exception is a gateway that charges the whole order: once one payment is taken, its tile is greyed out (see [A gateway tile is greyed out](#greyed-gateway)). When the order doesn't divide exactly, the last payment takes whatever is left, so it can be a cent different from the others. Once you've chosen a split, [take each payment](#take-each).

### By amount or percent {#amount}

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[6, 0], [6, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The Amount tab of Split: tiles for a first payment of $5.00, $10.00, $20.00 or $50.00 with what is left after it, Half, and Type the first payment" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **$20.00 then $84.00** — the first payment, and what the second one will be.
2. **Half** — two payments of half the order each.
3. **Type the first payment** — enter any amount on the keypad. The rest comes after.

</div>

**Percent** works the same way, with tiles from **10 %** to **75 %** of the order.

## Take each payment {#take-each}

Once you've chosen a split, the tender pane takes you through the payments one at a time. Press **Take** for each, on whichever tile the customer is paying with.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[-36, 0], [-34, 0], [21, -36], [108, 0], [14, 0], [-36, 0], [195, 0], [-78, 0], [-36, 0]]} alt="An order split two ways with the first payment taken in cash: the payment count, a ticked cash payment and the next one, Change split, the Take button for 2 of 2, Cancel payment, the cash payment and the remaining balance beside the order, and the order's tab marked Partly paid" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Payment 2 of 2 · … left** — which payment you're on, and what's still due on the order.
2. **Cash $52.00**, ticked — a payment already taken, with its payment method.
3. **$52.00**, outlined — the payment you're taking now. Payments still to come after it read **then …**.
4. **Change split** — opens Split again, for what's left. Choose another split there, or **No split** to drop it.
5. **Take** — names the amount, the method, and which payment this is.
6. **Cancel payment** — undoes the payments taken so far. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
7. **Payments** — each payment taken on this order, with what was handed over and the change.
8. **Remaining** — what's still due.
9. **Partly paid · … due** — the order's tab in the open orders while money is still owed. See [Open Orders](../cart/open-orders.mdx).

</div>

When the last payment is taken, the sale is done and the receipt is ready, as for any sale. See [See it paid](./index.mdx#paid).

:::warning
Once a payment is taken, **✕** at the top of the tender pane works two ways:
- **In Split** — **✕** goes back to the keypad.
- **At the keypad** — **✕** asks **Cancel this payment?** Cancelling means the money already taken has to be returned to the customer. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
- **To serve another customer meanwhile** — press **+** at the end of the open orders instead. See [The customer leaves before paying the rest](#customer-leaves).
:::

## Split by item {#by-item}

When each customer pays for what they had, open **Split** and choose **Item**. Tick this customer's items and press **Pay for these**. Then pick their payment method and press **Take**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[308, 0], [308, 0], [14, 0], [-14, -20]]} alt="The Item tab of Split: Aim Analog Watch ticked and Fusion Backpack not, the Pay for these button naming $45.00, and buttons to share the ticked items between two, three or four" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **A ticked item** — this customer is paying for it. Tick as many as they had.
2. **An item not ticked** — left for the next payment.
3. **Pay for these** — names what the ticked items come to. Press it, then take the payment.
4. **Share these between** — the ticked items paid in two, three or four even payments, such as a shared bottle.

</div>

After the payment, the tender pane moves on to **Rest of the order**. To take everything left in one go, pick the payment method and press **Take**. For the next customer's items, press **Pick next items**.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

### Less than planned {#less-than-planned}

If the customer hands over less than the planned payment, the tender pane says **Less than planned · … moves to the next payment**. Take it, and the next payment grows by that much.

### The customer wants to split it differently {#split-differently}

Press **Change split** at any time, and pick a new split for what's left. **No split** drops the plan, and the next payment is the whole balance.

### The customer leaves before paying the rest {#customer-leaves}

The payments already taken stay on the order. To serve the next customer meanwhile, press **+** at the end of the open orders: a new sale starts, and the order keeps its tab, marked **Partly paid · … due**. Tap that tab when the customer is back, and the tender pane opens again on what's left. See [Open Orders](../cart/open-orders.mdx). **✕** from the keypad of a partly paid order doesn't put it aside: it asks **Cancel this payment?**, because the money taken has to go back to the customer. Press **Keep taking payment** to stay. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).

### A gateway tile is greyed out {#greyed-gateway}

A gateway that charges the whole order, such as [Email Invoice](../../payment/gateways/email-invoice.mdx), says **Takes the whole order, so not once a payment has been taken**. The same goes for every gateway on [the Legacy tab](./legacy-tab.mdx). Take the rest on another tile, or cancel the payments taken so far to use one.
