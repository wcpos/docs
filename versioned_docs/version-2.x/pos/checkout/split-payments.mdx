One order can be paid in more than one go: part in cash and the rest on a card, or shared between friends. It stays one order, with every payment listed on it, until nothing is left to pay. This page picks up at the tender pane; [Checkout](./index.mdx) walks through a whole sale.

- **Part now, the rest another way** — [Take part of the payment](#part-payment).
- **Equal payments, or a set amount first** — [Split the order](#split).
- **Each customer pays for their own items** — [Pay by item](#by-item).
- **One payment was wrong** — [Cancel one payment](#cancel-one).

## Take part of the payment {#part-payment}

When the customer pays some now and the rest another way, tap the tile they're paying with, type the part they're paying and press **Take**. You don't need to set anything up first: the line under the amount reads **Part payment · … left after this**. After **Take**, the amount changes to what's left, ready for the next payment: pick the next tile and take the rest.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={12} offsets={[[-42, 0], [-40, 0], [70, 0], [-4, 0], [0, 0], [-40, 0]]} alt="A part payment with Split off: $50.00 typed on Cash, the line Part payment, $144.00 left after this, the Take button naming what is left, and the bill with $194.00 remaining">

1. **$50.00** — the part the customer is paying now.
2. **Part payment · $144.00 left after this** — what's still owed once this is taken.
3. **Split** — leave it off for a part payment.
4. **Cash** — the tile they're paying with.
5. **Take $50.00 in Cash · $144.00 left** — takes the part.
6. **Remaining** — the order's balance, until the part is taken.

</JourneyShot>

A card on your own terminal works as it does for a whole sale: take the card there first, then record it here. A paired reader takes the amount from the till; see [On a Card Reader](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader). While the reader is still taking a payment, **✕** leaves checkout and the reader carries on; nothing is cancelled.

:::warning
Once a payment is taken, **✕** doesn't put the sale aside. It asks **Cancel this payment?**, because money already taken has to go back to the customer. Press **Keep taking payment** to stay. See [The customer leaves before paying the rest](#customer-leaves).
:::

## Split the order {#split}

Press **Split** beside the amount. The tender pane shows a row of ways to split what's left, and the bill beside it becomes the list you pick items from.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[70, 0], [56, -46], [90, -46], [56, -46], [40, -18], [0, 0]]} alt="Checkout with Split on: the Split chip, the row of Percent and 2 ways to 6 ways tiles with the amount each, the items on the bill ready to pick, and the Take button">

1. **Split** — turns splitting on. Press it again to turn it off and drop the plan.
2. **2 ways** — two equal payments. Each tile names what every payment would be.
3. **3 ways** — and so on up to **6 ways**. The row scrolls sideways when the tiles don't fit.
4. **Percent** — take a share of what's left. See [A percent of the rest](#percent).
5. **An item on the bill** — tap it to pay for that item. See [Pay by item](#by-item).
6. **Take** — names the amount, the method and what this payment is.

</JourneyShot>

Each payment can be on a different payment method. When the order doesn't divide exactly, the shares are rounded and the last payment takes what is left, so one can be a cent different from the others. A gateway that charges the whole order can't take a part; see [A gateway is not available](#greyed-gateway).

Tap a tile, then [take each payment](#take-each).

### A set amount first {#amount}

Type the first payment on the keypad before you choose the tiles. The row then reads **After this payment · …**, and each tile shares what's left after it. Tap one: the typed amount comes first, then the rest in that many equal payments.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[-30, 0], [154, -50], [0, 0]]} alt="Close-up of $50.00 typed with Split on: the amount, the row of ways now headed After this payment with what remains, and the 2 ways tile with what each of the rest would be">

1. **The amount** — the first payment.
2. **After this payment · …** — what's left to share once it's taken.
3. **2 ways** — the rest in two equal payments after the first.

</JourneyShot>

To take only the amount typed, with no plan, press **Take** instead of a tile. With a tile, [take each payment](#take-each) in turn.

### A percent of the rest {#percent}

Tap **Percent**, then **10 %**, **20 %**, **25 %** or **50 %**, or **Type** to enter your own share. The amount becomes that share of what's left. Take it as one payment, then split or take the rest.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[18, 30], [70, 0], [-30, 0], [-32, 0]]} alt="Close-up of Percent of the rest: the 10, 20, 25 and 50 percent chips and Type, with 25 % chosen and the amount it comes to">

1. **25 %** — a quarter of what's left.
2. **Type** — enter any other share on the keypad.
3. **The amount** — the share, worked out for you.
4. **The share** — the percent and what it's a percent of.

</JourneyShot>

After **Take**, tap a tile to split what's left and [take each payment](#take-each), or take the rest in one.

## Take each payment {#take-each}

Once you've chosen a split, the tender pane takes you through the payments one at a time, with the next amount typed for you. Press **Take** for each, on whichever tile the customer is paying with. On a card reader the button reads **Send … to …**, and the reader takes that part.

Here the first customer has already paid $45.00 for their watch (see [Pay by item](#by-item)), and the rest is split three ways.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[-36, 0], [-26, 0], [33, -32], [33, -32], [33, -32], [110, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The rest split three ways after a first payment: Payment 2 of 4 with what is left, the paid payment, the payment now outlined and the two to come dimmed, Change split, and the Take button naming the payment's number">

1. **Payment 2 of 4 · … left** — which payment you're on, and what's still due. The first payment counts, so three ways after it makes four.
2. **Cash $45.00**, ticked — the payment already taken.
3. **The payment now**, outlined — its amount is typed for you.
4. **The next payment**, dimmed.
5. **The last payment**, dimmed — a cent less here, because the rest doesn't divide exactly.
6. **Change split** — back to the row of ways, for what's left.
7. **Take** — names the amount, the method, and which payment this is.

</JourneyShot>

### When they hand over more than their share {#cash-over}

A customer paying cash can hand over more than their share. In the next two shots the same $194.00 order is split three ways from the start, and the first customer hands over $100.00 for a $64.67 share. Type what they gave you on the keypad: the change becomes the big figure, and **Take** records only their share.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={7} offsets={[[-46, 0], [-40, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="A cash payment in a split with $100.00 handed over: the change to give as the headline, what was tendered under it, and the Take button for the share">

1. **Change** — what to give back.
2. **Tendered** — what the customer handed over.
3. **Take** — records the planned share, not the cash handed over.

</JourneyShot>

### The last payment {#last-payment}

The last payment's button reads **… of …** like the others, here **3 of 3**. When it's taken, the sale is done and the receipt is ready, as for any sale. See [See it paid](./index.mdx#paid).

:::warning
The change on the paid screen was already given with the payment it came from. Don't give it again.
:::

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={11} offsets={[[-22, 0], [-32, 0]]} alt="Close-up of the paid screen after three cash payments: Change $35.33 as the headline, and under it Paid $194.00, tendered $229.33 in cash, 3 payments">

1. **Change $35.33** — the change from the first customer's $100.00, shown again. With no change in the sale, this reads **Paid** and the order's total.
2. **Paid … · tendered … in cash · 3 payments** — the order's total, all the cash handed over, and the number of payments. Paid by more than one method, it names each method once, such as **Cash + Card**.

</JourneyShot>

Press **New sale** for an empty cart for the next customer.

## Pay by item {#by-item}

When each customer pays for what they had, the bill is the list. With **Split** on, tap an item's name to pay for every unit of it still unpaid. Use **−** and **+** to pay for fewer or more of a line, such as one of two coffees. Then pick the payment method and press **Take**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[40, -18], [-40, 34], [42, 40], [33, -32], [43, -32], [0, 0]]} alt="Paying by item: one of two Aim Analog Watches picked on the bill, the This payment column with its stepper, the picked item named above the keypad, Share, + amount, and the Take button">

1. **The item** — tap the name to pay for all of it that's left.
2. **1 of 2** — how many of this line this payment covers. **−** and **+** change it.
3. **1 Aim Analog Watch** — what this payment covers. **✕** on it takes the item off.
4. **Share** — the picked items paid in two, three or four equal payments, such as a shared bottle.
5. **+ amount** — adds an amount you type on top of the items.
6. **Take** — names the items' total, the method, and what's left after it.

</JourneyShot>

On a phone, there's no room for the bill beside the keypad. Press **Pick items** to show the list, pick there, and press **Back to keypad**.

After **Take**, pick the next customer's items the same way, or split what's left and [take each payment](#take-each).

### Items already paid for {#paid-items}

Once a payment covers an item, the bill marks it with how many units are paid and how. A paid unit can't be picked again, so the next customer only sees what's left. The marks are saved on the order, so they're still there if the order is reopened later or on another till.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={13} offsets={[[45, 0], [7, -31], [47, 0]]} alt="Close-up of the bill after one of two watches was paid in cash: the watch line marked 1 of 2 paid, Cash, and its stepper at 0 of 1">

1. **1 of 2 paid · Cash** — how many units are paid, and how.
2. **0 of 1** — what's left of the item to pick; the paid watch can't be picked again.
3. **Aim Analog Watch** — tap the name to pick what's left of it.

</JourneyShot>

The tender pane moves on to the next payment:

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[0, 0], [-36, 0], [-36, 0], [166, 0], [90, -42], [-34, 0]]} alt="After the first payment: one of two watches marked paid in cash on the bill, the paid payment above the keypad, Payment 2 with what is left, and the Legacy tab marked Whole bill only">

1. **1 of 2 paid · Cash** — units of this item already paid for, and how.
2. **Cash $45.00**, ticked — the payment taken. Tap it to see or cancel it. See [Cancel one payment](#cancel-one).
3. **Payment 2 · … left** — which payment you're on, and what's still due.
4. **Whole bill only** — the Legacy tab (WCPOS Pro) can't take a part. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).
5. **3 ways** — split what's left, as before.
6. **Partly paid · … due** — the order's tab in the open orders while money is still owed. See [Open Orders](../cart/open-orders.mdx).

</JourneyShot>

## Cancel one payment {#cancel-one}

If one payment was wrong, such as the wrong method or the wrong amount, cancel that one and keep the others. Tap the paid pill above the keypad to list every payment taken, with the items each one paid for. The till stays in checkout.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={8} offsets={[[-6, 0], [-6, 0], [-38, 0], [-28, 0], [-36, 0]]} alt="The list of payments taken on the order, each with its method, amount and items, a Cancel payment button on each, and Hide">

1. **The first payment** — its method, amount and the items it paid for.
2. **The last payment** — each payment has its own row.
3. **Cancel payment** on a row — cancels this payment only.
4. **Hide** — closes the list.
5. **Cancel payment** at the top — cancels every payment taken, not just one. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).

</JourneyShot>

Press **Cancel payment** on the row of the one that was wrong. The till asks **Cancel this payment?** first:

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={9} offsets={[[70, 44], [40, 44]]} alt="Cancel this payment? asking to return $49.67 cash to the customer, with Cancel and Cancel payment">

1. **Cancel payment** — cancels it. For cash, give the money back to the customer.
2. **Cancel** — keeps the payment.

</JourneyShot>

For cash, it reads **Return … cash to the customer**. For other methods, see [Cancel one payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx#cancel-one) on Cancelling a Payment. Once it's cancelled, the order lists it as **Cancelled**, its items are unmarked, and what's left is split again over the payments to come.

To cancel every payment taken and leave the sale unpaid, press **Cancel payment** at the top of checkout instead. It lists what has to go back to the customer before you confirm with **Cancel and void**. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

### Less than planned {#less-than-planned}

If the customer hands over less than the planned payment, the line under the amount says **Less than planned · … moves to the next payment**, or to the payments after it. Take it, and the payments still to come share what's missing.

### The customer wants to split it differently {#split-differently}

Press **Change split** and choose a new split for what's left. Payments already taken stay as they are. To drop the plan, press **Split** to turn it off.

### The customer leaves before paying the rest {#customer-leaves}

The payments already taken stay on the order. To serve the next customer meanwhile, press **+** at the end of the open orders: a new sale starts, and the order keeps its tab, marked **Partly paid · … due**. Tap that tab when the customer is back, and checkout opens again on what's left, with the paid items still marked. See [Open Orders](../cart/open-orders.mdx).

### A gateway is not available {#greyed-gateway}

A gateway that charges the whole order, such as [Email Invoice](../../payment/gateways/email-invoice.mdx), can't take a part. Once a payment is taken, it leaves the tiles and is listed under **… not available right now**. Open that line to see why: **Takes the whole order, so not once a payment has been taken**. The [Legacy tab](./legacy-tab.mdx) (WCPOS Pro) reads **Whole bill only** for the same reason. Take the rest on another tile, or [cancel the payments taken](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx) to use one.
