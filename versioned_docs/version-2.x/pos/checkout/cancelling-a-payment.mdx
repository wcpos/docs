The customer has paid part of the order and then changes their mind. **Cancel payment** undoes the payments taken so far, tells you what to hand back, and returns the order to the cart. This page walks through it with $20.00 cash taken on a $45.00 order.

To undo only one payment and keep taking the rest, see [Cancel one payment](#cancel-one). A sale that's already finished is undone with a refund instead. See [Refunds at the Till](../refunds.mdx).

## Open Cancel payment {#open}

**Cancel payment** appears at the top of the tender pane once a payment has been taken on the order.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[-32, 0], [185, -14], [-50, 0], [10, 40], [-34, 0]]} alt="The tender pane after $20.00 cash was taken on a $45.00 order: the Cancel payment button, the cash payment marked Captured, $25.00 remaining, the close button and the Cash $20.00 pill above the keypad">

1. **Cancel payment** — opens the cancel view for every payment on this order.
2. **Payment** — what was taken so far, with its method and status.
3. **Remaining** — what's still due.
4. **Close** — with a payment already taken, this opens the same cancel view instead of leaving checkout. While a card reader is still working on a payment, it leaves checkout without cancelling anything.
5. **Cash $20.00** — the payments taken so far. Press it to cancel one of them on its own; see [Cancel one payment](#cancel-one).

</JourneyShot>

While a card reader is still working on a payment, **Cancel payment** isn't offered. Press **Cancel on terminal** in the pane, or cancel on the reader itself when the till says **Cancel on the reader**; see [On a Card Reader](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).

## Check what goes back {#check}

The till lists what has to happen to each payment before anything is undone.

:::warning Keep the cash until the cancel goes through
Cancelling needs a connection to your store. If the till stays in checkout with a message, at least one payment wasn't undone and still counts. Keep the cash until the cancel succeeds; see [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong).
:::

Each line says one of three things:

- **Return** the amount in cash to the customer.
- **Void** the amount on its payment method.
- The amount is **already taken and cannot be voided**: refund it from the order. This is usually a card payment already charged to the customer; ask someone with WP Admin access to refund it. See [A payment can't be voided](#goes-wrong-taken).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[-26, 0], [-22, 0], [156, 0]]} alt="The Cancel this payment view: one line saying to return $20.00 cash to the customer, with the Keep taking payment and Cancel and void buttons">

1. **What goes back** — one line per payment. Here, $20.00 cash to hand back.
2. **Keep taking payment** — goes back to the tender pane. Nothing changes.
3. **Cancel and void** — undoes the payments.

</JourneyShot>

## Cancel and void {#cancel}

Press **Cancel and void**. When every payment is cancelled, the till leaves checkout and you're back in the cart. The order is still open, with everything still in it. Once you see the cart, hand back the cash the view listed.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[60, -36], [270, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The cart after the payment was cancelled: the same customer and product, and the Checkout button for the full $45.00">

1. **Customer** — the order keeps its customer.
2. **Checkout** — names the full total again. Press it to take a new payment.
3. **Order actions** — **Void** is here, if the customer no longer wants the order. See [Void the order](../cart/order-actions.mdx#void).

</JourneyShot>

Open checkout again and the cancelled payment is still on the order, struck through and marked **Cancelled**. It no longer counts: **Paid** is back to $0.00 and the whole amount is due.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[210, -14], [192, -1], [-45, 0]]} alt="The tender pane reopened on the same order: the $20.00 cash payment struck through and marked Cancelled, Paid $0.00 and Remaining $45.00">

1. **Cancelled** — the cancelled payment stays on the order's record so you can see what happened.
2. **Paid** — back to $0.00. A cancelled payment no longer counts.
3. **Remaining** — the full $45.00 is due again.

</JourneyShot>

## Cancel one payment {#cancel-one}

When one payment is wrong but the rest should stand, cancel just that one, for example one part of a [split bill](./split-payments.mdx). The sale stays in checkout and that amount is due again. Here, $20.00 and then $10.00 cash were taken on the $45.00 order, and the $10.00 is cancelled.

Press the pill above the keypad that shows what's been paid (**2 paid · $30.00**). It opens the list of payments taken.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[170, 0], [-34, 0], [-36, 0]]} alt="The tender pane with the paid pill opened: the $20.00 and $10.00 cash payments listed, each with its own Cancel payment button, and Hide below">

1. **Payment** — one row per payment taken. On a split bill it also names the items that payment covered.
2. **Cancel payment** — cancels this payment only.
3. **Hide** — folds the list away again.

</JourneyShot>

Press **Cancel payment** on the row. The till asks **Cancel this payment?** and names what goes back, in the same words as the [full cancel](#check): here, **Return $10.00 cash to the customer**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[-36, 0], [101, -40]]} alt="The Cancel this payment? question for the $10.00 cash payment, with Cancel and Cancel payment buttons">

1. **Cancel** — closes the question. Nothing changes.
2. **Cancel payment** — undoes this payment.

</JourneyShot>

While the cancel goes through, the pane can briefly say **The store has not answered the last payment**. Wait a few seconds for it to clear rather than pressing **Try again**.

The till says **Payment cancelled** and stays in checkout. The payment stays on the order, struck through and marked **Cancelled**, and only the payments left count towards **Paid**. Hand back the cash, then take the rest. On a split bill, the items the cancelled payment covered can be paid for again.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={7} offsets={[[210, -14], [192, -1], [-45, 0]]} alt="The tender pane after the $10.00 payment was cancelled: the $10.00 row struck through and marked Cancelled, Paid $20.00 and Remaining $25.00">

1. **Cancelled** — the $10.00 payment, kept on the record.
2. **Paid** — only the $20.00 still counts.
3. **Remaining** — $25.00 is due again.

</JourneyShot>

If the payment can't be cancelled, the till shows one of the messages below and the payment still counts.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

### You're offline {#goes-wrong-offline}

Reconnect to your store, then cancel again. Nothing was cancelled. See [What needs a connection](../../getting-started/offline.mdx#what-requires-a-connection).

The till says: **Cancelling these payments needs the store. Nothing was cancelled; try again when you are online.**

### A reader payment hasn't settled {#goes-wrong-reader}

Wait until the till is back online and the reader payment has reached the store, then cancel. The till shows this when a reader payment was approved offline, or when the till is offline with a reader payment on the order.

The till says: **This reader payment must settle before it can be cancelled.**

### Some payments weren't voided {#goes-wrong-not-voided}

Cancel again. Keep the cash until it goes through.

The till says: **Some payments couldn't be voided, so that money is still held. Try again, or refund it from the order.** The error code is [PAYMENT221](../../error-codes/PAYMENT221.mdx) (the store refused) or [PAYMENT201](../../error-codes/PAYMENT201.mdx) (the till couldn't tell whether it went through).

### A payment can't be voided {#goes-wrong-taken}

Ask someone with WP Admin access to refund this payment there. The till only offers **Refund** on completed, processing or on-hold orders, and this sale stays open. See [When the Refund Action Appears](../../orders/refunds.mdx#when-the-refund-action-appears). While the order still holds the payment, **Cancelled** isn't offered as its status either.

The till says: **Money already taken cannot be voided, so this sale stays open: refund it from the order.** It's a card payment already charged to the customer, or one the store counted towards completing the sale.

### Void won't remove the order {#goes-wrong-void}

Open checkout, cancel the payment, then void the order.

The till says: **This order has payments on it. Reverse them with Cancel in the tender pane, then void.**
