description: "Undo the payments taken so far on an order that isn't finished, and see what has to go back to the customer."
---

**Cancel payment** undoes the payments taken so far on this order. It appears at the top of the tender pane once a payment has been taken. While a payment is still running on a card reader it isn't offered; cancel on the reader instead.

Before anything is undone, the till lists what has to happen to each payment:

- **Return** the amount in cash to the customer.
- **Void** the amount on its payment method.
- A payment that's **already taken and can't be voided** is refunded from the order instead.

Press **Cancel and void** to go ahead, or **Keep taking payment** to go back.

Cancelling needs the store to be reachable; offline, nothing is cancelled and the till says so. The order stays open, and the cancelled payments stay on its record as voided.

If money already taken can't be voided, the sale stays open and the till tells you to refund it from the order.

A payment on a completed sale is undone with a [refund](../../orders/refunds.mdx) instead.
