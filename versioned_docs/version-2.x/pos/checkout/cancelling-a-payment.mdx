The customer has paid part of the order and then changes their mind. **Cancel payment** undoes the payments taken so far, tells you what to hand back, and returns the order to the cart. This page walks through it with $20.00 cash taken on a $45.00 order.

A sale that's already finished is undone with a refund instead. See [Refunds at the Till](../refunds.mdx).

## Open Cancel payment {#open}

**Cancel payment** appears at the top of the tender pane once a payment has been taken on the order.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[-30, 0], [100, -11], [-50, 0], [22, 40]]} alt="The tender pane after $20.00 cash was taken on a $45.00 order: the Cancel payment button, the cash payment marked Captured, $25.00 remaining and the close button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Cancel payment** — opens the cancel view for every payment on this order.
2. **Payment** — what was taken so far, with its method and status.
3. **Remaining** — what's still due.
4. **Close** — with a payment already taken, this opens the same cancel view instead of leaving checkout.

</div>

While a card reader is still working on a payment, **Cancel payment** isn't offered. Cancel on the reader instead; see [On a Card Reader](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).

## Check what goes back {#check}

The till lists what has to happen to each payment before anything is undone. Each line says one of three things:

- **Return** the amount in cash to the customer.
- **Void** the amount on its payment method.
- The amount is **already taken and cannot be voided**: refund it from the order. This is usually a card payment the store has already captured.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[-22, 0], [-22, 0], [156, 0]]} alt="The Cancel this payment view: one line saying to return $20.00 cash to the customer, with the Keep taking payment and Cancel and void buttons" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **What goes back** — one line per payment. Here, $20.00 cash to hand back.
2. **Keep taking payment** — goes back to the tender pane. Nothing changes.
3. **Cancel and void** — undoes the payments.

</div>

## Cancel and void {#cancel}

Press **Cancel and void**. The till leaves checkout and you're back in the cart. The order is still open, with everything still in it. Now hand back the cash the view listed.

:::warning Keep the cash until the cancel goes through
Cancelling needs a connection to your store. If the till stays in checkout with a message, at least one payment wasn't undone and still counts. Keep the cash until the cancel succeeds; see [If something goes wrong](#goes-wrong).
:::

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[0, -36], [270, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The cart after the payment was cancelled: the same customer and product, and the Checkout button for the full $45.00" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Customer** — the order keeps its customer.
2. **Checkout** — names the full total again. Press it to take a new payment.
3. **Order actions** — **Void** is here, if the customer no longer wants the order. See [Void the order](../cart/order-actions.mdx#void).

</div>

Open checkout again and the cancelled payment is still on the order, marked **Voided**. It no longer counts: **Paid** is back to $0.00 and the whole amount is due.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[100, -11]]} alt="The tender pane reopened on the same order: the $20.00 cash payment is marked Voided and the full $45.00 is due" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Voided payment** — stays on the order's record so you can see what happened.

</div>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Cancelling these payments needs the store. Nothing was cancelled; try again when you are online.** Cancelling needs a connection to your store. Nothing was undone. Try again when you're back online. See [What needs a connection](../../getting-started/offline.mdx#what-requires-a-connection).

**This reader payment must settle before it can be cancelled.** A card reader payment that was approved offline hasn't reached the store yet. Wait until it does, then cancel.

**Some payments couldn't be voided, so that money is still held. Try again, or refund it from the order.** The store refused a void ([PAYMENT221](../../error-codes/PAYMENT221.mdx)) or the till couldn't tell whether it went through ([PAYMENT201](../../error-codes/PAYMENT201.mdx)). Press **Cancel and void** again.

**Money already taken cannot be voided, so this sale stays open: refund it from the order.** A card payment the store has already captured can't be voided, and neither can a payment the store has already counted towards completing the sale. The sale stays open.

**This order has payments on it. Reverse them with Cancel in the tender pane, then void.** You pressed **Void** in the cart while the order still has a payment on it. Open checkout, cancel the payment, then void the order.
