The tender pane opens when you press **Checkout**, and holds everything you need to take payment for the order. [Checkout](./index.mdx) walks through a whole sale.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Payments** tab — the payment method tiles and the payments taken so far, with how much of the total is paid.
2. **Legacy** tab (WCPOS Pro) — gateways that still run on their own payment page. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).
3. **To pay** — the balance still due on the order.
4. **Split** — divides the order into several payments. See [Split Payments](./split-payments.mdx).
5. **Payment method tiles** — the payment methods your store offers at the till, in the order set in [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx). See [Payment Methods](./payment-methods.mdx). A tile that can't be used right now is listed separately with the reason; see [Needs a Connection](./troubleshooting.mdx#needs-a-connection).
6. **Quick amounts** — buttons for the balance, rounded-up notes and the exact amount.
7. **Keypad** — enter what the customer hands over. For cash, the change is shown as you type.
8. **Take** button — names the amount and the payment method. See [Taking a Payment](./taking-a-payment.mdx).
9. **The order** — its lines, the payments taken, the total, how much is paid and what remains.

</div>

**✕** closes the tender pane and takes you back to the cart without completing the sale. The order stays open.

Once a payment has been taken, **Cancel payment** appears at the top of the pane. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
