Every WCPOS store has two payment methods at the till:

- **Cash** — enter the amount handed over; WCPOS shows the change due. Cash (and, with Pro, WooCommerce's cash on delivery) is the only method that gives change.
- **Card** — for a card terminal that isn't connected to the POS. Take the payment on the terminal first, then record it at the till. A card payment can't be more than the balance due.

With [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) you can enable any other WooCommerce gateway for the POS:

- **Recorded at the till** — WooCommerce's cash on delivery, bank transfer and cheque gateways work like Card: the cashier records the payment and nothing has to be confirmed by a server. The order takes the status set for that gateway in [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#order-status), which is **On hold** by default for bank transfer and cheque because the money arrives later.
- **Card readers** — terminal gateways driven from the tender pane. See [On a Card Reader](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
- **Pay-later gateways** — for example, [Email Invoice](../../payment/gateways/email-invoice.mdx) sends an invoice instead of taking money at the till. The order leaves the till as sent, not paid.
- **Everything else** — a gateway that hasn't been built for the tender pane runs on [the Legacy tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).

See [Payment Methods](../../payment/index.mdx) for setting up gateways.
