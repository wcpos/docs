The payment screen opens when you press **Checkout** in the cart. These docs also call it the tender pane. It holds everything you need to take payment for the order: what is due, how the customer is paying, and how much they hand over. [Checkout](./index.mdx) walks through a whole sale. Using a phone? See [On a phone](#phone).

## The payment screen {#layout}

Here the customer owes $45.00 and hands over a $50 note: choose **Cash**, type **5000** for $50.00, press **Take $45.00 in Cash**, and give back the **$5.00** change.

<JourneyShot manifest={tenderManifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[28, 42], [29, 42], [-46, 0], [58, 0], [-38, 0], [108, 0], [0, 0], [116, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [-50, 0], [-34, 0]]} alt="The tender pane for a $45.00 cash sale with $50.00 typed: the Payments and Legacy tabs, To pay, Split, the amount, the change line, the Cash and Card tiles, the quick amounts, the keypad and the Take button, with the order and its totals on the right and the close button at the top">

1. **Payments** tab — the payment method tiles, the keypad and the payments taken so far.
2. **Legacy** tab — payment methods that run on their own payment page. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).
3. **To pay** — the balance due on the order. After a payment it reads **Remaining**.
4. **Split** — divides the order into several payments. See [Split Payments](./split-payments.mdx).
5. **Amount** — for cash, what the customer hands over; otherwise what this payment is for. It starts at the balance. **Take** records no more than is due, and cash over it is change. See [Type the amount](#keypad).
6. **Change** — the change to give, shown as you type. Other notes appear here too; see [The line under the amount](#hint).
7. **Payment method tiles** — the ways your store takes payment at the till. The selected tile is outlined. For **Card**, take the payment on your own terminal first; **Take** only records it. See [Choose a payment method](#choose).
8. **Quick amounts** — the balance, the balance rounded up to the next $5, $10 and $50, and **Exact**. A card gets **Full balance** instead.
9. **Keypad** — enter the cash handed over, or the amount to pay by another method.
10. **Take** — names the amount and the method, and records the payment. See [Taking a Payment](./taking-a-payment.mdx).
11. **The order** — its lines, the taxes, the payments taken, and the total, the paid amount and what remains.
12. **✕** — closes the tender pane. See [Close the tender pane](#close).

</JourneyShot>

When the customer hands over more cash than is due, **Take** records only the amount due. Here it records $45.00, and the $5.00 over it is the change.

When nothing is left to pay, the sale is done and the receipt is ready; see [See it paid](./index.mdx#paid).

## Type the amount {#keypad}

The amount starts at the balance. The first key you press starts a new number, and each digit comes in from the right, so **5**, **0**, **0**, **0** makes $50.00. There is no decimal point key.

- **C** clears the amount.
- **⌫** deletes the last digit.
- A quick amount fills the amount in one tap. It does not take the payment; you still press **Take**.

Type less than the balance to take part of it now: with $20.00 typed, the button reads **Take $20.00 in Cash · $25.00 left**.

### The line under the amount {#hint}

A short line under the amount says what this payment will do: the change to give, or what is left after it.

<details>
<summary>What each message means</summary>

| It says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Change $5.00** | The customer handed over more cash than is due. Give back this much. |
| **Part payment · $25.00 left after this** | This payment is less than the balance. The rest stays to pay. |
| **Only $45.00 is due — Card can't give change** | The amount is more than the balance, on a method that gives no change. **Take** stays greyed out until you lower the amount or tap **Full balance**. |
| **Less than planned · $5.00 moves to the next payment** | In a split, this payment is short of its share. The difference goes on the next one. |
| **Rounded from $45.03** | Cash rounding is on, and the cash due is rounded. See [Cash rounding](../../payment/index.mdx#cash-rounding). |
| **Enter** and the name of a detail | The payment method needs that detail before it can take the payment. |

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why Take is greyed out</summary>

- No payment method is chosen. Tap a tile.
- The amount is zero. Type an amount or tap a quick amount.
- The amount is more than is due, on a method that can't give change. The line says **Only $45.00 is due — Card can't give change**. Lower the amount or tap **Full balance**.
- The line says **Enter** and the name of a detail. Fill in that detail.
- A card reader shows **Choose a terminal**. Pick a terminal that is free.
- The method can't be used right now. See [Why a payment method is unavailable](#choose).
- The last payment is still saving. Wait for it to finish.

</details>

## Choose a payment method {#choose}

Each tile on the **Payments** tab of checkout is a payment method your store has turned on for the POS. **Cash** and **Card** come with the plugin. The tiles follow the order set in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#reordering-gateways).

When the pane opens, the method you last picked for this order is selected, or else the first one that can be used. If you picked a method for this sale and switched to another open order, it's still selected when you come back. Tap another tile to change it.

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, the till won't record a payment while the register is closed, and a card you've already taken would have to be refunded. See [Open the Register](../register/index.mdx#open).
:::

For Card, charge no more than the balance on your separate terminal, then enter the same amount here.

1. Tap the tile for how the customer is paying.
2. Enter the amount: for Cash, what the customer hands over; for Card, what you charged on the terminal. The amount starts at the balance, and the keypad types in cents, so **1**, **0**, **0** is $1.00.
3. Press **Take**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[0, 34], [0, 34], [-40, 0], [-50, 0], [-42, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [-115, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The Payments tab of checkout with Cash selected and $50.00 entered against $45.00 to pay: the change line, the Cash and Card tiles, the quick amounts and the Take $45.00 in Cash button">

1. **Payments** — choose how to take or record the customer's payment.
2. **Legacy** — your store's own payment page inside the POS. It offers Cash and Card on every store, plus any payment method that has no tile; those other methods need WCPOS Pro. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).
3. **To pay** — the balance of the order. After a part payment it reads **Remaining**.
4. **Amount** — what the customer hands over.
5. **Change** — what to give back. Only cash methods give change.
6. **Cash** — the selected tile has a coloured border.
7. **Card** — a card payment taken on a terminal that isn't connected to the POS. You record it here.
8. **Quick amounts** — the row of chips: the balance, the next round amounts above it, and **Exact $45.00**. The amount chips appear only for methods that give change.
9. **Take $45.00 in Cash** — records the payment. When the balance reaches zero, the sale completes and the paid screen opens; with **Automatically show receipt after checkout** off, a message names the order instead.

</JourneyShot>

- **Cash** and cash on delivery give change, so they get the quick amounts and the **Change** line.
- **Card**, and the other methods you record at the till, can't give change. They get **Full balance** instead of the quick amounts. On your own card terminal, take the card payment first, then record it here: **Take** does not charge the card.
- A card reader has its own tile, and **Send** instead of **Take**. See [On a Card Reader](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).

<details>
<summary>Using a card reader</summary>

A reader's tile shows a green dot when the reader is connected, and its battery on the selected tile. When the readers are set up on your store, the pane shows **Terminal:** and the reader in use, with **Change** when there's more than one.

</details>

Payment methods you can't use right now appear in a line below the tiles, such as **Card is not available right now** or **2 not available right now**. Tap the line to see why.

<details>
<summary>Why a payment method is unavailable</summary>

- It needs a connection, and the device is offline. See [Needs a Connection](./troubleshooting.mdx#needs-a-connection).
- The app needs an update to use it.
- The payment method has no terminals set up.
- Its reader is in use on another order.
- It takes the whole order, and a payment has already been taken.

More in [Unavailable tiles](../../payment/index.mdx#unavailable-tiles).

</details>

Payment methods other than Cash and Card need [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).

## Methods that can't give change {#no-change}

Methods that don't give change, including Card, take no more than the balance. Enter more and the POS says so and won't take it.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[0, 0], [-50, 0], [-44, 0], [-34, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="Card selected with $50.00 entered: the line Only $45.00 is due — Card can't give change, the Full balance chip and the Take $45.00 in Card button greyed out">

1. **Card** — selected.
2. **Amount** — $50.00, more than the $45.00 due.
3. **Only $45.00 is due — Card can't give change** — enter the balance or less.
4. **Full balance $45.00** — puts the balance back in the amount.
5. **Take $45.00 in Card** — greyed out until the amount fits.

</JourneyShot>

## Paying part of the balance {#part-payment}

1. Enter less than the balance. The line under the amount reads **Part payment · $44.00 left after this**, and the button says what will be left, such as **Take $1.00 in Card · $44.00 left**.
2. Press **Take**. The label now reads **Remaining**, with what's still owed.
3. Choose the method for the rest and take it. When it clears the balance, the button adds **· pays it off**.

Here $20.00 has been taken in cash, and $25.00 is left.

<JourneyShot manifest={tenderManifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[-34, 0], [-46, 0], [-38, 0], [-2, -18], [0, 0], [130, -16]]} alt="The tender pane after a $20.00 cash payment: Cancel payment at the top, Remaining $25.00, the Cash and Card tiles, the Take button that pays it off, and the cash payment listed as Captured in the order">

1. **Cancel payment** — appears once a payment has been taken. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
2. **Remaining** — the label once part of the order is paid.
3. **Amount** — starts at what is left.
4. **Another tile** — the rest can go on a different method: tap **Card** and the button reads **Take $25.00 in Card · pays it off**.
5. **Take** — **· pays it off** means this payment clears the order.
6. **The payment** — the method, the amount and its state, such as **Captured**. For cash it shows what was tendered and the change.

</JourneyShot>

Until the order is paid in full it stays open in the cart, marked **Partly paid · $25.00 due**. See [Open Orders](../cart/open-orders.mdx).

To plan the parts up front instead, press **Split**. See [Split Payments](./split-payments.mdx).

## What the button does {#the-button}

The button names the method and what will happen:

- **Take … in Cash** — records money you've taken yourself. Cash and Card work this way.
- **Send … to** a card reader — sends the amount to a connected reader. See [Taking a Payment](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
- The method's own words — some methods name their own action, such as sending an invoice.

With no tile selected, the button reads **Choose how the customer is paying**.

## Taking a payment offline {#offline}

:::warning Only a sale the store already knows
Methods you record yourself work offline only on a sale that reached your store before the connection dropped: its order number shows above the items. Card readers and online payment methods need a connection.
:::

When the POS loses its connection, **Offline** appears in the register bar and above the amount, next to **To pay**. You can still record payments taken at the till, such as cash or a card on your own terminal: their tiles say **Sent later** and **Store may refuse**. The payment is recorded on the device and sent when the store is reachable. A card reader or another method that needs a connection moves to the **not available right now** line until you are back online. A sale started while offline is the exception: until it reaches your store, every tile is under **not available right now**, Cash and Card included. See [Take a payment offline](../../getting-started/offline.mdx#take-payment-offline).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[50, 0], [225, 0], [-21, 34], [109, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="Checkout offline: Offline next to To pay and in the register bar, the order number above the items, and Sent later, Store may refuse under the Cash and Card tiles">

1. **Offline** — the POS can't reach your store.
2. **Order number** — the sale has reached your store, so it can be paid offline.
3. **Sent later · Store may refuse** — a cash payment waits on this device until the store is back, and the store can still turn the sale down when it arrives.
4. **Card** — the same for a card payment you record.
5. **Take $45.00 in Cash** — asks you to confirm first.

</JourneyShot>

Press the button and the POS asks **Take $45.00 in Cash while offline?** Confirm, and the sale is held on this device and sent when the store is back. If the store refuses it, the sale appears under **Needs attention** in Orders.

## Close the tender pane {#close}

**✕** goes back one step:

- With no payment taken, it closes the pane and takes you back to the cart. The order stays open.
- With a payment taken, it asks **Cancel this payment?** first. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
- In a split, it closes the split first.

In a browser, **Esc** does the same, unless you are typing in a field. On Android, so does the back button.

An order with nothing to pay, such as a fully discounted one, shows a single **Done** button instead of the keypad.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Take is greyed out.** Choose a tile, enter an amount above zero, or open [Why Take is greyed out](#hint).

**"Card is not available right now", or "2 not available right now".** The POS folds methods it can't use at the moment under one line. Tap it to see why each one is unavailable. See [Unavailable tiles](../../payment/index.mdx#unavailable-tiles).

**"No payment methods are enabled for the POS."** No payment method is turned on for the POS. A manager turns one on in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

**"Only … is due — Card can't give change".** You entered more than the balance on a method that gives no change. Enter the balance. If the customer is paying in cash, tap **Cash** instead.

**"Open the register before completing the sale".** The register is closed. See [Open the Register](../register/index.mdx#open).

**A payment method you turned on has no tile.** See [Gateway not appearing at checkout](../../payment/index.mdx#gateway-not-appearing-at-checkout).

**"The store isn't answering".** The order is still saving. Keep waiting, or go back to the cart. See [Slow store](./troubleshooting.mdx#slow-store).

**"Order not saved — the store refused it".** Open **Store health** from the message to see why.

**"The store has not answered the last payment".** Press **Try again** to confirm it. It will not be taken twice.

More fixes are in [Checkout Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.mdx).

## More payment methods with WCPOS Pro {#pro-methods}

:::info With WCPOS Pro
The free plugin turns on Cash and Card only. The methods below need WCPOS Pro. See [Pro license](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).
:::

Pro adds the other payment methods on your store:

- WooCommerce's **cash on delivery**, **bank transfer** and **cheque** methods get a tile and are recorded like Card. Cash on delivery gives change, like Cash. The order takes the status set for that method in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#order-status): **On hold** by default for bank transfer and cheque, because the money arrives later.
- **Card readers** get a tile. With it selected, the button reads **Send … to** and sends the amount to the reader. See [Taking a Payment](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
- **Email Invoice** sends the customer an invoice to pay later. See [Email Invoice](../../payment/gateways/email-invoice.mdx).
- Every other payment method is on the **Legacy** tab. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).

Turn payment methods on in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#payment-gateways).
