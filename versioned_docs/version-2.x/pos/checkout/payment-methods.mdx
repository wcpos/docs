Each tile on the **Payments** tab of checkout is a payment method your store has turned on for the POS. **Cash** and **Card** come with the plugin. The tiles follow the order set in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#reordering-gateways).

When checkout opens, the method already saved on the order is selected. If the order has none, the first tile is.

## Choose a payment method {#choose}

Tap a tile, enter what the customer hands over, and press the button at the bottom. The amount starts at the balance. The keypad types in cents, so **1**, **0**, **0** is $1.00.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Payments** — the methods the POS takes itself.
2. **Legacy** — your store's own payment page, for gateways that have no tile. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).
3. **To pay** — the balance of the order. After a part payment it reads **Remaining**.
4. **Amount** — what the customer hands over.
5. **Change** — what to give back. Only cash methods give change.
6. **Cash** — the selected tile has a coloured border.
7. **Card** — a card payment taken on a terminal that isn't connected to the POS. You record it here.
8. **Quick amounts** — the row of chips: the next notes above the balance, and **Exact**. They appear only for methods that give change.
9. **Take $45.00 in Cash** — records the payment. When the balance reaches zero, the sale completes and the receipt is shown.

</div>

## Methods that can't give change {#no-change}

Card, and every method other than cash, takes no more than the balance. Enter more and the POS says so and won't take it.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[0, 0], [-50, 0], [-44, 0], [-34, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="Card selected with $50.00 entered: the line Only $45.00 is due — Card can't give change, the Full balance chip and the Take $45.00 in Card button greyed out" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Card** — selected.
2. **Amount** — $50.00, more than the $45.00 due.
3. **Only $45.00 is due — Card can't give change** — enter the balance or less.
4. **Full balance** — puts the balance back in the amount.
5. **Take $45.00 in Card** — greyed out until the amount fits.

</div>

## Paying part of the balance {#part-payment}

Enter less than the balance and the line under the amount reads **Part payment · $44.00 left after this**. The button says what will be left, for example **Take $1.00 in Card · $44.00 left**. Once that payment is taken, the label reads **Remaining**, and the button adds **· pays it off** when the next payment clears the balance. To plan the parts up front, press **Split**. See [Split Payments](./split-payments.mdx).

## What the button does {#the-button}

The button names the method and what will happen:

- **Take … in Cash** — records money you've taken yourself. Cash and Card work this way.
- **Send … to** a card reader — sends the amount to a connected reader. See [Taking a Payment](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
- The gateway's own words — some gateways name their own action, such as sending an invoice.

With no tile selected, the button reads **Choose how the customer is paying**.

## Taking a payment offline {#offline}

When the POS loses its connection, **Offline** appears in the register bar and next to the amount. Methods you record yourself still work, on a sale that reached your store before the connection dropped: its order number shows above the items. Each tile says **Sent later** and **Store may refuse**. Card readers and online gateways need a connection. See [Take a payment offline](../../getting-started/offline.mdx#take-payment-offline).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[50, 0], [-21, 34], [109, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="Checkout offline: Offline next to To pay and in the register bar, and Sent later, Store may refuse under the Cash and Card tiles" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Offline** — the POS can't reach your store.
2. **Sent later · Store may refuse** — a cash payment waits on this device until the store is back, and the store can still turn the sale down when it arrives.
3. **Card** — the same for a card payment you record.
4. **Take $45.00 in Cash** — asks you to confirm first.

</div>

Press the button and the POS asks **Take $45.00 in Cash while offline?** Confirm, and the sale is held on this device and sent when the store is back. If the store refuses it, the sale appears under **Needs attention** in Orders.

:::info With WCPOS Pro
The free plugin turns on Cash and Card only. Pro adds the other payment gateways on your store:

- WooCommerce's **cash on delivery**, **bank transfer** and **cheque** gateways get a tile and are recorded like Card. Cash on delivery gives change, like Cash.
- **Card readers** get a tile with a **Send** button. See [Taking a Payment](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
- **Email Invoice** sends the customer an invoice to pay later. See [Email Invoice](../../payment/gateways/email-invoice.mdx).
- Every other gateway is on the **Legacy** tab. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).

Turn gateways on in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#payment-gateways). See [Pro license](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).
:::

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**"Card is not available right now", or "2 not available right now".** The POS folds methods it can't use at the moment under one line. Tap it to see why each one is unavailable. See [Unavailable tiles](../../payment/index.mdx#unavailable-tiles).

**"No payment methods are enabled for the POS."** No gateway is turned on for the POS. A manager turns one on in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#payment-gateways).

**"Only … is due — Card can't give change".** You entered more than the balance on a method that gives no change. Enter the balance, or switch to Cash.

**"Open the register before completing the sale".** The register is closed. See [Open the Register](../register/index.mdx#open).

**A gateway you turned on has no tile.** See [Gateway not appearing at checkout](../../payment/index.mdx#gateway-not-appearing-at-checkout).
