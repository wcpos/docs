Each tile on the **Payments** tab of checkout is a payment method your store has turned on for the POS. **Cash** and **Card** come with the plugin. The tiles follow the order set in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#reordering-gateways).

When checkout opens, the first method you can use right now is selected. If you picked a method for this sale and switched to another open order, it's still selected when you come back.

## Choose a payment method {#choose}

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, the till won't record a payment while the register is closed, and a card you've already taken would have to be refunded. See [Open the Register](../register/index.mdx#open).
:::

For Card, charge no more than the balance on your separate terminal, then enter the same amount here.

1. Tap the tile for how the customer is paying.
2. Enter the amount: for Cash, what the customer hands over; for Card, what you charged on the terminal. The amount starts at the balance, and the keypad types in cents, so **1**, **0**, **0** is $1.00.
3. Press **Take**.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Payments** — choose how to take or record the customer's payment.
2. **Legacy** — your store's own payment page inside the POS. It offers Cash and Card on every store, plus any payment method that has no tile; those other methods need WCPOS Pro. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).
3. **To pay** — the balance of the order. After a part payment it reads **Remaining**.
4. **Amount** — what the customer hands over.
5. **Change** — what to give back. Only cash methods give change.
6. **Cash** — the selected tile has a coloured border.
7. **Card** — a card payment taken on a terminal that isn't connected to the POS. You record it here.
8. **Quick amounts** — the row of chips: the balance, the next round amounts above it, and **Exact $45.00**. The amount chips appear only for methods that give change.
9. **Take $45.00 in Cash** — records the payment. When the balance reaches zero, the sale completes and the paid screen opens; with **Automatically show receipt after checkout** off, a message names the order instead.

</div>

## Methods that can't give change {#no-change}

Methods that don't give change, including Card, take no more than the balance. Enter more and the POS says so and won't take it.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Card** — selected.
2. **Amount** — $50.00, more than the $45.00 due.
3. **Only $45.00 is due — Card can't give change** — enter the balance or less.
4. **Full balance $45.00** — puts the balance back in the amount.
5. **Take $45.00 in Card** — greyed out until the amount fits.

</div>

## Paying part of the balance {#part-payment}

1. Enter less than the balance. The line under the amount reads **Part payment · $44.00 left after this**, and the button says what will be left, such as **Take $1.00 in Card · $44.00 left**.
2. Press **Take**. The label now reads **Remaining**, with what's still owed.
3. Choose the method for the rest and take it. When it clears the balance, the button adds **· pays it off**.

To plan the parts up front instead, press **Split**. See [Split Payments](./split-payments.mdx).

## What the button does {#the-button}

The button names the method and what will happen:

- **Take … in Cash** — records money you've taken yourself. Cash and Card work this way.
- **Send … to** a card reader — sends the amount to a connected reader. See [Taking a Payment](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
- The method's own words — some methods name their own action, such as sending an invoice.

With no tile selected, the button reads **Choose how the customer is paying**.

## Taking a payment offline {#offline}

:::warning Only a sale the store already knows
Methods you record yourself work offline only on a sale that reached your store before the connection dropped: its order number shows above the items. Card readers and online payment methods need a connection.
:::

When the POS loses its connection, **Offline** appears in the register bar and next to the amount. The **Cash** and **Card** tiles say **Sent later** and **Store may refuse**. See [Take a payment offline](../../getting-started/offline.mdx#take-payment-offline).

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Offline** — the POS can't reach your store.
2. **Order number** — the sale has reached your store, so it can be paid offline.
3. **Sent later · Store may refuse** — a cash payment waits on this device until the store is back, and the store can still turn the sale down when it arrives.
4. **Card** — the same for a card payment you record.
5. **Take $45.00 in Cash** — asks you to confirm first.

Press the button and the POS asks **Take $45.00 in Cash while offline?** Confirm, and the sale is held on this device and sent when the store is back. If the store refuses it, the sale appears under **Needs attention** in Orders.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**"Card is not available right now", or "2 not available right now".** The POS folds methods it can't use at the moment under one line. Tap it to see why each one is unavailable. See [Unavailable tiles](../../payment/index.mdx#unavailable-tiles).

**"No payment methods are enabled for the POS."** No payment method is turned on for the POS. A manager turns one on in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#payment-gateways).

**"Only … is due — Card can't give change".** You entered more than the balance on a method that gives no change. Enter the balance. If the customer is paying in cash, tap **Cash** instead.

**"Open the register before completing the sale".** The register is closed. See [Open the Register](../register/index.mdx#open).

**A payment method you turned on has no tile.** See [Gateway not appearing at checkout](../../payment/index.mdx#gateway-not-appearing-at-checkout).

## More payment methods with WCPOS Pro {#pro-methods}

:::info With WCPOS Pro
The free plugin turns on Cash and Card only. The methods below need WCPOS Pro. See [Pro license](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).
:::

Pro adds the other payment methods on your store:

- WooCommerce's **cash on delivery**, **bank transfer** and **cheque** methods get a tile and are recorded like Card. Cash on delivery gives change, like Cash. The order takes the status set for that method in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#order-status): **On hold** by default for bank transfer and cheque, because the money arrives later.
- **Card readers** get a tile. With it selected, the button reads **Send … to** and sends the amount to the reader. See [Taking a Payment](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
- **Email Invoice** sends the customer an invoice to pay later. See [Email Invoice](../../payment/gateways/email-invoice.mdx).
- Every other payment method is on the **Legacy** tab. See [The Legacy Tab](./legacy-tab.mdx).

Turn payment methods on in [Checkout settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#payment-gateways).
