:::info Pro Feature
Tips on a card reader require [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx).
:::

A tip the customer adds on a card reader is recorded on the order as a **Tip** line, so it counts in the order total and in reports like any other fee. The payment's row on the tender pane shows **Tip:** and the amount.

Whether a reader asks for a tip depends on the gateway and on how the reader connects. See your reader's page under [Payment Gateways](../../payment/gateways/index.mdx).
