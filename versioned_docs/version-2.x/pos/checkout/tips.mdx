The customer adds a tip on the card reader, not at the till. The till sends the amount due, the reader asks the customer for a tip, and the till records what the reader took. There's no tip button on the till.

## Take a card payment with a tip {#take-a-tip}

:::info Pro Feature
Tips on a card reader need [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) and a reader that asks for them.
:::

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, the till won't record a payment while the register is closed. [Open the register](../register/index.mdx#open) before you take the payment.
:::

Before you start, check that [your reader asks for tips](#which-readers).

1. Press **Checkout** and tap your reader's tile. See [On a Card Reader](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
2. Press **Send … to …**. The button names the amount due and the payment method, and the till sends that amount to the reader.
3. On the reader, the customer picks a tip, or no tip, then pays.
4. At the till, the payment's row on the tender pane shows the whole amount the reader took, with **Tip:** and the tip under it.

The order gets a fee line named **Tip**, so the order total goes up by the tip and the tip is already paid. The receipt lists it with the order's other fees. A tip isn't taxed.

<details>
<summary>Taxing tips (for developers)</summary>

A store that has to tax tips can turn it on with the `wcpos_payment_tip_fee_taxable` filter. The tax is then taken out of the tip, so the customer still pays what they chose on the reader.

</details>

## Which readers ask for a tip {#which-readers}

| Payment method | Asks for a tip |
|---|---|
| [Stripe Terminal](../../payment/gateways/stripe-terminal.mdx) | The WisePad 3, WisePOS E, S700 and S710, once tipping is on for your readers in the Stripe Dashboard. Not the M2, and not Tap to Pay. |
| [SumUp Terminal](../../payment/gateways/sumup-terminal.mdx) | A Bluetooth reader paired with the WCPOS app. Not an internet-connected reader. |
| [Mollie Terminal](../../payment/gateways/mollie-terminal.mdx) | Every reader. |

These payment methods don't ask for a tip:

- [Square Terminal](../../payment/gateways/square-terminal.mdx)
- [PayArc Terminal](../../payment/gateways/payarc-terminal.mdx)
- [Windcave Terminal](../../payment/gateways/windcave-terminal.mdx)
- [Mercado Pago Terminal](../../payment/gateways/mercadopago-terminal.mdx)
- [PayPal Reader (Zettle)](../../payment/gateways/paypal-reader.mdx)

The **Card** tile records a card you took on your own terminal. It has no tip. See [Payment Methods](./payment-methods.mdx).

## Pay tips out of the drawer {#pay-out-tips}

When you hand staff their tips in cash, record it as a paid out so the drawer still counts right. This works in the free plugin too. It needs **Open and close the register**; see [Move cash in and out](../register/index.mdx#cash-movements).

1. Tap your avatar at the end of the register bar, then **In the drawer**, then **Paid out**.
2. Type the amount you're handing out on the keypad.
3. Tap **Tips** under **Reason**. It fills in the reason, not the amount.
4. Press **Take … out**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[180, 0], [370, 0], [-4, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [100, 0], [-14, -36]]} alt="The Paid out sheet: two dollars typed on the keypad, the drawer figure before and after, the Tips reason picked and filled in, and the Take $2.00 out button">

1. **Amount** — what you're taking out. It fills in as you type.
2. **In the drawer** — the register, the cash it expects now, and what's left after this paid out.
3. **Keypad** — types the amount. A keyboard works too.
4. **Tips** — the reason. Tap it to fill in the reason box, and again to clear it.
5. **Other…** — clears the box and puts the cursor in it, so you can type your own reason.
6. **Reason** — every paid out needs one. Without it, the sheet says **Add a reason**.
7. **Take $2.00 out** — names the amount. Press it to record the paid out. **Cancel** leaves the drawer as it was.

</JourneyShot>

To check it, tap **In the drawer** to open the register panel, then tap **Expected in the drawer**. The panel opens the breakdown of that figure, and the paid out is listed under **Paid out**, with its reason. Press **Void** on it if it was a mistake. Void only works while the register is open, not during a count, and a voided paid out can't be restored: enter it again. See [If something goes wrong](../register/index.mdx#goes-wrong).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[249, 0], [0, 0], [54, 0]]} alt="The register panel with Expected in the drawer tapped open: the float, cash sales, cash refunds, and under Paid out one row with its reason, amount and Void">

1. **Expected in the drawer** — tap it to open or close the breakdown.
2. **Paid out** — how many paid outs and their total, with one row for each and its reason.
3. **Void** — takes that paid out off the drawer.

</JourneyShot>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**The reader didn't ask for a tip.** Check the [table](#which-readers): some payment methods and readers never ask. On Stripe, turn tipping on for the reader in the Stripe Dashboard.

**Payment failed: The terminal confirmed a different amount.** The reader took more than the till sent, using a payment method that doesn't take tips, so the till marked the payment failed and didn't count it towards the order.

:::warning
Check the payment with your provider before you charge the customer again, or you may charge them twice.
:::

If the provider shows the charge, refund it there: the till can't refund a failed payment. The amount is still due on the order: take it again on any payment method, or on a reader from the [table](#which-readers) if the customer wants to tip.
