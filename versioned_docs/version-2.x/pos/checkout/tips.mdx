:::info Pro Feature
Tips on a card reader need [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) and a reader that asks for them. Paying tips out of the drawer works in the free plugin too.
:::

The customer adds a tip on the card reader, not at the till. The till sends the amount due, the reader asks the customer for a tip, and the till records what the reader took. There's no tip button on the till.

## Take a card payment with a tip {#take-a-tip}

1. Press **Checkout**, then tap your reader's tile on the tender pane. The till sends the amount due to the reader; there's nothing else to press. See [On a Card Reader](./taking-a-payment.mdx#card-reader).
2. On the reader, the customer picks a tip, or no tip, then pays.
3. At the till, the payment's row on the tender pane shows the whole amount the reader took, with **Tip:** and the tip under it.

The order gets a fee line named **Tip**, so the order total goes up by the tip and nothing is left to pay. The receipt lists it with the order's other fees. A tip isn't taxed.

<details>
<summary>Taxing tips (for developers)</summary>

A store that has to tax tips can turn it on with the `wcpos_payment_tip_fee_taxable` filter. The tax is then taken out of the tip, so the customer still pays what they chose on the reader.

</details>

## Which readers ask for a tip {#which-readers}

| Gateway | Asks for a tip |
|---|---|
| [Stripe Terminal](../../payment/gateways/stripe-terminal.mdx) | The WisePad 3, WisePOS E, S700 and S710, once tipping is on for your readers in the Stripe Dashboard. Not the M2, and not Tap to Pay. |
| [SumUp Terminal](../../payment/gateways/sumup-terminal.mdx) | A Bluetooth reader paired with the WCPOS app. Not an internet-connected reader. |
| [Mollie Terminal](../../payment/gateways/mollie-terminal.mdx) | Every reader. |

These readers don't ask for a tip:

- [Square Terminal](../../payment/gateways/square-terminal.mdx)
- [PayArc Terminal](../../payment/gateways/payarc-terminal.mdx)
- [Windcave Terminal](../../payment/gateways/windcave-terminal.mdx)
- [Mercado Pago Terminal](../../payment/gateways/mercadopago-terminal.mdx)
- [PayPal Reader (Zettle)](../../payment/gateways/paypal-reader.mdx)

The **Card** tile records a card you took on your own terminal. It has no tip. See [Payment Methods](./payment-methods.mdx).

## Pay tips out of the drawer {#pay-out-tips}

When you hand staff their tips in cash, record it as a paid out so the drawer still counts right. This needs **Open and close the register**; see [Move cash in and out](../register/index.mdx#cash-movements).

Tap your avatar at the end of the register bar, then **In the drawer**, then **Paid out**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[70, 0], [290, 0], [15, 34], [24, 34], [110, 0], [-14, 0]]} alt="The Paid out sheet: twenty dollars typed, the drawer figure before and after, the Tips reason picked and filled in, and the Take $20.00 out button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Amount** — what you're taking out. Type it on the keypad.
2. **In the drawer** — the cash the register expects now, and after this paid out.
3. **Tips** — the reason. Tap it and it fills in the reason box.
4. **Other…** — clears the box so you can type your own reason.
5. **Reason** — every paid out needs one.
6. **Take $20.00 out** — names the amount. Press it to record the paid out. **Cancel** leaves the drawer as it was.

</div>

The paid out shows on the register panel under **Paid out**, with its reason. Press **Void** on it if it was a mistake.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**The reader didn't ask for a tip.** Check the [table](#which-readers): some gateways and readers never ask. On Stripe, turn tipping on for the reader in the Stripe Dashboard.

**Payment failed: The terminal confirmed a different amount.** The reader took more than the till sent, on a gateway that doesn't take tips, so the till didn't record the payment. Check the payment with your provider before you charge the customer again.
