The **Legacy** tab in checkout opens your store's own payment page inside the POS. Use it when the payment method you need isn't on the **Payments** tab. The page comes from your store, so the till needs a connection to it.

:::info Pro Feature
The **Legacy** tab is there without Pro, but its payment page offers only **Cash** and **Card**. [WCPOS Pro](../../getting-started/pro-license.mdx) adds every other gateway you enable for the POS.
:::

## Pay on the payment page {#take-payment}

:::note Open the register first
With **Open and close the register** switched on, open the register before you take payment here. See [Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register).
:::

1. Press **Checkout**, then the **Legacy** tab.
2. Wait for the payment page to load. **Process Payment** stays greyed out until it has.
3. Pick a payment method on the page and fill in what it asks for.
4. Press **Process Payment**.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Payments** tab — the method tiles. See [The Tender Pane](./tender-pane.mdx).
2. **Legacy** tab — this page.
3. **Checkout Settings** — switches off theme and plugin styles and scripts that break the page. See [Checkout Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.mdx#checkout-settings-troubleshooting).
4. **Paying as customer** — the order's customer. Click the name to show or hide their billing and shipping addresses.
5. **Coupon code** — **Apply coupon** adds a coupon to the order from here.
6. **The order** — its lines and totals, as your store has them.
7. **Payment methods** — every gateway enabled for the POS, under its POS title and in the order set in [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#payment-gateways). The default is already picked.
8. **Process Payment** — takes the payment with the method picked on the page.
9. **Close** — back to the cart, unpaid.
10. **Remaining** — the page charges the whole of it.

</div>

For **Cash**, type what the customer hands you in **Amount Tendered** and the change shows as you type. Leave it empty if they hand you the exact total.

The sale is done when the order reaches the **Order Status** set for that gateway in [Checkout Settings](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#order-status-at-the-till). The paid screen then opens, as for any sale. See [Receipts at Checkout](../../receipts/at-checkout.mdx).

## After a payment has been taken {#after-payment}

A payment page charges the whole order, so it can't take the rest of a [split payment](./split-payments.mdx). Once a payment has been taken, the tab shows a notice and lists its methods instead of the page.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[29, 42], [485, -24], [80, 0], [-44, 0], [130, -20], [-60, 0]]} alt="The Legacy tab after a $10.00 cash payment: a yellow notice reading A legacy gateway can't be used once a payment has been taken, the methods listed below it, Cancel payment at the top, and $35.00 remaining" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Legacy** tab — open.
2. **A legacy gateway can't be used once a payment has been taken** — the payment page charges the whole order.
3. **The methods** — listed, not picked from here.
4. **Cancel payment** — gives back what's been taken. See [Cancelling a Payment](./cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
5. **The payment taken** — $10.00 in cash.
6. **Remaining** — what's still due.

</div>

You can:

- **Take the rest on a tile.** Go back to the **Payments** tab and finish the sale there. See [Split Payments](./split-payments.mdx).
- **Pay it all on the payment page.** Press **Cancel payment**, give back what the screen lists, then press **Cancel and void**. The POS goes back to the cart with the order unpaid. Press **Checkout** and open the **Legacy** tab.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

:::warning Check the order before paying again
The tab says **The payment page failed to load after processing — close checkout and check the order's status before paying again.** The payment may already have gone through. Close checkout and look at the order's status before you take payment again.
:::

<details>
<summary>A grey placeholder instead of the page</summary>

The order is still saving to your store, or the till is still loading your store's payment methods. The page opens once both are done. Offline, it waits until you're back online.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Payment methods could not load</summary>

The tab says **Payment methods could not load. Try again.** Press **Try again**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The order has no payment link yet</summary>

The tab says **The payment form can't load — this order has no payment link from the server yet. Close checkout and try again.** Close checkout, wait for the order to save, then press **Checkout** again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The payment form didn't load</summary>

The tab says **The payment form didn't load — check the connection to your store, then retry.** Check that the till can reach your store, then press **Retry**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>No legacy gateways are enabled</summary>

The tab says **No legacy gateways are enabled for the POS.** No gateway enabled for the POS can run on the payment page. See [Enabling a Gateway](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#enabling-a-gateway).

</details>

<details>
<summary>The page looks broken, or a button on it does nothing</summary>

A theme or plugin script is probably interfering. See [Checkout Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.mdx#checkout-settings-troubleshooting).

</details>

<details>
<summary>The payment went through, but the sale doesn't close</summary>

The order hasn't reached the gateway's **Order Status**. See [How the till treats the status](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#order-status-at-the-till).

</details>

<details>
<summary>The gateway turns the payment down</summary>

The till shows the gateway's own message, or **The payment didn't go through, so this sale is not paid. The cart is unchanged.** See [CHECKOUT101](../../error-codes/CHECKOUT101.mdx).

</details>
