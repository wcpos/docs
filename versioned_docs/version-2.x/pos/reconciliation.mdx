At the end of a shift you close the register: count the cash, compare it with what the drawer should hold, check card totals, and file the record. WCPOS works out the expected cash from the opening float, the cash sales and refunds, and the paid in and paid out recorded during the shift.

:::info Free and Pro
Opening, counting and closing the register, and the numbered closing report, are in the free plugin. [WCPOS Pro](/getting-started/pro-license) adds more registers and the Reports screen's history beyond today.
:::

## The short version {#the-short-version}

1. **Open the register** at the start of the shift with the float in the drawer. During the day, record cash drops and payouts as **Paid in** / **Paid out** in the register panel.
2. **Stop taking sales** so nothing lands mid-count.
3. **Tap Close register** in the register panel and enter the cash you counted. If you can see expected cash, the screen shows whether the drawer is short, over or exact.
4. **Get a manager's approval** if the count is over your variance threshold.
5. **Tap Close & print.** The closing report (the Z report) prints and is filed as a numbered closure.
6. **Reconcile card:** the card terminal's batch total should match the card total on the closing report. Settle the batch if your terminal is manual.
7. **Drop the cash**, put tomorrow's float back, and sign off.

To check the drawer without closing, use **Print X-report** in the register panel.
