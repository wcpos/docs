At the end of the day you count the drawer, close the register and match the card terminal. The count is filed as a numbered closure you can print and find again. This page walks through one close, from checking the drawer to signing out.

:::note This page needs the register switched on
Everything here depends on **Open and close the register**. With it off, there's no drawer to count. See [Registers](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) for the setting, and [Open the Register](./register/index.mdx) for the start of the day.
:::

## Check the drawer {#check}

Open **Reports**. The strip at the top is the open session on this register: how the cash in the drawer should add up.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[0, -32], [31, -32], [0, 36], [0, 36], [0, 36], [0, 36], [0, 36]]} alt="The Reports screen with the register strip at the top: the register status, when it opened and who opened it, the opening float, cash sales, cash refunds, Print X-report and the closures arrow" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Open** — the register's status: **Open** now, **Closed** after the close.
2. **Since** — when the register opened and who opened it.
3. **Opening float** — the cash the register opened with.
4. **Cash sales** — how many, and how much.
5. **Cash refunds** — cash handed back.
6. **Print X-report** — prints the session so far without closing it.
7. **Closures** (WCPOS Pro) — every closure, with earlier days and other registers. See [Reconciliation](../reports/reconciliation.mdx).

</div>

The strip ends with **Expected in drawer**, the blue figure just left of **Print X-report**: the cash the register expects to find. That's what your count should come to.

Each figure joins the strip once the session has one: **Cash sales**, **Cash refunds**, **Paid in** and **Paid out** (see [Move cash in and out](./register/index.mdx#cash-movements)).

After the close, the same strip shows **Closed** with the time and who closed it, **Last closure** with its number, **Expected**, **Counted** and the **Drawer result**. Its button becomes **Print closure** and prints the closure again.

## Count the drawer {#count}

Finish the last sale first. Tap your avatar, then **In the drawer**, and press **Close register**. The register stops taking sales while you count; **Back to selling** at the top of the count takes you back to the cart if a customer walks up.

Count the cash, float included, and enter the total.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[10, 30], [196, 0], [-31, 0], [338, 0], [209, 0], [134, 0], [61, -34]]} alt="Counting the drawer: the counted cash, the expected cash with the drawer short, the note explaining the difference, Count by notes and coins, Other tenders and the Close & print button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Back to selling** — leave the count and sell again. Count again before you close.
2. **Cash counted, float included** — what's in the drawer.
3. **Expected** — what the register expects, and how far your count is **short** or **over**, or **Exact** with a tick. It appears once you've entered an amount. Blind cashiers don't see it; see [Access](../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).
4. **Note** — **Explain any difference**: a wrong change, a payout with no slip. The note is kept on the closure.
5. **Count by notes and coins** — tap a note or coin once for each one you count, or hold it to add ten. The amount becomes their total; **Clear** starts again.
6. **Other tenders** — the card total and any other payment method, counted from the terminal or slips.
7. **Close & print** — writes the closure. It reads **Approve & close** when the difference is over your limit, and a manager signs in.

</div>

## File the closure {#closure}

Press **Close & print**. The count is written as the register's next numbered closure.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[157, -24], [111, -25], [112, -25], [113, -25], [369, 0], [219, 0], [75, -34], [32, -34]]} alt="Closure written: the closure number and time, counted, expected and the result, the drawer lines from float to counted, Printed on, Print closure and Done" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Closure N written** — the closure's number, the register, the session's times and who closed it.
2. **Counted** — the cash you entered.
3. **Expected** — what the register expected.
4. **Result** — **Exact**, short or over.
5. **Note** — your note from the count, kept on the closure.
6. **Drawer** — every line from the float to the counted cash.
7. **Print closure** — prints it. Once it has printed, the sheet says **Printed on** and the time. A blind cashier has no print button here.
8. **Done** — back to a closed register, ready for tomorrow's float.

</div>

Which printer the closure goes to is set on each device: **Settings → Printing → Closure templates**. The X-report and the hand-over slip print on those templates too; **Report templates** cover the summaries printed from Reports. See [Print routing](../hardware/printers/index.mdx#print-routing).

## Match the card and drop the cash {#card}

1. **Run the batch total** on the card terminal and compare it with the card total on the closure. They should match. Settle the batch now if your terminal doesn't do it for you.
2. **Put tomorrow's float** back in the drawer.
3. **Bag the rest** with the printed closure for the safe.

## Sign out {#sign-out}

Tap your avatar, then **Sign out**. With the register closed, you're signed out straight away.

<details>
<summary>If the register is still open</summary>

The POS asks first.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Still open** — the register's name and the cash it expects. A blind cashier sees no amount.
2. **Close register** — goes straight to the [count](#count).
3. **Sign out anyway** — leaves the register open for the next cashier. The closure records whoever closes it.
4. **Cancel** — back to your cashier sheet.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**The count is out.** Count again first, coins included. Then enter what you counted and say why in the **Note**. If the difference is over **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than** ([Registers](../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register)), a manager approves with their WordPress username and password. The [Reconciliation](../reports/reconciliation.mdx#variance-handling) walkthrough lists where differences usually hide.

**"N sales not yet on the server — the closure will record them".** Sales from this device haven't reached the store yet. You can still close. When they arrive, they're added beside the closure as **Late corrections**, like any sale that arrives after the close.

**"Figures arrive with the next sync."** The register is waiting for its figures from the store. **Close register** stays off until they arrive. Check the connection and stay on the page.

**"N carts still open · they wait for their cashier".** Open carts don't block the close. They stay for whoever started them.

**A sale arrives after the close.** It's recorded as a late correction beside that closure. The closure itself is never rewritten. See [A sale went through after I closed](../reports/reconciliation.mdx#multi-cashier-or-multi-store-scenarios).

**Reports says "No register on this device".** This device hasn't picked a register, or the store doesn't use them. See [Choosing a store and a register](../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#choosing-a-store-and-a-register).

<details>
<summary>Store health flags a register</summary>

Open **Health**, then **Database**. The **Registers** block checks each till's numbering:

- **Closures numbered in order** — when this check fails, it names closure numbers that were skipped, closures printed with a different number, or closures whose totals disagree with the server. Closures are never renumbered, and this check covers every closure, not only recent ones.
- **N skipped numbers** — a receipt number was taken but the sale never completed. Nothing is lost.
- **Counter restarted on N till** — the same receipt number is on more than one sale. Nothing is lost.
- **N sales stamped by a clock running ahead** — set the date and time on the till it names.

See [Store Health](../support/store-health.mdx#database).

</details>

Pro adds more registers per store, the Reports history beyond today, and the **Closures** view. Each store can also choose its own closure and report templates in its store settings; see [Setting up stores](../stores/setup.mdx).
:::
