The cart is the order you're ringing up. Products land here when you tap them, and from here you choose the customer, add anything that isn't in your catalogue, check the totals and press **Checkout**. On a tablet or computer it sits beside the products; on a phone it has its own tab.

## The cart at a glance {#interface-overview}

- **Customer** — who the order is for. Tap to choose someone else. See [Choose the customer](#customer-selector).
- **Address** — the order's billing and shipping details. See [Edit the order's address](#address).
- **Add to cart** (**+**) — a miscellaneous product, a fee, a discount, shipping or a new customer. See [The add menu](#add-to-cart-options).
- **Cart Settings** — how the cart looks and behaves on this device. See [Cart Settings](#display-settings).
- **Line items** — tap the quantity, price or total to change them. See [Line Items](./line-items.mdx).
- **Fee** — fees and shipping sit below the products. Tap one for **Edit** and **Remove**.
- **Totals** — subtotal, fees, shipping, discounts and tax. See [The totals](#order-summary).
- **Order details** (**⋮**) — the order note, tax exempt, custom fields and **Void**. See [Order Actions](./order-actions.mdx).
- **Checkout** — names the total. See [Checkout](../checkout/index.mdx).
- **Open orders** — every sale in progress on this register. See [Open Orders](./open-orders.mdx).

## Choose the customer {#customer-selector}

Every new order starts with your store's default customer: whoever [Default POS Customer](../../settings/wp-admin/general.mdx#default-pos-customer) names, or the cashier when [Use Cashier Account](../../settings/wp-admin/general.mdx#use-cashier-account) is on.

Tap the customer's name to open **Select Customer**, then type part of a name, email or phone number. **Guest** at the top of the list turns the order back into a guest sale.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} alt="The Select Customer search with Smith typed, the Guest option at the top and matching customers below" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Select Customer** — opens the search. Type part of a name; "No customers found" means nothing matches.
2. **Guest** — a sale with no customer account.
3. **A customer** — tap to put them on the order. Their billing and shipping addresses come with them.

To add someone who isn't in the list yet, see [Add a new customer](#new-customer).

## Edit the order's address {#address}

Tap the address beside the customer's name to open **Edit Customer Address**. It's greyed until the billing address has a street, city or postcode, but you can still tap it to add one.

Changes save to this order as you type, so there's no **Save** button; press **Close** when you're done. The customer's own profile only changes when you press **Update customer too**. That button shows for a customer with an account, and only to a cashier who may edit customers.

<details>
<summary>Address fields and tax IDs</summary>

- **Name** — first and last name. **Email** and **Phone** sit below it.
- **Billing Address** — **Edit** opens the lines, **Remove** clears them.
- **Country** — pick from the list. Over ten countries, you can search it.
- **State** — a list for countries that have one, a text field for the rest.
- **Shipping Address** — **Same as billing** until you press **Use a different address**.
- **Tax ID** — the customer's tax or VAT number, saved on the order.
- **Update customer too** — saves these details to the customer's profile as well.

Rows you don't use, such as **Company**, **Billing Address**, **Shipping Address** and **Tax ID**, start as buttons; press one to add it. A new billing address starts with your store's country.

A **Tax ID** row has a **Type** (the types used in the address's country come first), the **ID** and a two-letter **Country**. If the number doesn't look like the type you picked, the row warns "Format does not match the selected type." The ID still saves. See [Customer Tax IDs](../../settings/wp-admin/customer-tax-ids.mdx).

</details>

## The add menu {#add-to-cart-options}

Tap **+** beside the customer for anything you can't tap in the product grid.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} alt="The add to cart menu open: Add new customer, Add Miscellaneous Product, Add Fee, Add Discount and Add Shipping" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Add new customer** (WCPOS Pro) — create a customer and put them on the order. See [Add a new customer](#new-customer).
2. **Add Miscellaneous Product** — sell something that isn't in your catalogue. See [Miscellaneous products](#miscellaneous-products).
3. **Add Fee** — a service charge, delivery fee or surcharge. See [Fees](#fees).
4. **Add Discount** — a quick discount or a coupon on the whole order. See [Discounts](./discounts.mdx#order-level-discounts).
5. **Add Shipping** — a shipping charge for an order you'll deliver. See [Shipping](#shipping).

</div>

Each dialog adds one line and closes. To change a fee or shipping line afterwards, tap it in the cart and press **Edit**; the same fields open again. For product lines, see [Line Items](./line-items.mdx).

### Miscellaneous products {#miscellaneous-products}

Press **Add Miscellaneous Product**, type a name and price, and press **Add to Cart**.

<details>
<summary>Every field in Add Miscellaneous Product</summary>

- **Name** — what shows on the receipt. Left empty, it's called "Product".
- **Price** — the price for one.
- **Tax** — **Not taxable**, or one of your store's tax classes.
- **SKU** — adds a SKU to the line.
- **Categories** — puts the line in your categories, for reports. Picking a parent category doesn't pick its children.
- **Virtual** — no shipping needed.
- **Downloadable** — a downloadable product.
- **Add to Cart** — adds the line.

**SKU**, **Categories**, **Virtual** and **Downloadable** are hidden until you press them. **Virtual** and **Downloadable** arrive switched on, and every added row has **Remove**.

</details>

### Fees {#fees}

Press **Add Fee**, type a name and amount, and press **Add to Cart**. A fee can't be negative: the form says "A fee cannot be negative. Use Add Discount instead."

<details>
<summary>Every field in Add Fee</summary>

- **Fee Name** — what shows on the receipt. Left empty, it's called "Fee".
- **Amount** — the fee.
- **$ / %** — a fixed amount, or a percentage of the products on the order.
- **Amount Includes Tax** — on, the tax is taken out of the amount you typed. Off, tax is added on top.
- **Tax** — **Not taxable**, or one of your store's tax classes.
- **Add to Cart** — adds the fee.

A percentage fee counts the product lines only, with their tax when your store's prices include tax.

</details>

### Shipping {#shipping}

Press **Add Shipping**, type a title and amount, and press **Add to Cart**.

<details>
<summary>Every field in Add Shipping</summary>

- **Shipping Method Title** — what shows on the receipt. Left empty, it's "Shipping".
- **Shipping Method** — one of your store's shipping methods. With none picked, the order records local pickup.
- **Amount** — the shipping charge.
- **Amount Includes Tax** — on, the tax is taken out of the amount. Off, tax is added on top.
- **Tax** — **Taxable** or **None**. Shipping is taxed at your store's [Shipping Tax Class](../../settings/store/tax.mdx#shipping-tax-class).
- **Add to Cart** — adds the shipping line.

</details>

### Add a new customer {#new-customer}

:::info With WCPOS Pro
**Add new customer** needs WCPOS Pro and a cashier who may create customers. Otherwise it's greyed, and resting on it says why: an upgrade, or a permission an administrator grants in [Access](../../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).
:::

Fill in the form and press **Add Customer**. The form starts empty each time. When the customer saves, a message names them and they go straight onto the order, with their addresses and tax IDs. The order links to their account once the store confirms it.

<details>
<summary>If the customer doesn't reach the order</summary>

- **"The customer was saved, but not added to this order."** Search for them in [Select Customer](#customer-selector) and add them again.
- **"… was not saved to the store."** The order keeps their addresses but stays a guest sale. Send the customer again from [Store Health](../../support/store-health.mdx).

</details>

To manage customers outside a sale, see [Customers](../../customers/index.mdx#add-new-customer).

## The totals {#order-summary}

The totals sit above **Checkout** once the order has something on it:

- **Subtotal** — the products.
- **Discounts** — one row per discount or coupon, as a minus amount. Tap the **✕** to remove it.
- **Fees** and **Shipping** — when the order has them.
- **Tax** — one row per tax rate, or a single **Total Tax**, as your store's [Display Tax Totals](../../settings/store/tax.mdx#display-tax-totals) sets. A tag beside it says whether the amounts include tax (**incl**) or exclude it (**excl**).
- **Refund** rows and **Net Payment** — on an order you've refunded part of.

There's no separate total row: the total is on the **Checkout** button. The amounts include tax when your store [displays cart prices](../../settings/store/tax.mdx#display-prices-during-cart-and-checkout) with tax.

## Tax exempt {#tax-exempt}

To sell without tax, for example to a charity, open **Order details** (**⋮** beside **Checkout**) and switch on **Tax exempt**. It removes all tax from the order, including fees and shipping, and the tax row in the totals reads **Tax exempt**.

**Tax exempt** is only there when your store enters prices without tax. When prices include tax, there's no row to switch. See [Prices Entered With Tax](../../settings/store/tax.mdx#prices-entered-with-tax).

When the switch is greyed, the line under it says why:

- **"Reopen the order to change tax"** — the order is no longer open, for example it's completed.
- **"Connect to the store to change tax on an order with a coupon"** — you're offline and the order has a coupon.
- **"Finish the payment in progress first"** — the order is at checkout, a payment is under way, or it's already paid.
- **"Coupons on a tax exempt order need WooCommerce 11.3 or later on the store."** — turning it on with a coupon needs a newer WooCommerce. Turning it off always works.

For the rest of **Order details**, see [Order Actions](./order-actions.mdx).

## Cart Settings {#display-settings}

Press the sliders button at the top of the cart to open **Cart Settings**. Changes apply straight away, on this device only.

- **Panel position** — **Cart left** or **Cart right**.
- **Open orders position** — **Top** or **Bottom** of the cart.
- **Sort items** — **Newest at top**, **Newest at bottom**, **Name** or **Price**. Fees and shipping always stay last.
- **Quick Discounts** — the percent keys on the price keypad. See [Quick Discounts](#quick-discounts).
- **Automatically show receipt after checkout** — on by default. See [Receipts at Checkout](../../receipts/at-checkout.mdx).
- **Automatically print receipt after checkout** — off by default.
- **Restore Default Settings** — puts every cart setting back. See [Restore defaults](#restore-default-settings).

### Quick Discounts {#quick-discounts}

When a cashier changes a product's price in the web POS, the keypad offers up to three percent keys, so 5% off is a tap rather than a sum. Cart Settings shows the keys as the cashier sees them. Tap a key to change it: any amount above 0 and up to 100. Enter 0 to empty a slot. With all three empty, the keypad shows only the digits.

The keys start as −5%, −10% and −15%. To use them, see [Discounts](./discounts.mdx#quick-discounts).

### Columns {#columns}

Lower in Cart Settings, **Columns** chooses what each line shows. Switch a column on or off and drag the handle to reorder. Some columns have extra switches.

| Column | On by default | Extra switches |
|---|---|---|
| **Qty** | Yes | **Split** (off) |
| **Image** | No | |
| **Name** | Yes | **SKU** (off) |
| **SKU** | No | |
| **Price** | Yes | **On Sale** (on) |
| **Regular Price** | No | |
| **Subtotal** | No | **Tax** (off) |
| **Total** | Yes | **Tax** (off), **On Sale** (on) |
| **Actions** | Yes | |

### Restore defaults {#restore-default-settings}

**Restore Default Settings**, at the foot of Cart Settings, puts the panel position, sorting, quick discounts, receipt switches and columns back to how they started.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Add new customer is greyed.** It needs WCPOS Pro and a cashier allowed to create customers. Rest on it to see which; an administrator grants the permission in [Access](../../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).

**Update customer too is missing.** The order is a guest sale, or the cashier may not edit customers. The address still saves to the order.

**"A fee cannot be negative."** Use **Add Discount** to take money off; see [Discounts](./discounts.mdx#order-level-discounts).

**There's no Tax exempt switch.** Your store enters prices with tax. See [Tax exempt](#tax-exempt).

**Checkout is dimmed.** Something on the order needs fixing first, such as a product that's out of stock. Tap **Checkout** and the cart scrolls to it.
