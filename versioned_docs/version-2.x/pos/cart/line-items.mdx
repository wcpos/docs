Every product, fee and shipping charge in the cart is a line. You change most of a line right where it sits: tap the quantity, the price or the name. Everything else is in the line's **Edit** dialog.

## A line in the cart {#line}

A product line shows its quantity, its name and its unit price, with the line total on the right. The [columns](./index.mdx#columns) you show decide what else is there.

![The cart with one line: quantity 3, Affirm Water Bottle, the regular price $23.00 struck through above the price $15.00, and the line total $45.00](/img/2.x/pos/checkout/en/tablet-light-regular--j2-3-change-quantity.png)

- **Qty** — the quantity. Tap it to change it; see [Change the quantity](#quantity).
- **Item** — the product's name. Tap it to rename the line for this sale. Underneath are the options a [variation](../product-panel/variations.mdx) was sold with and the line's [custom fields](#meta-data), as `Key: value`. Turn on the column's **SKU** option to show the SKU too.
- **Price** — the price of one. Tap it to change it; see [Change the price](#price). When the price is lower than the regular price, the regular price shows struck through above it.
- **Total** — quantity times price. Tap it to show **Edit** and **Remove**; see [Edit or remove a line](#remove-item).

Fees and shipping are lines too, below the products. Tap a fee's name or a shipping line's title to rename it, and tap its amount to change it. Add them from the **+** menu above the cart; see [Fees](./index.mdx#fees) and [Shipping](./index.mdx#shipping).

## Change the quantity {#quantity}

Tap the quantity box. On a tablet or computer it grows into a keypad, with the quantity at the top:

- **−** and **+** take one off or add one. At 1, **−** turns into a bin: pressing it removes the line.
- Type a new number on the keys. The decimal key, marked with your store's decimal separator, takes a part quantity such as 2.5 or 0.25 (up to six decimal places), for things sold by weight or length.
- Press **Done** (or Enter) to keep it. Escape closes the keypad and keeps the quantity you had.

On a phone the keypad opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen. In the iOS and Android apps you type the number into the box instead, and a quantity of 0 removes the line.

When your store prevents overselling, a quantity above what you have in stock turns amber; see [When checkout is blocked](#checkout-blocked).

### Split a line {#splitting-line-items}

**Split** turns one line of several into separate lines of one each, so you can give one of them a different price, discount or note. A part quantity keeps its fraction on a line of its own: a line of 2.5 splits into 1, 1 and 0.5.

The **Split** link sits under the quantity of any line with more than one. It's off until you turn it on: open **Cart Settings** (the sliders icon above the cart), find the **Qty** column and switch on its **Split** option.

## Change the price {#price}

Tap the price. A price keypad grows from it:

- Type the new price for one. The first digit replaces the old price.
- The **quick discount** keys above the digits (−5%, −10% and −15% unless you've [changed them](./discounts.mdx#quick-discounts)) take that percentage off the line's price. The keypad shows the sum above the new price. Press the lit key again to undo it.
- Press **Done** (or Enter) to keep the new price. Escape closes it unchanged.

On a phone the same keypad opens as a sheet.

:::note A price edit is not a WooCommerce "discount"
A lowered price is recorded as the item's sale price. Receipts can show the saving, but the order's **Discount** total is reserved for coupons, and reports count the sale at the price paid. See [Cart Discounts](./discounts.mdx#why-tracked-differently) for why WooCommerce treats the two differently.
:::

## Edit or remove a line {#remove-item}

Each line has **Edit** and **Remove** buttons hidden under it. To show them:

- **Tap the line's total.** Tap it again to hide them.
- **Hover** over the total with a mouse and the line slides aside to give you a peek.
- **Swipe the line** to the left on a touch screen, or drag it left with the mouse.

**Edit** opens the [Edit dialog](#edit-form). **Remove** takes the line out of the cart. Swipe further and **Remove** fills the whole row in red: let go and the line is removed in one move.

The message that appears after a removal has **Undo**, which brings the line back as it was.

## The Edit dialog {#edit-form}

**Edit** opens the line's details in a dialog named after it. Changes save as you make them; press **Close** when you're done.

For a product line:

- **Name** — what the receipt and order call it.
- **Quantity** and **Price** — the same as in the cart. When the line is on sale, the regular price shows beside the price with **was**; its **Remove** puts the regular price back as the price.
- **Tax** — whether the line is taxable, and its tax class.
- **Regular Price** and **SKU** — tap to add them to the line.
- **Categories**, **Virtual** and **Downloadable** — only on a [miscellaneous product](./index.mdx#miscellaneous-products), which isn't in your catalogue, so you set them here.
- **Custom field** — see [Custom fields](#meta-data).

Each added row has its own **Remove**.

### A fee line {#edit-fee}

A fee's Edit dialog has the same fields as the **Add Fee** form shown here. In the Edit dialog, changes save as you go, you can add [custom fields](#meta-data), and **Close** replaces **Cancel** and **Add to Cart**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={9} offsets={[[-35, 0], [-35, 0], [50, 0], [-40, 0], [-35, 0]]} alt="The fee form: Fee Name, the Amount with its currency and percent switch, Amount Includes Tax and the tax class" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Fee Name** — what the receipt calls the fee. It starts as "Fee".
2. **Amount** — a fixed sum.
3. **$ / %** — switches the amount to a percentage of the cart total.
4. **Amount Includes Tax** — on when the amount already has tax in it.
5. **Tax** — **Not taxable**, or the tax class charged on the fee.

</div>

A fee can't be negative: the form asks you to use **Add Discount** instead. See [Discounts](./discounts.mdx).

### A shipping line {#edit-shipping}

A shipping line's Edit dialog has the same fields as the **Add Shipping** form shown here. As with a fee, it saves as you go, takes [custom fields](#meta-data), and closes with **Close**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={10} offsets={[[-35, 0], [-40, 0], [-35, 0]]} alt="The shipping form: Shipping Method Title, Shipping Method, Amount, Amount Includes Tax and Taxable or None" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Amount** — the shipping charge. You type it: the POS doesn't work out shipping rates.
2. **Amount Includes Tax** — on when the charge already has tax in it.
3. **Tax** — **Taxable** or **None**.

</div>

Above them, **Shipping Method Title** is what the receipt shows (it starts as "Shipping"), and **Shipping Method** picks one of your store's shipping methods.

### The JSON tab {#raw-json-view}

The **JSON** tab beside **Form** shows the line exactly as it goes to WooCommerce. You won't need it for a sale. It helps when you're checking what a plugin will see, or why a field isn't saving.

#### The three kinds of line {#line-item-types}

WooCommerce stores products, fees and shipping as separate kinds of line:

| Kind | In WooCommerce | Examples |
|------|----------------|----------|
| Product | `line_item` | T-shirt, Coffee mug |
| Fee | `fee_line` | Gift wrapping, Service charge |
| Shipping | `shipping_line` | Standard shipping, Express delivery |

#### WCPOS's own fields {#protected-keys}

Keys that start with an underscore are WCPOS's own records (such as `_woocommerce_pos_data` and `_reduced_stock`). They stay out of the form and the cart, and you can see them on the **JSON** tab.

</details>

## Custom fields {#meta-data}

A custom field is a key and a value stored with the line, such as an engraving or a serial number. WooCommerce calls them meta data, and they show on the order in WP Admin.

To add one, open the line's **Edit** dialog, tap **Custom field** and fill in the **Key** and **Value**. The field shows under the line's name in the cart. Each one has its own **Remove**.

Some fields come with the product: any [Meta Data Keys](../product-panel/meta-data-keys.mdx) you've chosen are copied onto the line when you add it.

If the form says **This key is managed by WCPOS. Choose another key.**, you've typed one of [WCPOS's own keys](#protected-keys). Pick a different key.

## Cart Settings for lines {#cart-settings}

**Cart Settings**, the sliders icon above the cart, sets how the cart shows its lines:

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Cart left / Cart right** — which side of the screen the cart sits on. It starts on the right.
2. **Sort items** — the order of the product lines. See below.

</div>

The rest of the dialog, from open orders to the columns, is covered in [Display Settings](./index.mdx#display-settings).

**Sort items** has four orders:

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={7} offsets={[[380, 0]]} alt="The Sort items list open: Newest at bottom, Newest at top, Name and Price" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Sort items** — **Newest at top** (the default) puts the last product you added first. **Newest at bottom** keeps the order you added them. **Name** sorts A to Z. **Price** goes from the lowest price of one to the highest.

</div>

Whatever you pick, fees and shipping always stay last, in the order you added them.

## When checkout is blocked {#checkout-blocked}

When your store [prevents overselling](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#prevent-overselling), the cart checks every product line against your stock:

- A notice above the lines lists each one that's short: **Only 2 available for Hoodie**, with **Reduce**, which opens that line's quantity, or **Hoodie out of stock**, with **Remove**.
- The short lines are marked in amber with what's available.
- **Checkout** dims. Pressing it takes you to the first line to fix.

Fix every line and **Checkout** comes back. A product on backorder doesn't block the sale.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**"Couldn't update that item — it's no longer in the cart."** The line was removed while you were changing it. Check the cart and make the change again.

**"That item is no longer in the cart."** You pressed **Remove** on a line that had already gone. Nothing else is needed.

**There's no Split link.** Turn on **Split** under the **Qty** column in **Cart Settings**. The link only shows on lines with a quantity above 1.

**Checkout is dimmed.** A line is short of stock. See [When checkout is blocked](#checkout-blocked).

**A custom field won't save.** Its key starts with an underscore and belongs to WCPOS. Choose another key.
