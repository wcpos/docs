Anything you do to the sale as a whole, rather than to one line, lives in **Order details**. Open it with the **⋮** button beside **Checkout**.

## Open Order details {#open}

At the bottom of the cart:

- **Note row** — the customer note, under the totals, once the order has one. Tap it to open **Order details** and change it.
- **⋮** — opens **Order details**.
- **Checkout** — takes payment. See [Checkout](../checkout/index.mdx).

On a tablet the sheet opens beside the cart; on a phone it fills the screen. At the top are the **Form** and **JSON** tabs, then **Customer Note** and the **Hand over** box. Scroll down for the rest.

1. **Send** — passes on a change in **Hand over**. See [Hand the order over](#hand-over).
2. **Currency** — the currency the order is recorded in. See [Change the currency](#currency).
3. **Tax exempt** — takes all tax off the order, fees and shipping included. See [Remove tax from the order](#tax-exempt).

Changes to the fields save as you make them. There's no Save button: **Close** or **✕** puts the sheet away. The one exception is **Hand over**, which waits for **Send**.

## Add a note for the customer {#order-note}

Type in **Customer Note**. The note appears under the cart's totals, so you can see it at a glance; tap that row to change it.

:::warning The customer sees this note
It's the order's customer note in WooCommerce: the customer can read it on their order, and receipt templates can print it. Keep private notes out of it.
:::

## Hand the order over {#hand-over}

**Hand over** decides where the order waits:

- **Status** — open, pending payment, on hold or cancelled. On hold and cancelled take the order out of the cart; pending payment keeps it.
- **Cashier** — hand the order to someone else. After they sync, it waits in their open orders.
- **Store** and **Register** — move the order to another till. They show when your shop has more than one store, or registers to choose from.

To hand it over:

1. Change the **Status**, **Cashier**, **Store** or **Register**. The box turns amber.
2. Check the box: it says whether the order **Leaves this cart** or **Stays in this cart**, and shows each change from before to after.
3. Press **Send**, then confirm.

See [Open Orders](./open-orders.mdx) for how open orders follow a cashier and a register.

## Remove tax from the order {#tax-exempt}

Turn on **Tax exempt** to take all tax off the order, fees and shipping included. Turn it off to put the tax back.

The switch is greyed out while it can't change, and the hint under it says what to do: finish the payment in progress, reopen the order, or, when the order has a coupon, connect to the store. On a store whose prices include tax, the switch doesn't show at all.

## Change the currency {#currency}

:::warning Prices aren't converted
Changing the currency doesn't convert prices. The amounts stay the same.
:::

Press **+ Currency**, then tap the currency to open the list. Type in **Search Currencies** to narrow it down, and tap the one you want.

The order is recorded in that currency, and payments taken on it are recorded in it too. Use it when your store sells in more than one currency and this sale isn't in the default one.

The **Currency** row shows on its own when an order's currency differs from your store's.

## Custom fields and JSON {#order-meta}

Use **+ Custom field** to add extra information to the order, such as a table number or a reference from an outside payment terminal. Type a name in **Key** and the information in **Value**. **Remove** under a row deletes it.

<details>
<summary>Advanced: hidden fields and the order's data</summary>

Each custom field is saved on the order as WooCommerce order meta, under its key. Plugins and reports read it from there.

Keys that start with an underscore are private to the plugin that wrote them. They fold away under **Hidden fields**; press **Show** to see and edit them, **Hide** to put them away. The fields WCPOS keeps for itself never show, and a new field can't use one of their keys: the POS says **This key is managed by WCPOS. Choose another key.**

The **JSON** tab shows the whole order as data. It's for troubleshooting with support, or for a developer checking what a plugin sees.

</details>

## Sync the order {#sync-order}

The POS keeps a new order on this device until it needs your store: at checkout, or when you hand it over. **Sync order** sends it now. The message **Order #… synced** gives the number your store gave it.

Sync an order you're parking for later, so it's safe if this device is cleared. See [Open Orders](./open-orders.mdx).

## Print {#print}

**Print** opens the receipt for the order as it stands, before it's paid: handy as a quote or a bill for the table. See [Printing Receipts](../../receipts/at-checkout.mdx).

## Void the order {#void}

:::warning Cancel payments first
You can't void an order that has a payment on it, finished or in progress. The POS says **This order has payments on it. Reverse them with Cancel in the tender pane, then void.** Go back to checkout, cancel the payment there, then void. See [Cancelling a Payment](../checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
:::

**Void** deletes the order and empties the cart. The message **Order removed** has an **Undo** button if you pressed it by mistake.

- An order that was never synced only existed on this device, so it's gone.
- A synced order is deleted from your store too. If the store won't delete it, it's marked **Cancelled** instead, and the message says **Order voided and marked cancelled**.

A void moves no money, so it works with the register closed.

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Failed to save order.** The sync or hand-over didn't reach your store, and the order is back as it was. Check the connection and try again.

**This order is still on its way to your store.** The store hasn't answered yet. Wait for it rather than sending again; the order updates when the store confirms.

**Send stays grey.** Nothing in **Hand over** has changed yet, or a new store's registers are still loading. Pick a register if the store has some.

**Tax exempt is greyed out.** Read the hint under it. See [Remove tax from the order](#tax-exempt).

**Voided by mistake.** Press **Undo** on the message before it closes. It puts the order back in the cart as it was.
