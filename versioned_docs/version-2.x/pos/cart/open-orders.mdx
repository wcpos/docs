Every sale you start is an open order until it's paid. Keep several going at once: a customer who went back for one more thing, a table running a tab, a phone order waiting for collection. Each one waits in its own tab under the cart, saved on this device, and nothing is lost when you switch away.

:::tip Coming from 1.x?
**Save to Server** is now **Sync order**, and **Void** is no longer a red button beside Checkout. Both are in **Order details**: press **⋮** beside **Checkout**. See [Order details](#order-details).
:::

## Switch between orders {#switch}

The strip under the cart holds one tab per open order, oldest first. Tap a tab and its cart opens.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[74, 0], [70, 0], [-20, 0], [12, -30], [-20, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The POS with several open orders: the strip under the cart with the open order underlined, another order beside it, the count of open orders and the plus button, and above the strip the Order details button and Checkout" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Open cart** — the order on screen, underlined. The first line is the customer, or **Guest**; the second is the total.
2. **Another open order** — tap it to switch. The cart you leave stays as it was.
3. **Count** — how many orders are open. It appears when there are more tabs than fit. Tap it for the [list](#open-orders-list).
4. **+** — starts a [new order](#new-order).
5. **⋮** — [Order details](#order-details): hand the order over, print, sync or void it.
6. **Checkout** — takes payment for the order on screen. See [Checkout](../checkout/index.mdx).

</div>

- **Keyboard:** on the web, with a tab focused, the **Left** and **Right** arrow keys step to the next order.
- **Touch:** swipe the strip to scroll through the tabs.
- **Age:** an order open for an hour or more shows its age beside the total, in a warning colour, so a forgotten sale stands out.
- **Status:** while an order is part-way through checkout, its tab says so instead of showing the total: for example **In checkout**, **Partly paid · $20.00 due** or **Waiting for terminal · $45.00**. See [Split Payments](../checkout/split-payments.mdx).

The strip shows your own open orders on this store and register. A colleague's orders, and yours on another register, wait in their own strips.

## Start a new order {#new-order}

Press **+**. A blank cart opens and the cell reads **+ New**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[3, -41], [22, -42], [181, 0]]} alt="A blank cart: the plus cell at the end of the strip reads New and is underlined, the earlier order waits beside it, and the product search is ready" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **+ New** — the blank cart you're on.
2. **Earlier order** — still open; tap it to go back.
3. **Search** — add the first product. See [Search and Filtering](../product-panel/search-filtering.mdx).

</div>

The blank cart isn't an order yet. It becomes one, and gets its own tab, when you add the first product, so pressing **+** by mistake leaves nothing behind. On a phone, the **Cart** tab's badge reads **New** while the cart is blank.

## See every open order {#open-orders-list}

When the strip can't fit every tab, a count appears at its start. Tap it and **Open orders** lists them all, oldest first.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[0, 0], [83, -23], [0, 0], [-34, 0]]} alt="The Open orders list: one row per open order with the customer, the items, the total, how long it has been open and its status, the order on screen highlighted, and the close button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **An open order** — the customer, or **Guest**, with the total, how long it has been open and its status. Tap the row to switch to it.
2. **Items** — what's in it. With three or more products the count comes first: **3 items · Aim Analog Watch, …**
3. **The order on screen** — highlighted.
4. **Close** — back to the cart.

</div>

Once an order has been open for an hour, its age turns a warning colour, here and on its tab. Use the list to find a sale someone forgot, then finish it or [void it](#void).

## Order details {#order-details}

Press **⋮** beside **Checkout** to open **Order details** for the order on screen. The **Hand over** box decides where the order waits; the buttons along the bottom act on the order.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[-40, 0], [-40, 0], [-40, 0], [-40, 0], [-33, 0], [19, -30], [33, -30], [41, -30]]} alt="Order details for an open order: the Hand over box with Status, Cashier, Store and Register and its Send button, and along the bottom Void, Print and Sync order" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Status** — change it to send the order to the store. See [Change the status](#status).
2. **Cashier** — hand the order to a colleague. See [Hand an order to another cashier](#hand-over).
3. **Store** (WCPOS Pro) — send the order to another of your stores. It only appears when you have more than one.
4. **Register** — send the order to another register.
5. **Send** — sends the changes above. It stays greyed out until you change something.
6. **Void** — removes the order. See [Void an order](#void).
7. **Print** — prints a bill. See [Print a bill](#print).
8. **Sync order** — saves the order to your store now. See [Save an order to the store](#sync-order).

</div>

The rest of the sheet (the customer note, tax exempt, currency and custom fields) is on [Order Actions](./order-actions.mdx).

### Hand an order to another cashier {#hand-over}

At a shift change, hand your open orders to whoever takes over.

1. Open the order and press **⋮**.
2. Choose the colleague under **Cashier**. The box now reads **Leaves this cart**.
3. Press **Send**. The POS asks **Hand this order to …?** with your colleague's name.
4. Press **Send to …**, which names them too.

The order leaves your strip and **Order sent** appears. Once your colleague's POS syncs, it waits in their open orders.

### Send an order to another register or store {#send-elsewhere}

A customer who started at one counter can pay at another. Choose the other till under **Register**, or, with WCPOS Pro and more than one store, the other store under **Store**. A store with a single register gets that register picked for you; with several, choose one before you send.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[-40, 0], [-40, 0], [195, 0], [-33, 0]]} alt="The Hand over box with another store chosen: the Store and Register fields, the summary saying the order leaves this cart, and the Send button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Store** — the store the order is going to.
2. **Register** — the register there.
3. **Summary** — **Leaves this cart**, with what changes, before and after.
4. **Send** — asks before anything is sent.

</div>

Press **Send**. The POS names where the order is going, here **Send this order to AL Store · Register?**, with what changes.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[-31, 0], [94, 44]]} alt="The confirmation: Send this order to AL Store · Register?, the store and register before and after, Cancel, and Send to AL Store · Register" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Cancel** — keeps the order here; nothing is sent.
2. **Send to …** — sends it. **Order sent** appears, the order leaves this cart, and it waits in the open orders there.

</div>

If the send fails, **Failed to save order** appears and the order stays as it was, in your cart. Check your connection and try again.

### Change the status {#status}

**Status** offers **POS - Open**, **Pending payment**, **On hold** and **Cancelled**: the statuses a till may set. When the order already has a status the till can't set, such as a partly paid order, **Status** shows it greyed out.

- **POS - Open** or **Pending payment** keeps the order in this cart. Sending it saves it to the store, and the box reads **Stays in this cart**.
- **On hold** or **Cancelled** sends the order to the store and takes it off your strip. Find it afterwards on the [Orders](../../orders/index.mdx) screen.

### Print a bill {#print}

**Print** in Order details opens the receipt screen for the order before it's paid: a bill to hand to a table, or a quote.

1. Choose the **Template** and the **Printer**.
2. Check the preview. If it says **This template can't be previewed for this order**, as in the picture below, the store hasn't saved the order yet: press **Sync order**, or choose another template. Once the store has the order, the preview appears.
3. Press **Print Receipt**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={7} offsets={[[-37, 0], [-37, 0], [234, -46], [113, 0], [153, 0], [-26, -10]]} alt="The receipt screen for an open order the store hasn't saved yet: the template and printer pickers, the preview area saying this template can't be previewed for this order, Sync order, Print Receipt and Close" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Template** — the layout to print. See [Customising Receipts](../../receipts/customise.mdx).
2. **Printer** — where it prints. See [Print Receipt](../../receipts/at-checkout.mdx#print-receipt).
3. **Preview** — the bill as it will print. Here the order isn't saved at the store yet, so there's no preview.
4. **Sync order** — saves the order to the store, and the preview loads. It only appears while the store hasn't saved the order.
5. **Print Receipt** — prints it.
6. **Close** — back to the cart.

</div>

### Save an order to the store {#sync-order}

An open order is saved on this device as you go, so you can switch away and come back. Press **Sync order** to save it to your store as well. **Order #… synced** appears, with the number the store gave it. This was **Save to Server** in 1.x.

The order stays open, on your strip, until it's paid or sent somewhere else. To serve someone else while it waits, [start a new order](#new-order).

### Void an order {#void}

Press **Void** to remove an order you no longer need. There's no confirmation step. **Order removed** appears with **Undo**; press it straight away if you voided the wrong one. Voiding works with the register closed.

You can't void an order that has a payment on it. The POS says **This order has payments on it. Reverse them with Cancel in the tender pane, then void.** See [Cancelling a Payment](../checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx).

## Move the strip to the top {#position}

The strip sits under the cart by default. To put it above the customer instead, press the settings button at the end of the **Customer** row to open **Cart Settings**, and choose **Top** under **Open orders position**.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Open orders position** — **Bottom**, the default, or **Top**.

</div>

The change applies straight away, on this device only.

</div>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

- **An order I handed over isn't on my colleague's strip.** It arrives when their POS syncs. Check that it went to the right register and store; an order waits only on the strip of the cashier, store and register it was sent to.
- **An order disappeared from my strip.** Its status changed to **On hold**, **Cancelled** or a status the store set, or it was handed to someone else. Find it on the [Orders](../../orders/index.mdx) screen.
- **Void says the order has payments on it.** Cancel each payment in the tender pane first. See [Cancelling a Payment](../checkout/cancelling-a-payment.mdx).
- **Void says "Order voided and marked cancelled".** The store couldn't delete its copy, so it kept the order as **Cancelled** instead. It has still left your strip.
- **Send or Sync order says "Failed to save order".** Nothing changed. Check the connection and try again.
