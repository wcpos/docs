:::info Pro Feature
Refunding from the POS needs [WCPOS Pro](../getting-started/pro-license.mdx). Without Pro, the **Orders** page is a preview only: refund the order in WP Admin under **WooCommerce → Orders**.
:::

A customer brings something back. This page walks through one refund at the till: one watch from a cash sale of two watches and a backpack. A refund is made on the order the customer paid with, so you start from **Orders**, not from the cart.

## Open the order {#open-order}

Open **Orders** from the menu and search for the order number or the customer. Tap the order's row to open it; with a mouse, click the arrow at the end of the row.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[347, 0], [30, -26], [28, -26]]} alt="An order opened from the Orders page: the date, store and cashier line, the items, totals and cash payment, and the Receipt and Refund buttons in the footer" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Order details** — when it was paid, at which store, and by which cashier.
2. **Receipt** — the original receipt, to check what was bought. See [Receipts](../receipts/index.mdx).
3. **Refund** — opens the refund form.

</div>

Check **Items** and **Payment** before you start: they show what the customer bought and how they paid. A cash sale refunds as cash; see [Choose where the money goes](#destination).

**Refund** shows only when the order is **Completed**, **Processing** or **On hold**, and it has reached your store. An order still waiting to sync has no **Refund** button yet.

## Choose what to refund {#choose}

The refund form lists every line of the order. Tap a line's **Refund Qty**, type how many come back, and press **Done**. The refund amount for that line includes its tax.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[350, 98], [497, 97], [0, 0], [497, 87], [57, -40]]} alt="The refund form with one of two watches set to refund: the order total, the refund quantity, the line's refund amount, Custom Amount, the reason, and Process Refund" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Refund Qty** — how many of this line come back. **QTY** beside it is how many are still refundable, so items refunded before are already taken off.
2. **Refund Amount** — what this line refunds, tax included.
3. **Custom Amount** — money to refund that isn't tied to a line, such as a price adjustment. It is added to the line amounts.
4. **Reason** — optional. It is saved with the refund and shown on the order.
5. **Process Refund** — greyed out until the **Refund Total** at the bottom is above zero, and while it is more than is left to refund.

</div>

For a whole-order refund, set every line to its full quantity. When the order has been refunded before, a **Previously Refunded** row under **Total** shows how much.

## Choose where the money goes {#destination}

A cash sale has no choice to make: the refund is recorded as cash handed back from the drawer, as in the walkthrough.

<details>
<summary>Refund a sale paid another way</summary>

When the order was paid another way, the form adds **Refund destination** between **Reason** and **Refund Total**:

- **Refund to *(payment method)*** — sends the money back through the payment method the customer used. It is the default when that payment method can refund from WCPOS. When it can't, this option is greyed out with "Original payment method refunds are unavailable for this order."
- **Refund via cash** — you hand the money back from the drawer, and WCPOS records the refund without contacting the payment method.

</details>

## Confirm the refund {#confirm}

Press **Process Refund**. WCPOS asks you to confirm the amount and the order number.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[132, 0]]} alt="The Confirm Refund dialog asking to refund the amount for the order, with Process Refund" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Process Refund** — sends the refund to your store. A refund needs a connection; it can't wait offline like a sale.

</div>

When the store accepts it, "Refund of *(amount)* processed" appears and you're back on the order. For a cash refund, hand the customer the amount now.

:::warning Not sure it went through?
If WCPOS says "WCPOS couldn't confirm whether this refund went through," don't press **Refund** again straight away. Check **Refunds** on the order first: a second try could refund the customer twice. If the order hasn't updated, check it in WP Admin before trying again. See [PAYMENT311](../error-codes/PAYMENT311.mdx).
:::

## Check the refund on the order {#check}

Once WCPOS has refreshed the order from your store, it shows what was refunded. A part refund marks it **Partially refunded**, and **Payment** gains a **Refund** line with the amount taken off. When everything has been refunded, WooCommerce sets the order to **Refunded**.

Scroll down to **Refunds**. Each refund has its own card: the amount, who made it and when, the reason, and the lines it covered. **Receipt** on the card opens the refund receipt for that refund, to print or send.

## Cash refunds and the drawer {#drawer}

A cash refund made while your register is open is recorded against that register session, and the cash the register expects goes down by it. **In the drawer** on your [cashier sheet](./register/index.mdx#cashier-sheet) drops by the amount, and the register panel lists it as **Cash refunds**. When you [count and close](./register/index.mdx#close), the cash you handed back is already accounted for. See [Reconciliation](../reports/reconciliation.mdx).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

<details>
<summary>There's no Refund button</summary>

The order isn't **Completed**, **Processing** or **On hold**, or it hasn't reached your store yet. Sync the order and open it again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>"…no refund was made"</summary>

Nothing was refunded. Fix the cause the message names, then try again:

- **"You're offline, so no refund was made."** Reconnect first.
- **"No refund was made — sign in, then try again."** Your sign-in expired. Sign in first.
- **"WCPOS was in the background, so no refund was made."** The app was put away before the refund went through. Try again now.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The store refused the refund</summary>

You see your store's own message, or "Failed to process refund". Nothing was refunded. Read the message, fix what it names, and try again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Not sure the refund went through</summary>

"WCPOS couldn't confirm whether this refund went through" means the refund was sent but no answer came back. Open the order and check **Refunds** before you try again: a second try could refund the customer twice. See [PAYMENT311](../error-codes/PAYMENT311.mdx).

</details>

<details>
<summary>The order still shows no refund</summary>

If "Refund of *(amount)* processed" appeared, the refund was made: that message only appears once your store has accepted it. The order on the POS hasn't caught up yet. Don't refund it again; check the order in WP Admin if you need to see the refund now.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Refund to the payment method isn't available</summary>

- **Refund to *(payment method)* is greyed out.** That payment method can't refund from WCPOS. Refund via cash, or refund the order in WP Admin or in the payment provider's own dashboard.
- **"Couldn't verify original payment method refunds. Cash refunds are still available."** WCPOS couldn't check whether the payment method can refund. Refund via cash, or refund it later.

</details>
