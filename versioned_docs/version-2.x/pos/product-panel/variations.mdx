A variable product is one product that comes in options, such as a hoodie in five sizes and three colours. Each option has its own price, stock and barcode, and the option is what goes in the cart. WooCommerce calls the options variations.

## Spot a variable product {#identifying-variable-products}

- **In the grid** — the tile shows a starting price and how many options it has, such as **from $60.00** and **15 options**. With the **Popover** style, it shows a **Variants** badge and its price range instead.
- **In the table** — the row shows how many options it has. With the **Expanded rows** style, it lists the options under the name.

## Pick an option {#adding-variable-products-to-cart}

How a variable product opens is the **Variations** setting, and there are two styles for each view. A new device starts on **Open in place**.

| View | Styles |
| --- | --- |
| **Grid** | [Open in place](#open-in-place) or [Popover](#method-1-quick-popover) |
| **Table** | [Open in place](#open-in-place) or [Expanded rows](#method-2-expand-inline) |

To change it, see [Change how a product opens](#change-style). A [scan](#method-3-barcode-scanning) works the same in every style.

### Open in place {#open-in-place}

Tap the product, and its options fill the panel, one tile each. Tap an option to add it.

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Products** — tap it to go back to the list you came from.
2. **Where you are** — the product you opened.
3. **The product** — its price range and how many options it has. Tap it to go back too.
4. **One option** — tap it to add it to the cart. The chips show its size and colour.
5. **In the cart** — how many of this option are in the cart. You stay here, so you can add another.
6. **The cart line** — the product's name, with the option underneath.
7. **15 options** — the count under the panel now counts this product's options.
8. **In Stock** — while it's on, options that are out of stock are hidden. With it off, they show **Sold out**.

</div>

You can also go back by pressing Escape on a keyboard, swiping in from the left edge on a touch screen, using Android's back button, or typing a new search.

In **Table**, tap the row and the options open as rows, with an **Option** column. Tap a row or its **+** to add it.

### Popover {#method-1-quick-popover}

In **Grid**, with the **Popover** style, tap the product and pick each option from a popover beside it.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} offsets={[[-56, -54], [37, -62], [5, -38], [20, -38], [100, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="A popover beside the Abominable Hoodie tile: Size chips XS to XL with L picked, Color chips Blue, Green and Red with Blue picked, 100 in stock, and Add to Cart: $69.00" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Variants** — this tile has options. Tap it to open the popover.
2. **Size** — one row for each thing the options differ by.
3. **L** — the size you picked.
4. **Green** — tap another chip to change your pick.
5. **100 in stock** — the stock of the option you picked, or **Out of Stock** or **On Backorder**. There's none when the store doesn't track its stock.
6. **Add to Cart: $69.00** — adds the option and closes the popover.

</div>

On a phone, the popover opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen. The numbers are the same as above.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="phone" step={2} offsets={[[51, -513], [33, -61], [3, -40], [18, -38], [89, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="On a phone, the same choices in a sheet at the bottom of the screen: Size L and Color Blue picked, 100 in stock, and Add to Cart: $69.00" />

As you pick, chips that can't make an option are greyed and can't be tapped. When only one choice is left, it's picked for you.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[-57, -63], [39, -26], [34, -26], [46, -26], [55, -26], [41, -26], [0, 0]]} alt="The Hoodie popover with Logo Yes picked: Blue is picked for you, and Green and Red are greyed" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Hoodie** — a product whose options differ by colour and logo.
2. **Yes** — the logo you picked.
3. **No** — tap it to change your pick.
4. **Blue** — the only colour that comes with a logo, so it's picked for you.
5. **Green** — greyed, because there's no green hoodie with a logo.
6. **Red** — greyed for the same reason.
7. **Add to Cart: $45.00** — there's no stock line, because the store doesn't track this option's stock.

</div>

When a list of choices is long, it's a dropdown that reads **Select an option**, and one with more than 10 choices has a search box. A dropdown doesn't grey out choices, so you can pick a mix with no option behind it. Then the popover reads **Variant not available** and there's nothing to add. Pick something else.

### Expanded rows {#method-2-expand-inline}

In **Table**, with the **Expanded rows** style, the options open as rows under the product.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[129, 0], [151, -49], [89, 70], [406, 70], [-356, -2], [9, -26], [9, -27]]} alt="Abominable Hoodie in the table with its rows open: its sizes and colours listed under the name, a 15 variations button, Size and Color filters, and one row per option with its stock, price and a plus button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **15 variations** — opens and closes the rows.
2. **XS, S, M, L, XL** — tap one to open the rows and show only that size or colour.
3. **Size** and **Color** — narrow the rows to one size or colour.
4. **Collapse** — closes the rows.
5. **One option** — its size and colour, and how many are in stock.
6. **+** — adds it to the cart.
7. **›** — opens the [popover](#method-1-quick-popover) for this product instead.

</div>

## Scan an option {#method-3-barcode-scanning}

Scan an option's own barcode, and it goes straight into the cart, whichever style you use. If the option isn't on this device yet, the POS shows **Searching store…**, fetches it, and adds it.

Scan the product's barcode instead, and nothing is added. The POS shows **Choose a variation**, and puts the barcode in the search field so the product is in front of you. Pick the option there.

Which field counts as the barcode is set in [Barcode Field](../../settings/wp-admin/general.mdx#barcode-field). For every other result of a scan, see [Barcode Scanning](barcode-scanning.mdx).

## In the cart {#variations-in-the-cart}

The cart line shows the product's name, with the option underneath, such as **Size: L · Color: Blue**. Add the same option again, and its quantity goes up instead of adding a second line. To change the option, remove the line and add the right one.

## Change how a product opens {#change-style}

Press the settings button beside the search field. **Product settings** opens.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[84, -39], [59, 0], [299, 0], [234, -147], [71, -39], [34, 0], [294, 0]]} alt="Product settings: View Mode set to Grid, Show out-of-stock products off, and Variations set to Open in place, with Popover beside it" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **View Mode** — tiles or rows.
2. **Grid** — its second style is **Popover**.
3. **Table** — its second style is **Expanded rows**.
4. **Show out-of-stock products** — while it's off, options that are out of stock are hidden and a scan of one is refused.
5. **Variations** — how a variable product opens.
6. **Open in place** — where a new device starts.
7. **Popover** — reads **Expanded rows** while **View Mode** is **Table**.

</div>

The setting applies on this device only. For every other setting here, see [Display Settings](display-settings.mdx#variations).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

- **An option is missing** — it's out of stock and **In Stock** is on. Press the cross on the filter, or turn on **Show out-of-stock products**.
- **Add to Cart stays greyed** — the option is out of stock and your store doesn't let the POS sell more than it has. Pick another option, or update the stock in WooCommerce.
- **Variant not available** — no option matches what you picked. Change one of your choices.
- **Syncing…** — the options are still loading onto this device. Wait a moment.
- **Choose a variation** — you scanned the product, not one of its options. Pick the option, or scan the option's own barcode.
- **The wrong option is in the cart** — [remove the line](../checkout/index.mdx#goes-wrong) and add the right one.
