Some products carry extra fields besides their name and price: an engraving message, a supplier code, or a field another plugin adds. WooCommerce calls these meta data. Each field has a **key**, its name (such as `engraving`), and a **value** (such as `Happy Birthday`).

A product's extra fields stay behind when you add it to the cart, unless you name their keys in **Meta Data Keys**. A named field is copied onto the cart line and saved with that line of the order. A cashier can also [add a field to one line by hand](#custom-field), for this sale only.

## Choose the keys {#choose-keys}

1. In the Product Panel, press **Settings** and scroll down to **Meta Data Keys**.
2. Open **Meta Data Keys** and pick a key from the list. If it isn't there, type it and choose **Add "…" as a custom key**.
3. Pick more keys the same way, then press **Close**.
4. Add a product that has the field to the cart. Its name and value show under the product's name on the new line.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} alt="Product settings with Meta Data Keys: the engraving key chosen as a chip, the list open, and gift_message typed into the search with an Add as a custom key row under it" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Learn more** — opens this page.
2. **A chosen key** — this field is copied from every product that has it. Press the cross to stop copying it.
3. **Meta Data Keys** — opens the list of keys found on the products on this device.
4. **Search** — type a key. If it isn't on the list, choose **Add "…" as a custom key** under it. Use that for a key that only appears on products this device hasn't downloaded yet.

</div>

The keys are kept on this device for this store, so set them on every till that sells these products. **Restore Default Settings** at the foot of the sheet clears them along with your other panel settings.

:::note Keys must match exactly
`Engraving` and `engraving` are two different keys. Type the key exactly as the product stores it.
:::

### What gets copied {#what-gets-copied}

- **Only the keys you chose.** Every other field on the product stays behind.
- **Only lines added from now on.** A line already in a cart keeps what it had. Remove it and add the product again to pick up a new key.
- **A variation's own fields.** For a product with options, the field is copied from the option you picked, not from the main product.
- **Where it shows.** A copied field shows under the product's name on the cart line, as `key: value`, and is saved with that line of the order. A key that starts with `_` is copied too, but stays out of sight: see [Fields that start with _](#hidden-fields).

To see these fields in the product list before you add a product, switch to the table view, open **Settings**, and turn on **Meta Data** under **Product Name**.

## Add a field to a line {#custom-field}

A cashier can add a field to one line by hand, such as the message to engrave on this watch.

1. Tap the line's total, or swipe the line, then tap **Edit**.
2. Press **Custom field**, then type the key and the value.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={2} alt="The Edit sheet for an Aim Analog Watch line, with an Engraving field set to Happy Birthday under Custom fields, and the Regular Price and Custom field buttons under it" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Custom fields** — the line's fields, one key and value each. Fields copied by **Meta Data Keys** are listed here too.
2. **A field** — change the key or the value in place. Each change is saved as you make it.
3. **Remove** — takes the field off the line.
4. **Custom field** — adds another empty field.
5. **Regular Price** — other details you can add to the line sit beside it. See [Line Items](../cart/line-items.mdx).

</div>

Press **Close** when you're done. There is nothing else to save. The cart shows the field under the product's name:

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} alt="The cart with the Aim Analog Watch line, and Engraving: Happy Birthday under the product's name" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **The field on the line** — each field shows as `key: value`, and is saved with this line of the order.

</div>

## Fields that start with _ {#hidden-fields}

Fields whose keys start with `_` are saved with the order, but hidden from the cart line, its Edit sheet and receipts.

<details>
<summary>See private fields, or fix a refused key</summary>

These private fields are kept by WCPOS or another plugin, such as `_woocommerce_pos_data`, where WCPOS keeps the line's price and tax status.

A private key that is already on the line, such as `_woocommerce_pos_data`, can't be used for a new field. Paste it into a field's key and the POS refuses it: the key stays empty, and a note at the top of the sheet says **This key is managed by WCPOS. Choose another key.**

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} alt="The line's Edit sheet with a red note at the top, Please fix the following errors, This key is managed by WCPOS. Choose another key., and a new custom field whose key is still empty" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **The refused field** — its key stays empty. Give it a name of your own, one that starts with a letter.
2. **Remove** — takes the unfinished field away again.

</div>

To see every field on the line, private ones included, open the **JSON** tab at the top of the line's sheet:

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} alt="The JSON tab of the line's sheet, listing the line's data with its meta_data, including the private _woocommerce_pos_data field and the Engraving field" />

Other sheets, such as the order's details, fold their private fields into one **Hidden fields** row, with a count. Press **Show** to see and change them, and **Hide** to put them away.

</details>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

- **The field isn't on the line** — check the key's spelling and capitals in **Meta Data Keys**, and check that this product, or this option, has the field. A line added before you chose the key doesn't get it: remove the line and add the product again. On another till, set the key there too.
- **The key you want isn't suggested** — the list only knows the products on this device. Type the key and choose **Add "…" as a custom key**.
- **This key is managed by WCPOS. Choose another key.** — that name belongs to the POS. Give your field another key.
- **The field is on the order but not under the line** — its key starts with `_`. Open the line's **JSON** tab to see it.
