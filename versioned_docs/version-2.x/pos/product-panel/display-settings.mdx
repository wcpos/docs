The settings button (<Icon name="sliders" />) beside the search field opens the Product Panel's display settings. They apply on this device only, so each till can be set up its own way. To see the panel in use, start at [Find a product](index.mdx#find).

## The settings {#display-settings}

### View Mode {#view-mode}

**Grid** shows tiles with pictures, and **Table** shows rows. A new device starts on **Grid**. In **Grid**, **Tile Size** sets how many tiles sit across the panel, from 2 to 8.

### Browse by {#browse-by}

What the panel opens on: **All products**, **Categories**, **Tags**, **Brands** or **Shortcuts** (your [quick filters](#filter-bar)). Each choice shows how many it has, and one with nothing in it is dimmed. A new device starts on **Categories**.

### Show out-of-stock products {#show-out-of-stock-products}

Off, which is where a new device starts, out-of-stock products are hidden and a scan of one is refused. On, they stay in the list and the **In Stock** filter starts switched off.

### Sort {#sort}

**Sort By** picks the order: Name, SKU, Barcode, Type, Price, Date Created, Date Modified, Popularity, Stock Quantity, Stock Status or Menu Order. **Sort Direction** is **Ascending** or **Descending**.

### Variations {#variations}

How a variable product opens. **Open in place**, where a new device starts, shows its options in the panel, one tile each. The other style is **Popover** in **Grid** and **Expanded rows** in **Table**. See [Variable Products](variations.mdx).

### Tile Fields {#tile-fields}

In **Grid**, choose what a tile shows: Product Name, Price, Tax, On Sale, Categories, SKU, Barcode, Stock and Cost of Goods Sold. A new device shows the name and the price.

### Columns {#columns}

In **Table**, choose which columns appear:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Image** | Product thumbnail |
| **Product Name** | Product name and details |
| **SKU** | Stock Keeping Unit (separate column) |
| **Barcode** | Product barcode (separate column) |
| **Type** | Product type (simple, variable, etc.) |
| **Stock** | Current stock quantity |
| **Cost of Goods Sold** | Product cost price |
| **Price** | Selling price |
| **Actions** | Add to cart button |

A new device shows Image, Product Name, Price and Actions. Two columns have extra display options you can toggle on:

| Column | Display options |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Product Name** | Stock, SKU, Barcode, Categories, Tags, Brands, Attributes, Meta Data |
| **Price** | Tax, On Sale |

### Meta Data Keys {#meta-data-keys}

Which custom product fields are copied onto the cart line, and so onto the order. See [Meta Data Keys](meta-data-keys.mdx).

### Filter bar {#filter-bar}

Press **Customise filter bar** to choose what sits on the bar. Drag a filter to move it, or switch it off to take it off the bar. **Add quick filter** makes a button of your own that applies several conditions at once.

### Restore Default Settings {#restore-default-settings}

Puts every setting here back to where a new device starts.

## The panel's footer {#footer}

- **Showing … of …** — how many products are on screen, out of those on this device that match.
- **Tax basis**, such as **Shop base** — shows when your store calculates tax. Tap it to see the tax rates in use.
- **Sync button** (<Icon name="arrow-rotate-right" />) — refreshes products from your store. Press and hold it for two choices: **Sync**, the same refresh, and **Clear and Refresh**, which clears this device's product data and downloads it again.
