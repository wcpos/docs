Display settings decide how the Product Panel looks and where it starts. Press **Settings** beside the search field to open **Product settings**. Each change saves as you make it, on this device only, so each till can be set up its own way. To see the panel in use, start at [Find a product](index.mdx#find).

- [Choose how a product with options opens](#variations)

- [Set up the filter bar and your own filter buttons](#filter-bar)

3. **Browse by** — what the panel opens on. See [Browse by](#browse-by).

### Grid or table {#view-mode}

**Grid** shows each product as a tile: its picture, name and price. Tap a tile to add one to the cart. A product with options shows **… options** and opens [its variations](variations.mdx) instead. **Table** shows the same products as rows, with a **+** at the end of each row to add it.

A new device starts on **Grid**, four tiles across. The grid or table button beside the search field switches between the two without opening the settings.

Once a product is in the cart, its tile shows how many in a small circle in the corner. On a row the number takes the place of the **+**, and tapping it adds one more.

### Browse by {#browse-by}

What the panel opens on:

| Choice | The panel opens on |
|--------|--------------------|
| **All products** | every product, in the [sort order](#sort) |
| **Categories** | one tile per top-level category. Where a new device starts |
| **Tags** | one tile per tag |
| **Brands** | one tile per brand |
| **Shortcuts** | one tile per [quick filter](#quick-filters) |

Each choice shows how many it has, such as **6 categories**. One with nothing in it is dimmed and says so, such as **No brands yet**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={7} offsets={[[-22, 0], [-30, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [282, 297]]} alt="Browsing by category inside Gear: the breadcrumb Categories › Gear, the Gear tile with its back arrow, a tile for each sub-category with its product count, Gear's own products, and the count under the grid" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Categories** — the breadcrumb. Tap any step in it to go back up.
2. **Back arrow** — on the tile of the category you're in. Tap the tile to go back one level.
3. **A sub-category** — how many products it holds. Tap it to go in.
4. **A product** — tap it to add it to the cart, as anywhere in the panel.
5. **3 categories · Showing 20 of 43** — what this level holds: its sub-categories, and how many of its products are on screen.

</div>

Tap a tile to go in. A category shows its sub-categories, its products, or both, as it's set up in WooCommerce. At the top level, an **All products** tile comes first. A search, or a filter on the same thing you browse by, shows the matching products in place of the tiles.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[0, 0], [259, 0]]} alt="Two levels down, inside Watches: the breadcrumb Categories › Gear › Watches, the Watches tile with its back arrow, and the nine watches" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **A product** — Watches has no sub-categories, so it shows only its products.
2. **Watches** — the level you're on. Tap **Gear** or **Categories** to go back up.

</div>

To go back a level, tap the breadcrumb, press **Escape** on a keyboard, swipe in from the left edge of a touch screen, or use the back button on Android. On a phone the breadcrumb shows only the level above, with a back arrow.

In **Table**, the same levels show as rows, each with its count and an arrow.

### Out-of-stock products {#show-out-of-stock-products}

**Show out-of-stock products** is off on a new device. Off, out-of-stock products are hidden and a scan of one is refused. On, they stay in the list, greyed out and marked **Sold out**, and the **In Stock** filter starts switched off.

If your store has [Prevent Overselling](../../settings/wp-admin/checkout.mdx#prevent-overselling) on, the POS also checks stock as you add:

- **… out of stock** or **Only … available for …** — the product isn't added.
- **Stock for … hasn't loaded on this device yet** — the product's stock is still downloading. Try again in a moment.
- **… will be backordered** — the product is added. It's set to allow backorders and notify the customer.

### Sort {#sort}

**Sort By** picks the order: **Name**, **SKU**, **Barcode**, **Type**, **Price**, **Date Created**, **Date Modified**, **Popularity**, **Stock Quantity**, **Stock Status** or **Menu Order**. **Popularity** is total sales, best sellers first when **Descending**. **Menu Order** is the order set on each product in WooCommerce. A new device sorts by **Name**, **Ascending**.

In **Table** you can also tap a column heading to sort by it. That changes **Sort By** here too.

### Variations {#variations}

How a product with options opens. **Open in place**, where a new device starts, shows its options in the panel, one tile each. The other choice is **Popover** in **Grid** and **Expanded rows** in **Table**. See [Variable Products](variations.mdx).

### Tile Fields {#tile-fields}

In **Grid**, switch on what a tile shows: **Product Name**, **Price**, **Tax**, **On Sale**, **Categories**, **SKU**, **Barcode**, **Stock** and **Cost of Goods Sold**. A new device shows the name and the price. With **On Sale** on, a sale price shows the regular price struck through. With **Stock** on, a tile shows how many are left.

## Set up the filter bar {#filter-bar}

Press **Customise filter bar** to choose what sits on the bar above the products.

<div style={{ maxWidth: 512 }}>

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[-24, 0], [-42, 0], [160, 0], [-29, 0]]} alt="The Filter bar settings: a drag handle and a switch beside each filter, and the Add quick filter button" />

</div>

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Drag handle** — drag a filter up or down to move it along the bar.
2. **Switch** — switch a filter off to take it off the bar. Switch it on to bring it back.
3. **Add quick filter** — make a button of your own. See [Your own filter buttons](#quick-filters).
4. **Close** — your changes are already saved.

</div>

A new device has **Stock Status**, **Featured**, **On Sale**, **Categories**, **Tags** and **Brands** on the bar, in that order. To take one off without opening the settings, press and hold it on the bar (right-click with a mouse) and choose **Hide from bar**.

## Your own filter buttons {#quick-filters}

A quick filter is one button that applies several conditions at once, such as **On sale** in the **Shoes** category, sorted by price. Make one from what's on screen or from scratch. Your quick filters can also be the first thing the panel shows: set [Browse by](#browse-by) to **Shortcuts**.

- [Save what's on screen](#save-as-button)
- [Start from scratch](#add-quick-filter)
- [Change a quick filter](#edit-quick-filter)
- [Delete a quick filter](#delete-quick-filter)

### Save what's on screen {#save-as-button}

Set the filters you want on the bar, then press the bookmark at the end of the bar, **Save as button**. It shows once a filter is on.

<div style={{ maxWidth: 512 }}>

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[438, 0], [139, 0], [39, -40]]} alt="Save as button: the Button name field, a preview of the button, and a tick beside each condition it keeps" />

</div>

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Button name** — what the button says on the bar.
2. **Keeps what is on screen now** — tick the conditions to keep. Inside a category, that category and its sub-categories are one of them. A search is left unticked unless you tick it.
3. **Add to bar** — puts the button on the bar.

</div>

Under the ticks it says how many products match on this device. **Cancel** closes it without adding anything.

### Start from scratch {#add-quick-filter}

For example, a button for the shoes on sale:

1. In **Customise filter bar**, press **Add quick filter**.
2. Pick **On sale in a category**. Other starting points are **A category**, **Under a price**, **Best sellers**, **New arrivals**, **Low stock** and **A tag**. To pick nothing, press **Start from scratch**.
3. Under **Button name**, type a name, such as Sale shoes.
4. Under **Show products that are…**, **On sale** is already set. **In category** says **Choose…**: tap it and pick **Shoes**.
5. Under **Then**, press **Order by** and pick **Price, low to high**. Left alone, it keeps **The grid's usual order**.
6. Press **Add to bar**. It stays greyed out until the button has a name and every condition is chosen.

### Change a quick filter {#edit-quick-filter}

To change a quick filter, press and hold it on the bar and choose **Edit**, or tap it in **Customise filter bar**. Changes save as you make them.

### Delete a quick filter {#delete-quick-filter}

Press and hold it on the bar and choose **Remove from bar**, or open it in **Customise filter bar** and press **Remove from bar** at the foot. Confirm with **Delete**. A quick filter can't be switched off and kept: removing it deletes it.

## Start again {#restore-default-settings}

**Restore Default Settings**, at the foot of **Product settings**, puts every setting here back to where a new device starts: **Grid**, four tiles across, **Categories**, sorted by **Name**, and the six standard filters on the bar. It asks nothing first.

:::warning
Restore Default Settings also deletes your quick filters on this device, because they belong to the filter bar.
:::

## The panel's footer {#footer}

- **Showing … of …** — how many products are on screen, out of those on this device that match.
- **Tax basis**, such as **Shop base** — shows when your store calculates tax. Tap it to see the tax rates in use.
- **Sync button** — refreshes products from your store. Press and hold it for two choices: **Sync**, the same refresh, and **Clear and Refresh**, which clears this device's product data and downloads it again.
