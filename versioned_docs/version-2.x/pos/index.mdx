The POS is where you sell. Products sit on one side, the cart on the other, and a menu runs down the edge for everything else. This page is a map of the screen: each part links to the page that explains it.

**Jump to a task:** [Find a product](./product-panel/index.mdx) · [Take payment](./checkout/index.mdx) · [Open the register](./register/index.mdx) · [Open your cashier sheet](#cashier-sheet)

## The menu {#menu}

On a tablet or a computer the menu is a rail of icons down the left edge, always on screen. On a phone it hides behind **☰** at the top left and slides over the screen with the names written out. Tap a name to go there.

</div>

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **POS** — the till, where you sell. See [Checkout](./checkout/index.mdx).
2. **Products** — stock, prices and product details. See [Products](../products/index.mdx).
3. **Orders** — past orders: look one up, reprint the receipt, refund it. See [Orders](../orders/index.mdx).
4. **Coupons** — your store's coupons. See [Coupons](../coupons/index.mdx).
5. **Customers** — add and edit customers. See [Customers](../customers/index.mdx).
6. **Reports** — the day's takings by payment method and cashier, and the register closures. See [Reports](../reports/index.mdx).
7. **Store health** — how this device is keeping up with your store, and any problems it has logged. See [Store Health](../support/store-health.mdx).
8. **Settings** — this device's settings. See [Settings](../settings/index.mdx).
9. **Support** — the WCPOS Discord chat, inside the app. See [Support](../support/index.mdx).

</div>

<details>
<summary>What changes with WCPOS Pro?</summary>

**On the free plugin**, **Products**, **Orders**, **Coupons** and **Customers** open as a blurred preview with an **Upgrade to Pro** panel. You can still pick a customer and apply a coupon in the cart. **Reports** shows today on your register.

**With WCPOS Pro**, **Products**, **Orders**, **Coupons** and **Customers** open for editing, **Reports** goes back beyond today, for every register and store you're allowed to see, and with several stores you switch between them from the bar or your cashier sheet.

See [Free vs Pro](../getting-started/free-vs-pro.mdx#management-screens).

</details>

## The bar at the top {#bar}

Every screen has a bar across the top, and the bell and your avatar are always at its end.

On the POS it's the **register bar**: the store you're selling for, and the register's name when the store has more than one. With WCPOS Pro and several stores, tap the store name to change store. When something needs your attention, a status sits beside the name.

<details>
<summary>What the register status means</summary>

- **Closed**, **Counting**, **Approval needed** or **Overdue** — where the register is in its day. See [Open the Register](./register/index.mdx).
- **Choose register** or **No register** — this device isn't on a register yet. See [Choosing a store and a register](../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#choosing-a-store-and-a-register).
- **Offline**, or **Offline · 2 sales to send** — see [Offline](#goes-wrong) below.
- **1 sale awaiting store** — a sale the store hasn't confirmed yet. It goes by itself; there's nothing to do.

An open register with nothing to say shows no status.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The bar on other screens and the desktop app</summary>

On **Products**, **Orders**, **Coupons** and **Customers**, the search box takes the title's place and names the screen. **Reports** puts its store and register choice there instead.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[34, -28], [260, 0], [10, 40], [10, 40], [10, 40]]} alt="The Products screen with its bar: the Products icon lit in the menu, the search box, the display options, the bell and the avatar, above the product table" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Where you are** — the lit icon in the menu.
2. **Search** — finds records on this screen.
3. **Display options** — which columns the list shows. See [Display Settings](../products/index.mdx#display-settings).
4. **Bell** — the same [notifications](../support/notifications.mdx) as on the POS.
5. **Your avatar** — the same [cashier sheet](#cashier-sheet), from any screen.

</div>

In the desktop app the store name, the status, the bell and your avatar move up into the window's title bar. They come back to the bar when the window is full screen.

</details>

## Your cashier sheet {#cashier-sheet}

Tap your avatar on any screen and your cashier sheet slides in from the right. On a phone it fills the screen.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[130, 0], [230, -15], [180, -19], [-30, 0], [0, -32], [0, -32], [100, 0]]} alt="The cashier sheet over the POS: your name with the store and how long you've been signed in, Your sales today, the drawer tile, Hand the till to, the store with Switch, the WordPress Admin and Desktop App links, and Sign out" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Your sales today** — what you've rung up.
2. **In the drawer** — the cash the register expects. Without permission to view reports it shows a number of sales instead of money. Tap it for pay in, pay out and closing. See [Open the Register](./register/index.mdx#cashier-sheet).
3. **Hand the till to** — pass the till to another cashier without closing the drawer. Tap their name, or **Another account…** to sign them in. See [Open the Register](./register/index.mdx#cashier-sheet).
4. **Store · Switch** — move to another store (WCPOS Pro, with several stores). **Register · Switch** sits below it when the store has several registers.
5. **WordPress Admin** — opens your site's admin in a new tab, for a manager who has access to it.
6. **Desktop App** — the download page for the desktop app. These two links only show in a browser.
7. **Sign out** — if the register is open, the POS asks whether to close it first.

</div>

## Keyboard shortcuts {#keyboard-shortcuts}

With a keyboard, in a browser or the desktop app, these work anywhere on the POS screen, even while you're typing in a field. On a Mac, hold **Option** (⌥) instead of **Alt**.

<details>
<summary>Show keyboard shortcuts</summary>

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Alt` + `F` | Search products |
| `Alt` + `P` | Checkout |
| `Alt` + `N` | New cart |
| `Alt` + `←` / `Alt` + `→` | Previous cart / next cart |
| `Alt` + `D` | Add discount |
| `Alt` + `V` | Void order |
| `Alt` + `/` | Show the shortcuts |
| `Esc` | Back from checkout to the cart, when you're not typing in a field |

A shortcut only does something when it makes sense: **Checkout** needs something in the cart. The keys can't be changed. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](../settings/store/hotkeys.mdx).

</details>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**Offline in the bar.** This device can't reach your store: the internet is down, or your website isn't answering. Keep selling. Cash, and a card taken on your own terminal, still complete. The bar counts what's waiting as **sales to send**, and it goes to your store when it's reachable again. A card reader driven from the till and online gateways need the connection. See [Offline Mode](../getting-started/offline.mdx).

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[80, 0], [52, 26]]} alt="The POS just after the connection dropped: Offline beside the store name in the bar, and the message Device went offline, Keep working, sync resumes when the store is reachable" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Offline** — stays until the store answers again.
2. **Store name** — the store the sales will go to.

</div>

**"Local database unavailable".** A banner over the products and in checkout says scanning, checkout, saving and voiding are blocked.

1. Press **Reload the app**.
2. If the app says it can't reload itself (some phone and tablet apps), close it completely and open it again.
3. If it keeps happening, see [Clear Local Data](../support/troubleshooting/clear-local-data.mdx).

<details>
<summary>What went wrong</summary>

The device's own copy of your store has stopped working. **View status** on the banner opens the [Database](../support/store-health.mdx#database) part of Store health.

</details>

**A red number on Store health.** New errors were logged on this device. Open **Store health** and its logs to read them; the number clears once you've opened the logs. See [Logs](../support/store-health.mdx#logs).

**"This screen doesn't exist."** The address is out of date, often an old bookmark. Press **Back to the start**: it goes to the POS, or to the screen where you connect your store if you're signed out.

**A screen shows "Upgrade to Pro".** That screen needs WCPOS Pro. See **What changes with WCPOS Pro?** under [The menu](#menu), or [Free vs Pro](../getting-started/free-vs-pro.mdx).
