A register session runs from the moment you count the float into the drawer to the moment you count it out again. This page covers the start of the day and everything in between: opening with a float, the drawer figure, and cash paid in and out. Counting and closing at the end of the day are on [Close the Register](../reconciliation.mdx).

:::note This page needs the register switched on
Everything here depends on **Open and close the register**. With it off, the POS goes straight to the cart and there's nothing to open or count. See [Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) for the setting. With WCPOS Pro and stores, each store has its own setting: see [Setting up stores](../../stores/setup.mdx).
:::

## Open the register {#open}

While the register is closed, the register bar reads **Closed** and the products show **Price check only until the register is open.** You can search and read prices, but nothing goes in a cart. The cart column holds the **Open register** card instead:

- **Cash in the drawer** — a large figure, filled in with the float the register suggests: the **Opening float** from [Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register), or the last count if none is set.
- **Default float** and **Last count** — tap one to put that amount back in the figure. Blind cashiers don't see the last count.
- **Keypad** — count the cash in the drawer and type it. The first key you press replaces the suggested amount, so you never clear it first. **⌫** takes off the last digit.
- **Opening variance** — appears when your figure differs from the suggested float. The session keeps it alongside the float you counted.
- **Open register** — starts the session. A message confirms the float you opened with.
- **Last closure** — the number and the count of the closure before this one.

A register already open on another device doesn't ask for a float: this device joins the session. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#several-devices-on-one-register).

## Your cashier sheet {#cashier-sheet}

Tap your avatar at the end of the register bar. This is where the drawer lives, and where you hand over to someone else.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[325, -31], [140, 0], [147, -17], [-57, 0], [-57, 0], [112, 0]]} alt="The cashier sheet: the drawer figure, your sales today, Hand the till to, Switch register, Switch store and Sign out">

1. **In the drawer** — the cash the register expects right now. Tap it to open the [register panel](#cash-movements). Without the *view reports* capability you count blind, so this shows a number of sales instead of money. See [Access](../../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).
2. **Your sales today** — what you've rung up.
3. **Hand the till to** — pass the till to another cashier. The drawer stays open and the POS reloads as them. Open carts wait for their cashier.
4. **Switch register** — only with several registers. Finish or park the current sale first. See [Choosing a store and a register](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#choosing-a-store-and-a-register).
5. **Switch store** — only with several stores (WCPOS Pro).
6. **Sign out** — a register left open stays open for the next cashier, and the POS asks whether to close it first.

</JourneyShot>

## Move cash in and out {#cash-movements}

Tap **In the drawer** on your cashier sheet to open the register panel. It leads with the cash the drawer should hold.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={4} offsets={[[250, 0], [250, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [190, 0], [275, 0], [0, -34], [0, -34]]} alt="The register panel: the cash in the drawer first, session sales, Paid in, Paid out and No sale, Expected in the drawer folded, Other tenders, the last closure, Print X-report and Close register">

1. **In the drawer** — the cash the register expects, updated with every sale and movement. Blind cashiers don't see it.
2. **Session sales** — the total and the number of sales since the register opened.
3. **Paid in** — put cash into the drawer.
4. **Paid out** — take cash out of the drawer.
5. **No sale** — open the drawer with no money moving.
6. **Expected in the drawer** — tap it to unfold the sum behind the drawer figure: the float, cash sales, cash refunds and every movement. **Other tenders** below it unfolds card and the other payment methods.
7. **Last closure** — the closure before this session, with its number, its count and how it ended: **Exact**, **Short** or **Over**.
8. **Print X-report** — a report of the session so far, without closing it.
9. **Close register** — start the count. See [Close the Register](../reconciliation.mdx#count).

</JourneyShot>

### Pay in {#pay-in}

Press **Paid in**. The sheet asks *How much is going into the drawer?* Type the amount, then give a reason. Every movement needs one: tap a reason or **Other…** and type your own.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={5} offsets={[[392, 0], [100, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [150, 0], [0, -34]]} alt="The Paid in sheet: the drawer now and after, the amount, the keypad, the reasons in a grid, the typed reason and the Put in button">

1. **In the drawer, and after** — what the drawer holds now, and what it will hold after this movement.
2. **Amount** — what you typed.
3. **Keypad** — type the amount.
4. **Reasons** — **Float**, **Change** or **Bank**. Tap one to fill in the reason; tap it again to clear it.
5. **Other…** — clears the reason so you can type your own.
6. **Reason** — the reason kept with the movement. Type here directly, or edit the wording a reason filled in.
7. **Put … in** — records the movement. Until there's an amount and a reason, the sheet says which is missing.

</JourneyShot>

### Pay out {#pay-out}

Press **Paid out** for cash leaving the drawer: a supplier paid at the door, petty cash, banking or tips. The sheet asks *How much is leaving the drawer?* and works the same way. On a phone the reasons sit in two columns.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="phone" step={6} maxWidth={390} offsets={[[330, 8], [60, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [150, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The Paid out sheet on a phone: the drawer now and after, the amount, the keypad, the reasons in two columns, the typed reason and the Take out button">

1. **In the drawer, and after** — the drawer now and after the payout.
2. **Amount** — what you typed.
3. **Keypad** — type the amount.
4. **Reasons** — **Supplier**, **Petty cash**, **Bank**, **Tips** or **Change**.
5. **Other…** — type your own reason.
6. **Reason** — the reason kept with the movement.
7. **Take … out** — records the payout.

</JourneyShot>

**No sale** opens the drawer without moving any money. It still asks *Why open the drawer?*: tap **Change**, **Mistake**, **Drawer check** or **Other…**, then press **Open the drawer**.

### See the movements {#movements}

A message confirms each movement, such as **Paid in $1.00**, with **Undo** on it. To see every movement in the session, tap **Expected in the drawer**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={8} offsets={[[250, 0], [200, 0], [150, -42], [150, -56], [0, 26], [0, -34]]} alt="The register panel with Expected in the drawer unfolded: the float, cash sales and refunds, the paid in and paid out lines with their reasons and Void, and Close register">

1. **In the drawer** — already includes the movements.
2. **Float** — what the session opened with, and when. The sum starts here, then adds cash sales and takes off cash refunds.
3. **Paid in** — how many, their total, and each one with its reason.
4. **Paid out** — the same for cash taken out.
5. **Void** — cancels that movement. Only while the register is open.
6. **Close register** — when the day is done. See [Close the Register](../reconciliation.mdx#count).

</JourneyShot>

## Count and close {#close}

At the end of the day, press **Close register** on the panel, count the drawer and file the closure. The count, the closure and what to do when the drawer is out are on [Close the Register](../reconciliation.mdx#count).

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**You entered the wrong movement.** Press **Undo** on the message that appears, or **Void** on its line under **Expected in the drawer**. Either way it leaves the panel and the drawer figure goes back. A voided movement can't be restored: enter it again. Void only works while the register is open, not during a count.

**The button says "Enter an amount" or "Add a reason".** The movement isn't complete. Type an amount, then tap or type a reason.

**The panel says "Figures arrive with the next sync".** The register is waiting for its figures from the store, and **Close register** stays disabled until they arrive. Stay on the page and check the connection.

**A movement is refused.** A notice at the top of the panel says how many the store turned down. Press **Retry** on it.

**The register was closed on another till.** The Open register card says who closed it and when. Count the drawer and open it again.

**Someone else is counting.** "Counting on another till" names who started the count and when. Wait for them to finish.

**"No register for this store" or "Can't reach this store's registers".** The POS has nothing to open. Check the connection and press **Try again**. If the store really has no register, a manager adds one. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#where-the-settings-live).

**The count is out at closing.** See [Close the Register](../reconciliation.mdx#goes-wrong).

:::info With WCPOS Pro
Pro adds more registers per store and the full closure history in Reports. See [More registers with Pro](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#more-registers-with-pro).
:::
