A register session runs from the moment you count the float into the drawer to the moment you count it out again. This page walks through one session, from a closed register to the closure.

:::note This page needs the register switched on
Everything here depends on **Open and close the register**. With it off, the POS goes straight to the cart and there's nothing to open or count. See [Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register) for the setting. With WCPOS Pro and stores, each store has its own setting: see [Setting up stores](../../stores/setup.mdx).
:::

## Open the register {#open}

While the register is closed, you can look up prices but you can't add anything to a cart. Count the cash in the drawer, enter it and press **Open register**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={1} offsets={[[81, 0], [-34, 0], [0, 0], [255, 0], [0, 0]]} alt="The POS with the register closed: the register bar, the price check band above the products, and in the cart column the float field, its suggested amounts, the Open register button and the last closure" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Register status** — **Closed** here. It also reads **Counting**, **Approval needed**, **Offline**, or **Overdue** past closing time. An open register that needs nothing shows no status.
2. **Register bar** — the store this device is on, and the register's name when the store has more than one. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx).
3. **Price check band** — you can search and read prices, but nothing goes in a cart until the register is open.
4. **Cash in the drawer** — what you counted, which becomes the float. It starts filled in with the **Opening float** from [Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register), or the last count if none is set.
5. **Last count** — fills in the count from the last closure. Blind cashiers don't see it.
6. **Open register** — starts the session. If your figure differs from the amount it started with, the card shows the **Opening variance**.
7. **Last closure** — what the previous session closed with. See [Reconciliation](../../reports/reconciliation.mdx).

</div>

A register already open on another device doesn't ask for a float: this device joins the session. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#several-devices-on-one-register).

## Your cashier sheet {#cashier-sheet}

Tap your avatar at the end of the register bar. This is where the drawer lives, and where you hand over to someone else.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={3} offsets={[[325, -31], [140, 0], [147, -17], [-57, 0], [-57, 0], [112, 0]]} alt="The cashier sheet: the drawer figure, your sales today, Hand the till to, Switch register, Switch store and Sign out" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **In the drawer** — the cash the register expects right now. Tap it to open the [register panel](#cash-movements). Without the *view reports* capability you count blind, so this shows a number of sales instead of money. See [Access](../../settings/wp-admin/access.mdx).
2. **Your sales today** — what you've rung up.
3. **Hand the till to** — pass the till to another cashier. The drawer stays open and the POS reloads as them. Open carts wait for their cashier.
4. **Switch register** — only with several registers. Finish or park the current sale first. See [Choosing a store and a register](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#choosing-a-store-and-a-register).
5. **Switch store** — only with several stores (WCPOS Pro).
6. **Sign out** — a register left open stays open for the next cashier, and the POS asks whether to close it first.

</div>

## Move cash in and out {#cash-movements}

Open the register panel from **In the drawer**. Every movement needs a reason: choose one or pick **Other…** and type your own.

- **Paid in** — cash going into the drawer. Press **Put … in**.
- **Paid out** — cash leaving the drawer. Press **Take … out**.
- **No sale** — opens the drawer without moving money, still with a reason. Press **Open the drawer**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={6} offsets={[[169, 0], [174, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [0, 0], [142, 0], [114, 0], [0, 0], [-16, 0]]} alt="The register panel partway through a session: the float, Paid in, Paid out and No sale, the drawer figure, the movements so far, session sales, Print X-report and Close register" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **In the drawer** — the cash the register expects, updated with every sale and movement.
2. **Session sales** — sales rung up since the register opened.
3. **Paid in** — add cash to the drawer.
4. **Paid out** — take cash out of the drawer.
5. **No sale** — open the drawer with no money moving.
6. **Float** — what the session opened with, the first line of the sum that ends at the expected cash.
7. **Movements** — each one sits under its type with its reason. Press **Void** on one to cancel it.
8. **Print X-report** — a report of the session so far, without closing it. See [Reconciliation](../reconciliation.mdx).
9. **Close register** — start the count. See [Count and close](#close).

</div>

## Count and close {#close}

Press **Close register**, count the drawer and enter the total, float included. **Exact** fills in the expected amount, and **Count by notes and coins** adds it up for you. The difference appears as you type; add a **Note** to explain it.

Press **Close & print**. The closure is written and numbered, and you'll see **Closure N written**. Press **Print closure** if you want it on paper, then **Done**.

<JourneyShot manifest={manifest} device="tablet" step={8} offsets={[[150, -26], [-21, 0]]} alt="Closure written: the numbered closure with its counted, expected and variance figures, and the Done button" />

<div className="pinned-legend">

1. **Closure N written** — the numbered closure, with what you counted against what was expected. Closures are kept in [Reports](../../reports/reconciliation.mdx).
2. **Done** — back to a closed register, ready for the next float.

</div>

## If something goes wrong {#goes-wrong}

**You entered the wrong movement.** Press **Undo** on the message that appears, or **Void** on its row in the panel. Either way it leaves the panel and the drawer figure goes back. A voided movement can't be restored: enter it again. Void only works while the register is open, not during a count.

**The count is out.** Enter what you really counted and say why in the **Note**. If the difference is over the limit in **Ask a manager when the count is out by more than** ([Registers](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#one-register)), the button becomes **Approve & close** and a manager signs in with their WordPress username and password. Approval needs a connection.

**The figures say "Figures arrive with the next sync".** The register is waiting for its figures from the store, and **Close register** stays disabled until they arrive. Stay on the page and check the connection.

**A movement is refused.** A notice at the top of the panel says how many the store turned down. Press **Retry** on it.

**Someone else is counting.** "Counting on another till" names who started the count and when. Wait for them to finish.

**"No register" or "Can't reach this store's registers".** The POS has nothing to open. Check the connection and press **Try again**. If the store really has no register, a manager adds one. See [Stores, Registers and Devices](../../stores/registers-and-devices.mdx#where-the-settings-live).

:::info With WCPOS Pro
Pro adds more registers per store and the full closure history in Reports. See [More registers with Pro](../../settings/wp-admin/registers.mdx#more-registers-with-pro).
:::
