**Findings for the Sessions page**

1. **Find a person's devices: visuals.** The screenshot has 4 labels, but several things the text relies on aren't labelled:
   - the **You** chip
   - the session-count badge on each row in the people list
   - the green **Active now** dot, which steps 1 and 1 point the reader to, but which doesn't appear in the shot at all (the latest card says "22 minutes ago")

   The bottom half of the right pane is also empty, so on a phone the whole shot shrinks until the labels can't be read. **Smallest fix:** re-shoot with one session showing "Just now" so the dot appears, add labels for the dot and the count badge, and crop the empty space below the second card.

2. **Sign a device out / Read a session card: structure.**
   - Both procedures open with the same caution, "Ask the person first…", as a numbered step. A warning has been turned into a step.
   - The **Telling devices apart** tip only repeats step 3 of "Sign out one device".
   - The **Active now** tip holds the only definition of the green dot. That is body text placed in a call-out, and the "green dot" links jump down to it.

   **Smallest fix:**
   - Remove step 1 from both procedures and put one line, "Check with the person first: a till can be busy without the green dot", under the "Sign a device out" warning.
   - Delete the **Telling devices apart** tip.
   - Turn **Active now** into plain text: a label on the screenshot, or one line in "Find a person's devices".

3. **Sign out one device / Read a session card: accuracy signals.**
   - "For up to about 25 minutes" sounds like a guess. It is the 30-minute renewal minus the 5-minute dot window, but the page never says so.
   - "A POS in use renews about every 30 minutes" sits beside **Expires**: "Using the POS doesn't push it back." To a non-technical reader, "renews" and "doesn't push it back" contradict each other.

   **Smallest fix:**
   - Replace the 25-minute clause with "a till can be in use without the green dot".
   - Change the **Last active** line to "checks in with the store about every 30 minutes" so "renew" isn't used for something that doesn't extend **Expires**.
