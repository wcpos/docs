**1. Install an extension: visuals and task clarity**
- **What's wrong:** The directory screenshot comes after the four steps, not before them as on the approved pages. Its four labels skip both controls the steps tell you to press: **Install** and **⋮ More actions**. This round's new crop also removed the WordPress sidebar, so the **WP Admin > POS** path in step 1 is no longer shown anywhere. The shot is also small, so on a phone it reads as a thumbnail.
- **Smallest fix:** Move the shot and its labels above step 1. Add two labels, **Install** and **⋮ More actions**. Either put back a strip of the WordPress menu with **POS** labelled, or add one sentence saying where the menu is.

**2. "With WCPOS Pro" call-out, "Without Pro", and "Update, turn off or turn on": accuracy signals**
- **What's wrong:** The call-out says Activate, Deactivate and Auto-update need Pro. But "Without Pro" says a hand-installed extension shows as Active. "Auto-updates" and "The card says Inactive after Install" tell every reader to switch it on or press **Activate** without saying it needs Pro. So a free-version user can't tell whether they can turn on or auto-update the language extensions they installed by hand.
- **Also:** The section heading promises "turn on", but its list has only **Update** and **Deactivate**.
- **Smallest fix:** Add one sentence to "Without Pro" saying where a free user turns these extensions on or off and sets auto-update (for example **WP Admin > Plugins**, if that's right). Add an **Activate** bullet: "turns a stopped extension back on."

**3. Title, "What's in the directory" and the directory cards: consistency and language**
- **What's wrong:** The page title and side menu say "Add-ons", but every heading, step and button on screen says "extension". This round's "also called an add-on" patches over that rather than fixing it.
- The "By category" link says **Languages**, the heading says "Catalog: more than one language", and the tab on screen says **Catalog**.
- The names in the page's list don't match the names on the cards in the WordPress screen: "Email Invoice" vs **Email Invoice Gateway**, and "PayPal Reader (Zettle)" vs **PayPal Reader for WooCommerce**. Someone typing the page's name into **Search extensions...** is looking for a different label.
- The whole task needs a WordPress administrator, but the page never tells a cashier to stop and hand it to one.
- **Smallest fix:** Rename the title to "Extensions" and keep "add-on" only as the synonym. Change the link to "Catalog (languages)". Use the names the cards actually show. Add one line under the title: "Cashiers: ask your store's administrator to do this."
