**1. What the free version shows: visuals**
In the close-up of the upgrade strip, the numbered markers are in the wrong places. Markers 1, 3 and 4 sit on the toolbar row underneath the strip, not on the strip itself. Marker 2 points at "Free Plugin Dev Site" instead of **See what Pro adds**. A reader who matches numbers to the screen will look at the wrong things. On a phone the crop is so small the text can't be read.
**Smallest fix:** crop the shot to the yellow strip only, and put each marker on its own item: the title, **See what Pro adds**, **Upgrade to Pro** and the ✕.

**2. Buy a licence: layering and structure**
"Can't finish buying, or want to change plan? See If something goes wrong" links to the top of that whole section. The answer is actually the third collapsed item, below two long entries. The baseline gave the support email right there in this section. The new version moved the edge case one jump further away and also made it harder to find.
**Smallest fix:** point the link straight at the "You can't finish buying, or want to change plan" item. Alternatively, put back one line: "If you can't finish buying, email support@wcpos.com."

**3. Check the POS runs Pro: visuals, consistency and accuracy**
The About WCPOS screenshot is the only one on the page without numbered markers. The two rows the reader has to check (**WCPOS Pro Plugin** under Server, and **Status** under License) are only described in bullets below it. That breaks the numbered style every approved page uses. The version numbers also don't agree: the text says to tap "v 2.0.0", the free-version screenshot shows v 2.0.3, and the About shot lists the plugin as 2.0.0-beta.2.
**Smallest fix:** put marker 1 on the **WCPOS Pro Plugin** row and marker 2 on **Status: Pro**, and turn the two bullets into the matching numbered labels. Change the text to "tap the version number (for example v 2.0.0)" so a different number doesn't look like a mistake.
