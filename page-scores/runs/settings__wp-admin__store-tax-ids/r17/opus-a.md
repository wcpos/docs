1. **Add a tax number: the General screenshot (Visuals).** The top third of the shot is an empty, unlabelled text box. That box is the Refund & Returns Policy box, which step 1 tells the reader to use as the landmark, but nothing in the shot says so. The six markers sit on the column headings (TYPE, VALUE, COUNTRY, CUSTOM LABEL) and cover them. On a phone the shot shrinks to a strip where none of the headings can be read, so the reader can't match the shot to the screen. **Smallest fix:** add a seventh marker on the empty box, "Refund & Returns Policy — Store tax IDs is just below this". Then move markers 2–6 down onto the fields themselves, so the headings show.

2. **Set the numbers for one store (Pro): step 4 (Task clarity).** One step tells the reader to fill in four fields and also explains three different save rules: the row saves when you leave Value; after that, Type and Country save as soon as you pick them; Value and Label save when you leave the field. Then it describes the Saving… / ✓ Saved message. A reader has to read it twice to find what to do next. The General steps already split the same material into steps 4–6. **Smallest fix:** split it the same way: "4. Type the number in Value and click outside the box. The top corner shows ✓ Saved. 5. Optional: type wording in Label (optional). 6. On a saved row, a new Type or Country saves as soon as you pick it."
