1. **When to Use Cash vs. Original Method: visuals and task clarity.** This is the riskiest choice on the page. A wrong pick throws off the drawer count, and the page has to put a warning about it. But there is no screenshot of the choice. The only refund form shown is for a cash order, and on a cash order the form doesn't ask where the money goes. A cashier who reads "choose **Refund to (payment method)**" or "**Refund via cash**" never sees where those options are.
   **Fix:** add one shot of the refund form on a card order. Label the two destination options and the confirm button, whose text changes to name where the money goes.

2. **The Refund Form and If a Refund Fails: visuals.** Each section shows the same panel twice. First comes a full-width screenshot with no labels, then a cropped copy of the same form. On a phone, the cashier scrolls past the same refund form four times. The offline crop under If a Refund Fails also has no labels, so the greyed-out button and the "Refunds need a connection" line are not pointed out.
   **Fix:** delete the unlabelled full-width shot in The Refund Form, because the labelled crop already covers it. In If a Refund Fails, keep one shot and label the "Refunds need a connection" line and the greyed-out button.

3. **Exchanges: visuals and task clarity.** This round added a new way to do an exchange in one sale, using **Return item** on the quantity keypad as a line of −1. It is now the main exchange method on the page, but it is the only procedure written as text alone. The approved pages show every cart action with numbered labels. Here the cashier gets no picture of the Return item key, of what the −1 line looks like in the cart, or of where to check that the sale "comes to 0 or more". The cashier then has to decide between this method and the two-step one with nothing to look at.
   **Fix:** add one cart screenshot of an exchange in progress. Use three labels: the Return item key, the −1 line, and the total due. Then turn the opening paragraph into numbered steps, matching the two-step list below it.
