## Highest-leverage findings

**1. Turn the register on: Accuracy signals and Visuals**
- **What's wrong:** The new settings screenshot is worth adding, but label 3 now admits the screen is wrong: "The screen's hint says a manager's code; in the POS the manager approves by signing in with their WordPress username and password." Anyone who reads the shot sees "manager's code" in the hint, then reads the opposite in the label. On a phone the shot shrinks to a thumbnail you can't read, and badges 1 and 3 cover the hint text under the controls.
- **Smallest fix:**
  - Retake the shot with the hint corrected to "…needs a manager to sign in…". Until then, move the sentence out of label 3 and put it under "When the count is out".
  - Move badges 1 and 3 to the right edge of their rows so they stop covering the hint text.
  - Crop the shot to the Register card so the text stays readable on a phone.

**2. Turn the register on: Layering**
- **What's wrong:** The baseline's lead-in ("With Pro and at least one store, these settings become site defaults…") has been cut. The steps now only say that the float moves to the store (step 3). Nothing in the steps or labels says that "Ask a manager…" and "Remind the cashier to close at" can also be overridden per store. That fact now appears only in a troubleshooting entry ("Cashiers never see Overdue… a store that customised them doesn't follow General"). Someone setting the reminder at site level gets no warning that a store can override it.
- **Smallest fix:** Add one clause to labels 3 and 4, or one sentence after step 5: "With Pro, a store can set its own rules instead; see Cash drawer rules for one store."

**3. What the cashier sees: Visuals and Accuracy signals**
- **What's wrong:** The "Cash in the drawer" field in the shot reads "$ 115.0000" with four decimals, while the Last count chip next to it says "$115.00". To a cashier this looks like a bug, or like they should type four decimals. The same shot appears in the baseline and in the approved Open the Register page, but those use "$100.00" and similar values elsewhere, so this is the one shot in the set with that flaw.
- **Smallest fix:** Retake the closed-register shot with the field showing "115.00". The labels don't need to change.
