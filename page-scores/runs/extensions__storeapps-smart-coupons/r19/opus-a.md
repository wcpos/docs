1. **Take a gift card at the till · Visuals / Task clarity.** The only cashier task on the page has no screenshot. Each approved page gives every task section one shot with numbered labels and a matching key underneath. Here the cashier has to find **Coupon code**, **Apply coupon**, the lower total, the payment method and **Charge** on a page they have never seen, with nothing to look at. **Fix:** add one numbered shot of the checkout with that tab open. Label the tab, Coupon code, Apply coupon, the order total, the payment method and Charge, and write a one-line key entry for each, in the same style as Checkout's "Take the payment".

2. **Take a gift card at the till (and the WITH WCPOS PRO call-out) · Consistency / Accuracy.** This page calls the tab "Store checkout". The approved Checkout page and its screenshot call the same tab **Legacy** and mark it "(WCPOS Pro)". A cashier looking for "Store checkout" at the till won't find it. The Pro requirement also never appears in "Before you start". It is only in the intro and in the call-out at the bottom, so a store without Pro reads the steps and can't follow them. **Fix:** use the tab name the till actually shows, link it to The Legacy Tab, and add "This needs WCPOS Pro" to the "Before you start" sentence.

3. **If something goes wrong · Structure / Language.** Four admin sections sit between the task and its troubleshooting: What it does, Install the extension, The note on the order, and Refunds and cancellations. Step 4 sends the cashier to the bottom of the page. The first troubleshooting entry then opens with a long error message and a list of coupon types ("percentage, fixed cart and fixed product"). **Fix:** move "If something goes wrong" to just after "What the receipt shows". Shorten its first entry to "Add Discount never takes gift cards. Use the Legacy tab instead (see Take a gift card at the till)", and move the coupon-type detail into the "For developers" section that's already collapsed.
