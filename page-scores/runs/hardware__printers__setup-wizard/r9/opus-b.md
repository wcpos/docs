### Three highest-leverage findings

1. **"Print a test page" and "What the search finds" · Visuals.** The most important judgement on the page is checking the column ruler and then answering *Did the test page print?* That is described only in text. The cards and badges the cashier has to pick from also have no picture. Meanwhile less important panels (No printer found, More options) each get a shot with labels. The cashier has to match prose to a printed slip and a panel they have never seen.
   **Smallest fix:** add one shot of the *Did the test page print?* panel with labels on *Test page n · n characters per line* and each of the three answers. Next to it, add a small image of a correct ruler and a wrong one (wrapped, and stopping short).

2. **"Change a printer later" · Language / Layering / Consistency.** Callouts 6–7 on the new Edit printer shot put internals in the main body: *Web print endpoint*, HTTPS/HTTP, ESC/POS, StarPRNT, Star Line Mode. The "Connection and advanced settings" disclosure sits directly below, which is where these belong. Three other problems in the same block:
   - Callout 3 says Vendor is "Epson or Star Micronics in a browser" but drops "; a list in the apps". That contradicts the same field described a section earlier.
   - The shot labels the width field *Paper width*, but the page calls it *Printer text width*.
   - Callout 1 repeats the earlier Printer Name text word for word.

   **Smallest fix:**
   - Move callouts 6–7 into the "Connection and advanced settings" disclosure.
   - Restore "; a list in the apps" to callout 3.
   - Replace callout 1 with a label on the *Paper width* row ("same as Printer text width").

3. **"No printer found" → "Name, width and drawer" · Structure.** The button, the callout and the link all say **More options** ("See More options"). The section they lead to is titled "Name, width and drawer", and so is its entry in the side contents list. A cashier scanning for "More options" won't find it in under a second.
   **Smallest fix:** rename the heading to "More options: name, width and drawer".
