### Findings

1. **"Choose which payment method comes first", the tender-pane legend (item 2), and "If something goes wrong": consistency and accuracy.** The page calls the second tab the **Store checkout tab** and doesn't say it needs Pro. The approved Checkout page calls the same tab **Legacy tab (WCPOS Pro)**. A cashier moving between the two pages sees two names for one tab and can't tell which is right. **Smallest fix:** in legend item 2, write "Store checkout tab (WCPOS Pro; called Legacy on older versions)". Also log the approved Checkout page for a matching rename.

2. **"Rename a tile, or round cash totals", the ⋮ menu screenshot: visuals.** The text says the menu is open from the **Direct bank transfer** row. But markers 1 and 2 sit on the **Check payments** row, on top of a cut-off "On h…" dropdown. At phone width the menu text is too small to read. The eye lands on the wrong row. **Smallest fix:** crop the shot tighter around the open menu, put the markers on the menu items themselves, and change the sentence to "the menu covers the Check payments row below it."

3. **"Order Status": structure.** The **Send a payment link** expander sits in the middle of the section. After it comes a body paragraph with the default statuses ("Cash, Card and most gateways start on Completed…"). That paragraph is cut off from the list of statuses it explains, and it reads like a footnote to the expander. **Smallest fix:** move the defaults paragraph up to straight after the three bullet points, so the expander closes the section.
