1. **Recount a closure: visuals and structure.** The recount screenshot shows the Manager approval block (WordPress username and password), but it has no number. The new lockout rule ("Too many wrong passwords. This account is locked for 15 minutes.") is in a body paragraph under the legend. It's an error state, and the approved pages put those under "If something goes wrong", which is where a cashier looking at a locked screen would search. **Smallest fix:** put marker 6 on Manager approval, with a legend line: "Manager approval — a manager's WordPress username and password; hidden for users with Approving closures." Then move the lockout sentences into "If something goes wrong" as a bolded entry: **"Too many wrong passwords."** followed by what to do.

2. **Count and close, step 4: accuracy signals.** It says "If **Expected** shows the drawer short or over". But the labelled shot puts short or over on the difference row (callout 5, "Exact"), not on Expected. Callout 4 also says a blind counter sees neither, so a blind counter can't follow step 4 as written. **Smallest fix:** change it to "If the difference under Expected reads short or over (cashiers who count blind won't see it), count again and work through Variance handling."

3. **Open the register and Variance handling, step 4: language.** The links read "(REGISTER221)" and "(see REGISTER101)". These are internal codes that a cashier would never say out loud, and none of the approved pages use them. **Smallest fix:** replace them with the page names, as the approved pages do: "this device joins that session (see Stores, Registers and Devices)" and "with Retry (see Open the Register)".
