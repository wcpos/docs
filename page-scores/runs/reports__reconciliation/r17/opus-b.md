1. **Page order: "Check the closures" through "Export closures", ahead of "Open the register", "During the shift", "Count and close" and "Variance handling". Category: structure (also costs task clarity).** At the till, the cashier's task is to count and close. On this page it starts after the Reports list, reading a closure, recounting, exporting and setup. On a phone that's about five screens down. Three of those sections are for a manager in WP Admin or Pro. The approved pages put the at-the-till sequence first. **Smallest fix:** cut the block from "Open the register" to the end of "Variance handling" and paste it, unchanged, straight after the "Needs the register switched on" call-out. Reorder the five intro bullets to match.

2. **"Count and close", step 4. Category: accuracy signals.** "If **Expected** shows the drawer short or over" points the cashier at the wrong line. In the labelled shot, Expected (4) is just a figure. The short/over result is on the difference row (5). Label 4 also says a blind cashier sees neither, so for them the step can't be followed. **Smallest fix:** "If the line under **Expected** reads short or over, count again and work through Variance handling. (If you count blind you won't see it; count twice.) Say what you found in **Note**."

3. **"Open the register" ("(REGISTER221)") and "Variance handling", item 4 ("see REGISTER101"). Category: language (also consistency).** These links are named with internal codes, which a cashier would never say and can't tell apart. The approved Open the Register page sends readers to the same places by page title: "See Stores, Registers and Devices" and "Press Retry on it." **Smallest fix:** change the link text to "(see Stores, Registers and Devices)" and "(see Open the Register)". Keep the targets as they are.
